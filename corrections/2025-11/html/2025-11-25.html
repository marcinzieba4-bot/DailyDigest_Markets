🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  easing coalition grows / AI rebounds

[5] The stance remains mixed: enough policy support to allow a tactical risk rebound, but insufficient evidence to declare that growth, inflation and liquidity have all turned favourable together.

Lower front-end yields help, but the long end has not delivered a collapse in borrowing costs. Use the rate response as support for a recovery trade rather than proof that financing constraints have vanished.

New / Most Important: Monday's leadership returned to growth: Alphabet gained +6.31%, Broadcom +11.10%, and SOXX +4.38% (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2025-11-24). CNBC describes Broadcom joining Alphabet in the AI rally, while Google's Gemini draws high-profile endorsements. [6] [7] This is a more specific catalyst than a generic claim that all AI shares are cheap. Distribution, model performance and the infrastructure behind specialised chips are competing for attention with the established GPU story. Avoid inferring a permanent winner from a single session. The useful evidence is that investors are rewarding a differentiated route to monetisation alongside renewed confidence in the discount-rate outlook.

Playbook ✓: Stay invested in profitable growth while keeping a substantial quality and liquidity filter. Prefer an Alphabet-led platform position and selective infrastructure exposure to a broad leveraged technology allocation. Fund additions by trimming companies whose cash needs depend on continually improving capital markets. Add front-end duration on orderly yield back-ups, and keep some dollar exposure until credit and market breadth confirm the rebound. The call becomes more constructive if rallies broaden and credit holds firm even on less dovish communication. It becomes defensive again if a rise in volatility accompanies weak customer financing or if the committee's inflation concerns reverse the easing repricing. Good policy headlines are an entry catalyst; repeatable earnings and a funded balance sheet are the reasons to stay.

[12] [13] That mixture can support monetary easing without immediately supporting every domestic consumer stock. New / Most Important: The ECB warns that stablecoins could siphon deposits from euro-area banks, according to Reuters. [15] That is a structural funding warning, not evidence that deposit flight has already occurred. It shows why financial plumbing deserves attention even during a policy pause: a bank's ability to retain deposits can change the earnings benefit of cheaper benchmark rates. Price the risk conditionally through funding costs, disclosure and the durability of customer balances, rather than assuming every technology-enabled payment product has the same effect on every lender.

Playbook ✓: Hold resilient exporters and domestic cash generators selectively, and wait for the budget's actual fiscal arithmetic before increasing UK cyclicals. Consider currency-hedged gilt duration if credibility improves and long yields stabilise. Keep banks tied to specific funding and credit evidence. Upgrade the region if better activity broadens and fiscal risk eases; cut exposure if borrowing costs rise while household demand remains weak. A predictable ECB is useful, but it cannot resolve every bank, sovereign and tax issue investors are being asked to finance.

🇨🇳 China  Bearish  AI attention rises / demand stays soft

Macro Stance: China's technology franchises are gaining attention faster than the domestic economy is recovering. Alibaba's Qwen launch is drawing users and global comparisons, but October lending, investment and housing remain the stronger guides to the broad cycle. Bank loans fell sharply and missed expectations, while the economy faced weak factory and retail-sales growth. [16] [17] That is not the setting for a country-wide cyclical overweight. Keep the macro stance bearish and the technology opportunity selective. An export truce and an attractive consumer application can reduce particular risks without repairing household wealth or making companies willing to borrow. The key change would be stronger demand that persists without repeated price subsidies. Until then, pay for demonstrated earnings rather than the assumption that a successful product must lift every part of the economy.

PMIs & Activity: October's official manufacturing reading missed expectations as the factory slump deepened, and the private services survey slowed. [18] [19] These are a warning about the breadth of demand, even when individual export or technology businesses are doing well. The investment decline remains unusually severe, according to both the WSJ and Bloomberg. [20] [21] Look for orders, profitable sales and better capacity utilisation before widening exposure to industrial cyclicals. The mismatch between strong technological ambitions and weak domestic spending can persist. Producers may be encouraged to invest in strategic areas while households remain careful, leaving investors with a strong output story but a less convincing return on capital. A rise in production is only part of the earnings equation if customers continue demanding lower prices.

Credit Impulse: October's weak lending gives little reason to assume a conventional credit-led rebound has already begun. [16] The hurdle is not just the price of money; it is the willingness of a household or company to take on another obligation. A developer needs buyers, a manufacturer needs profitable orders, and a consumer needs confidence that income can service the debt. Treat policy support as an option until those channels improve. Lending that merely rolls an existing obligation can stabilise a balance sheet without creating new demand. For equity selection, favour companies capable of funding investment internally over businesses whose thesis depends on immediate and broad bank-credit growth. The next credit release matters most if its composition shows better transmission to real activity rather than a headline total that masks continuing caution.

Property: October new-home prices fell at their fastest pace in a year. [22] Bloomberg reports that officials are considering further property stimulus as the crisis continues. [23] That proposal can support a short-term rally, but it is not the same as completed measures or a stabilised market. Home prices influence household confidence, collateral and the appeal of buying before prices fall again. A liquidity package can help a developer service obligations while leaving those demand incentives unchanged. Keep developers and heavily property-dependent businesses underweight until transactions and prices improve together. Distressed equities can disappear before a sound macro argument has time to pay off, so choose instruments with explicit attention to the claim on assets and the amount of new capital required.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Trump describes relations with China as extremely strong after a call with Xi, and Reuters reports that the administration is weighing advanced Nvidia chip sales to China. [24] [25] Neither statement is a completed export authorisation or an assurance that Beijing will accept particular products. Meanwhile, China continues to criticise Japan over Taiwan. [26] These channels should not be merged into one all-clear signal for cross-border technology and travel. A friendlier US conversation can coexist with strategic restrictions and a difficult regional dispute. For investors, the attractive expression is a company that benefits under a range of plausible rules, with any reopening treated as incremental upside. Avoid financing an earnings forecast that requires several governments to change policy in the same direction at once.

Markets & Flows: The Hang Seng rose +1.97% to 25,716.50, KWEB +2.91% and ASHR +0.19%. Over 20 sessions, however, KWEB remained -9.26% and ASHR -5.15% (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2025-11-24). Bloomberg reports national-team-linked ETFs among the most bought during the sell-off. [27] That points to support in a particular buyer channel, not necessarily a broad return of foreign conviction. Separate an intervention-supported floor from a genuine improvement in earnings expectations. The first can make a tactical trade work while the second determines whether an allocation deserves more capital. Currency stability is helpful, but it does not guarantee that weak platform margins or falling property values have been priced correctly.

New / Most Important: Qwen is now a test of the bridge from product attention to earnings. Stocktwits reports downloads crossing 10 million in the first week and plans for a global rollout. [28] That is a concrete attention catalyst, but Bloomberg warns that the delivery price war is pressuring Alibaba and Meituan during the AI race. [29] The right question is whether usage improves the economics of the existing business or simply creates another spending commitment. Tencent offers a useful contrast: its recent results showed strong growth, while Reuters reports that a chip shortage limits cloud growth. [30] [31] A good business can still face physical constraints. Do not turn either user enthusiasm or constrained supply into an automatic assumption of higher next-quarter profit.

Playbook ✓: Underweight broad China beta and developers, but keep a focused position or event watch in profitable internet franchises. Alibaba's product momentum justifies attention rather than an unconditional earnings bet. Require a credible account of spending, competitive subsidies and monetisation before increasing the allocation. Upgrade the macro stance only when household credit, property and activity begin improving together; trim tactical positions if policy support fails to produce durable price and volume confirmation. Keep the Japan dispute in the risk budget for travel and consumer exposures. ⚠ Divergence: Qwen's rapid adoption and Tencent's growth coexist with weak loan demand and falling home prices. That supports selecting companies with independent engines, while resisting the temptation to extrapolate their success into a broad recovery that households and lenders have not yet confirmed.

Playbook ✓: Prefer banks and domestic pricing power to an unhedged, concentrated exporter basket. Take profits on exporter beta if a rapid yen appreciation begins to erode the translation advantage. The stimulus is a support, not a substitute for those confirmations.
