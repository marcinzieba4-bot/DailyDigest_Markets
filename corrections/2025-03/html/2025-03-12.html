New / Most Important: The most important signal is the collision between a funding fight and a tariff drumbeat. On tariffs, House Republicans blocked Congress from challenging Trump's tariffs [7], Trump doubled metals tariffs and then backed off [8], and the Canada hike was reversed after Ontario lifted its electricity threat [9]. The labour market is not the problem yet: job openings rose to 7.7 million in January [10]. Airlines cut their forecasts, citing softer consumer confidence [11], and that is where the data and the tape meet. A firm labour print with a shrinking consumer outlook is the mix that keeps the Fed on hold while equities reprice.

Playbook ✓: Bearish. Keep equity beta light into the Senate vote, and buy protection rather than chase the bounce. Own the front end of the Treasury curve, where the bull steepening is concentrated, and stay underweight the dollar. Avoid consumer-discretionary names and cyclical financials where guidance is being cut. The call changes if the S&P 500 closes back above its 20-session reference of 6,066.44, which would mean the correction is being repaired rather than bounced through, or if a Senate deal removes the shutdown tail without new tariff escalation.

Chinese rapeseed meal and oil contracts surged after 100% tariffs on Canadian imports, per Reuters [49], which shows how fast the tariff map is moving.

Flows are the crowd at the edges. Mainland investors snapped up a record amount of Hong Kong stocks to play AI [50], and Reuters reported that Chinese retail traders are embracing DeepSeek as a nod to quants [51]. The institutional side runs the other way: hedge funds cut China stocks for a fourth week as DeepSeek optimism faded [52].

New / Most Important: The most important item is the summit track rather than any data print. Reuters reported, citing the WSJ, that the two governments had discussed a Trump-Xi summit for June [45], and the WSJ's own exclusive said the same [53]. For Hong Kong and US-listed Chinese names, a confirmed summit is worth more than any PMI. The stimulus debate in the same headlines is about how much Beijing must add if the trade war intensifies [44], and a summit is the one thing that could shrink that need.

Playbook ✓: Mixed. Treat the Hang Seng and KWEB exposure as a trade on the summit headline rather than a call on the economy, and size it for a reversal. Keep the yuan in your model at 7.2591 rather than assuming a stronger currency will carry the rally. Stay away from property developers and from banks whose books run through the property channel. ⚠ Divergence: the Hang Seng's 11.68% gain over 20 sessions is running ahead of a deflation print that turned negative, so the equity rally is pricing a policy turn that the price data have not yet shown.
