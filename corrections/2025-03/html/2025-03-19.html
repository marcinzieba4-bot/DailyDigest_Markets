Hong Kong’s stock market is booming while Wall Street is in turmoil, Bloomberg reports [60], and China’s stock rerating is widening the premiums in the ADRs of Alibaba and Li Auto [61]. The tech leg is AI. Alibaba has revamped its Quark search engine into an AI super assistant [62], and Tencent is pushing open-source 3D generation tools [63].

New / Most Important: The new signal is the scale of the consumption push, paired with an equity market that is trading the policy rather than the property data. The plan to ‘vigorously boost consumption’ is an explicit bid to shore up growth [32], and the package is the most comprehensive in decades [34]. Tariffs are the offset. If the Hang Seng holds its five-session gain of 4.03% while house prices keep falling, the market is paying for a floor under policy, not for a property recovery.

Playbook ✓: Own the China internet and consumption names on pullbacks, through KWEB and the Hong Kong listings, and keep property out of the book. Size for tariff news. The call changes if the next official manufacturing PMI prints below 50 or if the Hang Seng gives back its five-session gain of 4.03%. ⚠ Divergence: home prices are falling faster [45] while the equity market prices a recovery.
