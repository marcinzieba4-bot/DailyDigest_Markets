Historical Analog

The closest analog is a mechanism rather than a dated episode, and it runs in two waves. First come the early call buyers: their options force the dealers who sold them to buy stock as a hedge, and that hedging pushes the price higher. Dealers who sold puts sell stock as the market falls, and the crowd buys protection after the first leg down. Retail is in the second wave for the same reason: the contract is easy to buy after a visible drop, while the first wave of hedgers is already positioned.
