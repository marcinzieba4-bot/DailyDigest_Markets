Historical Analog

The nearest mechanical analog is the GameStop call squeeze of early 2021, and it ran in two waves. First came the early call buyers: their options forced the dealers who had sold them to buy stock as a hedge, and that hedging pushed the price higher. Second came the crowd, which bought the same options after the move was already visible, so the squeeze depended on late buyers continuing to arrive. A crash bet runs the same loop in reverse. Dealers who sold puts sell stock as the market falls, and the crowd buys protection after the first leg down. Retail is in the second wave for the same reason as in 2021: the contract is easy to buy after a visible drop, while the first wave of hedgers is already positioned.
