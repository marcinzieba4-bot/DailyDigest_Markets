🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  resilient demand / cost uncertainty

Macro Stance: American demand remains resilient, but the market is being asked to reconcile that strength with inflation and a more disruptive prospective trade policy. The House Budget Committee's summary of the BEA release puts third-quarter GDP growth at 2.8% and October PCE inflation at 2.3%. Those are different economic measures, and neither establishes an automatic route to faster monetary easing. Reuters reports consumer confidence reached a sixteen-month high after the election, a constructive demand signal whose value still depends on what households actually spend. Keep a balanced stance: the economy offers earnings opportunities, while entry prices and the cost implications of policy deserve more scrutiny than a simple soft-landing slogan. [1] [2] [3]

(Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-11-27; US Treasury, prior close 2024-11-27)

New / Most Important: Reuters reports stocks fell after inflation data, with technology pulling the Nasdaq lower. Company guidance provides a concrete explanation for some of that caution: HP and Dell's weak forecasts triggered a selloff and renewed doubts about the PC recovery. At the policy level, Trump has threatened new tariffs on Canada, Mexico and China, and Reuters reports he has selected Jamieson Greer to advance the trade agenda. Reduce exposure when stronger sales require disproportionate promotion, capital spending or imported inputs. The next attractive trade needs a specific earnings bridge; a lower bond yield or a hopeful policy interpretation should improve that bridge rather than replace it.

[10] [11] [12] Bloomberg reports the ECB's McCaul thinks some private-credit firms appear systemic. That is a supervisory assessment, not evidence that a specific lender has failed. It adds a reason to examine funding structure, valuation and concentration before treating private credit as isolated from the broader financing system. New / Most Important: Reuters reports tariff pledges rattled American and European auto shares. Playbook ✓: Keep broad European autos and refinancing-sensitive cyclicals underweight. Investigate quality businesses where orders and cash generation provide an independent catalyst. Reduce a bearish position when earnings evidence changes, rather than anchoring to a negative regional narrative after the business case has improved.
