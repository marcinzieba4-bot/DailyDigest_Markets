New / Most Important: Trade-policy rhetoric is meeting negotiation and adaptation. Reuters reports new tariff threats aimed at Canada, Mexico and China. Playbook ✓: Keep selective exposure to profitable growth and resilient domestic demand, while reducing reliance on an uninterrupted multiple expansion. Add when operating evidence and the purchase price offer a workable return under several policy outcomes. Reduce positions whose earnings need a precise political timetable or a permanently lower financing benchmark. A balanced stance still allows conviction in a specific company; it simply requires the business to provide more support than the broad market story alone.
