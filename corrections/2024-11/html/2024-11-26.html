🇺🇸 United States  Bullish  bond relief / cash-flow discipline

That is a financing tailwind to examine, rather than a fiscal programme already delivered. The operating backdrop also offers support: jobless claims were at a seven-month low and October home resales rebounded. A lower financing hurdle alongside resilient activity is a better combination than a bond rally caused only by collapsing demand. Keep a constructive US allocation, but demand company evidence before adding the most leveraged expression. New / Most Important: The holiday consumer debate has another company result: Bloomberg reported Bath & Body Works’ shares jumping after an improved outlook. Playbook ✓: Keep the US core constructive and add selectively to businesses whose cash return benefits from the financing relief. Prefer confirmed operating upgrades to a general assumption that every small or expensive company deserves a larger multiple. Maintain exposure to domestic activity, with position size reflecting credit and execution quality. Reduce leverage-sensitive names if yields reverse without a compensating earnings improvement. A wider series of company upgrades would validate more beta; a rally that depends only on policy reassurance warrants more restraint. Keep the holiday event risk small enough to distinguish a sales headline from an actual change in the profit path.

The important distinction is between rolling an existing obligation and funding incremental productive activity. For a listed business, watch borrowing purpose, customer orders and cash collection.
