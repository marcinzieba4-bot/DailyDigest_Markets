Trim the longest-duration sleeve if longer yields keep rising while earnings revisions soften. A firmer dollar alongside weak overseas demand would also argue for more discrimination among international revenue exposures. The constructive call changes if credit deteriorates and activity weakens together: policy support can cushion that combination, but cannot make the near-term earnings loss disappear.
