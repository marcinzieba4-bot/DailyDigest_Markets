🇨🇳 China  Bullish  support is real demand is conditional

[12] [13] An effort to arrest deteriorating confidence can matter before the data turn: households and companies make spending decisions partly from expectations about what authorities will tolerate. That is a reason to retain exposure. It is not a reason to assume the entire adjustment in property, local-government finances and private demand has been completed. A repriced policy response and a recovered economy are different stages of the same possible investment story.

Credit Impulse: The credit channel has become more investable because policy is addressing both borrowing costs and asset-market confidence. The New York Times reports cuts to interest rates and mortgage down payments. [17] Bloomberg reports arrangements allowing funds and brokers to obtain PBOC funding for stock purchases. [18] The first mechanism can encourage eligible households to borrow; the second can directly support financial-asset demand. They should not be conflated into a measured rebound in household credit. A borrower who expects weak income or declining property values may still preserve liquidity. Watch the use of financing and the willingness to commit private capital, rather than treating the availability of money as proof that demand for it has recovered.

Property: Property is still the link most likely to decide whether confidence repair reaches consumption. Barron's describes homeowners' anger and continuing market trouble, while Bloomberg reports authorities weighing the removal of major buying restrictions. [19] [20] Considering a change is not the same as implementing it. Lower barriers can help a buyer already willing to transact, but restoring confidence also requires belief in delivery and the future usefulness of the asset. A household can use mortgage relief to improve its balance sheet rather than spend the saving. That would be financially constructive but slower for the consumer companies priced for an immediate revival. Favor liquid exposure while requiring more evidence before increasing highly leveraged property sensitivity.

[21] Supply discipline may help a particular industry's economics, yet it also highlights the risk of excess capacity and weak pricing. [22] [23] Overseas expansion can continue alongside growing market-access risk. A prudent earnings view therefore separates a company's domestic demand prospects, foreign route to market and ability to earn an adequate return on its production capacity.
