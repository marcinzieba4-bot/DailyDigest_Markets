🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  easing ahead / earnings selective

Macro Stance: The American trade is becoming a contest between cheaper money and the reason money is getting cheaper. CNBC reported that August payrolls increased by 142,000 while unemployment edged down to 4.2%; that combination supports an orderly slowdown, but leaves little room for a renewed loss of hiring momentum. [1] August consumer prices rose 0.2% on the month, according to CNBC, with annual inflation at its lowest since early 2021. [2] The practical implication is to keep exposure to durable earnings while resisting the assumption that every economically sensitive balance sheet deserves the same rerating. Disinflation can support valuations before it restores orders, household purchasing power or the willingness to borrow. A company still has to turn the financial tailwind into cash.

Rates & Curve: The official 2Y Treasury par yield was 3.64% and the 10Y 3.68%, leaving the 10Y−2Y spread at +4 bps. Over twenty observations, the 2Y fell 30 bps and the 10Y fell 15 bps; the spread widened 15 bps. The curve was positive but very flat, with bull steepening (both down, 2Y down more). That describes an easing expectation, not a clean bill of health for lending demand (US Treasury, prior close 2024-09-12). The S&P 500 closed at 5,595.76 index points, VIX at 17.07 index points and DXY at 101.37 index points. Equities can coexist with an anxious rates market, particularly when investors are distinguishing stronger franchises from weaker borrowers (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-09-12).

New / Most Important: The next policy decision is still an event to trade around. Reuters' economist poll anticipated a 25-basis-point cut on September 18, followed by further reductions during the year; it is a survey expectation rather than a Federal Reserve commitment. [3] Meanwhile, Reuters reported that bank shares extended their decline as investors considered executives' sobering forecasts. [4] That is the most useful challenge to an indiscriminate easing trade: a lower funding rate need not compensate for weaker income growth, a poor loan mix or rising business uncertainty. Treat company commentary as the bridge between a plausible soft landing and its earnings consequences. The first beneficiaries of an easier discount rate are not automatically the best beneficiaries of the ensuing economy.

Playbook ✓: Hold profitable growth and a measured duration hedge, adding cyclical exposure only when orders and company outlooks strengthen alongside the rates relief. Goldman Sachs chief David Solomon described the economy as being in pretty good shape, a constructive counterweight to the nervous bank commentary. [5] The trigger for broader risk is confirmation across earnings and credit, rather than the size of a policy move in isolation. Reduce discretionary leverage if weaker business forecasts spread beyond individual companies. Conversely, a resilient employment pulse accompanied by steadier profit expectations would justify rotating part of the hedge into businesses with operating leverage. Keep the election as unresolved policy risk rather than a basis for pretending a single sector already owns the future.

Playbook ✓: Add selective rate beneficiaries when refinancing conditions and operating commentary improve together; keep autos underweight until delivery disruption and outlook risk settle. 🇨🇳 China  Bearish  demand weak / repair selective

Macro Stance: China needs a recovery in private demand that is convincing enough to compete with the balance-sheet damage still working through the economy. Reuters reported rising consumer prices in August alongside persistent producer-price deflation; the South China Morning Post emphasized that consumer inflation missed expectations and left deflation concerns intact. [13] [14] This is a difficult combination for equities. Improving consumer prices do not establish manufacturers’ pricing power, while factory-gate deflation can burden debt service. Stay selective rather than buying the whole market as a delayed version of the American easing trade. The useful question is which companies can collect cash without requiring a rapid reversal in housing, confidence and industrial margins.

PMIs & Activity: The factory signal remains uncomfortable. Reuters described weak factory PMI readings as increasing pressure for consumer stimulus, while CNBC's discussion of the Caixin manufacturing survey was explicitly cautious. [15] [16] These reports belong to the activity baseline rather than a new acceleration thesis. The interpretation should remain tied to orders, production and household demand, not to a bounce in an industrial commodity. The Wall Street Journal reported that industrial profits had continued growing even as demand worries persisted. [17] That is the relevant conflict: profitable pockets can survive inside a weak demand environment, but aggregate profit resilience will be vulnerable if price competition broadens. An investable improvement would bring firmer activity and better pricing together, allowing output growth to translate into earnings instead of merely keeping factories occupied.

Credit Impulse: Liquidity support and a credit impulse are different things. Reuters reported that the central bank rolled over maturing loans and injected cash in August. [18] That can help the financial system manage funding, but it does not establish that households or private companies want to add debt. Reuters also reported that Ant Group was refinancing a credit line, which is a corporate financing process rather than proof of an economy-wide lending boom. [19] For banks, refinancing can preserve business while leaving investment cautious. Look for a turn in borrower confidence, stronger cash collection and less need to extend troubled exposures before treating easier liquidity as a durable earnings catalyst for the entire financial sector.

Property: Housing remains the main obstacle to that confidence. Standard Chartered chief Bill Winters said China's property market had not found a bottom, according to CNBC. [20] Goldman Sachs characterized policymakers' approach as risk containment in separate CNBC coverage. [21] Containment can prevent a disorderly financial accident without restarting the appetite to buy a home. Uncertain housing wealth can encourage saving, while developers conserve cash despite reduced immediate default risk. The South China Morning Post reported that struggling Country Garden had dropped PwC amid the sector's turmoil. [22] That reinforces the need to distinguish operating recovery from financial survival. Favor verifiable collections and completed transactions over optimism about a rescue's announcement effect.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Trade policy adds another layer of selectivity. Reuters reported that China had proposed negotiations with the EU over their electric-vehicle tariff dispute; it also reported a trimming of proposed tariffs on Tesla and other China-made EVs. [23] [24] Neither establishes a completed settlement. For investors, the upside is a less damaging barrier to overseas demand; the downside is a prolonged argument that encourages duplication of factories, pricing concessions and less efficient supply chains. Reuters' account of Lynk & Co's European chief saying it would not raise prices because of tariffs illustrates how the burden can remain with the producer. [25] Avoid equating rising overseas volumes with improving returns on capital. Price discipline and the location of profits may matter more than the headline export opportunity.

Markets & Flows: The market is expressing those doubts unevenly. ASHR closed at 22.50 USD, down 3.23% over twenty sessions, and KWEB at 25.53 USD, down 1.81% over that horizon (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-09-12). This divergence argues against treating offshore platform shares as a clean proxy for domestic activity. The Wall Street Journal reported Alibaba gains on hopes for greater mainland investor demand. [26] Hopes about a marginal buyer can support a stock before consumer spending improves, but the price effect and the underlying operating cycle should remain separate parts of the thesis.

New / Most Important: The most interesting counterweight is technological adaptation. CNBC described Chinese giants' efforts to challenge the United States in AI, while another CNBC report quoted a former Google China head saying local models lagged their American counterparts. [27] [28] Opportunity and execution risk therefore coexist. Domestic substitution can create customers for local suppliers, yet technical progress is not the same as commercial profitability. Reuters Breakingviews described China's banks as suffering from indigestion, a cautionary assessment beside the technology optimism. [29] Favor businesses with a demonstrable route from a product improvement to paying demand. Avoid using an industrial-policy aspiration to excuse uncertain cash flow, or assuming that every local supplier benefits equally from restrictions on imported technology.

Playbook ✓: Keep broad domestic cyclicals underweight; consider a small, explicitly tactical platform allocation only if company revenue and collections improve while the equity market holds its recovery. The Straits Times described difficult housing-rescue arithmetic as prolonging the crisis, making property absorption the crucial test of a more durable call. [30] If housing stabilizes, activity strengthens and banks' credit quality stops deteriorating, increase exposure beyond the offshore winners. If policy support merely reduces immediate distress while households stay defensive, retain liquidity and avoid leveraged developers. An export or technology winner should earn its place through company economics rather than serve as an excuse to dismiss the domestic demand problem. ⚠ Divergence: industrial profits have shown resilience and selected platforms can attract demand even while the broader housing and pricing backdrop remains weak.
