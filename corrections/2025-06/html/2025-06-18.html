🇨🇳 China  Mixed  targeted easing / uneven demand

Macro Stance: China's most useful message is that consumption and production are moving at different speeds. Reuters reported that factories slowed while consumers unexpectedly perked up; Bloomberg cautioned that the consumer boom could be temporary because sentiment remained weak. [14] [15] That supports selective domestic-demand exposure while arguing against a broad reopening-style trade. A subsidy can bring a purchase forward without creating a permanent new income stream. For equities, the distinction is between companies that convert supported spending into repeat demand and those that need ever larger discounts to sustain volume. Keep the first group on the buy list and make the second earn its place. The macro stance is balanced because the improvement is real at the level described, while its durability remains the main question.

PMIs & Activity: The May activity picture needs to be read alongside the earlier manufacturing warning. [16] [17] CNBC's report of faster May retail-sales growth ties the consumption improvement to subsidies. [18] A weaker factory survey should make investors more demanding about the quality of an industrial rebound, while firmer retail activity should prevent a reflexive dismissal of every consumer company. Look for breadth: better orders, steadier hiring intentions and improvement that does not rely entirely on promotional spending. A single supported category can make aggregate consumption look healthier without repairing the rest of the household budget. The more persuasive confirmation would be companies describing sustainable demand alongside a better production pulse, not simply faster shipment growth into a temporary trade window.

Credit Impulse: The policy baseline is easier financing. Reuters reported that China cut key rates to aid the economy as the trade war simmered, and the South China Morning Post described a mortgage-rate cut intended to spur property. [19] [20] Lower funding costs are helpful, but the transmission depends on willing borrowers, viable collateral and the expected return on investment. For a company facing uncertain orders, a cheaper loan can protect liquidity without producing an expansion plan. For a household worried about a property's future value, a lower mortgage payment need not create urgency to buy. Treat credit as an option on confidence recovering. Favour balance sheets that can withstand a slow response and avoid assuming that a policy adjustment automatically produces a new borrowing cycle.

Property: Housing is still the brake on a cleaner domestic recovery. [21] [22] The separate report of authorities stepping in without returning to the old path points toward managed repair rather than an unrestricted return to property-led stimulus. [23] That matters for stock selection. A housing transaction improves confidence only if buyers believe the home will be delivered and its financing remains manageable. A developer can benefit from a funding lifeline without becoming a sound equity investment. Prefer consumer and service companies with less dependence on land appreciation. For housing-linked names, require evidence of transaction stabilisation and healthier cash collection before treating financing relief as earnings recovery.

Fiscal and monetary support at home can cushion this disruption; neither can guarantee unrestricted access to foreign markets. Be careful with the assumption that a diplomatic agreement makes all existing compliance costs disappear.
