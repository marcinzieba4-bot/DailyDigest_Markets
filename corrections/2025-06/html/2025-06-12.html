🇨🇳 China  Bearish  weak demand / conditional truce

Macro Stance: The London framework reduces the immediate probability of another uncontrolled trade escalation, but it does not repair China's domestic pricing power. Reuters reported slower May exports and deeper deflation as tariffs bit; the same weakness makes a headline-led equity rally vulnerable if investors assume a framework is already a recovery. [17] The investment distinction is between avoiding a worse trade outcome and generating stronger nominal demand. A manufacturer can regain access to customers and still face falling selling prices, cautious household purchases and expensive competitive discounting. Keep the broad stance bearish while allowing tactical participation in companies with a specific licence, customer or earnings catalyst.

PMIs & Activity: May manufacturing activity fell into contraction as orders dropped, according to the Wall Street Journal. The latest factory evidence therefore argues for caution about an imminent broad earnings rebound. [18] The April activity baseline was already uneven: Reuters reported slowing industrial and retail-sales growth, with factory resilience sitting beside disappointing retail demand. [19] [20] May's weaker export performance adds an external constraint to that domestic mix. The useful confirmation would be a recovery in new orders accompanied by less discounting, not merely a stronger shipment month after a temporary trade reprieve. Front-loaded orders can improve production schedules without producing durable margins. Prefer evidence of repeat orders and cash collection to a single burst of export activity.

Credit Impulse: The PBOC has injected more liquidity to support growth, China Daily reported, while the May benchmark lending-rate cuts remain part of the policy baseline. [21] [22] Easier money helps lenders supply credit and lowers some borrowers' financing hurdle; it does not force households to buy homes or companies to expand into a price war. The equity transmission depends on who takes the credit and whether the spending earns an adequate return. Watch for financing that funds productive capacity with real customers rather than merely refinancing weak projects. An improving bank-liquidity backdrop can support a trading rally well before private demand strengthens, so do not confuse those stages when setting position size or valuation assumptions.
