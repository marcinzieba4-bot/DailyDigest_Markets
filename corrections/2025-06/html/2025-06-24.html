Rewarded: Jabil's annual forecast increase remains an example of an operating upgrade linked to a named spending channel. Add on a disappointment only when the expected earnings path remains credible after the news. Reduce or hedge when the company needs a new contractual, regulatory or funding solution before the old profit assumptions can be restored. Price discipline works best when it is paired with a precise view of what is being purchased.
