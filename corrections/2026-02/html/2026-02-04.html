🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Guidance over beats: the market pays for AI that earns and sells AI that costs

Rewarded: Meta jumped 10% on a stronger revenue forecast, Palantir rallied after beating estimates, and Caterpillar rose on beats driven by power-generation demand. Over 20 sessions Caterpillar is up 14.1% and Micron 34.4% (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2026-02-03): owners of AI bottlenecks are being paid.

Punished: Microsoft plunged the most since 2020, losing $357 billion of market value; PayPal sank on a CEO exit and a weak 2026 profit forecast; AMD fell as its first-quarter forecast disappointed; and Novo's steep 2026 sales-drop forecast hit the obesity complex.

Pattern: Beats are table stakes; the reaction is set by the guide and by who carries the AI cost. Investors rewarded Meta's revenue forecast and punished Microsoft's results because Meta showed AI revenue while Microsoft showed AI spending. Companies selling into the bottleneck (memory, power equipment) are rewarded; those whose product AI can replicate are sold even on beats.

Attention into EPS: Alphabet reports Wednesday and Amazon Thursday, with Eli Lilly also on the week's slate. The hyperscaler question is capex against cloud revenue: whichever shows more cloud growth per dollar of capex will be treated like Meta, the other like Microsoft.

Playbook: Into prints, own the bottleneck (memory, power equipment, grid) and avoid paying up for capex-heavy names without revenue acceleration. Sell beats with soft guides on the first rally; buy guide-raisers on the first pullback. Change the call if Alphabet and Amazon both raise capex and are rewarded — that would mean the market is paying for AI spend again.
