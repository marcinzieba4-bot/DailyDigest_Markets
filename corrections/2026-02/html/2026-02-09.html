Motivation & Bias: Buy-the-dip conditioning and anchoring to prior highs: a 24.08% 20-session drop in Bitcoin looks like a discount to holders who remember higher prices (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2026-02-08). The bias ignores that leverage, not value, drove the fall.

Instrument & Risk: IBIT closed at 39.68, up 9.92% on Friday but down 22.98% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2026-02-06). Use staged entries and a hard stop; volatility at these levels can erase a week's gain in a session.

Gold / GLD  "retail calls it a bubble"

Observed Attention: A survey finds 55% of individual investors see a bubble in gold CNBC [Feb 06], even as the metal rebounded. Retail attention has shifted from buying the dip to questioning the rally.

Motivation & Bias: Recency bias after the crash makes investors wary, while official buyers keep accumulating. That mismatch tends to favour patient holders over short-term traders.

Instrument & Risk: GLD closed at 455.46, up 3.07% on Friday and 10.69% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2026-02-06). The risk is another margin-driven shakeout; hold physical-backed ETFs rather than leveraged products.
