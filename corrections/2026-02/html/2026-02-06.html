Historical Analog: The analogy is the earlier social-platform monetisation trade. The mechanical similarity is the move from cultural relevance to an earnings model. It does not imply that every active community will earn the same margins or deserve the same multiple. A second wave of shareholders may arrive when monetisation becomes visible, after the easiest story about potential has already travelled.
