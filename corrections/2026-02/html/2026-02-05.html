SpaceX IPO / early index entry  "the access trade"

Observed Attention: SpaceX is seeking early index entry as it prepares a massive IPO WSJ [Feb 04], turning a private company into a retail talking point well before any listing.

Instrument & Risk: Until a listing exists, proxies carry premiums that can collapse on timing news. If early index entry is granted, index funds would need to sell other holdings to make room — a hidden cost for existing megacap holders.
