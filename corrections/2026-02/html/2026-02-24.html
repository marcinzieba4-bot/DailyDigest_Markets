🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Guidance beats the quarter — and AI exposure sets the multiple

Rewarded: Raised outlooks and capital returns: Deere stock jumped after a fiscal-year guidance boost Barron's [Feb 19], Occidental jumped on a $5.8 billion debt cut and dividend hike 24/7 Wall St. [Feb 19], and DoorDash climbed 14% after its miss as investors looked through to growth CNBC [Feb 18].

Punished: Beats with cautious guides: EPAM plummeted 20% on a tepid 2026 outlook despite a beat Barron's [Feb 19], Walmart was sold after a cautious outlook Yahoo Finance [Feb 19], and Microsoft was dumped despite the largest beat in its history Yahoo Finance [Feb 17].

Pattern: The market pays for forward visibility and balance-sheet discipline and de-rates AI-exposed services — IT services, seat-based software — whatever they print. The reaction function is guidance first, AI-disruption risk second, the reported quarter third.

Attention into EPS: Attention is concentrated on Nvidia’s report on Feb. 25 The Motley Fool [Feb 17] and on Home Depot and Lowe’s Investopedia [Feb 23]. With a beat widely assumed for Nvidia, the bar is the guide; for the home-improvement chains it is the housing-turnover outlook.

Playbook: Buy post-earnings dips only where guidance was raised; fade beats that come with cautious outlooks; avoid holding AI-exposed services into prints. Into Nvidia, prefer call spreads over outright stock; into Home Depot, position for a cautious guide.
