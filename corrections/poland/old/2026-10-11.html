🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: The NBP held rates as inflation overshoots the target band. Both Commerzbank and ING published notes this week highlighting caution on inflation. The zloty is under pressure, with USD/PLN hitting an 18-month high.

New / Most Important: The zloty's weakness is a capital-flow story — dollar strength and European fiscal fragility are pulling capital out of CEE.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Poland's real economy is actually outperforming most of Europe, but its currency is trading as an EM risk proxy. This creates an opportunity for those who can separate FX beta from fundamental alpha.
