🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, dovish pivot signaled

Macro Stance: The NBP held rates unchanged at its last meeting but Governor Glapiński turned more dovish, opening the door to rate cuts before end-2026. However, the 2027 inflation projection was revised higher, creating tension.

New / Most Important: The dovish pivot is weighing on PLN — multiple sources flag downside risk for the zloty. The juxtaposition of dovish rhetoric against higher inflation projections is unusual and suggests political pressure on the central bank.

Playbook ✓: Dovish shift → weaker currency, as expected.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Cutting rates while projecting higher inflation is a credibility risk. If the NBP follows through, PLN weakness could accelerate, importing more inflation and creating a self-reinforcing loop.
