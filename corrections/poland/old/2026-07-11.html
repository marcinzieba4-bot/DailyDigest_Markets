🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → Hold, dovish pivot underway

Macro Stance: The NBP held rates unchanged but the governor turned notably more dovish, opening the door to cuts before end-2026. The 2027 inflation projection was revised higher, creating a contradictory signal: dovish rhetoric vs. rising inflation forecasts.

New / Most Important: The dovish pivot is the week's key development. Multiple ING and sell-side notes flagged PLN downside risk as the dovish stance weighs on the zloty. This is a meaningful shift — the NBP had been the most hawkish CEE central bank, and its pivot puts PLN on the wrong side of the carry trade.

Playbook ✓: PLN weakening on dovish shift is textbook FX reaction.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Cutting rates while raising the inflation forecast is a credibility risk. If the NBP follows through with cuts into rising inflation, PLN could underperform CEE peers meaningfully. The consensus had Poland as a "hold forever" central bank — this pivot may catch long-PLN carry trades offside.
