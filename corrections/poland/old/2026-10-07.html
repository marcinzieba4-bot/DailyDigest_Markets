🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: The NBP held rates steady as inflation overshoots the target band. The zloty is under scrutiny as the central bank signals caution — USD/PLN hit an 18-month high for the dollar. The MPC is maintaining a hawkish hold, waiting for clearer disinflation signals before cutting.

New / Most Important: NBP cautious messaging on inflation overshoot reinforced this week by multiple analyst notes (Commerzbank, ING).  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Zloty weakness against the dollar is partly an imported phenomenon (broad USD strength from surging US yields) rather than Poland-specific.
