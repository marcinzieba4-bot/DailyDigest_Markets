🇵🇱 Poland  Bearish (PLN)  → rates on hold / cut path complicated

Macro Stance: The NBP faces a policy dilemma as inflation has reaccelerated, complicating the expected rate cut path. ABN AMRO notes policy divergence weighing on PLN vs EUR, while BNY warns that import pass-through risks could further complicate cuts.

New / Most Important: The zloty slipped as inflation reaccelerated — Commerzbank warned on this trend. The import pass-through channel is the key risk: if PLN weakens on policy divergence expectations (ECB hiking while NBP expected to cut), it feeds back into higher imported inflation, making cuts even harder. This is a classic EM "impossible trinity" squeeze.

Playbook ✓: PLN weakness on dovish divergence vs ECB is on-script.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The reacceleration of inflation may force the NBP to stay on hold far longer than markets expect, trapping Polish rates at elevated levels even as the economy slows. The PLN is caught between two forces — too weak for the NBP to cut, too strong for exporters to thrive.
