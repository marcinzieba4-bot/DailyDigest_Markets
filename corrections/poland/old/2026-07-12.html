🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold, dovish pivot signaled

Macro Stance: The NBP held rates at 3.75% at its recent meeting, but Governor Glapiński turned notably more dovish, "opening the door to rate cuts before end-2026" (ING, Jul 9). The 2027 inflation projection was revised higher, creating an unusual setup where the central bank is dovishly pivoting despite acknowledging stickier inflation ahead.

New / Most Important: The dovish NBP shift is weighing on PLN across multiple analyst desks — ING, TradingPedia, and others all flagged PLN downside risk. This is a clean short PLN setup if the NBP follows through on cuts while inflation projections are rising. The tension between political pressure for easing and an unfavorable inflation backdrop is the key dynamic.

Playbook ✓: PLN weakening on dovish signals is textbook.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The divergence is cutting into rising inflation projections — this is a credibility test for the NBP. If they cut before inflation convincingly declines, the zloty selloff could overshoot, and Polish local-currency bonds become a value trap rather than a duration opportunity.
