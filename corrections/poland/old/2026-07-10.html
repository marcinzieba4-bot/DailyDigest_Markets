🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, dovish pivot signaled

Macro Stance: The NBP held rates at its last meeting but the governor turned materially more dovish, opening the door to cuts before end-2026. This was despite a higher inflation forecast for 2027, creating a classic credibility tension.

New / Most Important: The dovish NBP pivot is weighing on the zloty. ING described the NBP as in a "sweet spot" but the dovish signals are putting pressure on PLN. The 2027 inflation forecast was raised even as the governor signaled cuts—this is the kind of forward guidance that erodes central bank credibility if inflation doesn't cooperate.

Playbook ✓: PLN weakening on dovish signals—textbook.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Signaling cuts while raising the inflation forecast is unusual and suggests political pressure on the NBP. If the ECB is pausing, an NBP cutting into rising inflation would widen the credibility gap and could trigger a more meaningful PLN selloff. Short PLN/long EUR is the cleanest expression.
