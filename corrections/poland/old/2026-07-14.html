🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, dovish pivot

Macro Stance: NBP held rates at its last meeting but Governor Glapiński has turned decisively more dovish, "opening the door to rate cuts before end-2026." This is happening despite higher 2027 inflation projections — a contradiction ING has flagged repeatedly.

New / Most Important: The dovish shift is putting sustained pressure on the zloty. ING has published three separate pieces in one week warning about PLN downside from dovish NBP signals. This is a classic EM-lite dynamic: central bank pivoting dovish while inflation projections rise = currency bears emerge.  |  Playbook ✓: PLN weakness on dovish pivot is textbook.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Cutting rates into rising inflation forecasts would be genuinely unconventional for an EU-aligned central bank. If Glapiński acts on this rhetoric, it could trigger a more significant PLN selloff and widen the credibility gap.
