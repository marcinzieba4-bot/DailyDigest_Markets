🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: NBP on hold, and July's data closed the door on a near-term cut. The GUS flash put July CPI at 3.0% y/y (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 31]). Composition is the point: fuel rose around 14% in a month (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Jul 31]) while food got cheaper, and the jump followed the expiry of the government fuel shield per Magazyn MANAGER+ [Aug 1]. The RPP left rates unchanged at its last decision, yet four banks are still lifting deposit rates (Bankier.pl, [Jul 31]). Parkiet's economist panel argued the RPP should not rush a cut [Jul 31], and Rzeczpospolita's panel framed the question as whether any cut comes this year [Jul 31]. Citi's call that fuel could push CPI above 3.5% by year-end stands. No GUS activity or wage releases this week.

Markets: Warsaw delivered a record. Bankier.pl called July a capital month with the WIG20 a step from its record and Żabka taken over [Jul 31], and Business Insider Polska reported July 31 went into GPW history with a new high [Jul 31], after Strefa Biznesu flagged the exchange could beat its 2007 record [Jul 29]. Session leadership rotated from Dino and CD Projekt to KGHM and the banks, then Żabka and energy on Jul 31 (Inwestycje.pl). The card confirms breadth: EPOL at 42.83 is up 4.7% on the week and 8.6% over four weeks; PKO and Pekao both gained 5.0% on the week; Dino (+11.2% 1W), CD Projekt (+10.5% 1W) and LPP (+7.8% 1W, +17.1% 4W) were the standouts. Energy lagged: Orlen fell 5.1% on the week despite a +11.4% four-week run, PGE lost 3.5%, and KGHM is still down 8.1% over four weeks. The zloty firmed: USD/PLN closed at 3.7357 (-1.8% 1W, -0.4% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3051 (-0.6% 1W, +0.4% 4W), after the dollar broke 3.80 PLN mid-week (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 28]) and returned toward 3.75 on a divided Fed (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Jul 31]). POLGBs are the weak leg: yields hit new highs (wGospodarce, [Jul 25]) and rose worryingly into the Fed decision (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jul 29]).

Fiscal & Politics: The Sejm granted the government discharge for the 2025 budget (PAP, [Jul 31]), but economists warn the deficit and debt are swelling (Zero.pl, [Jul 27]) and Poland ranks near the top of the EU for debt growth (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 27]). Pre-election loosening is building: the tax-free allowance increase is back (Zero.pl, [Jul 25]), possibly spread over three years (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 31]), and the government promises up to 300 zł monthly raises before the vote (INFOR.PL, [Jul 30]). Offsetting that, the Sejm approved a savings tax from 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 25]) and the fuel tax is being redesigned on the bank-levy model rather than dropped (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 27]). Bloomberg reported Poland may revive fuel-price caps [Jul 27], and Domański blamed the president's decision for the CPN funding gap (Money.pl, [Jul 31]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks remain the engine: a major turn in the CHF-mortgage cases gives lenders a chance of sizeable gains (Interia Biznes, [Jul 31]), while NBP named cybersecurity and legal risk as the sector's main risks (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 30]). Retail is the growth story, with Dino and LPP leading and the Żabka takeover resetting valuations. Housing policy is contested: the government floated a new cheaper-mortgage scheme (Money.pl, [Jul 29]) while Pełczyńska-Nałęcz refused to back renewed subsidies (Super Biznes, [Jul 29]). China sanctioned two Polish high-tech institutions (The Diplomat, [Jul 29]).

Geopolitics & Security: A Russian Kh-101 cruise missile overshot Ukraine during a mass strike and came down near Tarnawa-Kolonia in the Lublin region, leaving a 10-meter crater with no injuries (DW, [Jul 30]); NATO's Rutte confirmed it was Russian (Bloomberg, [Jul 30]) and Poland protested to the Russian ambassador (Al Jazeera, [Jul 31]). It flew nearly 100 km into NATO airspace (UNITED24 Media, [Jul 30]), and the six-minute shoot-down window is now the debate (EUobserver, [Aug 1]). Warsaw is hardening the east: 100 km of the border barrier is complete (RMF24, [Jul 31]).

New / Most Important: The July flash at 3.0% confirms the thesis from the global oil shock (+23.3% WTI in 4W): fuel, not demand, is re-accelerating Polish inflation, and with the shield expired and the RPP signalled as patient, 2026 cut optionality is gone. The striking part is that equities made a record and the zloty strengthened in the same week a Russian missile landed in Lublin and POLGB yields hit new highs: stocks price bank earnings and takeover premia, bonds price a fiscal and inflation problem.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold posture consistent with sticky inflation; stay long banks and domestic retail, which carried the record, and underweight duration while yields make highs.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Equities at a record versus bonds at yield highs and a missile on Polish soil; Orlen's -5.1% week against a +11.4% four-week run says energy is already being sold on the fuel-tax redesign.
