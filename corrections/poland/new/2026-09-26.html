🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The week closes with the inflation debate moving from fuel to electricity. Money.pl identifies an unexpected source of inflation that pushes cheap credit further away [Sep 26], wGospodarce says inflation will bite this year [Sep 26] and TVN24 reports a black scenario of power price rises of up to 60% [Sep 26], while the EU warned member states of an energy price crisis (XYZ, [Sep 26]). The RPP's 2027 assumptions see inflation falling but growth slowing too (XYZ, [Sep 25]), an RPP member has indicated a date for a hike (WNP.PL, [Sep 23]) and Kotecki told pb.pl the Council has chased inflation with rates before [Sep 24]. Demand keeps softening: GUS business climate is weakening before the fourth quarter (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 24]), unemployment rose to 5.8% (Warszawa w Pigułce, [Sep 23]) and real wage growth is the lowest in three years (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 22]); PZU expects third-quarter GDP to slow slightly (PAP Biznes, [Sep 25]). Next week brings inflation and labour-market releases (Bankier.pl, [Sep 25]).

Markets: Parkiet summed up the week as ending with a sign of demand fatigue [Sep 25] after Monday's records, when the banks carried the exchange to new highs (Bankier.pl, [Sep 21]); Friday closed with the WIG20 in the red and Tauron sliding after a Fitch decision (XYZ, [Sep 25]). The card, at Friday's close, shows the banks alone holding the gains: PKO at 124.54 is up 3.9% on the week and 9.7% over four weeks, Pekao at 280.3 up 4.0% and 9.7%, and LPP at 24900 still 18.6% higher over four weeks; PGE at 13.02 fell 6.5% on the week, Dino at 34.44 lost 3.6%, KGHM at 330.5 is 8.7% lower over four weeks and Orlen at 148.84 slipped 1.5%. EPOL closed at 44.85, up 1.0% on the week. The zloty ended the week bruised: the euro tested the 4.40 line (Bankier.pl, [Sep 25]) after the currency hit its weakest against the euro since 2023 (TVP World, [Sep 24]), though Money.pl argues Poland itself is not the source of the problem [Sep 26]; the card's Thursday closes were USD/PLN 3.8467 (+1.4% 1W, +3.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.376 (+0.5% 1W, +1.1% 4W). In rates, Parkiet says the record spread between bonds and WIBOR awaits an inevitable adjustment [Sep 26] and Rzeczpospolita called the bond sell-off the worst in years [Sep 24], yet CEO Magazyn notes the rating downgrade failed to rattle the bond market [Sep 26].

Fiscal & Politics: Kotecki's verdict that Poland's finances are on a collision course and only prayer remains (Money.pl, [Sep 26]) frames a week in which Moody's cut to A3 (BigGo Finance, [Sep 19]) was followed by the ministry admitting billions will be missing from the budget (wnp.pl, [Sep 23]), the EBRD questioning the 6.8% deficit target (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]) and Business Insider Polska saying the next downgrade is already priced [Sep 24]. Defence adds to the bill: Bloomberg reports Poland will pay up to 4.4 billion dollars for Fort Trump [Sep 26]. The windfall tax passed the Senate without amendments and now sits with the president (Money.pl, [Sep 24]), who has called the bill unconstitutional and proposed amendments (pb.pl, [Sep 22]). The ministry refuses to index PIT thresholds (Bankier.pl, [Sep 24]) while proposing a new 24% rate (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]) and extended tax equality to same-sex couples married abroad (BiznesINFO.pl, [Sep 26]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen will lift retail petrol to 9.14 zł from Monday amid a dispute over margins and VAT receipts (Agro Profil, [Sep 26]), after raising prices by more than 100 zł (wnp.pl, [Sep 25]); it is taking Energa private at 27.60 zloty a share (marketscreener, [Sep 24]). KGHM is betting on a large investment at Sierra Gorda in the Atacama (zmiedzi.pl, [Sep 26]), days after a miner died at Rudna (TVN24, [Sep 25]). PGE's second Rzeszów waste-to-energy line lifts capacity to 180 thousand tonnes (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 26]), while Rzeczpospolita reports coal-fired generation up more than 20% in surprising operator data [Sep 26]. Erste launched a voluntary tender to raise its stake in Erste Bank Polska (TradingView, [Sep 25]), to 75% (Briefs Finance, [Sep 25]), and three banks are dropping WIBOR from new loans without waiting for January (Bitcoin.pl, [Sep 26]). Dino is building a new logistics hub near Gniezno (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 26]), BitHub.pl asks whether CD Projekt can lift its shares with product news [Sep 26], and key elements of the nuclear contract are agreed, with giant assembly halls at Choczewo first (trojmiasto.wyborcza.pl, [Sep 26]).

Geopolitics & Security: DW expects Russia to intensify its hybrid war on Europe [Sep 26] after a week in which Danish intelligence said Russia could attack a NATO country within months (The Guardian, [Sep 24]), Poland called the Starlink station fire sabotage (BBC, [Sep 24]) and the Dorohusk and Zosin crossings were evacuated (Super Tydzień Chełmski, [Sep 24]). Fighters were airborne again on Friday with RCB alerts in Podkarpacie and Lublin (Goniec, [Sep 25]).

New / Most Important: The week's lesson is that the bond market, not the rating agency, now sets Poland's constraints. The bond-WIBOR spread hit a record while Moody's itself failed to rattle the market, which tells you investors are pricing the fiscal path, not the label: a ministry that will not fill the hole, a Fort Trump bill of 4.4 billion dollars and an energy shock that may push power bills up by 60% in the worst case. The RPP can project lower inflation for 2027, but it is being pushed toward hikes by the zloty at 4.40 and the energy pass-through, while demand already weakens.
