🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, October meeting in play

Macro Stance: The week ends with the fuel cap live and the Council on edge. GUS's September flash put CPI at 4.0% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Sep 30]), the highest in 15 months (Agro Profil, [Sep 30]) and outside the NBP band, with fuel up 36.1% (Farmer.pl, [Oct 2]); ING shows core ex food and energy fell (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 30]) and NBP experts raised their 2027 inflation forecast to 3.2% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 30]). Glapiński said the NBP cannot move energy prices but "will not allow inflation above target to become entrenched" (RadioMaryja.pl, [Oct 2]) and warned the shock "spreads" (Parkiet, [Oct 2]); an RPP member told TVN24 the Council "will be forced to tighten" [Oct 1] and Strefa Biznesu says the October decision "may surprise" [Oct 1], against PKO BP's call for unchanged rates despite a "4 in front" (Bankier.pl, [Sep 29]). The fuel cap is the swing factor: Rzeczpospolita reports economists revising inflation paths after the president's signature [Oct 2] and Euronext notes the cap could ease pressure on the central bank [Oct 2]. Growth signals are steadier: the PMI beat expectations with the industrial decline slowing (Bankier.pl, [Oct 1]) and the 2027 budget assumes 3% growth (TVP World, [Sep 29]). With eurozone inflation at 3.8% (Bankier.pl, [Oct 2]) the ECB hiking path remains the external constraint.

Markets: A week that broke the bank trade. WNP.PL recorded the WIG20's worst session since late August on Thursday [Oct 1], Bankier.pl the worst day in over a month with banks dumped [Oct 1], and Friday closed red with Orlen dragging the index after costing its windfall tax at 2 bn zł (Parkiet, [Oct 2]; Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 2]); Parkiet says investors "are turning away from banks" [Oct 2] and volatility "remains elevated" [Oct 2]. KGHM rose 4.39% on Friday (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 2]) on the Sierra Gorda expansion. The card to Friday's close tells the rotation: PKO 118.24 (-5.1% 1W, +1.1% 4W), Pekao 268.7 (-4.1% 1W, +1.8% 4W), Orlen 144.32 (-3.0% 1W, -8.7% 4W), LPP 24020 (-3.5% 1W, still +12.6% 4W), Dino 33.66 (-2.3% 1W, -6.4% 4W); against them PGE 15.02 (+15.4% 1W, +19.8% 4W), CD Projekt 253.5 (+3.6% 1W, +9.3% 4W), Allegro 50.39 (+1.7% 1W, +10.5% 4W) and KGHM 335.3 (+1.5% 1W, -5.8% 4W). EPOL fell to 43.03 (-4.1% 1W, -3.4% 4W). The zloty: the card's Thursday close is USD/PLN 3.89 (+1.1% 1W, +4.7% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3731 (-0.1% 1W, +1.2% 4W), after the dollar touched 3.90 zł and the franc 4.70 zł (Bankier.pl, [Oct 2]) with US ten-year yields at their highest since 2002 (Comparic.pl, [Oct 1]); pb.pl sees weak US data capable of pulling the dollar to 3.86 [Oct 2]. POLGBs: Parkiet called bonds "cheap again" [Oct 1] and the global sell-off eased slightly on Friday [Oct 2] while pb.pl warned bond losses leave banks less money for lending [Sep 29].

Fiscal & Politics: The president signed the windfall-tax law on fuel companies but is sending it to the Constitutional Tribunal (Polityka Super Express, [Oct 3]; Energetyka24, [Oct 2]); the cap took effect at stations today (blue News, [Oct 3]) and the energy minister forecast pump prices down 1.20 to 1.30 zł per litre from Saturday (pb.pl, [Oct 2]). PKO BP reckons the levy funds only 2.5 months of the CPN programme (wnp.pl, [Oct 2]). Domański told FXMAG there is "no room" for pre-election gifts [Oct 3] while PiS filed to dismiss him and the government defended him (Radio ESKA, [Oct 2]). The 2027 draft stands at 977.6 bn zł of spending and a 281.2 bn zł deficit (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 30]) with the 55% prudential threshold passed in 2028 (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 29]); Dudek says there is "no safety margin left" (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 29]) and Rzeczpospolita calls this "the last year to brake the debt" [Oct 2]. Italy and Czechia want ETS suspended and ETS2 delayed; the Tusk government is not interested (wPolityce.pl, [Oct 2]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen slashed wholesale prices overnight, diesel down 337 zł (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 3]), to levels not seen since late August (wnp.pl, [Oct 3]), on top of its 2 bn zł levy estimate (Bankier.pl, [Oct 2]) and a strategy review with RES spending in question (Interia Biznes, [Oct 1]). KGHM's 725 mln USD Sierra Gorda build will raise copper output by 20% (LCA, [Oct 3]) and the CEO says the group is in an M&A process (BIZNES24, [Oct 2]). Banks are racing to launch OKI accounts in January (Parkiet, [Oct 3]) and the POLSTR switch widened to Bank Millennium and Pekao (PRNews.pl, [Oct 1]; Bank Pekao, [Sep 30]) as WNP.PL warns WIBOR-linked instalments rise by 145, 193 or 242 zł [Sep 30]. Obajtek wants power prices cut by up to 35% (Fakt, [Oct 3]); Poland delayed its first nuclear plant to 2036 (UA.NEWS, [Oct 3]) while domestic industry gears up for the supply chain (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 3]). CD Projekt's Remaster "may bring dizzying money" (Gram.pl, [Oct 2]) with two titles in Steam's top ten (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 2]); Dino builds a 150 mln zł Silesian hub (Urbanity, [Oct 2]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland is easing rules for shooting down drones and missiles entering from Russia (The Kyiv Independent, [Sep 28]) and called Moscow's threats an escalation attempt (UA.NEWS, [Oct 2]) after Putin's "every weapon" warning over Kaliningrad (BBC, [Oct 1]); a Polish general says Russia cannot attack a NATO state now but escalates through hybrid operations (Informat.ro, [Oct 1]).

New / Most Important: Fuel at the pump just got cheaper by decree, and that single fact now dominates the October RPP: headline CPI will mechanically soften while the governor says the shock is spreading. The market has already picked a side, selling banks for five days and paying up for PGE. If the Council holds on the cap, banks have overshot; if it hikes through it, the rout is early.  |  Playbook ✓: Buy the bank dip only with a stop below this week's lows, keep PGE and CD Projekt, hold KGHM into Sierra Gorda, and use any zloty relief toward 3.86 to rebuild dollar hedges.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: PKO down 5.1% on the week while mortgage lending runs at records; a record 281.2 bn zł deficit funded by a levy that covers 2.5 months of its own flagship subsidy.
