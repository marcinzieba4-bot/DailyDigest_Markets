🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, Nov in play

Macro Stance: Sunday brings no new data, so the stance rests on the week's three facts: the RPP held at 3.75% (Strefa Inwestorów [Oct 7]); Glapiński does not foresee a November hike but says the Council is ready to act (PAP Biznes [Oct 8]); and the November projection is the trigger (Forex Club [Oct 7]). The hawkish tail is real. September inflation came in above the reference rate itself (Polskie Radio 24 [Oct 7]), Business Insider Polska [Oct 9] reports the RPP warning about 2027 as fuel and commodity prices climb, and ING expects hikes to arrive in 2027 with inflation able to exceed 3.5% (CEO Magazyn [Oct 8]). Against that, Strefa Biznesu [Oct 8] reads the governor as calming, and Newsmax [Oct 8] notes fuel is not feeding core prices. The growth side keeps improving: GUS raised 2025 GDP to 3.8% (wnp.pl [Oct 6]), xyz.pl [Oct 11] says the economy holds strong against a difficult backdrop even as its growth record is put to the test, and Domański reports more institutions raising forecasts (pb.pl [Oct 6]). Wages are the soft spot: the April median of 7695 zł, about 20% below the average (Business Insider Polska [Oct 7]) and a one-year low in economic sentiment (CEO Magazyn [Oct 7]).

Markets: The week closed with Warsaw up and rotation into energy and consumer names (Parkiet, Inwestycje.pl [Oct 9]). On the card, Orlen at 159.34 is up 10.4% on the week, PGE at 16.7 up 11.1% and 19.0% over four weeks, Dino at 35.81 up 6.4%, LPP at 25040 up 4.2% and 16.1% over four weeks, CD Projekt at 263.0 up 3.7% and 11.7% over four weeks, KGHM at 347.0 up 3.5%, Allegro at 51.75 up 2.7%. Banks are the drag: PKO at 113.94 is down 3.6% on the week and 5.5% over four weeks, Pekao at 261.1 down 2.8%. EPOL closed 43.82, up 1.8% on the week and down 3.8% over four weeks. USD/PLN closed 3.9015, up 0.3% on the week and 4.8% over four weeks; EUR/PLN closed 4.3752, flat on the week and up 1.2% over four weeks. The forecast community is turning: BGK economists now project EUR/PLN falling toward 4.3 (FXMAG [Oct 11]), and the dollar ended the week back under 3.90 (Comparic [Oct 9]) after its Oct 8 high. On rates, a ZBP and SGH report warns bank holdings of government debt may crowd out company lending (Portal Samorządowy [Oct 9]), and wGospodarce [Oct 11] puts it bluntly: state debt is blocking Polish banks. Parkiet [Oct 9] identifies new engines that could pull WIG20 higher.

Fiscal & Politics: The 2027 budget, with its 281.2bn zł deficit and almost 200bn zł for defence (Portal Samorządowy [Oct 8]), is in Sejm committee (Super Biznes [Oct 9]). Weekend coverage is about who pays: Rzeczpospolita [Oct 11] says politicians opened the public purse and Poland is now getting the bill, Business Insider Polska [Oct 11] reports economists floating new taxes as the government looks for money, INFOR [Oct 11] expects the March 2027 pension indexation to disappoint because of excessive debt, and Kuźmiuk of PiS argues every planned 2027 expense will have to be borrowed (wPolityce [Oct 11]). Money.pl [Oct 10] counters that the prudential threshold can be avoided without pain. On energy, Polskie Radio 24 [Oct 11] previews the EU summit on energy prices and asks what happens to Poland's reduced fuel VAT, and wholesale fuel prices stabilised over the weekend after a run of sharp increases (Super Biznes [Oct 11]). The NBP governance row persists after a Sejm commission recommended a State Tribunal vote on Glapiński (Bloomberg [Oct 9]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen's week was a study in contrasts: the stock led the WIG20 while former managers sat in custody over pre-election fuel pricing (TVN24 [Oct 8]), with wPolityce [Oct 11] recalling 2023 petrol sold at 5.99 zł; Orlen Neptun signed a training agreement for offshore wind in Świnoujście (GospodarkaMorska [Oct 11]). Dino's unions are threatening to halt trade just before the holidays (wGoleniowie, Kamienskie.info [Oct 11]), the first labour risk for a stock up 6.4% on the week. CD Projekt carries the Paramount Cyberpunk film (Reuters [Oct 9]) into the new week; KGHM has EU strategic-project status for two investments (StockWatch [Oct 9]). Housing: Kraków secondary prices are almost as high as Warsaw's (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK [Oct 11]), Zero.pl [Oct 11] argues government attempts to lower prices do not work, and Business Insider Polska [Oct 11] explains the statutory mechanism for pausing mortgage instalments. Interia [Oct 11] canvasses scenarios for power price rises, and the government is preparing 4.8bn zł of energy support for industry (PulsHR [Oct 8]). In banks, Millennium's 154m zł of FX provisions (StockWatch [Oct 8]) and the Suwałki WIBOR ruling against PKO Bank Hipoteczny (CHF24 [Oct 8]) remain the overhang.

Geopolitics & Security: Police foiled an alleged plot to attack Prime Minister Tusk and the defence minister (NBC, Al Jazeera [Oct 11]). Poland told NATO to prepare for a deadly Russian hybrid attack (Financial Times [Oct 11]), NATO's commander said Ukraine aid will deter Putin's threat to Europe (Bloomberg [Oct 11]), and Poland and Norway are creating a NATO outpost in the Baltic (News.by [Oct 11]). The Warsaw Security Forum opened on Saturday (TVP World [Oct 10]), and Wąsik wants the eastern barrier built as fast as possible (wPolityce [Oct 11]).

New / Most Important: The assassination plot headlines add a domestic security premium to a week already defined by the NBP governance fight and a budget the opposition says must be entirely borrowed. None of it changes the rate path by itself, but the stack of institutional risk explains why the zloty cannot hold a rally despite 3.8% growth and a WIG near records.

Playbook ✓: Into the new week, stay with energy and retail leadership, keep banks underweight until the sovereign-crowding debate and WIBOR rulings clear, and use any dollar strength above 3.90 to add hedges rather than chase.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: BGK's call for EUR/PLN at 4.3 says the zloty is cheap on fundamentals; the market says fundamentals are not what is being traded, and the gap between the two is the opportunity only if the November projection restores a credible path.
