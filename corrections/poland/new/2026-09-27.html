🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike talk building

Macro Stance: The RPP debate has flipped from when to cut to whether to hike. Kotecki argued for a pre-emptive move, recalling the Council "once already chased inflation with rates" (pb.pl, [Sep 24]), another member pointed to a timing (WNP.PL, [Sep 23]) and Bankier.pl asked whether the Council erred by setting rates too low [Sep 21]. The 2027 policy assumptions say inflation should fall next year while growth slows (XYZ, [Sep 25]; CEO Magazyn, [Sep 27]). Inflation stands at 3.4% (Rankia Polska, [Sep 23]), Credit Agricole sees food pushing it toward 4.5% (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 22]), Domański admits he fears a rise (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]) and money supply hit a record near 3 trillion zloty (TVP World, [Sep 23]). The consumer is softening: August retail sales rose 3.8% y/y against a 4.5% consensus and fell 0.3% m/m (wnp.pl, [Sep 22]); wages rose 5.6% while employment fell 0.8% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 21]), real wage growth is the lowest in three years (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 22]) and unemployment printed 5.8% (Warszawa w Pigułce, [Sep 23]). ING BSK still sees Q3 GDP near 3.5% (wnp.pl, [Sep 22]); the FT describes a central bank trapped in a "political contest" [Sep 25].

Markets: Records on Monday, fatigue by Friday. The WIG20 jumped 2.23% [Sep 21] with banks carrying the index to new highs (Bankier.pl, [Sep 21]), added 0.09% on Tuesday (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 22]), then retreated from the bull-market peaks on profit taking (Parkiet, [Sep 23]); Thursday put WIG-Banki at new maxima led by PKO BP (Investing.com Polska, [Sep 24]) while PGE lost 3% (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 24]), and Friday closed red with Tauron hit by a Fitch decision (XYZ, [Sep 25]) and Parkiet calling the week a signal of buyer exhaustion [Sep 25]. LPP hit an all-time high on results above expectations (Trojmiasto.pl, [Sep 24]) and Allegro launched buyback phase II for up to 800 mln zł (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 23]). The card: PKO 124.54 (+3.9% 1W, +9.7% 4W), Pekao 280.3 (+4.0% 1W), LPP 24900 (+18.6% 4W), CD Projekt 244.6 (+3.5% 1W); laggards PGE 13.02 (-6.5% 1W after +19.6% 4W), Dino 34.44 (-3.6% 1W), KGHM 330.5 (-8.7% 4W), Orlen 148.84 (-1.5% 1W). EPOL closed at 44.85 (+1.0% 1W, +2.9% 4W). The zloty is the weak link: EUR/PLN reached 4.39 (FXMAG, [Sep 24]), the weakest since 2023 (TVP World, [Sep 24]), and tested 4.40 before easing (Bankier.pl, [Sep 25]); USD/PLN touched 3.84 with the zloty among the world's weakest currencies (Comparic.pl, [Sep 23]) before correcting to 3.83 as EUR/USD recovered above 1.14 (Comparic.pl, [Sep 25]). The card has USD/PLN at 3.8467 (+1.4% 1W, +3.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.376 (+0.5% 1W, +1.1% 4W); Money.pl blames the dollar and the Iran war rather than Poland [Sep 26]. POLGBs shrugged off Moody's on Monday (marketscreener, [Sep 21]), but by Thursday Rzeczpospolita reported a sell-off "not seen in years" [Sep 24] and Parkiet flagged a record bond-WIBOR spread awaiting an "inevitable adjustment" [Sep 26]. Primary demand held: the MF sold 12.0 bn zł against 22.5 bn zł of bids (TradingView, [Sep 23]).

Fiscal & Politics: Moody's cut Poland to A3, stable (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 21]), the first downgrade in 24 years (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Sep 21]) and a pre-election blow to Tusk (Bloomberg, [Sep 21]); Business Insider Polska says the next cut is already priced [Sep 24]. The Fiscal Council attacked the 2027 draft for a large risk of breaching the prudential threshold and a miscalculated expenditure rule (INFOR.PL, [Sep 27]), Kotecki said "only prayer remains" (Money.pl, [Sep 26]), debt nears the 55% of GDP threshold (TV Republika, [Sep 20]) and the EBRD doubts the 6.8% deficit target (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]). The answer is tax: PIT and CIT changes hitting lump-sum payers, entrepreneurs and landlords (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 22]), a floated 24% PIT rate (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]) and higher alcohol excise (Money.pl, [Sep 21]). The Senate passed the windfall-tax fuel law unamended (Money.pl, [Sep 24]) to a president who calls it unconstitutional (pb.pl, [Sep 22]); the CPN package may only apply from November (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 24]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: mBank drops WIBOR from new mortgages in October (Parkiet, [Sep 24]) with ING Bank Śląski and Erste Bank Polska already on POLSTR (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 22]; Bankier.pl, [Sep 25]); a negative WIBOR-litigation scenario is costed at up to 284 bn zł (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 23]) though courts side with banks (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 27]). Erste plans a tender to lift its Polish stake by up to 26% (Reuters, [Sep 25]) and PKO BP targets 30% of the OKI market (Bankier.pl, [Sep 24]). Orlen set the Energa squeeze-out at 27.60 zł (marketscreener, [Sep 24]), raised pump prices (wnp.pl, [Sep 25]) and nears an SMR deal with Synthos (pb.pl, [Sep 24]). PGE opened a second Rzeszów waste line costing over 362 mln zł (WNP.PL, [Sep 25]); PEJ closed nuclear contract talks and Polimex won its first nuclear order (pb.pl, [Sep 24]). Brussels warns of an energy price crisis (WysokieNapiecie.pl, [Sep 27]). KGHM's Victoria made Ontario's strategic list (pb.pl, [Sep 24]); Trigon ranks CD Projekt a top gaming pick (Parkiet, [Sep 25]); Dino builds a hub near Gniezno (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 26]). Housing prices in Warsaw and Kraków rose over 10% (Money.pl, [Sep 24]) while creditworthiness fell sharply (Bizblog.pl, [Sep 24]).

Geopolitics & Security: A Russian Mi-8 entered Polish airspace and F-16s scrambled (BBC, [Sep 23]); the Dorohusk and Zosin crossings were evacuated during missile strikes (Super Tydzień Chełmski, [Sep 24]); Warsaw called the Starlink station fire sabotage (BBC, [Sep 24]). Danish intelligence warns Russia could attack a NATO state within months (The Guardian, [Sep 24]). MON is fortifying the eastern border with Patriots and 23 shelters (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 22]); Fort Trump may cost up to $4.4 billion (Bloomberg, [Sep 26]) and $53 billion of defence spending risks the EU's largest deficit (LIGA.net, [Sep 27]).

New / Most Important: The downgrade did not break equities, but the zloty at 2023 lows, record POLGB spreads and a Council openly discussing hikes say the fiscal-monetary mix is being repriced. A hike into a slowing consumer helps banks twice while rate-sensitive retail and bonds pay.  |  Playbook ✓: Stay with bank leadership, fade the PGE spike, keep POLGB duration short until the bond-WIBOR spread adjusts, and read zloty weakness as dollar-driven rather than a Poland credit event.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: WIG at records versus a first downgrade in 24 years and a Fiscal Council threshold warning; deep auction cover versus the worst secondary sell-off in years.
