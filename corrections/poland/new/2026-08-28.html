🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, cut path complicated

Macro Stance: The NBP is on hold at 3.75% with inflation pressure described as moderate (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]), but the debate inside the RPP has shifted: the minutes revealed which members wanted a hike (Tysol.pl, [Aug 22]), the Council expects inflation to persist and worries about global market turbulence (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 27]), and the question now asked is whether the next move could be up (Tysol.pl, [Aug 26]). The two-sided squeeze remains: ABN AMRO's divergence against a hawkish ECB and BNY's import pass-through risk. Activity holds up: July retail sales rose 3.9% y/y in constant prices (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 24]), slightly below expectations (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Aug 24]); industry was strong while construction surprised with a decline (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 26]); investment has become the engine of Q2 growth (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]). The GUS average wage crossed 9500 zł (Direct Money, [Aug 21]). The caveat is the horizon: the finance ministry itself warned of a slowdown (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]).

Markets: A nasty week. Thursday brought the biggest sell-off in over two months (Bankier.pl, [Aug 27]) with the WIG20 the weakest index in Europe (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 27]); Parkiet tied the outflow from GPW and the zloty to the CIA chief's visit and an aversion to Polish assets [Aug 27], Kruk fell 7% and CD Projekt traded on huge turnover (CrypS.pl, [Aug 27]). Friday was a draw (Parkiet, [Aug 28]): Allegro led while Modivo and Kruk closed lower (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 28]) and Orlen recovered to be the WIG20's main support (DNA Rynków, [Aug 28]). Before the shock the index had been welded to 4000 points (Parkiet, [Aug 25]) and PKO BP printed an all-time high (DNA Rynków, [Aug 26]). The card shows damage concentrated in gaming and energy: CD Projekt -8.4% on the week, Orlen -5.3%, Pekao -2.2%, EPOL at 43.58 (-1.3% 1W, +1.9% 4W); the winners were KGHM at 361.6 (+8.8% 1W, +16.9% 4W), Dino at 35.44 (+9.8% 1W, +11.3% 4W) and PGE (+5.4% 1W). The zloty reversed: after USD/PLN hit a two-month low (Comparic.pl, [Aug 22]) near 3.68 (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 21]), the euro became the dearest in over a month (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]) with EUR/PLN near 4.34 and USD/PLN heading for 3.75 ahead of the Fed chair's Jackson Hole speech (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]). The card has USD/PLN at 3.7164 (+0.7% 1W, -0.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3303 (+0.4% 1W, +0.6% 4W). POLGBs are the sore point: 10-year yields reached levels not seen since May (Interia Biznes, [Aug 28]), and CEO Magazyn argued the new peaks are not about the budget [Aug 28].

Fiscal & Politics: The government adopted the 2027 draft budget today: a state deficit of 282.6 bn zł, a general government deficit of 7.1% of GDP, growth of 3.6% this year and 3% next (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]; Reuters, [Aug 28]). ING called it not great, not terrible and the sixth consecutive year of excessive deficit [Aug 28]; Business Insider Polska quoted the line that the government is testing markets' patience [Aug 28]; Domański conceded a significant rise in debt-servicing costs (Interia Biznes, [Aug 28]). The mix is electoral: health up 26 bn zł (PAP, [Aug 28]), less on defence (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]), the alcohol excise hike returning and new PIT thresholds (Money.pl and Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]), after earlier reporting of a rate rising from 19 to 22% from 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 21]). Fitch affirmed the rating but warned on deficit and record debt (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 22]), and SocGen said budget risks point to further zloty weakness versus the euro [Aug 24]. President Nawrocki vetoed three of seven bills (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]) and energy-sector taxes hinge on his signature (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 28]). Almost 100% of EU recovery funds are allocated (TVP World, [Aug 28]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks mix records and risks: July mortgage lending hit a record 14.8 bn zł (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]), over a fifth of it refinancing [Aug 27]; the sector posted a historic profit and 2.1 trillion in deposits (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]), yet CIT held half-year earnings to only 21.4 bn zł [Aug 27], KNF warned free-credit sanction risk is rising fast (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 28]), PKO BP is contesting a 47.5 mln zł fine (pb.pl, [Aug 26]) and KNF recognised POLSTR as a significant benchmark (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 25]). KGHM is the winner: world's largest silver producer with 712 tonnes in a half year (Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 28]), copper resources up 61% (Parkiet, [Aug 26]), room to lift the dividend (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 25]), though unions want 6500 zł per employee [Aug 27]. Orlen's Baltic Power is 89% complete (WysokieNapiecie.pl, [Aug 28]); Tauron estimated 586 mln zł Q2 net profit (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 25]). CD Projekt announced a Witcher 3 remaster (Bankier.pl, [Aug 26]) but Parkiet says the fourth Witcher will take longer [Aug 26] and investors kept selling (FXMAG, [Aug 27]); half-year results come next Wednesday. Dino has a fresh buy rating (pb.pl, [Aug 25]) and is up 34% since July (Comparic.pl, [Aug 25]); Couche-Tard launched a formal tender for all of Żabka (Reuters, [Aug 26]). Diesel returns above 8 zł from September as the CPN repeat ends (Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 28]).

Geopolitics & Security: The CIA chief's reported warning to the Kremlin not to attack NATO (The Guardian, [Aug 27]) is what rattled Warsaw. Sikorski urged faster defence preparation (Українська правда, [Aug 28]); Polish F-16s suffered a system failure during a Russian missile breach (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Aug 28]); Poland signed a $2.2B Piorun missile deal (Anadolu, [Aug 24]); US Patriot stocks in Europe are beyond critical because of the Iran war (PBS, [Aug 27]).

New / Most Important: The 2027 budget is the story: a 7.1% deficit for a sixth year, financed into a market where 10-year yields are already at their highest since May and foreign money is leaving on security risk. Fitch's warning and SocGen's zloty call now have a number to anchor to.  |  Playbook ✓: PLN weakness on policy divergence is consensus; stay underweight duration, keep KGHM and Dino as earnings-backed longs.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The bond market blames geopolitics, not the budget, for the yield spike; if so, a de-escalation reprices POLGBs faster than any fiscal fix would.
