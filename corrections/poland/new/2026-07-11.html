🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → Hold, dovish pivot underway

Macro Stance: The RPP left the reference rate at 3.75% for a fourth month, as expected (PAP Biznes, [Jul 8]; Bloomberg, [Jul 9]), but the news came a day later: Governor Glapiński said he could himself table a cut motion "after the holidays" and no longer rules out a reduction in 2026 (StockWatch.pl, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 9]). ING read him as "opening the door to rate cuts before end-2026" (ING Think, [Jul 9]), even as the July projection raised the 2027 inflation path, partly on oil assumptions, and trimmed growth (Analizy.pl, [Jul 10]). The NBP's central band puts 2026 inflation at 2.4 to 3.3% with 50% probability (PAP, [Jul 8]). The Council is not united: two members told Reuters they see "no rush to cut after summer" ([Jul 10]) and Erste sees no cuts through end-2027 (Direct Money, [Jul 10]). On activity, PKO BP pencils in 3.5% GDP growth for 2026 (wnp.pl, [Jul 11]), nominal Q1 GDP rose 6.4% (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 9]) and unemployment fell again (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 8]). Shop-price inflation is slowing on the discounter price war and the strong zloty (wnp.pl, [Jul 10]), though the NBP's own report flags a fresh supply shock heading for shelf prices (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Jul 11]).

Markets: Equities and the currency went opposite ways. GPW printed an all-time high on Thursday with banks doing the lifting: PKO BP, Alior and Erste hit record prices after Goldman Sachs sharply raised its PKO BP valuation (Bankier.pl, StockWatch.pl, pb.pl, [Jul 10]). Pepco led gains at +4.66% while Żabka fell hard (FXMAG, [Jul 10]). The card confirms the bank bid: PKO at 112.88 is +7.3% on the week and +7.9% over four weeks, Pekao 244.3 (+4.8% / +7.1%), EPOL 40.37 (+2.4% / +3.8% in USD). KGHM is the laggard at 308.95, down 7.8% on the week and 14.4% over four weeks after a 6.69% single-day drop (FXMAG, [Jul 9]). Orlen 139.92 (+5.3% 1W, -4.7% 4W), PGE 9.37 (-2.6% / -6.2%), Allegro 39.83 (+3.6% / +15.7%), Dino 28.22, CD Projekt 233.0, LPP 19380 (+4.5% 1W). The zloty is the casualty of the dovish turn: the euro broke a key resistance and is at its most expensive since November (Bankier.pl, Comparic.pl, [Jul 10]), having only just bounced from a 19-month low before the decision (Reuters, [Jul 8]). On the card USD/PLN is 3.7853 (+0.9% 1W, +3.2% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3278 (+1.0% / +1.9%); Societe Generale's downtrend call eyes 4.48 (FXStreet, [Jul 10]). POLGB yields, at a four-month low before the meeting (Bankier.pl, [Jul 8]), fell again on the dovish signals per PKO BP (wnp.pl, [Jul 10]).

Fiscal & Politics: Finance Minister Domański previewed an "election-year budget" and cooled tax-cut talk, naming the deficit as the key obstacle (Money.pl, Business Insider Polska, [Jul 9]); Prof. Chojna-Duch warns public debt is growing "at lightning speed" (Radio Wnet, [Jul 9]). The Sejm passed OKI personal investment accounts with up to 100,000 zloty exempt from the Belka tax, framed by the press as a new levy on idle savings from 2027 (wnp.pl, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 8]), and buried the 2,403 zloty child-benefit idea (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 7]). A public row erupted between the finance ministry and UOKiK over 174.5 million zloty of Gazprom fine money the ministry declined to pursue (Interia Biznes, TVN24, [Jul 10]). The government unveiled a Security and Defence Fund (Gov.pl, [Jul 9]), with SAFE loans worth tens of billions of zloty for the army (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 6]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks are the winners but carry regulatory noise: UOKiK and police searched BIK, mBank and ING over credit-scoring practices (TVN24, Donald.pl, [Jul 8]), PKO BP is adding 297 million zloty of CHF provisions in Q2 (Bankier.pl, [Jul 10]), and the ministry wants to accelerate the WIBOR-to-POLSTR switch, with borrowers given two months to decide (Money.pl, Fakt, [Jul 10]). Energy: Orlen's Baltic Power sent first offshore power to the grid with 54 of 76 turbines installed (PAP Biznes, [Jul 10]), Bogdanka will sell Orlen 507 million zloty more coal (wnp.pl, [Jul 10]), and Noble Securities rates PGE "hold" while warning on debt (Parkiet, [Jul 10]). KGHM unveiled a 32 billion zloty investment plan to 2030 including a new mine (pb.pl, [Jul 9]), but a strong tremor at Lubin forced an evacuation on Saturday (TVN24, [Jul 11]). Retail: LPP shareholders approved a 900 zloty per share dividend (Bankier.pl, [Jul 10]), Dino opened 148 stores in H1 but pulled a product after a GIS alert ([Jul 10]), and Cyberpunk 2077 passed 40 million copies (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 6]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Poland-Ukraine WWII dispute is now a market variable: DW reports Warsaw could cut arms aid ([Jul 11]) and Poland has signalled a possible block on Kyiv's EU accession (Ukraine Business News, [Jul 6]), although Nawrocki and Zelensky met at the NATO summit (TVP World, [Jul 8]). Sikorski repeated that Poland holds "credible information" of planned Russian provocations in Europe (Euromaidan Press, [Jul 10]). MON declassified over 16 billion zloty of military donations to Ukraine (Lublin112, [Jul 6]) and Poland and allies agreed to build Europe's first PAC-3 missile centre (TVP World, [Jul 7]).

New / Most Important: The dovish pivot is the week's development, and it has already split asset classes. Glapiński volunteering to author a cut motion after the summer, in the same breath as a projection that lifts 2027 inflation, pushed EUR/PLN through resistance while banks rallied to records. Equity investors are pricing a lower-rate, higher-growth economy; FX investors are pricing a central bank less committed to real-rate defence. Both cannot be right.

Playbook ✓: PLN weakening on dovish shift is textbook FX reaction; banks outperforming on a record-high GPW fits a growth-plus-lower-rates narrative.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Cutting rates while raising the 2027 inflation forecast is a credibility risk, and the Council is not aligned: two members see no rush and Erste sees nothing until end-2027. If the guidance is walked back, the zloty rebounds but the bank rally loses its catalyst; if it is honoured, long-PLN carry trades stay offside. KGHM's slide and the Ukraine aid dispute are the tail risks the index is ignoring.
