🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, dovish pivot contested

Macro Stance: The governor's dovish pivot met open resistance inside the Council today. An RPP member publicly disagreed with Glapiński, calling a rate hike "more probable than a cut" (pb.pl, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 14]), on top of the two members who told Reuters last week there was no rush to ease after summer ([Jul 10]). That leaves the reference rate at 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Jul 8]) with guidance pointing in two directions: Glapiński's own motion "after the holidays" (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 9]) versus a projection with higher 2027 inflation (Analizy.pl, [Jul 10]) and now hawkish dissent. pb.pl's brief summed it up as Glapiński having "sown anxiety": the zloty weakens while investors trade for a cut ([Jul 10]). The real economy is mixed: GUS showed industry standing still with declines outnumbering gains (Forsal.pl, wnp.pl, [Jul 13]), while shop prices are turning and food surprised experts (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 14]) after a further month of slowing shelf inflation (Agro Profil, [Jul 13]). ING notes the current account deficit narrowed in May on softer imports ([Jul 14]), though PKO BP expects it to widen to 1.7% of GDP by year-end (wnp.pl, [Jul 14]). Household stress is visible: more than 1.9 million Poles are in arrears and in parts of the country average debt exceeds average pay (Zero.pl, [Jul 14]).

Markets: Allegro owned the session. Preliminary second-quarter figures of 3.34 billion zloty revenue and 1.03 billion zloty adjusted EBITDA, with the foreign business growing 82.4%, sent the stock up almost 10% on heavy turnover to its highest level in over four years and pushed WIG20 to a new high of the bull market, with KGHM and Orlen helping (Inwestycje.pl, Bankier.pl, Wiadomości Handlowe, WNP.PL, [Jul 14]). Parkiet's take: "global fear bypasses Warsaw" while banks "catch their breath" ([Jul 14]), and pb.pl notes Pepco chasing with a buyback while "the spectre of taxes hovers over banks" ([Jul 14]). The card, which predates today's move, has Allegro at 40.0 (+3.1% 1W, +14.9% 4W), Orlen 144.0 (+7.9% / +5.9%) after Monday's oil-led run, PKO 111.8 (+5.7% / +8.5%), Pekao 243.3 (+3.6% / +6.4%), PGE 9.48 (+0.5% / -6.9%), Dino 27.96, CD Projekt 231.0 (-1.0% / +3.8%), LPP 19260 (+1.8% / -2.5%) and KGHM 305.45, still -7.0% on the week and -19.1% over four weeks. EPOL at 40.16 is +0.7% 1W but -1.0% 4W, the dollar-denominated cost of a weak zloty. FX: "war returned to the Gulf", the zloty is in retreat and the dollar at this year's highs (Bankier.pl, [Jul 14]); Comparic says the zloty "continues its selloff" ([Jul 14]). USD/PLN stands at 3.8043 (+1.6% 1W, +3.8% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.3285 (+1.0% / +1.9%); FXMAG asks whether EUR/PLN is heading to 4.31 ([Jul 14]). Experts polled by Bankier blame the RPP, not the deficit, for the weakness ([Jul 14]). Rzeczpospolita says Poland and emerging-market bonds "remain strong" ([Jul 14]).

Fiscal & Politics: Fitch kept its negative rating outlook amid a sharp rise in public debt (wPolityce.pl, [Jul 13]), and the finance ministry itself is warning of a "black scenario" with sharp budget cuts (Strefa Biznesu, [Jul 14]). The sector risk for equities is a bank tax: minister Pełczyńska-Nałęcz proposed another CIT increase for banks, citing huge excess liquidity (pb.pl, [Jul 14]). The ministry also launched 16 changes to a single tax (WNP.PL, [Jul 14]). Hospital debt has reached 40 billion zloty as the health ministry withdraws a rescue programme (Rynek Zdrowia, [Jul 14]). Poland ruled out lifting its ban on Ukrainian agricultural imports (Ukrinform, [Jul 14]) and is building a registry to target Airbnb-style rentals (TVP World, [Jul 14]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen filed a criminal notice against former CEO Obajtek over the Solino mine incident, which Obajtek rebuffed (TVN24, Money.pl, [Jul 14]), and its dispute with Synthos over small modular reactors is "in the final stretch" (Parkiet, [Jul 14]); Orlen Charge joined the Elocity app, covering 80% of the charging market (Biznes Alert, [Jul 14]). KGHM confirmed 64 million zloty of NFOŚiGW funding for 19.4 MW of solar (Gramwzielone.pl, [Jul 14]) but reported the death of a young miner (TVP Wrocław, [Jul 14]). In banking, the mortgage-margin cuts have stalled into a "summer stalemate" (Bankier.pl, [Jul 14]), banks are finding ways not to pass on the full benefit of lower rates (Direct Money, [Jul 14]), and the NBP warned of a new wave of lawsuits as the CHF saga ends (Parkiet, [Jul 14]). Dino is still clearing the GIS poppy-seed recall (Super Biznes, [Jul 14]) after Monday's share-price drop amid an employee dispute (wPolityce.pl, [Jul 13]). The energy minister says the government is ready with shields if fuel prices spike (PAP, [Jul 14]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk said peace in Ukraine is unlikely soon and expects Russia to extend the war until winter (Arise News, [Jul 14]), while MON announced a new formation to strengthen the eastern border (Radio Zet, [Jul 14]). Sikorski's line that Russia lacks the capacity to attack but "is preparing something" stands (Kyiv Post, [Jul 13]), and the Poland-Ukraine dispute continues with the agricultural-import ban and a public that now leans against arms transfers (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 12]).

New / Most Important: Two things changed today. Allegro's pre-release showed that the domestic consumer story has an earnings engine beyond banks, which matters because a bank CIT hike is now on the table. And the RPP's hawkish wing went public, so the cut the market is positioned for is no longer a governor's gift but a contested vote. A split Council with a weakening currency, Gulf oil risk and a negative Fitch outlook is a less forgiving setup than a clean dovish pivot.

Playbook ✓: PLN weakness on dovish pivot is textbook; earnings-driven single-stock breakouts leading the index late in a rally is normal.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Cutting into rising inflation forecasts would be genuinely unconventional, and now a Council member says a hike is likelier. If the zloty keeps sliding while the hawks win, the cut trade unwinds without the currency recovering, the worst of both. Banks at record highs with a tax proposal in the open is the second gap to watch.
