🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold

Macro Stance: The RPP decides this week (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 7]) and nobody expects a move: wnp.pl reports a large bank has no doubts about the outcome [Jul 7], and Gazeta Prawna's line is a July hold with cuts a 2027 question [Jul 6]. Bankier.pl frames the real event as the July inflation projection rather than the rate itself [Jul 7]. The setup is the June GUS flash at 2.5% y/y, back at the NBP target and below forecasts (Parkiet, [Jun 30]), after which Commerzbank noted that rate hike bets have vanished and ING flagged a market starting to play for cuts in the second half of 2026 [Jun 30], all of which has kept the zloty capped amid mixed data. The counterweights are the end of the CPN fuel package, which Gazeta Prawna warned could push inflation toward 3% [Jun 30], and energy costs that WP Wiadomości says are hitting industry and threatening jobs and competitiveness [Jul 7]. The manufacturing PMI dropped to 46.1 in June versus a 49.4 consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 1]), though German industry is rebounding (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 7]). Labour is the strong leg: GUS counts 1.1 mn foreigners working in Poland (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 7]).

Markets: Tuesday was a rotation session: CD Projekt led gains while KGHM fell the most (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 7]), Allegro and Żabka defended an index that closed slightly lower (DlaHandlu.pl, [Jul 7]), and FXMAG recorded Modivo +4.59% and LPP +1.99% while Samsung dropped over 10% and the KOSPI lost 7% [Jul 7]. KGHM's slide is a verdict on Strategy 2055+: FXMAG says the shares plunged [Jul 7] and INNPoland.pl reports the company's largest-ever investment programme failed to convince analysts [Jul 7]; the card still shows 328.55 (+2.4% 1W, -3.4% 4W) on Monday's close. Banks face their own valuation question, with pb.pl reporting analysts now consider GPW bank shares too expensive, citing a long list of risk factors [Jul 7], even as PKO closed 105.82 (+2.4% 1W, +6.6% 4W) and Pekao 234.8 (+3.2% 1W, 0.0% 4W). Elsewhere on the card: Orlen 133.5 (+6.8% 1W, -7.8% 4W), Allegro 38.8 (+4.5% 1W, +12.6% 4W), CD Projekt 233.3 (+5.5% 1W, +2.7% 4W), LPP 18,920 (+2.3% 1W, -12.9% 4W), PGE 9.44 (-0.3% 1W, -7.3% 4W) and Dino 27.76 (-5.2% 1W, -5.9% 4W) at its lowest since 2022 after the Barclays recommendation (XTB.com, [Jul 3]). EPOL closed 39.9 (+4.3% 1W, -1.1% 4W). The zloty is drifting: EUR/PLN stays close to 4.29 (FXMAG, [Jul 7]) in what Bankier.pl calls a sideways range, with the pound at its most expensive in over a year [Jul 7]; Card: USD/PLN 3.7457 (-0.2% 1W, +1.9% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.2861 (flat 1W, +1.1% 4W). POLGBs: investors bought Polish bonds worth billions in May (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 3]).

Fiscal & Politics: Defence money is reshaping the fiscal debate. The finance ministry published calculations showing Poland will save billions through the EU's SAFE loans (Next Gazeta.pl, [Jul 7]), Business Insider Polska reports those loans are feeding the Polish army [Jul 7], and PKO BP estimates that defence modernisation could generate a 1.3 trn zł demand impulse by 2035 (Forsal.pl, [Jul 7]). Deregulation 2.0, presented by Berek and Domański with more than a dozen tax changes for 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 6]), is paired with a less welcome item: Gazeta Prawna says a new tax from 2027 will surprise savers because no profit is needed to owe it [Jul 7], and Business Insider Polska reads the OKI law as the Sejm forcing Poles to invest from 2027 [Jul 7]. The CPN fuel package has ended, and Interia Biznes reports the minister disclosing when it could return [Jul 7], while the windfall tax on fuel companies is meant to cover its 4 bn zł cost (Biznes Wprost, [Jul 2]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen placed 750 mn euro of green eurobonds with demand more than twice the offer (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 7]), wnp.pl notes it is now larger than Rosneft and every Russian oil company [Jul 7], and Rzeczpospolita sees a chance of cheaper Norwegian gas with the market already pricing part of the benefit [Jul 7]. KGHM's plan, over 32 bn zł and new mines (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 7]), is being sold rather than bought. PGE: Rzeczpospolita says the Turów dispute is not over and a key decision still lies ahead [Jul 7], and customers are waiting months for invoices under a new billing system (Spider's Web, [Jul 7]). PKO BP joined NATO's initiative to draw private capital into defence (Money.pl, [Jul 7]), while mBank's new offer was described as a flop (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 7]). LPP's Sinsay reached 2.5 thousand stores after opening 500 in the half (PropertyNews.pl, [Jul 7]). Dino faces an approaching general strike (mamNewsa, [Jul 7]), and the finance ministry has named the date WIBOR disappears for borrowers (Bankier.pl, [Jul 5]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Poland-Ukraine rift widened: Budanov said Ukraine will not accept ultimatums from Poland (Euromaidan Press, [Jul 7]), Gazeta Prawna reports Ukraine asking for Patriot missiles with the Netherlands saying it has exhausted its capacity [Jul 7], and The Times of India wrote of Tusk's warning to his own president amid the tussle with Zelensky [Jul 7]. Sikorski again warned of a possible Russian provocation against Poland, citing trust in US guarantees (polskieradio.pl, [Jul 7]), after the US warning of an armed test of NATO on Polish territory (The Telegraph, [Jul 3]). Poland and NATO allies struck a deal to build Europe's first PAC-3 missile centre (TVP World, [Jul 7]).

New / Most Important: The collapse in hike expectations is the story. Poland went from hike-pricing to neutral in a single data cycle, and the decision is now a non-event: what matters is whether the projection validates 2027 cuts or pushes them out on fuel. The market has meanwhile started differentiating: KGHM is punished for capex, banks are being called expensive, and Allegro and CD Projekt carry the index.  |  Playbook ✓: CEE FX range-bound amid mixed data; EUR/PLN pinned near 4.29 into the decision is exactly the script.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A 1.3 trn zł defence impulse financed by EU loans is a growth story the PMI at 46.1 does not yet show; if the gap persists, the fiscal cost arrives before the demand.
