🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike calls louder

Macro Stance: Monday brought the bluntest tightening call yet: "the RPP should raise rates", as Bankier.pl headlined while the dollar firmed and the euro held [Sep 28]. It caps a week in which Kotecki argued for a pre-emptive hike (pb.pl, [Sep 24]). Governance is now part of the story: an RPP member warned that the NBP faces paralysis (Interia Biznes, [Sep 28]) and the FT describes a central bank trapped in a "political contest" [Sep 25]. The Council's 2027 assumptions expect inflation to fall and growth to slow (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 27]); Fitch nonetheless raised its 2027 GDP forecast to 3% (IntelliNews, [Sep 28]). Inflation sits at 3.4% (Rankia Polska, [Sep 23]) with Credit Agricole seeing a path toward 4.5% on food (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 22]). The data backdrop remains soft: August retail sales rose 3.8% y/y against a 4.5% consensus (wnp.pl, [Sep 22]) and wages rose 5.6% while employment fell 0.8% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 21]). The week ahead brings the September flash CPI, the PMI and the Q4 treasury supply plan (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 28]).

Markets: The GPW stalled. Monday's WIG20 closed below the line as Orlen and the utilities failed to carry it (Bankier.pl, [Sep 28]); Parkiet said investors "are in no hurry to buy" [Sep 28], with PGE the top gainer and Modivo retreating, while Tauron was the session's biggest loser and Pepco rose strongly (FXMAG, [Sep 28]). XYZ warned the calm "may be only temporary" [Sep 28]. That follows a week that ended with Parkiet's "demand fatigue" signal [Sep 25]. Parkiet flags the weaker zloty as a results driver, exporters gaining and importers losing [Sep 28]. Allegro raised its buyback price to 65 PLN and proposed three new directors (Investing.com Polska, [Sep 28]) on top of the 800 mln zł phase II (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 28]). The card still reflects Friday's closes: PKO 124.54 (+3.9% 1W, +9.7% 4W), Pekao 280.3 (+4.0% 1W), LPP 24900 (+18.6% 4W); PGE 13.02 (-6.5% 1W, +19.6% 4W), Dino 34.44 (-3.6% 1W), KGHM 330.5 (-8.7% 4W), Orlen 148.84 (-1.5% 1W), Allegro 49.56 (+9.3% 4W). EPOL is 44.85 (+1.0% 1W, +2.9% 4W). The zloty recovered a little from the extremes: USD/PLN closed at 3.8413 (+1.1% 1W, +2.6% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3703 (+0.3% 1W, +0.8% 4W) after the euro set its highest level since late 2023 (Fresh Plaza, [Sep 28]), yet Business Insider Polska sees the currency pressured by rising bond yields with oil complicating matters [Sep 28] and Brent is back above 100 USD (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Sep 28]). POLGBs are the sore spot: Business Insider Polska described historic jumps in the cost of state debt where "nobody wants to catch a falling knife" [Sep 25], Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK calls Poland "drunk on debt" [Sep 28] and Parkiet sees a record bond-WIBOR spread awaiting adjustment [Sep 26]; auction cover was still 22.5 bn zł for 12.0 bn zł sold (TradingView, [Sep 23]).

Fiscal & Politics: The government will adopt the 2027 budget draft before the end of September with a deficit of up to 282.6 bn zł (Portal spożywczy, [Sep 28]), a day after the Fiscal Council warned of a large risk of breaching the prudential threshold and a miscalculated expenditure rule (INFOR.PL, [Sep 27]). An RPP member added that high debt and deficit make the state's finances difficult (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 28]). The Moody's cut to A3 (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 21]) remains the backdrop. Politics is the fuel law: Tusk appealed "President, sign" and the Palace responded (Money.pl, [Sep 28]) after the Senate passed the windfall-tax fuel bill unamended (Money.pl, [Sep 24]); Wyborcza.pl tallies the cost of Nawrocki's vetoes at 8 billion zloty for the budget [Sep 28]. On tax, the government adopted PIT and CIT changes hitting lump-sum payers, entrepreneurs and landlords (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 22]) and the MF will not penalise KSeF errors until the end of 2027 (INFOR.PL, [Sep 28]). The government is also preparing 4.8 bn zł of power-price support for energy-intensive industry (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 28]).

Corporate & Sectors: The mortgage boom is the sector headline: the value of housing loans granted in January to August rose 51.1% y/y per BIK (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Sep 28]), banks lent 41% more (PropertyNews.pl, [Sep 28]), August stayed strong without a record (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 28]) and the stock passed 100 bn zł (pb.pl, [Sep 28]); POLSTR mortgages are proliferating (Bankier.pl, [Sep 28]) with PKO BP, mBank, ING and Erste all moving off WIBOR. UOKiK fined firms 13.5 mln zł for bid-rigging in PGE Dystrybucja tenders (pb.pl, [Sep 28]) and NFOŚiGW granted over 107 mln zł to PGE's Gdynia heat project (enerad.pl, [Sep 28]). Orlen can breathe as a key crude supplier resumed exports (wnp.pl, [Sep 28]) and fuels are earning for Orlen and MOL (Parkiet, [Sep 28]). KGHM is eyeing Argentina's Los Azules (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 28]). CD Projekt's Witcher 3 Remaster launches with reports of problems that will not help shareholders (INNPoland.pl, [Sep 28]). Dino confirmed a Gniezno distribution centre (Omnichannel News, [Sep 28]). URE says power got cheaper, yet the price has risen nearly threefold (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 28]); GlobEnergia counts a 12% fall in energy prices [Sep 28].

Geopolitics & Security: A Russian drone crashed near the Dorohusk-Jahodyn crossing (Defence24, [Sep 28]), days after a Russian Mi-8 breached Polish airspace (BBC, [Sep 23]). The FM said Russia lacks the forces for a major attack on NATO (TRT World, [Sep 28]), Poland boosted air defence after the provocations (Al Jazeera, [Sep 25]) and offered to build a Patriot missile factory (Ukrinform, [Sep 27]); GE Aerospace won a Polish defence deal (Benzinga, [Sep 28]).

New / Most Important: A 282.6 bn zł deficit draft arrives the same week bond investors reprice Polish debt and hawks demand hikes. Fiscal expansion plus monetary tightening is a currency-positive, bond-negative, consumer-negative mix; banks sit on the right side of it with a 51.1% mortgage boom and POLSTR repricing.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold bank overweight, add to exporters flagged by Parkiet as zloty beneficiaries, stay short POLGB duration into the Q4 supply plan, and watch the flash CPI as the hike trigger.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Fitch raising 2027 growth to 3% while the RPP expects a slowdown; a mortgage boom running at +51.1% while creditworthiness is falling sharply.
