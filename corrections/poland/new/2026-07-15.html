🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, cut bets fading

Macro Stance: GUS confirmed June CPI at 2.5% y/y with prices down 0.5% m/m, exactly at the NBP target and matching the flash (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, StockWatch.pl, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 15]); food and fuel fell while services kept rising (Super Biznes, Radio ESKA, [Jul 15]). That is the good news. The bad news is that the market no longer believes it unlocks a cut: the end of the CPN fuel package will lift July inflation (Gazeta Prawna, Bankier.pl citing Erste, [Jul 15]), PKO BP expects a transitory rise above 3.5% that "will contribute to rate stabilisation" (PAP, [Jul 15]), pb.pl reports experts "do not believe in cuts after the holidays" as fuel prices climb again ([Jul 15]), and Parkiet says the largest bank's economists simply "do not believe Glapiński" ([Jul 15]). Add yesterday's RPP member calling a hike likelier than a cut (pb.pl, [Jul 14]) and the governor's post-holiday motion (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 9]) looks like one vote, not a majority. The reference rate remains 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Jul 8]). Industry stalled in the latest GUS print (Forsal.pl, [Jul 13]) and the foreign trade deficit reached 12.5 billion zloty, driven by China (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 15]). Energy prices in Poland rose 15.8% on heat and geopolitics (Gramwzielone.pl, [Jul 15]), with European gas at its highest since March (Energetyka24, [Jul 15]).

Markets: Tuesday's Allegro surge carried through the card: Allegro 43.92 is +11.5% on the week and +17.0% over four weeks after preliminary results showed 3.34 billion zloty of revenue and 1.03 billion zloty of adjusted EBITDA (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 14]), and Investing.com notes WIG20 at a bull-market record with StockWatch flagging the 2007 all-time high within reach ([Jul 15]). Wednesday was more nuanced: records were set again but decliners dominated, Pepco led and Modivo and the banks were under pressure as the government "hits the banks" with its CIT proposal; sWIG80 lost 0.94% (Bankier.pl, Inwestycje.pl, FXMAG, [Jul 15]). The card shows the bank fade already: PKO 110.5 (+5.5% 1W, +4.4% 4W), Pekao 237.0 (+2.2% / +1.8%). Orlen 146.0 (+8.0% / +5.5%) is the other leader. KGHM 319.4 is flat on the week (+0.1%) but -16.9% over four weeks and gave back gains on Wednesday (XTB, [Jul 15]); PGE 9.5, Dino 28.2, CD Projekt 231.6 (-2.5% 1W) with INNPoland calling its share price "still disappointing" ([Jul 15]), LPP 19650 (+4.5% / +3.8%). EPOL 40.78 is +3.4% on the week but -0.4% over four weeks. The zloty "caught its breath": the euro stopped rising (Bankier.pl, [Jul 15]) and the zloty firmed clearly against the dollar, which returned near 3.79 (FXMAG, CEO Magazyn, [Jul 15]); the card has USD/PLN 3.7832 (+0.3% 1W, +3.7% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3231 (+0.5% / +2.1%). FXMAG warns that forecasts of a 7% of GDP deficit and rising debt are "hitting the zloty" ([Jul 15]).

Fiscal & Politics: The half-year budget print is the week's fiscal shock: a deficit of 123.7 billion zloty after June, with VAT receipts undermined by KSeF teething problems and cheap fuel while CIT and PIT held up (Parkiet, Rzeczpospolita, XYZ, [Jul 15]); the state spent over 402 billion zloty in H1 (Zero.pl, [Jul 15]) and debt interest now costs more than the pension subsidy (wGospodarce, [Jul 15]). This lands days after Fitch kept a negative outlook (wPolityce.pl, [Jul 13]) and the ministry's own "black scenario" warning (Strefa Biznesu, [Jul 14]). The bank CIT increase floated by Pełczyńska-Nałęcz (pb.pl, [Jul 14]) is now the policy lever the market fears most. MAN committed more than 5 billion zloty to an electric-truck plant in Małopolska (Bankier.pl, [Jul 15]). President Nawrocki has hours left to decide on the CHF-mortgage law (Franknews.pl, [Jul 15]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: mortgages are getting cheaper and some lenders now offer over 1.3 million zloty of capacity (Forsal.pl, Direct Money, [Jul 15]), but the CJEU will rule on the "balance theory" in CHF cases in September (Prawo.pl, Franknews.pl, [Jul 15]) and the NBP has warned of a new wave of lawsuits (Parkiet, [Jul 14]). Orlen raised wholesale fuel prices with "powerful increases" at refineries (Money.pl, Super Biznes, [Jul 15]), Azoty's Polimery Police remain idle awaiting Orlen (Parkiet, [Jul 15]), and the criminal notice against Obajtek stands (TVN24, [Jul 14]). KGHM faces a German copper programme that could threaten it (wnp.pl, [Jul 15]). Retail: UOKiK is probing Lidl and Dino for transport collusion (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Jul 15]) as a protest by dismissed Dino employees continues (ePiotrkow, [Jul 15]); the Hindenburg-related investigation into LPP was suspended (Parkiet, [Jul 15]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland and the Baltics warned Russia could launch a limited military or hybrid provocation against NATO, and Sikorski said Moscow may stage a false-flag drone attack using Ukrainian UAVs (AP News, Kyiv Post, [Jul 15]); the FT reports Russia accused of planning attacks on Baltic or Polish infrastructure ([Jul 15]) and a Russian spy plane again tested NATO defences over the Baltic (Kyiv Post, [Jul 15]). Over 200 thousand now serve in the Polish military (Interia Biznes, [Jul 15]).

New / Most Important: The 123.7 billion zloty half-year deficit changes the policy mix. With VAT disappointing, rating outlook negative and the market now pricing cuts out rather than in, the easy "cheapest easing story in Europe" framing has lost both legs in one day: CPI at target is being overridden by the fuel-package expiry, and the fiscal side is pushing the government toward bank taxes that hit the index's largest sector. The record WIG20 is being carried by Allegro and Orlen while its traditional engine stalls.

Playbook ✓: Zloty stabilising as cut bets fade is the expected response; banks lagging on a tax threat and Allegro re-rating on earnings are both orthodox.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Inflation at 2.5% and on target while the bond and FX market trades a 7%-of-GDP deficit is a fiscal-dominance signal, not an easing signal. If the Council holds through the CPN-driven July CPI rebound and the ministry reaches for bank CIT, the equity leadership rotation away from banks will have further to run even as the headline index prints records.
