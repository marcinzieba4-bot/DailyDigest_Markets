🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → Rates on hold

Macro Stance: The NBP is on hold with the debate drifting toward September: Strefa Inwestorów [Aug 6] has analysts asking whether a cut is possible in September after the July flash showed CPI at 3.0% y/y (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 31]) on a fuel spike, and Super Biznes [Aug 3] reported that Glapiński wants to lower rates while experts sound the alarm; the Rzeczpospolita and Parkiet economist panels [Jul 31] still say the Council should not hurry. The activity data argue for patience from strength, not weakness: the manufacturing PMI rebounded with the first rise in employment in months (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 3]), Parkiet [Aug 4] expects GUS data to confirm a solid recovery, Interia Biznes [Aug 5] says the energy shock has not stopped Poland and growth of up to 4.1% is possible, Eurostat shows retail sales growing much faster than the EU (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 6]) and Poland has become the sixth-largest EU economy (TVP World, [Aug 6]). GUS's median wage print was 7690.82 zł gross for February (Prawo.pl, [Aug 5]). The political cycle favors patience; the macro gives no reason to cut.

Markets: WIG20 crossed 4000 points for the first time on [Aug 5] (pb.pl) and closed +0.94% on Thursday (BIZNES24, [Aug 6]) with Bankier [Aug 6] reporting the index above 4,020 and Allegro on the attack; XYZ [Aug 6] says banks took the GPW by the horns, a run Domański hailed as investor trust returning to Poland (Parkiet, [Aug 4]). Leadership rotates daily: on [Aug 5] banks were under pressure while Orlen and KGHM rose (Inwestycje.pl), with KGHM up 8% as copper hit 14 thousand dollars (FXMAG, [Aug 5]); on [Aug 6] Allegro led and CD Projekt was the biggest loser (Inwestycje.pl). The card: KGHM 346.1 (+17.6% 1W, +16.3% 4W), PGE 10.9 (+12.5% 1W, +16.4% 4W), Pekao 253.0 (+6.6% 1W, +7.7% 4W), Orlen 150.2 (+1.5% 1W, +7.3% 4W), LPP 22,000 (+3.0% 1W, +17.8% 4W), Allegro 45.15 (+13.7% 4W), Dino 31.85 (+14.6% 4W), CD Projekt 253.5 (+8.1% 4W), while PKO 108.42 (-1.3% 1W) lags as analysts cut bank recommendations and ask whether the sector has any upside left (pb.pl, [Aug 6]). EPOL 43.68 is +5.1% 1W and +9.8% 4W. The zloty: USD/PLN 3.7186 (-1.3% 1W, -1.3% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.2975 (-0.6% 1W, -0.1% 4W); the euro is fighting to break 4.30 (Bankier, [Aug 5]), Comparic [Aug 6] sees the dollar rebounding and the zloty softening, and Bankier [Aug 6] calls it a small stabilisation awaiting an impulse. Two new ETFs tracking Polish bonds have launched (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Aug 4]).

Fiscal & Politics: The MF is stitching together a 2027 budget with the largest deficit in the EU and pressure to cut taxes (pb.pl, [Aug 4]); Bloomberg [Aug 4] reports Poland weighing income tax relief for millions before the election, and Domański named the key date for unveiling the budget (Money.pl, [Aug 6]). Public spending is 50.9% of GDP, above Germany, Sweden and Denmark (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 5]), the state costs over 53 thousand zł per Pole with debt service now dearer than the 800+ benefit (Interia Biznes, [Aug 5]). The government's answer is the OKI savings account: Domański says it will strengthen the GPW and channel capital to growing firms (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 6]), PKO BP expects OKI assets above 100 bn zł by the third year (pb.pl, [Aug 6]), and WNP.PL [Aug 6] frames it as moving billions out of bank deposits. Business Insider Polska [Aug 6] lists five Nawrocki decisions that hit the budget and PSL is talking to Tusk about joint lists for 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 5]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen's Q2 was a beat: 7680 mn zł net profit and 13,915 mn zł adjusted EBITDA LIFO (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 6]), above consensus (PAP Biznes, [Aug 6]), with first-half profit 10 bn zł higher than a year earlier (Money.pl, [Aug 6]); it is talking to banks about a 2 bn euro syndicated loan (Bankier, [Aug 6]), trades at 11 times earnings near a record (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 6]), and Tusk told drivers to remember the profit when refuelling (TVN24, [Aug 6]). LPP's preliminary Q2 showed revenue up 18.5% in constant currency, LFL up 1.8% and EBIT up more than 40% (Bankier and StockWatch.pl, [Aug 6]), but it is cutting Bangladesh orders amid a supplier dispute over 40 mn dollars tied to its Russia exit (RMF24, [Aug 5]). Dino: analysts cut the valuation but say buy with Q2 the turning point (Bankier, [Aug 5]), LFL growth is 0% (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 5]) and unions threaten a general strike (Dziennik.pl, [Aug 6]). KGHM rides the copper boom and plans energy investments (Parkiet, [Aug 5]) as Congo banned copper and cobalt concentrate exports (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 6]). Banks had a stellar June with surprisingly good repayment (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 6]). Power prices spiked to 2202.13 zł per MWh in the Wednesday evening peak (WNP.PL, [Aug 5]). Bechtel took over the Lubiatowo-Kopalino nuclear site and Budimex began earthworks (Gov.pl and Parkiet, [Aug 5]).

Geopolitics & Security: Talks are under way on a permanent US base in Poland (NDTV, [Aug 5]); the foreign ministry reiterated the idea of shooting down Russian missiles over Ukraine (LIGA.net, [Aug 6]) after last week's crash, a series of Russian Il-20 flights near Poland is gathering intelligence (Fakt, [Aug 5]) and Lithuania warns Russia could use captured Ukrainian drones against NATO infrastructure (UNITED24, [Aug 6]). Defense spending continues to provide fiscal stimulus.

New / Most Important: Orlen's 13.9 bn zł quarter. It is the single largest earnings input to the 4000-point index, it funds the state's dividend and defence ambitions, and it arrives the same day the prime minister points drivers at it. With the CPN package unfunded and the president blocking a windfall levy, political pressure on Orlen's margins is now the main risk to the energy-led rally; the OKI plan is the government's attempt to turn that record into domestic ownership before the election.

Playbook ✓: Holding pattern is consensus; stay long GPW with exposure rotating from banks to KGHM and Orlen on earnings.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A 50.9%-of-GDP state with the EU's largest deficit promising pre-election tax relief is not what a 4000-point index and a sub-4.30 euro are pricing — the POLGB curve, not the WIG20, will register it first.
