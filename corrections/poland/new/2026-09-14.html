🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, market tightens

Macro Stance: The NBP is on hold and the market is doing the tightening for it. The RPP kept the reference rate at 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Sep 9]) and Glapiński said rates may stay stable until mid-2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 10]), but Bankier.pl now reports Polish debt at its weakest in over 5 years with the market mercilessly raising our rates [Sep 14]. Commerzbank's view that the NBP has turned cautious as inflation rises sits next to Millennium's path of CPI above 4% in early 2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 11]) and an ECB that hiked again (FXMAG, [Sep 11]). Oil passed 108 dollars (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 14]) and European gas is the most expensive since 2022 as the Middle East paralyses energy markets (Bankier.pl, [Sep 14]). Growth remains the counterweight: VeloBank's 2026 GDP forecast is 3.7% (WNP.PL, [Sep 10]) and ING says the current account stabilised in July [Sep 14]. The social bill is large: spending on social protection rose 18.6% to 757.2 bn zł (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 14]), 22.5% of GDP (Poinformowani, [Sep 14]). Specialist pay is slowing (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 14]) and half of employees earn no more than 7530 zł (Wszystko co najważniejsze, [Sep 14]).

Markets: Monday was a risk-off session: the WIG20 closed down 0.43% (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 14]) as investors avoided risky assets and KGHM fell 5% (Portal spożywczy, [Sep 14]) on the copper and silver slide (DNA Rynków, [Sep 14]); Pepco and Allegro led the gainers (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 14]) and CD Projekt rose against the market on the Blizzard tie-up that puts Cyberpunk 2077 on Battle.net and Geralt in Diablo 4 (FXMAG, [Sep 14]). The card at Friday's close still shows last week's leaders intact: EPOL 45.56 (+2.3% 1W, +3.6% 4W); PGE 14.03 (+11.9% 1W, +30.1% 4W); PKO 120.52 (+3.1%, +8.5%) and Pekao 267.4 (+1.3%, +3.2%); Orlen 161.12 (+2.0%, +5.9%); KGHM 351.9 (-1.2% 1W) before today's drop; CD Projekt 235.4 (+1.5%, -10.5% 4W); Dino 35.4 (+9.2% 4W); LPP 21560 (+1.0%) and Allegro 44.94 (-1.4%) ahead of results this week (XYZ, [Sep 14]). The zloty is the new weak link: USD/PLN closed at 3.7262 (+0.4% 1W, +0.2% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3193 (+0.2% 1W, +0.3% 4W), and today the euro rose to 4.3250 while the dollar broke 3.74 (FXMAG, [Sep 14]) and then 3.75 with the euro at 4.33 (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 14]); Bankier.pl says expensive oil is choking the zloty [Sep 14]. POLGBs are driving everything: debt-service costs have crossed another line (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 14]) and US yields near 5% matter for Poland (Rankia Polska, [Sep 14]).

Fiscal & Politics: The Fiscal Council's first opinion is the fiscal event of the month: a 38-page takedown of Domański's budget (Wyborcza.biz, [Sep 14]) warning of a very high risk of breaching the 55% of GDP threshold in 2027 (Bankier.pl and Tysol.pl, [Sep 14]) and a situation it calls very, very serious (Money.pl, [Sep 14]). The ministry hit back that the Council exceeded its competence (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 14]). The baseline is debt at 53.3% of GDP at end-Q2 (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 10]). The MF wants to cut mining subsidies by 5 bn zł, setting up a government row (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 14]) and its forecasts put the average pension at 5219 zł gross (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 14]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: PKO BP's 1.5 bn zł senior issue (Bankier.pl, [Sep 14]) follows UOKiK's searches of three banks and BIK over possible collusion (Money.pl, [Sep 13]); Forsal.pl says POLSTR replacing WIBOR is a revolution for banks but neutral for clients [Sep 14]. Energy: Orlen plans 380 bn zł of investment and is seeking contractors (WNP.PL, [Sep 14]), signed an infrastructure-protection agreement with PERN (wnp.pl, [Sep 14]) and priced wholesale diesel at 8.95 zł per litre (Agro Profil, [Sep 14]). Record gas made August the worst month of the year for power prices (Gramwzielone.pl, [Sep 14]) and Tauron dismissed deputy CEO Surma (pb.pl, [Sep 14]). Retail: Allegro opened a Shenzhen office and a Chinese company to vet sellers at source (pb.pl, [Sep 14]); Biedronka's owner was fined PLN 525 million for labour-market collusion (LIGA.net, [Sep 14]); Dino's union dispute carries a strike warning (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 10]). Housing: mortgages are getting dearer across the board (Bankier.pl, [Sep 14]), Money.pl says the housing crisis has reached Poland [Sep 14], yet the BIK demand index rose 9.3% y/y in August (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Sep 13]).

Geopolitics & Security: A Russian drone hit a train bound for Warsaw with politicians as the alleged target (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 14]), just missing a diplomatic train (Reuters, [Sep 14]); Tusk warned Russia could be preparing serious strikes on Poland in coming weeks and months (UNITED24 Media, [Sep 14]) and Siemoniak put the services on full readiness (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 14]). Rutte called it Putin's desperation and pledged more support (The Kyiv Independent, [Sep 14]) and Sikorski said NATO would defeat Russia quite quickly (TVP World, [Sep 14]) while proposing drills near Kaliningrad (Defense News, [Sep 14]).

New / Most Important: The Fiscal Council has said in writing what the bond market had been pricing: a very high risk of breaching 55% of GDP in 2027, met by a ministry disputing the Council's mandate rather than its arithmetic. With POLGBs at their weakest in five years, the zloty through 3.74 on 108-dollar oil and a drone strike on a Warsaw-bound train, the NBP's hold is no longer the anchor it was last week.  |  Playbook ✓: CEE central banks pausing as energy inflation resurges is on-script; stay in banks, keep duration short, treat PGE as the hedge against the power-price spiral.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The index lost only 0.43% while KGHM lost 5% and bonds hit five-year lows; equities are still pricing last week's foreign inflows, not this week's fiscal warning.
