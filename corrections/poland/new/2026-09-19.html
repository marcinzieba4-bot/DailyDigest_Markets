🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The week closed with the RPP's bias tilting toward tightening rather than easing. August CPI was confirmed at 3.4% y/y (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 15]), the highest in 14 months (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 15]), with transport services up 11.2% (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 15]) and food cheaper for a fourth month (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 15]); NBP's core reading rose again (Biznes Wprost, [Sep 16]). An MPC member said a hike is possible if energy prices get out of control (Forex Club, [Sep 18]) and Zarzecki told FXMAG he maintains his hike call [Sep 15]. The real economy is less convincing: August industrial output rose 4.3% y/y, below expectations (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 18]), and ING called the lengthening energy crisis a risk to second-half growth [Sep 18]. Labour cost growth slowed from 9.7% to 4.7% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 17]) and the 2027 minimum wage of 4950 zł is official (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Sep 16]).

Markets: The third Friday of September shook the GPW: FXMAG counted almost 6 billion zł of turnover and an LPP rally on triple witching [Sep 19], while the WIG20 still closed 0.50% lower (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 18]) with mBank leading the fall and PZU sliding as pressure hit financials (ad-hoc-news.de, [Sep 19]). Earlier the index slipped below 4100 points on PZU's record dividend (Parkiet, [Sep 16]) before Allegro's surge lifted it on Thursday (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 17]). The card tells a two-speed story: LPP at 24160 is up 12.1% on the week and 16.2% over four weeks, Allegro at 49.8 gained 10.8% and 10.4%, while Orlen at 151.14 lost 6.2% on the week, KGHM at 337.05 fell 4.2% and PGE at 13.93 eased 0.7% but retains a 36.7% four-week gain; PKO (119.84) and Pekao (269.6) were flat and EPOL at 44.41 dropped 2.5% on the week. The zloty closed the Fed week weaker: USD/PLN at 3.7934 (+1.9% 1W, +2.8% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3529 (+0.7% 1W, +0.9% 4W), after the dollar hit its dearest since July and the euro its highest this year (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]). POLGBs remain the pressure point: the 10-year yield broke 6.5% (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Sep 15]), the highest since 2007 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 15]), before yields fell after the Fed (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 17]).

Fiscal & Politics: The Moody's downgrade is now a political event. The agency cut Poland to A3, the lowest level since 2002 (Notes From Poland, [Sep 19]), citing a high deficit and fast-growing debt (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 19]); Parkiet said the agency lost patience with the first cut in its history of rating Poland [Sep 19]. Domański offered his own version of events (naTemat.pl, [Sep 19]), the government urged calm while experts sounded the alarm (Money.pl, [Sep 19]) and Grabiec blamed the president while Przydacz blamed the government (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 19]). The Fiscal Council, whose first opinion called the situation very serious (Money.pl, [Sep 14]), is openly at odds with the ministry over what Money.pl called an unfortunate ministerial reaction [Sep 19]. The arithmetic is unchanged: a state deficit of 151.1 billion zł after August (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 16]) and a Council warning about debt above 55% of GDP (Bankier.pl, [Sep 14]). On revenue, the Sejm re-passed the fuel windfall tax on Friday (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]) while the president's margin-cap bill drew a warning from Orlen that there would be no fuel (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 18]).

Corporate & Sectors: LPP is the week's winner: net profit of 768 million zł and EBITDA of 1744 million zł (BIZNES24, [Sep 17]), sales up 20% y/y in the quarter so far (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 18]), Sinsay back to positive like-for-like with 3 thousand stores targeted for 2026 (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 18]) and record-high shares (wnp.pl, [Sep 18]). Allegro raised its 2026 outlook and hit a five-year high (Reuters, [Sep 17]); the FedEx-led consortium secured over 89% of InPost (Reuters, [Sep 18]). Orlen's week went the other way: Saudi Aramco is cutting Europe off from crude and Orlen issued an urgent supply statement (Super Biznes, [Sep 19]), it notified prosecutors over Olefin III with Balczun saying Obajtek cannot hide behind the board (Bankier.pl, [Sep 19]), and the shares fell over 7% (Comparic.pl, [Sep 18]). Strefa Biznesu argued PGE, Tauron, Enea and Energa are only getting going [Sep 19], KGHM's board refused the unions' extra bonus (MyGlogow, [Sep 19]) and Dino filed to enter another large city (Portal spożywczy, [Sep 19]). Banks are digesting the TSUE ruling on refunding interest on commissions and a possible wave of SKD suits (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]). Europe is recording its steepest energy price rises since 2022 (Biznes Alert, [Sep 19]) and Business Insider Polska found Polish house prices against wages the worst in Europe [Sep 19].

Geopolitics & Security: Poland scrambled jets four times in a week as Russian drone provocations tripled (The Sun, [Sep 19]) and fighters were again airborne on Saturday as drones struck Ukraine (Radio ESKA, [Sep 19]). France, Poland and allies warned of escalating Russian hybrid attacks (NPR, [Sep 19]) after Tusk said Russia may stage accidental strikes on NATO's eastern flank (TVP World, [Sep 17]); the army is fortifying crossings with HESCO positions (Nowiny 24, [Sep 17]) and Trump touted a plan for a new US base in Poland (CBS News, [Sep 18]).

New / Most Important: Saturday's papers are about blame, and that is the point: the downgrade has shifted Poland's fiscal problem from technical to political just as the president and government fight over fuel taxes rather than spending. With oil at 110 dollars (Interia Biznes, [Sep 19]), an RPP member talking hikes and the finance ministry quarrelling with its own Fiscal Council, the pre-election path to consolidation is narrowing. Markets have so far separated the sovereign from its champions: LPP and Allegro carried the GPW while POLGBs and the zloty absorbed the fiscal premium.  |  Playbook ✓: Own the earnings leaders, LPP, Allegro and the banks, over state-controlled names exposed to windfall and margin politics; keep duration short while the ministry and Council trade accusations; respect 3.80 on USD/PLN as the line the Fed, not the NBP, controls.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: LPP up 12.1% on the week while Orlen fell 6.2% and the sovereign lost a notch; the government calling for calm while its own Fiscal Council calls the situation very serious.
