🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, November hike debate

Macro Stance: Inflation is back at 4%. GUS's September flash printed 4.0% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Sep 30]), a touch under the 4.1% consensus (BIZNES24, [Sep 30]) but the highest in 15 months (Agro Profil, [Sep 30]) and outside the NBP's target band (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 30]). The driver is fuel, up 36.1% y/y (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 30]); ING calculates almost half of the rise is fuel and that core inflation ex food and energy fell (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 30]), while pb.pl says core is "keeping inflation in check" [Sep 30] and food is cheaper than a year ago (Agro Profil, [Sep 30]). The policy read-through is split. Inwestycje.pl reports the RPP may open a tightening discussion in November [Sep 30], Gazeta Prawna and StockWatch.pl ask whether hikes start [Sep 30] and pb.pl says rate decisions "hang by a thread" [Sep 30]; PKO BP, against the market, expects inflation to exceed 4% at year-end yet rates to stay unchanged (Bankier.pl, [Sep 29]), with a "4 in front" for several months (Portal spożywczy, [Sep 30]). The draft budget assumes growth slowing to 3% (TVP World, [Sep 29]). With eurozone inflation re-accelerating and the ECB under pressure, the external backdrop pushes the same way.

Markets: Warsaw diverged from Europe today: the GPW rose while European bourses sank (XYZ, [Sep 30]), the WIG30 closed up 0.65% (Investing.com, [Sep 30]) after a 0.23% loss on Tuesday [Sep 29], and Parkiet logged "another quarter of gains" with energy and banks driving the rebound [Sep 30]. PGE led the index with Tauron and CD Projekt strong (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 30]) and its share price hit an 11-year high (Bankier.pl, [Sep 30]); CD Projekt is fighting an important resistance (XTB, [Sep 30]) after the Witcher 3 Remaster drew a record 121 thousand concurrent Steam players (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 30]). The weak spots were Tuesday's bank selling (Parkiet, [Sep 29]), with the WIG20 then close to breaking down from consolidation [Sep 29], Orlen struggling for a rebound after a 10% slide on falling turnover (Comparic.pl, [Sep 29]) and KGHM "on the edge" where a break could herald a solid correction (Parkiet, [Sep 30]). The card to Tuesday's close: PGE 14.27 (+4.1% 1W, +29.8% 4W), LPP 25020 (+1.7% 1W, +19.8% 4W), Allegro 50.46 (+1.6% 1W, +11.1% 4W), CD Projekt 248.7 (+6.1% 4W), PKO 123.38 (+0.1% 1W, +8.5% 4W); against them Pekao 276.3 (-2.4% 1W), KGHM 323.0 (-7.0% 1W), Orlen 146.58 (-2.0% 1W, -5.8% 4W), Dino 35.41 (-1.6% 1W). EPOL fell to 44.2 (-3.0% 1W). The zloty: USD/PLN closed at 3.8487 (+1.4% 1W, +2.9% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3667 (+0.5% 1W, +0.8% 4W); the dollar reached 3.865 zł before the zloty tried to recoup losses (Bankier.pl, [Sep 30]), EUR/PLN broke out of its consolidation with the ADP report seen deciding the next move (Comparic.pl, [Sep 30]) and Morgan Stanley sees EUR/USD at 1.10 (Comparic.pl, [Sep 29]). POLGBs: the sell-off continues and banks are already counting losses, leaving less money for lending (pb.pl, [Sep 29]), though Bankier.pl argues bonds "have already priced the negative scenario" [Sep 30].

Fiscal & Politics: The government adopted the final 2027 budget: 977.6 bn zł of spending and a 281.2 bn zł deficit (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 30]), a record (Bankier.pl, [Sep 29]), alongside a debt strategy under which the 55% prudential level is passed in 2028 and the 60% constitutional limit is held to 2030 (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 29]); the minister sees net debt at 57.4% of GDP in 2028 (wnp.pl, [Sep 29]) and rejects fears of breaching the constitutional ceiling (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 30]). Dudek warns there is "no safety margin left" (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 29]) and wnp.pl reads the strategy as implying spending cuts in 2030 [Sep 30]. The 2026 deficit stood at about 172.2 bn zł after September (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 29]). The tax package raises the first PIT threshold and lifts CIT and lump-sum rates (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 29]), fuel companies could pay up to 30% CIT (MotoGuru.pl, [Sep 30]) and the energy-support bill came with a higher CIT (Parkiet, [Sep 29]). Domański told the president his "stubbornness harms Poland" over the fuel law (Interia Biznes, [Sep 30]); Warsaw rules out a pre-election spending boost (Briefs Finance, [Sep 29]).

Corporate & Sectors: Bank balance sheets are doing fiscal work: PKO BP issued 1.35 bn zł of senior non-preferred bonds (Bankier.pl, [Sep 29]), lent Tauron 900 mln zł to refinance (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 29]) and, with BGK, refinanced PGE's Gryfino CCGT units with 3.05 bn zł (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 29]). Pekao launches POLSTR mortgages from the start of October (Bankier.pl, [Sep 30]). Orlen cut wholesale fuel prices (Money.pl, [Sep 30]) and formally launched the Energa squeeze-out (wnp.pl, [Sep 30]); Unipetrol is protesting a Czech windfall tax (pb.pl, [Sep 29]). PGE opened "Projekt MOC" to buy RES, storage and grid projects (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 30]); the updated nuclear programme targets up to 9 GWe (Energetyka24, [Sep 30]). KGHM shares moved on Chilean supply worries (FXMAG, [Sep 29]). Dino opened 256 stores through September (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 29]), LPP Logistics expands its Geekplus robot fleet (DlaHandlu.pl, [Sep 30]) and Allegro keeps buying back with Amazon and IKEA managers proposed for the board (wirtualnemedia.pl, [Sep 29]).

Geopolitics & Security: Russian strikes that killed three in Kyiv prompted NATO to scramble aircraft in Poland and Romania (ABC News, [Sep 30]); PBS says Poland is bracing for confrontation as Russia tests NATO [Sep 29] and NATO detailed eastern-flank reinforcement [Sep 29]. Business Insider Polska reports Poland is losing its NATO lead in defence spending [Sep 30].

New / Most Important: The flash CPI at 4.0% lands the same week as a 281.2 bn zł deficit and a debt path through 55% in 2028. Fuel explains almost half of the inflation jump and core fell, which is why PKO BP still sees no hike; the market is pricing a November debate anyway. The policy mix is drifting toward tighter money and looser fiscal, the combination that keeps the zloty bid and POLGBs heavy.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold energy momentum (PGE at 11-year highs) and banks, trim KGHM into Parkiet's correction warning, keep POLGB duration short until the November RPP signal, use the zloty's partial recovery to hedge rather than add.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Headline 4.0% versus falling core; a record deficit draft the day Bankier.pl says bonds have already priced the bad case.
