🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: The NBP remains in a prolonged hold, but the governor has broken ranks: Super Biznes reported Glapiński wants to cut and experts are alarmed [Aug 3], against a July GUS flash of 3.0% y/y (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 31]) in which fuel and commodity risks are the problem (300Gospodarka.pl, [Jul 31]) and food is the only restraint (xyz.pl, [Aug 2]). Parkiet's economist panel still says the RPP should not hurry [Jul 31]. With the ECB's hawkish pivot constraining the rate differential, a cut remains a governor's wish rather than a Council plan. Today's data were about income and growth: GUS put the February median gross wage at 7690.82 zł (Prawo.pl, [Aug 5]), meaning half of Poles earn less than 7691 zł (Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 5]), with the median rising strongly and women gaining more than men (pb.pl, [Aug 5]). Interia Biznes reported the energy shock has not stopped Poland, with growth possibly as high as 4.1% [Aug 5] and TVP World placed Poland among the EU's lowest-unemployment countries [Aug 5].

Markets: The WIG20 pierced 4000 points for the first time and pulled back, with KGHM, Orlen and PKO BP in play (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 5]). Leadership has rotated hard: KGHM jumped 8% as copper traded at 14 thousand dollars (FXMAG, [Aug 5]), a step from its historic high in New York (PKB24.pl, [Aug 5]), while banks retreated from a record open (Bankier.pl, [Aug 5]) and Alior fell sharply after results (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 5]). The card captures the switch: KGHM at 339.05 is up 15.9% on the week, PGE at 11.09 gained 12.0% (1W) and 17.9% (4W), and Pekao rose 7.3% (1W) to 255.0, while PKO slipped 0.9% to 109.02. EPOL at 44.07 is up 6.9% on the week and 11.7% over four weeks; CD Projekt gained 8.2% (1W), LPP 4.2% and Allegro 1.5%, Dino only 1.1% after its earlier run (+16.0% 4W), and Orlen 1.5% (1W, +8.7% 4W). The zloty keeps attacking: USD/PLN closed at 3.7239 (-1.9% 1W, -1.2% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.2945 (-0.7% 1W, -0.1% 4W), with Bankier.pl describing the euro fighting to hold 4.30 zł [Aug 5] and FXMAG asking whether 4.28 is next [Aug 5]. No POLGB-specific headlines today beyond the launch of two bond ETFs (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Aug 4]).

Fiscal & Politics: The cost of the state is the week's fiscal theme: public spending is 50.9% of GDP, above Germany, Sweden and Denmark (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 5]), over 53 tys. zł per Pole, with debt service now costing more than 800+ (Interia Biznes, [Aug 5]), and FOR says repair is needed (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 5]). On tax relief Domański walked back the day-old signal, saying no decision has been taken and no declaration made (TVN24, [Aug 5]), a day after Bloomberg reported Poland is weighing income tax relief for millions before the election [Aug 4] and pb.pl described the ministry gluing together a 2027 budget with the EU's largest deficit [Aug 4]. The ministry refused a cheaper VAT rate on bottled water (Direct Money, [Aug 5]). Politically, the NSA's final ruling left PiS without its state subsidy, with Tusk posting that the money is not there and will not be (Interia Wydarzenia, [Aug 5]), and PSL is talking to Tusk about joint 2027 lists with Kosiniak-Kamysz floated as premier (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 5]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen opened its maritime transshipment terminal in Gdańsk, an investment of close to 500 mln zł (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 5]), made a second fuel-price move (Money.pl, [Aug 5]) while the energy minister spoke of talks on large cuts (Interia Motoryzacja, [Aug 5]); WNP.PL expects record results [Aug 5]. KGHM is using the copper boom to prepare energy investments (Parkiet, [Aug 5]). The nuclear programme reached a milestone: PEJ handed the Lubiatowo-Kopalino site to Bechtel and earthworks start (Gov.pl, [Aug 5]), with Budimex taking a contract above 200 mln zł (Bankier.pl, [Aug 5]). Power prices were set to hit 2202.13 zł/MWh from 20:00 on Wednesday (WNP.PL, [Aug 5]), and DM BOŚ lifted its PGE target to 14 zł (Bankier.pl, [Jul 30]). Banks won at the WSA, with mBank fighting for a billion zł (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 5]), and PKO deployed its first sovereign AI agent to answer clients up to 70% faster (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 5]). Retail is mixed: analysts cut Dino's valuation but still say buy, with Q2 as the turning point (Bankier.pl, [Aug 5]) despite 0% LFL growth and margin-erosion risk (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 5]); Allegro passed 21.9 mln users (Bankier.pl, [Aug 5]); LPP suspended orders in Bangladesh and rejected claims tied to its Russia exit involving 40 mln dollars (RMF24, [Aug 5]).

Geopolitics & Security: NDTV reported talks on a permanent US base in Poland [Aug 5], and Fakt flagged a series of Russian Il-20 flights near Poland gathering information [Aug 5]. The Belarus flank stays live after Lukashenko sent special forces to the Ukrainian border (Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 3]) and a new assault brigade appeared there (Forsal.pl, [Aug 3]), while Ukrainians warn Poland is running out of time (Forsal.pl, [Aug 4]).

New / Most Important: WIG20 above 4000 is the headline, but the composition is the message: the record is now carried by KGHM on 14-thousand-dollar copper and by PGE on heatwave power prices, not by the banks that built it, and Alior's drop shows the earnings bar is high. Beneath that, the fiscal arithmetic hardened — 50.9% of GDP in spending, debt service above 800+ — just as Domański retreated from the tax-relief signal; a governor talking cuts into that mix is a credibility question the zloty has so far ignored.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold pattern continues; rotate from bank beta toward KGHM and utilities while copper and power prices run, keep Dino as a Q2-results call, and hold duration light.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Index at 4000 with banks in retreat; a dovish governor against a 3.0% print and an ECB that could hike in September, which would make the NBP's hold relatively dovish and pressure PLN; LPP's Russia-exit dispute surfacing with the stock up 16.2% over four weeks.
