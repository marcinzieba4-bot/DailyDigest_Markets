🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: The RPP is drifting hawkish while the data run hot. Kotecki said the inflation picture is diverging from the NBP projection and it is too early to talk about a September cut (pb.pl, [Jul 23]); another Council hawk called the earlier cuts premature (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 21]) and one member said hikes are now more likely than cuts (Super Biznes, [Jul 21]). The dovish side is thinner: Crédit Agricole still expects a 25 bp cut in September to 3.50% as the terminal level (PAP Biznes, [Jul 20]), but CEO Magazyn warned the oil shock could stop Polish cuts altogether [Jul 23]. Inflation itself looks tame: it fell to 3% (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Jul 22]) and Poland slid to 17th in the EU inflation table (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 27]). Activity is the doves' problem: June retail sales rose 6.2% y/y in constant prices against a 5.2% consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 22]), industrial production rose 7.6% (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 20]) and ING BSK sees roughly 3.8% y/y GDP growth in Q2 (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 22]). Wages rose 5.9% y/y in June with employment falling (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 20]), unemployment fell (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 23]) and producer prices rose for a fourth month (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 20]).

Markets: Warsaw is pressing on a record with one casualty. Parkiet had the WIG20 aiming at a record going into the week [Jul 27] after the index stalled at the 3900 mark (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 23]). On Monday Żabka fell 12.5% early (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 27]) and ended down around 14% after Seven & i walked away from stake talks (Briefs Finance, [Jul 27]), while Dino and CD Projekt led the gainers and the main indices closed higher (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 27]). Orlen keeps breaking records with analysts lifting targets (Comparic.pl, [Jul 27]); PGE jumped 7.37% on Jul 23 as banks sank (FXMAG), and KGHM lost 3.97% on Friday (FXMAG, [Jul 24]). The card confirms the energy tilt: Orlen at 156.0 is up 6.7% on the week and 26.4% over four weeks, PGE at 10.48 is up 10.5% and 9.2%, Allegro at 44.74 is up 20.3% in four weeks; KGHM at 303.5 is still down 7.4% over four weeks, Dino (-1.3% 1W) and CD Projekt (-2.6% 1W) lagged, PKO was flat at -0.2% and Pekao rose 2.1%. EPOL closed at 40.92, up 1.4% on the week and 6.1% over four weeks. The zloty is firmer: USD/PLN closed at 3.7864 (-0.3% 1W, +0.6% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3147 (-0.6% 1W, +0.7% 4W), with MyBank.pl putting EUR/PLN near 4.31 as the dollar weakened [Jul 27]. The weak leg is debt: yields jumped sharply even as the euro stayed stable (Bankier.pl, [Jul 24]) and the August retail bond offer was left unchanged (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 27]).

Fiscal & Politics: The debt story turned loud. Public debt reached 61.6% of GDP, the third fastest increase in the EU (XYZ, [Jul 21]), Treasury debt exceeds 2.1 trillion zł (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 24]), it has climbed above 500 billion euros at a record borrowing pace (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]) and Arak expects more than 70% of GDP by end-2027 (Filary Biznesu, [Jul 26]). Policy pulls the other way: the finance ministry has advanced variants of a 60 000 zł tax-free allowance (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 24]), PIT changes could leave up to 433 zł more in pockets (Fakt, [Jul 27]) and the ministry withdrew planned lump-sum tax increases (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 27]). On the revenue side the Sejm passed a savings tax from 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 25]) and the digital tax drew a US retaliation threat (Briefs Finance, [Jul 22]). The sharpest clash is fuel: President Nawrocki sent the windfall-profits tax to the Constitutional Tribunal, freezing 4 billion zł of CPN programme funding (Gov.pl, [Jul 27]), the government is redesigning the fuel levy on the bank-tax model (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 27]) and Bloomberg reported Poland may revive fuel-price caps [Jul 27] as diesel threatens 8 zł (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 27]).

Corporate & Sectors: Allegro named Katarzyna Ostap-Tomann of Cyfrowy Polsat as CFO after Jonathan Eastick resigned (Parkiet, [Jul 27]). PGE GiEK extended the oldest coal units at Rybnik to 2028 with a gas plant to follow (Dziennik Zachodni, [Jul 27]). Orlen flagged lower crude throughput and impairments in its Q2 estimates (Energetyka24.com, [Jul 22]). CD Projekt will show the Witcher 3 expansion at gamescom (pb.pl, [Jul 21]) with the stock around 230 zł (Comparic.pl, [Jul 21]). Banks face the CHF law from early August with instalments suspended by statute (Franknews.pl, [Jul 25]), yet pb.pl argued debt and credit could lift bank profits by up to half by 2030 [Jul 27]. Dino overtook Eurocash in grocery (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Jul 27]); Warsaw flat prices are falling (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jul 22]) and rental listings dropped 3.2% m/m in June (PAP Biznes, [Jul 22]).

Geopolitics & Security: Belarus moved special forces to the Ukrainian border (TVP Info, [Jul 22]) and troops are reported on the Polish border itself (Kresy24.pl, [Jul 23]); Warsaw warned Russia may turn captured Ukrainian drones against Poland and the Baltics (Defense News, [Jul 21]), and NATO's top general said Russia could move on Poland or the Baltics (National Security Journal, [Jul 20]). Poland launched a 205 million dollar defence innovation programme (Anadolu, [Jul 23]), MON will deploy a Smart Border (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 25]) and Poland is bidding for a Patriot factory (Tech Times, [Jul 24]).

New / Most Important: Seven & i dropping Żabka is the week's signal: a 14% hit to a WIG20 name on the day the index was reaching for a record shows how much of the rally rests on takeover premia and foreign bids rather than on domestic fundamentals, which are now fiscal (debt past 60% of GDP) and hawkish (Kotecki).
