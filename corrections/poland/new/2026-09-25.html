🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The RPP published its monetary policy assumptions for 2027, with inflation seen falling but GDP growth slowing too (XYZ, [Sep 25]), and Business Insider Polska parsed the new NBP projections for growth, prices and loan rates [Sep 25]. That arrives as the Financial Times describes the central bank as trapped in a political contest [Sep 25], Kotecki recalls that Poland has already chased inflation with rates once (pb.pl, [Sep 24]) and an RPP member has indicated a date for an increase (WNP.PL, [Sep 23]). PZU's economists told PAP Biznes the summer brought less growth and more inflation and that third-quarter GDP may slow a little [Sep 25], and Domański admitted he fears inflation may rise (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]). Behind this sit a GUS business climate weakening before the fourth quarter (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 24]), unemployment up to 5.8% (Warszawa w Pigułce, [Sep 23]) and real wage growth at a three-year low (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 22]). Bankier.pl flags a coming week dominated by inflation and labour-market releases [Sep 25].

Markets: The week ended with what Parkiet called a signal of demand fatigue [Sep 25]: the WIG20 closed in the red with Tauron falling after a Fitch decision (XYZ, [Sep 25]), Pepco led while Tauron and Budimex fell hard (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 25]) and Parkiet argues the domestic market may now set its direction for longer [Sep 25]. The card at Thursday's close shows banks still the only leadership: PKO at 124.46 is up 3.7% on the week and 10.8% over four weeks, Pekao at 281.1 up 3.3% and 10.7%; LPP at 24640 keeps a 10.1% weekly and 18.9% four-week gain, but PGE at 13.22 has lost 7.2% on the week, Dino at 34.63 is down 4.2%, KGHM at 335.85 is 7.1% lower over four weeks and Orlen at 150.18 is off 2.4%; EPOL closed at 44.72, flat on the week. The zloty found a floor only after testing 4.40 on the euro (Bankier.pl, [Sep 25]): Magazyn MANAGER+ had the currency recovering with EUR/PLN back below 4.40 [Sep 25] and Comparic.pl saw USD/PLN down to 3.83 as the dollar began a correction [Sep 25]; the card's Thursday closes were USD/PLN 3.8467 (+1.4% 1W, +3.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.376 (+0.5% 1W, +1.1% 4W). In rates, StockWatch.pl reports record spreads on Polish bonds [Sep 25] after the sell-off Rzeczpospolita called the worst in years [Sep 24], with US five-year yields above 5% for the first time since 2007 (XYZ, [Sep 25]).

Fiscal & Politics: The finance ministry spelled out the fiscal and economic effects of its announced tax package (Podatki.biz, [Sep 25]), bowed to business pressure on KSeF penalties (RMF24, [Sep 25]) and, in the day's political headline, signed an interpretation giving same-sex couples married abroad the same tax rights as other spouses (Bankier.pl, [Sep 25]), which Gazeta Prawna expects to cause chaos [Sep 25]. The Senate has sent the fuel windfall tax to the president (Money.pl, [Sep 24]) and the ministry refuses to index PIT thresholds (Bankier.pl, [Sep 24]). The debt side is darkening: Zero.pl says the giant debt weighs on the government and creditors will demand ever higher interest [Sep 25] and the EBRD questions whether the deficit will exceed the 6.8% target this year (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]). PKO BP again paid the highest CIT in the country with Orlen close to a record (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 25]).

Corporate & Sectors: Erste plans an offer to raise its stake in Erste Bank Polska by up to 26% (Reuters, [Sep 25]), to 75% (Briefs Finance, [Sep 25]), and the bank is already writing POLSTR mortgages (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 25]) after mBank said it will pull WIBOR from new loans from October (Parkiet, [Sep 24]). PKO BP is targeting 30% of the OKI market (Analizy.pl, [Sep 25]). Orlen raised petrol prices by more than 100 zł in its latest decision (wnp.pl, [Sep 25]) and is taking Energa private through a squeeze-out (Energetyka24, [Sep 24]), while Poczta Polska may absorb Orlen Paczka (Bankier.pl, [Sep 24]). KGHM declared mourning after a miner died at Rudna (TVN24, [Sep 25]), a day after its Victoria project won a place on Ontario's strategic list (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 24]). Trigon named CD Projekt among its top gaming picks (Parkiet, [Sep 25]) even as the Witcher 3 remaster drew a storm (CrypS.pl, [Sep 25]). Dino will build a distribution centre in Wielkopolska on 18 hectares bought for 14.5 million zł (TVP Poznań, [Sep 25]) while unions set a date for a demonstration at its Krotoszyn headquarters (Krotoszyn.dlawas.info, [Sep 25]). The infrastructure minister said the nuclear plant will be on time (Bankier.pl, [Sep 25]).

Geopolitics & Security: Polish fighters were airborne again on Friday with an RCB alert for Podkarpacie and Lublin (Goniec, [Sep 25]), and Newsweek mapped a week of Russian incursions and sabotage across Europe [Sep 25]. Danish intelligence says Russia could attack a NATO country within months (The Guardian, [Sep 24]), Poland called the Starlink station fire sabotage (BBC, [Sep 24]) and the Dorohusk and Zosin crossings were evacuated (Super Tydzień Chełmski, [Sep 24]).

New / Most Important: The RPP's 2027 assumptions, lower inflation bought with lower growth, land in the same week the FT calls the NBP politically trapped and the bond market prints record spreads. That is the real constraint on Polish policy: the Council can talk about hikes, but with a weakening consumer, a 4.40 euro and a budget the ministry admits it will not fill, every move is read as political. Erste's bid for control of its Polish bank is the counterpoint: foreign capital paying up for Polish banking exactly as the sovereign is repriced.  |  Playbook ✓: Stay with the banks, where both the rate path and now M&A work in favour; keep POLGB duration short while spreads are at records; use the zloty's bounce from 4.40 to reduce, not add, unhedged exposure; avoid PGE after a 7.2% weekly drop until the tariff-freeze debate clears.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Erste paying for 75% of a Polish bank while Polish bond spreads hit records; the RPP projecting lower inflation while a member names a date for a hike.
