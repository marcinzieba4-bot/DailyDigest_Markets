🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: A quiet Sunday on the data front leaves the picture from the week intact: the RPP is waiting (Parkiet, [Jun 25]), a hike vote was on record at the last meeting (Tysol.pl, [Jun 22]), and the NBP survey sees inflation under control with cuts uncertain (Parkiet, [Jun 24]). Disinflation is visible in the shops, where the pace of price growth is slowing fastest in food (Super Nowości, [Jun 22]), but Bankier.pl cautions that cheaper oil and gas will not free Europe from inflation quickly [Jun 24]. Growth is being carried by industry and defence: Business Insider Polska calls the jump in orders historic and the product of one programme [Jun 24], and Zero.pl says industry is drowning in orders [Jun 28]. The household side lags, with wages up 5.8% y/y in May below forecasts (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 22]), employment still falling (Bankier.pl, [Jun 22]) and Strefa Biznesu warning there is a catch behind the headline average pay figure [Jun 22]. Gazeta Prawna's forecast survey has the economy slowing in the coming years [Jun 23]. Poland is still described as the outlier dove in a hawkish world, but on this feed the right label is hold.

Markets: The week ended with Warsaw in correction mode: Friday's WIG20 fell with only Kęty and Żabka against the trend (Inwestycje.pl, [Jun 26]) as global equities sold off (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 26]). Friday's closes on the card: EPOL 38.27 (-3.7% 1W, -5.7% 4W); KGHM 327.75 (-10.3% 1W, -4.9% 4W) after the 7.5% slide on Wednesday (Portal spożywczy, [Jun 24]); Orlen 123.46 (-2.3% 1W, -12.6% 4W); PGE 9.6 (-1.9% 1W, -11.1% 4W); Allegro 37.19 (-5.7% 1W, +8.7% 4W) despite the buyback; CD Projekt 217.4 (-3.0% 1W, -4.7% 4W); LPP 18310 (-1.4% 1W, -16.4% 4W); PKO 103.62 (-1.6% 1W, +0.4% 4W); Pekao 228.8 (-2.8% 1W, -5.1% 4W); Dino 28.98 (-0.8% 1W, -6.6% 4W). The pattern is clear: four-week losses are concentrated in state-controlled energy, copper and LPP, while the banks are roughly flat and Allegro retains its monthly gain. FX: USD/PLN at 3.7661 (+1.4% 1W, +3.9% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.2788 (+0.6% 1W, +1.3% 4W), with the euro held under 4.30 zł (Bankier.pl, [Jun 26]) and bond yields falling as the dollar eased (pb.pl, [Jun 26]). Parkiet's verdict is that the GPW trades in the shadow of the zloty [Jun 25]. Debt-servicing costs are up almost 30% (Parkiet, [Jun 25]) and July retail bonds offer up to 5.65% (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jun 24]).

Fiscal & Politics: The weekend brought no new decisions, so the standing items are the debt pile and the tax response. Public debt rose almost 47 bn zł in a month (FXMAG, [Jun 23]), wPolityce.pl puts May borrowing at 1.5 bn zł a day [Jun 26], and Bankier.pl warns of a coming accumulation of spending on nuclear and CPK with an EU procedure in the background [Jun 27]. The windfall tax sits between the Sejm's 60% (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 19]) and the Senate's 74% fuse (Wyborcza.biz, [Jun 26]). MF is preparing joint liability rules in property tax (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 28]), alongside the alcohol and sugar levies (Bloomberg, [Jun 24]) and the 2027 tax that routes around the president's veto on raises (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 25]). The CHF law awaits the president's signature after the Senate passed it unamended (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 25]), with automatic suspension of instalments on filing (CHF24, [Jun 27]).

Corporate & Sectors: Nuclear moved: the head of Westinghouse Polska said key elements of the EPC contract will be agreed in the coming months (wnp.pl, [Jun 28]), after Money.pl reported a dispute over the contract with the Americans [Jun 24] and Forsal.pl identified the 2036 completion date as the main problem [Jun 26]; the heatwave forced Hungary's nuclear plant to cut output over river temperatures (Business Insider Polska, [Jun 28]), a reminder of the grid stress that sent Polish spot power to 1442 zł per MWh (wnp.pl, [Jun 25]). Dino took a decision involving more than a billion zł (Portal spożywczy, [Jun 28]) and is closing one of its stores (Portal spożywczy, [Jun 28]) while its mobbing allegations reached prosecutors (DlaHandlu.pl, [Jun 26]). CD Projekt set the premiere date for Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 (PPE, [Jun 28]) after analysts cut the stock (INNPoland.pl, [Jun 25]). Orlen's fuel prices rose again from Saturday (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 27]). The Allegro buyback of up to 42 mln shares at a maximum 45 zł (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 26]) and the four-utility Ukraine letter of intent (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 26]) remain the live corporate stories, and CEO Magazyn sees Poland's software industry entering a more selective growth phase [Jun 28].

Geopolitics & Security: The warnings escalated in specificity: Poland's intelligence chief said Russia may use "little green men" in the Baltic states (Українська правда, [Jun 28]), NATO countries were warned of a possible Russian provocation against Poland and the Baltics (The Guardian, via УНН, [Jun 28]), and RBC-Ukraine reports an alert over Russian covert operatives [Jun 28]. This follows The Guardian's report that NATO leaders fear they cannot rely on US help if Russia attacks [Jun 27] and Sikorski's false-flag warning (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Jun 27]). Warsaw is also seeking to take over the former Russian consulate building in Gdańsk (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Jun 28]).

New / Most Important: The security premium is the story of the weekend. Two consecutive days of intelligence warnings about Russian provocations, layered on doubts about US backing, explain why Polish assets could not hold Thursday's bounce and why the four-week losers are all state-linked or cyclical. The offset is real but slower: a defence-order boom that Zero.pl says is drowning industry in work, and a Westinghouse EPC timetable that finally has months, not years, attached.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold-rate NBP; keep banks as the core long, stay light in Orlen, PGE and KGHM until the tax and security premia stop widening, and treat the Allegro buyback as the one clean domestic catalyst.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Industry drowning in orders while employment falls; a stable zloty and falling yields against escalating security warnings; Dino committing over a billion zł while its labour dispute is with prosecutors.
