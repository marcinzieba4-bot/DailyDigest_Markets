🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, decision Wednesday

Macro Stance: The RPP is in session (Biznes Wprost, [Sep 8]) with the decision due Wednesday (TVN24, [Sep 7]), and the consensus is unchanged rates (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 8]) with no good news for borrowers (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 8]). Rzeczpospolita calls it the end of illusions about quick cuts, with fuel prices blocking the Council [Sep 8]; Super Biznes says instalments will not fall [Sep 8]. The hawkish camp built its case yesterday: Credit Agricole sees strong growth and a possibly persistent overshoot of 3.5% inflation as arguments for a hike (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 7]). The backdrop is the August flash CPI of 3.4% y/y (Agro Profil, [Sep 8]) and world food prices turning up per the FAO index (Agro Profil, [Sep 8]), while oil near $100 (XTB.com, [Sep 8]) is a direct headwind for a net energy importer. The real economy keeps outperforming: GUS reported a record tourist summer (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 8]) and Rzeczpospolita expects the best year-end in the labour market in two years [Sep 8]. The ECB's hike expected Thursday sets the tone for CEE; with the zloty waiting on the Council (Forsal.pl, [Sep 8]), Wednesday's statement is the week's domestic catalyst.

Markets: Another record session, with a wobble at the close (Parkiet, [Sep 8]): the WIG and WIG20 pushed to new highs and PGE reached its highest in 9 years (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]), KGHM rose 4.6% with energy names up (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]) while banks fell and weighed on the index (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 8]); XTB's desk summed it up as KGHM supporting, banks losing, oil near $100 [Sep 8]. Business Insider Polska attributes the records to visible foreign inflows [Sep 8]. Monday's leaders were PGE and Tauron, up 4.94% and 4.80% (FXMAG, [Sep 8]). The card, as of Monday's close, shows the extent of the energy trade: PGE at 13.16 (+18.6% 1W, +23.8% 4W), Pekao at 266.1 (+2.7% 1W, +4.8% 4W), Orlen at 158.6 (+2.8% 1W, +5.7% 4W), PKO BP at 116.9 (+2.6% 1W, +6.2% 4W), CD Projekt at 236.7 (+1.0% 1W, -7.9% 4W) and KGHM at 358.0 before today's jump; EPOL stood at 45.12 (+3.8% 1W). The zloty is cheapening today (Comparic.pl, [Sep 8]) as the euro waits for the ECB and the franc scrapes multi-month lows (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]); the card has USD/PLN at 3.7046 (-0.5% 1W, -0.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3073 (-0.5% 1W, +0.2% 4W). POLGBs got a supply signal: the finance ministry says 75.7 bn zł will flow from the budget to the market by year-end (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]), a day after Allianz Trade flagged the rising cost of financing Poland's needs (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Sep 7]).

Fiscal & Politics: Forsal.pl summarises the 2027 budget as record deficit, rising debt and new taxes [Sep 8]; Newsweek quotes a warning of fiscal shock therapy that will hit wages and inflation [Sep 8]. The veto risk is now quantified in direction if not size: Rzeczpospolita reports Nawrocki could block billions of zloty of budget revenue, with economists warning of the consequences [Sep 8]. The government wants to abolish the State Treasury asset reserve and move the money into the budget (XYZ, [Sep 8]) and the media bill is stuck between ministries over 2.5 bn zł (wirtualnemedia.pl, [Sep 8]). Poland allocated PLN 500 million to defence technologies, drones, AI and cybersecurity (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 8]) and PKO BP estimates defence investment could give the economy an impulse worth almost 1.3 bln zł [Sep 8].

Corporate & Sectors: KGHM is the day's story: it signed a letter of intent with BHP on copper-sector cooperation (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 8]), which WNP.PL links to a bid for a valuable deposit [Sep 8] and CrypS.pl says gives investors a reason to look beyond the copper price [Sep 8]. PGE is being wired into the defence budget: a BGK letter of intent for a loan of up to 300 mln zł from the KPO security and defence fund (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 8]), hundreds of millions from defence money (WNP.PL, [Sep 8]), additional 2027 capacity secured at an auction price above 325 zł (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 8]). Orlen has over 170 thousand retail shareholders and new loyalty rules (pb.pl, [Sep 8]), touts a return exceeding 120% (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 8]) and the defence minister spoke plainly about it taking PGZ (WNP.PL, [Sep 8]). CD Projekt wants to be a multi-project studio beyond three universes with capex still rising (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 8]) and Witcher 3 sales are up on the new expansion (Forsal.pl, [Sep 8]). Banks hold 508 bn zł of lending potential (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]) while WIBOR lawsuits reached nearly 1.4 thousand in a half year (300Gospodarka.pl, [Sep 8]). Energy costs keep climbing: power prices are up 61% when they were supposed to fall (WNP.PL, [Sep 8]), household bills could rise by 200 zł (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 8]) or even 800 zł (Farmer.pl, [Sep 8]), a higher CIT on energy will fund aid for energy-intensive firms (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 8]) and the energy minister rejected a 10 zł per litre scenario while revealing the CPN programme's cost (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland's security chief met the CIA director amid concerns over the Russian threat (TVP World, [Sep 8]); jets were scrambled over Podkarpacie and Lubelskie (FXMAG, [Sep 8]); a Polish MiG-29 intercepted a Russian Il-20 over the Baltic (The Defense Watch, [Sep 8]); Warsaw summoned the Russian envoy over state terrorism (TVP World, [Sep 3]) after the drone-factory attack was called deliberate sabotage (London Business News, [Sep 3]).

New / Most Important: The KGHM-BHP letter of intent matters beyond one stock: it is the first sign that the GPW rally is broadening from domestic energy earnings into globally priced assets, on the same day the finance ministry announced 75.7 bn zł of year-end budget outflows. Oil near $100 keeps the NBP on hold; any easing expectations should be pushed further out.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold pattern continues; energy and now mining over banks into the RPP statement.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Banks sold off on a record index day; if Wednesday's language is hawkish, that is the wrong trade, since higher-for-longer is their margin, not the sovereign's.
