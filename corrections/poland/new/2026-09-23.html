🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The hike debate has acquired a calendar. An RPP member indicated a date for a rate increase (WNP.PL, [Sep 23]) and Kotecki pointed to a timeframe in which hikes look unavoidable (Biznes Wprost, [Sep 23]). The data cut both ways. Money supply hit a record near 3 trillion zloty (TVP World, [Sep 23]), diesel reached PLN 8 per litre (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 22]) and Dziennik.pl warned energy may push inflation up again [Sep 23] from August's 3.4% (Rankia Polska, [Sep 23]). Against that, unemployment rose to 5.8% with more people without benefit entitlement (Warszawa w Pigułce, [Sep 23]), though better than the labour ministry's estimate (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 23]); CEO Magazyn found more industrial divisions falling than rising in August [Sep 23]; retail sales of +3.8% missed the 4.5% consensus (wnp.pl, [Sep 22]); and wages rose 5.6% while employment fell 0.8% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 21]). ING reads domestic price pressure as subdued while household finances weaken [Sep 21], the ambiguity the RPP must now price.

Markets: Supply returned to the GPW on Wednesday: Parkiet reported indices retreating from the bull-market peaks with energy names and KGHM going against the tide [Sep 23], XYZ called it profit-taking [Sep 23] and Allegro was the biggest faller as most stocks closed lower (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 23]). That follows Monday's bank-driven records (Bankier.pl, [Sep 21]), when the WIG30 rose 2.21% (Investing.com, [Sep 21]). The card, as of Tuesday's close, still shows a bank-led tape: Pekao at 283.1 is up 4.9% on the week and 10.1% over four weeks, PKO at 123.2 up 2.4% and 8.5%, KGHM at 347.25 rebounded 6.2% on the week, LPP at 24600 holds a 12.0% weekly and 18.8% four-week gain, Allegro at 49.65 is up 7.7%, while Orlen at 149.54 is down 5.7% and PGE at 13.7 has eased 1.7% but keeps a 30.0% four-week run; EPOL closed at 45.57, up 1.6% on the week. The zloty is the day's casualty: TVN24 called it the weakest in two years under pressure from across the ocean [Sep 23], Comparic.pl put USD/PLN at 3.84 with the zloty among the world's weakest currencies [Sep 23] and Forex Club had EUR/PLN above 4.3750 [Sep 23]; the card's Tuesday closes were USD/PLN 3.7973 (+1.0% 1W, +3.1% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3446 (+0.2% 1W, +1.0% 4W). POLGBs held up better: the finance ministry sold PLN 12.0bn of bonds against PLN 22.5bn of demand (TradingView, [Sep 23]) even as US yields shot through the roof (Bankier.pl, [Sep 23]).

Fiscal & Politics: The finance ministry admitted billions will be missing from the budget and said it will do nothing about it (wnp.pl, [Sep 23]), days after the Moody's cut to A3 (Investing.com, [Sep 18]) that ING attributed to the lack of fiscal consolidation [Sep 21]. The revenue push continues: PIT and CIT changes hitting lump-sum payers and landlords (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 22]), a VAT rise on beverages (Wyborcza.biz, [Sep 22]), a higher RTV fee from 2027 (Money.pl, [Sep 23]) and the fuel windfall tax the Sejm passed a second time (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]). The fuel standoff is unresolved: INNPoland.pl says the government has no fallback if Nawrocki vetoes [Sep 23], Czarzasty argued the president's price-cut bill makes citizens pay (Farmer.pl, [Sep 23]) and the energy minister said it would lead to shortages at stations (Dziennik Zachodni, [Sep 23]). The debt threshold that would hit pension indexation is approaching (Forsal.pl, [Sep 22]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen dominates. The company notified prosecutors over a 25-year contract and a mysterious Malaysia trip (Zero.pl, [Sep 23]), with 1.2 billion dollars of penalties at stake (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 23]) and Bankier.pl putting the exposure at 4.6 billion zł [Sep 23]; it cut wholesale fuel prices (INNPoland.pl, [Sep 23]), said it cannot lower margins (Auto Świat, [Sep 23]) and announced a squeeze-out of Energa minorities (Bankier.pl, [Sep 23]). The nuclear programme passed a milestone as PEJ and the Bechtel-Westinghouse consortium agreed key commercial terms (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 23]), though Business Insider Polska would hold the champagne [Sep 23]. KGHM plans 32 billion zł of investment with talk of an acquisition (wnp.pl, [Sep 23]). Allegro launched Phase II of its buyback for up to 800 million zł (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 23]). In banking, a report put the negative WIBOR-litigation scenario at up to 284 billion zł of costs (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 23]) and PKO BP's first POLSTR mortgage topped the ranking (Comparic.pl, [Sep 23]). Unions at Dino are threatening a strike (DlaHandlu.pl, [Sep 23]) and power in 2027 will be much dearer (wnp.pl, [Sep 23]).

Geopolitics & Security: A Russian Mi-8 helicopter briefly entered Polish airspace and F-16s were scrambled (Military Watch Magazine, [Sep 23]), Poland accused Russia of the violation (BBC, [Sep 23]), two border crossings with Ukraine were evacuated (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 23]) and NATO jets were scrambled in Poland and Romania as Russia struck Ukraine (ABC News, [Sep 23]). That caps a week of alerts: thousands received emergency warnings on Tuesday (The Mirror, [Sep 22]), 23 shelters are ready on the border with Patriots deployed (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 22]; Strefa Obrony, [Sep 22]) and European intelligence expects a Russian attack on NATO in months, not years (19FortyFive, [Sep 22]).

New / Most Important: A two-year low in the zloty on the day an RPP member names a hike date is the week's signal. The currency is being set by the Fed and a sovereign that just lost a notch, not by the NBP, and that transfers the inflation problem straight back to the Council through fuel and imports. Add the Orlen prosecutor filing with billions in penalties at stake, and the state-champion complex is where the risk sits while banks and retailers absorb the flows.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold banks and LPP through the profit-taking; avoid Orlen until the Malaysia matter and the margin bill clear; take the strong 12 billion zł auction as a reason to add POLGBs only at the front end; do not fight USD/PLN above 3.84 until the dollar turns.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Bonds drawing 22.5 billion zł of demand while the zloty hits its weakest in two years; KGHM up 6.2% on the week as the index turns down.
