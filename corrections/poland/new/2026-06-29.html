🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: The NBP's room is narrowing from the fiscal side: Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK argues the state's debt now ties the central bank's hands [Jun 29], and the RPP is waiting (Parkiet, [Jun 25]) after a hike vote at the last meeting (Tysol.pl, [Jun 22]). The inflation calendar turns this week: Wednesday brings the return of the higher fuel VAT and a free market in fuel prices after the energy minister's last maximum-price notice (Next Gazeta.pl, [Jun 29]), petrol is already at 6 zł with diesel clearly dearer (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 29]), and Gazeta Prawna warns the energy shock will feed food prices and lift inflation in 2027 [Jun 29]. The heatwave adds to it: power prices jumped over 500% on the exchanges (Next Gazeta.pl, [Jun 29]). Labour is cooling rather than tightening: unemployment is the lowest in months (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 24]) but employment has fallen for a 32nd consecutive month (Super Biznes, [Jun 22]) and wages show no clear acceleration (Portal spożywczy, [Jun 22]), with May pay up 5.8% y/y, below expectations (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 22]). Factories carry a record 8.6 bn zł of arrears (Strefa Biznesu, [Jun 24]) even as defence orders flood in (Business Insider Polska, [Jun 27]). The old reading of Poland as a central bank leaning toward easing no longer fits this feed.

Markets: Monday was mixed on the WIG20, with Orlen and Żabka finishing higher (Inwestycje.pl, [Jun 29]) and Rzeczpospolita noting rising geopolitical tension did not favour buying [Jun 29]. INNPoland.pl attributes Orlen's gain to Slovak expansion, renewables and geopolitics [Jun 29], while KGHM is being hit by a plunge in a key metal (wnp.pl, [Jun 29]) and silver is falling alongside gold (Money.pl, [Jun 29]). Trigon DM moved to buy LPP and Pepco and sell Modivo (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jun 29]). FX: USD/PLN at 3.7641 (+1.5% 1W, +3.7% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.2855 (+0.8% 1W, +1.4% 4W); CEO Magazyn has the euro at 4.29 zł with the dollar losing and investors cautious on the zloty [Jun 29], while Business Insider Polska sees the dollar regaining strength and the zloty on the defensive [Jun 29]. Bonds: servicing costs are up almost 30% (Parkiet, [Jun 25]) and FXMAG raises the risk of Poland being cut off from EU funds over the state of its finances [Jun 29].

Fiscal & Politics: The windfall tax escalated again: a Senate amendment means the maximum levy could reach 100% of profit (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jun 29]), on top of the 74% safety valve reported last week (Wyborcza.biz, [Jun 26]) and the Sejm's 60% base (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 19]); Money.pl carries an economist urging Poland to change the tax, citing Hungary [Jun 29]. Rzeczpospolita says it is time to review state spending [Jun 29] after Bankier.pl flagged a pile-up of outlays on nuclear and CPK with an EU procedure in the background [Jun 27] and debt growth of 1.5 bn zł a day (wPolityce.pl, [Jun 26]). The promised pension recalculation is stuck in government (Fakt, [Jun 29]), and MF confirmed a three-euro customs charge on non-EU parcels from the start of July (Prawo.pl, [Jun 29]). The CHF law awaits the president's signature after the Senate passed it unamended (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 25]).

Corporate & Sectors: PKO BP shareholders approved a dividend of 6.14 zł per share from 2025 profit (Bankier.pl, [Jun 29]), a raise for the country's largest bank (Business Insider Polska, [Jun 29]); the sector context is tougher, with Polish banks earning 13.3 bn zł as tax and margin pressure cut earnings (CEO Magazyn, [Jun 29]), ZBP forecasting a near 23% drop in sector profit from the free-credit sanction (Bankier.pl, [Jun 26]) and Erste Bank Polska booking over 438 mln zł of CHF legal-risk losses (Bankier.pl, [Jun 29]). Orlen pre-paid the higher VAT before it took effect (TOK FM, [Jun 29]), launched HVO100 fuel in Slovakia (Money.pl, [Jun 29]) and, with Synthos, applied for the EU's first contract for difference for SMRs, seeking guarantees for 14 reactors (Bankier.pl, [Jun 29]). Nuclear negotiations are intensifying toward an EPC contract (Dziennik Bałtycki, [Jun 29]). Dino's owner lost the title of richest Pole as his fortune nearly halved (WP Finanse, [Jun 29]) and the state labour inspectorate took up the staff complaints (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Jun 29]). CD Projekt dated Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 for autumn with a trailer (Antyweb, [Jun 29]), and Blik's chief is moving to Allegro (pb.pl, [Jun 29]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland's spy chief said the country must operate as if war with Russia were imminent (TVP World, [Jun 29]) and warned Russia could use unmarked troops against the Baltic states (UNITED24 Media, [Jun 29]); the Dutch government assesses Russia could attack NATO a year after the Ukraine war ends (Kyiv Post, [Jun 29]). Saab won a Polish submarine order worth 4.3 billion dollars (Investing.com, [Jun 29]), adding to the defence-order wave.

New / Most Important: The windfall tax has gone from 60% to a theoretical 100% in ten days, in the same week the fuel VAT returns and power prices spike on the heat. That is a stagflationary policy mix for the state-controlled complex and for CPI, which is why Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK now says debt ties the NBP's hands: the market has stopped pricing a Polish easing cycle and started pricing a fiscal one. PKO's 6.14 zł dividend is the counterweight, proof that bank capital return survives the tax squeeze.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold with upside inflation risk from Wednesday; stay long PKO into the dividend, keep Orlen tactical only, and respect KGHM's metal-price drawdown rather than fading it.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Orlen shares rising on the day its tax ceiling rose to 100%; unemployment at multi-month lows while employment falls for a 32nd month; a 4.3 billion dollar submarine order against FXMAG's warning that EU funds could be cut off.
