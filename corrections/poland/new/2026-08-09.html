🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: NBP has been holding rates as inflation remains above target, and the week ahead decides the tone: Gazeta Prawna [Aug 9] previews the inflation and balance-of-payments data due after the weekend, and ING [Aug 7] lists Polish GDP and inflation on the CEE calendar. Parkiet [Aug 9] notes the easy money on deposits is over and real returns now depend on inflation. The internal debate is unresolved: Glapiński is reported to want lower rates against expert objections (Super Biznes, [Aug 3]), analysts weigh a September cut (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 6]), and the bank is also in an argument with the ECB, which is assessing the NBP's controversial plan to sell gold (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 6]) and the transfer of profit to defence (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 7]). Growth gives no cover for easing: analysts raised their GDP forecasts (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 3]), Interia Biznes [Aug 5] sees up to 4.1% possible, the PMI rebounded with the first rise in employment in months (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 3]) and Euronews [Aug 9] confirms Poland is now the EU's sixth-largest economy, ahead of Belgium and Austria. The one soft spot is agriculture, with drought cutting the grain harvest by close to 5% (PKB24.pl, [Aug 3]).

Markets: Friday's closes leave the index just above its breakout: WIG20 fell while mWIG40 set a record (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 7]) but held 4000 points (Bankier, [Aug 7]), after a mid-week dip of 0.29% on Wednesday (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 5]). The card shows the rotation of the past month: KGHM 352.95 (+14.6% 1W, +14.2% 4W) and PGE 10.75 (+6.3% 1W, +14.7% 4W) carry the week, consumer names carry the month with Allegro 46.21 (+16.0% 4W), Dino 32.44 (+15.0% 4W), LPP 22,220 (+14.7% 4W) and CD Projekt 256.1 (+9.9% 4W), while the financials that led the breakout have stalled, PKO 109.0 (-2.6% 1W, -3.4% 4W) and Pekao 252.9 (+3.0% 1W, +3.5% 4W), and Orlen 149.06 (+0.7% 1W, +6.5% 4W) gave back its results pop (Comparic, [Aug 7]). EPOL 44.19 is +3.2% 1W and +9.5% 4W. The zloty: USD/PLN 3.7297 (-0.2% 1W, -1.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.2961 (-0.2% 1W, -0.7% 4W); the euro has been fighting to break 4.30 (Bankier, [Aug 5]) and weak US labour data have weighed on the dollar (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 7]), so PLN benefits from broad EUR strength and EM carry appetite. POLGBs rebounded at the start of August (Bankier, [Aug 7]) and households bought nearly 10 bn zł of retail Treasury bonds in July (Bankier, [Aug 7]).

Fiscal & Politics: Rzeczpospolita [Aug 9] asks whether Poland has just crossed the constitutional debt limit, days after Konkret24 [Aug 7] answered the same question with "it depends"; public spending is 50.9% of GDP, above Germany, Sweden and Denmark (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 5]), the state costs over 53 thousand zł per Pole (Interia Biznes, [Aug 5]) and wGospodarce [Aug 8] says the budget's fate hangs on the EU SAFE loan. Against that, the election sausage is roasting: Gazeta Prawna [Aug 9] prices the higher tax-free allowance and second tax threshold at up to 70 bn zł, Bloomberg [Aug 4] reported income tax relief for millions being weighed, and Domański says the budget bill will come this month with the allowance work ongoing (Pit.pl, [Aug 7]) while the MF assembles a 2027 budget with the EU's largest deficit (pb.pl, [Aug 4]). Poland has also published draft legislation introducing a digital services tax (EY, [Aug 6]).

Corporate & Sectors: Housing is the weekend's fresh signal: home sales are accelerating and developers' supply has started to shrink (Bankier, [Aug 9]). Retail: LPP, with sales up 18.5%, plans as many as 750 new Sinsay stores this year (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 9]); DlaHandlu.pl [Aug 9] reports Dino, Biedronka and Lidl will have to disclose private-label producers. Banks: the CHF law is in force and borrowers may stop paying instalments once they sue (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 9]), borrowers are warned not to sign WIBOR-to-POLSTR annexes blind (Bizblog.pl, [Aug 9]), lenders may be heading for a wave of credit refusals (Zero.pl, [Aug 8]). Energy: compensation for curtailing renewables has exceeded 100 mn zł (Interia Biznes, [Aug 9]), heat and power-market dynamics are feeding food prices (Energetyka24, [Aug 9]), the nuclear contractor has taken over the site (Radio Bezpieczna Podróż, [Aug 9]) though Fakt [Aug 8] reports hard American negotiating and possible delay. Orlen moved on fuel prices for the first time in months (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 8]) after a 7.7 bn zł quarter (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 7]) and an indictment over 1.5 bn zł of damage (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 7]).

Geopolitics & Security: General Kraszewski says the Lublin-region explosion taught the Russians the state of Poland's civil defence systems (Polskie Radio Lublin, [Aug 9]), as the US intelligence assessment that Putin may test NATO with a limited attack on Poland or the Baltics keeps circulating (The Week, [Aug 8]); the foreign minister wants allies to reconsider intercepting Russian missiles over Ukraine (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Aug 6]) and talks on a permanent US base continue (NDTV, [Aug 5]).

New / Most Important: The constitutional debt question. When the national paper of record asks whether the constitutional ceiling has been breached in the same week the government prices 70 bn zł of pre-election tax cuts and admits the budget rests on an EU defence loan, the fiscal premium stops being an abstraction. It arrives before a GDP and CPI week and with the NBP fighting the ECB over gold and profit transfers; POLGBs have rebounded, which is exactly where a reassessment would show first.

Playbook ✓: Rates held and PLN steady; the data week is the next catalyst.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: None today — PLN benefits from broad EUR strength and EM carry appetite, but no catalyst for repositioning until the debt-limit question is answered by the budget bill rather than by commentators.
