🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: This week's data closed the cut debate for now. GUS confirmed July CPI at 3.0% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]), with fuel up 13.9% in a month (300Gospodarka.pl, [Aug 13]) and PAP Biznes attributing the acceleration mainly to fuel [Aug 13]; core inflation then rose to its highest in ten months (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]), which CEO Magazyn reads as rates staying unchanged for longer [Aug 14]. Growth gave the hawks cover: the Q2 flash showed GDP up 3.8% y/y and 0.9% q/q (PAP Biznes, [Aug 13]), more than forecast per Bloomberg [Aug 13], driven by investment, KPO money and credit while consumption sagged (pb.pl, wGospodarce, [Aug 13]); only Lithuania grew faster in the EU (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 13]). The soft spots are wages, where xyz.pl says the boom is running out of steam [Aug 13], and the external side, where ING flags a record primary income deficit in the current account [Aug 13]. mBank's call of no rate change to year-end (Bankier.pl, [Aug 11]) now looks like the base case.

Markets: The record run paused. Wednesday brought further gains with XTB among the stars (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 12]) and a reshuffle by Norway's oil fund, which swapped Żabka for Allegro and bought JSW (Parkiet, FXMAG, [Aug 12]); Thursday saw the whole WIG20 in the red (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]), Parkiet asked whether the bull market is catching its breath [Aug 13], and Orlen was sold after the government's fuel decision (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 13]). Banks led the rebound on Friday (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 14]). The card is consolidation rather than reversal: EPOL 44.45 (+0.6% 1W, +10.2% 4W); Pekao 259.2 (+2.5% 1W, +13.2% 4W) and PKO 111.08 (+1.9% 1W) lead on results, CD Projekt 263.1 (+2.7% 1W) held up despite layoffs, while KGHM 352.5 (-0.1% 1W, +20.0% 4W), Dino 32.41 (-0.1% 1W) and Allegro 46.46 (+0.6% 1W) stalled and LPP 21,820 (-1.8% 1W) slipped. The zloty is drifting weaker: USD/PLN 3.7353 (+0.1% 1W, -1.1% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3084 (+0.3% 1W, -0.5% 4W), with FXMAG targeting 4.31 or higher on EUR/PLN [Aug 12]. POLGBs: the finance ministry sold 10.72 billion zł of bonds at a switch tender (TradingView, [Aug 12]) and NBP is buying gold faster than China (FXMAG, [Aug 12]); the backdrop is record US yields (Parkiet, [Aug 13]) and Germany paying the most for debt since the euro crisis (Money.pl, [Aug 15]).

Fiscal & Politics: The policy response to the fuel-driven CPI was fiscal, not monetary. Tusk announced the return of the CPN programme with a lower VAT rate on fuel for two weeks from Monday (Do Rzeczy, Gov.pl, [Aug 13]; [Aug 14]), promising fuel cheaper by 1 zł (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 13]) with Pb95 capped at 6.41 zł (e-magazyny.pl, [Aug 14]). The tax plan is firming: a 60 thousand zł tax-free amount would cost over 60 billion zł (INFOR.PL, [Aug 14]) and a new middle-class threshold is being designed (Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 14]), but seniors will not get a 1600 plus benefit (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 15]). The Fiscal Council is scrutinising the 2027 budget with no time left (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 12]), pb.pl says the debt snowball is rolling faster while the government denies it [Aug 12] and Money.pl counts a record 229 days worked for the state [Aug 13]. Nawrocki signed the OKI law (PAP, [Aug 13]) and the nuclear special-act amendment that may bring the plant forward by two years (GlobEnergia, [Aug 14]).

Corporate & Sectors: PKO BP earned 2.77 billion zł in Q2 and 5.29 billion zł in the first half (StockWatch.pl, Parkiet, [Aug 13]), despite much higher sector taxation (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 13]), with the CEO seeing grounds for 2026 to be the strongest year in its history (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]); CHF24 counts 7666 free-credit lawsuits against no reserve [Aug 14] and Franknews.pl reports settlements collapsing at mBank as repaid CHF borrowers sue [Aug 15]. CD Projekt cut staff on the Witcher multiplayer project Sirius (IGN Polska, [Aug 12]) and analysts doubt it will reach the 2 billion zł incentive-plan target by end-2026 (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 12]). Orlen started a 42.2 MW farm at Mažeikiai (Elektromobilni.pl, [Aug 12]), but PiS demands explanations for profits made under CPN (RadioMaryja.pl, [Aug 14]). Allegro keeps buying back shares (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 12]); Dino hit a one-month high (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 12]) with results due next week (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 13]); Modivo revenue fell to 806 million zł (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 10]). Power prices exceeded 100 euro/MWh in 21 European countries (Gramwzielone.pl, [Aug 13]), Romania shut its entire nuclear fleet on the low Danube (Energetyka24, [Aug 13]) and a cyberattack hit Polish renewables (Gramwzielone.pl, [Aug 14]).

Geopolitics & Security: On Armed Forces Day Tusk set out four pillars of security and pledged Europe's largest army (wnp.pl, The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 15]), warning against treason and stupidity weakening the country (TVN24, [Aug 15]), while Nawrocki countered with God, honour and fatherland, not SAFE (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 15]); the army targets half a million soldiers and reservists (Forsal.pl, [Aug 14]). Poland foiled a Russian plot to kill a Ukrainian-American in Warsaw (The Guardian, [Aug 13]), NATO condemned Russian airspace violations over Poland and Romania (Euronews.com, [Aug 12]), Bloomberg reports Russia eyeing sabotage in Europe [Aug 12] and NATO plans thousands of AI drones along the Russian and Belarusian borders (UNITED24 Media, [Aug 14]).

New / Most Important: The combination of a 3.8% GDP beat, 3.0% headline and core at a ten-month high removes the September cut from the table and reframes the NBP as on hold into the autumn. The government's answer to fuel-driven inflation is a VAT cut and price caps, which flatters the next CPI print but moves the cost onto Orlen and the deficit at the moment the Fiscal Council says time has run out on the 2027 budget. For equities this swaps an energy leader for a bank leader: PKO's record half and Pekao's momentum now carry the index.  |  Playbook ✓: Holding pattern consistent with sticky CEE inflation; overweight banks on rates-for-longer and results, underweight Orlen into CPN, keep duration short while global yields reset higher.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Tusk promised Europe's largest army on the day Nawrocki rejected SAFE, the very loan the budget hangs on: defence ambition and its funding are now on opposite sides of the presidential veto.
