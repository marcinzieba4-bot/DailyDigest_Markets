🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold / November hike risk

Macro Stance: The inflation picture turned uglier this week. GUS's final September print came in at 4.1% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Oct 14]), above the 4% consensus (BIZNES24, [Oct 14]) and the highest in 17 months (Agro Profil, [Oct 14]); transport prices rose 17.1% (Newsmax Polska, [Oct 14]) and ING calculated that more than half of the annual rate is fuel and energy [Oct 14]. The RPP left rates unchanged at its October meeting, with Glapiński saying the Council "is ready to act" (wnp.pl, [Oct 8]) and Bloomberg reporting he is ready to raise rates if inflation risks mount [Oct 8], though he did not announce a November hike (Gazeta Prawna, [Oct 8]). ING sees hike odds rising but no rush [Oct 8], with hikes pencilled for 2027 (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 8]). Today's relief valve: NBP's September core inflation fell (Business Insider Polska, [Oct 15]), and Credit Agricole expects the fuel intervention to pull October CPI temporarily down to 3.3% (Portal Samorządowy, [Oct 14]). Eurostat put August industrial output up 1.8% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 15]), Coface sees 3.6% growth in 2026 (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 8]) and GUS detail showed the average wage at 9651 zł gross (Super Biznes, [Oct 13]).

Markets: The WIG set a historic record on Monday (Business Insider Polska, [Oct 13]), but the US inflation print cut the rally short: Wednesday was a correction after three up sessions, the WIG20 down 0.40% (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 14]), KGHM off 3.7% (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 14]) and Auto Partner down 11% after a shareholder sold 4 million shares (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 14]). Thursday closed flat, the WIG20 up 0.01% (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 15]); KGHM lost another 3.93% (FXMAG, [Oct 15]), breaking an 11-month trendline on copper-surplus forecasts (Comparic.pl, [Oct 15]), while Dino, Pepco and the banks rose (Bankier.pl, [Oct 15]). The card shows the rotation: PGE at 17.28 is up 12.3% on the week and 25.0% over four weeks, Dino +9.1% 1W, Allegro +5.7% 1W and +18.3% 4W, LPP +19.5% 4W, CD Projekt +11.8% 4W, whereas PKO (115.18, -3.1% 1W) and Pekao (261.6, -3.3% 1W) still carry last week's bank sell-off on delayed hikes (Bankier.pl, [Oct 8]). EPOL at 44.01 is +0.9% 1W. The zloty stabilised: USD/PLN closed at 3.9038 (-0.1% 1W, +2.8% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3677 (-0.2% 1W, +0.2% 4W), with the dollar back below 3.90 zł today (Comparic.pl, [Oct 15]) after its strongest level since April 2025 last Thursday (FXMAG, [Oct 8]). MPC member Litwiniuk called the zloty still "strong" after the slide (Bloomberg, [Oct 14]); Commerzbank sees it pressured by policy and fiscal risks (FXStreet, [Oct 13]) and ING calls it exposed as NBP delays tightening (FXStreet, [Oct 15]). POLGBs: CEO Magazyn counted a third month of bond losses while stocks rose on OKI inflows [Oct 13], yet the MF sold 12.0 bn zł of bonds against 20.2 bn zł of demand (TradingView, [Oct 14]).

Fiscal & Politics: The MF reported the state budget deficit at about 172.2 bn zł after September (PAP Biznes, [Oct 15]), up more than 21 bn zł in a month (wGospodarce, [Oct 15]), though Rzeczpospolita stressed it is smaller than a year ago with CIT driving revenue [Oct 15]. Fitch revised its deficit and debt forecasts for Poland higher (Forsal.pl, [Oct 12]), the Fiscal Council warned of a snowball effect (Interia Biznes, [Oct 14]), and INFOR.PL noted 115 bn zł of debt servicing in 2026 [Oct 12]. The Sejm began work on the 2027 budget with the opposition warning of a debt avalanche (Business Insider Polska, [Oct 8]). Property-tax maximum rates rise in 2027 (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 14]). Bloomberg framed political dysfunction as a threat to the economy [Oct 14], and Poland joined 17 states protesting a 141 bn euro cut in the EU budget (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 15]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: PKO raised CHF-loan provisions (Strefa Biznesu, [Oct 14]), UOKiK fined four banks close to 150 mln zł over mortgages (PropertyNews.pl, [Oct 12]), POLSTR replaces WIBOR from 2027 (300Gospodarka.pl, [Oct 12]), and lenders are seeking state risk-sharing to fund the defence build-up (marketscreener, [Oct 15]). Energy: PGE gained more than 25% in a month ahead of a strategy update (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 14]), signed a data-centre deal with NASK (Bankier.pl, [Oct 15]) and, with the Energy Ministry and PEJ, an agreement on the second nuclear plant (CIRE.pl, [Oct 13]); spot power hit its highest since the start of the year (Zielona gospodarka, [Oct 14]). Orlen faces a Czech refinery tax (Money.pl, [Oct 14]) but will take four crude cargoes in October (Money.pl, [Oct 15]); the minister said fuel would be 1.30-1.40 zł dearer without the CPN package (Interia Biznes, [Oct 15]). Retail: Allegro opened an export corridor to Ukraine with over a million offers (pb.pl, [Oct 14]) as Temu's share slid from 36 to 6% (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Oct 15]); Dino unions threaten a pre-Christmas strike (TOK FM, [Oct 14]). Housing: mortgage applicants rose 10.1% m/m in September (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Oct 12]) with prices flat at 16 229 zł per sqm (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 12]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk told the Warsaw Security Forum the Russian threat is "real" (Breaking Defense, [Oct 12]) and NATO detailed a plan to shield Ukraine's European allies (The New York Times, [Oct 12]). Police are probing an alleged plot against Tusk and the defence minister (Al Jazeera, [Oct 11]). Poland blamed Starlink sabotage on Russia (The Tribune-Democrat, [Oct 13]) and grilled Musk over new restrictions in Ukraine (Bloomberg, [Oct 14]). Patriot deliveries will be delayed (Reuters, [Oct 12]), Sweden will deploy Patriots to defend the Ukraine aid hub in Poland (The Defense Post, [Oct 12]), and Warsaw wants frozen Russian assets back on the agenda for Kyiv (Bloomberg, [Oct 15]).

New / Most Important: The 4.1% final CPI is the pivot: the RPP's hold is now a bet that the fuel shock does not spill over, and today's falling core print is the first evidence for that bet. Markets are already split: banks sold on delayed hikes, PGE and retail ran, bonds lost for a third month while auction demand stayed deep.  |  Playbook ✓: Stay with domestic retail and energy winners, keep banks tactical until the November meeting, and treat strong auction cover as a reason not to chase POLGB weakness.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: WIG at a record versus a 172 bn zł deficit and a third month of bond losses; the zloty firmer on the week despite the hottest CPI in 17 months.
