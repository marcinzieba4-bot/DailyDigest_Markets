🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hawkish minutes

Macro Stance: The RPP is on hold, but the minutes released today show which members wanted a hike (Tysol.pl, [Aug 22]), the first documented push for tightening this cycle. PKO BP's economists counter that labour data will not sway the Council (wnp.pl, [Aug 20]), yet the data are strong: July enterprise wages rose 6.8% y/y with employment down 0.8% (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 20]), the average wage hit 9509 zł (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 20]), industrial output grew 5.1% y/y (PAP Biznes, [Aug 20]); construction stayed below the line (Parkiet, [Aug 20]) and Q3 forecasts point to a slowdown (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 20]). On prices, PPI reaccelerated to 2.8% in July (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 20]); HICP was 3.1% (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 19]). Citi expects fuel measures extended at least through September (PAP Biznes, [Aug 17]), yet maximum fuel prices rise from Saturday (Portal Spożywczy, [Aug 21]) and refineries pushed another wave of wholesale increases (Super Biznes, [Aug 22]).

Markets: The week closed with gains and Dino as the star (Parkiet, [Aug 21]): up 5.5% with the WIG20 up 1% mid-session (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]), more than 8% at the close (CrypS.pl, [Aug 21]), on close to 1.5 billion zł of turnover in Dino and KGHM (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]). That reversed a three-day slide in which Parkiet saw indices drifting from bull-market peaks [Aug 19]. The card confirms the rotation: Dino 34.78 (+7.3% 1W, +20.8% 4W), PKO 113.96 (+2.6% 1W) and Pekao 258.8 (+10.7% 4W) carry the index, while KGHM 342.35 (-2.9% 1W, +12.8% 4W), PGE 10.19 (-5.6% 1W), LPP 20,800 (-4.7% 1W), Allegro 45.1 (-2.9% 1W) and CD Projekt 258.7 (-1.7% 1W, +14.4% 4W) lag; EPOL 44.42 is flat on the week (+8.6% 4W). The structural headline is S&P Dow Jones Indices upgrading Poland to developed-market status (Reuters, [Aug 21]). The zloty is at a two-month high against the dollar, with Comparic.pl asking whether another 5 groszy comes off [Aug 22]; the dollar fell to 3.68 (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 21]), and the card has USD/PLN 3.6918 (-1.2% 1W, -3.0% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.3146 (+0.1% 1W, -0.4% 4W). POLGBs have only a global catalyst: Parkiet calls it an SOS from the US bond market [Aug 22], the US 30-year is at 5.27% (BeInCrypto, [Aug 17]), US debt passed 40 trillion dollars (Bankier.pl, [Aug 20]).

Fiscal & Politics: Fitch affirmed Poland at A- with a negative outlook (PAP, [Aug 22]; Gov.pl, [Aug 21]). Domański called it very good news (PolsatNews.pl, [Aug 22]), but the agency attached a serious warning on deficit and rising debt (Portal Samorządowy, CEO Magazyn, [Aug 22]) and flagged pre-election risks (Parkiet, [Aug 22]). The draft is public: lower taxes for citizens, higher for giants and lump-sum payers (Bankier.pl, [Aug 22]), a rate rising from 19 to 22% from 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 21]), CIT at 30% for the largest energy firms (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 20]) under which Orlen, PGE, Enea and Tauron add close to 7 billion zł (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 21]), and a new levy funding 4.8 billion zł of power-price compensation for energy-intensive industry (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 21]). The veto risk is explicit: Gazeta Prawna reports Nawrocki may veto the package [Aug 21]. Tusk called an extra cabinet meeting on the budget (Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 21]) with the deficit past 141 billion zł after July (Bankier.pl, [Aug 17]).

Corporate & Sectors: KGHM followed its record half, nearly 5.6 billion zł of profit (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 21]) and EBITDA up 89% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 21]), with expansion talk: WNP.PL reports it is quietly going after a second mine in Chile [Aug 22] and is eyeing a US listing (wnp.pl, [Aug 21]). Dino's Q2 was in line (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 21]): 400 million zł net and 3176 stores (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 21]), profitability slightly lower (Parkiet, [Aug 21]) and only a slow LFL recovery expected in H2 (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 21]); pb.pl's verdict was that the absence of disappointment was enough [Aug 21]. StockWatch.pl worked through what a 30% CIT means for Orlen dividends [Aug 21]; PGE and Ørsted finished all 111 Baltica 2 monopiles on schedule (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 21]). CD Projekt is rising into its Gamescom showcase (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 21]) after the Edgerunners 2 date and trailer (CD PROJEKT RED IR, [Aug 21]). Banks: PKO BP got KNF approval for portfolio management (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]), but Franknews says over 68 thousand repaid CHF contracts could be challenged [Aug 21] and BNP Paribas admits a 73.6% loss rate in CHF suits [Aug 22]; Poland has the third most expensive mortgages in the EU (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Aug 22]) even with margins near multi-year lows (Bankier.pl, [Aug 20]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tarcza Wschód reached initial operational readiness in many sections after two years (Strefa Obrony, [Aug 21]), with Bundeswehr support (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 22]) and MON saying the border can be mined within 48 hours (Interia, [Aug 21]). The US deployed a spy plane to Poland (Firstpost, [Aug 21]), NATO ran unannounced exercises near Kaliningrad (UNN, [Aug 21]), Romania says Russian strikes increasingly spill into NATO (ABC News, [Aug 21]). Defence spending is to reach 5% of GDP by 2027 (TodayPress.tv, [Aug 21]).

New / Most Important: Two verdicts on Poland landed within a day: S&P Dow Jones promoted the market to developed status while Fitch kept the A- rating on a negative outlook with a warning on deficit and debt. The equity story is institutionally accepted but the sovereign is on probation, with the pre-election budget and a vetoable tax package as the test; minutes showing hike advocates on the RPP tilt the rate path up, not down.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold with a hawkish bias; banks and Dino are the earnings-backed longs, zloty strength is a dollar story to ride rather than chase, and POLGB duration stays light while US yields set the tone.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Dino jumped more than 8% on results Parkiet calls weak with falling profitability, and the zloty sits at two-month highs under a negative rating outlook: positioning, not fundamentals, is driving both.
