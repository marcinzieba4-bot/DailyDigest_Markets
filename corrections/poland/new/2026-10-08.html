🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, ready to act

Macro Stance: A day after the RPP kept the reference rate at 3.75% (MyBank [Oct 7]), Governor Glapiński used his press conference to say the Council "is ready to act" and would raise rates if inflation risks mount (Bloomberg, Business Insider Polska [Oct 8]), while stopping short of signalling a November move (Gazeta Prawna [Oct 8]). His core argument is that fuel prices are not feeding core inflation, so no hike is needed for now (Newsmax Polska [Oct 8]), and that the November projection will be decisive (Portal spożywczy). He added the NBP will not bow to any pressure (Interia [Oct 8]). The sell side is split: ING now forecasts hikes in 2027 with inflation able to exceed 3.5% (CEO Magazyn [Oct 8]), and 300Gospodarka notes banks differ widely on the path. The data behind this are a September flash CPI of 4.0% y/y and 0.7% m/m with fuel up 36.1% (PAP Biznes [Sep 30]), a 2025 GDP revision to 3.8% from 3.6% (wnp.pl [Oct 6]), an April median wage of 7695 zł against a 9651 zł average (Podatki.biz [Oct 7]), and consumer sentiment at a one-year low (CEO Magazyn [Oct 7]).

Markets: The post-record hangover deepened. Main indices closed slightly lower today with Orlen and Dino offsetting bank losses (Portal spożywczy [Oct 8]); WIG-Banki fell 2.41% (FXMAG [Oct 8]), PKO is dragging WIG20 down (Comparic) and CrypS describes the GPW as still bleeding. The zloty is the bigger problem: USD/PLN closed 3.9072, up 1.5% on the week and 5.4% over four weeks, the dollar's strongest this year and the highest since April 2025 (Bankier, FXMAG [Oct 8]); Comparic puts the euro at 4.38 and the dollar at 3.92 intraday and says the zloty is losing for three reasons at once, while CEO Magazyn ties the move to growing rate-hike bets. EUR/PLN closed 4.3765, up 0.3% on the week and 1.5% over four weeks. Rzeczpospolita [Oct 7] reported a continuing POLGB sell-off alongside the currency, against a global backdrop of 30-year US yields at 24-year highs (Investing.com [Oct 7]). EPOL closed 43.6, down 1.6% on the week and 4.3% over four weeks. PKO 118.88 (down 4.1% on the week) and Pekao 270.6 (down 3.7%) carry the bank damage; Orlen 150.22 is up 2.8% on the week; over four weeks PGE 15.39 is up 13.3%, LPP 24800 up 15.5%, Allegro 50.54 up 12.3% and CD Projekt 256.0 up 10.2%, while KGHM 334.6 is down 11.5%; Dino 33.38 is down 4.8% on the week.

Fiscal & Politics: The Sejm rejected the PiS motion to throw out the 2027 budget and sent the draft to committee (Interia, Money.pl [Oct 8]): nearly 1 trillion zł of spending and a 281.2bn zł deficit (Bankier [Oct 8]), which XTB notes is the fourth consecutive year of deficits above 200bn. Domański argued the budget funds long-term growth (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK) and said that had he listened to the Fiscal Council, the deficit would be larger (Bizblog), while the ministry is reported to fear a presidential veto (Biznes Wprost [Oct 8]). The Finance Ministry also sent a PPK review to consultation with more frequent auto-enrolment (CEO Magazyn [Oct 8]), confirmed KSeF will stay penalty-free for another year (Pit.pl) and is targeting family foundations that have shown 33bn zł of tax-exempt income (Wyborcza [Oct 8]). On fuel, maximum prices rise again for Friday after a cut for Thursday (Rzeczpospolita [Oct 8]), and the government is readying 4.8bn zł of energy-cost support for companies (PulsHR [Oct 8]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen's former deputy CEO and three directors were placed in pre-trial detention over pre-election fuel pricing (TVN24 [Oct 8]), yet the stock rose; Orlen Neptun filed environmental reports for Baltic West (BIZNES24) and the group keeps flagging higher 2027 gas bills (GlobEnergia). CD Projekt got a new catalyst: Paramount is developing a live-action Cyberpunk 2077 film with the studio involved (Bankier, Filmweb [Oct 8]), on top of The Witcher 3's Steam record. Dino led gainers (Inwestycje.pl [Oct 8]) even as unions set a strike date just before the holidays (Portal spożywczy). Banks have a growing legal overhang: a Suwałki court awarded 188 thousand zł against PKO Bank Hipoteczny in a WIBOR case (CHF24 [Oct 8]), mBank and VeloBank are dropping WIBOR for new mortgages (Bezprawnik) and Millennium is booking 154m zł of FX-loan provisions for Q3 (StockWatch [Oct 8]). PEJ and Westinghouse began ordering components for the first reactor (Dziennik Bałtycki [Oct 8]). Warsaw flat prices rose 6.3% in a quarter while Łódź fell 5% (CEO Magazyn [Oct 8]).

Geopolitics & Security: Sweden is sending a Patriot unit and 100 troops to guard the Ukraine aid gateway in Poland (Kyiv Post [Oct 7]), and an investigation found Russian drones that entered Poland carried Polish SIM cards (UNITED24 [Oct 8]). Russia is moving troops to the border per Forsal [Oct 8], RCB alerts put the military on highest readiness (Fakt), and Russia ordered an intelligence surge for NATO sabotage (Kyiv Post [Oct 3]). A UN study confirms Ukrainian refugees lifted 2025 GDP by 2.4% (Notes From Poland [Oct 8]).

New / Most Important: Glapiński's "ready to act" is the week's pivot. The market had treated the hold as dovish; the conference re-opened the hike tail without committing to it, and the zloty's slide through 3.90 shows investors are not sure the NBP will defend the currency with rates before the November projection. With the 2027 budget now in committee at a 281.2bn zł deficit, fiscal and monetary signals are pulling in opposite directions.

Playbook ✓: Keep POLGB duration light and hedge USD/PLN rather than EUR/PLN, where the 0.3% weekly move is manageable; fade the bank sell-off only after the WIBOR litigation path clears; hold Orlen and CD Projekt on company-specific catalysts.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The old script of CEE central banks on hold still fits, but Glapiński has moved from "on hold" to "conditional hawk" while the government cuts fuel prices by decree; if the November projection shows core inflation holding, the hike bets will unwind and the zloty could recover sharply from 3.90.
