🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: Going into the last week of July the Council's public line is hawkish and the data give it cover. Kotecki says the situation is starting to differ from the NBP projection and a September cut cannot be declared yet (Bankier.pl, pb.pl, [Jul 23]); a Council hawk told Gazeta Prawna the earlier cuts were premature ([Jul 21]) and another member called hikes more probable than cuts (Super Biznes, [Jul 21]). Against that stand Crédit Agricole's 25 bp September cut to 3.50% (PAP Biznes, [Jul 20]) and Money.pl's economists who had "no doubts" about a post-holiday cut ([Jul 20]). The inflation worry is energy: CEO Magazyn says the oil shock may halt cuts ([Jul 23]), FXMAG's scenario has diesel at 9 or 10 zloty and inflation around 10% in a long Middle East war ([Jul 24]). Growth is not the problem: Rzeczpospolita reads the complete data set as solid ([Jul 24]), retail sales rose 6.2% y/y against 5.2% expected (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 22]), industrial production 7.6% (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 20]), ING BSK sees Q2 GDP near 3.8% y/y (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 22]). The labour market is cooling at the edges: wage growth is the slowest since 2021 (FOCUS ON Business, [Jul 23]), vacancies fall as GDP rises (pb.pl, [Jul 25]) and INFOR-kadry says employers are back on top, so threatening to quit no longer wins a raise ([Jul 26]). Poland is caught between proximity to the conflict zone driving energy costs higher and a still-resilient domestic economy supported by EU structural funds.

Markets: The record is still one step away. The WIG20 was held at the 3900 barrier (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 23]); Orlen set records all week (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 23]) and the energy names carried the index while Żabka, Dino and banks lost heavily (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 23]). Friday's session had KGHM down 3.97% and Orlen up 2.62% (FXMAG, [Jul 24]), Pepco in the lead and PGE closing the week under pressure (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 24]). On the card, Orlen at 156.0 is up 6.7% on the week and 26.4% over four weeks, PGE at 10.48 is up 10.5% and 9.2%, Allegro at 44.74 is up 20.3% in four weeks and LPP at 20140 up 10.0%; KGHM at 303.5 is still 7.4% below four weeks ago, Dino at 28.78 and CD Projekt at 226.2 are down 1.3% and 2.6% on the week, PKO at 106.58 is flat and Pekao at 233.7 gained 2.1%. EPOL closed at 40.92, up 1.4% on the week and 6.1% over four weeks. Debt is where the stress sits: wGospodarce reports yields at new peaks ([Jul 25]) after Friday's sharp rise (Bankier.pl, [Jul 24]), with core European yields at historic highs ([Jul 24]). The zloty is holding: FXMAG asks whether its resilience is returning as it holds firm near EUR/PLN 4.33 ([Jul 24]); the card has EUR/PLN at 4.3303 (+0.1% 1W, +1.2% 4W) and USD/PLN at 3.8051 (+0.7% / +1.0%).

Fiscal & Politics: Sunday's reading is about trajectory, not levels. Piotr Arak expects public debt above 70% of GDP by the end of 2027 (Filary Biznesu, [Jul 26]), wnp.pl says Polish debt is rising at its fastest in years and the EU could impose severe sanctions ([Jul 26]) and, in a second piece, that the government is borrowing "like mad" and Poles will be forced into a painful repayment ([Jul 26]). The starting points are Eurostat's 61.6% of GDP (XYZ, [Jul 21]) and State Treasury debt above 2.1 trillion zloty (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 24]). The policy flow of the past week cuts both ways: the Sejm passed a savings tax from 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 25]), the president sent the windfall tax to the Constitutional Tribunal at a cost of 4 billion zloty (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 24]) with Tusk telling voters to remember it at the pumps (TVN24, [Jul 25]), and the ministry keeps 60 000 zloty tax-free-allowance variants alive (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 24]). Wyborcza.biz prices a wealth tax at 13 billion zloty extra for the budget ([Jul 26]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen launched a Baltic Energy Initiative, with regional firms to lobby jointly for their energy interests in the EU (GospodarkaMorska.pl, [Jul 26]), and formalised its alliance with the State Fire Service (Biznes Alert, [Jul 26]) after warnings that Russia could attack the company (wnp.pl, [Jul 24]). For banks, CHF24 says the free-credit sanction has finally started to bite and the government is preparing a change the banks have been waiting for ([Jul 26]), while Franknews.pl expects those disadvantaged by the CHF law to head to court en masse ([Jul 26]) ahead of the statutory instalment freeze (Franknews.pl, [Jul 25]). The Solorz group is borrowing hundreds of millions of zloty for wind farms (wirtualnemedia.pl, [Jul 26]) and ZE PAK has up to 340 million zloty from UniCredit (Bankier.pl, [Jul 25]). Pellet prices have passed 2 thousand zloty a tonne and experts ask what the energy ministry will do (Gramwzielone.pl, [Jul 26]).

Geopolitics & Security: Fakt places Poland at a key point on Europe's strategic map ([Jul 26]). Belarus is sending "lazy workers" into the army under a strange new law (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 26]) after moving special forces to the Ukrainian border (Forsal.pl, [Jul 23]). The MiG-29 queue (Forsal.pl, [Jul 24]) and the Patriot factory bid (Tech Times, [Jul 24]) are the defence-industrial threads to watch.

New / Most Important: Arak's 70% is the number to carry into the week. It converts a Eurostat statistic into a path, and the path runs through a savings tax, a blocked windfall tax and a promised allowance, which is to say through politics rather than consolidation. Yields at new peaks are the market pricing that path; a zloty holding 4.33 says FX is still pricing the growth.  |  Playbook ✓: The hold stance is consistent with elevated CPI and geopolitical uncertainty; stay long the energy leaders the card rewards, underweight long POLGBs.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The rising oil/energy complex worsens Poland's inflation outlook and pushes any easing further out, yet the WIG20 sits a step from a record and the zloty is resilient; if the debt path is as Arak describes, one of currency or bonds is wrong, and bonds have moved first.
