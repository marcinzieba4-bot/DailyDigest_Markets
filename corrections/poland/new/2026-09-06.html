🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: Bankier.pl's weekend preview asks what the RPP and the ECB will do with rates as central bankers return from holiday [Sep 6], and the answer on the Polish side was given on Friday: an RPP member said there will be no hike in 2026 but a cut is not to be counted on (FXMAG, [Sep 4]). The inflation side keeps getting worse at the margin. The August flash was 3.4% y/y against 3.1% expected (BIZNES24, [Aug 31]), fuel was up 24.2% y/y (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]), and wPolityce argues the September jump in fuel prices will push inflation above 4% [Sep 2], consistent with mBank's year-end view of slightly over 4% (netTG.pl, [Aug 31]). Growth is the offset: Q2 GDP at 3.9% y/y with investment up 8.4% (PAP Biznes and CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]), and July retail sales up 3.6% y/y against 1% in the EU (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 4]). Labour costs are set to rise again: the 2027 minimum wage goes to 4950 zł a month and 32.3 zł an hour (FXMAG, [Sep 5]) while the GUS median wage stands at 7530 zł gross (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Sep 4]). The ECB is expected to raise rates this week (WSJ, [Sep 4]); the European hiking cycle puts upward pressure on Polish rates even without NBP action, and the carry differential matters.

Markets: The week ended with the WIG20 at a historic peak (Parkiet, [Sep 4]), 4090 points per Rankia Polska [Sep 4], and Strefa Biznesu names the engines: Orlen, PGE, Tauron, Enea and KGHM are driving Warsaw [Sep 5]. The card confirms a utilities-and-banks market: PGE at 12.55 (+15.2% 1W, +16.7% 4W), Orlen at 158.0 (+6.1% 1W, +6.0% 4W), PKO BP at 116.9 (+3.0% 1W, +7.2% 4W), Pekao at 263.9 (+3.3% 1W, +4.3% 4W), EPOL at 45.12 (+3.8% 1W, +2.1% 4W) and Dino at 35.98 (+10.9% 4W). The laggards are KGHM at 356.0 (-1.7% 1W, +0.9% 4W), even as Money.pl crowns it a world leader in production [Sep 6], and CD Projekt at 232.0 (-1.9% 1W, -9.4% 4W), which gave back Thursday's gain on Friday (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 4]). The zloty firmed into the weekend after Waller weakened the dollar (Bankier.pl, [Sep 4]); the card has USD/PLN at 3.715 (-0.4% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.32 (-0.2% 1W, +0.6% 4W). POLGBs are the weekend's worry: Subiektywnie o finansach asks whether the 840 bn zł of government bonds on bank balance sheets is a problem [Sep 5], and Bankier.pl relays Wall Street's alarm that 5% yields are a signal to cut risk [Sep 5], a week after Polish 10-year yields went back above 6% (Interia Biznes, [Aug 31]).

Fiscal & Politics: xyz.pl's English summary captures the shift in tone: a 7% deficit is the new normal, and the government is not tightening its belt [Sep 6]; a Money.pl interview argues Poland has built itself an internal tax haven and the budget shows it [Sep 6]. The 2027 draft carries a 282.6 bn zł deficit and 977.6 bn zł of spending (Inforlex, [Aug 31]). Rzeczpospolita's warning is not a second Greece but a second Romania [Sep 4]; Senator Bierecki says Poland is indebting itself fastest in the EU (wPolityce.pl, [Sep 2]); wGospodarce says the rating is under threat [Sep 2]. Bochniarz warns that presidential vetoes are blocking billions and the government will eventually reach for tax rises (Biznes Wprost, [Sep 4]). Local governments will receive 206.7 bn zł from PIT in 2027 (XYZ, [Sep 5]), almost 13 bn zł more (pb.pl, [Sep 5]); CPK's funding this year is cut by billions (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 4]). The president's veto of the crypto bill stands and the law has failed a third time (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 6]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks hold the sovereign risk and the equity gains at once: the 840 bn zł bond pile (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Sep 5]) sits behind PKO BP and Pekao's weekly gains. The POLSTR transition has started with PKO BP and Gazeta Prawna warns borrowers to be careful with the annex [Sep 6]. Energy is the sector in favour and the household's problem at once: electricity bills could rise by as much as 200 zł (PCH24.pl, [Sep 6]), power and gas tariffs rise from January (Warszawa w Pigułce, [Sep 6]), pellet is up 30% before the season (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 6]) and fuel prices are up sharply with the energy minister commenting (BiznesINFO.pl, [Sep 6]). CD Projekt disclosed that part of its first-half licensing revenue came from unannounced projects (PAP Biznes, [Sep 4]) and Press.pl notes the Witcher and Cyberpunk franchises still earn well [Sep 6]. Allegro is YouTube's first shopping partner in Poland (GSMonline, [Sep 5]); Dino is opening three stores at once (Fakty Kaliskie, [Sep 6]).

Geopolitics & Security: NATO's official line is that there is no imminent threat of attack (Reuters, [Aug 30]) but it warns the coming autumn and winter could be decisive for Ukraine and Europe (London Business News, [Sep 1]). Polish jets intercepted a Russian spy plane 30 km from the coast (TVP World, [Aug 31]); US cargo jets landed at Poland's NATO hub near Ukraine (The Times of India, [Sep 6]).

New / Most Important: The sovereign-bank loop is the week's structural story. Banks carry 840 bn zł of government paper into a 7% deficit that commentators now call the new normal, while their shares lead the WIG20 to a record on the strength of rates staying high. That works until the bond market decides the deficit is the problem; this week it decided on Wall Street's 5% rather than Warsaw's 6%.  |  Playbook ✓: ECB hikes pressure Polish rates regardless of the NBP; stay in banks and utilities as rate and energy beneficiaries.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: KGHM is lagging while being declared a world production leader and copper peers hit records; the GPW is paying for domestic energy earnings over global metal earnings, which reverses if the dollar keeps weakening.
