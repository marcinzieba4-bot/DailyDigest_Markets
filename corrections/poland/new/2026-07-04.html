🇵🇱 Poland  Bullish  ↓ rate cuts repriced in

Macro Stance: Polish rate expectations have fully inverted. June CPI came in at 2.5% per the GUS flash, exactly at the NBP target (Parkiet, [Jun 30]); Bankier.pl's weekend wrap calls it the inflation target achieved [Jul 4]. Commerzbank noted that rate hike bets have vanished, and ING sees investors starting to play for cuts in the second half of 2026 [Jun 30]. The next event is the RPP's July meeting, which Bankier.pl flagged as the last decision before a longer summer break [Jul 3]; CEO Magazyn expects rates most likely unchanged [Jul 2] and the finance minister said low inflation gives no grounds for hikes (Money.pl, [Jul 2]). Two complications: Gazeta Prawna warned the end of the CPN package could push inflation toward 3% [Jun 30], and the manufacturing PMI dropped to 46.1 in June versus a 49.4 consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 1]). On labour, GUS reported a January median gross wage of 7,447.16 zł (Prawo.pl, [Jul 2]) and unions called the government's 2027 minimum wage proposal a "mockery" (Dziennik.pl, [Jul 4]).

Markets: Friday was a breadth session: the WIG20 rose 1.57% with 19 of 20 members higher (FXMAG, [Jul 3]), after Żabka, XTB and GPW itself set all-time highs on Thursday (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 2]) and a second-quarter gain of 7.38% for the index, its third consecutive positive quarter (FXMAG, [Jul 1]). Orlen was the leader, up 4.78% on a record net profit (Investing.com Polska, [Jul 3]); the card now shows 132.92, +7.7% on the week, with the four-week loss narrowed to -7.0%. CD Projekt is the other weekly winner at 233.4 (+7.4% 1W) after Cyberpunk 2077 crossed 40 mn copies (PAP Biznes, [Jul 3]). KGHM closed 335.0 (+2.2% 1W, -3.0% 4W) on its strategy day. Banks firmed: PKO 105.24 (+1.6% 1W, +7.1% 4W), Pekao 233.2 (+1.9% 1W). Allegro 38.44 (+3.3% 1W, +9.5% 4W), PGE 9.63 (+0.3% 1W) and LPP 18,550 (+1.3% 1W, -14.1% 4W) were quiet, and Dino is the outlier: 27.7, -4.4% 1W, -6.3% 4W, at its lowest since 2022 after Barclays cut its target on slower expansion (pb.pl, [Jul 3]). EPOL's last close was 39.44, +2.3% 1W, -1.3% 4W. The zloty was whipsawed: EUR/PLN tested 4.30 (wnp.pl, [Jul 1]) before weak US payrolls sank the dollar and the zloty recovered (Comparic.pl, [Jul 3]); the card has USD/PLN at 3.7522 (-0.4% 1W, +2.9% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.2859 (+0.2% 1W, +1.2% 4W). On POLGBs, investors came back for Polish debt, buying bonds worth billions in May (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 3]).

Fiscal & Politics: The Sejm's week produced the OKI personal investment account, up to 100 thousand zł invested free of the Belka tax (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 3]), and a final decision on the 800 plus benefit after the 2,403 zł universal-payment idea was costed (INFOR.PL, [Jul 4]). The fuel shield has ended with pump prices up around 60 groszy per litre (wPolityce.pl, [Jul 1]), to be recouped through a windfall tax on fuel companies covering the 4 bn zł CPN package (Biznes Wprost, [Jul 2]). The backdrop is weaker: the EU convergence report ranked Poland's public finances worst in the bloc (wPolityce.pl, [Jun 30]), Rzeczpospolita argued it is time to review state spending [Jun 29], and Bankier.pl warned of an accumulation of outlays on nuclear and CPK with an EU procedure in the background [Jun 27]. The finance minister said there is "no chemistry" with the president (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 2]).

Corporate & Sectors: Saturday belonged to energy. Tusk visited Kleczew, where Orlen switched on one of the largest hybrid renewable farms in the region, enough for 100 thousand homes (Money.pl, [Jul 4]), and declared the end of coal, naming Bełchatów and Konin as good sites for the second nuclear plant (Bankier.pl, [Jul 4]) with experts to decide between the two finalists (Interia Biznes, [Jul 4]). The Sejm had already passed the fast-track law rescuing the nuclear schedule (Bankier.pl, [Jul 3]). KGHM's Strategy 2055+ promises over 32 bn zł of investment to 2030 (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 3]) and a polyhalite mine near Puck on a 300 mn tonne deposit (Wieści Rolnicze, [Jul 4]), with 10 bn zł and 800 jobs in play (Kaszuby24, [Jul 4]). Orlen raised 750 mn euro in bonds with demand more than twice the offer (pb.pl, [Jul 3]) and holds 95% of Energa with a squeeze-out on the table (Money.pl, [Jul 2]). For banks, the TSUE ruling of Jul 2 preserved lenders' right to recover disbursed capital on void CHF loans (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 2]). Dino opened 148 stores in the first half to reach 3,176 (BIZNES24, [Jul 2]) but is under pressure from OPZZ and UOKiK (Biznes Wprost, [Jul 3]).

Geopolitics & Security: The US warning that Russia is discussing an armed provocation or border incursion on Polish territory to test NATO (The Telegraph, [Jul 3]) dominated the weekend: Poland is assessing security scenarios (Kyiv Post, [Jul 4]), the UK warned Moscow (Kyiv Post, [Jul 4]), and Tusk's line that the coming months may be "critical" (BBC, [Jul 3]) travelled worldwide. Ukraine warned of a Russian plot to release fabricated WWII documents to divide Warsaw and Kyiv (Kyiv Post, [Jul 4]), while Tusk set a limit on further Ukraine aid ahead of the NATO summit (Forsal.pl, [Jul 3]).

New / Most Important: The rapid repricing from potential hikes to potential cuts is the story: a full reversal in rate expectations within weeks, confirmed by a 2.5% print and a PMI at 46.1 that argues the economy needs the relief. The market consequence is already visible in the card: a weekly WIG20 breadth rally led by Orlen (+7.7%) and CD Projekt (+7.4%) while Dino (-4.4%) is being sold for growth deceleration. Tusk's Kleczew appearance adds a political layer: an explicit coal exit with Bełchatów or Konin for nuclear is a capex signal for PGE and Orlen that cuts both ways on balance sheets.  |  Playbook ✓: Lower inflation = cuts = weaker currency, textbook; equities rerate first and the zloty lags.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A record-high GPW alongside a US warning of a Russian armed test of NATO on Polish soil; the market is pricing the rate cycle and ignoring the tail.
