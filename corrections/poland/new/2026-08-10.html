🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold

Macro Stance: The rate debate has reopened without resolving. Citi argues today that the inflation path may not allow the RPP to cut at its next meeting (PAP Biznes, [Aug 10]), after wnp.pl noted inflation back at 3% with fuel prices working against easing [Aug 3] and Strefa Inwestorów asked whether a September cut survives the July print [Aug 6]; Glapiński wants lower rates while experts sound the alarm (Super Biznes, [Aug 3]). Today's GUS release helps the doves: the Q2 average wage was 9233.13 zł, up 5.5% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 10]), the weakest wage dynamics in a decade outside the pandemic (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 10]), and Rzeczpospolita stresses the average fell from the prior quarter [Aug 10]. Growth is the counterweight: the manufacturing PMI rebounded with the first employment rise in months (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 3]), Interia sees growth possibly reaching 4.1% despite the energy shock [Aug 5] and Poland is now the sixth-largest EU economy (Euronews, [Aug 9]). Final July CPI and the balance of payments are due this week (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 9]).

Markets: Warsaw is in record territory. The WIG20 broke its peak from almost two decades ago (Parkiet, [Aug 3]), the WIG closed above 150,000 points for the first time (Bankier.pl, [Aug 4]) with Domański citing returning investor confidence (Parkiet, [Aug 4]), and the WIG20 pierced 4000 before pulling back (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 5]) as KGHM jumped 8% on copper at 14 thousand dollars (FXMAG, [Aug 5]). Orlen dragged on Friday while the WIG20 defended 4000 (Bankier.pl, [Aug 7]); today the WIG20 posted a record close led by Budimex and Dino with PGE the weakest large cap (Bankier.pl, Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 10]). The card shows the breadth: EPOL 44.19 (+3.2% 1W, +9.5% 4W); KGHM 352.95 (+14.6% 1W) and PGE 10.75 (+14.7% 4W) lead, Allegro 46.21 (+16.0% 4W), Dino 32.44 (+15.0% 4W), LPP 22,220 (+14.7% 4W) and CD Projekt 256.1 (+9.9% 4W) follow, Orlen 149.06 (+0.7% 1W) and Pekao 252.9 (+3.0% 1W) are steady, and PKO 109.0 (-2.6% 1W, -3.4% 4W) is the only name in the red. The zloty is firm but stalled: USD/PLN 3.7173 (-0.3% 1W, -2.0% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.2956 (-0.2% 1W, -0.7% 4W), with the euro eyeing the 4.30 line (Bankier.pl, [Aug 10]). POLGBs rebounded at the start of August and retail buyers took nearly 10 billion zł of savings bonds (Bankier.pl, [Aug 7]).

Fiscal & Politics: The finance ministry is assembling the 2027 budget with the largest deficit in the EU and pressure to cut taxes (pb.pl, [Aug 4]); the tax-free amount and a higher second threshold could cost 70 billion zł (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 9]), Domański says the budget bill comes in August (Pit.pl, [Aug 7]) and Bloomberg reports income tax relief for millions being weighed before the election [Aug 4]. Rzeczpospolita asked whether Poland has just crossed the constitutional debt limit [Aug 9], Konkret24 says it depends on the definition [Aug 7], public spending has reached 50.9% of GDP (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 5]) and debt service now costs more than 800+ (Interia Biznes, [Aug 5]). The budget hangs on the SAFE loan (wGospodarce, [Aug 8]) and Poland wants unused SAFE defence funds (Reuters, [Aug 10]); mineral tax receipts have already beaten plan (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 10]). Domański says OKI accounts will strengthen the GPW (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 6]) with assets seen above 100 billion zł (Bankier.pl, [Aug 9]). The ministry has asked the ECB about NBP-rate-based mortgages (Bankier.pl, [Aug 10]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen earned 7.7 billion zł in Q2 with record investment and a dividend (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 7]) and keeps its 36.3 billion zł capex target (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 7]); Strefa Inwestorów asks whether the government will retry a windfall tax [Aug 7], the shares fell despite the beat (Comparic.pl, [Aug 10]) and Allegro may buy Orlen Paczka (Cashless.pl, [Aug 10]). LPP's Q2 revenue rose 18.5% in constant currency with EBIT up more than 40% and LFL up 1.8% (StockWatch.pl, Bankier.pl, [Aug 6]), it plans 750 Sinsay stores this year (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 9]) but faces a 40 million dollar Bangladesh supplier dispute tied to its Russian exit (RMF24, [Aug 5]). Dino is rebounding after months of declines (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 10]); Allegro has 21.9 million users (Bankier.pl, [Aug 5]). Congo banned copper concentrate exports (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 6]). CD Projekt will reveal the Witcher 3 expansion at Gamescom (Parkiet, [Aug 7]). The CHF act took effect on Aug 7, letting borrowers stop instalments after suing (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 10]); banks had a strong June (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 6]). Couche-Tard agreed to buy Żabka for 32.62 billion zł (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 3]). Earthworks began at the nuclear site (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 6]); heat sent power prices soaring (wGospodarce, [Aug 6]).

Geopolitics & Security: US intelligence warns Putin could test NATO with a limited attack on Poland or the Baltics as early as this autumn (The Kyiv Independent, The Telegraph, [Aug 7]), and Poland and the Baltics are shielding infrastructure against a false-flag strike (Reuters, [Aug 10]). A Russian missile landed in Poland, prompting Lithuanian calls for a firm response (LRT, [Aug 4]), the foreign ministry revived intercepting missiles over Ukraine (LIGA.net, [Aug 6]) and talks on a permanent US base are under way (NDTV, [Aug 5]). Europe is running short of Patriots (POLITICO.eu, [Aug 10]).

New / Most Important: The record on the GPW is the story, and its composition matters more than the level. The WIG above 150,000 and the WIG20 through 4000 are driven by KGHM on copper, PGE on power prices and consumer names on the PIT-relief narrative, while PKO lags and Orlen is sold on a record quarter because a windfall tax is back in the conversation. The same week brought a 5.5% wage print, the softest in a decade, and a Citi note ruling out a near-term cut: the market is pricing growth and fiscal generosity, the RPP is still pricing inflation.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold is consensus; ride the index breadth but prefer Pekao over PKO, treat Orlen as a policy risk and the zloty as range-bound around 4.30.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A fresh US intelligence warning of a limited Russian strike on Polish territory landed in the same week as an all-time high: equity investors are assigning that scenario no premium at all.
