🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, hike talk resurfacing

Macro Stance: The inflation picture is the best in years and the rate debate is moving the other way. Core inflation fell in June per the NBP's latest reading (Biznes Wprost, pb.pl, [Jul 16]), food is "cheapening the most in years" (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 16]), and headline CPI was confirmed at 2.5% y/y with a 0.5% m/m decline (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 15]). Yet the Council's hawks are louder: Business Insider carries a "surprising" RPP comment that hikes are more probable than cuts ([Jul 16]), following the member who contradicted Glapiński on Tuesday (pb.pl, [Jul 14]), while the governor's own offer of a post-holiday cut motion (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 9]) still stands after the Council kept rates unchanged (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 9]). The swing factor is fuel: the end of the CPN package will push fuel prices and inflation back up (Energetyka24.com, [Jul 16]), PKO BP expects a transitory move above 3.5% (PAP, [Jul 15]), Rzeczpospolita sees a food-price turn in autumn ([Jul 16]), and the energy and finance ministers are now weighing whether to bring CPN back (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 16]). The FAZ verdict is "high growth, high debts" (Onet, [Jul 16]). Mortgage demand is solid with a new record average loan, though BIK expects declines and fixed rates are converging on variable ones (Rzeczpospolita, Bankier.pl, [Jul 16]).

Markets: Thursday belonged to Żabka: the stock jumped by double digits to a record after reports that Seven & i, the owner of 7-Eleven, is nearing a stake purchase (Reuters, Parkiet, Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 16]),. WIG20 still closed in the red despite it (XYZ, [Jul 16]) after Parkiet described consolidation around 3800 points ([Jul 15]). The card shows a rally that has rotated hard: Allegro 44.49 (+12.1% 1W, +17.2% 4W) and Orlen 145.5 (+4.0% / +12.5%), with Comparic noting Orlen +20% in two weeks and a new all-time high "within reach" ([Jul 16]), versus PKO 109.1 (+2.9% / +2.4%) and Pekao 235.5 (+0.2% / 0.0%) as the bank-tax threat (pb.pl, [Jul 14]) caps the sector. KGHM 313.4 bounced 5.3% on the week but remains -20.1% over four weeks; PGE 9.35 (-0.1% / -6.6%), Dino 28.55 (+2.7%), CD Projekt 231.9 (-1.1%), LPP 19850 (+6.3% / +4.4%). EPOL 41.05 is +3.2% on the week and +2.9% over four weeks, helped by a steadier currency. The zloty "no longer weakens" even as Trump escalates, with the euro suspended just above 4.32 (Bankier.pl, [Jul 16]) and FXMAG arguing the EUR/PLN rise may be ending ([Jul 16]); the card has USD/PLN 3.7657 (-0.1% 1W, +2.2% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3168 (+0.3% / +1.7%). The debt side is less comfortable: wnp.pl reports the latest POLGB auction "again did not go as it should" ([Jul 16]),.

Fiscal & Politics: The deficit crossed "another threshold" at 123.7 billion zloty after June, with the CPN programme exerting "powerful pressure" on public finances (TVN24, Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 16]); Business Insider says the budget is less ready for further shocks ([Jul 16]) and the opposition is calling it ruin (RadioMaryja.pl, Fronda.pl, [Jul 16]). Domański countered with KAS audit findings of 120 billion zloty of irregularities in public spending, about 3% of GDP, and 250 notices to prosecutors (TVN24, wnp.pl, Interia Biznes, [Jul 16]). The tax side moved: a decision was taken to lift the first PIT threshold to 171 thousand zloty from 120 thousand (PulsHR.pl, [Jul 16]), a revenue cost on top of the half-year hole. Construction began on a deepwater Baltic port meant to rival Hamburg (Brussels Signal, [Jul 16]). PiS wants Orlen to take over Qemetica; the state-assets ministry sees no basis (RadioMaryja.pl, [Jul 16]).

Corporate & Sectors: Energy policy clarified: the government's mix to 2040 is nuclear plus renewables instead of coal (Strefa Biznesu, Portal Samorządowy, [Jul 16]), an SMR roadmap is due by end-July (pb.pl, [Jul 16]), Stalowa Wola moved closer to an SMR with a Sejm committee approving land transfer (Portal Samorządowy, [Jul 16]), and PGE argues ETS changes could cut Polish power prices by 10 to 15% with the EU reform expected this week (Portal Samorządowy, Bankier.pl, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 16]). KGHM wants to produce more of its own power to cut costs (Parkiet, [Jul 16]) and the Reda-Władysławowo rail line will be expanded to serve its future Pomeranian mine, an investment of up to 10 billion zloty (Bankier.pl, Strefa Biznesu, [Jul 16]). Retail: Dino is suing a union leader for 50 thousand zloty as unions prepare a strike, yet the shares rose on market successes (Bankier.pl, Fakt, INNPoland.pl, [Jul 16]); Allegro and InPost are back at the negotiating table and Allegro Pay now covers holiday bookings (DlaHandlu.pl, Cashless.pl, [Jul 16]).

Geopolitics & Security: Support for helping Ukraine has fallen from 77% to 52% (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 16]), and attacks on Ukrainians in Poland are being described as "not the last incident" (Fakt, [Jul 16]). Poland and the Baltics continue to warn of a limited military or hybrid Russian provocation against NATO (European Interest, [Jul 16]; AP News, [Jul 15]), after Sikorski's false-flag drone warning (Kyiv Post, [Jul 15]).

New / Most Important: The Żabka bid is the single most important signal for GPW this week: a Japanese strategic buyer paying up for Polish convenience retail at a record price validates the consumer story at the exact moment the bank trade is losing its rate-cut and tax tailwinds. Set against the budget hole and a bond auction that disappointed again, the message is that foreign capital wants Polish equity cash flows but is pickier about Polish sovereign paper.

Playbook ✓: Zloty stabilising as cut bets fade is the expected sequence; strategic M&A premium in retail is orthodox late-cycle behaviour.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Headline CPI at target and core falling while RPP members talk hikes is a central bank reacting to fiscal risk, not price risk. A PIT threshold raise into a 123.7 billion zloty deficit widens that gap; if auctions keep underwhelming, the next move in Polish rates may be set by the bond market rather than the Council.
