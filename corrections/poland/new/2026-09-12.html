🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold to mid-2027

Macro Stance: The week's monetary message is settled: the RPP held the reference rate at 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Sep 9]), Glapiński said rates may stay stable until mid-2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 10]) with no room for cuts (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 10]), and he signalled the NBP will not follow the ECB's latest hike (pb.pl, [Sep 10]), a move Parkiet says does not end ECB tightening [Sep 11]. Commerzbank's observation that the NBP has turned cautious as inflation rises is now the consensus, and the risk has flipped to tightening: Briefs Finance reads the guidance as a hike if inflation tops 4% [Sep 11], and Millennium's path has CPI above 4% in early 2027 and back at target only in the second half of 2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 11]). The growth side keeps improving: VeloBank lifted its 2026 GDP forecast to 3.7% (WNP.PL, [Sep 10]), Pekao raised both inflation and GDP forecasts (Analizy.pl, [Sep 10]), Poland set a summer tourism record (TVP World, [Sep 11]). GUS estimated the war's cumulative cost to GDP at 490.1 bn zł (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 9]). On wages, ZNP insists the 3% indexation in the 2027 budget is not a raise (Prawo.pl, [Sep 11]).

Markets: The WIG20 closed the week up 0.74% on Friday (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 11]) with CD Projekt leading and energy retreating (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 11]), repairing Thursday's 0.67% fall (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 10]) when cheaper bonds hit the index and KGHM lost 7% while PGE gained 5.3% (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 10]) after Monday and Tuesday records (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]). The card at Friday's close: EPOL 45.56 (+2.3% 1W, +3.6% 4W); PGE 14.03 (+11.9% 1W, +30.1% 4W) remains the month's trade; PKO 120.52 (+3.1% 1W, +8.5% 4W) after a record capitalisation above 150 bn zł (XYZ, [Sep 11]); Pekao 267.4 (+1.3%, +3.2%); Orlen 161.12 (+2.0%, +5.9%); CD Projekt 235.4 (+1.5% 1W, -10.5% 4W) after Friday's lead; KGHM 351.9 (-1.2% 1W, -0.2% 4W) with LME copper falling (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 11]); Dino 35.4 (-1.6% 1W, +9.2% 4W); LPP 21560 (+1.0%, -1.2%); Allegro 44.94 (-1.4%, -3.3%) ahead of results (DlaHandlu.pl, [Sep 11]). The zloty is flat but pressured: USD/PLN 3.7241 (+0.2% 1W, -0.3% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.3227 (+0.1% 1W, +0.3% 4W), with Bankier.pl seeing the euro holding its line [Sep 11] and Commerzbank calling NBP signals dovish for PLN (FXStreet, [Sep 10]). POLGBs were the week's weak asset: Parkiet asks why bonds cheapen when everyone buys [Sep 11] and the US 10-year is at the 5% line (Forex Club, [Sep 11]). BGK nonetheless sold PLN 1.23 bln on PLN 2.13 bln of demand (TradingView, [Sep 10]).

Fiscal & Politics: State public debt rose 7.0% q/q in Q2 (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 10]) to 53.3% of GDP (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 10]); WNP.PL says only 1.7 percentage points separate Poland from the threshold that triggers cuts to pensions and wages [Sep 11], and Wszystko co najważniejsze calls the budget a state hiding its bill [Sep 12]. Glapiński said the draft shows no tightening and that a 7.1% deficit has no inflation effect (wnp.pl, [Sep 10]). The NFZ carries a 12 bn hole (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 9]), topping it up gets harder (Rynek Zdrowia, [Sep 11]) and a new tax lands in September (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 11]). Wprost says the budget depends on Nawrocki's vetoes [Sep 8]; Motyka blames the president for blocking cheaper fuel (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 11]).

Corporate & Sectors: Energy is the weekend story: Saudi Arabia shut a key oil pipeline after drone attacks and Orlen issued a statement reassuring on supplies to Poland (Business Insider Polska and TVN24, [Sep 12]), while Rzeczpospolita asks whether deliveries are threatened [Sep 12]. Pump prices are whipsawing: Gazeta Prawna reports Orlen cut petrol sharply [Sep 12] while Niezależna expects diesel to pass 9 zł [Sep 12]; Orlen defended its 2.94 zł per litre export price to Ukraine (Farmer.pl, [Sep 12]) and Business Insider Polska counts record investment by Orlen and the state giants [Sep 12]. Power plants received almost 17.5 bn zł of compensation, mostly PGE (Gramwzielone.pl, [Sep 11]), while household bills could rise 19% (Next Gazeta.pl, [Sep 11]) or nearly 300 zł (Money.pl, [Sep 11]) and one gas customer paying 6 tys. zł a year fears 8 tys. (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 12]); Tauron's profit was solid but lower (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 10]). Banks: POLSTR entered mortgages with a first bank cutting its rate (PropertyNews.pl, [Sep 11]), the TSUE allowed the balance theory in CHF cases if it does not harm borrowers (Prawo.pl, [Sep 10]), and ZBP says most mortgages are repaid early, in 8-9 years (Interia Biznes, [Sep 12]). Allegro holds over half of a 2 bn zł retail media market (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 12]); Dino is building a Zawiercie distribution centre (Omnichannel News, [Sep 11]) under a strike warning (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 10]). Housing: Pełczyńska-Nałęcz called flat prices a verdict (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 12]) and creditworthiness shrank by over 30 tys. zł in a month (Warszawa w Pigułce, [Sep 12]).

Geopolitics & Security: An urgent RCB alert covered Podkarpacie during a Russian attack on Ukraine (Nowiny 24, [Sep 12]) and NATO aircraft were scrambled with a warning issued in Poland (Daily Mirror, [Sep 12]); Tusk said a Russian attack in Poland had its foretaste (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 11]) and Poland expects strikes on border crossings (Kyiv Post, [Sep 10]). Poland will hand Ukraine 50 mln euro of military aid (RadioMaryja.pl, [Sep 12]) and Sikorski is in Kyiv (Kyiv Post, [Sep 11]).

New / Most Important: Poland's proximity to the geopolitical epicentre is no longer only about Iran: a Saudi pipeline outage lands on a market where the NBP has just ruled out cuts to mid-2027, debt is 1.7 points from the sanction threshold and POLGBs are cheapening into demand.
