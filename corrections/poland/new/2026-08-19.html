🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hawkish hold

Macro Stance: Strefa Biznesu sums up the policy picture today: the ECB and the RPP are both holding and further decisions depend on inflation pressure [Aug 19]. The NBP's slow hawkish shift is weighing on the zloty per Commerzbank, and the latest prices justify patience rather than easing: Eurostat put Polish HICP at 3.1% y/y in July against 3% in the EU (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 19]), GUS CPI is 3.0% with fuel up 13.9% m/m (Bankier.pl, 300Gospodarka.pl, [Aug 13]), core is at a ten-month high (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]) and Citi expects the fuel measures to be extended at least into September (PAP Biznes, [Aug 17]). Consumer sentiment slipped as fuel hit wallets (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 19]) and wGospodarce says inflation is sinking the prime minister [Aug 19]. Growth remains the support, Q2 GDP at 3.8% y/y (PAP Biznes, [Aug 13]), and mortgage demand is up more than a fifth per BIK (TVN24, [Aug 18]). The domestic data cluster and the Fitch decision come at the end of the week (ING, [Aug 17]).

Markets: The session had two halves. Strefa Inwestorów counted most indices deepening the correction with KGHM down a fifth straight time [Aug 19], CrypS.pl a third down day led by CD Projekt and KGHM [Aug 19]; then the tax plan gave the exchange a tailwind with banks in demand and PKO BP up almost 5% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 19]), while FXMAG saw Dino jump and mBank and KGHM drag [Aug 19]. The card, which predates the bank rally, shows the damage: EPOL 43.63 (-1.7% 1W, +5.6% 4W); KGHM 334.5 (-8.2% 1W, +9.2% 4W), PGE 10.29 (-7.7% 1W), LPP 21,020 (-6.3% 1W), Allegro 45.1 (-3.7% 1W, 0.0% 4W) and Dino 32.47 (-2.9% 1W) have given back most of August, CD Projekt 258.3 (-0.8% 1W, +11.9% 4W) and Orlen 153.22 (-0.0% 1W) are flat, and PKO 109.86 (+0.8% 1W) and Pekao 253.8 (+0.1% 1W, +7.5% 4W) are the only names holding. The zloty is weakening despite a soft dollar: USD/PLN 3.7312 (+0.2% 1W, -1.7% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.3193 (+0.5% 1W, -0.1% 4W), with FXMAG noting the breach of local resistance at 4.32 [Aug 19] and wGospodarce saying the zloty failed to seize its opportunity [Aug 19]. POLGBs are the bright spot: the finance ministry raised over 13.4 billion zł on strong demand (Bankier.pl, [Aug 19]) while the US Treasury's bigger buybacks pulled yields down (pb.pl, [Aug 19]).

Fiscal & Politics: Tusk and Domański unveiled the tax reform: 3.5 million taxpayers will pay less PIT (Rzeczpospolita, Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 19]), the second threshold rises to 140 thousand zł (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 19]), a third bracket is introduced and the gain reaches 3600 zł a year (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 19]), financed by a higher rate for the largest companies (Reuters, [Aug 19]) and a CIT increase for family foundations (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 19]); Domański wants thresholds indexed annually (Money.pl, [Aug 19]), the changes would apply from 2027 and he is counting on the president's signature (Portal Samorządowy, [Aug 19]). Reactions split along predictable lines: the Palace spoke of more confusion (Money.pl, [Aug 19]), Mentzen called it a massive blow to small firms (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 19]) and PKO BP's economist sees a positive GDP effect (wnp.pl, [Aug 19]). The fiscal backdrop is loud: the cabinet met suddenly on the budget (Super Biznes, [Aug 19]), the Fiscal Council chief warned on debt (Money.pl, [Aug 19]), wnp.pl says the deficit is choking Poland on its way to a record [Aug 19] and Morawiecki claims debt grew 684 billion zł under Tusk (Money.pl, [Aug 19]); the deficit after July was 141.1 billion zł (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 17]). On energy, the government is preparing relief for firms (TVP Info, [Aug 19]) with Domański promising lower power prices for business (Super Biznes, [Aug 19]).

Corporate & Sectors: KGHM delivered: Q2 net profit of 2.05 billion zł (Bankier.pl, [Aug 19]) with revenue up nearly 50% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 19]), in line with consensus (XYZ, [Aug 19]); exploration drilling is jumping (Parkiet, [Aug 19]). Banks are the reform's winners, with PKO BP up almost 5% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 19]) after absorbing the 47.5 million zł KNF fine (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 18]). Orlen is set to replace PGE as sponsor of the National Stadium, paying well over 10 million zł (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 19]), as PGE customers fear bills of horror from a new billing system (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 19]) and Thursday's maximum fuel prices rose again (Super Biznes, [Aug 19]). Allegro completed its Piotrków logistics hub with about 300 jobs (Dziennik Łódzki, [Aug 19]); the Allegro-InPost conflict remains open (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 18]). CD Projekt starts five days of Gamescom streams and FXMAG expects the shares to react [Aug 19]. Dino is pushing one-plus-one promotions ahead of results (RMF FM, [Aug 19]). Housing may get pricier again as analysts see a revival (Bankier.pl, [Aug 19]).

Geopolitics & Security: Fox News describes Poland bracing for war as tensions take an ominous turn [Aug 19] and Forsal.pl explains who would be mobilised in a conflict [Aug 19], after the Defence Ministry said it expects a major Russian provocation within months (UNITED24 Media, [Aug 17]). The Patriot decision for Ukraine is still pending under US pressure (UNN, [Aug 18]).

New / Most Important: The tax reform is the week's pivot and it is fiscally two-sided by design: PIT relief for 3.5 million people paid for by the largest corporates and family foundations. The market read it instantly as a bank story, which is why PKO rallied 5% on a day KGHM fell for a fifth session despite a 2.05 billion zł quarter. The unresolved variable is the veto, and a reform that needs Nawrocki's signature lands in the same week as a Fitch review and a record deficit narrative.  |  Playbook ✓: Gradual hawkish tilt as expected; banks over miners and utilities, treat KGHM's pullback as positioning rather than fundamentals, keep POLGB exposure at the front end where demand is strong.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The ministry raised 13.4 billion zł on strong demand while the zloty sold through 4.32: investors are buying Poland's carry but not its currency, which is the AUD dynamic transplanted to CEE, where sovereign stress and geopolitics outweigh the rate differential.
