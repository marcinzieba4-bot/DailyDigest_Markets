🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, dovish pivot signaled

Macro Stance: The NBP held the reference rate at 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Jul 8]) and the governor then turned materially more dovish: Glapiński said he could submit a motion after the holidays (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 10]), ING described him as opening the door to rate cuts before end-2026 [Jul 9], and CEO Magazyn summarised the outcome as rates held but a cut possible [Jul 10]. The pushback came within a day. Reuters reported two Polish central bankers see no rush to cut rates after summer [Jul 10], an RPP member spoke of a discussion "in the dovecote" (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 10]), Erste sees no cuts even to the end of 2027 (Direct Money, [Jul 10]), and Business Insider Polska's verdict on the raised hopes was "too many unknowns" [Jul 10]. The projection cuts against the governor: CEO Magazyn headlines revised growth and higher inflation [Jul 10], Gazeta Prawna says inflation is to fall but NBP flags two serious threats [Jul 10], and Commerzbank's read is an NBP cautious as inflation overshoots (FXStreet, [Jul 10]). Supporting disinflation, wnp.pl reports shop price growth slowing on the discount war and a strong zloty [Jul 10]; against it, the Social Dialogue Council ended without agreement on public-sector pay and the 2027 minimum wage (Głos Nauczycielski, [Jul 10]).

Markets: Parkiet's headline captures the day: the zloty goes one way, the exchange another, and records are in the air again [Jul 10]. Bankier.pl reported the Polish stock market at its highest in history, with the governor flooring the zloty while banks were in demand [Jul 10]; banks drove the WIG20 and every large cap closed higher (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 10]), PKO BP, Alior and Erste shares were the most expensive in their history (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 10]), and Goldman Sachs strongly raised its PKO BP valuation (pb.pl, [Jul 10]). Pepco led with +4.66% while Żabka fell sharply (FXMAG, [Jul 10]). Societe Generale's downtrend view eyes 4.48 on firm support (FXStreet, [Jul 10]). Card: USD/PLN 3.7853 (+0.9% 1W, +3.2% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.3278 (+1.0% 1W, +1.9% 4W). POLGBs rallied: PKO BP says the dovish RPP signals became the impulse for a fall in Treasury yields (wnp.pl, [Jul 10]).

Fiscal & Politics: The finance minister has cooled tax-cut hopes with the deficit as the key obstacle (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 9]) and previewed the election-year budget (Money.pl, [Jul 9]); and the air traffic agency PAŻP needs an additional 1 bn zł (Money.pl, [Jul 10]). The Gazprom fine dispute over 174.5 mn zł continues, with UOKiK surprised by the minister's stance and analysing its legal options (wnp.pl, [Jul 10]) and the ministry answering the charges (Interia Biznes, [Jul 10]). The ministry wants to speed up the end of WIBOR, with millions of borrowers to receive letters from banks (Money.pl, [Jul 10]) and two months to decide on the new POLSTR benchmark (Fakt, [Jul 10]), which Next Gazeta.pl calls a controversial reform [Jul 10]. Gazeta Prawna reports NATO preparing new banks to finance armaments for a long war [Jul 10].

Corporate & Sectors: Energy delivered the week's structural event: Orlen's Baltic Power offshore farm sent first power to the grid with 54 of 76 turbines installed (PAP Biznes, [Jul 10]), a new chapter for Polish energy (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 10]); Tusk set ministers targets for a lower-energy-price package (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 10]) while wnp.pl warns ETS2 could push prices up sharply [Jul 10]. Bogdanka will sell more coal to Orlen Termika for 507 mn zł (wnp.pl, [Jul 10]). Banks: PKO BP added 297 mn zł to CHF provisions in the second quarter (Bankier.pl, [Jul 10]) yet hit a record high on the Goldman upgrade; UOKiK's inspection of banks and BIK continues (Super Biznes, [Jul 10]). LPP shareholders approved a dividend of 900 zł per share including the advance (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 10]) and the stock rose (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 10]). Geopolitics & Security: Poland says it has credible information that Russia is planning new provocations in Europe (Euromaidan Press, [Jul 10]), after the US warning of an armed test of NATO on Polish territory (The Telegraph, [Jul 3]); IOL argues the Ukraine-Poland fallout threatens NATO's proxy war against Russia [Jul 10]. Nawrocki and Zelensky met in Ankara after the spat (TVP World, [Jul 8]).

New / Most Important: The dovish NBP pivot is weighing on the zloty while equities make history, and both are the same trade: lower expected real rates re-rate banks and growth names on the GPW while the currency absorbs the credibility cost. ING described the NBP as in a "sweet spot", but the 2027 inflation forecast was raised even as the governor signalled cuts, and two colleagues have already said there is no rush.  |  Playbook ✓: PLN weakening on dovish signals, textbook; POLGB yields falling on the same signal is the carry trade working.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Signalling cuts while raising the inflation forecast is unusual and suggests political pressure on the NBP. If the ECB is pausing, an NBP cutting into rising inflation would widen the credibility gap and could trigger a more meaningful PLN selloff. Short PLN/long EUR is the cleanest expression, with Societe Generale's 4.48 as the marker.
