🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike risk

Macro Stance: The week ends with the NBP's reaction function redrawn. After holding at 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Sep 9]), Glapiński said there is no room for cuts (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 10]), that rates may stay stable until mid-2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 10]) and that the NBP will not follow the ECB (pb.pl, [Sep 10]); Briefs Finance distils it as a hike if inflation tops 4% [Sep 11]. That threshold is live: Millennium expects CPI to exceed 4% in early 2027 and return to target only in the second half of 2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 11]) and the ECB has not finished tightening (Parkiet, [Sep 11]) after hiking again (FXMAG, [Sep 11]). Commerzbank's read that the NBP has turned cautious as inflation rises stands, with Hormuz-driven energy costs behind it. Growth is not the problem: VeloBank raised its 2026 GDP forecast to 3.7% (WNP.PL, [Sep 10]), Pekao lifted both inflation and GDP forecasts (Analizy.pl, [Sep 10]), Poland broke its summer tourism record (TVP World, [Sep 11]) and shopping-centre turnover rose 2.9% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 11]). GUS priced the war's cumulative cost at 490.1 bn zł (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 9]), about €113.5bn (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 10]). The 2027 minimum wage rises 144 zł (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 9]) to 4950 zł (Parkiet, [Sep 7]).

Markets: Friday was green: the WIG20 rebounded with CD Projekt the leader and energy in retreat (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 11]), after Thursday's 0.67% drop (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 10]) in which cheaper bonds hit the index and KGHM was routed (Bankier.pl, [Sep 10]), losing 7% while PGE gained 5.3% (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 10]). wnp.pl counts new GPW records this week [Sep 11]. The card at Thursday's close: EPOL 45.15 (+2.2% 1W, +1.2% 4W); PGE 14.2 (+16.1% 1W, +29.7% 4W), the week's engine; PKO 119.44 (+4.1% 1W, +9.2% 4W) at a record capitalisation above 150 bn zł (XYZ, [Sep 11]); Pekao 265.6 (+2.6%, +4.2%); Orlen 159.56 (+2.3%, +5.6%); KGHM 347.05 (-1.5% 1W, -2.5% 4W) as LME copper falls (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 11]); LPP 21440 (+3.0% 1W); Dino 35.0 (-2.3% 1W, +7.0% 4W); Allegro 45.0 (-3.0% 4W) ahead of results (DlaHandlu.pl, [Sep 11]); CD Projekt 230.6 (-11.7% 4W). The zloty is under pressure with the euro holding its line (Bankier.pl, [Sep 11]): USD/PLN 3.7241 (+0.2% 1W, -0.3% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.3227 (+0.1% 1W, +0.3% 4W); FXMAG asks whether FX already priced the ECB hike [Sep 11] and Commerzbank sees dovish NBP signals weighing on PLN (FXStreet, [Sep 10]). POLGBs are the stress: Parkiet asks why bonds get cheaper when everyone is buying [Sep 11] and the global backdrop is a US 10-year at the 5% threshold (Forex Club, [Sep 11]).

Fiscal & Politics: State public debt rose 7.0% q/q in Q2 (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 10]) to 53.3% of GDP (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 10]); WNP.PL says only 1.7 percentage points separate Poland from the threshold that forces cuts to pensions and wages [Sep 11]. Glapiński said the draft budget shows no tightening and that a 7.1% deficit has no inflation effect (wnp.pl, [Sep 10]). The health budget grows but topping up the NFZ gets harder (Rynek Zdrowia, [Sep 11]) after its 12 bn hole (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 9]); the ministry is adding a new tax that hits one group hardest (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 11]). Wprost says the budget hangs on Nawrocki's decisions and vetoes could cost billions [Sep 8]; Motyka blames the president for blocking cheaper fuel (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 11]); Obajtek accused the government of turning Orlen into a budget cash machine (wPolsce24, [Sep 11]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: POLSTR is entering mortgages and a first bank has already cut its rate (PropertyNews.pl, [Sep 11]); the TSUE allowed the balance theory in CHF settlements as long as it does not harm borrowers (Prawo.pl, [Sep 10]); banks earn less while the economy borrows more (Bs.net.pl, [Sep 11]). Energy: Orlen announced a big change in fuel pricing (wnp.pl, [Sep 11]) and denied Obajtek's claims (Money.pl, [Sep 11]). Power plants received almost 17.5 bn zł of compensation, most of it PGE (Gramwzielone.pl, [Sep 11]); household bills could rise 19% (Next Gazeta.pl, [Sep 11]), by 276 zł (wnp.pl, [Sep 11]), the worst wind in ten years is pulling coal back in (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 11]) and Poland opens a months-long ETS battle in Brussels (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 11]). KGHM is reshaping Sierra Gorda and adding Victoria (Parkiet, [Sep 10]). Retail: Dino is building a distribution centre in Zawiercie (Omnichannel News, [Sep 11]) under a strike warning (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 10]); LPP is adding thousands of robots and an e-commerce hub (DlaHandlu.pl, [Sep 11]). Housing: creditworthiness fell in August (Biznes Wprost, [Sep 11]) while Develia sold over half a thousand flats over the summer (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 11]).

Geopolitics & Security: Sikorski arrived in Kyiv (Kyiv Post, [Sep 11]) as Poland warned Russia will likely intensify provocations on Ukraine's borders with European countries (News4JAX, [Sep 11]) after a direct threat at the border was averted (POLITICO.eu, [Sep 10]). NATO foiled a Russian undersea-cable sabotage drill (The Guardian, [Sep 11]) and NATO aircraft deployed in Poland against jet-powered drones (ABC News, [Sep 10]). Poland has NATO's third-largest army and wants foreign arms makers to invest locally (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 10]).

New / Most Important: The easing cycle expected for 2026 is officially shelved, and the market is now arguing about a hike: Millennium's path has CPI above 4% in early 2027, exactly the trigger the NBP has flagged. For equities that is a bank story, and PKO's record 150 bn zł capitalisation says so; for POLGBs it is a second blow on top of 53.3% debt, with yields rising even as everyone buys.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold pattern consistent with broader EM central bank caution amid oil shock; banks over duration, PGE over KGHM while copper falls.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Poland was supposed to be Europe's easing story and now carries hike risk; PGE is up 29.7% in four weeks on the same power-price surge that will cost households 276 zł a year.
