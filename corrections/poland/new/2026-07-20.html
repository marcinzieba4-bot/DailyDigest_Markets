🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, September cut debated

Macro Stance: Monday's GUS batch was strong across the board. Industrial production rose 7.6% in June (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 20]), beating forecasts even if Bankier notes the calendar helped ([Jul 20]), and PAP Biznes reads it as a good omen for second-quarter GDP ([Jul 20]). Corporate wages rose 5.9% y/y with the average above 9,400 zloty while employment fell (StockWatch.pl, Super Biznes, [Jul 20]); Bankier says real wage growth accelerated on lower inflation ([Jul 20]) and Interia Biznes has the average nearing the 10 thousand zloty barrier ([Jul 20]). ING says the data lift Poland's growth outlook despite rising external risks ([Jul 20]) and CEO Magazyn now calls Poland a trillion-dollar economy ([Jul 20]). Yet the rate debate swung back toward easing on the same day: Crédit Agricole expects the RPP to cut by 25 bp to 3.50% in September and treat that as the terminal level (PAP Biznes, [Jul 20]), a Council member did not rule out a cut this year (pb.pl, [Jul 20]), and Money.pl says economists have no doubts about a post-holiday cut ([Jul 20]), only three days after the NBP revealed who voted for a hike (Tysol.pl, [Jul 17]). The inflation side is less clean: Magazyn MANAGER+ warns oil back above 90 dollars may re-accelerate inflation ([Jul 20]), producer prices in industry rose for a fourth month (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 20]), and June CPI of 2.5% (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 15]) predates the end of CPN fuel relief that Erste expects to lift July prices (Bankier.pl, [Jul 15]).

Markets: Banks came back into favour on Monday while Modivo was again under pressure (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 20]), a relief after Friday's rout that left PKO at 106.8 (-5.4% 1W, +1.4% 4W) and Pekao at 229.0 (-6.3% / -2.7%) on the card. Investing.com and Comparic describe euphoria in Żabka and a bull run in Allegro and Pepco, asking whether CD Projekt and LPP are ready to end their big corrections ([Jul 20]); Parkiet counts a growing list of listed "billionaires" as domestic valuations rise like yeast ([Jul 20]). Allegro began a buyback with an initial 141 million zloty (wirtualnemedia.pl, [Jul 20]); the card has it at 43.4 (+9.0% / +10.1%), Orlen 146.2 (+4.5% / +15.7%), KGHM 293.7 (-4.9% / -19.6%), PGE 9.48 (+1.2% / -3.1%), Dino 29.15 (+3.3% / -0.2%), CD Projekt 232.2 (-0.3% / +3.6%), LPP 19960 (+3.0% / +7.5%), EPOL 40.35 (flat 1W, +0.3% 4W). PGE is a new name on Puls Biznesu's list of exchange favourites ([Jul 20]) and DM BOŚ picked 17 stocks for the coming weeks (Bankier.pl, [Jul 20]). The zloty paused its sell-off on Monday (Comparic.pl, [Jul 20]) but with oil near 90 dollars the euro is attacking this year's highs (Bankier.pl, FXMAG, [Jul 20]); the card has USD/PLN 3.7973 (+0.1% 1W, +2.4% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3393 (+0.3% / +2.1%). No fresh POLGB headlines beyond last week's sell-off (FXMAG, [Jul 17]).

Fiscal & Politics: The Finance Ministry said there is no room for new spending, singling out pay rises in public administration (XYZ, Bankier.pl, Money.pl, [Jul 20]), and State Treasury debt hit a record per new MF data (Interia Biznes, [Jul 20]; Business Insider Polska, [Jul 20]). The government is raising a tax rate to 15% from 2027 (Rzeczpospolita, Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 20]), wPolsce24 reads this as plugging the budget hole at the expense of small firms ([Jul 20]), and cider makers face a record excise rise (Portal spożywczy, [Jul 20]). Puls Biznesu reports MF backed away from its squeeze-out changes under corporate pressure ([Jul 20]). The June deficit of 123.7 billion zloty (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 15]) remains the anchor, and fuel politics intensifies: Polskie Radio 24 calls petrol a "powder keg" for the government ([Jul 20]) and RadioMaryja asks why VAT is not cut when fuel promised at 5.19 zloty sells above 7 ([Jul 20]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen has dethroned every Russian oil company by size as Russia's fuel crisis deepens (Euronews, [Jul 20]), but is fighting criticism at home over pump prices (Interia Biznes, [Jul 20]), publishing a doctored Morawiecki photo under the slogan "enough populism" (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 20]) while a litre of petrol passes 7 zloty (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jul 20]). PGE Energia Odnawialna signed a 152.6 million zloty KPO grant for the Porąbka-Żar pumped-storage plant (StockWatch.pl, Bankier.pl, [Jul 20]) and called the ETS reform a good starting point ([Jul 20]). KGHM is in focus as Chile's copper belt is paralysed (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 20]), China tightens metal supplies (pb.pl, [Jul 20]) and German interest grows (Radio Wnet, [Jul 20]). Dino is accused of sacking staff over strikes (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 20]). Banks face Franknews' warning on WIBOR annexes as the government changes the rules ([Jul 20]). Bankier's house-price index rose again though prices fall in some cities ([Jul 20]).

Geopolitics & Security: Lithuania's defence minister joined Poland in naming the Baltics and Poland as Russia's next targets (LRT, The Kyiv Independent, [Jul 20]) while NATO's top general said Moscow would "lose a lot" by attacking (CNBC, [Jul 20]); Warsaw will discuss the threat of provocations using intercepted Ukrainian drones (Українська правда, [Jul 20]). ISW says Russia is firing more missiles than it produces and dipping into reserves (Fakt, [Jul 20]). The Border Defence Component began its tasks on Sunday (Portal Obronny, [Jul 19]).

New / Most Important: The 7.6% industrial print and 5.9% wage growth arrived on the same day the doves reopened the September cut. That combination is the story: Poland's real economy is strong enough to argue for patience, and the RPP's own members now disagree in public about the direction of the next move. With oil above 90 dollars and the Finance Ministry declaring the budget tapped out, the burden of supporting growth falls on an NBP that cannot agree on the sign of its next decision.

Playbook ✓: Strong data plus falling inflation should support both the currency and domestic cyclicals; banks recovering on Monday fits.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Energy price resurgence from Hormuz tensions complicates any easing timeline for CEE broadly; a September cut to 3.50% is being priced while the Council's hawks talk hikes and fuel re-prices above 7 zloty, so the zloty is weakening into good data, which is not what the playbook says should happen.
