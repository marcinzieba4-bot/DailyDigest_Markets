🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, October surprise risk

Macro Stance: The hawks are no longer hedging. An RPP member told TVN24 "we will be forced to tighten monetary policy" [Oct 1], Strefa Biznesu says the October decision "may surprise" [Oct 1] and Business Insider Polska argues the market's pricing of sharp rate moves "may be right this time" [Oct 1], a day after the September flash CPI printed 4.0% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Sep 30]) with fuel up 36.1% (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 30]). Parkiet's verdict: inflation has left the NBP target and "will be hard to herd back" [Oct 1]. The counterweights are real: ING shows core ex food and energy fell (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 30]), PKO BP still expects rates unchanged despite a "4 in front" for months (Bankier.pl, [Sep 29]; Portal spożywczy, [Sep 30]). Activity is bottoming rather than booming: September PMIs show Poland's downturn easing (marketscreener, [Oct 1]), industry still below the line but with a signal of improvement (Strefa Biznesu, [Oct 1]), while the eurozone PMI jumped to 52.9 (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 1]). With eurozone inflation surging to multiyear highs and the ECB's hawkish pivot, the pull on Polish rates is in one direction.

Markets: October opened with blood on the floor. Bankier.pl called it the worst session in over a month with bank shares dumped [Oct 1]; the WIG30 fell 1.72% (Investing.com, [Oct 1]), the WIG20 slid to the 4100 area (Parkiet, [Oct 1]) and KGHM and Allegro were hit alongside the banks (CrypS.pl, [Oct 1]), with PGE the lone riser (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 1]). StockWatch.pl's answer to what frightened investors: "bonds scare" [Oct 1], while Parkiet noted bonds "became cheap again" [Oct 1]. DM PKO BP sees Orlen and XTB in downward corrections with further declines possible (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 1]), and Citi cut Allegro to neutral after a 63% run [Oct 1] even as the company lifts its buyback cap to 65 zł (StockWatch.pl, [Oct 1]). The card, to Wednesday's close, predates the rout: PGE 15.14 (+10.9% 1W, +27.8% 4W), Allegro 50.63 (+4.1% 1W, +13.1% 4W), CD Projekt 254.0 (+1.9% 1W, +9.9% 4W), LPP 24840 (+18.9% 4W), PKO 124.0 (+0.6% 1W, +8.5% 4W), Pekao 280.9 (flat 1W, +9.2% 4W); KGHM 329.55 (-3.8% 1W), Orlen 146.06 (-1.4% 1W, -7.6% 4W), Dino 35.07 (-1.2% 1W). EPOL 44.32 (-0.6% 1W, +1.2% 4W). The zloty took a "sudden jump" weaker (TVN24, [Oct 1]) as oil went back above 100 dollars on Trump's declarations (Interia Biznes, [Oct 1]), the dollar hit a 19-month high and the pound a 21-month high against it (Bankier.pl, [Oct 1]) and US yields broke 5.3% (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 1]); FXMAG calls the zloty "defenceless" with USD/PLN able to break above 3.86 [Oct 1]. The card's Wednesday close had USD/PLN at 3.8497 (+0.1% 1W, +3.1% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3618 (-0.3% 1W, +0.8% 4W).

Fiscal & Politics: The president signed the fuel-price and windfall-tax law but will refer it to the Constitutional Tribunal, which does not block the price cut (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 1]); he attached conditions for the government (Interia Wydarzenia, [Oct 1]), Domański gave a date for cheaper fuel (Polskie Radio 24, [Oct 1]) and Szłapka said cuts are possible at the weekend (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 1]). Business Insider Polska cautions the windfall tax will not lower prices automatically [Oct 1]. Debt is the other thread: the 2027 budget carries a 281.2 bn zł deficit on 977.6 bn zł of spending (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 30]), Rzeczpospolita warns of a 2028 event the government wants to avoid as debt nears the prudential line [Oct 1], pb.pl notes interest costs next year will exceed family spending [Oct 1] and Bankier.pl sees prudential procedures possible in 2030 [Sep 30]; the president's adviser says the 2027 election will decide (Forsal.pl, [Oct 1]). The cabinet approved up to 70% energy subsidies for energy-intensive industry (e-magazyny.pl, [Oct 1]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks fell on bonds and rate fear, not on volumes: mortgage lending has reached 75.7 bn zł (Bankier.pl, [Oct 1]) with almost 10.6 bn zł in a month (Portal Samorządowy, [Oct 1]), consumer loans jumped nearly 30% (Strefa Biznesu, [Oct 1]) and the NBP's Q2 report shows credit accelerating with prices stable (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Oct 1]); the government's new housing subsidies had developers "opening champagne" with a stock reaction (Bankier.pl, [Oct 1]). Orlen cut wholesale prices, diesel at 9.14 zł and petrol 8.19 zł on forecourts (Faktura.pl, [Oct 1]), is reviewing its strategy with RES investment in question (Interia Biznes, [Oct 1]). Sołowow raised 500 mln zł from Polish entrepreneurs for SMRs and said he regrets working with Orlen (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 1]); the first large nuclear plant slips three years (GlobEnergia, [Oct 1]). KGHM finished the Głogów smelter overhaul early (wnp.pl, [Oct 1]) and says Sierra Gorda repays its debt in about four years at current prices (wnp.pl, [Oct 1]). Allegro now delivers half its orders (wirtualnemedia.pl, [Oct 1]) as Temu loses users (Bankier.pl, [Oct 1]); Dino launched a Saturday promotion aimed at Biedronka and Lidl shoppers (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Oct 1]); LPP runs over 1600 Geekplus robots (e-biznes.pl, [Oct 1]). CD Projekt patched the Remaster within two days (PPE, [Oct 1]).

Geopolitics & Security: Putin warned Russia is ready to use "every weapon" to protect Kaliningrad (BBC, [Oct 1]) after Lavrov invoked nuclear doctrine against Poland's Patriot plan (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Sep 29]); Kubilius said a Russian attack on an arms factory would trigger a NATO military response (POLITICO.eu, [Oct 1]) and a Polish general called Russia unable to invade but "constantly escalating" (TVP World, [Oct 1]).

New / Most Important: The bank rout is the tell. An RPP member saying tightening is "forced" and bonds "cheap again" on the same day means the market is now pricing hikes into a sector that led the rally; Citi's Allegro downgrade and DM PKO BP's Orlen call show the leadership narrowing to PGE.  |  Playbook ✓: Cut bank exposure to neutral until the October RPP, keep PGE and CD Projekt momentum, start scaling into POLGBs only as Parkiet's "cheap again" meets the fuel-law price cut, and hold USD/PLN hedges above 3.86.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Record mortgage volumes versus the worst bank session in a month; the president signing the fuel law while sending it to the Tribunal.
