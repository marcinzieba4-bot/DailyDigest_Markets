🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: The week ends with the inflation debate reframed around fuel. FXMAG modelled diesel at 9 or 10 zloty and inflation of the order of 10% in a prolonged Middle East conflict ([Jul 24]), Energetyka24.com says the energy shock is feeding inflation with the full effects still ahead ([Jul 24]) and CEO Magazyn argued the oil shock could stop Polish rate cuts altogether ([Jul 23]). That is the backdrop to Kotecki's statement that inflation is diverging from the NBP projection and that a September cut cannot yet be promised (pb.pl, [Jul 23]), after one Council hawk called the earlier cuts premature (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 21]); Crédit Agricole's 25 bp September cut to 3.50% (PAP Biznes, [Jul 20]) now looks like the optimistic end of the range. The real economy is the hawks' ally: Rzeczpospolita's "chwilo trwaj" reads the complete data set as solid growth ([Jul 24]), retail sales rose 6.2% y/y against a 5.2% consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 22]), industrial production 7.6% (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 20]), ING BSK sees Q2 GDP near 3.8% y/y (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 22]) and unemployment fell in June (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 23]). Wages are the soft spot: June pay rose 5.9% y/y (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 20]) but growth has slowed to its lowest since 2021 (FOCUS ON Business, [Jul 23]). The broader European energy-shock dynamic argues for a long hold.

Markets: Friday's session was a rotation out of the week's winners: KGHM fell 3.97% while Orlen rose 2.62% (FXMAG, [Jul 24]), Pepco led the gainers and Modivo and PGE ended the week under pressure (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 24]). Business Insider Polska says Orlen's shares surprised in their reaction to President Nawrocki's decision on the windfall tax ([Jul 24]). The card, with Thursday's closes, still records a very strong week for energy: Orlen 155.86 (+6.8% 1W, +23.8% 4W) and PGE 10.6 (+14.2% / +8.4%), against KGHM 302.0 (-0.3% / -8.3%), Dino 28.86 (-1.9% / -1.5%), CD Projekt 226.3 (-3.5% / +1.3%), Allegro 44.88 (+1.2% / +19.5%), LPP 20300 (+1.3% / +10.0%), PKO 107.22 (-0.7% / +2.9%) and Pekao 232.9 (-0.2% / +1.3%); EPOL closed at 40.78, up 0.3% on the week and 7.3% over four weeks. The bond market is the week's loser: yields rose sharply on Friday even as the euro stayed stable (Bankier.pl, [Jul 24]), with German and French bonds at historic highs ([Jul 24]), a day after the Treasury raised more than 11.2 billion zloty at a successful auction (Bankier.pl, [Jul 23]). The zloty is trying to rebound with the dollar and euro losing on Friday (Comparic.pl, [Jul 24]) though FXMAG asks whether USD/PLN is heading for new records ([Jul 24]); the card has USD/PLN 3.8051 (+0.7% 1W, +1.0% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3303 (+0.1% / +1.2%).

Fiscal & Politics: President Nawrocki sent the new windfall tax to the Constitutional Tribunal, so the budget will not see the 4 billion zloty it was counting on (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 24]). The same day the finance ministry confirmed advanced variants of a 60 000 zloty tax-free allowance (Polskie Radio 24, Farmer.pl, [Jul 24]), TVN24 reports "various variants" on the table ([Jul 24]) and Bankier quotes the government's "we will not win if we do not deliver this" ([Jul 24]). The arithmetic is unforgiving: State Treasury debt exceeds 2.1 trillion zloty (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 24]), xyz.pl marks the historic moment of debt passing 60% of GDP ([Jul 24]) after Eurostat's 61.6% (XYZ, [Jul 21]), and the ministry has already ruled out public-sector pay rises for several years (Super Biznes, [Jul 21]). Bolesta pushed back on earmarking 50% of ETS revenue for decarbonisation, saying the finance minister knows best where to invest (Bankier.pl, [Jul 24]), and the president renewed his bid for a referendum on EU climate policy (Notes From Poland, [Jul 24]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen is in the political crossfire: its deal opening the Polish market to Ukraine's Nova Post enraged the right as "anti-Polish economic sabotage" (Money.pl, Do Rzeczy, [Jul 24]), Onet calls a PiS deputy's claim of fuel sold to Ukraine at 3 zloty a manipulation ([Jul 24]) and the company explained again why its Ukrainian prices differ (Rzeczpospolita, Super Biznes, [Jul 24]). It also signed a critical-infrastructure agreement with the fire service after reports Russia could attack it (XYZ, [Jul 24]). KGHM's shares rose on its June data earlier in the week (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 24]) before Friday's drop. CD Projekt has set the date for presenting the third Witcher 3 expansion (CrypS.pl, [Jul 24]). Dino sued the head of the Konfederacja Pracy union, which Głos Wielkopolski calls an attempt to gag unions ([Jul 24]). Banks are tempting with lower mortgage rates but not instant credit (Bizblog.pl, [Jul 24]); the state has only begun counting free-credit cases where banks may earn no interest (Money.pl, [Jul 24]), and the signed CHF law also covers foreign borrowers (Wprost, [Jul 24]).

Geopolitics & Security: There is now a queue for Poland's MiG-29s beyond Ukraine (Forsal.pl, [Jul 24]), Poland will use Ukraine's war experience to modernise its army (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Jul 24]). Poland is bidding to host a Patriot factory though a rocket-motor shortage keeps output years away (Tech Times, [Jul 24]); Belarusian special forces remain at the Ukrainian border (Forsal.pl, [Jul 23]).

New / Most Important: The veto-by-tribunal is the week's development. Blocking the windfall tax removes 4 billion zloty from a budget whose debt just passed 2.1 trillion zloty and 60% of GDP, on the day the ministry confirmed it is costing a 60 000 zloty tax-free allowance. Bond yields rising sharply on Friday is the market's answer; Orlen rising on the same news is the equity market's. Those are the same trade from opposite sides: Orlen keeps the profit the state cannot tax.

Playbook ✓: Hawkish hold consistent with CEE central banks facing imported energy inflation; energy over banks and short duration remain the positions the week rewarded.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A government promising tax relief into a 60%-of-GDP debt while its own windfall tax is blocked is fiscal loosening by default, yet EUR/PLN sits at 4.33 as if nothing happened; the yield move says the bond market has stopped waiting for the currency.
