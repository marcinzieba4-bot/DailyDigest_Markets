🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The consumer has become the soft spot. GUS put August retail sales at +3.8% y/y in constant prices against a 4.5% PAP consensus, with a 0.3% monthly fall where +0.3% was expected (wnp.pl, [Sep 22]); Business Insider Polska said fuel rescued the headline [Sep 22], e-commerce grew 17.5% (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Sep 22]) and ING described two-speed spending favouring durables over essentials [Sep 22]. Rzeczpospolita put real wage growth at its lowest in three years [Sep 22] after wages rose 5.6% while employment fell 0.8% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 21]), and PAP Biznes read slowing pay plus rising inflation as pressure on consumers [Sep 22]. Prices have not relented: August CPI was confirmed at 3.4%, the highest in 14 months (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]), diesel hit PLN 8 per litre (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 22]) and pb.pl counted more voices for a hike inside the RPP [Sep 22] after an MPC member told Bloomberg an increase is possible on energy risks [Sep 18]; FXMAG relayed a bank warning of an inflation surge in the fourth quarter [Sep 22] and a Money.pl interview warned the NBP faces paralysis at the top with 50 days left [Sep 22]. Growth still holds: ING BSK sees third-quarter GDP near 3.5% (wnp.pl, [Sep 22]).

Markets: After Monday's record close, Tuesday was a pause: the WIG20 added 0.09% (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 22]), Parkiet called it consolidation at the top of the bull market [Sep 22] and XYZ said the exchange was catching its breath [Sep 22]. Pepco led with Modivo and LPP strong (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 22]), LPP set another all-time high (DNA Rynków, [Sep 22]) while PGE and Orlen fell (Portal spożywczy, [Sep 22]). The card confirms the bank bid: PKO at 123.1 is up 2.6% on the week and 8.8% over four weeks, Pekao at 279.2 up 4.3% and 8.4%; PGE at 14.41 carries a 41.1% four-week move, KGHM at 344.9 rebounded 3.3% on the week, LPP (24180, +10.9% 1W, +17.6% 4W) and Allegro (49.43, +7.9% 1W) hold their gains, while Orlen at 152.48 is still down 6.2% on the week. EPOL closed at 45.67, up 1.2% on the week. The zloty ignored the rating cut: EUR/PLN slipped below 4.35 and the dollar back under 3.79 (FXMAG, [Sep 22]); the card shows USD/PLN at 3.7871 (+0.9% 1W, +2.7% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3411 (+0.1% 1W, +0.8% 4W). POLGBs have calmed since the 10-year broke 6.5% (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Sep 15]): Parkiet says yields are braking [Sep 21] and marketscreener reported Polish bonds shrugging off the rating action [Sep 21].

Fiscal & Politics: The Moody's cut to A3, the first since 2016 (Bloomberg, [Sep 18]), is rippling into the banks: Moody's lowered PKO BP's unsecured debt rating from A3 to Baa1 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 22]). The government's answer is taxes rather than cuts. Cabinet adopted PIT and CIT changes under which lump-sum payers, entrepreneurs and landlords lose (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 22]), a VAT rise on beverages (Wyborcza.biz, [Sep 22]), on top of the fuel windfall tax the Sejm passed a second time (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]) and an alcohol excise rise (Money.pl, [Sep 21]). TEP appealed to the ministry to respect the Fiscal Council's independence (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 21]). The backdrop is a deficit of 151.1 billion zł after August (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 16]) and debt approaching the threshold that would hit pension indexation (Forsal.pl, [Sep 22]). Politically, Nawrocki said he is very disappointed with Tusk (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 22]), keeping the fuel-price standoff alive.

Corporate & Sectors: Allegro completed Phase I of its buyback with 17.88 million shares for close to 800 million zł and launched Phase II for up to 800 million zł (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 22]) after lifting the cap from 45 to 50 zł (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 21]). KGHM reported August copper sales of 53.2 thousand tonnes, down 3% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 22]), and plans a first-of-its-kind mine near Puck with resources estimated at 300 million tonnes (Money.pl, [Sep 22]). PGE collected 135 million zł from the Turów unit settlement (Parkiet, [Sep 22]), said first power from Baltica 2 will flow in the second quarter of 2027 with 111 foundations installed (Bankier.pl, [Sep 22]). Orlen signed LNG and fuel memoranda with Naftogaz worth billions of zloty (TVN24, [Sep 21]), while Czechia restores a fuel price cap with a windfall tax on Orlen (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 22]). In banking, ING Bank Śląski became the second lender to drop WIBOR early and will offer POLSTR mortgages this month (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 22]), ZBP's chief said 30 thousand lawsuits have been filed as claim-buyers aggregate cases (Bankier.pl, [Sep 22]) and PKO BP saw consumer loans up 10% y/y in August (wnp.pl, [Sep 22]). Developers started the most new homes in four years (Bankier.pl, [Sep 22]).

Geopolitics & Security: NATO jets were scrambled over Poland and Latvia during another Russian attack and thousands of Poles received emergency alerts (The Mirror, [Sep 22]); Euromaidan Press reported a drone reached the Redzikowo missile shield this month [Sep 22]. The defence ministry has 23 shelters ready on the Ukraine border and is adding air defence (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 22]), with Patriots deployed at the crossings (Strefa Obrony, [Sep 22]). European intelligence officials forecast a Russian attack on NATO in months, not years (19FortyFive, [Sep 22]) and Sikorski told Moscow that Poland has more combat aircraft (uatv.ua, [Sep 22]).

New / Most Important: The retail miss matters more than its size suggests. Alongside the weakest real wage growth in three years and falling employment, it shows the Polish consumer, the pillar of the GDP story, absorbing the fuel shock just as the RPP debates hiking into it. The government is answering with higher VAT, PIT and excise rather than spending restraint, which taxes the same consumer; equities can run on bank margins and LPP's execution, but the domestic demand leg of the bull case is weakening.  |  Playbook ✓: Keep bank and quality-retail exposure while the WIG20 consolidates near highs; fade Orlen rallies while windfall and Czech levies stack up; the zloty's calm after the downgrade argues against chasing USD/PLN above 3.80.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: PGE up 41.1% in four weeks while its shares fell today; a consumer slowing in the data while e-commerce grows 17.5% and Allegro commits another 800 million zł to buybacks.
