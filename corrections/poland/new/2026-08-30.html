🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold at 3.75%, G20 week

Macro Stance: Domański and Glapiński fly to the US together for the G20 meeting that opens on Monday (Parkiet, [Aug 30]; TVN24, [Aug 30]), an unusual joint appearance in the week after a budget the Council will have to live with. Policy is parked at 3.75% with inflation pressure moderate (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]), but the direction of travel is not toward cuts: Subiektywnie o finansach reads Jackson Hole as a signal that rates will be higher [Aug 29] and Bankier.pl asks whether debt growth and expensive oil will reignite inflation [Aug 29]. Gazeta Prawna's economist lays out the consumer's year ahead as a 7.1% of GDP deficit, dearer food and unchanged rates [Aug 29]. The fuel programme ends with diesel above 8 zł a litre from September (Wyborcza.biz, [Aug 28]). The growth picture is investment-led, with Bankier.pl calling investment the economy's new engine [Aug 28] while the finance ministry warns of a slowdown [Aug 28] and Bloomberg reports the government itself sees growth down to 2% by 2030 on fiscal tightening [Aug 29].

Markets: The week's story was foreign money. Thursday's biggest sell-off in over two months (Bankier.pl, [Aug 27]) left Warsaw the weakest in Europe after the CIA chief's visit (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Aug 28]), yet BNY's flow data show EMEA emerging-market equity inflows led by Poland, South Africa and Turkey (VT Markets, [Aug 27]), so the selling was a reversal of fresh positioning rather than a long-standing exit. Friday was a draw (Parkiet, [Aug 28]) with Allegro the top gainer (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 28]). On the card, EPOL closed the week at 43.48 (-2.1% 1W, +1.5% 4W); CD Projekt 236.5 (-8.6% 1W, -5.4% 4W), Orlen 148.98 (-2.7% 1W), Pekao 255.5 (-1.3% 1W) and PKO 113.5 (-0.4% 1W) lost, while PGE 10.89 (+6.9% 1W, +7.7% 4W), KGHM 362.0 (+5.7% 1W, +17.6% 4W), Dino 35.56 (+2.2% 1W, +11.2% 4W) and LPP 21,000 (+1.0% 1W) gained. The zloty is under geopolitical pressure with the euro testing 4.34 (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]) and USD/PLN heading for 3.75 after Jackson Hole (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]); the card has USD/PLN 3.7164 (+0.7% 1W, -0.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3303 (+0.4% 1W, +0.6% 4W). POLGBs: 10-year yields are at levels last seen in May (Interia Biznes, [Aug 28]) and CEO Magazyn argues the peaks are not about the budget [Aug 28].

Fiscal & Politics: Bloomberg's framing is that Poland walks a fiscal tightrope with an election-year budget [Aug 28]: a 282.6 billion zł state deficit, 7.1% of GDP for the general government, growth of 3.6% this year and 3% in 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, Reuters, [Aug 28]). The weekend commentary is about limits: Gazeta Prawna says debt is a hair from a key threshold [Aug 29], wm.pl asks whether a line Poland has never crossed will fall [Aug 30], Obserwator Gospodarczy says the deficit breaks further records [Aug 30] and Money.pl expects it to exceed the government's own forecast [Aug 29]; ING's verdict stays not great, not terrible, a sixth year of excessive deficit [Aug 28]. The spending detail is now visible: almost 5.8 billion zł for housing (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 30]; Money.pl, [Aug 30]), 16 billion zł for rail stations and key lines (Portal Samorządowy, [Aug 30]), close to 20 billion zł for energy including nuclear and storage (Portal Samorządowy, [Aug 28]), plus the EU contribution rising by billions (Money.pl, [Aug 29]). Nawrocki vetoed three of seven bills (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]) and the fuel-tax programme's end is being blamed on him by the energy minister (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 29]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks are the paradox of the week: a historic half-year profit and 2.1 trillion in deposits (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]), a mortgage boom WNP.PL calls unseen since 2007 [Aug 28], and shares that fell with the index. The regulatory tail is thickening: KNF warns free-credit sanction risk is rising fast (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 28]) and lawyers are warning borrowers about POLSTR annexes to loan agreements (Olsztyn.com.pl, [Aug 30]). KGHM is the world's largest silver producer with 712 tonnes in half a year (Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 28]) and leads the four-week table. Orlen's Baltic Power is 89% complete (WysokieNapiecie.pl, [Aug 28]), ARP, Orlen and PGE are joining forces on Baltic contracts (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 28]) and Orlen opened an unprecedented truck charger on the A1 (Auto Świat, [Aug 30]). CD Projekt has good news on Witcher 4 for players (Telepolis.pl, [Aug 30]) ahead of its half-year report.

Geopolitics & Security: An RCB alert woke the Lublin region overnight as a massed air attack hit western Ukraine (Lublin112, [Aug 30]). Meduza reports Western officials fear Putin is preparing to escalate beyond Ukraine, with Bild and The Telegraph outlining possible moves against NATO [Aug 30]. Tusk's warning that the next seven to eight months are critical (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Aug 29]) stands. US instructors are coaching Polish forces in a drone-focused drill drawn from Ukraine (AP News, [Aug 30]) and Polish troops joined US-led drone warfare drills in Germany (TVP World, [Aug 30]).

New / Most Important: The finance minister and the central bank governor travelling together to the G20 is the week's tell: Poland is presenting a 7.1% deficit and a 3.75% policy rate as one package to the people who fund it, days after foreign money sold Warsaw on a security scare. With BNY showing Poland led EM inflows into the sell-off, the bid is there, but it is conditional on the debt threshold holding and the next seven to eight months passing without the escalation Tusk is warning of.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold with a defensive tilt; PGE, KGHM and Dino as the cores that outperformed through the shock, banks on earnings not on the index, PLN hedged against the euro, duration short until yields stop making highs.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The government budgets billions for housing on top of a 2007-style mortgage boom and record household debt: fiscal stimulus is being added to the one sector already running hot, and the KNF's sanction warning is the cost of that choice arriving early.
