🇵🇱 Poland  Bearish  → hold at 3.75%, fiscal and security overhang

Macro Stance: Rates stay at 3.75% with inflation pressure described as moderate (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]), but the cut case is weakening on both sides. From Jackson Hole, Subiektywnie o finansach reads an important signal that rates will be higher [Aug 29], and Bankier.pl asks whether debt growth and expensive oil will reignite inflation, too much too fast [Aug 29]. Gazeta Prawna's economist sums up what awaits consumers as a 7.1% of GDP deficit, dearer food and unchanged rates [Aug 29]. The fuel cushion is going: the tax-cut programme is ending and the energy minister blames the president (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 29]) and diesel returns above 8 zł a litre from September (Wyborcza.biz, [Aug 28]). Growth is still fine for now, with investment the new engine of GDP (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]), but the finance ministry itself warns the fast phase may be ending (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]) and Bloomberg reports the government sees growth slowing to 2% by 2030 on fiscal tightening [Aug 29].

Markets: The week ended in a draw (Parkiet, [Aug 28]) after Thursday's panic, the biggest sell-off in over two months (Bankier.pl, [Aug 27]) that Obserwator Gospodarczy tied to the CIA chief's mysterious visit, with Warsaw the weakest in Europe [Aug 28]. Friday was calmer with light gains (XYZ, [Aug 28]): Allegro rose most while Modivo and Kruk closed lower (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 28]), CD Projekt bounced after a Gamescom award following a dreadful week (CrypS.pl, [Aug 28]) and the Fed chair slightly spoiled the mood (Parkiet, [Aug 28]). The card shows the week's scorecard: EPOL 43.48 (-2.1% 1W, +1.5% 4W), CD Projekt 236.5 (-8.6% 1W, -5.4% 4W), Orlen 148.98 (-2.7% 1W), Pekao 255.5 (-1.3% 1W) and PKO 113.5 (-0.4% 1W) on the losing side; PGE 10.89 (+6.9% 1W, +7.7% 4W), KGHM 362.0 (+5.7% 1W, +17.6% 4W), Dino 35.56 (+2.2% 1W, +11.2% 4W) and LPP 21,000 (+1.0% 1W) held up. The zloty was hit but not like the forint (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]): the euro is the dearest in over a month, EUR/PLN neared 4.34 and USD/PLN is heading for 3.75 after the dollar rose on Jackson Hole (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]); the card has USD/PLN 3.7164 (+0.7% 1W, -0.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3303 (+0.4% 1W, +0.6% 4W). POLGBs are the pressure point: 10-year yields are at levels unseen since May (Interia Biznes, [Aug 28]) and CEO Magazyn insists the new yield peaks are not about the budget [Aug 28].

Fiscal & Politics: The weekend is for digesting Friday's draft: a 282.6 billion zł state deficit, a 7.1% of GDP general government gap, growth of 3.6% this year and 3% in 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, Reuters, [Aug 28]). The reviews are harsh. Gazeta Prawna says debt is a hair from crossing a key threshold [Aug 29], Money.pl argues the deficit will be bigger than the government projects [Aug 29], Rzeczpospolita quotes an economist calling it an insanely risky plan [Aug 28], Business Insider Polska carries the line that the government is testing markets' patience [Aug 28] and ING lands on not great, not terrible, a sixth year of excessive deficit [Aug 28]. Domański admits a significant rise in debt-servicing costs (Interia Biznes, [Aug 28]), billions are to flow from state-owned companies in 2027 (Interia Biznes, [Aug 29]) and Poland's EU contribution rises by billions (Money.pl, [Aug 29]). The mix is electoral, more on health and less on defence (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]), the alcohol excise rise returns (Money.pl, [Aug 28]) and Tusk cooled young people's hopes of relief after 26 (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 29]). Nawrocki vetoed three of seven bills (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]) and the minister called for the president to come to his senses (Onet, [Aug 28]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks closed the half with a historic profit and 2.1 trillion in deposits, rebounding after years of CHF uncertainty (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]), and WNP.PL calls the mortgage boom a shock unseen since 2007 [Aug 28] and FXMAG notes Poles are borrowing heavily just to buy a flat [Aug 29]. The risks are regulatory: KNF warns free-credit sanction exposure is rising fast (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 28]), and POLSTR annexes need not be signed as there is no corrective spread (Frankowicze.net, [Aug 29]). KGHM is the world's silver leader with 712 tonnes in half a year (Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 28]). Orlen's Baltic Power is 89% complete (WysokieNapiecie.pl, [Aug 28]) and Orlen opened Poland's first public megawatt charger for trucks (Biznes Alert, [Aug 29]). The government plans nearly 20 billion zł for energy in 2027 including nuclear and storage (Portal Samorządowy, [Aug 28]). Retail chains are expected to contribute 5 billion zł to the budget (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 28]); CD Projekt says Witcher 4 is already playable (Telepolis.pl, [Aug 28]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk warned that Russia is preparing a new phase of escalation and the next seven to eight months are critical (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Aug 29]), that provocations cannot be ruled out against any NATO member (LIGA.net, [Aug 29]) and discussed them with Rutte (UA.NEWS, [Aug 29]); The Telegraph says Russia has several scenarios for an attack on NATO (UA.NEWS, [Aug 29]). Sikorski urged faster defence preparation (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Aug 28]) and police hold a Ukrainian and a Latvian suspected of testing NATO's reaction (Kresy24.pl, [Aug 28]).

New / Most Important: Tusk has put a clock on the security risk that sank the market on Thursday: seven to eight critical months, running through the same window in which a 7.1% deficit must be financed at 10-year yields already at their highest since May and with fuel relief expiring. The budget's own long-range forecast of 2% growth by 2030 concedes the tightening is coming either way.  |  Playbook ✓: Stay defensive; keep KGHM, PGE and Dino as the cores that held through the sell-off, avoid Orlen and CD Projekt until the tax and timeline overhangs clear, stay short duration, hedge PLN against the euro.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Banks just printed a historic profit and a 2007-style mortgage boom while their index peers fell on the week: the sector is being sold on sovereign and sanction risk, not on earnings, and that is where the rebound starts if the security window passes quietly.
