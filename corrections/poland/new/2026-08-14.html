🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold

Macro Stance: The hawkish side of the ledger got heavier. NBP's core inflation measure rose in July to its highest level in ten months (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]), which Super Biznes reads as an important signal for the RPP before its next rate decision [Aug 14]; that follows yesterday's GUS confirmation of July CPI at 3.0% with fuel up 13.9% in a month (Bankier.pl and 300Gospodarka.pl, [Aug 13]) and the flash estimate of Q2 GDP growth at 3.8% y/y and 0.9% q/q (PAP Biznes, [Aug 13]). Activity is not the problem: Super Biznes says Europe is braking while Poland races, with Polish industry third in the EU [Aug 14], Money.pl puts European industrial growth near absolute zero [Aug 14], and xyz.pl says the economy keeps its winning streak [Aug 14]. The dovish counterweight is wages, where xyz.pl argues the boom is running out of steam [Aug 13] after GUS reported Q2 pay growth of 5.5% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 10]), and ING's record primary-income deficit in the current account [Aug 13]. With core turning up, Citi's call that inflation leaves no room for a cut at the next meeting (PAP Biznes, [Aug 10]) is now the consensus; nothing in the feed suggests a hike is on the table.

Markets: A quiet, holiday-thinned session: main indices barely moved with KGHM and the energy names lower (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 14]) while banks led the gainers (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 14]), a day after the whole WIG20 closed red on a KGHM-and-Orlen drag (Bankier.pl and Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 13]) and Parkiet asked whether the bull run is short of breath [Aug 13]. The card, at Thursday's close, shows the pullback: EPOL at 43.96 is -0.2% on the week but still +8.2% over four weeks; Orlen at 151.04 is -1.1% 1W after the fuel-cap decision, LPP at 21,620 -1.5% 1W, Pekao at 255.0 -0.7% 1W and PKO at 109.42 -0.2% 1W despite record results. Four-week leadership is intact: KGHM at 355.8 is +17.4% 4W, PGE at 10.95 +18.0% 4W, Dino at 32.71 +11.1% 4W, CD Projekt at 261.2 +5.1% 1W and +11.3% 4W. The zloty is a touch weaker: USD/PLN closed at 3.7353 (+0.1% 1W, -1.1% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3084 (+0.3% 1W, -0.5% 4W); Comparic sees the zloty stabilising [Aug 14] and FXMAG relays a bank forecast for a lower euro [Aug 14]. POLGB coverage is thin: the ministry sold PLN 10.72 billion at this week's switch tender (TradingView, [Aug 12]) and Parkiet links rising oil to rising yields [Aug 13]; Bankier.pl notes an analyst view that the new OKI accounts could channel savings into corporate bonds [Aug 14].

Fiscal & Politics: The fuel programme now has a price tag and a shape. The return of CPN as a lower VAT rate on fuel will cost the budget about 495 mln zł (XYZ, [Aug 13]), it runs for two weeks (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Aug 14]), and the energy minister set maximum prices with Pb95 capped at 6.41 zł from Monday (e-magazyny.pl and Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]); Notes From Poland describes the package as a VAT cut plus daily price caps [Aug 14], while RadioMaryja carries Horbaczewski's view that it merely moves money from one pocket to another [Aug 14] and a former finance minister warns about Orlen and a windfall tax (Biznes Wprost, [Aug 14]). On the tax side the government is preparing a gift of several hundred zł in raises via a new middle-class threshold (Business Insider Polska and Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 14]), but INFOR.PL reports the full 60 thousand zł tax-free allowance would cost over 60 bn zł [Aug 14], Tusk has warned ministers there is less money for election promises (Interia Biznes, [Aug 12]) and Radio Wnet says the debt picture is worse than it looks [Aug 14]. The ministry is tightening family-foundation rules from the new year (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]), and Notes From Poland frames the newly signed Personal Investment Accounts as an investment-boosting tax shelter [Aug 14].

Corporate & Sectors: Banks are the story. PKO BP's 2.77 bn zł Q2 and 5.29 bn zł first-half profit (StockWatch.pl and Parkiet, [Aug 13]) came with guidance that 2026 could be its strongest year (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]) and Interia notes the bank is positioned as the main capital provider for the investment upturn [Aug 14]; the risk side is 7,666 free-credit-sanction lawsuits against PKO with no reserve (CHF24, [Aug 14]) and Interia's warning that demographics are a blow to the sector [Aug 14]. WIBOR-clause challenges are declining (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Aug 14]). Orlen is under political fire for earning on fuel despite CPN, with PiS demanding explanations (RadioMaryja, [Aug 14]), after FXMAG noted the shares had turned and the group is pushing into Germany without a refinery there [Aug 12]. KGHM faces Congo's restriction of mineral exports (XYZ, [Aug 14]) and a management shake-up at KGHM ZANAM [Aug 14]. CD Projekt's Sirius layoffs span Poland and the US (PC Format, [Aug 14]) and a competitor's collapse on results pulled CDR lower (FXMAG, [Aug 14]). Energy: the heatwave lifted power demand 28% with photovoltaics preventing a crisis (Gramwzielone.pl, [Aug 14]), dynamic-price contracts grew 36-fold in a year [Aug 14], and the signed nuclear amendment could bring the plant forward by two years (GlobEnergia, [Aug 14]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Russian hit plot against an American in Warsaw that Tusk disclosed (London Business News, [Aug 14]; The Guardian, [Aug 13]) was followed by a Moscow court giving a Russian 23 years for spying for Poland (Global Banking & Finance Review, [Aug 14]). Forsal reports the army's target of half a million soldiers and reservists [Aug 14], NATO plans thousands of AI drones along the Russian and Belarusian borders (UNITED24 Media, [Aug 14]), Wyborcza's deputy interior minister interview admits civil defence was asleep at night and promises changes [Aug 14], and Cohort shares rose 5.8% on a Polish submarine contract win (Investing.com, [Aug 14]).

New / Most Important: Core inflation at a ten-month high is the week's decisive datapoint: it removes the argument that July's 3.0% was only fuel, and arrives as the government reintroduces a fuel VAT cut that flatters the next headline print at a 495 mln zł cost. For the RPP that is the worst mix: a true inflation signal plus a fiscal measure that hides it.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold stays the base case; banks leading on a down day, with PKO's record half and a 2026 best-year call, confirms the rotation into lenders.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Orlen -1.1% on the week and under PiS fire for CPN-era profits while the state caps its prices; and the tax-free allowance plan priced above 60 bn zł beside a debt snowball the Fiscal Council says is out of time, a gap bonds, not equities, will arbitrate.
