🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold

Macro Stance: The week closed with the September cut demoted from base case to open question. Kotecki said the inflation situation is diverging from the NBP projection and it is too early to declare a September cut (pb.pl, [Jul 23]), Business Insider Polska reads the RPP as in no hurry ([Jul 23]) and wnp.pl calls it a big change from what was supposed to happen on rates ([Jul 23]), against Crédit Agricole's call for a 25 bp cut to 3.50% as the terminal rate (PAP Biznes, [Jul 20]) and a Council member who still does not exclude a cut this year (pb.pl, [Jul 20]). Fuel is the swing factor: CEO Magazyn says the oil shock may stop Polish cuts ([Jul 23]), FXMAG modelled diesel at 9 or 10 zloty and inflation near 10% in a long Middle East conflict ([Jul 24]), petrol has passed its March peak though consumers surprise with optimism (pb.pl, [Jul 22]), and Saturday's wholesale list shows a heavy hit to diesel alongside cuts in premium petrol (Super Biznes, [Jul 25]). Food pulls the other way, with farm-gate pig prices down 27.6% and milk 22.7% (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 22]). The activity data are strong but not clean: retail sales rose 6.2% y/y against a 5.2% consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 22]), industrial production 7.6% (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 20]), ING BSK sees Q2 GDP near 3.8% y/y (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 22]) and unemployment fell in June (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 23]), yet wage growth has slowed to its lowest since 2021 (FOCUS ON Business, [Jul 23]) and pb.pl asks why GDP is rising while vacancies fall ([Jul 25]). The broader European energy shock narrative from the US-Iran conflict applies as a second-order risk to Polish inflation and the NBP's reaction function.

Markets: The WIG20 spent the week pressing on 3900 points without breaking it (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 23]) and Business Insider Polska had the index close to an all-time record with Orlen breaking its historic barrier again ([Jul 23]). The sector split was stark: PGE rose 7.37% and Tauron 9.54% on Thursday (FXMAG, [Jul 23]) while banks sank (XYZ, [Jul 23]); Friday brought KGHM down 3.97% and Orlen up 2.62% (FXMAG, [Jul 24]) with Pepco leading and Modivo and PGE finishing the week under pressure (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 24]). The card, with Friday's closes, confirms an energy week: Orlen 156.0 (+6.7% 1W, +26.4% 4W), PGE 10.48 (+10.5% / +9.2%), KGHM 303.5 (+3.3% / -7.4%), Allegro 44.74 (+3.1% / +20.3%), LPP 20140 (+0.9% / +10.0%), Pekao 233.7 (+2.1% / +2.1%), PKO 106.58 (-0.2% / +2.9%), Dino 28.78 (-1.3% / -0.7%) and CD Projekt 226.2 (-2.6% / +4.0%); EPOL closed at 40.92, up 1.4% on the week and 6.1% over four weeks. Bonds were the weak leg: yields rose sharply on Friday while the euro stayed stable (Bankier.pl, [Jul 24]), German and French yields hit historic highs ([Jul 24]), and the Treasury's successful auction that raised more than 11.2 billion zloty (Bankier.pl, [Jul 23]) looks like good timing. The zloty tried to rebound on Friday with the dollar and euro losing (Comparic.pl, [Jul 24]); the card has USD/PLN at 3.8051 (+0.7% 1W, +1.0% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3303 (+0.1% / +1.2%).

Fiscal & Politics: Two decisions in two days define the fiscal mood. The Sejm voted a new tax on savings from 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 25]), and President Nawrocki sent the windfall tax to the Constitutional Tribunal, denying the budget 4 billion zloty (Rzeczpospolita, Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 24]); Tusk's answer was "remember this at the pumps" (TVN24, [Jul 25]). Meanwhile the ministry is working on advanced variants of a 60 000 zloty tax-free allowance (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 24]) and Zero.pl asks whether a budget can bear a pre-election allowance rise ([Jul 25]). The constraint is visible: State Treasury debt exceeds 2.1 trillion zloty (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 24]) and Eurostat has public debt at 61.6% of GDP, the third-fastest increase in the EU (XYZ, [Jul 21]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen's week ended in politics: its cooperation with Ukraine's Nova Post drew "scandal" from Janusz Kowalski (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 24]), the Ukraine fuel-price row rumbles on (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 24]). ZE PAK secured up to 340 million zloty of investment credit from UniCredit (Bankier.pl, [Jul 25]). The CHF law is in the Journal of Laws, with instalments suspended by statute from early August and consequences for courts and euro borrowers too (Franknews.pl, [Jul 25]); banks are offering lower mortgage rates but no instant credit (Bizblog.pl, [Jul 24]). Dino opened another store in Środa Wielkopolska (GPŚ, [Jul 25]) a day after suing the head of the Konfederacja Pracy union (Głos Wielkopolski, [Jul 24]). CD Projekt has dated the presentation of its third Witcher 3 expansion (CrypS.pl, [Jul 24]). Household energy is getting dearer: pellet costs more than a year ago (Fakt, [Jul 25]) and house-building costs are rising fast again (Money.pl, [Jul 25]).

Geopolitics & Security: MON will launch a "Smart Border" system to protect Poland (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 25]) as Belarus moves special forces to the Ukrainian border (Forsal.pl, Strefa Obrony, [Jul 23]). There is a queue for Poland's MiG-29s beyond Ukraine (Forsal.pl, [Jul 24]) after an unnamed NATO country requested them (Цензор.НЕТ, [Jul 23]), and Poland is bidding for a Patriot factory despite a rocket-motor shortage (Tech Times, [Jul 24]).

New / Most Important: Fiscal policy is now being made by veto and by promise. In one week the president blocked 4 billion zloty of windfall revenue, the Sejm reached for savers from 2027 and the ministry costed a 60 000 zloty allowance, all with debt past 2.1 trillion zloty and bond yields at multi-year highs. The RPP's hawks did not need to argue; the politicians argued for them. That is why Kotecki's "too early" matters more than the 6.2% retail print.  |  Playbook ✓: Holding pattern as expected; the week rewarded energy over banks and short duration over long, and nothing in the weekend feed changes that.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Consumers ignoring petrol above its March peak and a WIG20 a step from a record sit against yields at highs and a tax on savings; the equity market is pricing Orlen's untaxed profits, the bond market the budget that will not get them.
