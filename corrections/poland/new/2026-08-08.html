🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: The NBP remains in a holding pattern, and the weekend adds no reason to move. Inflation is back at 3% after the fuel spike that followed the end of the government shield (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Aug 1]); Glapiński is reported to want lower rates while experts object (Super Biznes, [Aug 3]) and analysts are debating a September cut (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 6]), but the central bank's attention this week was its dispute with the ECB over transferring profit to defence (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 7]). The real economy keeps outrunning the region: the manufacturing PMI rebounded with the first rise in employment in months (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 3]), Interia Biznes [Aug 5] says the energy shock has not stopped Poland and growth of up to 4.1% is possible, Eurostat shows retail sales growing much faster than the EU (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 6]), and Poland has become the EU's sixth-largest economy and is aiming at Switzerland next (Telegrafi, [Aug 8]). A study finds Ukrainians brought more money into Poland than Ukraine received in aid (Ukrainian news, [Aug 8]). Next week brings the scheduled Polish GDP and inflation releases (ING, [Aug 7]); until then, hold is the only live option and the PLN sits stable within the CEE complex.

Markets: The week ended with the record intact but the leaders tired. WIG20 fell on Friday while mWIG40 set a record on Orlen's and Modivo's results (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 7]), with Bankier [Aug 7] noting emotions cooling at the top and WIG20 defending 4000 points. Friday's closes on the card tell the story: Orlen 149.06 (+0.7% 1W, +6.5% 4W) after the market sold a record quarter (Comparic, [Aug 7]), PKO 109.0 (-2.6% 1W, -3.4% 4W) as the bank trade fades following analysts' cuts to bank recommendations (pb.pl, [Aug 6]), Pekao 252.9 (+3.0% 1W, +3.5% 4W). The week belonged to KGHM 352.95 (+14.6% 1W, +14.2% 4W) on the copper boom (Parkiet, [Aug 5]) and PGE 10.75 (+6.3% 1W, +14.7% 4W); consumer and growth names held up, with CD Projekt 256.1 (+2.5% 1W, +9.9% 4W) leading Friday's gains (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 7]), Allegro 46.21 (+16.0% 4W), Dino 32.44 (+15.0% 4W), LPP 22,220 (+14.7% 4W). EPOL 44.19 is +3.2% 1W and +9.5% 4W. FX: USD/PLN 3.7297 (-0.2% 1W, -1.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.2961 (-0.2% 1W, -0.7% 4W), the euro still fighting to break 4.30 (Bankier, [Aug 5]) and weak US labour data weighing on the dollar (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 7]). Bonds were the quiet winner: POLGBs rebounded at the start of August (Bankier, [Aug 7]) and households bought nearly 10 bn zł of retail Treasury bonds in July (Bankier, [Aug 7]).

Fiscal & Politics: wGospodarce [Aug 8] says the fate of the budget hangs on the EU SAFE loan, the week after pb.pl [Aug 4] reported the MF stitching together a 2027 budget with the largest deficit in the EU and pressure to cut taxes, and Bloomberg [Aug 4] reported income tax relief for millions being weighed before the election; Domański confirmed work on the higher tax-free allowance with the budget bill due in August (Pit.pl, [Aug 7]). On fuel, wGospodarce [Aug 8] says Domański is washing his hands of pump prices, while the OKI investment-account plan is the government's growth-capital answer: the minister insists it is not a new tax (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 7]) and PKO BP expects OKI assets above 100 bn zł by its third year (pb.pl, [Aug 6]). Strefa Inwestorów [Aug 7] asks whether Orlen's results bring a second attempt at a windfall tax.

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen decided on fuel prices, the first such move in months (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 8]), a day after posting 7.7 bn zł Q2 profit with record investment and a dividend (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 7]) and keeping its 36.3 bn zł 2026 capex target (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 7]); prosecutors indicted former managers over 1.5 bn zł of damage to the company (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 7]). Energy: the first power from offshore wind on the Baltic has flowed and the results surprise (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 8]), but Fakt [Aug 8] reports the Americans negotiating hard and asks whether the nuclear plant is delayed, after WNP.PL [Aug 7] warned of a huge risk hanging over the project; August power prices opened with a strong rebound (300Gospodarka.pl, [Aug 7]). Banks: Zero.pl [Aug 8] says lenders are announcing a change and a wave of credit refusals may be coming; the CHF law is now in force, suspending instalments on filing suit (Dziennik.pl, [Aug 7]). Retail: LPP grew revenue 18.5% and added 230 stores in a quarter (Omnichannel News, [Aug 7]) while fending off 40 mn dollar supplier claims (Well.pl, [Aug 7]); Dino's unions threaten a general strike (Dziennik.pl, [Aug 6]).

Geopolitics & Security: The US intelligence warning that Putin may test NATO with a limited attack on Poland or the Baltics, possibly within weeks, continues to reverberate (The Week and News.com.au, [Aug 8]; The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 7]); the Polish foreign minister wants allies to revisit intercepting Russian missiles over Ukraine (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Aug 6]), Ukrainians warn the Eastern Shield fortifications may fail (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 3]) and talks on a permanent US base continue (NDTV, [Aug 5]).

New / Most Important: Orlen's price decision. Two days after a record quarter the company moved on pump prices for the first time in months, with the finance minister stepping back from the issue and the windfall-tax question reopened. That is the margin-politics risk this report flagged crystallising: the energy-led leg of the rally now depends on how much of Orlen's profit the state wants back, directly or through the forecourt, and the market's "sell the facts" reaction already priced some of it.

Playbook ✓: Rates held; PLN stable within CEE complex — the POLGB rebound and KGHM are the week's validated trades.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: None today on rates, but the pairing of a budget that hangs on SAFE with a US warning of a limited Russian attack is the combination a 4000-point index is least prepared for.
