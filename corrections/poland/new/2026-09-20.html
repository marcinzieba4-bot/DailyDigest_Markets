🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: Going into the new week the NBP debate is about hikes, not cuts. August CPI was confirmed at 3.4% y/y (Fronda.pl, [Sep 15]), the highest in 14 months (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]), with fuel the driver (RadioMaryja.pl, [Sep 15]); core inflation rose again (TVN24, [Sep 16]). An MPC member warned Bloomberg a rate increase is possible on energy risks [Sep 18], Zarzecki maintains his hike call (FXMAG, [Sep 15]) and Magazyn MANAGER+ framed the weekend question as what the Fed, ECB, RPP and Russia together mean for the zloty [Sep 20]. Activity is mixed: industrial output rose 4.3% y/y in August but disappointed (PAP Biznes, [Sep 18]), while xyz.pl reported Polish services growth far outpacing the EU [Sep 20]. Labour cost growth slowed from 9.7% to 4.7% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 17]) and GUS put the gender pay gap at 23% within the same sector (Zero.pl, [Sep 20]).

Markets: The week ended with a triple-witching Friday of almost 6 billion zł in turnover and an LPP rally (FXMAG, [Sep 19]), though LPP only saved the GPW from a slump as mBank led the WIG20 lower (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 18]) and PZU fell under pressure on financials (ad-hoc-news.de, [Sep 19]). Earlier the index had sagged under PZU's dividend (Parkiet, [Sep 16]) and then rallied on Allegro's surge (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 17]). The card shows consumer names leading and state champions lagging: LPP at 24160 is up 12.1% on the week and 16.2% over four weeks, Allegro at 49.8 gained 10.8% and 10.4%, Dino at 35.74 added 1.0%, while Orlen at 151.14 lost 6.2% on the week, KGHM at 337.05 fell 4.2% and PGE at 13.93 slipped 0.7% but keeps a 36.7% four-week gain; EPOL closed at 44.41, down 2.5% on the week. The zloty remains the soft spot after the Fed: USD/PLN at 3.7934 (+1.9% 1W, +2.8% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3529 (+0.7% 1W, +0.9% 4W), with the euro at its highest this year and US rates now above Poland's (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]). In rates, the 10-year yield had broken 6.5%, its highest since 2007 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 15]), before easing after the Fed (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 17]); Bankier.pl now flags a record gap between bond yields and WIBOR as a problem for the market [Sep 20].

Fiscal & Politics: The weekend is being spent absorbing the Moody's cut to A3, the lowest since 2002 (Notes From Poland, [Sep 19]), which cited the high deficit and fast-growing debt (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 19]). The next shoe is the debt rule: TV Republika reports public debt ever closer to the 55% of GDP threshold [Sep 20], Money.pl warns pensions and wages face the knife as millions pay for runaway debt [Sep 20] and Tysol.pl quotes experts saying the country has hit a wall [Sep 20]. The state deficit stood at 151.1 billion zł after August (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 16]) and the Fiscal Council is at odds with the ministry over what Money.pl called an unfortunate reaction [Sep 19]; politics has turned to blame, Grabiec faulting the president and Przydacz the government (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 19]). On fuel, the Sejm re-passed the windfall tax (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]) as BiznesINFO.pl notes another rise in pump prices [Sep 20], and the president's margin-cap bill drew Orlen's reply that there would simply be no fuel (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 18]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen is now sounding the alarm that Poland could run short of fuel (WNP.PL, [Sep 20]) after the Saudis halted crude deliveries to Europe and the company went looking for barrels wherever it can (WysokieNapiecie.pl, [Sep 20]); it also reported Olefin III to prosecutors (Bankier.pl, [Sep 19]) and the stock had fallen more than 7% (Comparic.pl, [Sep 18]). The retail leaders remain the counterweight: LPP earned 768 million zł net and 1744 million zł of EBITDA (BIZNES24, [Sep 17]), with sales up 20% y/y so far this quarter and Sinsay back to positive like-for-like (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 18]); Allegro lifted its 2026 outlook and hit a five-year high (Reuters, [Sep 17]), and the FedEx-led consortium secured over 89% of InPost (Reuters, [Sep 18]). Strefa Biznesu revisited KGHM's 3 billion dollar foreign purchase, the largest in Polish history [Sep 20], while the board rejected the unions' bonus demand (MyGlogow, [Sep 19]). Dino backed down on tracking workers, which unions call a success (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 20]). Banks face TSUE-driven refunds of interest on commissions (Parkiet, [Sep 18]); salon24.pl says Europe is trembling before a hard winter [Sep 20].

Geopolitics & Security: European spy chiefs warn Moscow is planning more decisive action against NATO (The Guardian, [Sep 20]), NATO says it is ready for any Russian false-flag attack after Poland's warnings (TVP World, [Sep 20]). Kosiniak-Kamysz said Russian drones could reach Poland if Ukraine lacks interceptors (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Sep 20]), days after a Russian drone hit a train on the border (Reuters, [Sep 14]) and Polish fighters were again in the air on Saturday as Russian drones struck Ukraine (Radio ESKA, [Sep 19]). The army is building HESCO positions at the crossings (Nowiny 24, [Sep 17]) and GUS put the war's cost at 490 billion zł of GDP (o2, [Sep 18]).

New / Most Important: Fuel supply has joined fuel price as the problem. Orlen moving from reassurance to a shortage warning within days of the Aramco cut, while Sejm and president fight over how to tax its margins, adds a physical constraint to the fiscal one just as the debt rule starts to bite on pension and wage indexation. A fuel squeeze would hit CPI and the RPP's hike debate together. The GPW's answer has been to rotate into LPP, Allegro and the banks and out of the state champions, and nothing this weekend argues against that.  |  Playbook ✓: Keep the retail and bank overweight; avoid Orlen until supply, Olefin III and the windfall tax are resolved; stay short duration with the 10-year near 6.5% and the debt threshold in view; treat 3.80 on USD/PLN as a Fed line, not an NBP one.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Oil above 108 dollars yet Orlen down 6.2% on the week; services growth far outpacing the EU while the sovereign is rated its lowest since 2002.
