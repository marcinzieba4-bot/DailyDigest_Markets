🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hawkish hold

Macro Stance: The RPP left rates unchanged at its last decision, and Strefa Biznesu notes both the ECB and the RPP are holding with further moves tied to inflation pressure [Aug 19]. Today's GUS batch argues for patience: enterprise wages rose 6.8% y/y in July while employment fell 0.8% y/y (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 20]), the average wage reached 9509 zł (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 20]) and Parkiet called the beat a cold shower for the RPP [Aug 20]. Industrial output grew 5.1% y/y (PAP Biznes, [Aug 20]), credited by Parkiet to KPO projects while construction stayed below the line [Aug 20]. GUS confirmed July CPI at 3.0% with fuel up 13.9% in a month (Bankier.pl, 300Gospodarka.pl, [Aug 13]), core inflation hit a ten-month high (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]) and Eurostat put HICP at 3.1% (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 19]). Q2 GDP flash was 3.8% y/y and 0.9% q/q (PAP Biznes, [Aug 13]). Commerzbank's warning that the slow hawkish shift at the NBP is weighing on the zloty stands.

Markets: Warsaw is correcting but finding bids. Parkiet saw indices moving away from the bull-market peaks [Aug 19] after a third straight down day led by CD Projekt and KGHM (CrypS.pl, [Aug 19]); the tax plan then gave banks a tailwind with PKO BP up almost 5% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 19]). Today the WIG20 gave back part of Wednesday's gains with KGHM weakest (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 20]), PZU and Orlen the only gainers (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 20]), and Parkiet calling 4000 points a magnet [Aug 20]. The card shows the rotation: EPOL flat on the week at 44.6 but +7.8% over four weeks; PKO 115 (+4.7% 1W) and Pekao 260 (+10.2% 4W) lead, while KGHM 348 (-5.2% 1W), PGE 10.45 (-5.8% 1W), CD Projekt 256.5 (-3.7% 1W) and LPP 21,420 (-3.6% 1W) gave back summer gains; Dino 32.49 (-2.7% 1W, +7.4% 4W). The zloty is firm on a soft dollar: USD/PLN 3.6948 (-1.0% 1W, -2.5% 4W), with the dollar testing 3.70 and the euro below 4.32 (Comparic.pl, [Aug 20]); EUR/PLN 4.3131 (+0.2% 1W) sits at the 4.32 resistance FXMAG flagged [Aug 19]. POLGBs: the finance ministry raised over 13.4 billion zł on strong auction demand (Bankier.pl, [Aug 19]), yet Business Insider Polska argues AI firms competing for capital are lifting Poland's debt-service cost [Aug 20] and Hungarian bonds now yield less than Polish ones (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Aug 20]), with the US 30-year at 5.27%, highest since 2007 (pb.pl, [Aug 17]).

Fiscal & Politics: The tax package is the fiscal event. Tusk announced PIT changes under which 3.5 million taxpayers pay less (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 19]): a second threshold at 140 thousand zł (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 19]), a 24% rate and new bracket from 2027 (Parkiet, [Aug 20]), gains of up to 3600 zł a year (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 19]), funded by CIT at 30% for the largest companies (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 20]); ING Think called it fiscally neutral [Aug 20] and Gazeta Prawna put the taxpayer gain at 10 billion zł [Aug 20]. The politics are hostile: the Presidential Palace issued a long list of objections (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 20]), Newsweek judged a Nawrocki veto certain [Aug 20] and Mentzen called it a blow to small firms (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 19]). The deficit reached 141.1 billion zł after July (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 17]), smaller than a year earlier (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 17]); Tusk convened the cabinet on the budget (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 18]) and the Fiscal Council chief warned on debt (Money.pl, [Aug 19]). ING's calendar flagged a Fitch rating decision at the end of this week [Aug 17].

Corporate & Sectors: KGHM posted 5579 million zł of H1 net profit and 9198 million zł of adjusted EBITDA (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 20]), nearly tenfold on the year (TVP Wrocław, [Aug 20]), yet the shares fell (Comparic.pl, [Aug 20]); it is in an acquisition process looking at the Americas and Morocco (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 20]) and negotiating a 1 billion USD loan for Sierra Gorda (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 20]). Dino earned 400.1 million zł in Q2 against a 391.8 million consensus (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 20]), revenue up 10.5% with 341 more stores (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 20]), but LFL is near zero (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 20]). Orlen signed a three-year Equinor deal for 5-9 million tonnes of crude a year (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 20]), up to 25% of needs (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 20]); TVN24 expects the energy CIT to feed into power prices [Aug 20]. PKO BP was fined 47.5 million zł by KNF over structured deposits (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 18]) but won approval for portfolio management (Parkiet, [Aug 20]); XTB fell nearly 7% on the open KNF CFD review (TradingView, [Aug 18]). Mortgage demand is up more than a fifth per BIK (TVN24, [Aug 18]) with margins near multi-year lows (Bankier.pl, [Aug 20]); the CHF act took effect on Aug 7 (INFOR.PL, [Aug 18]).

Geopolitics & Security: Overnight a mass Russian strike killed 16 in Kyiv and Poland activated its air defences (AnewZ, [Aug 20]), with jets scrambled and RCB alerts in two eastern voivodeships (Radio ESKA, [Aug 20]). The Defence Ministry says it is preparing for war and expects a major provocation within months (UNITED24 Media, [Aug 17]), The Telegraph reported Russian drone bases within reach of NATO [Aug 16]. Tusk pledged Europe's largest army (The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 15]) and the Tarcza Wschód barrier is going up with German help (TVN24, [Aug 20]). The Patriot decision for Ukraine is pending (UNN, [Aug 18]).

New / Most Important: The tax package, not the data, is the pivot. Wages at 6.8% and industry at 5.1% confirm the economy runs hot into 3.0% CPI with rising core, so the RPP has no reason to cut; shifting the burden from households to the largest corporates lands on the sectors the WIG20 depends on, which is why banks rallied while energy and miners sold even as KGHM printed a record half. A near-certain veto leaves the fiscal path unresolved into the Fitch decision.  |  Playbook ✓: Hawkish hold intact; stay with banks on rates and the PIT boost, keep duration light while yields drift up, treat the firm zloty as a dollar story.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: KGHM's tenfold profit met a sell-off while PKO absorbed a 47.5 million fine and still led: the market is pricing the energy CIT, not earnings.
