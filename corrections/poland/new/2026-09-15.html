🇵🇱 Poland  Bearish  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: GUS confirmed August CPI at 3.4% y/y and 0.3% m/m (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 15]), the highest in 14 months (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]); transport prices rose 11.2% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 15]) while food fell for a fourth month, its fastest decline in a decade (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 15]). PAP Biznes warns that fuel-driven CPI raises the risk of second-round effects [Sep 15] and PKO BP expects inflation to rise in coming months (Portal spożywczy, [Sep 15]). The reaction function is shifting: a day after Glapiński tied stable rates to mid-2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 10]), an RPP member told Bankier.pl the Council could raise rates and named what will decide [Sep 15]. Commerzbank's cautious-NBP read stands, but the pressure now comes from both domestic inflation and the European energy shock: oil is at 108 USD and gas up 90% (FXMAG, [Sep 15]). External accounts are turning: China became Poland's largest goods supplier for the first time and the current account deficit widened to PLN 10.4bn (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 15]). The 2027 minimum wage is now official in the Journal of Laws (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 15]).

Markets: Equities are holding while rates markets break. The WIG20 closed down 0.02% on Tuesday (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 15]); Kęty stole the show while Orlen and KGHM held the index back (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 15]), Parkiet says profit-taking has begun [Sep 15], yet a UniCredit banker sees a bigger rally in soaring Polish equities (Bloomberg, [Sep 15]). The card at Monday's close: EPOL 45.14 (0.0% 1W, +1.6% 4W); KGHM 333.75 (-6.8% 1W, -3.3% 4W) the week's casualty; PGE 13.77 (+4.6% 1W, +32.8% 4W) into today's results; Orlen 162.6 (+2.5%, +6.9%) before the FT story; PKO 119.94 (+2.6%, +7.6%), Pekao 267.8 (+0.6%, +4.2%); LPP 21800 (+2.4%, +2.3%); Dino 35.0 (-3.5% 1W, +10.5% 4W); Allegro 45.8 (-0.5%, +1.6%); CD Projekt 237.3 (+0.3%, -8.0%). The zloty broke: USD/PLN closed at 3.7547 (+1.4% 1W, +0.9% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3386 (+0.7% 1W, +0.6% 4W); Bankier.pl says the euro hit resistance and the zloty may get weaker still [Sep 15]. POLGBs are the headline: the 10-year yield pierced 6.5% (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Sep 15]), the highest since 2007 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 15]) and a 3-1/2-year high (TradingView, [Sep 15]); pb.pl says the finance minister has reason to worry with yields at crisis levels [Sep 15].

Fiscal & Politics: The state budget deficit passed 150 bn zł after eight months (Money.pl and XYZ, [Sep 15]), 151 bn and still rising (Interia Biznes, [Sep 15]), with 10 bn added in August alone (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]); the MF holds over 13 bn euro on its FX accounts (Bankier.pl, [Sep 15]). The Fiscal Council answered the ministry's rebuke with "we cannot be blind" (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]) after warning of a very high risk of breaching the threshold (TOK FM, [Sep 15]). The government adopted a 60% windfall tax on fuel companies worth 4 bn zł for the budget (Parkiet, [Sep 15]), Tusk appealed to Nawrocki to sign (TVN24, [Sep 15]) and made a new CPN fuel scheme conditional on that signature (PAP, [Sep 15]). Polls show close to 40% of Poles favour cutting social spending to close the gap (TVN24, [Sep 15]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen is in a governance storm: the FT revealed a Venezuelan oil deal in which Orlen lost 1.6 bn zł (wnp.pl, [Sep 15]), 230 mln dollars paid with crypto on pendrives (Interia Biznes, [Sep 15]) for cargoes that never arrived (Wiadomości Onet, [Sep 15]); prosecutors issued a statement (Money.pl, [Sep 15]), Tusk called it a shame before the whole world (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 15]). Operationally, Orlen raised wholesale fuel prices to the year's high (TVN24, [Sep 15]), is buying cargoes from distant suppliers as Saudi deliveries are disrupted (wnp.pl, [Sep 15]). PGE delivered: H1 net profit of 4.05 bn zł against a 7.12 bn loss a year earlier (GospodarkaMorska.pl, [Sep 15]), over 2 bn zł in the quarter (Bankier.pl, [Sep 15]) and 4.9 bn zł of capex including 510 mln zł on Żarnowiec storage (e-magazyny.pl, [Sep 15]); power prices set a new record, the highest in 3 years (Wyborcza.biz, [Sep 15]), and energy costs are now critical for a fifth of firms (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 15]). Banks: the Financial Ombudsman demands full refunds of interest on fees (Bankier.pl, [Sep 15]) and WIBOR complaints to the RPO are rising (Radio Zachód, [Sep 15]). Retail: Spyrosoft built the Sinsay marketplace (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 15]) and Wiadomości Handlowe calls Dino's model resilient to food inflation [Sep 15]. Poles are piling into mortgage covered bonds (Super Biznes, [Sep 15]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland is reinforcing crossings with Ukraine (Defence24, [Sep 15]), with the army building fortifications at Medyka and Korczowa (Rzeszów Info, [Sep 15]) after the drone strike on a Warsaw-bound train (Reuters, [Sep 14]). NATO jets shot down a drone over Lithuania (BBC, [Sep 15]), a night RCB alert in Lublin region saw Polish fighters scrambled (Lublin24.pl, [Sep 15]) and Tusk warned of serious strikes in coming weeks and months (UNITED24 Media, [Sep 14]).

New / Most Important: The shift from easing bias to hike risk is now priced in the long end, not just the rhetoric: a 6.5% 10-year at 2007 highs, a 151 bn zł deficit, 3.4% CPI with fuel still feeding through and the zloty at 3.75. Add an Orlen scandal that lands on the day the government asks the president to sign a 60% windfall tax on the same company, and the state-asset trade that carried the WIG20 to records has a credibility problem.  |  Playbook ✓: CEE rates on hold amid inflation; banks remain the carry, PGE's results justify the rally, but Orlen moves to the watch list and duration stays short.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: UniCredit calls for a bigger equity rally while the bond market prices crisis-level yields; the WIG20 lost 0.02% on a day the 10-year pierced 6.5%.
