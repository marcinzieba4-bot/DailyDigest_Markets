🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: The hold is being extended in the forecasts rather than challenged. mBank now expects no rate changes until year-end, with GDP growing 3.7% in 2026 and inflation staying around 3% (Bankier.pl, wnp.pl, [Aug 11]), a day after Citi argued the inflation path may not permit a cut at the next RPP meeting (PAP Biznes, [Aug 10]); WNP.PL frames the coming rate decision as a set-up not seen for 12 months [Aug 11]. The price side turned friendlier today: shop prices have never risen so slowly and food inflation is at a historic minimum (TVN24, Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 11]), though Rzeczpospolita flags beverages rising sharply [Aug 11] and Strefa Biznesu warns fuel could reverse the trend [Aug 11]. Wages are cooling: the Q2 average was 9233.13 zł, up 5.5% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 10]), the slowest non-pandemic pace in a decade (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 10]). Activity is firmer, with economists seeing Poland shifting into second gear (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 11]) and more than a million foreigners now in work (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 11]), though only 13% of retailers expect higher Q3 sales (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Aug 11]). ING's calendar has Polish GDP and inflation due this week [Aug 7].

Markets: Another record day. The WIG20 and WIG set new highs while foreign markets wobbled (CrypS.pl, Business Insider Polska, [Aug 11]), Bankier.pl notes only the sWIG80 still lacks a record [Aug 11], but Parkiet saw weakness creep in at the end of a strong session [Aug 11]. PGE exploded higher while Budimex and PKO BP were among the biggest losers (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 11]), e-commerce names drew buyers and retail pulled the market up (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 11]), and pb.pl's read is that Orlen is unafraid of the tax talk with Allegro, Ryvu and Columbus in focus [Aug 11]. The card confirms the rotation: EPOL 44.16 (+2.8% 1W, +10.0% 4W); KGHM 358.8 (+14.7% 1W, +17.5% 4W) and Dino 33.56 (+3.4% 1W, +20.0% 4W) lead, Allegro 45.95 (+3.5% 1W), LPP 22,200 (+15.3% 4W), PGE 10.63 (+12.1% 4W) and CD Projekt 257.1 (+11.3% 4W) follow, Pekao 254.0 (+3.1% 1W) outpaces PKO 110.1 (-2.1% 1W, -1.5% 4W), and Orlen 150.06 (+0.7% 1W) is flat. The zloty rebounded today (Comparic.pl, [Aug 11]) after a soft Monday: USD/PLN 3.7228 (-0.6% 1W, -2.1% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.2989 (-0.2% 1W, -0.7% 4W); FXMAG's Q3 forecast calls for large EUR/PLN declines by year-end [Aug 11]. POLGBs rebounded in early August and households bought nearly 10 billion zł of savings bonds (Bankier.pl, [Aug 7]); Parkiet notes POLSTR is trading with a premium [Aug 11].

Fiscal & Politics: The tax package is taking shape ahead of the budget bill Domański promised for August (Pit.pl, [Aug 7]): PulsHR describes a PIT revolution worth up to 3600 zł a year for millions [Aug 11], while the ministry simultaneously tightens with a new PIT and CIT bill (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 11]). The constraint is unchanged: the largest deficit in the EU (pb.pl, [Aug 4]), tax relief that could cost 70 billion zł (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 9]), public spending at 50.9% of GDP (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 5]) and a budget that hangs on the SAFE loan (wGospodarce, [Aug 8]), with Poland asking for unused SAFE funds (Reuters, [Aug 10]). A new political problem surfaced today: a billion-zloty subsidy in the waste-trade affair around Elemental Holding, with Domański insisting not a single zloty has been paid and the contract will be cancelled if grounds emerge (Bankier.pl, TVN24, [Aug 11]). Mortgage instalments may be tied to the NBP rate, possibly for old contracts too (Direct Money, [Aug 11]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen decided on pump prices as oil rose sharply (WNP.PL, [Aug 11]), switched on a solar farm at the Mažeikiai refinery that cuts costs by 4 million euro a year (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 11]),; its 7.7 billion zł Q2 profit (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 7]) keeps the windfall-tax question alive (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 7]). Allegro extended its Lux Med partnership into medical services (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 11]) and kept buying back shares, nearly 60 million zł worth (wirtualnemedia.pl, [Aug 11]). CD Projekt formally changed its name (Gram.pl, [Aug 11]) and the shares rose on expectations for Gamescom (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 11]; Parkiet, [Aug 7]). Dino is adding new categories beyond fresh food (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 11]) and pb.pl sees the stock approaching its downtrend line [Aug 11]. Analysts are revising full-year expectations for LPP and Modivo (Parkiet, [Aug 11]) after LPP's 18.5% Q2 revenue growth (Bankier.pl, [Aug 6]). Canada is interested in KGHM competences beyond copper mining (WNP.PL, [Aug 11]). The heatwave pushed European power to 717 euro/MWh (e-magazyny.pl, [Aug 11]). Foreigners now take up to 7% of the housing market (Interia Biznes, [Aug 4]).

Geopolitics & Security: Zelensky appealed to Poland (Fakt, [Aug 11]) as TVP World argues Ukraine discarded its best Polish Patriot card [Aug 11] and Europe struggles to find Patriots for the winter (Kyiv Post, [Aug 11]). The UK ambassador warns Russia is testing Europe's limits (RBC-Ukraine, [Aug 11]), Poland and the Baltics are tightening infrastructure security over false-flag fears (AnewZ, [Aug 11]), and the US intelligence warning of a limited strike on Poland or the Baltics as early as this autumn (The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 7]) remains the backdrop.

New / Most Important: The macro mix has quietly turned into the one equities want: shop-price inflation at a record low, wages decelerating to 5.5%, growth forecasts at 3.7%, and a central bank the sell-side now expects to sit still for the rest of the year. That combination, not foreign flows, explains why Warsaw keeps printing records while global markets shake, and why the rotation inside the index favours retail and utilities over PKO. The risk is sequencing: a PIT giveaway on top of the EU's largest deficit, with a subsidy scandal now attached to the finance ministry.  |  Playbook ✓: Rates on hold, zloty stable; stay long domestic demand through Dino, Allegro and LPP, keep Pekao over PKO, and fade the idea of a September cut.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Food inflation at a historic low on the same day Orlen reset pump prices on a sharp oil rise: the headline disinflation is grocery-driven and the fuel leg can reverse it before the RPP meets again.
