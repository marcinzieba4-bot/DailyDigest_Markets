🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike odds up

Macro Stance: GUS revised September CPI up to 4.1% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes [Oct 14]), above the 4% consensus (BIZNES24) and the highest in 17 months (Agro Profil), with transport prices up 17.1% y/y (Newsmax Polska). ING calculates that more than half of the annual rate is fuel and energy, and the sell side reads it two ways: Credit Agricole expects the fuel intervention to pull October inflation down temporarily to 3.3% (Portal Samorządowy [Oct 14]) and Strefa Inwestorów sees no broad spillover with October CPI below 4.0%, while Erste warns inflation could reach about 5% once the CPN scheme expires (PortalKomunalny [Oct 14]) and Inwestycje.pl [Oct 14] carries a forecast of pre-emptive hikes taking the reference rate to 4.25% from the first quarter of 2027. The rate is 3.75% today (MyBank [Oct 7]) and Rankia notes inflation has overtaken it, so real rates are negative. RPP member Litwiniuk told Bloomberg [Oct 14] the zloty is still "strong" after its slide. The external side is weakening: ING [Oct 14] sees the current-account gap deepening on energy costs and primary-income outflows, though PKO BP expects lower oil to narrow it in 2027 (Portal Samorządowy [Oct 14]). Bloomberg [Oct 14] headlines political dysfunction as a threat to the $1 trillion economy.

Markets: Warsaw pulled back after three up sessions (Parkiet [Oct 14]): WIG20 closed down 0.40% (Inwestycje.pl), US inflation undercut the index (Bankier), KGHM fell 3.7% (Strefa Inwestorów) and Auto Partner dropped 11% after a shareholder sold 4 million shares (Yahoo Finance [Oct 14]), while Kęty, PGE and Tauron rose and Tauron's 300m zł failed to convince (FXMAG). The zloty absorbed the data: Bankier [Oct 14] has inflation accelerating but the zloty holding with the euro below resistance, FXMAG quotes the euro at 4.37 zł, the dollar at 3.90 and the franc at 4.69 in the morning, and Comparic sees the dollar back below 3.90. USD/PLN closed 3.9008, up 0.5% on the week and 3.8% over four weeks; EUR/PLN closed 4.369, flat on the week and up 0.8% over four weeks. Bonds found buyers: the MF sold 12bn zł of bonds with 20.2bn zł of demand (TradingView, Bankier [Oct 14]). EPOL closed 44.48, flat on the week and down 0.8% over four weeks. PGE at 17.29 is up 15.1% on the week and 24.1% over four weeks, up over 25% in a month ahead of a strategy update (Strefa Inwestorów [Oct 14]); Dino at 36.51 is up 8.5%, Allegro at 53.3 up 5.8%, LPP at 25260 up 5.2%, CD Projekt at 265.9 up 4.9%, Orlen at 154.0 up 6.7% but down 2.9% over four weeks, KGHM at 344.65 up 2.8%; PKO at 116.62 (down 1.4%) and Pekao at 265.1 (down 1.3%) lag.

Fiscal & Politics: The Fiscal Council warned of a snowball effect in the government's debt plan (Interia [Oct 14]), the IMF put global public debt near 100% of GDP (Yellow [Oct 14]), and INFOR [Oct 14] says the 55% of GDP threshold will cap the scale of pension indexation. The Finance Ministry published maximum property-tax rates for 2027, a wave of increases for owners (Rzeczpospolita [Oct 14]). Tusk is at the EU summit where leaders are fighting over energy prices and the budget (Euronews, TVP Info [Oct 14]); at home the market talks of a power price rise of around 200 zł per MWh that the government does not want to allow (Rzeczpospolita [Oct 14]), 4.1bn zł of support for large-scale energy storage is on offer (PortalKomunalny [Oct 14]), and Thursday's maximum fuel prices were set (Forsal).

Corporate & Sectors: PKO BP set a target of 30% of the new OKI investment-account market (Bankier, pb.pl [Oct 14]) and is raising CHF provisions (Strefa Biznesu [Oct 14]). Allegro opened an export corridor to Ukraine with over a million offers (pb.pl, Bankier [Oct 14]) while Temu consolidates its lead in Polish e-commerce (PortalTechnologiczny). Orlen faces a Czech refinery tax that would likely hit only Orlen, worth hundreds of millions of euro (Money.pl, Interia [Oct 14]). PGE selected Apator's 169m zł net bid for meters (Inwestycje.pl [Oct 14]) and is pairing with NASK on heat from data centres (pb.pl). Dino's unions extended the planned pre-holiday strike to warehouses (WP Finanse [Oct 14]) even as Dino and LPP gain (DlaHandlu). An Elbląg foundry is laying off almost its entire workforce over energy costs (Strefa Biznesu, Money.pl [Oct 14]), spot power is the most expensive since the start of the year (Zielona gospodarka); housing supply is rising, but not for every buyer (Rzeczpospolita [Oct 14]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland grilled Musk over new Starlink restrictions in Ukraine (Bloomberg [Oct 14]) a day after blaming Russia for Starlink sabotage (Tribune-Democrat [Oct 13]). Former BBN chief Koziej said NATO would have to shoot down Russian missiles over western Ukraine if aggression escalates (Censor [Oct 14]), and Escudo Digital [Oct 14] describes Poland strengthening its military role as NATO outlines plans to counter a Russian attack. Reuters [Oct 12] reported Patriot interceptor deliveries to Poland will be delayed.

New / Most Important: The 4.1% final print changes the arithmetic without changing the decision: real rates are negative, the current account is deteriorating on energy, and forecasters now openly model a 4.25% reference rate in early 2027. The market's reaction, a flat zloty, a 20.2bn zł bond book and a mild equity correction, says investors still trust the fuel cap to buy the RPP time; Erste's 5% post-cap scenario is the risk that trust is misplaced.

Playbook ✓: Use the strong MF auction to hold rather than add POLGB duration; keep PGE but recognise a 24.1% four-week move will be sold into the strategy update; lean into Allegro's Ukraine corridor and PKO's OKI push as structural stories; keep USD/PLN hedges modest with the dollar below 3.90.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Inflation above the policy rate normally weakens the currency, yet the zloty held and bonds rallied; the market is pricing the October drop to 3.3% rather than the September 4.1%, which works only until the CPN expiry debate starts in earnest.
