🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hawkish hold

Macro Stance: The NBP's hawkish shift is proceeding slowly, which Commerzbank notes is weighing on the zloty, and this week's inflation news gives the council no reason to hurry in either direction. Super Biznes says inflation is accelerating again and the government is reacting [Aug 17]; the July print was confirmed at 3.0% with fuel up 13.9% m/m (Bankier.pl, 300Gospodarka.pl, [Aug 13]), core inflation sits at a ten-month high (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]) and health services rose by as much as 8.6% (Alert Medyczny, [Aug 17]). Citi now expects the fuel measures to be extended at least through September (PAP Biznes, [Aug 17]), which keeps the headline administered and the core signal intact. The growth backdrop is strong, Q2 GDP at 3.8% y/y and 0.9% q/q (PAP Biznes, [Aug 13]). ING's calendar points to a cluster of domestic data at the end of the week and the Fitch rating decision [Aug 17].

Markets: Monday was a weak session: PKO BP led the gainers while PGE fell most (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 17]), KGHM and the energy names declined with banks relatively strong (Portal spożywczy, [Aug 17]) and Allegro and PZU were among the losers (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 17]). The nerves are pre-results: INNPoland.pl says KGHM is falling because the market fears Wednesday's report [Aug 17] even as TVN24 describes copper in a rush to a record [Aug 17]; CD Projekt is also sliding into its publication (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 17]), and DNA Rynków asks whether Dino, down 50% from its peak, has seen the worst [Aug 17]. Friday's card, the last available, had EPOL 44.45 (+0.6% 1W, +10.2% 4W), banks leading with Pekao 259.2 (+2.5% 1W, +13.2% 4W) and PKO 111.08 (+1.9% 1W), CD Projekt 263.1 (+2.7% 1W), and the summer winners flat: KGHM 352.5 (-0.1% 1W, +20.0% 4W), PGE 10.79 (+0.4% 1W), Dino 32.41 (-0.1% 1W), LPP 21,820 (-1.8% 1W). The zloty is being carried by the dollar: USD/PLN 3.7202 (+0.1% 1W, -2.0% 4W) on the card, with wGospodarce seeing the pair back at 3.71 [Aug 17], Comparic.pl targeting 3.63 [Aug 17]; EUR/PLN 4.3059 (+0.2% 1W, -0.8% 4W) edged up into the close (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 17]) and FXMAG expects 4.32 [Aug 17]. In bonds, Analizy.pl asks whether lower PIT means higher yields [Aug 17] while the US 30-year hit 5.27%, the highest in 19 years (pb.pl, [Aug 17]).

Fiscal & Politics: The ministry published the January-July execution: the deficit reached 141.1 billion zł (Inwestycje.pl, Gov.pl, [Aug 17]), which Money.pl calls a confirmed gigadeficit [Aug 17], though Business Insider Polska notes it is smaller than a year earlier [Aug 17] and pb.pl stresses it did not widen despite CPN because VAT and corporate tax receipts are improving [Aug 17]. The opposition is escalating: Czarnek equates Tusk with bankruptcy (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 17]) and Morawiecki warns of a rating downgrade this week (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 17]), a question CryptoRank also poses [Aug 17] ahead of Friday's Fitch review (ING, [Aug 17]). Tax policy is moving in both directions: the Sejm sent to committee a proposal to lift the tax threshold to 171 thousand zł (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 17]), while the ministry wants 19% tax and a 36-month holding period for family foundations (Pit.pl, [Aug 17]). pb.pl explains CPN leaves excise untouched because the excise revenue plan is already in trouble [Aug 17].

Corporate & Sectors: KNF fined PKO BP a combined 47.5 million zł over structured deposits (pb.pl, Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 17]), which the bank contests (TVN24, [Aug 17]), days after a strong second quarter (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]). The CPN caps took effect today (salon24.pl, [Aug 17]) with Tuesday's maximum prices unchanged (TVP Kielce, [Aug 17]); Orlen ended July with 1966 stations against 574 for bp and 547 for Moya (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 17]), is under ministry scrutiny (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 17]), and T&E is calling for a windfall tax after oil majors doubled profits (Green-news.pl, [Aug 17]). Allegro's AI assistant has 500 thousand monthly users (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 17]) and the company plans to base 40% of its technology portfolio on AI by year-end (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 17]). Dino's management and unions return to mediation with five dates set (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 17]). Deutsche Telekom is buying Fiberhost and Inea for 1 billion euro (Global Banking & Finance Review, [Aug 17]). Bechtel signed another nuclear contract (Nadmorski24.pl, [Aug 17]) and the Gulf war is lifting polyester costs for clothing retailers (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 17]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Defence Ministry says it is preparing for war with Russia and expects a major provocation within months (UNITED24 Media, [Aug 17]). Russia has set up a network of secret drone bases near the Ukrainian and Belarusian borders that puts Poland within range (Firstpost, Kyiv Post, [Aug 17]), and the foiled assassination of a Ukrainian-American in Warsaw was acted on with a US intelligence tip (National Security Journal, [Aug 17]). Tusk's pledge of Europe's largest army (The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 15]) is the policy response.

New / Most Important: The 141.1 billion zł deficit lands four days before Fitch and in the same news cycle as a proposal to lift the PIT threshold to 171 thousand zł. The composition is better than the headline, revenues are improving and the gap is smaller than last year, but the market is being asked to price a sovereign that is simultaneously capping fuel, cutting PIT and promising Europe's largest army. That is why the zloty's gains are a dollar story and why Analizy.pl's question about yields matters more than the RPP this week.  |  Playbook ✓: CEE currencies underperforming on rate-differential compression; keep banks over utilities and miners into results, stay short duration into the rating decision, and do not chase USD/PLN below 3.71.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: PKO BP topped the WIG20 on the day KNF fined it 47.5 million zł: the market is treating conduct penalties as noise against a record half, which works until the free-credit suits are provisioned.
