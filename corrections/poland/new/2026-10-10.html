🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike tail open

Macro Stance: A quiet Saturday lets the week's policy signal settle. The RPP held the reference rate at 3.75% (StockWatch [Oct 7]), Governor Glapiński said he does not expect a November hike but that the Council is ready to act if inflation risks mount (PAP Biznes [Oct 8]), and the debate has moved to timing: FXMAG [Oct 10] carries BNP economists' view on hikes, ING expects them only in 2027 with inflation able to exceed 3.5% (CEO Magazyn [Oct 8]), and PKB24 [Oct 8] points out the real policy rate is already below zero after a fourth consecutive hold. Briefs Finance [Oct 10] frames the Sejm commission's recommendation of a State Tribunal vote on Glapiński (Bloomberg [Oct 9]) as turmoil that puts a spotlight on how Polish rates get set, and an RPP member told pb.pl [Oct 9] the priority is that the currency not pay the price. The inflation backdrop is unchanged: September inflation came in above the reference rate (Polskie Radio 24 [Oct 7]) and above the target (Money.pl [Oct 8]), while growth support is strong after GUS lifted 2025 GDP to 3.8% (Podatki.biz [Oct 6]). On incomes, Money.pl [Oct 10] revisits GUS's April median wage of 7695 zł, roughly 20% below the average (Business Insider Polska [Oct 7]).

Markets: Friday's close was constructive: Parkiet [Oct 9] saw stocks "back in favour", with PGE, KGHM and Tauron leading (Inwestycje.pl), Orlen up 3.49% and Dino up 6.35% (FXMAG [Oct 9]) while Alior, mBank and PKO BP fell. The card shows the rotation clearly. Over the week Orlen at 159.34 is up 10.4%, PGE at 16.7 up 11.1% and 19.0% over four weeks, Dino at 35.81 up 6.4%, LPP at 25040 up 4.2% and 16.1% over four weeks, CD Projekt at 263.0 up 3.7% after the Paramount Cyberpunk film news (Reuters [Oct 9]), KGHM at 347.0 up 3.5% on EU strategic-project status for 9.5bn zł of investments (pb.pl [Oct 9]), and Allegro at 51.75 up 2.7% and 15.1% over four weeks. The losers are the banks: PKO at 113.94 is down 3.6% on the week and 5.5% over four weeks, Pekao at 261.1 down 2.8%, after WIG-Banki fell 2.41% on Oct 8 (FXMAG). EPOL closed 43.82, up 1.8% on the week but still down 3.8% over four weeks. The zloty closed the week with USD/PLN at 3.9015, up 0.3% on the week and 4.8% over four weeks, and EUR/PLN at 4.3752, flat on the week and up 1.2% over four weeks; the dollar slipped back under 3.90 on Friday (Comparic [Oct 9]) after an Oct 8 high not seen since April 2025 (FXMAG).

Fiscal & Politics: The 2027 budget is in committee after the Sejm rejected the PiS motion to reject it (Interia [Oct 8], Super Biznes [Oct 9]), with a 281.2bn zł deficit and almost 200bn zł for defence (Portal Samorządowy [Oct 8]). The weekend commentary splits: Money.pl [Oct 10] argues Poland will not trip over the prudential debt threshold and that reduction need not hurt, while wGospodarce [Oct 10] attacks what it calls 450bn zł of "tricks", and INFOR [Oct 10] notes the draft budget freezes public-sector pay in 2027 because of excessive debt. The Finance Ministry published September retail bond sales (Gov.pl [Oct 10]), and a ZBP and SGH report warns that banks' rising holdings of government debt could limit lending to companies (CEO Magazyn [Oct 9]). The Energy Minister raised maximum fuel prices through Monday (Portal spożywczy [Oct 10]), and Business Insider Polska [Oct 10] previews a visit abroad by Tusk and Domański it calls historic.

Corporate & Sectors: Dino's unions have fixed a general strike for the days before the holidays and extended it to warehouses (Bankier, WP Finanse [Oct 9]; Wiadomości Handlowe [Oct 10]), a test for a stock that just led the index. PGE lost its lawsuit against Greenpeace, with the court calling the campaign free speech (Energetyka24 [Oct 10]), a minor blemish on a 19.0% four-week run. Orlen's former deputy CEO and three directors remain in custody over pre-election fuel pricing (TVN24 [Oct 8]) and the group raised wholesale prices sharply (wnp.pl [Oct 8]). KGHM's Sierra Gorda expansion in Chile is under way (MyGlogow [Oct 10]). In banks, a Suwałki court awarded 188 thousand zł against PKO Bank Hipoteczny in a WIBOR case (CHF24 [Oct 8]), Millennium is provisioning 154m zł for FX loans (StockWatch [Oct 8]) and mBank and VeloBank have dropped WIBOR for new mortgages (Bezprawnik [Oct 8]). Housing data show Warsaw up 6.3% in a quarter and Łódź down 5% (CEO Magazyn [Oct 8]); PEJ and Westinghouse have begun ordering reactor components (Dziennik Bałtycki [Oct 8]) even as Money.pl [Oct 9] warns of multi-year delays.

Geopolitics & Security: The Warsaw Security Forum opened with the Ukraine war and European defence on top of the agenda (TVP World [Oct 10]), the same morning an alert was declared and aircraft scrambled over Russia's attack on Ukraine (UNN [Oct 10]). Defence24's weekly [Oct 9] pairs Russian military flights near Poland with Sweden's Patriot deployment, and Forsal [Oct 9] reports Poland signalling its fighters could fly in Ukraine's defence. Investigators found Polish SIM cards in Russian drones that entered Poland (UNITED24 [Oct 8]).

New / Most Important: The institutional fight over the NBP is now the macro story. A commission vote to send the governor to the State Tribunal, landing in the same week the Council declared itself "ready to act", means the market must price a central bank whose independence is contested just as it may need to tighten. Equities have shrugged, led by state-controlled energy names; the zloty and bank stocks have not.

Playbook ✓: Keep the energy and consumer long (PGE, Orlen, Dino, LPP) that carried the week; treat bank weakness as a litigation and crowding-out story rather than a rate call; hedge USD/PLN tactically rather than structurally with the dollar back under 3.90.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Strong GDP, a record-chasing WIG and a currency that cannot hold gains is an unusual mix; the missing ingredient is policy credibility, and no data release fixes that before the November projection.
