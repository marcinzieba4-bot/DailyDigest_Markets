🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold into 2027

Macro Stance: A day after the RPP held the reference rate at 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Sep 9]), Glapiński's press conference did the real work: rates will stay stable for a long time, possibly until mid-2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 10]), nothing suggests any change soon (Bankier.pl, [Sep 10]), and he hinted the NBP will not follow the ECB, which hiked again today (pb.pl, [Sep 10]); Rzeczpospolita notes this is a change from the pre-holiday stance [Sep 10]. Domestic forecasts are drifting hawkish: a hike is back in projections (Faktura.pl, [Sep 10]), Gazeta Prawna warns instalments could rise by up to 200 zł [Sep 10]. Growth revisions back that up: VeloBank raised its 2026 GDP forecast to 3.7% (WNP.PL, [Sep 10]) and Pekao lifted both inflation and GDP forecasts (Analizy.pl, [Sep 10]). GUS put the war's cumulative cost to the economy at 490.1 bn zł (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 9]), about €113.5bn (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 10]), with households the biggest losers (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 10]). The NBP's gold reserves rose to 648 tons (BigGo Finance, [Sep 10]).

Markets: Thursday turned the tape: the WIG20 closed down 0.67% (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 10]) as cheaper bonds hit the index and KGHM was routed (Bankier.pl, [Sep 10]), falling 7% while PGE gained 5.3% (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 10]); copper reversed from records with the White House wavering on tariffs (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 10]), and Rzeczpospolita says it increasingly smells like a downward correction [Sep 10], though the WIG20 still defends 4100 points (XYZ, [Sep 10]). The card, as of Wednesday's close, still shows the leaders fully priced: KGHM 378.0 (+9.8% 1W) going into its 7% drop; PGE 13.57 (+14.6% 1W, +22.4% 4W) and StockWatch.pl calls September a rally in PGE, Enea and Tauron [Sep 10]; EPOL 45.55 (+4.0% 1W, +2.6% 4W); PKO 118.32 (+3.6%, +7.7%), Pekao 266.3 (+3.5%, +4.2%); Orlen 159.52 (+1.0%, +2.5%) waiting for an impulse (FXMAG, [Sep 10]); Dino 35.8 (+7.2% 4W); LPP 21480 (-3.3% 4W); Allegro 45.02 (-3.0% 4W); CD Projekt 232.3 (-12.8% 4W) with a market that cannot decide (INNPoland.pl, [Sep 10]). FX is calm: Bankier.pl says nothing new came from the RPP and the zloty stayed where it was [Sep 10], USD/PLN closed at 3.7077 (-0.7% 1W, -0.7% 4W), EUR/PLN at 4.3124 (-0.3% 1W, +0.2% 4W) and FXMAG expects EUR/PLN to drift higher as the market waits on the ECB [Sep 10]; Commerzbank reads the NBP signals as dovish and a weight on PLN (FXStreet, [Sep 10]). POLGBs were the day's driver: cheaper bonds pulled equities down (Bankier.pl, [Sep 10]), while BGK still sold PLN 1.23 bln of bonds on PLN 2.13 bln of demand (TradingView, [Sep 10]) and closed a US$2.5 billion issuance (White & Case, [Sep 10]).

Fiscal & Politics: The MF reported state public debt up 7.0% q/q at end-Q2 (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 10]) to 53.3% of GDP (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 10]); WNP.PL says the dangerous threshold is nearer [Sep 10] after Rzeczpospolita called 55% dangerously close [Sep 9]. Glapiński added that the draft budget shows no tightening but that a 7.1% deficit has no effect on inflation (wnp.pl, [Sep 10]). The 2027 draft remains a record deficit with new taxes (Forsal.pl, [Sep 8]), the NFZ has a 12 bn hole (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 9]). Presidential vetoes stay the political constraint (Money.pl, [Sep 9]). Tusk said Poland will block any EU initiative that raises power prices (TVN24, [Sep 10]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: the TSUE ruled in C-510/25 that the balance theory is allowed in CHF settlements as long as it does not harm borrowers (Prawo.pl, [Sep 10]); PKO BP says the ruling simplifies settlements (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 10]). POLSTR is entering mortgages, so instalments may respond differently to RPP decisions (Bankier.pl, [Sep 10]) and the NBP objects to the reference rate as a mortgage base (pb.pl, [Sep 9]). Energy: Tauron posted a solid but lower profit (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 10]); PGE's deputy CEO says ETS costs weigh too heavily on industrial power prices (wnp.pl, [Sep 10]); gas is wrecking the power market in the worst period of the year (WNP.PL, [Sep 10]), power could cost 19% more in 2027 (Interia Biznes, [Sep 10]) and a court dismissed the complaint against the nuclear plant's environmental decision (Prawo.pl, [Sep 10]). Orlen faced a black Thursday at the pumps (Dziennik.pl, [Sep 10]) and will not take over PGZ (Bankier.pl, [Sep 9]). KGHM is reshaping Sierra Gorda and adding the Victoria project (Parkiet, [Sep 10]) after the BHP letter of intent (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]). Retail: Dino's union dispute is escalating with a strike warning (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 10]); LPP trimmed openings to 750 from 950 but not in Poland (PropertyNews.pl, [Sep 4]). Housing: 7% more applicants for mortgages (Bankier.pl, [Sep 10]) against Fakt's reading that lending slumped to its worst in a year [Sep 10].

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk warned of future attacks after a direct threat was averted at the Poland-Ukraine border (POLITICO.eu, [Sep 10]) and Poland expects Russia to strike its border crossings (Kyiv Post, [Sep 10]); NATO aircraft deployed in Poland as jet-powered drones hit Ukraine (ABC News, [Sep 10]). MON confirmed a new Ukraine aid package with a hard condition (Bankier.pl, [Sep 10]), all 250 Abrams tanks arrive in about 24 months (19FortyFive, [Sep 10]) and KUKE launched domestic guarantees for the defence industry (Bankier.pl, [Sep 10]).

New / Most Important: Glapiński has put a date on the pause: stable rates possibly to mid-2027 while the ECB tightens, with the NBP explicitly declining to follow. That is dovish for the zloty and for borrowers, but the same day the MF printed 53.3% debt and the governor waved away a 7.1% deficit, and POLGBs sold off hard enough to drag the WIG20 red and take 7% out of KGHM.  |  Playbook ✓: CE FX weakness on oil shock is textbook; a long NBP hold with a widening ECB gap argues for banks over duration and for PGE over KGHM near term.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The ECB hiking today while the NBP talks of holding to mid-2027 should pressure the zloty against the euro, yet the card shows EUR/PLN lower on the week; the bond market, not FX, is where the policy gap is being priced.
