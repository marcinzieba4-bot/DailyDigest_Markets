🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The RPP conversation has turned from when to cut to whether to hike. GUS confirmed August CPI at 3.4% y/y (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 15]), the highest in 14 months (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]), with transport up 11.2% while food fell for a fourth month at the fastest pace in a decade (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 15]); PAP Biznes flagged second-round risk from fuel [Sep 15] and the NBP core measure rose again (Forsal.pl, [Sep 16]). An MPC member told Bloomberg a rate increase is possible on energy risks [Sep 18] and Zarzecki maintains his hike call (FXMAG, [Sep 15]). Activity is softer: August industrial output rose 4.3% y/y but missed forecasts (PAP Biznes, [Sep 18]), PPI sent an inflationary signal (Comparic.pl, [Sep 18]) and ING said weaker industry underscores risks to GDP [Sep 18]. Labour cost growth slowed from 9.7% to 4.7% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 17]) and the 2027 minimum wage was set at 4950 zł (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Sep 16]).

Markets: Triple witching closed a volatile week: the WIG20 fell 0.50% on Friday (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 18]) with mBank leading the decline and LPP saving the index, on huge turnover (Bankier.pl, [Sep 18]). Thursday was the opposite, Allegro's surge driving the WIG20 with Orlen at the bottom (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 17]) and the WIG30 up 1.66% (Investing.com, [Sep 17]); mid-week the index slipped below 4100 points under PZU's record dividend (Parkiet, [Sep 16]). The card shows the rotation: Allegro at 49.44 is up 9.9% on the week, LPP at 22380 gained 4.4% and 8.1% over four weeks, Dino at 36.15 added 3.3% and 12.0%, while Orlen at 153.9 lost 3.5% and KGHM at 340.9 fell 1.8%; PGE at 14.25 still carries a 39.7% four-week run and EPOL closed at 44.78, down 0.8% on the week. The zloty is the weak leg: USD/PLN at 3.7934 (+1.9% 1W, +2.8% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3529 (+0.7% 1W, +0.9% 4W) after the Fed hiked and the euro became the dearest since the end of 2024 (FXMAG, [Sep 17]), with Bankier.pl noting US rates are now above Poland's [Sep 17]. POLGBs swung: the 10-year yield broke 6.5%, the highest since 2007 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 15]), the ministry still sold PLN 10bn at auction (TradingView, [Sep 16]) and yields then fell after the Fed in what Business Insider Polska called the best result in the world [Sep 17].

Fiscal & Politics: Moody's cut Poland's rating for the first time since 2016 (Bloomberg, [Sep 18]), to A3 on fiscal deterioration (Investing.com, [Sep 18]). The numbers explain it: the state deficit reached 151.1 billion zł after August (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 16]), up 10 billion zł in the month (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]), the budget will breach the prudential threshold (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 16]) and the Fiscal Council's first opinion called the situation very serious (Money.pl, [Sep 14]) while warning of debt above 55% of GDP (Bankier.pl, [Sep 14]); the ministry and the Council are now on opposite sides over spending (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]). The answer is revenue: the Sejm re-passed the fuel windfall tax today (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]), the 60% levy the president blocked once (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 15]), while Nawrocki pushes a fuel-margin bill that could disrupt diesel imports (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]). KSeF penalties were deferred to end-2027 (Gov.pl, [Sep 16]).

Corporate & Sectors: Retail earnings carried the week. LPP posted 768 million zł of net profit and 1744 million zł of EBITDA (BIZNES24, [Sep 17]), sales up 20% y/y so far this quarter, a 56.5-57% gross-margin guide (PAP Biznes, [Sep 17]) and record-high shares (wnp.pl, [Sep 18]). Allegro raised its 2026 outlook and hit a five-year high (Reuters, [Sep 17]), guiding Polish GMV growth of 11-13% (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 17]), and the FedEx-led consortium completed its €7.8 billion InPost buyout (Bloomberg, [Sep 18]). Orlen is the problem child: Saudi Aramco cut deliveries to Europe and Orlen is sourcing crude worldwide (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]), the shares are down over 7% (Comparic.pl, [Sep 18]), StockWatch.pl asks whether windfall tax 2.0 ends the rally [Sep 18] and the Orlen Termika chief was dismissed after a winter coal-shortage warning (TVP World, [Sep 17]). PGE plans 235 billion zł of investment by 2035 (Gazeta Lubuska, [Sep 18]), KGHM refused a 6500 zł per-employee bonus demand (netTG.pl, [Sep 18]) and CD Projekt's record half-year came from licences rather than games (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 18]). Banks face TSUE-driven refunds of interest on commissions and free-credit suits (Parkiet, [Sep 18]) and the NBP asked for a lifeline for lenders in the WIBOR-to-POLSTR switch (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]). Heat tariffs will rise on gas and CO2 (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]).

Geopolitics & Security: Russia hit a passenger train on Ukraine's border with Poland, just missing a diplomatic train (Reuters, [Sep 14]), NATO jets downed a drone over Lithuania (BBC, [Sep 15]), and a drone fell 4 km from Poland (Kyiv Post, [Sep 17]), briefly closing Lublin and Rzeszów airports [Sep 17]. Tusk warned Russia may stage accidental strikes on NATO's eastern flank (TVP World, [Sep 17]); the army is building HESCO positions at Medyka, Korczowa and Dorohusk (Nowiny 24, [Sep 17]). Poland will send Patriot missiles to Ukraine (Fakt, [Sep 16]), Trump touted progress on a US base (CBS News, [Sep 18]) and GUS put the war's cost to Poland at 490 billion zł of GDP (o2, [Sep 18]).

New / Most Important: The overnight Moody's downgrade defines the week because it converts a market complaint into an institutional one: a deficit past 151 billion zł, debt nearing the constitutional limit and a government whose only tool is new taxes on fuel companies, with the president blocking even those. It lands days after the Fed pushed US rates above Poland's and the 10-year touched its highest since 2007, while an RPP member discusses hiking into an energy shock. Record retail earnings keep equities insulated, but the zloty and POLGBs now carry a fiscal premium that was not there a month ago.  |  Playbook ✓: Stay with LPP, Allegro and the banks where earnings momentum is verifiable; keep POLGB duration short until the Fiscal Council and ministry stop trading blows; treat USD/PLN near 3.80 as a dollar story the NBP can only partly answer.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Polish equities beating the world (INNPoland.pl, [Sep 11]) against a first-ever rating cut; Orlen down 3.5% on the week with oil above 108 dollars, because windfall tax and Aramco supply cuts outweigh the margin tailwind.
