🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: The RPP is in wait mode (Parkiet, [Jun 25]; wGospodarce, [Jun 23]) and the labour data are the argument for staying there: Bankier.pl noted employment is still falling while pay grows slowly, and said the figures should discourage the RPP from discussing hikes [Jun 22], after NBP revealed a vote for a hike at the last meeting (Tysol.pl, [Jun 22]). The NBP survey has inflation under control but cuts uncertain (Parkiet, [Jun 24]). Prices are easing at the shelf, with food inflation clearly weakening (CEO Magazyn, [Jun 19]), but Rzeczpospolita notes Polish price levels have converged from 58% to 73% of the EU average in a decade [Jun 27]. The supply side is the bright spot: Business Insider Polska says the wave of defence orders is a stimulus Poland has never had [Jun 27], while pb.pl reports credit growth at its fastest in 14 years as part of a global cycle [Jun 23]. Against that, construction slowed despite EU billions (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 22]) and retail sales missed (Bankier.pl, [Jun 22]). Poland is still cast as the outlier dove in a world of hawks, but the feed supports a hold with a hawkish minority, not easing.

Markets: The week closed with a correction: Inwestycje.pl has the WIG20 lower on Friday with Kęty and Żabka against the market [Jun 26], StockWatch.pl tying it to a global sell-off with Allegro, Kęty, PGE and Text in play [Jun 26]. The card now carries Friday's closes. EPOL at 38.27 is down 3.7% on the week and 5.7% over four weeks. KGHM at 327.75 is still 10.3% lower on the week after Wednesday's 7.5% drop (wnp.pl, [Jun 24]). Orlen at 123.46 (-2.3% 1W, -12.6% 4W) and PGE at 9.6 (-1.9% 1W, -11.1% 4W) gave back Thursday's energy rebound. Allegro at 37.19 lost 5.7% on the week despite the buyback, though it is still up 8.7% over four weeks; CD Projekt at 217.4 (-3.0% 1W) confirmed the loss of 220 zł (CrypS.pl, [Jun 26]); LPP at 18310 is 16.4% below its level four weeks ago; PKO at 103.62 and Pekao at 228.8 are off 1.6% and 2.8%; Dino at 28.98 is roughly flat on the week. FX held: USD/PLN at 3.7661 (+1.4% 1W, +3.9% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.2788 (+0.6% 1W, +1.3% 4W), with the euro below 4.30 zł (Bankier.pl, [Jun 26]) and pb.pl seeing a weaker dollar, a stable zloty and falling bond yields [Jun 26]. The bond story is cost: Parkiet reports Treasury debt galloping with servicing costs up almost 30% [Jun 25], and July retail bonds pay up to 5.65% (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jun 24]).

Fiscal & Politics: Bankier.pl flags an accumulation of state spending ahead, with billions for nuclear and the CPK airport against the backdrop of an EU procedure [Jun 27]; economists already appealed for caution on borrowing given the pile-up of costly investments (Newseria BIZNES, [Jun 24]). The arithmetic is stark: debt rose 46.8 bn zł in a month (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 22]) and wPolityce.pl puts it at 1.5 bn zł a day in May 2026 against 0.9 bn zł a day in 2025 [Jun 26]. On the windfall tax, the Senate added a safety valve after a 74% rate for oil firms was raised (Wyborcza.biz, [Jun 26]). Revenue measures keep coming: alcohol and sugar levies for healthcare (Bloomberg, [Jun 24]) and a new tax from 2027 after the president blocked raises (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 25]). The CHF law passed the Senate unamended (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 25]) and CHF24 describes automatic suspension of instalments once a case is filed, with a real chance of the president's signature [Jun 27].

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen is raising prices again: wholesale fuel went up as crude rose (Bankier.pl, [Jun 26]) and station prices rise from Saturday (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 27]), while the company confirmed two more Naftogaz agreements (Inwestycje.pl, [Jun 25]) and the Cerisa North Sea investment decision (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 25]). The PGE-Enea-Orlen-Tauron letter of intent on Ukrainian energy reconstruction stands (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 26]), and Rzeczpospolita argues east-west integration runs through energy [Jun 27]. Allegro's buyback runs at up to 42 mln shares and a maximum 45 zł (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 26]). Dino's labour problem deepened: unions went to prosecutors (DlaHandlu.pl, [Jun 26]) and FXMAG reports staff overwhelmed by workloads [Jun 27]. Energy: the heatwave is hitting generation and may lift power prices (Interia Biznes, [Jun 27]) after spot hit 1442 zł per MWh (wnp.pl, [Jun 25]). Taiwan plans chip and EV plants in Poland (The Diplomat, [Jun 26]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Guardian reports NATO leaders fear they can no longer rely on US help if Russia attacks [Jun 27], a day after it reported Russia preparing a possible provocation in the Baltics or Poland [Jun 26]. Sikorski repeated that Russia may stage a false flag to justify an attack on NATO (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Jun 27]), and Firstpost framed the aim as testing NATO unity [Jun 27]. Warsaw's answer is capacity: Europe's largest anti-drone system (DW, [Jun 26]) and the defence-order stimulus. BGK entered the Ukraine reconstruction fund with 15 mln euro, rising to as much as 500 mln (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 26]).

New / Most Important: The cost of money for the state is now the headline risk: servicing costs up almost 30%, debt at 1.5 bn zł a day and a spending pile-up for nuclear and CPK that Bankier.pl explicitly links to an EU procedure. That is why the zloty is weak at a hold-rate NBP and why the windfall tax is being pushed toward 74%. Private credit at a 14-year high and record defence orders say the economy can take it; the bond market says the budget cannot for long.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold, not cut; lean into banks on credit growth and the CHF endgame, keep energy and KGHM underweight through the tax debate, and fund it from LPP and Allegro, where the four-week drawdown and the buyback have already done the work.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Credit growth at a 14-year high while employment falls; falling POLGB yields against a 30% rise in servicing costs; NATO doubts about US backing in the same week Poland is called the alliance's most powerful fortress (Forsal.pl, [Jun 25]).
