🇵🇱 Poland  Bullish  → hold, cut talk returns

Macro Stance: A split is opening at the top of the NBP. Super Biznes [Aug 3] reports Glapiński wants to lower rates and that experts are sounding the alarm, days after GUS's flash put July CPI at 3.0% y/y (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 31]) on fuel prices up roughly 14% in a month (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Jul 31]) once the government's fuel shield expired (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Aug 1]). The Rzeczpospolita and Parkiet economist panels [Jul 31] argued the Council should not hurry, and Citi's call of above 3.5% by year-end still constrains the easing path. Today's data cut the other way for the doves: the manufacturing PMI rebounded in July with the first rise in employment for months, though still below the growth threshold (Rzeczpospolita and BIZNES24, [Aug 3]), and GUS statistics show Poland growing twice as fast as the EU as a whole (Agro Profil, [Jul 31]). The NBP's credit survey says banks will tighten for households and loosen for firms (Money.pl and Direct Money, [Aug 3]), a quiet form of tightening the Council does not have to vote on. Stance: hold, but a governor talking cuts with CPI at 3% is the new variable.

Markets: WIG20 closed at an all-time high, beating the peak from almost two decades ago (Parkiet and TVN24, [Aug 3]); StockWatch.pl [Aug 3] put Orlen, PKO BP and Żabka at the centre of the record session, while PGE led the gains and Allegro was the weakest blue chip (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 3]). The move is energy-led: Strefa Biznesu [Aug 3] lists companies beyond Orlen that gain from dear fuel, and the card shows PGE 10.11 (+5.0% 4W) and Orlen 148.04 (+11.4% 4W, -5.1% 1W) with Comparic [Aug 3] asking whether Orlen's pause after the rally is a buying opportunity. The rest of the card (prior session): PKO 111.92 (+5.0% 1W, +6.3% 4W), Pekao 245.5 (+5.0% 1W), Dino 31.99 (+11.2% 1W, +15.5% 4W), LPP 21,720 (+7.8% 1W, +17.1% 4W), CD Projekt 249.9 (+10.5% 1W), Allegro 44.99 (+17.0% 4W), KGHM 307.95 (-8.1% 4W); EPOL 42.83 is +4.7% 1W and +8.6% 4W. The zloty caught a geopolitical bid: Trump announced a halt to the attack on Iran and oil fell (OKO.press, [Aug 3]), Bankier [Aug 3] reports the zloty reacting positively to talk of a new Iran deal with the euro lower, and FXMAG [Aug 3] has the zloty gaining more than 6 grosz against the dollar. The card has USD/PLN 3.7286 (-1.5% 1W, -0.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.304 (-0.2% 1W, +0.4% 4W). No fresh POLGB headlines today.

Fiscal & Politics: Defence is the new spending priority: Domański said the government will strongly support defence investment and needs as much of it as possible (WNP.PL, [Aug 3]), while the finance ministry published how long repayment of the EU SAFE loan will take (Fakt and Do Rzeczy, [Aug 3]). The MF has withdrawn its controversial tax changes, with Interia [Aug 3] checking what remains in the draft; bottled water keeps its 23% VAT rate for the good of the budget (Podatki.biz, [Aug 3]). The Sejm granted discharge for the 2025 budget (Prawo.pl, [Jul 31]) against debt above 2 trillion zł (naTemat.pl, [Jul 27]); the president is pushing a deficit hammer with penalties for overspending politicians (BitHub.pl, [Aug 1]). Fuel politics has shifted from caps to arm-twisting: the government wants to force Orlen to cut pump prices and widen promotions (Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 3]) with the minister pointing to minimal margins (Bankier, [Aug 3]); the CPN package itself has no financing after the president's decision (Money.pl, [Jul 31]).

Corporate & Sectors: Energy: Enea, PGE, Orlen and Unimot signed up to the FSRU2 floating LNG terminal (Bankier, [Aug 3]); Poland's largest power plant is running without a key unit (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 3]) and e-magazyny.pl [Aug 3] expects spot power above 2000 zł per MWh on Tuesday evening, while Rzeczpospolita [Aug 3] sees Poland becoming a power exporter as Hungary shuts the Paks nuclear plant for lack of Danube water. KGHM's Nitroerg will invest 300 million zł in explosives for defence (Bankier, [Aug 3]). Banks: Pekao beat forecasts despite lower rates and higher CIT (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 30]), mBank posted a record result (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 29]), mortgages ran above 13 bn zł for a fourth month (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 30]); Money.pl [Aug 3] says the state sided with banks on interest for undisbursed funds. Retail: the Żabka takeover is worth 32.62 bn zł (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 3]); Dino is rebounding after a difficult first half (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 3]) with union mediation resuming (Farmer.pl, [Aug 1]); an appeals court ordered Allegro to unlock the full view of delivery options (NowyMarketing, [Aug 3]) as Allegro reported buyback trades; LPP sought admission of series N shares (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 3]).

Geopolitics & Security: Belarus is sending special forces to the Ukrainian border on Lukashenko's order and forming a new assault brigade (Polskie Radio 24 and Forsal.pl, [Aug 3]); TVP World [Aug 3] reports Russian plans to use captured Ukrainian drones to provoke NATO after last week's Kh-101 crash in the Lublin region, and the defence minister said Russia's war stocks are exhausted (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 31]).

New / Most Important: The 2007 ceiling is gone. WIG20's close above a high that stood for almost two decades, on the same day Trump paused the Iran strikes and oil fell, matters because it changes who owns Polish equities: the index is now a breakout, not a value trade. The risk is that the breakout is energy-led while the government leans on Orlen's margins and the governor floats cuts into a 3% CPI, so leadership could rotate back to banks quickly. With WTI having run 15.9% into this, the Iran pause is the single input that makes the record sustainable.

Playbook ✓: Hold pattern as inflation risks persist; stay long GPW with a bank core and treat Orlen on any government-imposed price cut as the hedge.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Glapiński's cut talk versus the Council's panels — if the governor wins, the zloty's Iran-driven bid reverses and the import channel for inflation reopens.
