🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: The fuel shock is now in the data. GUS's flash estimate put July CPI at 3.0% y/y and 0.8% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Jul 31]), back to a three-handle after fuel prices jumped roughly 14% in a month while food got cheaper (Obserwator Gospodarczy and Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 31]); ING [Jul 31] ties the rise to the end of the CPN fuel support and the renewed Iran war, and Citi had already flagged above 3.5% by year-end. That seals the RPP's summer: Rzeczpospolita's and Parkiet's economist panels [Jul 31] say the Council should not hurry to cut, and INFOR.PL [Jul 31] calls it the end of dreams of cheaper credit. Growth is no excuse for easing either: GUS data show Poland growing twice as fast as the EU as a whole (Agro Profil, [Jul 31]) and real wages grew strongly in June (xyz.pl, [Jul 24]). Abroad, euro-area inflation accelerated in July with markets awaiting the ECB (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 31]) and the Fed held (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 29]). Stance unchanged: hold, with the easing window narrowing rather than opening.

Markets: Record day. Business Insider Polska [Jul 31] says July 31 went into the history of the Polish exchange, FXMAG [Jul 31] reported WIG20 a step from the 4000-point barrier with KGHM and banks pulling it up, and Bankier [Jul 31] called it a superb month with WIG20 one step from the record and Żabka taken over. The tape was choppier intraday: PKO BP and Pekao dragged WIG20 lower (Portal Samorządowy, [Jul 31]), PKO pulled back after its all-time high (DNA Rynków, [Jul 31]), while Żabka led with energy names strongly higher (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 31]). The card captures the pre-session positioning: PKO 113.44 (+5.8% 1W, +8.2% 4W), Pekao 246.3 (+5.8% 1W), CD Projekt 252.9 (+11.8% 1W), Dino 31.83 (+10.3% 1W, +10.9% 4W), LPP 21,420 (+5.5% 1W, +15.5% 4W), KGHM 309.25 (+2.4% 1W), Allegro 45.12 (+16.5% 4W); the losers are Orlen 146.48 (-6.0% 1W) after Comparic [Jul 31] asked whether the post-rally slide is a correction or the start of something bigger, and PGE 9.74 (-8.1% 1W) despite two analysts raising energy valuations (pb.pl, [Jul 31]). EPOL 42.76 is +4.9% 1W and +10.5% 4W, the dollar leg helping: USD/PLN 3.7357 (-1.8% 1W, -0.4% 4W) as the dollar fell back toward 3.75 after the Fed (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Jul 31]), EUR/PLN 4.3051 (-0.6% 1W, +0.4% 4W) with Bankier [Jul 31] noting the euro still above 4.30. Bonds: yields sat at new highs (wGospodarce, [Jul 25]) after rising worryingly ahead of the Fed (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jul 29]).

Fiscal & Politics: The Sejm granted the government discharge for the 2025 budget with 240 votes amid a row over the deficit and public finances (Bankier, [Jul 31]) and accusations of devastating the public finances (Interia Biznes, [Jul 31]). The backdrop: Treasury debt above 2.1 trn zł (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 24]), debt past 60% of GDP (xyz.pl, [Jul 24]) and Prawo.pl [Jul 31] asking whether the budget can afford tax cuts. On the fuel front, finance minister Domański says the CPN package has no financing after the president sided with the oil companies (Money.pl and Business Insider Polska, [Jul 31]). The higher tax-free allowance may be phased over three years (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 31]); the MF withdrew the lump-sum tax hike for sole traders (Radio Zet, [Jul 31]) and is pressing ahead with a tax on global digital giants despite Trump's threats (PolsatNews, [Jul 31]).

Corporate & Sectors: Couche-Tard will buy Żabka for about $8.7 billion in Poland's biggest-ever deal (Reuters, [Jul 31]) after Seven & i walked away (Bloomberg, [Jul 26]); WNP.PL [Jul 31] already asks whether a Circle K owner means new fuel-station competition for Orlen. Banks: Pekao beat forecasts (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 30]) though Subiektywnie o finansach [Jul 31] sees new risk in the machine; four banks nudged rates up even with the RPP on hold (Bankier, [Jul 31]); a CJEU and Supreme Court turn in CHF cases gives banks a chance to gain substantially (Interia Biznes, [Jul 31]); borrowers get 60 days to decide on the new rate formula (wnp.pl, [Jul 31]). Energy: PKP Cargo signed coal-haulage contracts with PGE GiEK worth up to 76.1 mn zł (Bankier, [Jul 31]), DM BOŚ lifted its PGE target to 14 zł (Bankier, [Jul 30]). KGHM unveiled a multi-commodity Strategy 2055+ with record investment (24jgora.pl, [Jul 31]). CD Projekt slipped on a gloomy conspiracy theory about Witcher 4 timing (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 31]) while IGN [Jul 31] argues no delay. Allegro must change how it displays delivery methods pending trial (Cashless.pl, [Jul 31]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland summoned and rebuked the Russian ambassador over the Kh-101 missile that crashed near Tarnawa-Kolonia (Al Jazeera and TVP World, [Jul 31]); the debate has moved to why the F-16 pilot did not fire (Gazeta, [Jul 31]) and General Jakubczyk's view that Russia is testing Poland but still fears NATO (Dziennik Wschodni, [Jul 31]). The army is preparing forward depots near the border (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 31]) and 100 kilometres of the fortification line are ready (RMF24, [Jul 31]).

New / Most Important: The 3.0% flash. A week ago the fuel pass-through was a Citi forecast; now it is a GUS print, with core food falling and one component doing almost all the work (Farmer.pl, [Jul 31]). That is exactly the "transitory" shape central banks dislike acting on either way, so the RPP stays frozen while the government, having lost the CPN financing fight, has no fiscal lever to blunt it. For equities the inflation print and the Żabka premium land on the same session as a record, which is why the tape wobbled rather than ran.

Playbook ✓: Sticky inflation keeping the NBP frozen — consensus view intact, and the record close validates the bank-led overweight.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: If the ECB hikes in September while the NBP holds, the EUR/PLN dynamic shifts in favor of zloty weakness, which would further import inflation via energy channels — and with fuel up 14% in a month that channel is already open.
