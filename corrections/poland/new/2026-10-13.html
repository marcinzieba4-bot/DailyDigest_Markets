🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, CPI final due

Macro Stance: The RPP's 3.75% hold (Strefa Inwestorów [Oct 7]) is being tested by the price pipeline. Parkiet [Oct 13] reports the Future Inflation Indicator clearly higher, Bezprawnik says shop prices are accelerating again, TVN24 reports prices at the highest level in years, and German inflation hit the highest in almost three years (PAP [Oct 13]). Bizblog [Oct 13] flags the final September CPI as due tomorrow and argues the revived CPN fuel scheme will not shield against rising costs; September inflation is already above the reference rate (Polskie Radio 24 [Oct 7]). Analizy.pl [Oct 13] nonetheless concludes economists think the RPP can wait, and Glapiński's guidance is a November hike only if risks mount (PAP Biznes [Oct 8]). The IMF raised 2026 global growth to 3.1% as AI investment offsets the energy shock (CEO Magazyn [Oct 13]) and its new Poland forecasts pair fast growth with rising debt (XYZ [Oct 13]); Domański says Poland will overtake Switzerland (Rzeczpospolita [Oct 13]). GUS confirms the average wage at 9651 zł (Super Biznes [Oct 13]) against a 7695 zł median (Rynek Zdrowia [Oct 9]).

Markets: The WIG set a historic record today (Business Insider Polska, Parkiet [Oct 13]) in a low-volatility session, with WIG30 up 0.49% (Investing.com), Grupa Kęty and PGE leading while LPP and KGHM fell (Inwestycje.pl), and Parkiet sees an open road to a WIG20 record even as XYZ warns new highs will be hard. CEO Magazyn [Oct 13] notes stocks are rising despite a third month of bond losses as investors look to OKI inflows. The zloty is not sharing the rally: USD/PLN closed 3.9278, up 0.6% on the week and 4.6% over four weeks, Comparic has the dollar above 3.92, Bankier [Oct 13] sees it heading toward 4 zł with the pound at its highest in nearly two years, and Commerzbank says the zloty is pressured by policy and fiscal risks (FXStreet [Oct 13]). EUR/PLN closed 4.3834, up 0.1% on the week and 1.0% over four weeks. EPOL closed 43.92, up 0.9% on the week and down 2.7% over four weeks. On the card, PGE at 16.79 is up 11.7% on the week and 21.9% over four weeks, Dino at 36.86 up 9.5%, LPP at 25660 up 6.8% and 17.7% over four weeks, Orlen at 153.88 up 6.6% but down 5.4% over four weeks, KGHM at 349.7 up 4.3%, CD Projekt at 262.6 up 3.6%, Allegro at 52.05 up 3.3%; PKO at 116.34 (down 1.6%) and Pekao at 264.6 (down 1.5%) remain the laggards.

Fiscal & Politics: Fitch's warning on the deficit (salon24 [Oct 13]) follows its revision of Poland's deficit and debt forecasts (Forsal [Oct 12]), and Parkiet [Oct 13] says politicians of every stripe have sinned on debt and now avoid hard decisions. INFOR [Oct 12] puts 2026 debt service at 115bn zł. The ministry's denial that it ceded GPW and KDPW supervision to Brussels (Gov.pl [Oct 12]) still echoes and the VAT exemption limit stays at 240 thousand zł (Gazeta Prawna [Oct 13]). The 4.8bn zł energy-cost support bill for energy-intensive industry awaits EC notification (Do Rzeczy, Strefa Biznesu [Oct 13]), while the Energy Minister set Wednesday's maximum fuel prices (Forsal [Oct 13]).

Corporate & Sectors: The day's structural news is nuclear: the Energy Ministry, PEJ and PGE signed an agreement on the second nuclear plant, with PGE to prepare site analyses for Bełchatów and Konin (wnp.pl, Bankier [Oct 13]), and ZE PAK contracted six SMR reactors with 1800 MW at Pątnów (Parkiet [Oct 13]); Westinghouse placed conditional orders with Polish suppliers for the first AP1000 unit (Yahoo Finance [Oct 13]). Bankier credits PGE's atomic plans for holding WIG20 in the green. In banks, PKO BP booked 371m zł of FX-mortgage legal risk costs for Q3 (Inwestycje.pl [Oct 13]) and the UOKiK fines total 147m zł with ING heading to court (CHF24 [Oct 13]). Dino will spend about 300m zł on reverse-vending machines for the deposit system, with almost 3300 already installed (Inwestycje.pl, Omnichannel News [Oct 13]), and Comparic asks why the stock is up 12% in a few days while the GPW weakens; unions are mobilising for the strike (WLKP24). LPP will automate its Tczew warehouse with over 1500 robots, the largest such installation in the world (Bankier [Oct 13]). Housing is reviving: JLL sees new-home sales in the largest cities reaching 50 thousand (Bankier [Oct 13]), and Warsaw prices are rising faster in some segments (Business Insider Polska). Wholesale power is rising from 431 to 556 zł (WNP [Oct 13]) and pellet is at a record (TVN24).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland blamed Russia for Starlink sabotage (Tribune-Democrat [Oct 13]), signed a security declaration with Norway (Notes From Poland [Oct 13]) and wants EU military production in "war mode" (Big News Network [Oct 13]). NATO's new Russia war plan is to "block, blunt, break" (Al Jazeera [Oct 13]), jets scrambled during a massive Russian air attack (Daily Express), and Tusk said Europe and NATO will not succumb to Russian pressure (Fakti [Oct 13]). Reuters [Oct 12] reported Patriot interceptor deliveries to Poland will be delayed; police detained a man over threats to the prime minister and the Sejm speaker (marketscreener [Oct 13]).

New / Most Important: A record WIG on the same day the second nuclear plant gets its governance structure and PKO books another 371m zł of legal costs captures the market's split: state-energy capex is the new growth narrative and banks are the funding sink. With final CPI due tomorrow and the Future Inflation Indicator turning up, the hold is safe only if the fuel cap keeps the headline from spreading.

Playbook ✓: Stay long PGE and the nuclear supply chain, keep Dino despite the strike risk given its 300m zł deposit-system capex, and hold bank underweights until the UOKiK and FX-provision cycle ends; keep USD/PLN hedges while the dollar eyes 4 zł.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Equities at records against three months of bond losses is the widest stock-bond gap of the year; OKI inflows are the bull case, Fitch and Commerzbank's fiscal warnings the bear case, and tomorrow's CPI will tilt it.
