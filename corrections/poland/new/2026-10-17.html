🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold / 2026 hike not ruled out

Macro Stance: Both halves of the RPP's dilemma arrived this week. GUS's final September CPI printed 4.1% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Oct 14]), above the 4% consensus (BIZNES24, [Oct 14]) and the highest in 17 months (Agro Profil, [Oct 14]), with transport up 17.1% (Newsmax Polska, [Oct 14]). The counterweight: NBP core inflation fell to 3.1% (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 15]), HICP rose to 4% but underlying pressure is weakening (Forex Club, [Oct 16]), PAP Biznes sees no broad spill-over of the fuel shock and October CPI below 4.0% [Oct 14], and Credit Agricole expects the fuel intervention to pull it temporarily to 3.3% (Portal Samorządowy, [Oct 14]). The RPP left rates unchanged at its October meeting (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 12]); members do not rule out moves (Business Insider Polska, [Oct 12]) and Masłowska said a 2026 hike cannot be excluded (marketscreener, [Oct 14]). Growth runs hotter than the hold assumes: GUS revised Q2 GDP to 4.1% from 3.9% and 2025 to 3.8% (BIZNES24, [Oct 16]), investment accelerated to 8.5% (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 16]), Pekao lifted its 2026 forecast to 4% (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 16]) and Domański spoke of shifting into a higher gear (wnp.pl, [Oct 16]). The average wage is 9651 zł gross (Super Biznes, [Oct 13]). Governance noise persists: the president will resubmit four NBP board nominations (Stooq, [Oct 16]) and Rzeczpospolita ran a sharp comment on the governor rarely performing his duties [Oct 17].

Markets: A record week. The WIG crossed 160,000 points for the first time (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 16]) and the WIG20 rose 0.92% on Friday to a historic high (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 16]), with Pepco leading, PGE and Tauron under profit-taking and Allegro at its most expensive in five years (Bankier.pl, [Oct 16]). Wednesday's US-CPI correction had taken the WIG20 down 0.40% (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 14]); Thursday closed flat at +0.01% with KGHM down 3.93% (FXMAG, [Oct 15]), breaking an 11-month trendline on copper-surplus forecasts (Comparic.pl, [Oct 15]). The card confirms the rotation: EPOL 44.56 (+1.7% 1W); Allegro 55.66 (+7.6% 1W, +11.8% 4W), Dino 37.77 (+5.5% 1W), LPP 26,400 (+5.4% 1W, +9.3% 4W), PKO 117.36 (+3.0% 1W) and Pekao 267.2 (+2.3% 1W) rebounding, PGE 16.5 (-1.2% 1W, +18.4% 4W), Orlen 154.98 (-2.7% 1W), KGHM 327.75 (-5.5% 1W), CD Projekt 262.4 (+11.0% 4W). Comparic flagged shorts building in Modivo, Dino, Kruk, JSW and Allegro [Oct 17]. The zloty regained ground: USD/PLN closed 3.8937 (-0.2% 1W, +2.6% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3656 (-0.2% 1W, +0.3% 4W); the dollar hit its weakest since early October at 3.88 zł and the euro slipped below 4.37 (Comparic.pl, [Oct 16]; FXMAG, [Oct 16]). ING still calls the zloty exposed while NBP delays tightening (FXStreet, [Oct 15]), Commerzbank cites policy and fiscal risks (FXStreet, [Oct 13]), while CEO Magazyn carries a 4.30 end-2026 EUR/PLN forecast [Oct 16]. POLGBs recovered: yields fell with the market targeting 6% (StockWatch.pl, [Oct 16]) after a third month of losses (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 13]) and an MF sale of 12.0 bn zł against 20.2 bn zł of demand (TradingView, [Oct 14]).

Fiscal & Politics: The state deficit reached 172.2 bn zł at end-September (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 15]), 63.4% of the annual plan with debt service an increasing burden (Gazeta Prawna, [Oct 16]); CIT receipts are up 30% while VAT barely moves (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 15]) and the gap is smaller than a year ago (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 15]). On 2027, Do Rzeczy calls the deficit and debt astronomical [Oct 17], economists question the revenue plan (TVP Wilno, [Oct 16]), the Fiscal Council warns of a snowball effect (Interia Biznes, [Oct 14]) and its chair points at 800 plus for savings (Biznes Wprost, [Oct 17]). The president signed the PIT and CIT changes for 2027 (Biznes Wprost, [Oct 16]). Bloomberg says political dysfunction threatens the $1 trillion economy [Oct 14], while QNB says developed-market reclassification reflects progress (Gulf Times, [Oct 16]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: KNF data show sector profits down 12% (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 14]); POLSTR will replace WIBOR with ZBP saying clients should not feel it (pb.pl, [Oct 16]); ZBP sees mortgage sales possibly above 130 bn zł (XYZ, [Oct 15]); lenders seek state risk-sharing to fund defence (marketscreener, [Oct 15]). Energy: PGE will pay its first dividend after a 10-year break (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 16]) under a strategy targeting over 30 bn zł EBITDA in 2035 and around 210 bn zł of investment over 10 years (WysokieNapiecie.pl, [Oct 16]); PEJ and PGE will prepare the second nuclear plant (enerad.pl, [Oct 16]) while NIK says the first is three years late and 2036 unrealistic (Forsal.pl, [Oct 16]). Orlen faces extra taxes in Czechia and now Lithuania (Next Gazeta.pl, [Oct 16]), will take four crude cargoes in October (Money.pl, [Oct 15]), and fuel caps fall from Saturday (Farmer.pl, [Oct 17]). Retail: Allegro opens Ukraine to its sellers (MamBiznes.pl, [Oct 16]) and takes Temu and Shein customers after new tariffs (Fakt, [Oct 16]); Dino faces a strike threat (Dzień Dobry Bełchatów, [Oct 16]); LPP will automate Tczew with over 1500 robots (ITwiz, [Oct 16]). CD Projekt started Project Hadar (IGN Polska, [Oct 16]). Housing: mortgage applicants rose 10.1% m/m in September (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Oct 12]) with prices flat at 16,229 zł per sqm (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 12]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland scrambled fighters as Russia struck western Ukraine (Al Jazeera, [Oct 16]) and RCB alerts covered two voivodeships (Interia Wydarzenia, [Oct 16]). NATO troops will train in Poland for potential peacekeeping in Ukraine (TVP World, [Oct 17]) with British units joining a wargame next month (The Independent, [Oct 17]). Warsaw links NATO troop presence to arms purchases (Briefs Finance, [Oct 16]) and Siemens targets the defence automation boom (TradingView, [Oct 13]). Poland wants frozen Russian assets back on Kyiv's agenda (Bloomberg, [Oct 15]).

New / Most Important: GUS's upward GDP revision to 4.1% is the pivot: it lands alongside 4.1% CPI, a WIG above 160,000 and a 172.2 bn zł deficit, so the RPP's hold is now an explicit bet that falling core and the fuel cap buy time while growth, not weakness, is the problem.
