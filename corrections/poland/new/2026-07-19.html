🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike talk

Macro Stance: The weekend reading from Puls Biznesu puts it bluntly: Polish interest rates now depend to a large degree on the situation in the Middle East (pb.pl, [Jul 18]). That is the frame the RPP's hawks built during the week. The NBP disclosed who voted for a hike at an earlier meeting (Tysol.pl, [Jul 17]), a Council member contradicted Governor Glapiński and called a hike more probable than a cut (pb.pl, [Jul 14]; Radio ESKA, [Jul 17]), and Direct Money warns mortgage holders that this forecast may mean higher instalments for many months ([Jul 17]). The data are better than the rhetoric: GUS confirmed June CPI at 2.5% y/y with prices down 0.5% m/m (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 15]), core inflation fell (Biznes Wprost, [Jul 16]), and food is cheapening at the fastest pace in years, though Rzeczpospolita expects a turn in the autumn ([Jul 16]). The problem is the fuel path: the CPN package has expired, Erste and Gazeta Prawna expect July inflation to rise ([Jul 15]), PKO BP sees a transitory move above 3.5% that will in its view stabilise rates (PAP, [Jul 15]), Eurostat's HICP already shows Poland at 3% and rising faster than the EU average (Do Rzeczy, Business Insider Polska, [Jul 17]), and Dziennik.pl says drivers wake up to a new reality of higher pump prices from Tuesday ([Jul 19]). No GUS activity data this week.

Markets: Friday's session left the week on the defensive. Parkiet reports that copper and geopolitics sank KGHM and the banks while Orlen and Żabka stayed green, and Rzeczpospolita saw investors fleeing before the weekend ([Jul 17]). Żabka jumped 10.87% and KGHM fell 3.32% on the day (FXMAG, [Jul 17]), with the Seven & i stake talks now described as advanced (Yahoo Finance, Reuters, [Jul 17]). Bankier's Masłowski calls this an advanced phase of the bull market with Allegro, Pepco and Żabka at their most expensive in years ([Jul 17]). The card after Friday's close: PKO 106.8 (-5.4% 1W, +1.4% 4W) and Pekao 229.0 (-6.3% / -2.7%) took the bank hit, KGHM 293.7 (-4.9% / -19.6%), Orlen 146.2 (+4.5% / +15.7%), Allegro 43.4 (+9.0% / +10.1%), LPP 19960 (+3.0% / +7.5%), Dino 29.15 (+3.3% / -0.2%), PGE 9.48 (+1.2% / -3.1%), CD Projekt 232.2 (-0.3% / +3.6%); EPOL 40.35 is flat on the week and +0.3% over four weeks. The zloty closed the week at its weakest against the euro since November 2024, with EUR/PLN testing 4.35 (Interia Biznes, TVN24, Business Insider Polska, [Jul 17]). The card has USD/PLN 3.7782 (-0.2% 1W, +1.8% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.328 (0.0% / +1.7%). POLGBs sold off hard with the currency (FXMAG, [Jul 17]); Business Insider Polska notes a summer wave of retail bond redemptions with the government offering switches ([Jul 19]).

Fiscal & Politics: The half-year deficit of 123.7 billion zloty, with VAT undermined by KSeF and cheap fuel (Parkiet, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 15]), is now the lens through which German media read Poland: high growth, high debts (Zero.pl, [Jul 19]; Money.pl, [Jul 17]). GlobEnergia prices CPN at 4.7 billion zloty for 92 days of relief ([Jul 17]), FXMAG circulates forecasts of a deficit at 7% of GDP ([Jul 15]) and the treasury's auction misfired (wnp.pl, [Jul 16]). Domański quantified the cost of presidential vetoes (Wiadomości Onet, TVN24, [Jul 17]) and left a door open to a higher PIT threshold (Fakt, [Jul 17]). The presidential VAT cut on electricity was rejected by the Sejm (Bankier.pl, [Jul 17]) and the president has responded with a referendum motion on energy prices (wnp.pl, [Jul 18]), while the government published draft industrial tariffs meant to deliver cheaper power for industry (Bankier.pl, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 18]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks end the week with the CHF law signed and Franknews predicting a new wave of suits as borrowers can stop repaying once they sue ([Jul 18]), on top of the CIT debate that Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK says would weaken banks' capacity to finance the economy ([Jul 18]). The sector wants the mortgage law amended to use the NBP reference rate because the WIBOR reform has stalled (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 18]). Credit demand is hot: Fakt reports record mortgage refinancing ([Jul 19]), Next Gazeta.pl says Poles are rushing for housing loans ([Jul 18]), and prices diverge with Warsaw slowing while Gdańsk races (Super Biznes, [Jul 19]). KGHM will build a 10 billion zloty mine in Pomerania with state rail and port support (e-legnickie, [Jul 19]) but reported a strong tremor at Rudna injuring two miners (TVP Wrocław, [Jul 18]). Dino unveiled a strategy of local shopping centres in 11 cities (Fakt, [Jul 19]) while still suing a union leader (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 17]). Orlen started biofuel sales in Slovakia (Energetyka24, [Jul 17]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Border Defence Component began operating, with four WOT brigades given new tasks on the frontier (Portal Obronny, [Jul 19]). The week's warnings of a limited Russian military or hybrid provocation against NATO (AP News, [Jul 15]) and Sikorski's false-flag drone scenario (Kyiv Post, [Jul 15]) remain the backdrop. Support for aiding Ukraine has slid from 77% to 52% (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 16]).

New / Most Important: The most consequential sentence of the week is that Polish rates are now hostage to the Gulf. With CPI at target but fuel re-pricing from Monday, a Council leaning toward hikes, a 123.7 billion zloty half-year deficit and EUR/PLN at post-2024 highs, the NBP's reaction function has shifted from "when to cut" to "whether to hike", and the market has already begun pricing that through the banks and the zloty.

Playbook ✓: A current-account and fiscal-sensitive currency weakening on an oil shock, and a central bank turning hawkish in response, is textbook.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Equities refuse to confirm: Allegro, Orlen and the Żabka bid keep the WIG20 near its 2007 record even as PKO and Pekao drop more than 5% in a week. Rising oil prices via Iran escalation disproportionately affect CEE energy importers; Poland's inflation trajectory is at risk of re-acceleration if Hormuz disruptions persist, making the anticipated easing cycle harder to initiate.
