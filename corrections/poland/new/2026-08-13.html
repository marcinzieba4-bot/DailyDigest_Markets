🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: Two GUS releases landed today and together they lock the RPP in place. The flash estimate put Q2 GDP growth at 3.8% y/y and 0.9% q/q (PAP Biznes, [Aug 13]), an acceleration from the prior quarter (Forsal.pl, [Aug 13]) that Bloomberg called more than forecast and a boost for Tusk [Aug 13]; pb.pl attributes the higher gear to investment fed by KPO money and credit [Aug 13], while wGospodarce notes consumption sagged as investment rose [Aug 13], and Gazeta Prawna says only Lithuania grew faster in the EU [Aug 13]. The same morning GUS confirmed July CPI at 3.0% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]), with fuel prices up 13.9% in a month (300Gospodarka.pl, [Aug 13]) and PAP Biznes describing the acceleration as mainly a fuel story [Aug 13]; Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK notes both prints matched market expectations [Aug 13]. The softer side is still there: xyz.pl argues the wage boom is running out of steam [Aug 13] after GUS reported Q2 average pay of 9,233.13 zł, up 5.5% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 10]), and ING flags a record primary-income deficit in the current account [Aug 13]. Citi's view that inflation leaves no room for a cut at the next meeting (PAP Biznes, [Aug 10]) looks safer after today.

Markets: The rally paused. After three days of gains the WIG20 fell with the whole index in the red (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]), dragged mainly by KGHM and Orlen (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 13]) while PKO BP was among the strongest names on its results; Modivo and Pepco were the session's biggest losers (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 13]) and Parkiet asked whether the bull market is finally short of breath [Aug 13]. The card, struck at Wednesday's close, still shows a strong tape: EPOL at 44.60 is +2.1% on the week and +8.6% over four weeks; KGHM at 367.15 is +6.1% 1W and +17.2% 4W, PGE +18.6% 4W, Dino at 33.38 +4.8% 1W and +16.9% 4W, CD Projekt at 266.4 +5.1% 1W. Banks woke up: PKO at 109.82 is +1.3% 1W and Pekao at 255.6 +1.0% 1W, +8.5% 4W. Orlen at 155.68 carried +3.6% 1W into today's selloff. The zloty softened slightly: USD/PLN closed at 3.7333 (+0.4% 1W, -0.9% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3038 (+0.1% 1W, -0.3% 4W), above the 4.30 line Bankier was watching [Aug 10] and in line with FXMAG's call for 4.31 or higher [Aug 12]. On POLGBs, the ministry sold PLN 10.72 billion at Wednesday's switch tender (TradingView, [Aug 12]) while Parkiet notes rising oil is lifting yields [Aug 13].

Fiscal & Politics: The headline decision is fuel: Tusk announced the return of the CPN programme as a reduced VAT rate on fuel (Forsal.pl, [Aug 13]), effective from Monday, with Gazeta Prawna costing it for the budget [Aug 13] and TVN24 publishing the ministry's estimate [Aug 13]; TVP World framed it as a cut ahead of summer's end amid volatile oil [Aug 13]. It comes as Tusk warns ministers there is less money for election promises (Interia Biznes, [Aug 12]), pb.pl says the debt snowball is rolling faster [Aug 12], the Fiscal Council warns there is no time left on the 2027 budget (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 12]), Prof. Chojna-Duch warns of Greek-style dependence (Radio Wnet, [Aug 13]) and Money.pl counts a record 229 days of work for the state [Aug 13]. The president signed the Personal Investment Accounts law (PAP, [Aug 13]), the ministry targets family foundations (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 13]) and tomorrow was declared a day off by prime-ministerial order (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 13]).

Corporate & Sectors: PKO BP earned 2.77 bn zł in Q2 and 5.29 bn zł in the first half (StockWatch.pl and Parkiet, [Aug 13]), results better than its strategy assumed (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 13]) despite a sharp rise in sector taxation (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 13]); the CEO sees grounds for 2026 to be the strongest year in the bank's history, a third retail covered-bond issue is planned and KNF cleared 500 mln euro of bonds as Tier II (Bankier.pl and Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 13]). Orlen fell on the government's fuel decision (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 13]) a week after reporting 15.9 bn zł of profit (Energetyka24, [Aug 10]). CD Projekt's confirmed layoffs on the Witcher Sirius project weighed (FXMAG, [Aug 13]); Dino rebounded strongly with investors positioning for next week's results date (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 13]); KGHM began a new borehole at Siedlisko for a future mine (Gazeta Lubuska, [Aug 13]). Mortgages are getting dearer again as banks lift margins (Comparic.pl, [Aug 13]) and the government hints borrowers may gain from a mass WIBOR annex action (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 13]). Nawrocki signed the nuclear special-act amendment allowing staged building permits (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]). Household assets hit a record 4 trillion zloty (Notes From Poland, [Aug 13]). Power prices exceeded 100 euro/MWh in 21 European countries (Gramwzielone.pl, [Aug 13]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk said Poland detained a Russian over a contract plot to kill a Ukrainian-American citizen in Warsaw (The Guardian and Euronews, [Aug 13]), which Kyiv Post called the first such plot on NATO soil [Aug 13]. NATO condemned Russian breaches of Polish and Romanian airspace (Romania Insider, [Aug 13]), Poland would need allied backing to shoot down Russian weapons over Ukraine (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Aug 13]), and the US intelligence warning that Putin may test NATO this fall (The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 7]) still frames the infrastructure hardening reported by Reuters [Aug 10].

New / Most Important: Growth of 3.8% with CPI back at 3.0% is a hawkish combination for the RPP: there is no growth case for cutting and the fuel channel is re-accelerating headline inflation, now to be partly masked by a VAT cut that costs the budget money the Fiscal Council says it does not have. The equity pullback on the day of the best GDP print in the cycle shows how much was already priced.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold on growth and inflation grounds; bank earnings (PKO's record half) justify leadership rotating from miners and energy back to lenders.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: If the ECB hikes again in September, EUR strength becomes imported tightening for an economy whose consumption is already sagging, and a fiscal fuel subsidy plus a record primary-income deficit is what the zloty will trade on, not the GDP beat.
