🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold through 2026

Macro Stance: The RPP gave the clearest guidance of the week: a Council member reassured borrowers there will be no hike in 2026, but said a cut is not to be counted on either (FXMAG, [Sep 4]). That is a hawkish hold with the hike tail removed, and it follows Commerzbank's observation that NBP language turned cautious after the FX pressure (tmgm.com, [Sep 3]). The data behind it are unchanged: August flash CPI at 3.4% y/y against 3.1% expected (BIZNES24, [Aug 31]), fuel up 24.2% y/y (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]), Q2 GDP at 3.9% y/y with investment up 8.4% (PAP Biznes and CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]). Today added a consumer data point in Poland's favour: Eurostat shows July retail sales up 3.6% y/y against 1% growth across the EU (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 4]), even though the national median wage sits at 7530 zł gross (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Sep 4]). The external constraint tightens next week: the ECB is expected to raise rates (WSJ, [Sep 4]) and economists are almost certain of one hike with markets expecting more (pb.pl, [Sep 4]), so the divergence that has weighed on the zloty widens rather than narrows.

Markets: The WIG20 closed the week at an all-time high (Parkiet, [Sep 4]), 4090 points per Rankia Polska [Sep 4], with banks and apparel driving the rally (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 4]) and Budimex leading the broad market (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 4]); Business Insider Polska attributes the new peaks to the big blue chips [Sep 4]. Thursday's session had set it up with energy names strong, PGE up 3.21% and KGHM 2.34% (FXMAG, [Sep 4]), and Bankier.pl describing a renaissance of listed utilities [Sep 3]. The card, as of Thursday's close, is broadly green: PGE at 12.23 (+13.7% 1W, +13.2% 4W), Orlen at 155.96 (+5.6% 1W, +2.1% 4W), PKO BP at 114.78 (+2.2% 1W, +4.7% 4W), Pekao at 258.9 (+2.0% 1W), Allegro at 45.09 (+2.4% 1W), Dino at 35.84 (+11.9% 4W), EPOL at 44.55 (+2.2% 1W, +1.1% 4W); the laggards are KGHM at 352.2 (-2.6% 1W) and CD Projekt at 232.7 (-0.6% 1W, -6.4% 4W), and LPP at 20820 is still -5.2% over four weeks. Bankier.pl notes the paradox in copper: the giants are losing volumes while their shares hit records [Sep 4]. The zloty turned: Fed governor Waller weakened the dollar and the euro fell to its lowest in a week (Bankier.pl, [Sep 4]), EUR/PLN is testing 4.32 (FXMAG, [Sep 4]), and both the zloty and bonds strengthened on Thursday (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 3]); the card has USD/PLN at 3.715 (-0.4% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.32 (-0.2% 1W, +0.6% 4W). POLGBs got a reprieve with that bond firming, but the backdrop is Xinhua's read that inflation and fiscal risks are pressuring Europe's sovereign markets [Sep 4] and Business Insider Polska's warning that rising yields may force a budget correction [Sep 3].

Fiscal & Politics: Piotr Arak's warning in Rzeczpospolita that Poland is approaching the constitutional debt limit with no exit strategy [Sep 4] is the sharpest framing yet of the 2027 draft, which carries a 282.6 bn zł state deficit and a 7.1% of GDP general government gap (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]). The expenditure side is being trimmed at the margin: CPK will get several billion less than it requested (Money.pl, [Sep 4]), and Interia reports the government will reach deeper into Poles' pockets through tax changes [Sep 4]. The government blamed the president for rising debt in the Sejm (pb.pl, [Sep 3]) and experts fear the rating agencies' response (Filary Biznesu, [Sep 3]).

Corporate & Sectors: Energy is where the money is moving. PGE contracted at least 1859 MW in the supplementary capacity auction for 2027 (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 4]); new power tariffs from Monday should save industry 500 mln zł (pb.pl, [Sep 4]); Orlen's wholesale prices are the highest in four years (Money.pl, [Sep 4]) and it is building a Baltic energy alliance and LNG bunkering in Gdynia (MamStartup and Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 4]). The cost side is building too: WNP.PL expects gas and power prices to shoot up in the hardest winter in years [Sep 4], Europe's gas storage is low (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 4]) and miners and steelworkers blocked central Katowice in protest (Super Express, [Sep 4]). In retail, LPP is trimming expansion to 750 from 950 new stores, but not in Poland (PropertyNews.pl, [Sep 4]), Allegro began testing bulky-goods delivery into a 10 bn zł market (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 4]) and has over 21.8 mln users (Bankier.pl, [Sep 4]); the Dino dispute escalated with unions threatening to walk out of mediation and notifying the labour inspectorate and police (Dziennik.pl and Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 4]). CD Projekt is still selling the 11-year-old Witcher 3 in growing numbers after Gamescom (pb.pl, [Sep 4]) with over 1000 people on new games (Interia Gry, [Sep 3]), but the Witcher remake is paused (MiastoGier.pl, [Sep 4]). PKO BP's POLSTR mortgages should lower instalments (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 4]).

Geopolitics & Security: EUobserver reports a wave of Russian sabotage attacks exposing counter-intelligence gaps on NATO's eastern flank [Sep 4]; Polish fighters intercepted a Russian Il-20 reconnaissance aircraft over the Baltic (UNITED24 Media, [Aug 31]); the NATO Secretary General briefed Tusk on regional security (PAP, [Aug 30]); Ukrainian troops are training on NATO soil (Business Insider, [Aug 31]).

New / Most Important: A record WIG20 on the same day a former government statistician warns Poland is nearing its constitutional debt limit is the week's defining contrast. The equity rally is real and earnings-backed, led by PGE's energy-price windfall and banks that benefit from a hold through 2026, but it is being financed by a fiscal path that the sovereign market has already flagged.  |  Playbook ✓: Holding rates in line with ECB tightening is standard; stay with energy and banks, which are the direct beneficiaries of no cuts.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The zloty strengthened on a dovish Fed voice, not on anything Polish; with the ECB set to hike next week, EUR/PLN at 4.32 looks like the floor of the range rather than the start of a trend.
