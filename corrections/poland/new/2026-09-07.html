🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, hike talk returns

Macro Stance: The RPP decides on Wednesday and the pre-meeting commentary has flipped from when, not if, cuts to whether the next move is up. TVN24 says experts are extinguishing hopes [Sep 7]; Bankier.pl reports economists naming a specific moment and scale for a hike [Sep 7]; Credit Agricole argues that strong growth and a possibly persistent overshoot of 3.5% inflation are arguments for tightening (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 7]); Business Insider Polska says a leading bank's economists now forecast a change in rates [Sep 7]. The data case is the August flash of 3.4% y/y (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 31]) against a 3.1% consensus (BIZNES24, [Aug 31]) and Q2 growth of 3.9% y/y (PAP Biznes, [Aug 31]); EY now names Poland the new growth leader as Europe slows (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 7]). The external side is the opposite: German industrial production surprised with a fall (pb.pl, [Sep 7]) and TVP World warns that Germany's weakness may become Poland's problem [Sep 7]. With the ECB hiking and oil surging, the zloty faces imported inflation that may force the NBP toward tightening sooner than domestic data alone would justify; the Council's language on Wednesday, not the decision itself, is the event.

Markets: Monday was another record: the WIG20 set a new all-time high (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 7]), took 4100 points (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 7]) and the WIG30 closed up 0.47% (Investing.com, [Sep 7]), with PGE and Tauron up strongly and energy the session leader (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 7]). Bloomberg frames the move as Polish stocks beating US and European peers as foreigners pile in [Sep 7]. The card, as of Friday's close, already showed the leadership: PGE at 12.55 (+15.2% 1W, +16.7% 4W), Orlen at 158.0 (+6.1% 1W, +6.0% 4W), PKO BP at 116.9 (+3.0% 1W, +7.2% 4W), Pekao at 263.9 (+3.3% 1W), EPOL at 45.12 (+3.8% 1W, +2.1% 4W); KGHM at 356.0 (-1.7% 1W) and CD Projekt at 232.0 (-1.9% 1W, -9.4% 4W) lagged. The zloty is still holding ahead of the ECB and NBP decisions (Bankier.pl, [Sep 7]) with the dollar losing momentum (FXMAG, [Sep 7]); the card has USD/PLN at 3.7098 (-0.9% 1W, -0.2% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3107 (-0.6% 1W, +0.4% 4W). POLGBs remain the weak leg: Allianz Trade flagged the rising cost of financing Poland's budget needs (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Sep 7]), Subiektywnie o finansach asks whether yields are the highest in 20 years [Sep 7] yet BlackRock and JPMorgan are betting on EM as turmoil grips global bonds (The Edge, [Sep 7]).

Fiscal & Politics: The question has moved to consequences: Business Insider Polska asks whether the EU will punish Poland for its debt and deficit [Sep 7] and Poinformowani whether the excessive-deficit status threatens penalties and EU funds [Sep 7]. The government is postponing a key reform, with presidential vetoes not the only problem (Interia Biznes, [Sep 7]); CPK will get 7 bn zł instead of 11 (Super Biznes, [Sep 7]); almost 100 bn zł goes to public-sector wages as the state starts to brake spending (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 4]). Domański denied starving the Constitutional Tribunal (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 7]) and cut off speculation about 800 plus (Radio Zet, [Sep 7]).

Corporate & Sectors: The state is reshuffling its assets around Orlen: Rzeczpospolita reports Orlen may receive part of PGZ [Sep 7], XYZ names it the favourite to buy the defence group's shares [Sep 7], and Bankier.pl reads it as another way to pull billions out of the oil champion [Sep 7], with investors reacting calmly (Interia Biznes, [Sep 7]). Orlen earned a record 7.7 bn zł abroad (Polskie Radio, [Sep 7]) and has risen almost four times to join Europe's energy giants (Bankier.pl, [Sep 7]). Allegro struck an AI voice-agent partnership with ElevenLabs for its delivery hotline (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 7]) and deepened the PKO BP tie-up, with Allegro Klik at 370 thousand users (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 7]). LPP passed 4000 stores on 47 markets with Sinsay driving growth (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 7]). Dino's unions issued an ultimatum to the board (Fakt, [Sep 7]). Banks: WIBOR court cases reached 2293 in Q2, slower but still flowing (Frankowicze.net, [Sep 7]), UOKiK ordered four banks to refund customers (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 7]). Energy prices are the common thread: exchange power prices are the highest in three years (Money.pl, [Sep 7]), wholesale jumped 93 zł in a month (Next Gazeta.pl, [Sep 7]), household bills may rise by 200 zł (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 7]), a German grid failure sent prices above 17 thousand zł per MWh (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 7]), the government is widening industrial power relief (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 7]) funded by a proposed CIT rise (Gramwzielone.pl, [Sep 7]) and the EIB is ready to finance Polish nuclear (wnp.pl, [Sep 7]).

Geopolitics & Security: Ukraine's intelligence chief says Russia deliberately fired a Kh-101 into Poland to test NATO's red lines (UNITED24 Media, [Sep 7]) and that Russia can sustain the war through 2027 and 2028 (Fakt, [Sep 7]); Poland says allies are ready to expand its role in nuclear deterrence (Anadolu, [Sep 3]); Germany blamed Russia for the Leipzig airport drone attack (NPR, [Sep 2]); TVP World argues the Russian attack NATO expects is already here [Sep 2].

New / Most Important: Foreign money is pouring into a record GPW two days before an RPP meeting at which the debate is about a hike. That is the rarest of combinations for Poland: equities priced for an energy-earnings boom while rates and energy bills squeeze the consumer and the budget.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold on Wednesday with hawkish language is consensus; stay long PGE and Orlen, short duration.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: If the Council signals a hike, banks gain but the sovereign loses; with EU penalty talk now explicit, the next leg of the rally has to come from foreigners accepting Polish fiscal risk at 20-year-high yields.
