🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike risk

Macro Stance: No new rate action, but the global backdrop moved against the NBP: the Fed hiked and, as Bankier.pl puts it, the US now has higher rates than Poland [Sep 17]; PKO BP counts 16 of 18 Fed members expecting at least one more hike this year (wnp.pl, [Sep 17]). The NBP has turned cautious as inflation rises, per Commerzbank, and the data keep pushing the same way: GUS confirmed August CPI at 3.4% y/y, the highest in 14 months (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]), and the NBP's core measure excluding food and energy rose to 3.3% (Portal spożywczy, [Sep 16]) with the Middle East conflict feeding core prices (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 16]). An RPP member told Bankier.pl the Council could raise rates [Sep 15], against Glapiński's line of stable rates possibly to mid-2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 10]). The labour side is cooling in a helpful way: labour cost growth slowed sharply from 9.7% to 4.7% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 17]) while 54% of workers do not fear losing their job (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 17]). The 2027 minimum wage of 4950 zł is set (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Sep 16]).

Markets: Warsaw ignored the Fed: the GPW closed all green with Allegro up almost 7% (CrypS.pl, [Sep 17]), the WIG30 gained 1.66% (Investing.com, [Sep 17]), Allegro's surge drove the WIG20 while Orlen sat at the bottom (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 17]). The card at Wednesday's close, before the results day, shows the setup: Allegro 45.15 (+0.3% 1W, +0.3% 4W) and LPP 21680 (+0.9%, +1.2%) both flat into prints that beat; EPOL 44.17 (-3.0% 1W, +1.2% 4W) reflecting the weaker zloty; KGHM 331.65 (-12.3% 1W, -4.7% 4W) the month's loser; Orlen 156.82 (-1.7% 1W, +0.9% 4W); PGE 13.83 (+1.9%, +32.3%); PKO 120.42 (+1.8%, +4.7%), Pekao 268.3 (+0.8%, +3.2%); CD Projekt 238.5 (+2.7%, -7.0%); Dino 35.8 (0.0%, +10.2%). The zloty is the casualty: USD/PLN closed at 3.799 (+2.5% 1W, +2.8% 4W), the dollar's dearest since July (Comparic.pl, [Sep 17]), and EUR/PLN at 4.3586 (+1.1% 1W, +1.1% 4W) has broken 4.36 with 4.38 in view (FXMAG, [Sep 17]), the euro at its most expensive since end-2024 (FXMAG, [Sep 17]). POLGBs turned: Business Insider Polska reports Polish yields falling after the Fed decision, the best performance in the world [Sep 17], after the MF sold PLN 10 bln at auction (TradingView, [Sep 16]).

Fiscal & Politics: The deficit after August is 151.1 bn zł (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 16]) but Bizblog.pl calls August a good month for the budget, provided it is not an exception [Sep 17]. The Fiscal Council dispute has moved to health: a 16 bn hole in the NFZ with Domański wagging a finger at the Council (Rynek Zdrowia, [Sep 17]). On fuel, the president filed his own bill to lower prices (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 17]) as the Sejm takes a second run at the 60% windfall tax (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 16]). The MF deferred KSeF penalties to end-2027 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]) and the NBP is asking for a lifeline worth billions for banks in the WIBOR phase-out (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]).

Corporate & Sectors: Consumer names delivered. Allegro raised its 2026 outlook as growth accelerates and shares hit a five-year high (Reuters, [Sep 17]): Q2 profit above half a billion zloty (CRN Polska, [Sep 17]), Polish GMV guided to +11-13% and adjusted EBITDA +11-14% (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 17]), a hoped-for InPost contract to 2031 (Portal spożywczy, [Sep 17]) and a Shenzhen office (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]). LPP beat strongly with net profit of 768 mln zł and EBITDA of 1744 mln zł (BIZNES24, [Sep 17]), sales up 20% y/y so far in Q3 (BIZNES24, [Sep 17]), a 2026 gross margin of about 56.5-57% (PAP Biznes, [Sep 17]), 750 new stores (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 17]), improving Sinsay LFL (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]) and no Red Sea delays (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]). Energy is the other side: Orlen's fuel-price decision fulfils a grim prophecy (wnp.pl, [Sep 17]), it has found an alternative to Saudi crude that will not return soon (Parkiet, [Sep 17]) and the Orlen Termika CEO was dismissed after warning the company would scrape the bottom on coal (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 17]); the Venezuelan loss is put at $230 million in crypto handed to scammers (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 17]). PGE has 300 tys. customers without bills as backlogs accumulate (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 17]). KGHM appointed a new corporate VP from the ministry (WNP.PL, [Sep 17]) and refuses a 6.5 tys. zł profit bonus the unions demand (Radio Wrocław, [Sep 17]). Creditworthiness is slowly falling as mortgages get dearer (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk warned Russia is planning drone or rocket strikes on NATO territory (AP News, [Sep 17]), possibly staged as accidents on the eastern flank (TVP World, [Sep 17]), and headed to Kyiv (The Guardian, [Sep 17]). A very massive explosion from a Russian drone 4 km from Poland (Kyiv Post, [Sep 17]) had Polish jets scrambled (Fakt, [Sep 17]) and Lublin and Rzeszów airports briefly closed (Global Banking & Finance Review, [Sep 17]). Poland plans an electronic security system along 112 km of the Ukraine border (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Sep 17]), will send Patriot missiles to Ukraine (UkrMedia News, [Sep 17]) and Trump says progress has been made toward a US Army base in Poland (Global Banking & Finance Review, [Sep 17]).

New / Most Important: The Fed hike split Polish assets in two. The zloty broke to 3.80 with the euro at a post-2024 high, yet POLGBs posted the world's best day and the WIG20 rallied on Allegro and LPP beats that have nothing to do with oil. The domestic consumer is carrying the market while the state-energy complex, Orlen above all, absorbs the shock.  |  Playbook ✓: PLN under pressure from rising inflation and a hawkish global backdrop; rotate from state energy toward Allegro, LPP and the banks, keep duration short but stop adding to shorts after the auction and today's bond rally.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Bonds rallied on the day the zloty fell hardest, and the index neared its peaks with Orlen at the bottom and the Fed above the NBP.
