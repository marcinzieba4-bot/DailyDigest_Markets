🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, hawks vocal

Macro Stance: The week ends with the NBP's hawks setting the tone. The bank disclosed which members voted for a hike at an earlier meeting (Tysol.pl, [Jul 17]), a Council member repeated that increases are more probable than cuts (Radio ESKA, [Jul 17]; Business Insider Polska, [Jul 16]), and Direct Money warns mortgage holders the forecast may mean higher instalments for many months ([Jul 17]). That is a long way from the governor's offer, only a week old, to file a cut motion after the holidays (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 10]). Rates are unchanged after the July hold (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 10]) and Erste still sees no cuts before end-2027 (Direct Money, [Jul 10]). The data give both camps ammunition: June CPI was confirmed at 2.5% y/y with prices down 0.5% m/m (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 15]) and core inflation fell (Biznes Wprost, [Jul 16]), but Eurostat's HICP measure puts Polish inflation at 3% y/y in June (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 17]), prices are rising faster than the EU average (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 17]), and the CPN fuel package has expired, which Gazeta Prawna and Erste expect to lift July inflation ([Jul 15]) while PKO BP sees a transitory move above 3.5% (PAP, [Jul 15]). The oil connection is explicit: the finance minister has "no good news" on pump prices (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 17]) and does not rule out bringing CPN back (Interia Motoryzacja, FXMAG, [Jul 17]).

Markets: Friday was risk-off: "copper and geopolitics sank KGHM and the banks", Orlen and Żabka stayed green, and Rzeczpospolita saw investors "fleeing before the weekend" (Parkiet, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 17]). Żabka rose a further 10.87% on reports that Seven & i is in advanced talks over a multibillion-dollar stake (FXMAG, Yahoo Finance, Inside Retail Asia, [Jul 17]), while KGHM lost 3.32% (FXMAG, [Jul 17]). Bankier's Masłowski calls this "an advanced phase of the bull market", with Allegro, Pepco and Żabka no longer cheap and at their most expensive in years ([Jul 17]), and Parkiet notes the WIG20 laggards that never joined the rally are leaving investors counting losses ([Jul 17]). The card before Friday's session: Allegro 44.37 (+13.9% 1W, +15.3% 4W), Orlen 145.88 (+4.6% / +16.6%), LPP 20040 (+6.6% / +6.9%), Dino 29.43 (+6.1% / +1.1%), CD Projekt 234.6 (+1.3% / +5.2%), PGE 9.28 (-0.3% / -4.9%), KGHM 303.0 (-0.5% / -19.0%), PKO 107.98 (+0.3% / +2.4%) and Pekao 233.3 (-0.9% / -1.0%); EPOL 40.64 is +2.7% 1W but -0.1% 4W. StockWatch counts five blue chips still due to pay 13.7 billion zloty of dividends this season ([Jul 17]). The zloty broke down: EUR/PLN tested 4.35, the highest for the euro since November 2024, and FXMAG reports "the zloty and Polish bonds strongly down" with EUR/PLN back above 4.33 and EUR/USD at 1.1440 (Interia Biznes, Business Insider Polska, FXMAG, TVN24, [Jul 17]). The card has USD/PLN 3.7782 (-0.2% 1W, +1.8% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.328 (0.0% / +1.7%). The week's POLGB auction had already disappointed (wnp.pl, [Jul 16]).

Fiscal & Politics: Domański put numbers on the cost of presidential vetoes and said they "weaken the whole potential of the economy" (TVN24, Wiadomości Onet, [Jul 17]), while GlobEnergia prices the CPN package at 4.7 billion zloty for 92 days of relief, deepening a deficit of 123.7 billion zloty after June (GlobEnergia, [Jul 17]; Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 16]). Public debt stands at 2.5 trillion zloty (wPolityce.pl, [Jul 14]), German media rate the economy as strong growth with a weak point in rising debt (Interia Biznes, Money.pl, [Jul 17]), and Bulgaria's post-euro deficit procedure is the cautionary tale (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Jul 17]). Tax changes for 2027 are settled with new rates (wnp.pl, [Jul 17]) and Domański left the door ajar to a higher PIT threshold (Fakt, [Jul 17]). The Sejm rejected the president's bill to cut VAT on electricity (Bankier.pl, [Jul 17]) and the KAS audits' 120 billion zloty of irregularities are now with prosecutors (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 17]).

Corporate & Sectors: The bank sector took two hits: President Nawrocki signed the CHF law, which speeds court handling and lets borrowers suspend repayments once they sue (Bankier.pl, Next Gazeta.pl, Radio ESKA, [Jul 17]), the Supreme Court sided with CHF borrowers (Super Biznes, [Jul 17]), and the NBP's warning of a new wave of lawsuits (Parkiet, [Jul 14]) sits alongside the proposed CIT increase (pb.pl, [Jul 14]). Also, PKO BP will switch off services and mobile authorisation for scheduled maintenance this weekend (Polskie Radio 24, GSMonline, [Jul 17]). The Sejm passed the Water Law amendment Orlen Termika lobbied for (wnp.pl, [Jul 17]), and Żerań's heat accumulator, Europe's largest, is largely complete (PAP, [Jul 16]). Dino's lawsuit against a union leader has become a scandal (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 17]). The EU published its ETS reform and electrification plan (Gramwzielone.pl, [Jul 17]).

Geopolitics & Security: Türkiye Today frames Poland's "deepest fear" as a Russian test of NATO in the coming months ([Jul 17]), after a week of Polish and Baltic warnings of a limited military or hybrid provocation (AP News, [Jul 15]). Washington detailed new sanctions on Russia (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 17]). Domestic support for helping Ukraine has fallen from 77% to 52% (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 16]).

New / Most Important: The zloty's break to a post-2024 low against the euro while bonds sold off is the week's verdict on Poland's policy mix: a 123.7 billion zloty half-year deficit, a central bank whose public voices now talk hikes, and a CHF law that raises bank litigation risk. Equity has escaped so far on Allegro, Orlen and the Żabka bid, but Friday's bank and KGHM selling shows the index is not immune to the same oil-and-fiscal pressure the currency is pricing.

Playbook ✓: CEE central banks leading rate-cycle turns is the pattern; a currency selling off on fiscal slippage plus an oil shock is orthodox.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The NBP is being pushed toward holding or hiking while oil prices surge (WTI up 6.1% over four weeks) and the government debates re-subsidising fuel, which would widen the deficit that is weakening the zloty in the first place. Banks at records a week ago now carry CIT, CHF and rate-path risk simultaneously; the strategic bid for Żabka says foreign buyers prefer Polish consumers to Polish balance sheets.
