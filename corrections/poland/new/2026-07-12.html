🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold, dovish pivot signaled

Macro Stance: The weekend read on the July meeting is that the hold at 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Jul 8]) matters less than the guidance around it. Governor Glapiński's post-meeting line that he could file a cut motion after the summer holidays and does not exclude a cut in 2026 (Gazeta Prawna, Do Rzeczy, [Jul 9]) was dovish enough for ING to call it "opening the door to rate cuts before end-2026" (ING Think, [Jul 9]), yet the same institution published a projection with higher 2027 inflation and lower growth (Analizy.pl, [Jul 10]) and a 2026 inflation band of 2.4 to 3.3% at 50% probability (PAP, [Jul 8]). Inflation is inside the NBP target and gold reserves keep rising (XYZ, [Jul 9]), with the bank having bought 82 tons this year (Bloomberg, [Jul 9]). Dissent is visible: two Council members see no rush to cut after summer (Reuters, [Jul 10]), Gazeta Prawna reads the Council as cautious with room for cuts only in the first half of 2027 ([Jul 9]), and Erste sees no cuts before end-2027 (Direct Money, [Jul 10]). Growth is holding up: PKO BP forecasts 3.5% GDP growth in 2026 (wnp.pl, [Jul 11]) and unemployment fell again (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 8]). Housing has cooled, with prices having "stopped rising" (Wszystko co najważniejsze, [Jul 9]).

Markets: Friday's card leaves the split intact: Warsaw at a record and the zloty at its weakest against the euro since November (Comparic.pl, [Jul 10]). Banks carried the index, with PKO BP, Alior and Erste at all-time highs after Goldman lifted its PKO BP valuation (pb.pl, StockWatch.pl, [Jul 10]) and Goldman also calling for Erste shares to rally 30% on Polish growth (Bloomberg, [Jul 9]). PKO closed 112.88 (+7.3% 1W, +7.9% 4W), Pekao 244.3 (+4.8% / +7.1%), EPOL 40.37 (+2.4% / +3.8%). The rest of the board is uneven: Orlen 139.92 (+5.3% / -4.7%), Allegro 39.83 (+3.6% / +15.7%), LPP 19380 (+4.5% / -5.9%), Dino 28.22 (+1.9% / -4.5%), CD Projekt 233.0 (-0.2% / +5.5%), PGE 9.37 (-2.6% / -6.2%), and KGHM 308.95 is down 7.8% on the week and 14.4% over four weeks following a near-7% session drop (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 8]). INNPoland notes CD Projekt "was supposed to be an investment hit" and so far disappoints ([Jul 12]). FX: USD/PLN 3.7853 (+0.9% 1W, +3.2% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.3278 (+1.0% / +1.9%); the euro has broken a key resistance (Bankier.pl, [Jul 10]), Societe Generale's downtrend target is 4.48 (FXStreet, [Jul 10]). POLGBs rallied on the dovish signals according to PKO BP (wnp.pl, [Jul 10]).

Fiscal & Politics: The finance minister is managing expectations ahead of an election-year budget, with the deficit the stated obstacle to tax relief (Money.pl, Business Insider Polska, [Jul 9]). Rzeczpospolita reports Germany now spends twice as much on its army as Poland ([Jul 12]), a reminder that the defence race is a fiscal one, while Brussels opened an excessive-deficit procedure against the euro area's newest member (TVN24, [Jul 12]), a signal of how the Commission treats CEE deficits. Domestically the Sejm passed the OKI accounts with up to 100 thousand zloty free of the Belka tax (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 8]), the finance ministry and UOKiK are at odds over 174.5 million zloty from the Gazprom fine (Interia Biznes, [Jul 10]), and President Nawrocki has less than a week to decide on the CHF-mortgage law, with borrowers hoping for a veto (Franknews.pl, [Jul 12]). The new Security and Defence Fund was presented as a resilience tool (Gov.pl, [Jul 9]).

Corporate & Sectors: KGHM dominated the weekend: a tremor of magnitude above 4 at Lubin and a series of shocks across the copper basin ended with all miners safe (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 10]; TVP Info, wnp.pl, [Jul 11]), while management framed a "Strategy 2055+" that includes a return to dividends (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 12]), a 32 billion zloty capex plan to 2030 (pb.pl, [Jul 9]) and a polyhalite mine in Pomerania billed as the second in the world (trojmiasto.wyborcza.pl, [Jul 12]). Orlen distanced itself from Germany's Schwedt refinery, citing the spectre of a Russian return (Rzeczpospolita, Next Gazeta.pl, [Jul 12]), days after Baltic Power began delivering power with 54 of 76 turbines installed (PAP Biznes, [Jul 10]). Banks face a UOKiK probe into credit-scoring after raids on BIK, mBank and ING (TVN24, [Jul 8]), PKO BP booked 297 million zloty of CHF provisions for Q2 (Bankier.pl, [Jul 10]), and the WIBOR-to-POLSTR migration gives borrowers two months to decide (Fakt, [Jul 10]). Retail: LPP's 900 zloty dividend (Bankier.pl, [Jul 10]), Sinsay at 2,500 stores with 500 opened this half (PropertyNews.pl, [Jul 7]), Dino's 148 new shops in H1 (Retailnet.pl, [Jul 10]) and a new Allegro warehouse near Łódź with 250 jobs (TVP Łódź, [Jul 12]).

Geopolitics & Security: Public opinion is shifting: a Rzeczpospolita poll finds more Poles now oppose than support transferring weapons to Ukraine ([Jul 12]), and the UPA dispute has escalated into threats to block Kyiv's EU membership (Hindustan Times, [Jul 12]) after DW reported possible cuts to arms aid ([Jul 11]). Sikorski said Russia lacks the resources to attack Poland "but is preparing something" (Ukrainska Pravda, [Jul 12]), echoing a week of warnings about staged provocations. MON's declassified donations to Ukraine exceed 16 billion zloty (Lublin112, [Jul 6]).

New / Most Important: Beyond the NBP, the most consequential shift this week is political: a public that has turned against arming Ukraine and a government openly linking Kyiv's EU path to historical disputes. For investors this changes the Poland premium from a pure Russia-risk story into a Poland-Ukraine relations story, with implications for reconstruction contracts, border trade and EU-fund politics. The dovish pivot remains the price driver, but the geopolitical floor under the zloty is also moving.

Playbook ✓: PLN weakening on dovish signals is textbook; bank equity at records on a growth-plus-easing narrative is consistent with the Goldman calls.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The NBP is cutting into a rising 2027 inflation projection with a visibly split Council, so local-currency bonds risk becoming a value trap if the zloty selloff overshoots. KGHM at a 14.4% four-week drawdown with a 32 billion zloty capex plan and a seismically active mine is a second divergence the record index is not pricing.
