🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → Rates on hold

Macro Stance: RPP week opens with the hold priced and the debate shifted to timing. Gazeta Prawna's preview says the Council will leave rates unchanged in July and that cuts are a 2027 question [Jul 6], while Rzeczpospolita observes that instead of hikes the market has begun dreaming of cuts [Jul 6]. The trigger was the June GUS flash at 2.5% y/y, back at the NBP target and below forecasts (Parkiet, [Jun 30]); Commerzbank noted that rate hike bets have vanished and ING sees the market starting to play for cuts in the second half of 2026 [Jun 30]. Two offsets keep the NBP on hold rather than easing. Fuel: the end of the CPN package could lift inflation toward 3% (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 30]), and heatwaves are pushing European power prices to records (wnp.pl, [Jul 6]). Activity: the manufacturing PMI fell to 46.1 in June against a 49.4 consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 1]), yet Poles are spending more in shops (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 6]). Wages stay firm: GUS put the January median gross salary at 7,447.16 zł (Prawo.pl, [Jul 2]).

Markets: Warsaw opened the week higher despite weakness in energy and KGHM (Bankier.pl, [Jul 6]), after Friday's 1.57% WIG20 gain with 19 of 20 members rising (FXMAG, [Jul 3]). The card, on Friday's closes: Orlen 132.92 (+7.7% 1W, -7.0% 4W), CD Projekt 233.4 (+7.4% 1W) with FXMAG asking whether the stock is heading for a new all-time high after Cyberpunk 2077 passed 40 mn copies [Jul 6], Allegro 38.44 (+3.3% 1W, +9.5% 4W), KGHM 335.0 (+2.2% 1W, -3.0% 4W), Pekao 233.2 (+1.9% 1W), PKO 105.24 (+1.6% 1W, +7.1% 4W), LPP 18,550 (+1.3% 1W, -14.1% 4W), PGE 9.63 (+0.3% 1W, -6.1% 4W). Dino, 27.7 (-4.4% 1W, -6.3% 4W), sits at its lowest since 2022 after the Barclays recommendation (XTB.com, [Jul 3]); EPOL last closed at 39.44 (+2.3% 1W, -1.3% 4W). The zloty is being driven from outside: Bankier.pl reports a strong dollar weighing on the zloty with the euro close to its limit [Jul 6], and wnp.pl wrote that the zloty stood at the edge of an abyss until help came from the US [Jul 6], a reference to the soft payrolls print that sank the dollar (Comparic.pl, [Jul 3]). Card: USD/PLN 3.7483 (-0.4% 1W, +1.8% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.2869 (0.0% 1W, +1.0% 4W). POLGBs: investors bought Polish bonds worth billions in May (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 3]).

Fiscal & Politics: The government launched Deregulation 2.0: Berek and Domański presented a package of more than a dozen tax changes to take effect in 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 6]), including a VAT overhaul (Next Gazeta.pl, [Jul 6]) and a property tax change under which one owner no longer pays for all (wnp.pl, [Jul 6]). It lands days after the president vetoed 21 deregulation measures, with Domański saying the government is not discouraged (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 6]). Defence financing is the other thread: Rzeczpospolita describes the EU's SAFE programme as a financial shield for the army with tens of billions of zloty at stake [Jul 6], and Strefa Biznesu puts MON's and the EU's financial plan under scrutiny at 36 bn zł of savings [Jul 6]. The CPN fuel package has ended with pump prices up around 60 groszy per litre (wPolityce.pl, [Jul 1]), to be recouped by a windfall tax on fuel companies covering 4 bn zł (Biznes Wprost, [Jul 2]). The EU convergence report still ranks Poland's public finances worst in the bloc (wPolityce.pl, [Jun 30]).

Corporate & Sectors: KGHM's Strategy 2055+ got its first market verdict: the stock was weak on Monday (Bankier.pl, [Jul 6]) as investors weighed over 32 bn zł of investment, which Energetyka24 calls only the beginning [Jul 6], against a dividend policy kept at up to 30% of profit (Comparic.pl, [Jul 6]) that pb.pl says is now in the background [Jul 6]; the company is studying investments in Morocco, Argentina, Canada and the US (Dziennik.pl, [Jul 6]) and a polyhalite mine near Puck on a deposit of over 303 mn tonnes (Głogów Nasze Miasto, [Jul 6]). Allegro signed a five-year agreement with Arvato for One Fulfillment with a new logistics centre near Łódź opening in autumn (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 6]) and 250 jobs (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 6]). CD Projekt's Cyberpunk 2077 topped 100,000 concurrent Steam players for the first time since version 2.0 (GRYOnline.pl, [Jul 6]). LPP opened Sinsay's 2,500th store, in Uzbekistan (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 6]). PGE's Żarnowiec storage project received its 260-tonne transformer (wnp.pl, [Jul 6]). For banks, the finance ministry has named the date WIBOR disappears for borrowers (Bankier.pl, [Jul 5]) after the TSUE ruling of Jul 2 preserved lenders' right to recover disbursed capital on void CHF loans (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 2]). Warsaw flat prices fell and lost to inflation (BitHub.pl, [Jul 6]).

Geopolitics & Security: MON declassified Poland's military donations to Ukraine, worth more than 16 bn zł (Lublin112, [Jul 6]), with Kosiniak-Kamysz presenting the data (TVN24, [Jul 6]) and the ministry stating every donation followed prior analysis by the Polish Army (Gov.pl, [Jul 6]); Poland said Patriot missiles were sent at the request of NATO and the US military (Anadolu Ajansı, [Jul 6]). Sikorski warned Russia could stage a NATO provocation, saying "they are up to no good" (TVP World, [Jul 6]), following the US warning of an armed test on Polish territory (The Telegraph, [Jul 3]). Ukraine Business News reported Poland signalling a possible block on Ukraine's EU accession [Jul 6].

New / Most Important: The evaporation of rate-hike pricing is the key development, and Gazeta Prawna's "cuts are a 2027 question" is the honest version: Poland has shifted from a potential tightening outlier to a boring hold in the CEE space, with the July projection the only thing that could move the date. The more actionable news is micro: KGHM has told the market that capex outranks dividend, and the share price answered on day one.  |  Playbook ✓: Falling inflation removing hike pressure is textbook; the zloty trading on the dollar rather than on the NBP is the expected state for a hold.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Deregulation 2.0 promises tax relief in 2027 while SAFE loans and a worst-in-EU fiscal ranking say the budget cannot afford it; the bond market, not the Sejm, will arbitrate.
