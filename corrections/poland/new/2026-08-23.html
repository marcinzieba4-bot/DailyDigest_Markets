🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike talk surfacing

Macro Stance: The weekend reading of the RPP minutes is that the hold is no longer unanimous in spirit: NBP disclosed who wanted a hike (Tysol.pl, [Aug 22]), and ING's summary says the economy remains resilient, strong industry and labour offsetting weaker construction [Aug 20]. That is not a backdrop for cuts. July wages rose 6.8% y/y with employment down 0.8% (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 20]), though wGospodarce asks whether the jump was seasonal [Aug 20]; industrial output grew 5.1% y/y (PAP Biznes, [Aug 20]). The inflation debate has moved from July's print to what comes next. Gazeta Prawna warns a second wave of price rises is approaching, not a 2022-style crisis but bills heading up again [Aug 23]; Super Biznes says everyone will pay for the record heat and drought [Aug 23]; PPI reaccelerated to 2.8% (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 20]); maximum fuel prices rose from Saturday (Portal Spożywczy, [Aug 21]) after another wave of wholesale increases (Super Biznes, [Aug 22]), while Citi expects the fuel measures extended at least through September (PAP Biznes, [Aug 17]).

Markets: Friday's close left Warsaw higher on the week with Dino the star (Parkiet, [Aug 21]) after a jump of more than 8% on in-line results (CrypS.pl, [Aug 21]), in a week that began with Parkiet seeing indices drift from their bull-market peaks [Aug 19] and turned when banks pulled the GPW up with PKO BP almost 5% higher (Bankier.pl, [Aug 19]). The card is unchanged from Friday: Dino 34.78 (+7.3% 1W, +20.8% 4W) and PKO 113.96 (+2.6% 1W, +6.9% 4W) lead, while KGHM 342.35 (-2.9% 1W, +12.8% 4W), PGE 10.19 (-5.6% 1W), LPP 20,800 (-4.7% 1W), Allegro 45.1 (-2.9% 1W) and CD Projekt 258.7 (-1.7% 1W) lag; EPOL 44.42 is flat on the week (+8.6% 4W). The S&P Dow Jones upgrade to developed-market status (Reuters, [Aug 21]) frames the weekend commentary. The zloty keeps grinding: USD/PLN 3.6918 (-1.2% 1W, -3.0% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3146 (+0.1% 1W, -0.4% 4W) on the card, after the dollar fell to 3.68 (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 21]) and Comparic.pl asked whether another 5 groszy comes off from a two-month low [Aug 22]. POLGBs have no domestic headline; the global one is loud: Parkiet's SOS from the US bond market [Aug 22], US debt above 40 trillion dollars with an economist discussing the safety limit (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 23]).

Fiscal & Politics: Fitch's affirmation at A- with a negative outlook (PAP, [Aug 22]; Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Aug 22]) is read two ways: Domański called it very good news (PolsatNews.pl, [Aug 22]), while CEO Magazyn says deficit and rising debt are a growing problem [Aug 22], Parkiet notes Fitch sees pre-election risks [Aug 22] and Gazeta Prawna adds the agency does not take the tax package as budget-neutral at face value [Aug 22]. The package itself is now a document: lower taxes for citizens, higher for giants and lump-sum payers (Bankier.pl, [Aug 22]), a rate rising from 19 to 22% from 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 21]), CIT at 30% for the largest energy firms (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 20]) worth close to 7 billion zł from Orlen, PGE, Enea and Tauron (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 21]). The political risk widened today: RadioMaryja.pl asks whether Nawrocki will veto the budget-related act, under the slogan of no more indebting Poland [Aug 23], on top of Gazeta Prawna's report that he may veto the tax changes [Aug 21] and the Palace's long list of objections (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 20]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks face a plumbing change: Fakt reports POLSTR is replacing WIBOR in loans and clients have two months to decide [Aug 23], and Bizblog argued the new benchmark is no better than WIBOR [Aug 18]. PKO BP has KNF approval for portfolio management (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]) but carries the 47.5 million zł KNF fine over structured deposits (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 18]) and Franknews' claim that over 68 thousand repaid CHF contracts could be challenged [Aug 21]; BNP Paribas admits a 73.6% loss rate in CHF suits [Aug 22]. Housing: Poland has the third most expensive mortgages in the EU (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Aug 22]) even with margins near multi-year lows (Bankier.pl, [Aug 20]). Dino added 148 stores in half a year and is not stopping (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 23]), though Parkiet notes profitability slipping as capex nears a billion zł [Aug 21] and management expects only a slow LFL recovery in H2 (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 21]). KGHM, fresh from EBITDA up 89% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 21]), is quietly pursuing a second mine in Chile (WNP.PL, [Aug 22]) and eyeing a US listing (wnp.pl, [Aug 21]). PGE and Ørsted finished all 111 Baltica 2 monopiles on schedule (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 21]). StockWatch.pl worked through what a 30% CIT means for Orlen dividends [Aug 21]. CD Projekt heads into Gamescom with investors waiting on the showcase (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 21]) and an October Netflix date for Edgerunners 2 (MiastoGier.pl, [Aug 22]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tarcza Wschód is at initial operational readiness in many sections (Strefa Obrony, [Aug 21]) with Bundeswehr help (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 22]). The US deployed a spy plane to Poland (Firstpost, [Aug 21]), NATO held unannounced exercises near Kaliningrad (UNN, [Aug 21]) and Romania says Russian strikes increasingly spill into NATO (ABC News, [Aug 21]); the Lviv Herald makes the case for Poland intercepting Russian missiles over Ukraine [Aug 23]. Defence spending is to reach 5% of GDP by 2027 (TodayPress.tv, [Aug 21]).

New / Most Important: The veto threat has spread from the tax package to the budget-related act itself. With Fitch keeping a negative outlook and explicitly citing pre-election risk, a presidential block on the fiscal framework is the one domestic event that could turn the warning into a downgrade; the second-wave inflation warnings make it harder for the NBP to offset that with easier policy.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold with a hawkish tilt; keep banks and Dino as the earnings-backed longs, treat zloty strength as a dollar story, keep POLGB duration light into the budget.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Developed-market status arrived in the same week a British fund is short Polish equities and the sovereign sits on a negative outlook: the index promotion is a flow event, not a verdict on the fiscal path.
