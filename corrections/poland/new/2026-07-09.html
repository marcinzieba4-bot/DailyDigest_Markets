🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, cut after summer possible

Macro Stance: The RPP held the reference rate at 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Jul 8]), Bloomberg's fourth straight month on hold as inflation risks decline [Jul 9], and then the governor moved the goalposts. Glapiński said he does not rule out a rate cut in 2026 (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 9]), that a cut is possible after the holidays (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 9]), and that he may himself table a motion to change rates (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 9]); PAP quoted him saying inflation is unlikely to grow significantly [Jul 9]. That sits awkwardly with the projection: NBP puts 2026 inflation between 2.4 and 3.3% with 50% probability (RadioMaryja.pl, [Jul 8]), ING's note headlined rates unchanged but 2027 inflation projected higher [Jul 8], and Gazeta Prawna reads the Council as cautious, with room for cuts only in the first half of 2027 [Jul 9]. The data support patience either way: June inflation surprised to the downside and cemented the hold (Parkiet, [Jul 6]), shop prices are rising slower than inflation (Interia Biznes, [Jul 9]), and nominal GDP rose 6.4% in the first quarter of 2026 (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 9]). Wages are the hawkish residual: Gazeta Prawna reports mega raises for hundreds of thousands from July [Jul 9], and the government will act after talks on the 2027 minimum wage failed (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 9]).

Markets: The zloty paid for the dovish turn: Business Insider Polska reported the currency losing after the governor's cut suggestion surprised markets [Jul 9], Bankier.pl saw the euro testing resistance as Middle East tension rose, with the pound breaking away from 5 zł [Jul 9], and FXMAG argued the zloty needs lower inflation or a weaker dollar [Jul 9]. The card, on Wednesday's close and before the press conference: USD/PLN 3.7692 (flat 1W, +2.3% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.303 (+0.3% 1W, +1.2% 4W), after the 19-month low that Reuters said the zloty pulled back from once rates were left unchanged [Jul 8]. Equities recovered: the WIG20 rose with Pepco leading and Alior Bank and KGHM strongly higher while Żabka and Kruk were under pressure (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 9]), reversing Wednesday's session in which the index began recouping losses while KGHM fell 6.69% (FXMAG, [Jul 9]). The card still carries that damage: KGHM 297.7 (-9.4% 1W, -8.7% 4W) after FXMAG said the shares tumbled again and investors are seriously worried [Jul 9], against Orlen 139.96 (+10.5% 1W, -2.9% 4W) as the oil-shock beneficiary. Banks held: PKO 106.0 (+1.3% 1W, +7.9% 4W), Pekao 235.0 (+1.8% 1W, -0.2% 4W). Allegro 39.7 (+2.9% 1W, +14.6% 4W) and CD Projekt 234.5 (+4.5% 1W, +5.9% 4W) stay the growth leaders; Dino 27.8 (-4.1% 1W, -6.1% 4W) after a Wall Street bank warned Dino, Biedronka and Żabka are overvalued (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Jul 9]); LPP 18,670 (+0.9% 1W, -14.7% 4W); PGE 9.36 (-0.7% 1W, -3.6% 4W). EPOL 39.78 (+3.0% 1W, +0.9% 4W). POLGBs: Bankier.pl noted yields had left their 4-month low [Jul 8] and ING blamed dearer oil [Jul 8].

Fiscal & Politics: The finance minister previewed the election-year budget (Money.pl, [Jul 9]) and cooled expectations on tax cuts, naming the deficit as the key obstacle (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 9]), while Prof. Chojna-Duch warned that debt is rising at lightning speed and the cost may be very high (Radio Wnet, [Jul 9]). The government is routing 23 bn zł of KPO money into a new Security and Defence Fund (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 9]), presented as a resilience tool (Gov.pl, [Jul 9]). The Gazprom row deepened: the ministry is blocking collection of a 174.5 mn zł fine, UOKiK's chief is outraged (Bankier.pl, [Jul 9]) and pb.pl reported the inside story of the decision [Jul 9]. On fuel, Motyka said the government does not currently foresee a return of the CPN shield (Radio Kraków, [Jul 9]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: the UOKiK and police searches of BIK and major banks over credit-scoring practices (Interia Biznes, [Jul 8]) hang over the sector, and Franknews.pl counts 25 thousand free-credit sanction cases, calling the "patho-law-firm" wave worse than the CHF crisis [Jul 9]. Orlen is in a governance fight: a scuffle at Solino involving former CEO Obajtek led to prosecutor notifications (Interia Biznes, [Jul 9]) and Orlen blames him for an unfavourable contract (wnp.pl, [Jul 9]); wholesale fuel prices jumped at all suppliers (Super Biznes, [Jul 9]). KGHM launched its largest investment plan at over 32 bn zł (pb.pl, [Jul 9]), with 10 bn zł for the Puck polyhalite mine (Strefa Biznesu, [Jul 9]). Dino had a sanitary recall of poppy seed with opium alkaloids (Onet, [Jul 9]). Dividend names are back in favour (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 9]). NBP bought 82 tons of gold this year and will bring it to a vault in Poland (Bloomberg, [Jul 9]; Bankier.pl, [Jul 9]). Play raised PLN 1.25 bn in bonds (Dealroom, [Jul 9]).

Geopolitics & Security: Sikorski warned again of possible new Russian provocations (Українська правда, [Jul 9]) after the US alert of an armed test of NATO on Polish territory (The Telegraph, [Jul 3]); the US will resume troop rotations in Poland, the defence minister told Bloomberg [Jul 6]. At the Ankara summit Nawrocki said Russia could use its military against Central and Eastern Europe (UNITED24 Media, [Jul 8]) and met Zelensky after the bilateral spat (TVP World, [Jul 8]).

New / Most Important: The divergence between dovish rhetoric and a hawkish hold is creating a confusing signal for the zloty. ING describes the NBP as in a "sweet spot", inflation contained enough to avoid hikes but projected high enough to prevent cuts, yet the governor has just said a cut after the summer is possible, and the currency sold off on the sentence rather than the decision. This is carry-positive for POLGBs and FX-negative.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold as expected, zloty softening on dovish guidance; stay long bonds, hedge the currency.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A governor hinting at cuts while his own staff raises the 2027 inflation path, and a finance minister citing the deficit against tax cuts in the same week the budget absorbs 23 bn zł of KPO for defence; the NBP is no longer the one central bank executing exactly to script.
