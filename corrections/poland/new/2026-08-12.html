🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: Rates stay on hold and the week's data argue for patience in both directions. GUS put the average Q2 wage at 9,233.13 zł, up 5.5% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 10]), which Gazeta Prawna called the weakest wage dynamic in a decade outside the pandemic [Aug 10]; wGospodarce asked what the RPP makes of it [Aug 10]. Disinflation is visible on the shelf: food prices in shops are rising only 1.2% (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 12]), the slowest pace ever recorded per Polskie Radio 24 [Aug 11], yet every outlet carrying the figure warns that fuel can reverse the trend (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 11]). Citi's view is that the inflation path may not permit a cut at the next RPP meeting (PAP Biznes, [Aug 10]), while Strefa Inwestorów still asks whether September is live [Aug 6]. ING flags Polish GDP and inflation as the week's CEE releases [Aug 7]; Interia floated growth of up to 4.1% despite the energy shock [Aug 5]. The economic index reading below neutral for August remains the caution flag.

Markets: Warsaw keeps printing highs. Bankier.pl reported new bull-market peaks with only the sWIG80 short of a record [Aug 11], CrypS.pl noted the WIG20 and WIG set records while foreign markets wobbled [Aug 11], and Wednesday brought further gains with XTB among the stars (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 12]). Leadership rotated: Dino and Budimex led on Aug 10, PGE exploded on Aug 11 and Modivo led on Aug 12 (Inwestycje.pl). The Norwegian oil fund switched from Żabka into Allegro and bought JSW (Parkiet and FXMAG, [Aug 12]). The card shows the breadth: EPOL at 44.39 is +0.7% on the week and +8.9% over four weeks; KGHM at 364.5 leads with +7.5% 1W and +14.1% 4W, Dino at 33.43 is +4.5% 1W and +18.5% 4W, PGE +17.4% 4W, LPP +14.2% and CD Projekt +12.5%. Orlen at 153.28 added 4.3% on the week. Banks lag: PKO flat on the week and -1.3% over four weeks, Pekao -0.6% 1W. The zloty drifts: USD/PLN closed at 3.7235, flat on the week and -1.6% over four weeks, EUR/PLN at 4.2985 (+0.1% 1W, -0.6% 4W), with Bankier seeing the euro waiting at the 4.30 line [Aug 10] and FXMAG expecting 4.31 or higher [Aug 12]. POLGBs are better bid: early August brought a rebound in treasuries (Bankier.pl, [Aug 7]) and the ministry sold PLN 10.72 billion of bonds at a switch tender (TradingView, [Aug 12]).

Fiscal & Politics: The 2027 budget is the fight. pb.pl says the debt snowball is rolling faster and the government is denying the state of public finances [Aug 12], and the Fiscal Council has the draft under scrutiny with a warning that there is no time left (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 12]). Rzeczpospolita asked whether Poland has just crossed the constitutional debt limit [Aug 9], Konkret24 answered that it depends on the definition [Aug 7], and public spending runs at 50.9% of GDP, above Germany, Sweden and Denmark (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 5]), with the state costing over 53 thousand zł per citizen (Interia Biznes, [Aug 5]). The ministry loosens and tightens at once: a middle-class tax relief bill is being written (RMF24, [Aug 12]), PulsHR sized the PIT change at up to 3,600 zł [Aug 11], savings up to 100 thousand zł stay tax-free from 2027 before a new levy (INFOR.PL, [Aug 12]), while MF files new PIT/CIT tightening (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 11]). Mining-tax receipts have beaten plan (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 10]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen earned 15.9 bn zł with record foreign profits (Energetyka24, [Aug 10]) and the shares are rising on expansion abroad (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 12]). Allegro bought back nearly 60 mln zł of stock (wirtualnemedia.pl, [Aug 11]), extended the Lux Med offer and is fighting Poczta Polska for Orlen Paczka (MamStartup, [Aug 11]). LPP's preliminary Q2 showed revenue up 18.5% with 230 new stores and EBIT up over 40% (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 6]), and analysts are revising full-year expectations for LPP and Modivo (Parkiet, [Aug 11]). CD Projekt confirmed layoffs on the Witcher Sirius project (IGN Polska, [Aug 12]) and analysts doubt the 2 bn zł incentive target for end-2026 (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 12]). KGHM's Nitroerg will invest 300 mln zł in defence explosives (PKB24, [Aug 12]). Banks face the CHF law in force since Aug 7 that suspends instalments once a suit is filed (Wyborcza.biz, [Aug 7]), the WIBOR-to-POLSTR annex wave (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 6]) and MF's request to the ECB for mortgages priced off the NBP rate (Bankier.pl, [Aug 10]); June lending was strong with good repayment (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 6]). Housing sales are accelerating as developer supply shrinks (Bankier.pl, [Aug 9]).

Geopolitics & Security: US intelligence warns Putin may test NATO with a limited attack on Poland or the Baltics as early as this fall (The Kyiv Independent via WSJ, [Aug 7]); Poland and the Baltics are shielding infrastructure against a false-flag strike (Reuters, [Aug 10]), and NATO condemned fresh airspace breaches in Poland and Romania (The Brussels Times, [Aug 12]). The foreign ministry repeated its call to intercept Russian missiles over Ukraine (LIGA.net, [Aug 6]), the government is building an F-16-plus-SMS alert system (Forsal.pl, [Aug 12]), talks on a permanent US base continue (NDTV, [Aug 5]) and Warsaw wants unused EU SAFE defence funds (Reuters, [Aug 10]).

New / Most Important: The wage print is the real signal: 5.5% nominal growth is the slowest non-pandemic pace in a decade, and with shop food inflation at 1.2% the demand impulse the RPP has feared is fading just as fuel threatens the headline. Records on the GPW and the Norwegian fund's rotation into Allegro and JSW say foreign money is still arriving, but the 2027 budget debate, the debt-limit question and the US intelligence warning sit underneath.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold is right while Citi sees no room at the next meeting; stay with the record-making domestic names (Dino, LPP, KGHM) and welcome the bond rebound and switch demand.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Banks flat while the index makes highs, and a possible ECB hike would tighten Poland through the euro channel just as wages roll over; a slowing economy with a 50.9% spending ratio is the combination bonds will eventually price.
