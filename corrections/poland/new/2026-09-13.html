🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike risk

Macro Stance: The RPP left the reference rate at 3.75% at its September meeting, as expected (PAP Biznes, [Sep 9]), with a statement little changed from July (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 9]). The guidance is what moved: Glapiński said rates will stay stable for a long time, possibly until mid-2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 10]), that there is no room for cuts (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 10]) and that the NBP will not follow the ECB, which hiked again (pb.pl, [Sep 10]). He sees a risk of overshooting the inflation target (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 9]) and the market has started to trade for hikes (26. Piętro, [Sep 9]): Credit Agricole argues strong growth and a possible lasting breach of 3.5% inflation justify one (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 7]), and Millennium sees CPI above 4% in early 2027, back at target only in the second half of 2027 (PAP Biznes, [Sep 11]). Fuel prices end the illusion of a quick cut (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 8]). Growth supports the hawks: VeloBank raised its 2026 GDP forecast to 3.7% (WNP.PL, [Sep 10]), Pekao lifted both inflation and GDP forecasts (Analizy.pl, [Sep 10]) and EY names Poland Europe's new growth leader (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 7]). GUS put the cumulative GDP loss from the war on Ukraine at 490.1 bn zł (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 9]). The draft 2027 minimum wage is 4950 zł gross (Parkiet, [Sep 7]), a 144 zł rise (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 9]).

Markets: Warsaw set records early in the week, WIG and WIG20 at all-time highs (Parkiet, [Sep 7]) and the WIG20 up 1.28% on Tuesday with PGE at a nine-year high (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]), before cheaper bonds hit the index on Thursday, the WIG20 falling 0.67% with a rout in KGHM (Bankier.pl, [Sep 10]); Friday recovered 0.74% with CD Projekt leading and energy in retreat (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 11]). Bloomberg reported Polish stocks beating US and European peers as foreigners pile in [Sep 7]. The card shows the rotation: EPOL 45.56 (+2.3% 1W, +3.6% 4W); PGE 14.03 the standout (+11.9% 1W, +30.1% 4W); PKO 120.52 (+3.1%, +8.5%), Pekao 267.4 (+1.3%, +3.2%); Orlen 161.12 (+2.0%, +5.9%); KGHM 351.9 (-1.2%, -0.2%) as LME copper slides (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 11]); Dino 35.4 (-1.6% 1W, +9.2% 4W); CD Projekt 235.4 (+1.5%, -10.5% 4W); Allegro 44.94 (-1.4%, -3.3%) ahead of results (DlaHandlu.pl, [Sep 11]); LPP 21560 (+1.0%, -1.2%). The zloty is stable but tired: USD/PLN 3.7241 (+0.2% 1W, -0.3% 4W) after testing 3.70 (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 9]), EUR/PLN 4.3227 (+0.1%, +0.3%). Bankier.pl noted oil at 100 USD yet the zloty holding [Sep 9], then pressure after the ECB hike [Sep 11]; Commerzbank says dovish NBP signals weigh on PLN (FXStreet, [Sep 10]) and UniCredit flags 2027 election risk (Exchange Rates UK, [Sep 9]). POLGBs are weak: Parkiet asks why bonds get cheaper when everyone is buying [Sep 11], yields may be the highest in 20 years (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Sep 7]). BGK sold PLN 1.23 bln of bonds on PLN 2.13 bln demand (TradingView, [Sep 10]) and completed a US$2.5 billion issuance (White & Case, [Sep 10]).

Fiscal & Politics: The MF reported state public debt up 7.0% q/q at end-Q2 (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 10]) to 53.3% of GDP (Newsmax Polska, [Sep 10]); Rzeczpospolita calls the 55% threshold dangerously close [Sep 9]. The 2027 draft budget carries a record deficit, rising debt and new taxes (Forsal.pl, [Sep 8]); Glapiński said it shows no tightening and that a 7.1% deficit has no effect on inflation (wnp.pl, [Sep 10]). The NFZ already has a 12 bn hole (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 9]). Nawrocki may block billions for the budget (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 8]), a deputy minister says the vetoes have already cost several billion zloty (Super Biznes, [Sep 9]), and Motyka blames the president for blocking cheaper fuel (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 11]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: the TSUE C-510/25 ruling allowed the balance theory in CHF settlements as long as it does not harm borrowers (Prawo.pl, [Sep 10]); POLSTR is entering mortgages in place of WIBOR, a first bank already cutting its rate (PropertyNews.pl, [Sep 11]); the NBP objected to the reference rate as a base for mortgage pricing (pb.pl, [Sep 9]); UOKiK searched three banks and BIK over possible collusion (Money.pl, [Sep 13]). Energy: Orlen cut petrol prices sharply (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 12]) and reassured on supply after Saudi Arabia shut a key pipeline (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 12]); the state may hand it part of PGZ (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 7]) and a negative ABW opinion reshaped its SMR plans with Synthos (Forsal.pl, [Sep 9]). A court dismissed the complaint against the nuclear plant's environmental decision (RMF24, [Sep 10]). Household power bills could rise 19% (Next Gazeta.pl, [Sep 11]). Retail: a union dispute at Dino carries a strike warning (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 10]); LPP has 4 thousand stores (INNPoland.pl, [Sep 8]); Allegro holds over half of a 2 bn zł retail media market (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 12]). CD Projekt showed the Witcher 3 remaster for Switch 2 (CD PROJEKT RED, [Sep 9]). Housing: the BIK mortgage demand index rose 9.3% y/y in August (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Sep 13]) even as creditworthiness fell (Biznes Wprost, [Sep 11]).

Geopolitics & Security: A Russian drone hit the Kyiv–Warsaw passenger train near the Polish border (DW.com, [Sep 13]), about 2 km from Poland (WION, [Sep 13]); trains carrying Boris Johnson and a former CIA chief may have been targeted (The Guardian, [Sep 13]). Tusk convened the services at the RCB and said Russian escalation is a fact (naTemat.pl, [Sep 13]), Poland scrambled jets (inkl, [Sep 13]) after RCB alerts in Podkarpacie (Nowiny 24, [Sep 12]), and NATO aircraft deployed earlier in the week against jet-powered drones (ABC News, [Sep 10]). Sikorski proposed NATO drills to pressure Kaliningrad (Kyiv Post, [Sep 13]), and Poland pledged 50 mln euro of military aid to Ukraine (RadioMaryja.pl, [Sep 12]).

New / Most Important: The NBP has moved from cautious to explicitly closed: no cuts possibly until mid-2027, with hike scenarios entering bank forecasts while the ECB tightens and oil sits at 100 USD. For a market that rallied to records on foreign inflows, a 53.3% debt ratio, a record deficit, POLGB yields at multi-year highs and a drone strike 2 km from the border make a harder backdrop than the index suggests.  |  Playbook ✓: Inflation caution still tracks ECB direction; stay with banks and PGE, which carry the record, and keep duration short while the debt debate runs.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: KGHM down with copper while the index made highs; the zloty flat while POLGBs sell off.
