🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, consumer cooling

Macro Stance: Today's GUS retail print cools the overheating narrative without breaking it: July retail sales rose 3.9% y/y in constant prices (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 24]), below analysts' expectations (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Aug 24]), with furniture and electronics driving the statistics (Money.pl, [Aug 24]) and Gazeta Prawna noting outright deflation in shops [Aug 24]. ING says consumers shrugged off higher fuel prices [Aug 24] but its GDP forecast has consumption slowing (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]); mBank sees the economy braking with 2027 growth below 3% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]); Pekao's economists still call the consumer in decent shape (Portal Spożywczy, [Aug 24]). The investment leg is stronger: Rzeczpospolita reports an investment turn with firms already spending over 100 billion zł [Aug 24]. For the RPP, which disclosed hike advocates in its minutes (Tysol.pl, [Aug 22]), the mix of softer consumption, 6.8% wage growth (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 20]) and PPI at 2.8% (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 20]) argues for an extended hold. Price pressure is building from fuel: Tuesday's maximum prices are 6.57 zł for Pb95 and 7.61 zł for diesel (Farmer.pl, [Aug 24]), and Biznes Wprost warns of a spectre of new inflation over Europe into year-end [Aug 24].

Markets: A quiet opening to an important week (Parkiet, [Aug 24]) turned into a GPW session without shine (Parkiet, [Aug 24]): the WIG20 sat slightly below 4000 points with KGHM's gain offsetting bank losses (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 24]), banks weighed on the index (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 24]), Pepco was the biggest faller (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 24]) and Bankier.pl credited KGHM with keeping the WIG20 afloat [Aug 24]. The day's casualty was CD Projekt, down sharply after confirming a 2028 Witcher 4 release, which pb.pl called an opportunity [Aug 24]. On the card, Friday's closes stand: Dino 34.78 (+7.3% 1W, +20.8% 4W) and PKO 113.96 (+2.6% 1W) lead, KGHM 342.35 (-2.9% 1W, +12.8% 4W) entered the day as a laggard, CD Projekt 258.7 (-1.7% 1W, +14.4% 4W) had its summer gains at risk, LPP 20,800 (-4.7% 1W) and PGE 10.19 (-5.6% 1W) trail; EPOL 44.42 (-0.1% 1W, +8.6% 4W). The zloty eased with the euro calm and investors watching Washington and the Fed (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]); the dollar tested 3.69 and the euro held below 4.32 (Comparic.pl, [Aug 24]); the card has USD/PLN 3.6861 (-0.9% 1W, -2.7% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3079 (flat 1W, -0.2% 4W). FXMAG sees a large correction risk in USD/PLN around the Jackson Hole speech [Aug 24], and Societe Generale says budget risks point to further zloty weakness against the euro (tmgm.com, [Aug 24]). POLGBs: Analizy.pl declares it the time of Polish bonds [Aug 24], against a backdrop of US debt slipping out of control (Interia Biznes, [Aug 24]) and European yields at multi-year highs on Iran-war inflation fears (Euronews, [Aug 18]).

Fiscal & Politics: The post-Fitch argument is about the president's pen. Money.pl reports the warning explicitly cites Nawrocki's vetoes, with revenues to be limited [Aug 24]; TVP Info says the ministry has priced the vetoes at billions for the budget [Aug 24]; PropertyNews.pl notes the negative outlook unsettles [Aug 24]. Domański is appealing to the president on the tax changes (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 24]) and says he has an idea how to convince him (TOK FM, [Aug 24]), while PiS has filed its own reform with a PIT threshold of 180 thousand zł (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 24]). The energy package is shaping up as the next dispute: the government will cut power prices for industry but pairs it with the CIT hike (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 24]), paid for by the energy and fuel sector (Gramwzielone.pl, [Aug 24]); Rzeczpospolita's analysts sketch a black scenario for energy giants' valuations [Aug 24]. On fuel, Poland and other EU states have a proposal to lower prices (Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 24]) and six countries want September EU talks on taxing oil windfall profits (Reuters, [Aug 24]).

Corporate & Sectors: CD Projekt's joint CEO confirmed at Gamescom that Witcher 4 is planned for 2028 (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 24]), calling it the most ambitious game worth waiting for (GRYOnline.pl, [Aug 24]); the stock fell hard (pb.pl, [Aug 24]). KGHM reported July copper sales up 4% y/y (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 24]) with strong TPM growth (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]), is preparing a large acquisition (FXMAG, [Aug 24]) and is looking across the Oder for a new mine (WNP.PL, [Aug 24]). Banks: July mortgage lending hit a record 14.8 billion zł (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]) even as borrowing capacity slips (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]); the finance ministry asked NBP about using the reference rate for mortgage pricing (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]); PKO BP issued 500 million euro of senior non-preferred bonds (XYZ, [Aug 24]) and PKO Bank Hipoteczny is coming with a third covered-bond issue for retail investors (pb.pl, [Aug 24]). Dino spent almost 1 billion zł on capex in H1 (Investmap, [Aug 24]) and keeps opening (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 24]); Allegro reported buyback trades (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]). GUS data show housing construction costs fell in Q2 (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland signed a 2.2 billion dollar deal for domestically made Piorun air-defence missiles (Anadolu, [Aug 24]). UK Defence Journal says Poland and the Baltics face rising Russian sabotage [Aug 24]; Sikorski warned a German exit from Schengen would have serious consequences for Poland (TVP World, [Aug 24]). Tarcza Wschód is at initial operational readiness in many sections (Polskie Radio Lublin, [Aug 21]).

New / Most Important: The consumer is no longer the engine: retail sales missed and consumption forecasts are being cut while investment carries growth. That removes the demand-pull argument for a hike even as fuel-driven headline inflation rebuilds, which keeps the RPP parked and pushes the Fitch question squarely onto the veto fight over taxes and the energy CIT.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold; stay with KGHM on copper volumes and banks on record mortgage flow, treat Witcher 4's 2028 slip as a known timeline rather than a thesis break, keep PLN exposure hedged into Jackson Hole.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: SocGen calls for zloty weakness on budget risk while Analizy.pl calls time for Polish bonds: both cannot be right for long, and the veto outcome decides which.
