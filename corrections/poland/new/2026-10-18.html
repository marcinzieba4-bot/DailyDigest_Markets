🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold / hike risk into November

Macro Stance: With markets shut, the weekend press re-litigated the RPP's hold. Parkiet notes depositors can be pleased with the Council's decision while borrowers have less to celebrate [Oct 18], Gronkiewicz-Waltz praised it as coming at the right moment (wPolityce.pl, [Oct 17]), and PiS says work on an anti-inflation package is under way (wPolityce.pl, [Oct 17]). The data behind the debate: final September CPI at 4.1% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Oct 14]), the highest in 17 months (Agro Profil, [Oct 14]), against NBP core inflation down to 3.1% (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 15]), HICP at 4% with underlying pressure weakening (Forex Club, [Oct 16]) and PAP Biznes expecting October CPI below 4.0% as the fuel shock fails to spread [Oct 14]. Masłowska's line that a 2026 hike cannot be ruled out (marketscreener, [Oct 14]) keeps November live. Growth cuts the other way: GUS lifted Q2 GDP to 4.1% from 3.9% (BIZNES24, [Oct 16]), investment accelerated to 8.5% (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 16]) and Pekao raised its 2026 forecast to 4% (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 16]). The institutional fight sharpened: the Palace accuses the government of trying to seize NBP reserves to patch the budget (wPolityce.pl, [Oct 16]) as the president resubmits four NBP board nominations (Stooq, [Oct 16]).

Markets: Friday's close is the reference: the WIG crossed 160,000 points for the first time (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 16]), the WIG20 closed 0.92% higher at a historic high (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 16]) and the WIG30 added 0.08% (Investing.com, [Oct 16]), led by Pepco and Allegro, the latter at its most expensive in five years, while energy names saw profit-taking (Bankier.pl, [Oct 16]); Allegro closed +2.72% (Stooq, [Oct 16]). Card: EPOL 44.56 (+1.7% 1W); Allegro 55.66 (+7.6% 1W, +11.8% 4W), Dino 37.77 (+5.5% 1W), LPP 26,400 (+5.4% 1W, +9.3% 4W), PKO 117.36 (+3.0% 1W, -2.1% 4W), Pekao 267.2 (+2.3% 1W), CD Projekt 262.4 (+11.0% 4W), PGE 16.5 (-1.2% 1W, +18.4% 4W), Orlen 154.98 (-2.7% 1W), KGHM 327.75 (-5.5% 1W) after Thursday's 3.93% drop (FXMAG, [Oct 15]). Shorts are building in Modivo, Dino, Kruk, JSW and Allegro (Comparic.pl, [Oct 17]). Zloty: USD/PLN 3.8937 (-0.2% 1W, +2.6% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.3656 (-0.2% 1W, +0.3% 4W); Friday saw the dollar at 3.88 zł and the euro below 4.37 (FXMAG, [Oct 16]). Views stay split: ING calls the zloty exposed while NBP delays tightening (FXStreet, [Oct 15]), Litwiniuk calls it still strong (Bloomberg, [Oct 14]), and Comparic debates a 4 zł dollar this year [Oct 17] against a 4.30 end-2026 EUR/PLN call (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 16]). POLGBs: Business Insider Polska today dissects record demand and yields [Oct 18]; yields fell last week with the market eyeing 6% (StockWatch.pl, [Oct 16]) after the MF sold 12.0 bn zł against 20.2 bn zł of bids (TradingView, [Oct 14]) and a third month of losses (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 13]).

Fiscal & Politics: The 172 bn zł deficit after September (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 15]) now carries a sharper gloss: wPolityce.pl puts the tax-revenue shortfall at around 48 bn zł [Oct 17], Gazeta Prawna has the gap at 63.4% of the annual plan with debt service a growing burden [Oct 16], and RadioMaryja.pl reports experts warning of rising debt and possible spending cuts [Oct 18]. CIT is up 30% while VAT stands still (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 15]). On the 2027 draft, the government writes new pension-indexation indicators into the budget (Gazeta Prawna, [Oct 18]), Do Rzeczy calls the deficit and debt astronomical [Oct 17], the Fiscal Council chair points at 800 plus for savings (Biznes Wprost, [Oct 17]) and the IMF warns European public debt could double (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Oct 17]). Domański counters that Poland must invest to stay among the richest (XYZ, [Oct 16]); the president signed PIT and CIT changes for 2027 (Biznes Wprost, [Oct 16]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: sector profits fell 12% per KNF (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 14]); Strefa Biznesu calls the POLSTR switch the end of loans as we know them [Oct 18] while ZBP says clients should not feel it (pb.pl, [Oct 16]) and mortgage sales may exceed 130 bn zł (XYZ, [Oct 15]); lenders seek state risk-sharing to fund defence (marketscreener, [Oct 15]). Energy: NASK and PGE plan server farms that heat homes (GSMonline, [Oct 18]), PEJ and PGE will jointly prepare the second nuclear plant (enerad.pl, [Oct 16]), and NIK says the first is three years late and 2036 unrealistic (Forsal.pl, [Oct 16]), which Konfederacja calls incompetent execution [Oct 18]. Orlen faces extra taxes in Czechia and Lithuania (Next Gazeta.pl, [Oct 16]); wholesale fuel prices stabilised over the weekend (Super Biznes, [Oct 18]) under new lower retail caps (TVP Bydgoszcz, [Oct 16]). Retail: Allegro already earns 1.4 bn zł from ads and will sell them off-platform (Interaktywnie.com, [Oct 16]), takes share from Temu (MyCompanyPolska.pl, [Oct 17]) and opens Ukraine to sellers (MamBiznes.pl, [Oct 16]); Dino weighs a strike threat (Dzień Dobry Bełchatów, [Oct 16]). CD Projekt changed its name, will pay no dividend and is building reserve capital (PPE, [Oct 18]) while Project Hadar continues (EGW News, [Oct 18]). Housing: Kraków is full of new flats as developers enter districts they once avoided (Bankier.pl, [Oct 18]); mortgage applicants rose 10.1% m/m in September (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Oct 12]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland is investigating a suspected violation of its airspace (Bloomberg, [Oct 18]) after Lithuania sent residents to shelters over a suspected drone (Reuters, [Oct 18]); RCB alerts hit Podkarpacie as Russia struck Ukraine and Polish jets flew (Rzeszów Info, [Oct 18]). ISW expects Russia to escalate sabotage in Poland this winter (RBC-Ukraine, [Oct 18]) and Tusk warned of possible Russian escalation before Christmas (Ukrainian news, [Oct 17]). NATO troops will rehearse Ukraine peacekeeping in Poland (Polska Agencja Prasowa, [Oct 17]) and Warsaw links NATO troops to arms purchases (Briefs Finance, [Oct 16]).

New / Most Important: The fiscal-monetary border is the story: a 48 bn zł revenue shortfall and a Palace charge that the government wants NBP reserves to plug it land in the week the RPP held with CPI at 4.1% and GDP revised to 4.1%.
