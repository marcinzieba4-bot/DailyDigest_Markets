🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The GUS flash for September landed today: CPI jumped to 4.2% (Interia Biznes, [Sep 29]), against the 3.4% the market had been working with a week earlier (Rankia Polska, [Sep 23]). The reaction function is openly contested: one RPP member told Bankier.pl [Sep 28] the Council "should raise rates", Ludwik Kotecki floated a pre-emptive hike (pb.pl, [Sep 24]), another member warned the NBP risks "paralysis" (Interia Biznes, [Sep 28]) and the Financial Times [Sep 25] called the central bank trapped in a political contest. Against that, PKO BP holds that inflation will exceed 4% at end-2026 yet rates stay unchanged (Bankier.pl, [Sep 29]), with a cut pencilled in for mid-2027 (XYZ, [Sep 29]). Demand is cooling: August retail sales rose 3.8% y/y against a 4.5% consensus and fell 0.3% m/m (PAP Biznes via wnp.pl, [Sep 22]); wages rose 5.6% y/y (INFOR, [Sep 22]) but real wage growth is the lowest in three years (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 22]); unemployment is 5.8% (GUS via Money.pl, [Sep 23]). ING BSK still sees Q3 GDP near 3.5% (wnp.pl, [Sep 22]) but GUS sentiment is softening into Q4 (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 24]).

Markets: Warsaw has lost its bid: Parkiet has WIG20 "close to breaking down" from its consolidation [Sep 29], a week after the indices retreated from bull-market highs [Sep 23]; the WIG30 closed 0.23% lower (Investing.com, [Sep 29]) with banks "under supply pressure" (Parkiet), Orlen and Budimex the heaviest fallers and PGE the lone large-cap leader for a second session (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 29]). From the card: EPOL 44.66, -2.2% 1W but +2.7% 4W; KGHM 321.5 (-6.8% 1W, -10.0% 4W) as Chilean supply worries unsettle copper (FXMAG, [Sep 29]); PGE 13.55 (-6.0%, +22.1%); Orlen 150.64 (-1.2%, -2.3%); PKO 124.36 (+1.0%, +9.1%); Pekao 280.0 (+0.3%, +8.1%); LPP 24,820 (+2.6%, +19.1%) after a results beat (Trojmiasto.pl, [Sep 24]). The zloty is the weak link: USD/PLN 3.8419 (+1.4% 1W, +3.2% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3685 (+0.6%, +1.0%), after the euro tested 4.40 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 25]), the weakest zloty against the euro since 2023 (TVP World, [Sep 24]); the dollar sits near 3.85 (Comparic.pl, [Sep 29]), up close to 8% since the Iran war began (XYZ, [Sep 25]), and oil is back above USD 100 (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 24]). POLGBs: the sell-off continues and banks are counting losses, meaning less money for credit (pb.pl, [Sep 29]); the spread over WIBOR is at a record (Parkiet, [Sep 26]); the rating downgrade failed to rattle the bond market (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 26]). Primary still works: PLN 12.0bn sold against PLN 22.5bn of bids (TradingView, [Sep 23]).

Fiscal & Politics: The cabinet adopted the final 2027 draft budget on [Sep 29] (PAP): a record PLN 282bn deficit (Bankier.pl), spending of almost PLN 978bn (Forsal.pl) and growth assumed to slow to 3% (TVP World). The 2027-2030 debt strategy adopted alongside (Gov.pl) puts net public debt at 57.4% of GDP in 2028, passing the 55% precautionary threshold and keeping the constitutional 60% limit in check to 2030 (Gazeta Prawna; Domański via wnp.pl); Sławomir Dudek warns there is no safety margin left (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 29]) and Parkiet asks whether banks can fund the gap. The tax package travels with the budget: a new 24% PIT rate, a higher first threshold, CIT increases and ryczałt changes (Rzeczpospolita; Gov.pl, [Sep 29]). The fuel-price law and the windfall tax cleared the Senate without amendments (Money.pl, [Sep 24]) and sit with President Nawrocki, Tusk demanding a signature (Money.pl, [Sep 28]) and the ministry costing his vetoes (Wprost, [Sep 29]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: PKO BP placed PLN 1.35bn of senior non-preferred bonds (Bankier.pl, [Sep 29]); August mortgage sales rose over 50% in value (Bankier.pl, [Sep 28]) and the book passed PLN 100bn (pb.pl, [Sep 28]); mBank drops WIBOR from new mortgages from October (Parkiet, [Sep 24]) while pb.pl [Sep 24] warns WIBOR litigation would be worse than CHF for capital. Erste is tendering to lift its Erste Bank Polska stake to 75% (Briefs Finance, [Sep 25]). Energy: PGE refinanced the Gryfino CCGT units with PLN 3.05bn from BGK and PKO BP (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 29]) and the cabinet's aid bill offers PLN 4.8bn to energy-intensive industry funded partly by higher CIT (Energetyka24; Parkiet, [Sep 29]). Orlen Unipetrol is protesting a Czech windfall tax (pb.pl, [Sep 29]); diesel is at PLN 8 per litre (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 22]). KGHM commits to Sierra Gorda in Chile (zmiedzi.pl, [Sep 26]). Dino opened 256 stores through September (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 29]). CD Projekt's free Witcher 3 remaster launched [Sep 29] with around 120 thousand concurrent Steam players (Bankier.pl).

Geopolitics & Security: A Russian Mi-8 briefly entered Polish airspace and F-16s scrambled (BBC, [Sep 23]); the Dorohusk and Zosin crossings were evacuated during Russian strikes (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 24]) and a drone crashed near Dorohusk (Defence24, [Sep 28]). Warsaw called the Starlink station fire sabotage (BBC, [Sep 24]). Danish intelligence says Russia could attack a NATO state within months (The Guardian, [Sep 24]). Lavrov invoked nuclear doctrine over the Patriot plan (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Sep 29]). Defence spending has more than doubled to USD 53bn (LIGA.net, [Sep 27]) and Fort Trump could cost up to USD 4.4bn by 2029 (Bloomberg, [Sep 26]).

New / Most Important: Two releases collided today: a 4.2% flash CPI and a PLN 282bn deficit adopted by cabinet. Fiscal expansion into re-accelerating inflation, with the zloty at its weakest against the euro since 2023 and POLGB spreads at records, pushes the RPP toward a hike it does not want while the market has already concluded the next rating move is down. Banks are the transmission channel: they hold the bonds, book the losses, are asked to fund the 2027 gap and carry the WIBOR overhang, which is why bank supply leads WIG20 lower.

Playbook ✓: Rates on hold with risk skewed to a hike; stay light on long POLGB duration and bank equity until the Sejm budget path and presidential signatures are known; keep PLN hedges on while USD/PLN holds above 3.80.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: PKO BP's house view (no hike, cut in mid-2027) is directly opposed to RPP voices calling for pre-emptive tightening after 4.2%; and PGE's rally into higher CIT and new levies (Parkiet, [Sep 29]) is the odd one out in an energy sector that Brussels says faces a difficult winter (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 29]).
