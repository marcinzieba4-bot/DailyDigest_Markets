🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, tight labour market

Macro Stance: GUS put the July unemployment rate at 5.8% (Forsal.pl, [Aug 25]), which with 6.8% wage growth and employment down 0.8% (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 20]) describes a labour market that is tight on price and shrinking on volume, exactly the combination the hike advocates revealed in the RPP minutes (Tysol.pl, [Aug 22]) point to. Demand is softer: retail sales rose 3.9% y/y in constant prices in July (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 24]) but missed forecasts (Strefa Biznesu, [Aug 24]), ING sees consumption slowing (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]) and mBank expects 2027 growth below 3% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]). pb.pl describes two faces of industry, the KPO booster driving output while Asian competition sours sentiment [Aug 25]. On prices the near-term signal is benign, with Portal Spożywczy noting prices rising ever more slowly but a turning point approaching [Aug 25], while PPI at 2.8% (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 20]) and fuel caps that rose on Tuesday (TVP Kielce, [Aug 25]) keep the pipeline warm. A hold with no cut in sight remains the base case.

Markets: The WIG20 is welded to 4000 points, up only 0.2% while CD Projekt fell 6% (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 25]); Parkiet calls the stock a ball and chain for the Warsaw market [Aug 25] after the 2028 Witcher 4 date wiped 1.5 billion zł off its value (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 25]). PGE and Dino led the gainers (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 25]); Comparic.pl says Dino is back in the game, up 34% since July [Aug 25]. The card captures Monday's rotation: KGHM 358.4 (+3.8% 1W, +18.5% 4W) and Dino 35.05 (+10.6% 1W, +17.0% 4W) are the leaders, EPOL rose to 44.52 (+1.3% 1W, +8.4% 4W), Orlen 154.0 (+1.2% 1W), PKO 113.14 (+1.5% 1W), while LPP 20,560 (-3.6% 1W), PGE 10.21 (-1.5% 1W), Allegro 44.71 (-0.8% 1W) and CD Projekt 257.2 (-0.2% 1W, +9.7% 4W, before today's drop) lag. pb.pl lists CD Projekt, Asbis and 15 others among its favourites for coming weeks [Aug 25]. The zloty has the balance of forces on its side: Bankier.pl asks whether the euro goes to 4.30 or lower [Aug 25], the dollar tests 3.69 (Comparic.pl, [Aug 25]) and the card has USD/PLN 3.6881 (-0.9% 1W, -2.9% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3059 (-0.1% 1W, -0.3% 4W); SocGen's call for euro-zloty weakness on budget risk (tmgm.com, [Aug 24]) is the counter-view. POLGBs: the finance ministry published its September retail bond offer (Bankier.pl, [Aug 25]), and Parkiet reports Bessent reaching for reserves to calm the US debt market [Aug 25] while Germany's deficit crossed a symbolic line (WNP.PL, [Aug 25]).

Fiscal & Politics: The cost of the presidential vetoes now has a ministry number behind it: Rzeczpospolita says it has learned the figure and it runs to billions of zloty [Aug 25], Dziennik.pl and Onet report the finance ministry's tally [Aug 25], and the presidential side responded through Szefernaker (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 25]). That follows Money.pl's reading that Fitch's warning cites the vetoes and limited revenues [Aug 24] and Business Insider Polska's note that the agency warns of record public debt [Aug 22]. The PIT race is now two-sided, with the government and PiS both proposing higher thresholds (Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 25]) after PiS asked for 180 thousand zł (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 24]). Gazeta Prawna expects energy prices to be a central theme of next year's campaign [Aug 25]. The customs duty on non-EU parcels halved their number (wiadomosci.com, [Aug 25]).

Corporate & Sectors: KGHM had its best news day in a while: Sierra Gorda's resources are significantly larger than assumed after the partner updated estimates (pb.pl, XYZ, [Aug 25]), drilling found real riches at a domestic mine (WNP.PL, [Aug 25]), Bankier.pl crowns it the world's largest silver producer [Aug 25], management sees room to raise the dividend (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 25]). CD Projekt was the star of Gamescom with the Witcher 3 expansion, a free remaster and a Blizzard crossover (pb.pl, Komputer Świat, [Aug 25]), but the market only priced the 2028 date. Dino got an updated buy rating (pb.pl, [Aug 25]). LPP started marketplace operations (Parkiet, [Aug 25]). Orlen opened its sea transshipment terminal in Gdańsk (Dziennik Płocki, [Aug 25]) and wants to double Norwegian output and expand in Canada (pb.pl, [Aug 25]). Banks: KNF officially recognised POLSTR as a significant benchmark (Bankier.pl, Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 25]) and wants mortgage rates based on the NBP rate (Bankier.pl, [Aug 25]), a revolution in instalments that the government and banks are preparing (Super Biznes, [Aug 25]); July mortgage sales set another record at almost half a million zł per loan (pb.pl, [Aug 24]); PKO BP's 47.5 million zł KNF fine resurfaced (Biznes Wprost, [Aug 25]) and the bank warned clients about phishing e-mails (TVN24, [Aug 25]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Piorun deal is bigger than first reported: a 1.86 billion euro contract for Poland and three NATO allies (Defense Express, [Aug 25]), with European allies teaming up to buy the system (Defense News, [Aug 25]). Poland told the UN that Russia's war endangers the whole neighbourhood (The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 25]), LRT examined Russia's threats against the Baltics and Poland [Aug 25]. Ukraine's foreign ministry said Ukrainians added 2.7% to Poland's GDP in 2024 (Ukrinform, [Aug 25]), while the Washington Post reports rising anti-Ukrainian sentiment [Aug 25].

New / Most Important: The veto bill has been priced. With Fitch's negative outlook explicitly tied to blocked revenue and the finance ministry now attaching billions to Nawrocki's vetoes, the fiscal argument has moved from forecasts to arithmetic, and every further veto is a direct rating input. Against that, KGHM's resource upgrade and the record mortgage flow show the earnings side of the market is still improving.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold; KGHM and Dino remain the earnings-backed longs, banks hold on mortgage volume, the zloty is bid on the dollar leg, and POLGB duration stays light until the budget and veto arithmetic settle.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: CD Projekt lost 1.5 billion zł on a release date while being named Gamescom's star and a top pick for coming weeks: the gaming trade has become a timing trade, not a content one.
