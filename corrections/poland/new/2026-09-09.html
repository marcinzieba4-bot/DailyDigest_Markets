🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike talk

Macro Stance: The RPP kept the reference rate at 3.75% today, as expected (PAP Biznes, [Sep 9]), with a statement little changed from July (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 9]). The press conference was firmer than the text: Glapiński sees a risk of overshooting the inflation target (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 9]) and left borrowers no illusion that instalments will fall soon (wnp.pl, [Sep 9]). 26. Piętro says the market has begun to trade for hikes [Sep 9], and Credit Agricole argued that strong growth and a possible lasting breach of 3.5% inflation justify tightening (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 7]). PKO BP sees no conditions for quick cuts but no need to hike (wnp.pl, [Sep 9]). Rzeczpospolita blames fuel for blocking the Council [Sep 8], while Domański reassures that inflation sits at 3% (Bankier.pl, [Sep 9]). Growth gives the hawks cover: EY names Poland Europe's new growth leader (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 7]), GUS reported a record tourist summer (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 8]) and Eurostat retail sales rose 3.6% y/y in July against 1% in the EU (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 4]). GUS put the cumulative GDP loss from Russia's war on Ukraine at 490.1 bn zł (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 9]). The draft 2027 minimum wage is 4950 zł gross (Parkiet, [Sep 7]).

Markets: Warsaw's run paused on decision day: the WIG20 fell after five sessions of gains, with KGHM and Orlen the exceptions (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 9]) and Dino, CD Projekt and LPP lost most (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 9]). The setup was euphoric: WIG and WIG20 set records on Tuesday with PGE at a nine-year high (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]), PGE and Tauron rose 4.94% and 4.80% (FXMAG, [Sep 8]), the WIG20 took 4100 points on Monday (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 7]) and Bloomberg reported Polish stocks beating US and European peers as foreigners pile in [Sep 7]. The card confirms the rotation into state assets: EPOL 45.76 (+3.8% 1W, +3.6% 4W); PGE 13.7 (+24.6% 1W, +22.9% 4W) after five sessions worth nearly 25% (Comparic.pl, [Sep 9]); KGHM 374.7 (+8.0% 1W) on the BHP letter of intent; Pekao 268.6 (+4.8% 1W) and PKO 117.92 (+3.7%, +8.1% 4W); Orlen 158.6 (+2.0%), Trigon's new favourite (Parkiet, [Sep 8]); Dino 37.37 (+11.8% 4W); LPP 22080 (+5.7% 1W, -1.6% 4W); CD Projekt 239.4 (-8.1% 4W); Allegro 45.88 (-2.1% 4W). The zloty holds despite Brent: USD/PLN closed at 3.7075 (-0.8% 1W, -0.4% 4W) and tests 3.70 (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 9]); EUR/PLN 4.3108 (-0.5% 1W, +0.3% 4W) as the euro waited on the RPP (Bankier.pl, [Sep 9]); UniCredit flags 2027 election risk (Exchange Rates UK, [Sep 9]). POLGBs remain the weak leg: Subiektywnie o finansach asks whether yields are the highest in 20 years [Sep 7] and Allianz Trade warns of the rising cost of financing the budget (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Sep 7]), yet pb.pl calls it a moment to start buying [Sep 3]. Households are the marginal buyer, with over 26.5 bn zł into savings bonds over the summer (Interia Biznes, [Sep 9]).

Fiscal & Politics: Rzeczpospolita says the 55% of GDP threshold is dangerously close [Sep 9], Forsal.pl sums up the 2027 draft as a record deficit, rising debt and new taxes [Sep 8], and the NFZ already carries a 12 bn hole (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 9]). The ministry will push 75.7 bn zł from the budget into the market by year-end (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]). Politics is the swing factor: Nawrocki may block billions for the budget (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 8]), Domański calls the three vetoes a stalemate (Money.pl, [Sep 9]) and a deputy minister says they have already cost several billion zloty (Super Biznes, [Sep 9]). The lump-sum tax limit drops from 2 mln euro to 250 tys. euro (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 9]). The crypto bill failed a third time (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 6]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks: the NBP responded sharply to proposals to detach instalments from the policy rate (Bankier.pl, [Sep 9]); WIBOR litigation reached 2293 cases in Q2 (Frankowicze.net, [Sep 7]); UOKiK ordered Alior, mBank, BNP Paribas and Millennium to change procedures (Prawo.pl, [Sep 7]); PKO BP puts the defence investment impulse at nearly 1.3 bln zł (XYZ, [Sep 8]). Energy: the MAP chief cut speculation that Orlen will take over PGZ (Bankier.pl, [Sep 9]), and Orlen and Synthos are agreeing a new SMR contract after a negative ABW opinion (pb.pl, [Sep 9]). PGE will receive hundreds of millions from defence money with a BGK loan of up to 300 mln zł (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 8]) and secured 2027 capacity at over 325 zł (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 8]); power prices are up 61% (WNP.PL, [Sep 8]), and a higher CIT on energy will fund relief for energy-intensive firms (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 8]). KGHM and BHP signed a letter of intent (Bankier.pl, [Sep 8]). Retail: Dino launched a -25% campaign (RMF FM, [Sep 9]) under a union ultimatum (Fakt, [Sep 7]); LPP passed 4000 stores on 47 markets (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 7]); Allegro added an ElevenLabs voice agent (Parkiet, [Sep 7]). CD Projekt wants to be a multi-project studio with capex still rising (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 8]). Mortgage demand fell over 14% (FXMAG, [Sep 9]) while the average loan tops half a million zloty (Bankier.pl, [Sep 9]).

Geopolitics & Security: Jets were scrambled over Podkarpacie and Lublin (FXMAG, [Sep 8]). US and Polish officials met as concern grows over Russia's NATO threat (The Independent, [Sep 9]), the security chief met the CIA director (TVP World, [Sep 8]), and Ukraine's intelligence chief says Russia deliberately fired a Kh-101 into Poland to test red lines (UNITED24 Media, [Sep 7]). NATO allies are ready to widen Warsaw's role in nuclear deterrence (Anadolu Ajansı, [Sep 3]) and NATO countries are queuing for Polish weapons (Forsal.pl, [Sep 9]).

New / Most Important: Today's hold matters for its guidance, not the number: the RPP now talks about overshooting the target with Brent at 100 USD, and the curve has flipped from cuts to hike risk. That anchors bank margins and keeps PGE and KGHM momentum intact, but leaves POLGBs carrying a record deficit at two-decade-high yields with no help from the NBP.  |  Playbook ✓: NBP anchored while ECB hikes, classic CE policy divergence; banks and state energy remain the carry, duration stays short.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Foreigners pile into a record WIG20 while households mop up POLGBs and the zloty sits at 3.70 with oil at 100 USD; the first red session came from Dino, CD Projekt and LPP, not the leaders.
