🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: The NBP is on hold and the market is arguing about which way the bias points. wGospodarce says the RPP can calmly wait [Jun 23] and Parkiet's read is simply that the Council is waiting [Jun 25], but NBP's disclosure of who voted for a hike (Tysol.pl, [Jun 22]) and its own survey showing inflation under control yet cuts uncertain (Parkiet, [Jun 24]) argue against the dovish narrative; Barclays expects zloty underperformance on central-bank policy (Investing.com, [Jun 25]), which contrasts with the ECB's hawkish posture. Prices are soft at the margin: a family's shopping basket got cheaper in May, if only by about 5 zł (Polskie Radio 24, [Jun 25]), food inflation is clearly weakening (CEO Magazyn, [Jun 19]) and wages still grow faster than prices (XYZ, [Jun 22]). The real economy is uneven: May industrial output rose 4.1% y/y above consensus (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 19]) and industrial orders jumped over 140% on the SAFE effect (Interia Biznes, [Jun 24]), but construction slowed despite EU billions and disappointed the market (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 22]), retail sales missed again (pb.pl, [Jun 22]) and corporate wages rose 5.8% y/y, below expectations (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 22]). A new angle: economists warn the heatwave is costing jobs and GDP (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 26]).

Markets: Friday was a correction after Thursday's 1.26% WIG20 bounce (BIZNES24, [Jun 25]): Inwestycje.pl reports the WIG20 lower with Kęty and Żabka moving against the market [Jun 26], StockWatch.pl ties it to a global equity sell-off with Allegro, Kęty, PGE and Text in play [Jun 26],. The card, which captures Thursday's close, shows a partial repair: EPOL at 38.55 is still down 4.2% on the week and 4.7% over four weeks; KGHM at 329.5 remains 12.0% lower on the week after Wednesday's 7.5% drop (wnp.pl, [Jun 24]); Orlen recovered to 125.94 (+0.7% 1W, -9.2% 4W) and PGE to 9.78 (+0.2% 1W, -8.8% 4W); PKO at 104.2 and Pekao at 230.0 are off 1.2% and 2.4%; Allegro at 37.58 is down 2.4% on the week but up 9.7% over four; LPP (18450) is still 15.5% below its level four weeks ago; CD Projekt at 223.4 is losing its 220 zł level in Friday trade (CrypS.pl, [Jun 26]). FX stabilised: USD/PLN closed at 3.7661 (+1.4% 1W, +3.9% 4W), EUR/PLN at 4.2788 (+0.6% 1W, +1.3% 4W), the euro held below 4.30 zł (Bankier.pl, [Jun 26]) and pb.pl reports a weaker dollar, a stable zloty and falling bond yields [Jun 26], though Comparic.pl saw the dollar resume its rally intraday [Jun 26]. Bonds: Poland pays record interest on its debt (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 25]) and July retail bonds pay up to 5.65% (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jun 24]).

Fiscal & Politics: The Senate inserted a safety valve into the windfall-tax bill after a 74% effective rate on oil companies was floated (Wyborcza.biz, [Jun 26]), a week after the Sejm passed the 60% levy (Money.pl, [Jun 19]). Debt dynamics dominate: wPolityce.pl calculates borrowing of 0.9 bn zł a day in 2025 and 1.5 bn zł a day in May 2026 [Jun 26], Treasury debt rose 46.8 bn zł in a month (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 22]) and a rating agency voiced concern (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 23]). The government is reaching for levies: higher alcohol and sugar duties for healthcare (Bloomberg, [Jun 24]), a sugar tax widened from 2027 (PolitykaZdrowotna.com, [Jun 26]), higher tourist climate fees (WP Turystyka, [Jun 26]) and a new tax from 2027 after the president blocked raises (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 25]). BGK joined the Ukraine reconstruction fund with 15 mln euro now and up to 500 mln eventually (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 26]).

Corporate & Sectors: Allegro's buyback is live: up to 42 mln shares at a maximum 45 zł (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 26]) within a first phase of up to 800 mln zł (pb.pl, [Jun 25]), and GPW Benchmark will make an extraordinary adjustment to Allegro's index weighting after the last June session (Bankier.pl, [Jun 26]). Orlen raised wholesale fuel prices again as crude rose (Portal spożywczy, [Jun 26]) while launching a summer driver promotion (Super Biznes, [Jun 26]); its Naftogaz agreements were framed as business for both sides (Business Insider Polska, [Jun 26]), and the PGE-Enea-Orlen-Tauron letter of intent on Ukrainian energy was confirmed (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 26]). Taiwan plans chip and EV factories in Poland in place of China and the US (The Diplomat, [Jun 26]). Dino's labour dispute escalated, with unions going to prosecutors over alleged mobbing (DlaHandlu.pl, [Jun 26]). Fund managers earned 1.4 bn zł, up 38% (CEO Magazyn, [Jun 26]); PKO BP extended its promotional 4% savings rate (Direct Money, [Jun 26]). Forsal.pl says the 2036 completion date is the main problem for the nuclear plant [Jun 26], and Sołowow warned that without cheaper energy "we go bankrupt" (Interia Biznes, [Jun 26]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Guardian reported Russia is preparing a possible provocation in the Baltic states or Poland [Jun 26], and Poland's deputy prime minister told CBS News Russia could launch a false-flag operation to justify an attack on a NATO state [Jun 26]; Anadolu carried Warsaw's call for the eastern flank to prepare for escalation [Jun 26]. DW highlighted Poland's build-out of what it calls Europe's largest anti-drone system [Jun 26]. On the Kyiv relationship, EUalive reports reconstruction efforts advanced at the Gdańsk conference despite bilateral tensions [Jun 26], a day after it convened without Zelensky (France 24, [Jun 25]).

New / Most Important: The Senate's 74% safety valve is the week's pivot: the windfall tax is no longer a fixed 60% number but a political variable, and that is what Orlen's +0.7% week against a -9.2% four-week run is pricing. The second signal is the Treasury borrowing 1.5 bn zł a day at record interest cost while the zloty stabilises only because the dollar paused.
