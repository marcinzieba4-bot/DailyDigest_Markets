🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → On hold

Macro Stance: The RPP is waiting (Parkiet, [Jun 25]) and the tone has tilted hawkish: NBP disclosed who voted for a hike at the last meeting (Tysol.pl, [Jun 22]), and the new NBP survey has inflation under control but rate cuts uncertain (Parkiet, [Jun 24]). Barclays sees zloty underperformance driven by central-bank policy (Investing.com, [Jun 25]). No GUS CPI release this week. Activity is two-speed: May industrial production rose 4.1% y/y, above consensus (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 19]), industrial orders jumped more than 140% on the "SAFE effect" (Interia Biznes, [Jun 24]), and producer prices are rising again after almost two years of deflation (CEO Magazyn, [Jun 22]). The consumer is the weak leg: May retail sales again missed expectations (Bankier.pl, [Jun 22]). Corporate wages grew 5.8% y/y in May, below the market (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 22]), with the average at 9,173.24 zł gross (Głos Nauczycielski, [Jun 22]). Poland remains caught between ECB re-tightening spillovers and its own inflation dynamics.

Markets: Warsaw has had its worst stretch of the quarter. The WIG20 lost 1.7% on Jun 23 with KGHM down 6.4% (Bankier.pl), then fell 2.49% on Jun 24 (BIZNES24) as KGHM dropped 6.9%, Orlen 4.9%, PGE 4.6% and Allegro 4.2% (Bankier.pl, [Jun 24]); Parkiet called it a black scenario with the strong dollar choking Polish equities, and has the index eyeing the 3500 area [Jun 24]. Thursday brought relief: the WIG20 closed up 1.26% (BIZNES24, [Jun 25]) with Tauron and PGE leading an energy rebound (Inwestycje.pl) and LPP, Modivo and Pepco in focus (pb.pl, [Jun 25]). The card shows the damage: EPOL at 37.99 is down 6.6% on the week; KGHM at 324.2 has lost 17.3% in a week; Orlen (122.78) is down 5.1% on the week and 13.2% over four weeks; PGE (9.28) is off 7.3% and 13.5%; LPP (18100) has fallen 17.8% in four weeks; PKO (103.36) and Pekao (228.8) are down 3.0% and 2.8%. The zloty is the driver: USD/PLN closed at 3.7713, up 2.3% on the week and 3.5% over four weeks, with the dollar at its strongest in over a year (Money.pl, [Jun 25]) and Bankier.pl asking whether 4 zł is next [Jun 24]; EUR/PLN at 4.2816 (+0.9% 1W, +1.1% 4W) after a fifth straight day of zloty losses (wnp.pl, [Jun 23]). Poland is paying record interest on its debt (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 25]), and July retail bonds will pay up to 5.65% (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jun 24]).

Fiscal & Politics: State Treasury debt grew 46.8 bn zł in a single month (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 22]), a rating agency said it is worried by the rise in Polish debt (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 23]), and NIK found the government broke the law by artificially raising the spending limit (wPolityce.pl, [Jun 19]). The revenue response is tax: the Sejm passed the 60% windfall tax on fuel companies effective from the start of August (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 19]), higher alcohol and sugar levies are planned to fund healthcare (Bloomberg, [Jun 24]), and after the president blocked raises the government found a route via a new tax from 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 25]). The CPN fuel package is being wound down despite price worries (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 24]). The Senate adopted the CHF-loan law without amendments (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 25]), and the WIBOR-to-POLSTR reform gives banks nine months to offer a swap (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 23]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen is the political casualty: wPolityce.pl counted around 20 bn zł of value lost in days [Jun 22]; operationally it took the investment decision on the Cerisa North Sea field (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 25]) and signed two more agreements with Naftogaz on gas and LNG (Bankier.pl, [Jun 25]). PGE, Orlen, Enea and Tauron signed a letter of intent on rebuilding Ukraine's energy system (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 25]) and Business Insider Polska says reconstruction names are soaring [Jun 25]; PGE also closed the Turów court dispute after the NSA ruling (xyz.pl, [Jun 25]). Allegro launched a buyback of up to 800 mln zł in its first phase (pb.pl, [Jun 25]). CD Projekt was cut by analysts (INNPoland.pl, [Jun 25]) and dropped from DM BOŚ's favourites (pb.pl, [Jun 24]). KGHM returned to a dividend after a year's break (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jun 23]), yet neither dividend nor government is helping the shares (INNPoland.pl, [Jun 23]). Only one of nine GPW bank recommendations says buy (pb.pl, [Jun 24]), while investment lending grows at a double-digit pace (pb.pl, [Jun 23]). Spot power spiked to 1442 zł per MWh in the heat (wnp.pl, [Jun 25]), and Domański is negotiating World Bank money for nuclear (Bankier.pl, [Jun 25]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk warned NATO's eastern flank must brace for Russian escalation (TVP World, [Jun 25]) and Sikorski said Moscow may stage a false-flag attack as a pretext (Kyiv Post, [Jun 24]); Latvia warned of Russian missile and drone provocations targeting the Baltics and Poland (Kyiv Post, [Jun 23]). The EU's military Schengen could bring billions of euros to Poland (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 23]). The row with Kyiv is the cost: Zelensky returned the White Eagle order [Jun 20], Tusk called the dispute a strategic mistake [Jun 21], and the annual reconstruction conference went ahead without Zelensky (France 24, [Jun 25]).

New / Most Important: The windfall tax has become a market event. A 60% levy legislated in days, a dollar at a one-year high and a copper-led commodity sell-off combined to knock 2.49% off the WIG20 in one session and 17.3% off KGHM in a week, while the Treasury adds almost 47 bn zł of debt a month at record interest cost. The hard data (industry +4.1%, orders +140%) are fine; the policy mix is being priced as a risk premium on the zloty and on state-controlled equities.  |  Playbook ✓: NBP on hold with a hawkish vote on record; stay underweight state-controlled energy and KGHM until the tax and dividend picture settles, prefer Allegro's buyback and banks with a visible CHF endgame, and treat Thursday's 1.26% bounce as tactical.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: EM equities at fresh highs as oil falls (Bloomberg, [Jun 22]) versus Warsaw lagging; industrial orders exploding while retail sales miss; reconstruction stocks rallying the same week Warsaw and Kyiv are at diplomatic odds.
