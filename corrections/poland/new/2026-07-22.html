🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: Wednesday's retail print closed the case for a strong second quarter. GUS reported June retail sales up 6.2% y/y in constant prices against a 5.2% consensus (Do Rzeczy, Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 22]), with one category up almost 15% (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 22]); Bankier says the consumer has finally started to spend ([Jul 22]). ING BSK now puts Q2 growth at about 3.8% y/y (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 22]), with investment doing the lifting despite a consumption slowdown in ING's reading ([Jul 22]), on top of Monday's 7.6% industrial production (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 20]) and 5.9% wage growth (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 20]). The NBP released a new inflation projection (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 22]); Obserwator Gospodarczy's European comparison has Polish inflation down to 3% ([Jul 22]), while GUS's final June CPI is 2.5% (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 15]). None of this settles the Council: a hawk told Gazeta Prawna the earlier cuts were premature ([Jul 21]), another member rates hikes more likely than cuts (Super Biznes, [Jul 21]), against Crédit Agricole's call for a 25 bp September cut to 3.50% (PAP Biznes, [Jul 20]). With refinery fuel prices still rising sharply (Super Biznes, [Jul 22]) and the end of the CPN package expected to lift July inflation (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 15]), a hold is the base case.

Markets: Parkiet says a historic moment on the GPW is imminent, the WIG20 record within arm's reach with the index around 3900 points ([Jul 22]). PGE led Wednesday's gains (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 22]) and StockWatch.pl has the WIG and Orlen both at records with PGE, KGHM and Onde in play ([Jul 22]). Orlen's price is the highest in its history (Money.pl, [Jul 22]) and Bankier counts only 5 sector peers in Europe worth more ([Jul 22]). KGHM rose after June sales data (WNP.PL, [Jul 22]) a day after copper broke 13,600 dollars (Investing.com Polska, [Jul 21]). The card, which precedes Wednesday, has Orlen 149.16 (+2.2% 1W, +15.6% 4W), PGE 9.5 (flat / -2.4%), KGHM 306.45 (-4.1% / -12.4%), Allegro 45.08 (+2.6% / +15.8%), Dino 29.78 (+5.6% / +2.3%) with DNA Rynków noting the rebound extending above 30 zł ([Jul 22]), LPP 19950 (+1.5% / +8.0%), CD Projekt 230.9 (-0.3% / +1.0%), PKO 108.88 (-1.5% / +4.3%), Pekao 236.2 (-0.3% / +2.3%); EPOL closed at 41.32, up 1.3% on the week and 5.0% over four weeks. The zloty is caught between oil and the ECB: Bankier says crude is rising and the zloty stays under pressure though the euro is not climbing for now ([Jul 22]), MyBank.pl has EUR/PLN near 4.33 awaiting the ECB ([Jul 22]) and FXMAG asks whether 4.35 breaks after all ([Jul 22]). The card shows USD/PLN 3.7945 (+0.3% 1W, +1.0% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3241 (flat / +1.1%). POLGB yields rose as the zloty firmed on Tuesday (FXMAG, [Jul 21]); no fresh bond headlines on Wednesday.

Fiscal & Politics: A day after Eurostat put public debt at 61.6% of GDP, the third-fastest rise in the EU (XYZ, [Jul 21]), Zero.pl notes Poland is also on the podium for its deficit ([Jul 22]) and Tysol.pl asks when Poland will double its debt ([Jul 22]). The politics run the other way: Money.pl's columnist calls the deputy prime minister's promise of a higher tax-free allowance "amok" ([Jul 22]) and Professor Orłowski told TVN24 the finance minister cannot block political pressure ([Jul 22]). The ministry tightens at the margins instead: lump-sum tax changes with identified losers (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 22]), a rental tax and higher lump-sum rates that INNPoland.pl says already chill entrepreneurs ([Jul 22]) and no public-sector pay rises for several years (Next Gazeta.pl, [Jul 22]). The government announced a tax on internet giants (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 22]) and Briefs Finance reports a US retaliation threat over it ([Jul 22]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen filled in its Q2 estimates: throughput of 9.17 million tonnes (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 22]), less crude through its refineries and 1.9 billion zloty of write-downs (Bankier.pl, [Jul 22]), and it spent the day explaining the Ukraine fuel-price storm as a mix of price categories (Bankier.pl, Auto Świat, [Jul 22]) while a Skolim station undercutting Orlen reached the government spokesman (Wprost, [Jul 22]). KGHM's June copper sales rose 1% y/y and production 3% y/y (Inwestycje.pl, pb.pl, [Jul 22]), Poland stable and the US mine slumping (Energetyka24, [Jul 22]). PKO BP closed a synthetic securitisation of corporate loans that lifts its capital ratio (Bankier.pl, [Jul 22]). PGE proposed its own revision of the EU ETS (PortalKomunalny.pl, [Jul 22]) and 1.3 billion zloty is going into Poland's only underground power plant (Portal Samorządowy, [Jul 22]). Dino is suing a union leader (TOK FM, [Jul 22]) as unions allege illegal dismissals (PulsHR.pl, [Jul 22]), a day after opening its contested Wilanów store (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 21]). CD Projekt's Witcher 3 expansion gets its August gamescom showing (IGN Polska, [Jul 22]). Warsaw flat prices are falling (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jul 22]) and rental listings dropped 3.2% m/m in June (PAP Biznes, [Jul 22]).

Geopolitics & Security: Belarusian commandos are at the Ukrainian border on Lukashenka's order (TVP Info, [Jul 22]), a day after Kosiniak-Kamysz warned Russia may use intercepted Ukrainian drones against Poland (Defense News, [Jul 21]). Poland is weighing a competing NATO offer for the MiG-29s promised to Ukraine (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Jul 22]).

New / Most Important: The 6.2% retail beat is the development: consumption is joining investment and industry, so the quarter is strong on every leg, while the government promises a bigger tax-free allowance with debt above 60% of GDP. For the RPP that is hawk fodder; for equities it is why Orlen and the WIG20 are at records. The oil shock via US-Iran raises energy import costs for Poland, and a consumer this strong lets the NBP extend the hold beyond what consensus expected.

Playbook ✓: Strong domestic demand with a 3900-point WIG20 and Orlen at all-time highs is the late-cycle textbook; stay with energy and the index leaders, keep the hold call.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Debt on the EU podium, a fiscal "amok" and oil-driven zloty pressure argue for wider POLGB spreads and a weaker currency, yet EUR/PLN is flat on the week and equities are at records; one side is wrong, and bonds are the side that has already started to move.
