🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: The RPP is in no hurry to ease. Council member Kotecki said inflation is diverging from the NBP's own projection and it is too early to talk about a September cut (pb.pl, [Jul 23]). The fuel channel changed the arithmetic: Citi now sees CPI reaching 3% as early as July on pump prices (PAP Biznes, [Jul 28]) and above 3.5% by year-end. The real economy gives the doves nothing: Rzeczpospolita [Jul 24] called the full June data set solid growth, Business Insider Polska [Jul 28] noted that no large EU economy grows faster, GUS showed fewer unemployed in June (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 23]) and real wages grew strongly (xyz.pl, [Jul 24]), though nominal wage growth is the weakest since 2021 (FOCUS ON Business, [Jul 23]) and pb.pl [Jul 25] flagged GDP rising while vacancies fall. Online sales rose 12.3% y/y in June, e-commerce share 9.1% (e-biznes.pl, [Jul 23]). The Fed held against Trump's pressure (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 29]) and the divided vote strengthened the zloty (Bankier, [Jul 30]). Net: hold through the summer, September cut odds fading.

Markets: Warsaw is on the cusp of history. Parkiet [Jul 30] says the historic moment on the GPW is imminent, banks signalling an attack and XTB shining; Strefa Biznesu [Jul 29] says the GPW can beat the 2007 record, after the 3900-point barrier had held WIG20 a week earlier (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 23]). CD Projekt led the bulls (CDR +6.44% on [Jul 29], Stooq), mBank's 2 bn zł half-year profit and dividend promise lifted WIG20 (Bankier, [Jul 29]), PKO BP's share price hit a record (WNP.PL, [Jul 30]) and KGHM led gains on [Jul 30] as banks drove the session (Inwestycje.pl). The card confirms the rotation: CD Projekt 253.0 (+8.6% 1W, +12.7% 4W), LPP 21,360 (+5.3% 1W, +15.5% 4W), Dino 31.8 (+5.1% 1W), PKO 109.86 (+1.2% 1W, +5.0% 4W); the laggards are KGHM 294.35 (-6.4% 1W, -10.4% 4W) and PGE 9.69 (-5.0% 1W), with Orlen 147.92 (-2.6% 1W, still +16.7% 4W) after Comparic [Jul 30] noted a drop of more than 7% from the rally peak with investors eyeing 135 zł. EPOL 41.56 is +0.5% 1W, +7.6% 4W. The zloty: USD/PLN 3.769 (-0.6% 1W, flat 4W) after briefly piercing 3.80 ahead of the Fed (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 28]); EUR/PLN 4.3224 (-0.1% 1W, +0.7% 4W), with FXMAG [Jul 30] asking whether it stays above 4.32. Bankier [Jul 29]: the war goes on, the zloty holds. Bonds: yields rose worryingly ahead of the Fed (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Jul 29]), yet the Ministry of Finance sold over 11 bn zł of paper on strong demand (Bankier, [Jul 29]).

Fiscal & Politics: Debt is the political fault line. Treasury debt exceeded 2.1 trn zł (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 24]), xyz.pl [Jul 24] called debt above 60% of GDP a historic moment, Euronews [Jul 23] ranked Poland third in Europe for debt growth and Arak sees 70% of GDP by end-2027 (Filary Biznesu, [Jul 26]). Yet the government is spending into the election: a 60,000 zł tax-free allowance costing 58.6 bn zł a year (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 29]), a pledge of up to 300 zł a month more for everyone before the vote (INFOR.PL, [Jul 30]), and a 2027 PIT bill of 70 bn zł that forces spending cuts (wnp.pl, [Jul 29]). Offsets are modest: the MF dropped the 15% lump-sum rate for sole traders (Bankier, [Jul 29]), the Sejm voted a new savings tax from 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 25]), public-sector pay gets 3% not 15% in 2027 (Głos Nauczycielski, [Jul 30]). President Nawrocki is blocking the fuel windfall tax (Notes From Poland, [Jul 27]) while Tusk weighs reviving fuel-price caps (Bloomberg, [Jul 27]) and a fuel levy modelled on the bank tax (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 27]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks are the engine: Pekao beat forecasts despite lower rates and higher CIT (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 30]), mBank booked a record result (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 29]), mortgage origination topped 13 bn zł for a fourth month (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 30]) and consumers borrowed 183 bn zł in H1 (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 30]). Legal risk persists: Millennium set aside 61 mn zł on consumer-loan disputes (Bankier, [Jul 28]), the CJEU line on WIBOR hardened (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 28]) and the CHF law is in the Journal of Laws, with instalments suspended by statute from early August (Franknews.pl, [Jul 25]). Retail: Dino rose 1.7% on heavy volume despite the labour ministry intervening over layoffs (Investing.com Polska, [Jul 30]); Couche-Tard agreed to buy control of Żabka with a tender offer (TradingView, [Jul 30]) after Seven & i walked away (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 27]); Allegro hired BLIK's Dariusz Mazurkiewicz for financial services (Bankier, [Jul 29]); LPP opened a Romanian distribution centre for 35 mn pieces (Bankier, [Jul 28]). Energy: Orlen paid a record 8 zł dividend (Bankier, [Jul 28]); DM BOŚ raised its PGE target to 14 zł (Bankier, [Jul 30]) even as PGE slipped below 10 zł and delayed Rybnik coal closures again (Energetyka24, [Jul 28]). KGHM's new strategy drops the earlier issuance target (Parkiet, [Jul 29]); six Polish firms entered final qualification for the nuclear plant (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 30]).

Geopolitics & Security: A Russian Kh-101 cruise missile overshot Ukraine and crashed in the Lublin region during the overnight barrage, leaving a 10-metre crater; Rutte confirmed it was Russian (Bloomberg, [Jul 30]), Tusk said the military was ready to shoot it down (wnp.pl, [Jul 30]) and NATO jets scrambled (Polskie Radio, [Jul 30]). Belarus moved special forces to the Ukrainian border (Forsal.pl, [Jul 23]); Poland launched a $205M defence innovation programme (Anadolu, [Jul 23]) and is bidding to host a Patriot factory (Tech Times, [Jul 24]).

New / Most Important: The missile. It lands as WIG20 sits at the 2007 high, PKO at a record and foreign demand for POLGBs strong, so a risk premium the market had priced out just got a live test. The first reaction (Bankier, [Jul 29]: zloty holds, resistances intact) says investors treat it as a one-off, but it hands the RPP hawks cover, adds to a defence bill on top of debt already past 60% of GDP, and makes the record attempt a geopolitical rather than an earnings call.

Playbook ✓: Hold pattern continues as expected; the bank beats justify staying overweight financials into the record attempt.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Oil remains the key transmission mechanism — a Russian missile on Polish soil plus diesel threatening 8 zł (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 27]) is a combination the record-chasing index is not pricing.
