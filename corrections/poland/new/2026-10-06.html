🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold expected, Nov hike risk

Macro Stance: The RPP's October decision is the scheduled event and consensus has swung to a hold: Bloomberg [Oct 6] expects rates unchanged because the fuel-price cap is seen cooling inflation, Bankier [Oct 6] says the Council will wait until November and may hike then, and ING's line is that policy on hold is consistent with zloty stability (tmgm [Oct 6]). The case for tightening has not gone away: the GUS September flash put CPI at 4.0% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes [Sep 30]), just under the 4.1% consensus (BIZNES24) but the highest in 15 months and outside the target band (Rzeczpospolita [Sep 30]), with fuel up 36.1% y/y; pb.pl notes core inflation still holds the headline in check, while Governor Glapiński warned the energy shock "is contagious" (Parkiet [Oct 2]). Growth is the cushion: GUS today revised 2025 GDP to 3.8% from 3.6% on stronger consumption and investment (wnp.pl, CEO Magazyn [Oct 6]), and the World Bank lifted its 2026 forecast to 3.6% (UA.NEWS [Oct 6]). Retail sales rose 2.4% y/y in August on Eurostat data (Strefa Inwestorów [Oct 6]); industry is still contracting, if more slowly (wnp.pl [Oct 1]).

Markets: Warsaw is at records: Bankier [Oct 6] reports new highs and Inwestycje.pl has Budimex and mBank leading with only two names lower. That follows the first session on the new WATS system, which launched without disruption (Forex Club [Oct 5]) as WIG20 rebounded 1.7% with banks recovering (FXMAG [Oct 5]), after a bank-led sell-off on Oct 1 that Bankier called the worst session in over a month and that took WIG20 to the 4100 area (Parkiet). The zloty is the weak leg: USD/PLN closed 3.9046, up 1.6% on the week and 5.4% over four weeks, while EUR/PLN at 4.3791 is up only 0.2% and 1.7%, so the move is dollar strength with US 10-year yields at 24-year highs above 5.3% (CEO Magazyn [Oct 1], pb.pl [Oct 5]). Bankier [Oct 6] reports the zloty recovering today ahead of the RPP with the dollar at 3.89 zł (MyBank). EPOL closed 43.51, down 2.6% on the week and 3.6% over four weeks, lagging the local indices in dollar terms. In the card, PGE at 15.02 is up 10.9% on the week and 14.1% over four weeks, KGHM at 335.3 is up 4.3% and CD Projekt at 253.5 up 3.4%, while PKO at 118.24 (down 4.9%), Pekao at 268.7 (down 4.0%), Orlen at 144.32 (down 4.2%, down 9.0% over four weeks), LPP at 24020 (down 3.2%) and Dino at 33.66 (down 2.7%) carry the week's damage.

Fiscal & Politics: The 2027 budget carries 977.6bn zł of spending and a 281.2bn zł deficit (CEO Magazyn [Sep 30]), with the 2026 deficit after September already around 172.2bn zł (MF via Strefa Inwestorów [Sep 29]). The government's own path has debt passing the 55% prudential level in 2028 and spending cuts by 2030 (Gazeta Prawna [Sep 29], wnp.pl [Sep 30]); Balcerowicz today called for privatisation and social cuts (Bankier [Oct 6]), and the Council of Ministers adopted its report under the excessive-deficit procedure (Gov.pl [Oct 6]). The tax package in the Sejm sets a 130 thousand zł second PIT threshold, a new 24% rate from 150 thousand zł and 22% CIT for the largest firms (INFOR [Oct 5]); a cadastral tax bill at 0.02% on first and second homes and 0.5% to 1.5% beyond went to first reading. President Nawrocki signed the fuel windfall tax but referred it to the Constitutional Tribunal (Rzeczpospolita [Oct 1]); the retail price cap is in force (blue News [Oct 3]). A parliamentary probe largely backed the accusations against the NBP governor (Bloomberg [Oct 6]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen dominates: ABW detained five former and current managers over alleged pre-election fuel underpricing, with Justice Minister Żurek putting the damage at 4.1bn zł (PAP, Bankier [Oct 6]), and the company warned it may seek a higher gas tariff for 2027 (Business Insider Polska [Oct 6]). KGHM launched the $725m Sierra Gorda expansion to lift copper output 20% (MuratorPlus [Oct 6]). CD Projekt broke another level as The Witcher 3 set a Steam record 11 years after release (FXMAG [Oct 6]). Allegro hit a five-year high (Parkiet [Oct 6]), is running up to 800m zł of buybacks (Parkiet [Oct 1]) and is bidding for Orlen Paczka (Business Insider Polska [Oct 2]). Dino opened 256 stores in the first three quarters to reach 3283 (Inwestycje.pl [Oct 6]). In banks, Pekao and Millennium have moved new mortgages to POLSTR with mBank next (Bankier [Oct 6]), profits are shrinking (Business Insider Polska [Oct 2]) and WIBOR litigation is put at up to 284bn zł (Radio Zet [Oct 2]). Power on the TGE is the most expensive in four years (Rzeczpospolita [Oct 6]) and the first nuclear reactor has slipped to 2036 (Magazyn MANAGER+ [Oct 1]).

Geopolitics & Security: Sweden will deploy Patriot batteries to protect the NATO logistics hub in Poland (Reuters [Oct 6]), NATO's "Theory of Victory" is to be unveiled in Poland (TVP World [Oct 6]), and Poland and Lithuania are preparing to evacuate one million Baltic residents in the event of war (Notes From Poland [Oct 6]). Russia is targeting the rail corridor to Ukraine (Defence24 [Oct 6]). Poland and the Baltics are pushing defence spending toward 5% of GDP (IntelliNews [Oct 4]).

New / Most Important: The GDP revision to 3.8% and a record WIG land on the same day as the Orlen detentions and a consensus shift toward an RPP hold. Stronger growth plus a 4.0% CPI print would normally argue for tightening, yet the fuel cap has let the market price patience. A hold with a hawkish November signal extends the equity run but leaves the zloty hostage to US yields; a surprise hike would hit an already fragile bank tape.

Playbook ✓: Stay long domestic cyclicals and PGE into the decision, keep bank exposure light after the Oct 1 sell-off and the POLSTR migration, and avoid adding zloty risk with USD/PLN near 3.90.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Equities at records while EPOL is down 2.6% on the week is a pure currency wedge; the local long is intact, the dollar one is not, and the Orlen headlines are a reminder that state-controlled names carry political beta.
