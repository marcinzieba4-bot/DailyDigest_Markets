🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, cuts delayed

Macro Stance: The inflation overshoot still sets the tone: August flash CPI of 3.4% y/y and 0.4% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Aug 31]) against 3.1% expected (BIZNES24, [Aug 31]), with Farmer.pl noting fuel shot up at the pumps and the VAT cut came too late [Sep 2]. The NBP governor, speaking at the G20, promised a decisive reaction to mega-shocks (wGospodarce, [Sep 2]) a day after cooling rate hopes (Interia Biznes, [Sep 1]), so the easing cycle is delayed at best. Today's new input is the labour side, and it cuts against the consumer: GUS put the median wage at 7.5 thousand zł gross (DlaHandlu.pl, [Sep 2]), the median fell more than 2% (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Sep 2]) and wGospodarce says real wages are starting to decline [Sep 2]. XYZ's monthly summary captures the mix: growth holding firm, inflation lifting its head [Sep 2]. Q2 GDP of 3.9% y/y with investment up 8.4% (PAP Biznes and CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]) is the cushion; the manufacturing PMI below the expansion line (Parkiet, [Sep 1]) and Trans.INFO's finding that Poland sits at the bottom of the European industrial table [Sep 2] are the warning. BNY's import pass-through and ABN AMRO's ECB divergence remain the zloty's structural headwinds.

Markets: Earnings rescued Wednesday. PGE jumped 7.7% and the WIG20 rose 0.5% (Bankier.pl, [Sep 2]) after the utility pre-released an estimated 2.06 bn zł Q2 net profit, above expectations, with distribution driving results (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 2]); mBank and Tauron also had strong sessions (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 2]), though Parkiet still sees the WIG20 in consolidation [Sep 2] and global markets souring the mood on Middle East risk (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 2]). Tuesday was the reverse: KGHM fell 2.86% while CD Projekt did not move (FXMAG, [Sep 2]). The card, as of Tuesday's close, shows the rotation: Orlen at 155.54 (+2.0% 1W, +5.8% 4W) after its record above 160 zł (CrypS.pl, [Sep 1]), Dino at 36.2 (+13.1% 4W), PKO BP at 113.7 (+4.3% 4W), against KGHM at 347.0 (-5.2% 1W, +2.3% 4W), CD Projekt at 234.4 (-3.5% 1W, -8.9% 4W) and EPOL at 43.78 (-2.0% 1W, -0.7% 4W). The zloty keeps slipping: the dollar is clearly dearer (Interia Biznes, [Sep 2]) and Bankier.pl sees risk of further weakening with the euro able to rise more [Sep 2]; the card has USD/PLN at 3.739 (+1.5% 1W, +0.4% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3305 (+0.7% 1W, +0.8% 4W), although Comparic carries a contrarian forecast of USD/PLN falling to 3.567 [Sep 2]. POLGBs are caught in a global sovereign sell-off that pb.pl says is no longer only about oil [Sep 2]; with yields above 6% bond funds are in the red and Analizy.pl asks whether that is an opportunity or a trap [Sep 2].

Fiscal & Politics: Budget scrutiny moved from the headline to the lines. Parkiet reports the budget's sacred cows refuse to economise [Sep 2]; Rzeczpospolita finds energy is where the state will spend most next year [Sep 2]; the Constitutional Tribunal's president wrote to the finance minister invoking the Constitution over the court's funding (Prawo.pl, [Sep 2]) after Brussels Signal framed the record-deficit budget as defunding the top court [Aug 31]; the health ministry admits an NFZ financing gap (Rynek Zdrowia, [Sep 2]). The CPN fuel-cap is history because the tight budget cannot carry it (TVP Wrocław, [Sep 2]). ING Think published its market read of the 2027 budget [Sep 1], and Bankier.pl's warning of a 7% deficit and a rating-cut threat [Sep 1] still hangs over POLGBs.

Corporate & Sectors: A dense results day. CD Projekt's Q2 net profit beat forecasts by 44% (Bankier.pl, [Sep 2]) with first-half net profit of 249.11 mln zł and EBIT of 245.34 mln zł (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 2]), yet the shares fell (Investing.com, [Sep 2]) because the 3 bn profit target for 2024-27 is no longer current (pb.pl, [Sep 2]) and the company gave bad news on Witcher 4 and the Witcher 1 remake timing (GRYOnline.pl, [Sep 2]). Dino, now Poland's third-largest employer with 60 thousand staff (Głos Wielkopolski, [Sep 2]) and 3245 stores (Money.pl, [Sep 2]), announced 700 mln zł for raises and new positions on the eve of mediation (WP Finanse, [Sep 2]) while unions threaten a general strike (BiznesINFO.pl, [Sep 2]). LPP launched the Sinsay marketplace targeting 100 thousand products in 2027 (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 2]). MidEuropa is exiting Allegro, selling 4.3 mln shares in an ABB (pb.pl, [Sep 2]). Orlen set new fuel prices of a kind not seen in four years (WNP.PL, [Sep 2]), hit a record (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 2]), but its Polimery takeover is challenged before a tribunal by a foreign group (WNP.PL, [Sep 2]). PKO BP's POLSTR mortgages start tomorrow (PKB24.pl, [Sep 2]) and Interia Biznes expects the benchmark to stay niche because banks are afraid [Sep 2]. TTF gas is at its highest since January 2023 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 2]).

Geopolitics & Security: Slovakia will send troops and anti-drone radars to Poland (TVP World, [Aug 28]); Russia is intensifying sabotage in Europe (pb.pl, [Sep 2]); Carnegie describes Russia's long drone shadow [Sep 2]. Europe officially rejected reports of a direct Russian threat to NATO (Kyiv Post, [Aug 29]) even as a former Polish intelligence chief linked the CIA's Moscow visit to a possible Baltic threat (Türkiye Today, [Aug 29]). Billions in defence spending are reshaping Polish industry (Bankier.pl, [Sep 2]).

New / Most Important: The falling median wage is the development that changes the picture. With CPI at 3.4% and real pay starting to decline, the consumption leg that made Poland an EU growth leader weakens just as the NBP loses room to cut; growth is now carried by investment and by utilities and refiners earning on higher energy prices, as PGE's 2.06 bn zł quarter shows.  |  Playbook ✓: PLN weakness on delayed cuts is consensus; favour regulated and energy earners (PGE, Orlen) over consumer names.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Dino at +13.1% over four weeks and a 700 mln zł wage bill sit uneasily with a falling median wage; the market is pricing the retailer as a volume winner while its labour cost and its customer's budget are moving the wrong way.
