🇵🇱 Poland  Bullish  → hold, core easing

Macro Stance: Two data points reset the debate this week. GUS revised second-quarter GDP growth to 4.1% y/y from 3.9% with investment accelerating to 8.5% (Strefa Inwestorów, CEO Magazyn [Oct 16]), Pekao's economists lifted their 2026 forecast to 4% (Strefa Inwestorów [Oct 16]), and Domański says the economy is "shifting up a gear" (wnp.pl [Oct 16]). At the same time core inflation fell to 3.1% in September while headline CPI was 4.1% (CEO Magazyn [Oct 15]), ING calls the core drop clear, HICP came in at 4% with weakening underlying pressure (Forex Club [Oct 16]), and Rzeczpospolita [Oct 16] finds no domino effect from the fuel shock. That mix, strong growth and a narrowing core, is what lets the RPP stay on hold without looking behind the curve, and Polskie Radio 24 [Oct 16] has an RPP member explaining whether a hike is being prepared; ING's warning that the zloty is exposed as the NBP delays tightening (FXStreet [Oct 15]) is the dissenting view. Offsets: industrial production fell 1.8% on Eurostat data (CEO Magazyn [Oct 15]) and the president will resubmit his request to appoint four NBP board members (Stooq [Oct 16]).

Markets: WIG20 closed up 0.92% at a historic high to end the week (Inwestycje.pl, Parkiet [Oct 16]), WIG crossed 160,000 points (XYZ), and Bankier reports WIG, WIG20 and mWIG40 all at records with profit-taking in energy and Allegro at its most expensive in five years; Pepco led while PGE and Tauron slipped (Inwestycje.pl). Bonds turned: Polish yields fell with the market targeting 6% (StockWatch, Bankier [Oct 16]). The zloty followed: USD/PLN closed 3.8937, down 0.2% on the week but still up 2.6% over four weeks, and today the dollar slid to 3.88, its weakest since the start of October (Comparic [Oct 16]); EUR/PLN closed 4.3656, down 0.2% on the week and up 0.3% over four weeks, and CEO Magazyn [Oct 16] carries a 4.30 forecast for year-end. EPOL closed 44.45, up 2.3% on the week and down 0.7% over four weeks. LPP at 26400 is up 8.2% on the week and 18.0% over four weeks, Allegro at 54.04 up 7.2%, PGE at 17.26 up 9.8% and 21.1% over four weeks, Dino at 37.44 up 5.5%, CD Projekt at 264.5 up 4.8%; PKO at 115.84 is up 0.8%, Pekao at 263.0 flat, Orlen at 154.44 down 0.7%, and KGHM at 324.25 down 1.2% after breaking an 11-month trendline (Comparic [Oct 15]).

Fiscal & Politics: The budget deficit reached about 172.2bn zł after September (MF via PAP Biznes [Oct 15]), 63.4% of the annual plan with debt service an ever larger burden (Gazeta Prawna [Oct 16]); CIT receipts are up 30% while VAT is flat (CEO Magazyn [Oct 15]) and the gap is smaller than a year ago (Rzeczpospolita [Oct 15]). pb.pl [Oct 16] says a record deficit is not a catastrophe but the challenges are real, and Prof. Radło argues fiscal consolidation must start immediately (Gazeta Prawna [Oct 16]). INFOR [Oct 16] concludes a cadastral tax has no chance for several years. At the EU summit a coalition of the V4, Italy and Greece demanded ETS cuts (Bankier [Oct 15]) and the region wants ETS2 delayed (Farmer [Oct 16]); the energy minister promises the lowest fuel prices in the EU (Euronews [Oct 16]) but cheap fuel only to year-end (Fakt), with Friday's maximum prices lower (Energetyka24). Next Gazeta [Oct 16] notes the NBP may post a profit for the first time in years.

Corporate & Sectors: PGE will pay its first dividend in 10 years (Strefa Inwestorów [Oct 16]) and its strategy targets over 30bn zł of EBITDA in 2035 with about 210bn zł of investment over ten years (WysokieNapiecie [Oct 16]). Nuclear took a hit: NIK says the first plant lost three years through a missing contract and personnel churn (Forsal, WNP [Oct 16]), and the government proposes changes to the PEJ state-aid schedule (pb.pl). Orlen will take four crude cargoes in October (Money.pl [Oct 15]) and is waiting for Saudi oil (Energetyka24 [Oct 16]), but after the Czech tax Lithuania is now considering an extra levy (Next Gazeta [Oct 16]). Allegro rose 2.72% (Stooq [Oct 16]) as Temu's share collapsed from 36% to 6% (Wiadomości Handlowe [Oct 15]) and its ad business reached 1.4bn zł (Interaktywnie). CD Projekt set The Witcher 4 for 2028 (CEO Magazyn [Oct 15]). KGHM earns billions but Parkiet [Oct 16] sees cause for concern as a copper surplus is forecast. Dino faces a pre-holiday strike that Investing.com [Oct 15] says threatens profits. Banks are seeking state risk-sharing to fund the defence build-up (marketscreener [Oct 15]), mortgages are getting dearer again (Bankier [Oct 16]), and the ZBP says clients should not feel the switch to POLSTR (pb.pl [Oct 16]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland scrambled fighter jets as Russia struck western Ukraine (Al Jazeera [Oct 16]), with RCB alerts for two voivodeships (Interia [Oct 16]) after a Lublin-region alert on Thursday (Goniec). The Czech military suggests Russia may launch a partial attack to test NATO cohesion (Yevropeiska Pravda [Oct 15]), Poland is pushing Russia's frozen assets back onto the agenda for Kyiv (Bloomberg [Oct 15]), and Notes From Poland [Oct 15] marks one year to the parliamentary elections.

New / Most Important: The combination of 4.1% Q2 growth, core inflation down to 3.1% and a record WIG20 is the strongest macro-market alignment in months, and it arrived with bonds rallying and the zloty recovering. The 172.2bn zł deficit and NIK's nuclear verdict are real, but this week the growth-disinflation mix, not the fiscal gap, is setting prices.

Playbook ✓: Add to POLGBs toward the 6% target analysts cite; rotate from PGE after its dividend and strategy news into Allegro and LPP, which have earnings and share-gain stories; keep USD/PLN hedges light with the dollar at 3.88.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The old script was a zloty exposed by a dovish NBP; this week says a central bank that waits while core falls and growth runs at 4.1% is being rewarded, not punished. The script breaks only if the post-CPN inflation rebound or the deficit forces the hand.
