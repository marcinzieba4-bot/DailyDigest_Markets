🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The RPP is being asked whether to hike pre-emptively. Kotecki told pb.pl that Poland has already once chased inflation with rates [Sep 24], a day after an RPP member indicated a date for an increase (WNP.PL, [Sep 23]), and even Domański said he fears inflation may rise (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]). Credit Agricole sees inflation possibly reaching 4.5% on food (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 22]), oil is back above 100 dollars (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 24]) and money supply sits at a record near 3 trillion zloty (TVP World, [Sep 23]). Activity is softening at the same time: mBank describes an economy temporarily below forecasts [Sep 24], GUS business climate is weakening before the fourth quarter with industry and construction negative (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 24]), unemployment rose to 5.8% (Warszawa w Pigułce, [Sep 23]) and real wage growth is the lowest in three years (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 22]), though the EBRD still says the economy maintains momentum [Sep 24].

Markets: Thursday's session was a recovery from an early dip: the WIG20 quickly made up a morning slide, which Parkiet asks may be a trap [Sep 24], and XYZ called it calm with Polimex Mostostal surging on a large contract [Sep 24]; Strefa Inwestorów had PGE down 3% while Allegro and Orlen rose [Sep 24] and WIG-Banki set new highs with PKO BP leading (Investing.com Polska, [Sep 24]). The card, at Wednesday's close, shows the bank trade intact: Pekao at 281.0 is up 4.7% on the week and 7.3% over four weeks, PKO at 123.2 up 2.3% and 6.4%; LPP at 24520 holds a 13.1% weekly and 15.7% four-week gain, Allegro at 48.63 is up 7.7%, KGHM at 342.65 is 6.0% lower over four weeks; Orlen at 148.2 is down 5.5% on the week and PGE at 13.65 keeps a 26.0% four-week run. EPOL closed at 44.58, up 0.9% on the week. The zloty broke lower again: the euro is the dearest in nearly three years (Bankier.pl, [Sep 24]), EUR/PLN reached 4.39 in what FXMAG called a Thursday shock [Sep 24] and TVP World put the currency at its weakest against the euro since 2023 [Sep 24]; the card's Wednesday closes were USD/PLN 3.8443 (+1.2% 1W, +4.0% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.374 (+0.4% 1W, +1.6% 4W). POLGBs cracked with the global bond rout: Rzeczpospolita says investors rushed to sell and it has not been this bad in years [Sep 24], Analizy.pl reads rising Polish yields as a warning to Warsaw [Sep 24] and Business Insider Polska says the next rating cut is already priced [Sep 24]. BGK still placed PLN 1.46 billion across four maturities (Investing.com, [Sep 24]).

Fiscal & Politics: The windfall tax cleared the Senate without amendments and now goes to the president (Money.pl, [Sep 24]), though Newsmax Polska notes the new CPN package could only take effect in November [Sep 24]. The tax package is being reshaped on the fly: a new 24% PIT rate worth up to 3600 zł a year (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]) sits beside a firm refusal to index PIT thresholds (Bankier.pl, [Sep 24]) and the Senate raised the tax-free limit on Allegro and OLX sales to 3 thousand zł (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 24]). The EBRD asks whether the deficit will exceed the 6.8% target in 2026 (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 24]), 800+ cost 64.9 billion zł (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 24]) and the agencies are warning Poland about its debt (Parkiet, [Sep 24]). The government is openly considering a power price freeze for 2027 (WNP.PL, [Sep 24]) and seeks an alternative plan for the delayed PZU-Pekao merger (Euronext, [Sep 24]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen announced a squeeze-out of Energa minorities at 27.6 zł a share (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 24]), is closer to an SMR agreement with Sołowow's Synthos (Money.pl, [Sep 24]) and may hand Orlen Paczka to Poczta Polska in a state logistics consolidation (Bankier.pl, [Sep 24]), while another matter worth billions went to prosecutors (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 24]). KGHM's Victoria project made Ontario's strategic investment list (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 24]). PGE broke ground on an 886 million zł gas cogeneration unit in Kraków (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 24]). Polimex won the first domestic contract on the nuclear plant (pb.pl, [Sep 24]), though there is still no signed contract with the US consortium (wm.pl, [Sep 24]). mBank will pull WIBOR from new mortgages from October (Parkiet, [Sep 24]), pb.pl warns that challenging WIBOR would drain banks of capital [Sep 24] and PKO BP is targeting 30% of a 100 billion zł OKI market (Bankier.pl, [Sep 24]). House prices are accelerating again with double-digit rises in Warsaw and Kraków (Money.pl, [Sep 24]) and Dino's management hired lawyers as unions say it is trying to silence them (Infostrow.pl, [Sep 24]).

Geopolitics & Security: Danish intelligence says Russia could attack a NATO country within months (The Guardian, [Sep 24]), Sikorski warned Russia is preparing something big this year (TVP World, [Sep 24]) and Poland called a fire at a Starlink station sabotage (BBC, [Sep 24]). The Dorohusk and Zosin crossings were evacuated as Russian strikes hit across the border (Super Tydzień Chełmski, [Sep 24]) and a Russian helicopter's intrusion into Polish airspace marked the latest breach on the flank (Stars and Stripes, [Sep 24]).

New / Most Important: The bond market has stopped giving Poland the benefit of the doubt. A sell-off Rzeczpospolita calls the worst in years, a zloty at its weakest against the euro since 2023 and a next downgrade already priced arrive in the same week the ministry admits it will not fill the gap and the EBRD questions the 6.8% deficit target. The RPP is being pushed toward a pre-emptive hike by the currency rather than by demand, which is weakening, and the fiscal answer remains a tax rate here and a threshold freeze there.  |  Playbook ✓: Stay long the banks that are printing new highs; keep POLGB exposure short-dated until the global rout and the local warning fade; treat EUR/PLN at 4.39 as a level where an NBP response becomes likely rather than one to chase.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: WIG-Banki at record highs while the sovereign sells off; Orlen rising on the day it announces a squeeze-out while a billions-of-zloty case goes to prosecutors.
