🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↓ cut bias

Macro Stance: The GUS flash reset the rate debate: June CPI fell to 2.5%, the NBP target, which Parkiet says finally kills the chance of hikes [Jun 30]; Rzeczpospolita notes inflation has not been this low since February [Jun 30] and Fakt credits fuel for the surprise [Jun 30]. ING's economists say the market may start playing for cuts in the second half of 2026 [Jun 30], TVN24 carries the same line [Jun 30], and Bankier.pl reports the market has withdrawn its bet on dearer credit [Jun 30], a sharp turn from a week in which NBP disclosed a vote for a hike (Tysol.pl, [Jun 22]). The caveats are immediate: Gazeta Prawna estimates the withdrawal of the CPN fuel package could push inflation back toward 3% [Jun 30], Bankier.pl expects inflation to jump again while the NBP stays calm [Jun 30], and Direct Money gives one reason not to celebrate early [Jun 30]. Domański acknowledged fuel prices will rise as CPN passes into history (Biznes Wprost, [Jun 30]). GUS also published its GDP release (Interia Biznes, [Jun 30]), and Business Insider Polska describes the economy's biggest weakness as a collision with a wall [Jun 30]. No new rate action this cycle, but the bias has shifted from hike risk to a cut debate.

Markets: The zloty did not read the data as good news: Business Insider Polska reports it unexpectedly losing on the print [Jun 30], Bankier.pl has the euro reaching 4.30 zł and the pound nearly 5 zł [Jun 30], and Comparic.pl sees losses deepening [Jun 30], with Next Gazeta.pl pointing to a strong dollar and a possible Japanese intervention [Jun 30]. The card, at Monday's close, shows USD/PLN at 3.7538 (+0.5% 1W, +3.2% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.2864 (+0.5% 1W, +1.2% 4W). Fiscal & Politics: wPolityce.pl reads the EU convergence report as giving Poland the worst public finances in the bloc [Jun 30], days after the same report said Poland is not ready for the euro (Reuters, [Jun 24]); Biznes Alert calculates the state takes 229 days of earnings a year [Jun 30]. The windfall tax now carries a Senate amendment allowing up to 100% of profit (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jun 29]). The Central Register of Contracts goes live at the start of July, letting anyone see officials' spending (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 30]), and municipalities received millions more under the local-finance reform (INFOR.PL, [Jun 30]). Rzeczpospolita's call to review state spending [Jun 29] stands. The WIBOR exit is nearer, with banks obliged to make clients a new offer (Bankier.pl, [Jun 30]).

Corporate & Sectors: Energy is on alert: PSE declared a warning state and issued capacity-market calls (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 30]), the de minimis deadline for capped power prices falls today (Gazeta Prawna, [Jun 30]), and the government is changing energy law to reshape bills (Polskie Radio 24, [Jun 30]). Housing is at an inflection: mortgage servicing cost has equalled rent (Bankier.pl, [Jun 30]), mortgage lending set new records (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 30]) and creditworthiness is rising faster than prices (Business Insider Polska, [Jun 30]). CD Projekt released a new Edgerunners 2 trailer with the series due this year (CD PROJEKT RED, [Jun 30]).

Geopolitics & Security: The NATO summit in Ankara is imminent (World Socialist Web Site, [Jun 30]), with Poland's spy chief saying the country must act as if war with Russia were imminent (TVP World, [Jun 29]) and Decode39 reporting Russia's influence operations turning toward Poland [Jun 30]. The Dutch assessment that Russia could attack NATO a year after the Ukraine war ends (Kyiv Post, [Jun 29]) and the Saab submarine order worth 4.3 billion dollars (Investing.com, [Jun 29]) set the defence tone.

New / Most Important: Inflation at 2.5% is the week's pivot, but the market reaction is the lesson: the zloty fell on a target-consistent print because a cut debate at a time of 1.5 bn zł a day of borrowing (wPolityce.pl, [Jun 26]) and a 100% windfall-tax ceiling reads as fiscal dominance, not disinflation.
