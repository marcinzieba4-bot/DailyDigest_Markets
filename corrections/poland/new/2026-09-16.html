🇵🇱 Poland  Bearish  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The inflation picture hardened again: the NBP put August core inflation excluding food and energy at 3.3% (Portal spożywczy, [Sep 16]), up on the month (Forsal.pl, [Sep 16]), and Rzeczpospolita says the Middle East conflict is now feeding core prices [Sep 16]. That follows GUS confirming headline CPI at 3.4% y/y, the highest in 14 months (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]), with transport up 11.2% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 15]). The NBP has turned cautious as inflation rises, per Commerzbank's FXStreet note, and the hawkish-but-uncertain stance now has company: an RPP member told Bankier.pl the Council could raise rates [Sep 15] and the Fed hiked, sending the dollar sharply higher (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 16]) after the ECB's hawkish turn (Biznes Alert, [Sep 16]). Activity is not the issue: Eurostat shows industrial production improving in Poland while the euro area fell (XYZ, [Sep 16]) and the 2027 minimum wage is official (Forsal.pl, [Sep 16]). The current account deficit widened to PLN 10.4bn as China became Poland's largest supplier (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 15]).

Markets: Wednesday was the most nervous day on markets in years, with bonds at 20-year levels (FXMAG, [Sep 16]) and Wall Street falling hard after the Fed (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 16]). In Warsaw the WIG20 slipped below 4100 points (Parkiet, [Sep 16]), weighed by PZU going ex a record dividend (CrypS.pl, [Sep 16]): KGHM and CD Projekt rose (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 16]) and PGE, Tauron, Pepco and Dino gained (FXMAG, [Sep 16]). The card at Tuesday's close shows the damage concentrated: EPOL 44.86 (-2.0% 1W, +2.0% 4W); KGHM 326.95 (-12.7% 1W, -2.3% 4W); Dino 35.5 (-5.0% 1W, +9.3% 4W); CD Projekt 236.0 (-1.4%, -8.6%); Orlen 158.56 (-0.0% 1W, +3.5% 4W) after the FT revelations; LPP 21960 (-0.5% 1W, +4.5% 4W) and Allegro 46.12 (+0.5%, +2.3%) with both results imminent and shares slipping on supply-chain worries (FXMAG, [Sep 16]); the holders are PGE 13.94 (+1.7% 1W, +35.4% 4W), PKO 120.3 (+2.0%, +9.5%) and Pekao 270.0 (+0.5%, +6.4%). The zloty weakened for another day (wGospodarce, [Sep 16]): USD/PLN closed at 3.7593 (+1.4% 1W, +0.8% 4W) and is approaching 3.80 after the Fed hike (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 16]); EUR/PLN 4.3363 (+0.6% 1W, +0.4% 4W) did not break resistance, and Bankier.pl says there will be no stronger zloty without cheaper oil [Sep 16]. POLGBs: 10-year yields are back at 6.5% (Analizy.pl, [Sep 16]), a day after Bankier.pl called them the highest since 2007 [Sep 15], yet the MF sold PLN 10 bln of bonds at auction (TradingView, [Sep 16]) and Domański called the sell-off a global wave and the tender a success (Bankier.pl, [Sep 16]).

Fiscal & Politics: The MF put the budget deficit after August at 151.1 bn zł (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 16]) and Business Insider Polska now expects the budget to breach the prudential threshold, with households paying [Sep 16]; the Fiscal Council told the ministry "we cannot be blind" (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]). The Sejm is taking a second run at the 60% windfall tax on fuel companies, on which fuel prices depend (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 16]), the opposition calling it blackmail and fraud (TVN24, [Sep 16]) and the energy minister telling the president there is no option A or B (wnp.pl, [Sep 16]). The MF deferred KSeF penalties to end-2027 (Prawo.pl, [Sep 16]) and is bringing a new tax on savings from 2027 via OKI (INFOR.PL, [Sep 16]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen: the company secured 16 crude cargoes from around the world (Interia Biznes, [Sep 16]) as the Saudi pipeline repair will take weeks, not days (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 16]) and minister Balczun called the situation super serious (Next Gazeta.pl, [Sep 16]); the Orlen Termika CEO was dismissed after warning of a winter coal shortage (XYZ, [Sep 16]), and Obajtek denies any knowledge of the Venezuelan deal (TVN24, [Sep 16]). PGE: Q2 net profit of 2015 mln zł and recurring EBITDA of 3963 mln zł (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 16]), 8.1 bn zł EBITDA in H1 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 16]), guidance raised in three segments (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 16]), Baltica 3 and 9+ targeted for 2032 with a partner decision by year-end (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 16]) and no PGZ investment (Bankier.pl, [Sep 16]). Winter power already trades above 700 zł/MWh (e-magazyny.pl, [Sep 16]). KGHM appointed Beata Jurkschat corporate VP (Bankier.pl, [Sep 16]) and its Puck polyhalite mine could yield 150 mln tons in about 15 years (Dziennik Bałtycki, [Sep 16]). Retail: Allegro and LPP report Q2 next (pb.pl, [Sep 16]) and LPP opened its hundredth store in Kazakhstan (Omnichannel News, [Sep 16]); labour tension spans Biedronka, Lidl, Dino and Stokrotka (DlaHandlu.pl, [Sep 16]). Housing: 118 thousand mortgage enquiries mark a sudden return of buyers (Comparic.pl, [Sep 16]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland will convene a security meeting amid rising tensions (The Kyiv Independent, [Sep 16]) as a defence official warned Russia is stepping up aggression toward NATO (The Times, [Sep 16]); the army reinforced posts at Dorohusk (Polskie Radio 24, [Sep 16]) and Poland will again send Patriot missiles to Ukraine (INNPoland.pl, [Sep 16]).

New / Most Important: The NBP is caught between European energy-driven inflation and a Fed that just hiked: core at 3.3% means the fuel shock is spreading, the zloty is at 3.76 heading for 3.80, and the only relief is that the MF still cleared 10 bln of paper at 6.5%. Orlen's supply scramble and the windfall-tax fight are the same story seen from the fiscal side.  |  Playbook ✓: CE3 central banks lagging the ECB's hawkish turn as expected; banks and PGE still earn the environment, KGHM and Orlen do not, duration stays short.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The WIG20 drop was a dividend artefact, not a sell-off, while the bond and FX markets are repricing in earnest; PGE lifted guidance on the very power prices that lift core inflation.
