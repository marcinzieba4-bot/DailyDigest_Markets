🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: The NBP remains on hold and the market has stopped arguing about it. Parkiet's summary is that the economy is growing while inflation ties the RPP's hands [Sep 3], and Commerzbank notes the NBP's language has turned cautious after the FX pressure (tmgm.com, [Sep 3]). The inputs are unchanged: August flash CPI of 3.4% y/y and 0.4% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Aug 31]) versus 3.1% expected (BIZNES24, [Aug 31]), Q2 GDP revised up to 3.9% y/y with investment up 8.4% (PAP Biznes and CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]), and mBank's warning that inflation may slightly exceed 4% at year-end (netTG.pl, [Aug 31]). ING Think's CEE note frames it as resilient growth meeting fiscal and geopolitical headwinds [Sep 3]. The wage picture firmed up: GUS reported the March 2026 median monthly gross wage at 7530.45 zł (Prawo.pl, [Sep 3]), well below the average, after yesterday's reports that the median fell more than 2% (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Sep 2]). Rising eurozone energy prices and a faster euro-area inflation print (TVP Info, [Sep 3]) keep the ECB hawkish, which constrains any dovish pivot from the NBP.

Markets: Equities are being carried by earnings while debt sells off. PGE and KGHM led the GPW on Thursday with Pepco the biggest loser (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 3]); PGE is at its highest in 270 days (Investing.com, [Sep 3]) after its above-forecast estimated Q2 net profit (pb.pl, [Sep 2]), and XTB's desk had CD Projekt and PGE pulling the index up [Sep 3]. The card, as of Wednesday's close, shows the rotation into energy: PGE at 11.85 (+9.4% 1W, +8.7% 4W), Orlen at 158.0 (+6.0% 1W, +5.2% 4W); against them KGHM at 344.15 (-5.5% 1W, -0.6% 4W), PKO BP at 114.24 (-1.3% 1W, +5.4% 4W), Pekao at 257.2 (-1.8% 1W), CD Projekt at 231.2 (-2.9% 1W, -8.8% 4W), Dino at 35.57 (-1.4% 1W, +11.7% 4W) and EPOL at 44.16 (-1.3% 1W, +1.1% 4W). Supply is a theme: the tenth ABB in Allegro saw MidEuropa sell a block worth over 192 mln zł (XYZ, [Sep 3]) with the price reacting calmly (Comparic.pl, [Sep 3]), and XTB's founder sold shares worth 1.5 bn zł (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 3]). The zloty bounced today (Comparic.pl, [Sep 3]) after September opened with the euro erasing Monday's jump (Bankier.pl, [Sep 1]); the card has USD/PLN at 3.7337 (+1.0% 1W, +0.4% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3272 (+0.5% 1W, +0.7% 4W). POLGBs are the alarm bell: Money.pl asks whether the bond move is a prelude to panic and counsels against giving in [Sep 3], Business Insider Polska says rising yields may force a budget correction [Sep 3], while pb.pl argues the higher yield makes this a good moment to start buying [Sep 3].

Fiscal & Politics: The government defended its rising debt in the Sejm and blamed the president (pb.pl, [Sep 3]); Morawiecki called the budget glued together with zip ties (WP Wiadomości, [Sep 3]); Filary Biznesu reports experts fear the rating agencies' reaction [Sep 3]. The 2027 draft stands at a 282.6 bn zł state deficit and 7.1% of GDP general government (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]). The Constitutional Tribunal row escalated: the court has no 2027 budget and the finance ministry gave its reason (Bankier.pl, [Sep 3]) while the opposition says Tusk wants to block it (Money.pl, [Sep 3]). On revenue the ministry is removing a popular tax relief (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 3]). Poland will receive up to €430 million for weapons supplied to Ukraine (Мілітарний, [Sep 3]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen dominated the tape. It assembled a Baltic energy alliance of 14 players from 9 countries covering offshore wind, LNG, hydrogen and SMRs (Biznes Alert and Bankier.pl, [Sep 3]), its CEO said the 2026 result could improve and spoke about the dividend (Bankier.pl, [Sep 3]) and Termika plans over 1 bn zł of investment (WNP.PL, [Sep 3]). The offsets: a sharp fuel price hike (WNP.PL, [Sep 3]), a criminal complaint against the former management with Orlen counting over a billion dollars of losses (Radio Zet, [Sep 3]), an unfavourable Qatar decision the company played down (Money.pl, [Sep 3]) and an SMR programme waiting on PFR money (TVN24, [Sep 3]). CD Projekt's quarter-billion profit (Parkiet, [Sep 3]) enchanted analysts (Bankier.pl, [Sep 3]) but the 2024-2027 incentive target will likely be missed (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 3]) and the Witcher remake is paused (Interia Gry, [Sep 3]). Dino's mediation failed after lawyers rather than the board showed up, and unions threaten a strike (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 3]). Allegro is launching bulky-goods delivery into a market worth 10 bn zł (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 3]) just as LPP's Sinsay marketplace targets 100 thousand products (PropertyNews.pl, [Sep 3]). PKO BP's POLSTR mortgages went live (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Sep 3]); Polish banks earned a record 49bn zł in 2025 (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]); a TSUE ruling for CHF borrowers is scheduled for next week (SBB, [Sep 3]). Gas prices crossed a psychological line (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 3]) though Poland's storage is near 94% full (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 3]). Housing stress: 40% of Poles sit in the rent gap (Forsal.pl, [Sep 3]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk said that if Russia attacked Estonia, Poland would have to be first to its aid (vijesti.me, [Aug 28]); PEJ signed a security agreement with the territorial defence force for the nuclear plant (WNP.PL, [Sep 3]); a fire broke out at a drone factory in Poland (1Lurer, [Aug 31]); Poland ruled out hosting nuclear weapons (УНН, [Aug 27]).

New / Most Important: The gap between the two Polish markets is the story. Equities are at or near highs in PGE and Orlen on energy-price earnings, while the sovereign curve is sending what Money.pl calls an alarm and Business Insider Polska links to a possible budget correction. One of them is wrong about the next six months.  |  Playbook ✓: ECB hawkishness and eurozone energy prices keep the NBP on hold; energy earners over banks and consumer remains the trade.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: pb.pl's call to start buying POLGBs at these yields is the first contrarian bond voice in weeks; if the budget-correction talk becomes policy, duration outperforms the equities that have been leading.
