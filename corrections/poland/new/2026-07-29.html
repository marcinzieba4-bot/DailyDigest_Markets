🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → Hold

Macro Stance: NBP remains on hold, and the fuel pass-through is now the consensus worry rather than a tail. Citi expects CPI to reach 3% as early as July on fuel prices (PAP Biznes, [Jul 28]) and Business Insider Polska warned inflation could breach the NBP's limit with more price rises to come ([Jul 28]). Parkiet's framing is that inflation is turning back up from Poland's lowest EU ranking in more than seven years ([Jul 29]), and Grzegorz Maliszewski of Bank Millennium says the economy is running on stimulants and asks whether braking is next ([Jul 29]). The Council has already moved: Kotecki says the situation is starting to differ from the NBP projection and it is too early to declare a September cut (Bankier.pl, pb.pl, [Jul 23]). Activity remains firm, with retail sales up 6.2% y/y against 5.2% expected (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 22]), ING BSK at about 3.8% y/y for Q2 GDP (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 22]) and unemployment down in June (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 23]), while wage growth is the slowest since 2021 (FOCUS ON Business, [Jul 23]). Abroad, the Fed left rates unchanged, ignoring Trump (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 29]).

Markets: Wednesday was a bull day with a gaming face. CD Projekt led the GPW higher (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 29]), closing up 6.44% (Stooq, [Jul 29]), StockWatch.pl credits mBank's and Orlen's results for lifting the index ([Jul 29]) and Strefa Biznesu now talks of beating the 2007 record ([Jul 29]). mBank earned 2 billion zloty in half a year and promised a dividend (Bankier.pl, [Jul 29]), a record despite higher CIT (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 29]). The losers were the commodity names: FXMAG has Pepco and Dino pulling up while KGHM and Orlen slid ([Jul 29]) and Parkiet reports KGHM's new strategy surprised by dropping its earlier issuance target ([Jul 29]). The card, with Tuesday's closes, shows the rotation already under way: Dino 31.66 (+6.3% 1W, +11.5% 4W), LPP 20960 (+5.1% / +14.7%), CD Projekt 237.7 (+2.9% / +5.9%), PKO 109.96 (+1.0% / +6.6%), Pekao 237.7 (+0.6% / +3.9%), PGE 9.9 (+4.2% / +4.9%), Allegro 45.0 (-0.2% / +19.0%), Orlen 144.84 (-2.9% / +14.4%) after its record 8 zloty dividend (Bankier.pl, [Jul 28]), KGHM 292.55 (-4.5% / -11.7%); EPOL closed at 41.24, down 0.2% on the week and up 7.2% over four weeks. The zloty shrugged off the war: Bankier.pl says neither the euro nor the dollar broke resistance ([Jul 29]) after the dollar had pierced 3.80 on Tuesday (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 28]); the card has USD/PLN 3.7971 (+0.1% 1W, +0.9% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3248 (flat / +0.6%). POLGBs: the ministry sold more than 11 billion zloty of paper into strong demand (Bankier.pl, [Jul 29]) even as Subiektywnie o finansach called the rise in yields before the Fed worrying ([Jul 29]).

Fiscal & Politics: The allowance now has a price tag: Polskie Radio 24 puts the 60 000 zloty tax-free amount at 58.6 billion zloty a year to start ([Jul 29]) and wnp.pl's Hirsch counts the 2027 PIT change at 70 billion zloty for the budget with spending cuts unavoidable ([Jul 29]). At the same time the ministry withdrew the higher lump-sum rates and the 100 thousand zloty limit for sole traders (Bankier.pl, Polskie Radio 24, wnp.pl, [Jul 29]), a day after dropping its PIT and CIT anti-avoidance package (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 28]). DW reports a record for public debt per person ([Jul 29]), Treasury debt is above 2.1 trillion zloty (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 24]) and Arak sees more than 70% of GDP by end-2027 (Filary Biznesu, [Jul 26]). Housing policy is splitting the coalition: the government is preparing a new cheap-flat credit with up to 100 thousand zloty for TBS participation (Money.pl, Zero.pl, [Jul 29]) while Pełczyńska-Nałęcz said her party will not back a return to mortgage subsidies (Super Biznes, [Jul 29]).

Corporate & Sectors: Allegro hired BLIK's chief executive Dariusz Mazurkiewicz to run financial services (Bankier.pl, Cashless.pl, [Jul 29]), two days after naming a new CFO (Press.pl, [Jul 28]) and extending its buyback by 100 million zloty (wirtualnemedia.pl, [Jul 28]). Orlen's Nova Post deal keeps drawing fire, with Nasz Dziennik saying it is handing the market to Ukrainians ([Jul 29]). Dino launched its first large advertising campaign, challenging Biedronka and Lidl (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Jul 29]) and set new mediation dates with the unions after a ministry intervention (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 29]). PKO BP faces questions over 228 million dollars of exposure to bankrupt GoldenPeaks (Wyborcza.biz, [Jul 29]); banks raised mortgage rates again (Money.pl, [Jul 29]). The government is preparing a cheaper-energy package that industry doubts (Business Insider Polska, Money.pl, [Jul 29]) as Strefa Biznesu warns factories may not survive current power prices ([Jul 29]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland blamed Russia for firing a missile into its territory after an explosion near a village during Moscow's massive strike on Ukraine (CNN, The Guardian, [Jul 29]); Sybiha identified it as a Kh-101 (УНН, [Jul 29]) and Forsal.pl says Russia has crossed a new line with Poland in the zone of greatest threat ([Jul 29]). NATO generals say Russia may attack Europe by 2029 while the alliance will not be ready before 2035 (Kyiv Post, [Jul 29]).

New / Most Important: A Russian missile on Polish soil is the development, and the market's non-reaction is the message. The zloty held its resistances, the Treasury sold 11 billion zloty of bonds into strong demand and the WIG20 looked at its record, all on the day. Investors are treating the eastern flank as priced; the fiscal arithmetic is what they are not pricing, with a 58.6 billion zloty allowance, a 70 billion zloty PIT cost and a ministry retreating from every revenue measure it floats.  |  Playbook ✓: Rates on hold, inflation sticky; the rotation from Orlen and KGHM into Dino, LPP, CD Projekt and banks fits a market moving from commodity beta to domestic earnings.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Yields rising "worryingly" into a successful 11 billion zloty auction and a flat currency on a missile strike say the bond market is pricing fiscal risk while FX and equities price growth; if the 2027 PIT package passes at 70 billion zloty, the bond market is right.
