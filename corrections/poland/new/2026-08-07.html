Talks on a permanent US base in Poland are under way (NDTV, [Aug 5]). Defense spending continues to provide fiscal stimulus.

New / Most Important: The US intelligence leak. A "limited attack" scenario with a named timeframe is categorically different from last week's stray missile: it is a risk the zloty, POLGBs and a 4000-point WIG20 do not carry a premium for, and it lands as the NBP argues with the ECB over diverting profit to defence and the MF promises tax cuts on the EU's largest deficit. Orlen's record quarter being sold tells you positioning is full; the geopolitical tape tells you where the next repricing comes from.
