🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, Nov projection key

Macro Stance: The week's monetary picture is now complete. The RPP left the reference rate at 3.75% (Strefa Inwestorów [Oct 7]), with a communiqué that named energy prices and fiscal policy as the main inflation risks (Investing.com [Oct 7]), and Governor Glapiński followed by saying he does not foresee a November hike but the Council "is ready to act" if risks mount (PAP Biznes, Bloomberg [Oct 8]); the November projection will decide (Portal spożywczy [Oct 8]). Today the message hardened at the edges: Business Insider Polska [Oct 9] reports the RPP warning about a 2027 scenario as fuels and commodities rise, an RPP member told pb.pl [Oct 9] the priority is that the currency not become the victim of the turmoil around the NBP, and ING sees hikes delayed while the zloty's range edges up (TradingPedia [Oct 9]). PKB24 [Oct 8] notes the real rate has turned negative after a fourth straight hold, while ING pencils in hikes in 2027 with inflation able to exceed 3.5% (CEO Magazyn [Oct 8]). The data: September flash CPI 4.0% y/y with fuel up 36.1% (PAP Biznes [Sep 30]); 2025 GDP revised to 3.8% (wnp.pl [Oct 6]); April median wage 7695 zł against a 9651 zł average (Podatki.biz [Oct 7]); consumer sentiment at a one-year low (CEO Magazyn [Oct 7]).

Markets: Warsaw ended the week higher with stocks "back in favour" (Parkiet [Oct 9]), PGE, KGHM and Tauron leading and Kruk falling (Inwestycje.pl [Oct 9]); FXMAG has Orlen up 3.49% and Dino up 6.35% today while Alior, mBank and PKO BP lost ground. That caps a choppy stretch: a bearish engulfing candle on WIG20 on Oct 7 (Parkiet) and WIG-Banki down 2.41% on Oct 8 (FXMAG). WATS has handled 1.02m trades and 9.4bn zł of turnover in four days without incident (Inwestycje.pl [Oct 9]). The zloty found a floor: USD/PLN closed 3.9015, up 0.3% on the week and 4.8% over four weeks, and today the dollar dropped back below 3.90 on a signal from the oil market (Comparic [Oct 9]) after printing its highest since April 2025 on Oct 8 (FXMAG); EUR/PLN at 4.3752 is flat on the week and up 1.2% over four weeks. Bonds are catching their breath (CEO Magazyn [Oct 9]), though a ZBP and SGH report warns banks' growing exposure to government debt may crowd out corporate lending (Portal Samorządowy [Oct 9]). EPOL closed 43.44, up 0.4% on the week and down 3.8% over four weeks. Orlen at 155.46 is up 6.4% on the week, Dino at 35.5 up 2.7%, KGHM at 328.35 up 2.2%, Allegro at 50.43 up 12.1% over four weeks and PGE at 15.72 up 10.7%; PKO at 114.92 (down 4.0%), Pekao at 263.0 (down 2.9%) and LPP at 24400 (down 1.4%) lag.

Fiscal & Politics: The Sejm rejected the PiS motion to throw out the 2027 budget and sent the draft to committee (Interia [Oct 8], Super Biznes [Oct 9]): nearly 1 trillion zł of spending, a 281.2bn zł deficit (Bankier [Oct 8]) and almost 200bn zł for defence (Portal Samorządowy [Oct 8]), with XTB noting a fourth straight year of deficits above 200bn. Domański says listening to the Fiscal Council would have made the deficit larger (Bizblog [Oct 8]); the ministry is reported to fear a presidential veto (Biznes Wprost [Oct 8]). Retail treasury bonds sold 7.9bn zł in September (CEO Magazyn [Oct 9]). The Sejm also passed a nationwide ban on night-time alcohol sales (Gazeta Prawna [Oct 9]). The political overhang on the NBP grew: a Sejm commission recommended a vote on putting Glapiński before the State Tribunal (Bloomberg [Oct 9]).

Corporate & Sectors: CD Projekt jumped after Paramount said it will develop a live-action Cyberpunk 2077 film (Reuters [Oct 9]). KGHM won EU strategic-project status for two investments worth over 9.5bn zł (StockWatch, pb.pl [Oct 9]) on top of its $725m Sierra Gorda expansion. Orlen's former deputy CEO and three directors were remanded in custody over pre-election fuel pricing (TVN24 [Oct 8]) and the group raised wholesale fuel prices sharply (wnp.pl [Oct 8]). Dino's unions set a general strike for just before the holidays (Bankier [Oct 9]), yet the stock led today. LPP is approaching 2700 stores with 13 new locations (PropertyNews [Oct 9]). Banks face a widening legal front: a Suwałki court awarded 188 thousand zł against PKO Bank Hipoteczny in a WIBOR case (CHF24 [Oct 8]) and Millennium is booking 154m zł of FX-loan provisions for Q3 (StockWatch [Oct 8]). Housing: Warsaw prices rose 6.3% in a quarter while Łódź fell 5% (CEO Magazyn [Oct 8]), and Bankier [Oct 9] reports owners pulling rental flats after legal changes in "the craziest market in 30 years". The government is readying 4.8bn zł of energy-cost support for industry (PulsHR [Oct 8]).

Geopolitics & Security: Russian military flights near Poland and Sweden's Patriot deployment headline Defence24's weekly [Oct 9], after Russian strikes near the border triggered Polish aviation deployment and an air-defence alert (UNITED24 [Oct 9]). Forsal [Oct 9] reports Poland signalling that its fighters could fly in Ukraine's defence, and [Oct 8] that a neighbour is moving troops to the border. An investigation found Russian drones over Poland carried Polish SIM cards (UNITED24 [Oct 8]).

New / Most Important: The week ends with the NBP in a two-front squeeze: the Council has promised readiness to hike without a date, while the Sejm moves toward a State Tribunal vote on the governor. An RPP member's plea that the zloty not become the casualty is the tell. For markets, policy credibility, not the 3.75% rate, is now the variable; the currency's Oct 8 low and Friday's rebound show how quickly that premium moves.

Playbook ✓: Hold the week's winners, Orlen, Dino and KGHM, into the EU strategic-status and Chile catalysts; keep POLGB duration short while banks' sovereign exposure is under scrutiny; stay hedged on USD/PLN above 3.90 but do not chase the dollar after today's reversal.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Banks are falling while the index rises, which inverts the usual Warsaw pattern; the script said hold-and-watch, but the market is pricing a mix of litigation, POLSTR migration and sovereign crowding-out that a rate hold does not fix.
