🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike question asked

Macro Stance: The question has been put in print: Tysol.pl asks whether rates go up, calling it an important signal for Poland [Aug 26], days after reporting which RPP members wanted a hike [Aug 22]. The activity backdrop supports the hawks on balance. CEO Magazyn sums up the July batch as an economy still growing, industry strong, construction a surprise decline [Aug 26]; PAP asks whether large firms investing again marks a new growth phase [Aug 26]. The softer side is the consumer, with July retail sales up 3.9% y/y in constant prices (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 24]) but below forecasts (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Aug 24]), and unemployment at 5.8% (Forsal.pl, [Aug 25]) with wages up 6.8% (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 20]). Fuel relief arrives tonight, with cheaper prices from just after midnight (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 26]), while Portal Spożywczy sees prices rising ever more slowly but a turning point ahead [Aug 25].

Markets: The WIG20 finally bounced off 4000 points (Parkiet, [Aug 26]), up 0.7% with PGE more than 5% higher (Bankier.pl, [Aug 26]) as cheaper oil lifted the WIG and brought PGE and KGHM back to gains (XTB.com, [Aug 26]), and PKO BP printed a new all-time high (DNA Rynków, [Aug 26]). Żabka was the centre of attention (Parkiet, [Aug 26]) after Couche-Tard launched a tender offer for all its shares (Reuters, [Aug 26]); Strefa Biznesu argues the proceeds may go to tax havens and that Dino gains from the shake-up [Aug 26]. The card reflects Tuesday's close: KGHM 366.0 (+9.4% 1W, +25.1% 4W) and Dino 36.18 (+11.4% 1W, +14.3% 4W) are the leaders, PKO 113.52 (+3.3% 1W), Pekao 257.1 (+1.3% 1W, +8.2% 4W) and PGE 10.54 (+2.4% 1W, +6.5% 4W) firm, EPOL 44.69 (+2.4% 1W, +8.4% 4W), while CD Projekt 242.9 (-6.0% 1W) carries the Witcher 4 damage and LPP 20,700 (-1.5% 1W) and Allegro 44.87 (-0.5% 1W) drift. The zloty is pushing the euro to a key level while the dollar froze ahead of the US reading (Bankier.pl, [Aug 26]); the card has USD/PLN 3.684 (-1.3% 1W, -3.0% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3005 (-0.4% 1W, -0.6% 4W), though Comparic.pl flagged an intraday reversal today [Aug 26]. POLGBs: billions are flowing into bond funds but Analizy.pl asks where the returns are [Aug 26], Pimco says bonds are attractive because the term premium will stay elevated (pb.pl, [Aug 24]).

Fiscal & Politics: The veto fight turned personal. After the finance ministry presented its bill for the president's vetoes, the Palace called it an inept manipulation (Interia, Fakt, [Aug 26]) and Nawrocki's spokesman answered the salty invoice (TVN24, [Aug 26]); Rzeczpospolita had put the cost at billions of zloty [Aug 25]. The government decided the lump-sum taxation rules, including the fate of the 15% rate (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 26]). The 4.8 billion zł support for energy-intensive firms comes with conditions on renewables and decarbonisation investment (ESG Trends, [Aug 26]), and KGHM, Orlen and Grupa Azoty are lobbying for changes to the energy ministry's tariff draft, which Interia says could exclude the largest firms [Aug 26]. A rare positive from the public sector: finance ministry data show local governments running surpluses instead of deficits (Serwis Samorządowy PAP, [Aug 26]). Fitch's A- with negative outlook (PAP, [Aug 22]) remains the frame.

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen started production from three new Norwegian fields a year ahead of plan (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 26]), adding over 4 billion cubic metres of gas (WNP.PL, [Aug 26]); an analyst raised valuations of Orlen and Unimot on exceptional macro conditions (pb.pl, [Aug 26]). KGHM and its partner reported copper resources up 61% (Parkiet, [Aug 26]), the company plans polyhalite mining at Puck Bay (PCH24.pl, [Aug 26]) and Energetyka24 confirms it as the world's largest silver producer [Aug 26], though wnp.pl warns record copper prices could become a problem for it [Aug 26]. CD Projekt announced a Witcher 3 remaster and showed the Songs of the Past trailer (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 26]); DM BOŚ expects the expansion in the first quarter of 2027 (BIZNES24, [Aug 26]), Parkiet asks whether the sell-off is a buying opportunity [Aug 26], pb.pl's analyst says hold [Aug 26] and Bankier.pl reports short positions rising [Aug 26]; half-year results are due next week (CD PROJEKT RED IR, [Aug 26]). PKO BP is contesting the 47.5 million zł KNF fine and asked for the case to be reconsidered (pb.pl, [Aug 26]). Dino keeps rising despite weaker results as analysts revisit the stock (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Aug 26]) and opens stores at scale while Biedronka fights on price (DlaHandlu.pl, [Aug 26]); LPP has 150 stores in Hungary (Retailnet.pl, [Aug 26]); Allegro was cheaper in nearly 77% of almost 20 thousand price comparisons (aboutmarketing.pl, [Aug 26]).

Geopolitics & Security: Forsal.pl reports Russian exercises near the NATO border rehearsing saboteurs first and an armed attack second [Aug 26]; NATO is weighing the cost of shooting back at escalating drone incursions (The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 24]) and is taking measures against Moscow's direct actions from drones to sabotage (Il Foglio, [Aug 26]). Poland detained a Ukrainian over an alleged Russian-backed assassination plot (TVP World, [Aug 20]).

New / Most Important: Orlen's Norwegian gas and the Żabka tender matter more than the index bounce. Upstream volume a year early is a direct hedge on the energy CIT the company is about to pay, and a full takeover of Żabka is a foreign strategic buyer validating Polish consumer assets at the moment a British fund is short the market.
