🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold

Macro Stance: The rate debate has reopened from the top. Super Biznes reported that Glapiński wants to cut rates and that experts are sounding the alarm [Aug 3], days after the GUS flash put July CPI back at 3.0% y/y (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 31]) with fuel up around 14% in a month (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Jul 31]). Global Banking & Finance Review read the print as strengthening the hold outlook [Jul 31], and Parkiet's economist panel told the RPP not to rush [Jul 31]. Only food is restraining the index (xyz.pl, [Aug 2]); Citi's call for CPI above 3.5% by year-end stands. Activity is firmer: the manufacturing PMI rebounded in July with the first rise in employment for months (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 3]) and Parkiet expects GUS data to confirm a solid recovery [Aug 4]. The NBP's lending survey points to easier credit for firms and tighter terms for households (Bankier.pl, [Aug 3]). No GUS wage or retail release this week.

Markets: Two consecutive records. The WIG20 closed Aug 3 at an all-time high, beating the 2007 peak (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 3]); on Aug 4 it added 1.35% at the close (BIZNES24) as banks and KGHM drove the market while Dino came under pressure (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 4]). Domański called it the effect of growing confidence in Poland (TVN24, [Aug 4]). The card shows breadth: EPOL at 42.94 is up 4.6% on the week and 7.6% over four weeks; PKO at 112.5 gained 4.2% (1W) and Pekao 3.7%; Dino (+8.3% 1W, +16.9% 4W), CD Projekt (+8.2% 1W) and LPP (+6.3% 1W, +15.8% 4W) led; KGHM rose 3.4% on the week but is still down 4.8% over four weeks. Orlen is off 1.3% on the week despite a +11.6% four-week run, while PGE (+1.9% 1W, +10.6% 4W) holds up. The zloty consolidated: USD/PLN closed at 3.7442 (-1.5% 1W, flat 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3081 (-0.3% 1W, +0.5% 4W); FXMAG saw a technical bounce from 4.30 on EUR/PLN and 3.72 on USD/PLN [Aug 4] after the zloty gained more than 6 groszy against the dollar on Iran deal hopes (Bankier.pl, [Aug 3]). Two new ETFs tracking Polish bonds launched (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Aug 4]) a week after yields rose worryingly into the Fed [Jul 29].

Fiscal & Politics: The 2027 budget is being assembled against the largest deficit in the EU and pressure to cut taxes (pb.pl, [Aug 4]), and Bloomberg reported Poland is weighing income tax relief for millions before the election [Aug 4]. Signals are contradictory (Money.pl, [Aug 4]): Domański said work on tax changes is ongoing (wnp.pl, [Aug 4]) and outlined a scenario for raising the second threshold and the tax-free allowance (Interia Biznes, [Aug 4]), while Gazeta Prawna judged the allowance increase unlikely despite being a key KO promise [Aug 4]. Rzeczpospolita called public debt a millstone and urged a return to privatisation [Aug 4]. Smaller items: a ministry simulation of cutting VAT on water from 23 to 8 percent (Bankier.pl, [Aug 4]) and a five-year limit on tax interpretations (XYZ, [Aug 4]). The Sejm voted on a 62-year retirement age for both sexes, with the outcome hanging on a single decision (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 4]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen cut pump prices for the first time in months (MotoGuru.pl, [Aug 4]) after the minister demanded bigger reductions (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 3]), and bought a 216 MW hybrid wind-solar project from Onde and Neo Energy Group (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 4]) in contracts worth more than 500 mln zł (pb.pl, [Aug 4]). The heatwave is the utilities story: the largest unit of Poland's largest power plant is offline for a long period (WNP.PL, [Aug 4]), prices were set to exceed 2000 zł/MWh on Tuesday evening (e-magazyny.pl, [Aug 3]), and PGE, Enea and Tauron rallied on it (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 4]). KGHM's Nitroerg committed 300 mln zł to a defence explosives plant (Bankier.pl, [Aug 3]). Banks keep delivering: Pekao beat forecasts despite lower rates and higher CIT (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 30]), mortgage lending topped 13 bn zł for a fourth month (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 30]), and PKO ran the first contactless BLIK payment on iPhone (Cashless.pl, [Aug 4]); the risk is regulatory, with the minister attacking the benchmark that will set instalments from 2027 (Money.pl, [Aug 4]). Couche-Tard's Żabka deal was priced at 32.62 bn zloty (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 3]). Parkiet flagged a test at 35.6 zł for Dino [Aug 4]. The government's new housing loan offers a 400 zł instalment over 15 years (Portal Samorządowy, [Aug 2]).

Geopolitics & Security: The missile aftermath has shifted to Belarus. Lukashenko ordered special forces to the Ukrainian border (Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 3]), a new assault brigade appeared there (Forsal.pl, [Aug 3]), and Russia is pushing Belarus to the front line against the West (Tomorrow's Affairs, [Aug 4]). Ukrainian officials warn Poland is running out of time for the worst scenario (Forsal.pl, [Aug 4]) and that Russia may use captured Ukrainian drones for provocations (TVP World, [Aug 3]). The army is preparing forward depots near the border (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 31]) with 100 km of barrier complete (RMF24, [Jul 31]).

New / Most Important: The WIG20 clearing its 2007 high is the week's fact, but the policy collision behind it matters more: the NBP governor is floating cuts into a 3.0% CPI print driven by a 14% fuel jump, while the finance ministry builds a 2027 budget on the EU's largest deficit and pre-election tax relief. Equities price bank earnings, takeover premia and a confidence narrative the minister endorses; bonds and the zloty will price whether cut talk and fiscal loosening are real.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold remains the base case; stay with the record's leaders in banks and domestic retail, treat utilities as a heatwave trade, and keep duration light until the governor's cut signal is tested.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A dovish governor against hawkish panels and a 3.0% print; Dino sold on the record day after a +16.9% four-week run; Orlen cutting pump prices under political pressure while its 4W return says the market had priced fuel margins.
