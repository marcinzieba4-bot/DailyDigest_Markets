🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, Council split

Macro Stance: The RPP's internal argument is now fully public. A Council hawk told Gazeta Prawna that the earlier cuts "were premature" and warned of inflation's return ([Jul 21]), repeating the line that hikes are more probable than cuts (Super Biznes, [Jul 21]), one day after Crédit Agricole pencilled in a September cut of 25 bp to 3.50% as the terminal rate (PAP Biznes, [Jul 20]) and another member refused to exclude a cut this year (pb.pl, [Jul 20]). The macro backdrop favours patience rather than either camp: wnp.pl's Hirsch reads June data as the economy growing faster ([Jul 21]), Parkiet credits investment and the arms industry for the factory rebound ([Jul 21]) after production rose 7.6% (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 20]), and real wages are rising fast with pay up 5.9% y/y (XYZ, [Jul 20]; StockWatch.pl, [Jul 20]). June CPI was 2.5% (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 15]) but the end of the CPN fuel package and oil are the swing factors: Comparic sees crude heading for 100 dollars with reserves at 40-year lows ([Jul 21]) and diesel wholesale prices jumped again (Super Biznes, [Jul 21]). Colliers, for its part, says the economy is ready for a new phase of growth ([Jul 21]).

Markets: The WIG set another record and the WIG20 had a shot at a historic close, with large caps pulling the exchange up (XYZ, Parkiet, [Jul 21]). KGHM led the WIG20, up 4.5% as copper broke 13,600 dollars a tonne (Strefa Inwestorów, Investing.com Polska, [Jul 21]) and Forex Club has it reaching five-year highs on the commodity wave ([Jul 21]), though FXMAG flags a warning signal for the stock ([Jul 21]). Allegro also drove the index (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 21]) and Bankier notes hundreds of millions of zloty flowing into Allegro, PKO, Orlen and KGHM as the WIG20 knocks on its old record ([Jul 21]). Prior-close card: KGHM 293.45 (-3.9% 1W, -20.3% 4W) before Tuesday's jump, Allegro 44.0 (+10.0% / +14.1%), Dino 29.9 (+6.9% / +3.1%), LPP 19910 (+3.4% / +8.5%), Orlen 146.62 (+1.8% / +15.0%), PKO 106.74 (-4.5% / +1.9%), Pekao 232.5 (-4.4% / +0.4%), PGE 9.48 (flat / -3.7%), CD Projekt 230.4 (-0.3% / +0.4%); EPOL 40.45 is +0.7% 1W and +1.8% 4W. DNA Rynków notes media are warning of a mass exit from PPK pension plans even as Żabka and Allegro surge ([Jul 21]). The zloty is recovering losses: EUR/PLN sits near 4.33 while bond yields rise (FXMAG, [Jul 21]), Bankier calls the euro's move a "very local" correction ([Jul 21]). The card has USD/PLN 3.7943 (-0.3% 1W, +1.6% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3317 (+0.1% / +1.5%). The POLGB story is the yield rise against a stronger currency, which the debt data below explain.

Fiscal & Politics: Eurostat put Polish public debt at 61.6% of GDP, the third-fastest increase in the EU (XYZ, [Jul 21]), a "historic moment" with debt above 60% (XYZ, [Jul 21]); Polskie Radio 24 says the warning threshold has been breached ([Jul 21]), wnp.pl tallies 138.6 billion zloty of new debt as unprecedented ([Jul 21]), and TVN24, Bankier and Money.pl all place Poland on the EU podium for debt growth ([Jul 21]). CEO Magazyn says a debt crisis is not imminent but risk is rising systematically ([Jul 21]); Business Insider Polska finds the deficit-and-debt comparison with the EU "not encouraging" ([Jul 21]). The Finance Ministry's response is austerity at the margin: no room for public-sector pay rises (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 21]), unions warn of a budget-sphere wage freeze (Fakt, [Jul 21]), a tax for one group could rise by as much as 40% (Money.pl, [Jul 21]), sugar-tax and e-cigarette measures are in the works (Topagrar.pl, Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 21]), and NFZ has a huge financing gap (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 21]). The cabinet did adopt a VAT simplification package for importers and exporters (Gazeta Prawna, Gov.pl, [Jul 21]) and a defence-industry strategy to 2030 (XYZ, [Jul 21]). Presidential vetoes cost the budget 5-6 billion zloty a year by Domański's count (Wprost, [Jul 17]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen pre-announced 1.9 billion zloty of second-quarter write-downs, mainly on the Nowa Chemia project (pb.pl, wnp.pl, Parkiet, [Jul 21]), alongside its estimated operating data (Bankier.pl, [Jul 21]); it is now worth more than every Russian oil major (Fakt, [Jul 21]) and had to deny selling fuel to Ukraine cheaper than at home (Money.pl, [Jul 21]). Its Żerań heat accumulator is ready to warm 75 thousand flats (Gramwzielone.pl, [Jul 21]). CD Projekt will present the Witcher 3 "Songs of the Past" expansion at gamescom's opening night (StockWatch.pl, pb.pl, [Jul 21]). Dino finally opened its contested Wilanów store, its second in Warsaw (Business Insider Polska, Portal spożywczy, [Jul 21]). PGE's Porąbka-Żar plant gets 150 million zloty from KPO (Dziennik Zachodni, [Jul 21]). Energy companies' hopes for big ETS changes were dashed (Parkiet, [Jul 21]) and the ministry's new invoice layout for 16.5 million households drew accusations of hiding the ETS share (WP Wiadomości, Do Rzeczy, [Jul 21]). Banks are winning most of the 6.6 thousand free-credit-sanction complaints after the TSUE ruling (Bankier.pl, [Jul 21]).

Geopolitics & Security: After the security committee, Kosiniak-Kamysz said Russia may use intercepted Ukrainian drones against Poland (Interia Wydarzenia, Defense News, [Jul 21]). Lukashenka deployed special forces to the Ukrainian border (Polskie Radio 24, Wiadomości Onet, [Jul 21]). wnp.pl argues Poland has overtaken Europe's key defence programmes, with Ukraine as an extra opportunity ([Jul 21]), and Russian media are focusing on exercises in Poland (Fakt, [Jul 21]).

New / Most Important: Debt at 61.6% of GDP is the number that reframes everything else this week. The equity market is at records and the zloty is stabilising, but POLGB yields rose on the day Eurostat confirmed Poland is adding debt faster than almost anyone in the EU, and the ministry's answer is a pay freeze and small tax rises rather than a plan. For a central bank already split on direction, a fiscal stance that stays loose into an oil shock is the strongest argument the hawks have.

Playbook ✓: Copper above 13,600 dollars lifting KGHM and a commodity-heavy index is orthodox; a currency recovering as yields rise is a normal term-premium move.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Record equities and 60%-plus debt are coexisting only because growth is strong; if the Gulf keeps oil bid, the NBP's hawks get their inflation and the Finance Ministry loses the growth that is masking the deficit, and gold under pressure from USD strength offers no hedge on the day.
