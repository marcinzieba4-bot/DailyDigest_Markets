🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, September cut in doubt

Macro Stance: Kotecki moved the Council's centre of gravity on Thursday: the inflation situation is diverging from the NBP projection and it is too early for declarations about a September cut (pb.pl, Bankier.pl, [Jul 23]); Business Insider Polska reads the RPP as in no hurry to cut, with one detail that may surprise borrowers ([Jul 23]). That lands two days after a hawk called the earlier cuts premature (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 21]) and three after Crédit Agricole pencilled a 25 bp September cut to 3.50% as terminal (PAP Biznes, [Jul 20]), so the dovish case now rests on tame prices, with Polskie Radio 24 noting a single person's shopping basket went back more than half a year in price ([Jul 23]). The data argue for patience: unemployment fell in June with the season helping (Rzeczpospolita, Business Insider Polska, [Jul 23]) and Forsal.pl calls the labour market resistant to slowdown ([Jul 23]), yet wage growth has slowed to its lowest since 2021 (FOCUS ON Business, [Jul 23]) even after June's 5.9% y/y corporate print (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 20]). Retail sales rose 6.2% y/y against a 5.2% consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 22]) and ING BSK sees Q2 GDP near 3.8% y/y (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 22]). The external constraint is explicit: PKO BP thinks the ECB may raise rates in September (PAP, [Jul 23]), and the ECB's hawkish hold today reinforces the NBP's own patience, with oil almost at 100 dollars (Bankier.pl, [Jul 23]).

Markets: The 3900-point barrier is holding the WIG20 for now (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 23]) though Business Insider Polska has the index close to an all-time record with Orlen breaking its historic barrier again ([Jul 23]). Thursday was a split tape: PGE rose 7.37%, Tauron 9.54% and Pepco 4.31% as the WIG20 gained (FXMAG, [Jul 23]), while Inwestycje.pl saw PGE and Orlen resisting a sell-off in which Żabka, Dino and banks lost heavily ([Jul 23]) and XYZ summed it up as banks sinking while Orlen set a record ([Jul 23]). The card, as of Wednesday's close, already shows the energy tilt: Orlen 151.88 (+4.4% 1W, +23.7% 4W), PGE 10.2 (+9.1% / +9.9%), KGHM 314.5 (+0.4% / -3.0%), Allegro 45.08 (+1.3% / +20.8%), Dino 30.26 (+6.0% / +4.3%), LPP 20280 (+2.2% / +12.0%), CD Projekt 233.0 (+0.5% / +3.1%), PKO 108.52 (-0.5% / +5.0%), Pekao 236.0 (+0.2% / +3.1%); EPOL closed at 41.37, up 0.8% on the week and 6.8% over four weeks. The zloty is steady into the ECB: Bankier.pl says the euro stays stable with crude near 100 dollars ([Jul 23]); the card has USD/PLN 3.7901 (+0.6% 1W, +0.5% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3253 (+0.2% / +1.0%). POLGBs had a good day on the primary side: the Treasury raised more than 11.2 billion zloty at a successful auction (Bankier.pl, [Jul 23]) even as yields rose before the ECB with Bunds at 15-year highs (pb.pl, [Jul 23]).

Fiscal & Politics: The debt headlines went international: Euronews ranks Poland third in Europe for rising public debt ([Jul 23]) and Yahoo Finance has Polish debt above 500 billion euros with borrowing at a record pace ([Jul 23]), after Eurostat's 61.6% of GDP (XYZ, [Jul 21]). The revenue hunt continues: Gazeta Prawna reports a tax increase of more than 40% for one group of entrepreneurs ([Jul 23]), the ministry will not retreat from taxing owners of building plots (Portal Samorządowy, [Jul 23]), and it promises a free app in place of cash registers in a third-quarter VAT bill (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 23]). Poland launched a 205 million dollar defence innovation programme (Anadolu, [Jul 23]). The amendment speeding up the nuclear special act, cutting investment time by up to two years, now awaits the president's signature (Bankier.pl, Energetyka24, [Jul 23]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen keeps rebutting the charge of underpricing fuel for Ukraine (e-petrol.pl, INNPoland.pl, [Jul 23]). KGHM is developing foreign projects with Morocco and Argentina on the radar (XYZ, [Jul 23]). Banks fell on the day Franknews.pl asked whether the CHF law opens a wide road to free credit ([Jul 23]), Gazeta Prawna explained what POLSTR replacing WIBOR means for instalments ([Jul 23]) and BIK tightened checks from July (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 23]). LPP tied up with Polish start-up Pirxe to put AI into marketing (wirtualnemedia.pl, [Jul 23]). Polish business is paying record energy costs in the EU (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 23]), PKEE says the ETS still needs changes (Energetyka24, [Jul 23]) and wnp.pl reports the nuclear plant may miss its deadline ([Jul 23]).

Geopolitics & Security: Belarus has moved special forces to the Ukrainian border (Forsal.pl, Strefa Obrony, [Jul 23]) and Kresy24.pl reports Belarusian army units right at the Polish frontier ([Jul 23]); German troops are at Poland's border with Russia, which Ukrainians read as a signal (Forsal.pl, [Jul 23]). An unnamed NATO country has asked for the Polish MiG-29s Ukraine expected (Цензор.НЕТ, [Jul 23]).

New / Most Important: Kotecki's "too early" is the development. He is the Council member markets listen to, and he said it on the day the ECB held hawkishly, oil approached 100 dollars and the Treasury sold 11.2 billion zloty of paper into rising yields. A September cut was consensus three days ago; today it is a question. The equity market has not noticed, because Orlen and PGE are an oil and power trade, not a rates trade, which is exactly why banks and Dino were the day's losers.

Playbook ✓: Hold confirmed; energy outperforming banks when the cut is pulled is textbook, and a successful auction keeps the fiscal story funded.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: PLN trades as a beta to EUR and regional risk sentiment, so a currency flat into an ECB hawkish hold while debt tops 500 billion euros and yields climb is the quiet mispricing; if the September cut is dead, the zloty should be stronger and the WIG20 banks should not be falling.
