🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: The RPP held, as expected: the reference rate stays at 3.75% (PAP Biznes, [Jul 8]), and Bankier.pl noted the Council once again did not surprise the market [Jul 8]. The July projection is the substance. NBP published its 2026 inflation range with 50% probability (Polska Agencja Prasowa, [Jul 8]), and ING called the communiqué neutral while flagging a 2027 inflation path somewhat above its own forecast, possibly on oil-price assumptions [Jul 8]; ING's English note headlined rates unchanged but 2027 inflation projected higher [Jul 8]. The reading on timing is uniform: TVN24 quoted that rates stay where they are at least to year-end [Jul 8], Bankier.pl says cuts will have to wait with the breakthrough only in 2027 [Jul 8], and Business Insider Polska asked whether cuts are months away [Jul 8]. PAP Biznes reported the Council stressing global macro, commodities and CPI, with further decisions data-dependent [Jul 8]. The governor's press conference follows the next day (BIZNES24, [Jul 8]). Behind the hold sits a "wait-and-see" posture that ING has described as a sweet spot: June inflation surprised to the downside (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 8]), while the end of the CPN package has raised inflation uncertainty (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Jul 2]) and the Iran energy shock is the key upside risk. Activity is split: the manufacturing PMI fell to 46.1 in June (Forsal.pl, [Jul 1]), yet unemployment is falling again (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 8]).

Markets: The zloty hit a 19-month low ahead of the decision (Global Banking & Finance Review, [Jul 8]) and pulled back from it after rates were left unchanged (Reuters, [Jul 8]); Comparic.pl flagged the same 19-month low [Jul 8], and Bankier.pl reported EUR/PLN testing a resistance line as the Strait of Hormuz heated up again [Jul 8]. Card: USD/PLN 3.7706 (+0.2% 1W, +2.6% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.2995 (+0.1% 1W, +1.4% 4W). POLGBs turned: Bankier.pl noted Polish yields had been at a 4-month low but no longer are [Jul 8], and ING's daily blamed dearer oil for weakening bonds [Jul 8]. Equities were nervous: the WIG20 fell with KGHM sharply lower (XYZ, [Jul 8]), the index is in contact with 3,700 points (Parkiet, [Jul 8]), and StockWatch.pl said the US-Iran escalation was ravaging exchanges with KGHM, Orlen, Text and Creotech in play [Jul 8]. KGHM lost close to 7% on the session while Orlen led gains (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 8]) after Morgan Stanley downgraded the copper miner (pb.pl, [Jul 8]); the card, on Tuesday's close, already shows KGHM at 319.05 (-3.7% 1W, -4.9% 4W) and Orlen at 135.22 (+6.8% 1W, -6.4% 4W), with Comparic.pl asking whether Orlen could fall below 100 zł on Goldman Sachs's oil-oversupply warning [Jul 8]. Retail is being re-rated: Morgan Stanley says the market is wrong on Dino, Biedronka and other retailers (pb.pl, [Jul 8]), and Parkiet reported Żabka overtaking Dino in valuation and approaching LPP [Jul 8]; Dino closed 27.58 (-2.9% 1W, -8.3% 4W), LPP 18,800 (+2.8% 1W, -14.6% 4W), Allegro 39.4 (+4.2% 1W, +13.4% 4W). CD Projekt 237.5 (+5.8% 1W, +5.3% 4W) is the week's winner; PKO 104.72 (+1.5% 1W, +4.5% 4W), Pekao 231.9 (+1.4% 1W, -2.6% 4W), PGE 9.4 (-0.4% 1W, -6.3% 4W). EPOL 39.45 (+2.5% 1W, +1.4% 4W).

Fiscal & Politics: The finance ministry refused to collect a 174.5 mn zł fine from Gazprom although the means exist (pb.pl, [Jul 8]), prompting UOKiK's objection (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 8]) and wGospodarce's charge that the minister is acting in Russian interests [Jul 8]. The ministry is at war with the agriculture ministry over alcohol excise (Bankier.pl, [Jul 8]), and reported a "record June" for household savings (Interia Biznes, [Jul 8]). The Sejm's OKI law is through, with up to 100 thousand zł sheltered from the Belka tax (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 8]), though RMF24 frames it as an entirely new tax on savings [Jul 8]. Fuel policy is live again: the energy ministry set a condition for the CPN package to return (Motofakty, [Jul 8]) and Business Insider Polska says a new shield mechanism is in play as fuel prices rise [Jul 8].

Corporate & Sectors: Banks got a regulatory shock: UOKiK and police searched BIK and the largest banks, including mBank and ING, over credit-scoring practices (TVN24, [Jul 8]), checking whether customers are penalised for comparing offers (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 8]). Orlen faces a security review after Money.pl reported Russians were involved in building Baltic Power with the ABW in action [Jul 8]. KGHM's Strategy 2055+, with a new mine, foreign acquisitions and over 32 bn zł of investment (PAP MediaRoom, [Jul 8]), is now a second day of losses (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 8]). Nuclear: three countries, the US, Canada and France, are competing for the second plant and Motyka says the electricity price will decide (Bankier.pl, [Jul 8]), while GlobEnergia doubts first power by 2036 [Jul 8]. PKO BP will finance NATO armaments (CIRE.pl, [Jul 8]).

Geopolitics & Security: At the NATO summit in Ankara, Nawrocki said Russia could use its military against Central and Eastern Europe (UNITED24 Media, [Jul 8]) and met Zelensky after the bitter bilateral spat (TVP World, [Jul 8]), speaking of a common enemy and the need for dialogue (Українська правда, [Jul 8]). The New York Times reported escalating tensions between Russia and NATO's European members [Jul 8], following the US warning of a possible armed provocation on Polish territory (The Telegraph, [Jul 3]).

New / Most Important: ING's preview described the NBP as in a "sweet spot": inflation risks keep cuts off the table, but no urgency to hike either, and the decision confirmed it. The projection is the new information, and a higher 2027 path on oil assumptions means the Iran energy shock, not domestic demand, now sets the easing timeline. The market read it correctly: the zloty's 19-month low came before the decision and reversed on it, while bonds lost their 4-month yield low to oil.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold pattern continues as expected; dovish hold plus energy risk equals a capped zloty.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: None on rates; the anomaly is KGHM losing close to 7% on a downgrade in the same week Orlen leads on the oil spike, which says the market is pricing the Iran shock as an energy trade, not a Poland trade.
