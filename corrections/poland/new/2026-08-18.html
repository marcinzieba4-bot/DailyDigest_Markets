🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hawkish hold

Macro Stance: The NBP's hawkish shift is proceeding slowly, weighing on PLN per Commerzbank, and nothing in the last day changes the picture of a council that will hold while fuel policy does the work on the headline. July CPI stands at 3.0% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]) with fuel up 13.9% in a month (300Gospodarka.pl, [Aug 13]) and core at a ten-month high (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]); Super Biznes says inflation is accelerating again [Aug 17], Citi expects the fuel measures to run at least into September (PAP Biznes, [Aug 17]) and Money.pl now adds the European drought as upward pressure on inflation [Aug 18]. Q2 GDP rose 3.8% y/y (PAP Biznes, [Aug 13]). Credit demand is the surprising tell: BIK data show mortgage demand up by more than a fifth (TVN24, Bankier.pl, [Aug 18]), which is not what a central bank near easing would want to see. The end of the week brings the domestic data cluster and the Fitch decision (ING, [Aug 17]).

Markets: Parkiet calls it a deepening correction with the exchange in holiday mode [Aug 18]: the WIG20 extended its sell-off after a run of gains, with XTB trading volumes as large as KGHM's and Orlen's (Bankier.pl, [Aug 18]) after the shares fell nearly 7% as KNF kept its CFD review open (TradingView, [Aug 18]); Dino led the gainers and KGHM was the biggest loser (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 18]) and JSW slid on fears over its giant state loan (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 18]). The card has turned: EPOL 43.96 (-0.5% 1W, +8.7% 4W); the summer leaders are giving back, KGHM 345.2 (-3.8% 1W, +17.6% 4W) ahead of results FXMAG expects to be among the strongest of the season [Aug 18], Dino 31.68 (-5.6% 1W, +6.0% 4W), LPP 21,320 (-4.0% 1W), PGE 10.37 (-2.4% 1W) and Allegro 45.06 (-1.9% 1W, +2.4% 4W), while banks hold, PKO 111.42 (+1.2% 1W, +4.4% 4W) and Pekao 257.0 (+1.2% 1W, +10.5% 4W), with Orlen 152.16 (+1.4% 1W) and CD Projekt 257.8 (+0.3% 1W). FX is quiet: USD/PLN 3.7207 (-0.1% 1W, -1.9% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.3119 (+0.3% 1W, -0.5% 4W), with Bankier.pl noting low volatility, a slight dollar bounce and the euro at 4.32 [Aug 18], and BofA calling for zloty gains from Q4 on expected dollar weakness (Investing.com, [Aug 18]). POLGBs have no domestic auction headline; the pressure is imported, with the US 30-year yield at its highest since 2007 (Investing.com Polska, [Aug 18]) and Analizy.pl asking whether lower PIT means higher yields [Aug 17].

Fiscal & Politics: Tusk convened the cabinet on the state budget (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 18]) a day after the ministry reported a 141.1 billion zł deficit after July (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 17]) that pb.pl stressed did not widen despite CPN as VAT and CIT receipts improved [Aug 17]; Newsweek's verdict is that the money for KO's key promise is not there [Aug 18] and Money.pl's expert warns about the debt trajectory two years out [Aug 18]. The revenue search is visible: the government says it will end the structures used by Mentzen, Fritz and Polish billionaires (Wyborcza.biz, [Aug 18]), while the Sejm works on lifting the tax threshold to 171 thousand zł (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 17]). Tusk also announced a special act for key investments covering ports, rail, gas and the nuclear plant (Energetyka24.com, StockWatch.pl, [Aug 18]). Morawiecki's warning of a downgrade this week (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 17]) hangs over Friday's Fitch review.

Corporate & Sectors: The PKO BP fine is now fully digested: 47.5 million zł from KNF over structured deposits (StockWatch.pl, Cashless.pl, [Aug 18]) with the bank promising a response (Money.pl, [Aug 18]); BOŚ reported net profit down more than 80% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 18]). POLSTR replaces WIBOR in new loans from 2027 (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 18]) and the CHF act has been in force since Aug 7 (INFOR.PL, [Aug 18]). The Allegro-InPost conflict is escalating, with Money.pl floating a nuclear-option divorce [Aug 18] and Rzeczpospolita saying customers already see the change [Aug 18]. Orlen's shares rose with government risk still lurking (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 18]) as Wednesday's maximum fuel prices edged up (Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 18]); Energetyka24 says Poland now has the most expensive energy in Europe and industry is appealing for change [Aug 18]. Dino staff are preparing a September protest (Dziennik.pl, [Aug 18]) even as Wiadomości Handlowe projects revenue above 50 billion zł in 2028 [Aug 18]. CD Projekt unveiled the free Witcher 3 expansion with daily Gamescom streams (ITHardware, Eurogamer.pl, [Aug 18]). Parkiet flags expensive polyester as a risk for apparel [Aug 18] and a drone killed a worker at the Zaporizhzhia plant (PolsatNews.pl, [Aug 18]).

Geopolitics & Security: Poland has not yet decided on Patriot missiles for Ukraine but the US is pressing (UNN, [Aug 18]), a day after the Defence Ministry said it is preparing for war and expects a major provocation within months (UNITED24 Media, [Aug 17]) and reports placed Russian drone bases within reach of Poland (Kyiv Post, [Aug 17]); US warnings that Russia may target Poland to test NATO persist [Aug 18]. Tusk's pledge of Europe's largest army (The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 15]) remains the frame.

New / Most Important: The correction has found its shape. It is concentrated in the summer's momentum names, KGHM, Dino, LPP and PGE, plus XTB on a regulatory overhang, while banks hold and Orlen rises, and it is happening in thin holiday volumes on the eve of KGHM's results and the Fitch decision. That is positioning, not a change in fundamentals: credit demand is up a fifth, growth is near 4% and the deficit did not widen. The risk is that the budget cabinet and a hostile rating narrative turn a technical pullback into a sovereign one.  |  Playbook ✓: PLN weakness on a gradual NBP pivot is expected; stay with banks, use the pullback in KGHM only after the report, keep duration short while the US long end sets records.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: BofA sees zloty gains from Q4 while Commerzbank sees the slow NBP shift weighing on it: the two are talking about different drivers, the dollar versus the differential, and only one of them is in Warsaw's control.
