🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold, dovish pivot signaled

Macro Stance: Rates stay at 3.75% after last week's hold (PAP Biznes, [Jul 8]), and the market is still digesting Governor Glapiński's offer to file a cut motion after the summer (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 9]) against a projection that lifts 2027 inflation and lowers growth (Analizy.pl, [Jul 10]). Monday's data added a softer growth note: GUS industrial figures show Polish industry "standing still", with declines outnumbering gains across branches even as a few sectors post records (Forsal.pl, wnp.pl, Business Insider Polska, [Jul 13]). On prices, shelf inflation decelerated for another month in a row (Agro Profil, [Jul 13]), consistent with inflation sitting inside the NBP target (XYZ, [Jul 9]), while the NBP's own report warns of a new supply shock working through to shops (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Jul 11]). The Council's split is the constraint: two members told Reuters there is no rush to cut after summer ([Jul 10]) and Erste sees no move before end-2027 (Direct Money, [Jul 10]). PKO BP still expects 3.5% GDP growth this year (wnp.pl, [Jul 11]). Transmission is already loosening without a cut: banks are "massively" cutting mortgage rates and offers starting with a five are back (Bankier.pl, [Jul 13]).

Markets: Monday extended the record run but with a different engine. Orlen, lifted by rising oil, pulled WIG20 to new highs "in a difficult environment" and energy names drove the session (Bankier.pl, Inwestycje.pl, XYZ, [Jul 13]), while copper fell on the Hormuz conflict and KGHM came under pressure again (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 13]). Friday's card had PKO 112.88 (+7.3% 1W, +7.9% 4W) and Pekao 244.3 (+4.8% / +7.1%) after Goldman's upgrade (pb.pl, [Jul 10]), Orlen 139.92 (+5.3% / -4.7%), KGHM 308.95 (-7.8% / -14.4%), PGE 9.37 (-2.6% / -6.2%), Allegro 39.83 (+3.6% / +15.7%), Dino 28.22, CD Projekt 233.0, LPP 19380, and EPOL 40.37 (+2.4% / +3.8%). The zloty had a worse morning than the index: the dollar is the most expensive in 14 months and the zloty is "under pressure from the war over the Strait of Hormuz" (Bankier.pl, [Jul 13]), with USD/PLN at 3.7944 (+1.2% 1W, +3.8% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3271 (+0.9% / +2.0%) on the card. Business Insider reports the zloty broke its losing streak, with economists attaching one condition ([Jul 13]), and ING's EMEA FX note argues zloty weakness "looks temporary" ([Jul 13]); CEO Magazyn warns it may be a difficult week as the dollar waits for a US impulse ([Jul 13]). POLGB yields had eased on the dovish signals per PKO BP (wnp.pl, [Jul 10]).

Fiscal & Politics: Brussels opened an excessive-deficit procedure against Bulgaria over a record deficit (Business Insider Polska, Money.pl, [Jul 13]), a warning shot for a Polish finance minister who already calls the deficit the main obstacle to tax cuts before an election-year budget (Money.pl, [Jul 9]). The PIT threshold fight continues, with pb.pl expecting only modest gains for scale taxpayers ([Jul 13]). Germany plans a large rise in army spending, pulling well ahead of Poland (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 13]). A coalition quarrel surfaced around Petru (wPolityce.pl, [Jul 13]). On the plus side, Warsaw will host a new ESA security centre and BGK is launching a space investment fund (SpaceNews, Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 13]).

Corporate & Sectors: Allegro grew second-quarter EBITDA by 11.5% with record growth abroad (XYZ, pb.pl, [Jul 13]). Orlen's Grudziądz CCGT started feeding the grid, days after Baltic Power's first offshore power (WysokieNapiecie.pl, [Jul 13]; PAP Biznes, [Jul 10]); the company appointed Diana Batko to the board (Bankier.pl, [Jul 13]) and Bogdanka extended its coal contract with Orlen Termika to 2028 (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 13]). KGHM secured a 64 million zloty NFOŚiGW loan for 19.4 MW of solar (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 13]) after a weekend of mine tremors that ended with all miners safe (Portal Samorządowy, [Jul 11]) and a 32 billion zloty investment plan to 2030 (pb.pl, [Jul 9]). The energy ministry talks of 1 trillion zloty for the transition and sees scope for lower power prices in 2027 (wnp.pl, XYZ, [Jul 13]), Tauron detailed a 7 billion zloty project and signed on Rożnów II (Portal Samorządowy, TVP Kraków, [Jul 13]), and negative power prices are setting records (WysokieNapiecie.pl, [Jul 13]). Dino shares fell amid a dispute with employees and the GIS poppy-seed recall (wPolityce.pl, Gov.pl, [Jul 13]). Banks: PKO BP is launching settlements on euro-denominated loans (Franknews.pl, [Jul 13]) on top of 297 million zloty of Q2 CHF provisions (Bankier.pl, [Jul 10]).

Geopolitics & Security: Sikorski told Poles there is "no panic": Russia lacks the capacity to attack Poland but is preparing something (Kyiv Post, Ukrainska Pravda, [Jul 13]), as France summoned Russia's ambassador over sabotage in a dozen European countries (CBS News, [Jul 13]). US Army troop rotations to Poland are back on, for now (The National Interest, [Jul 9]). The Ukraine file is deteriorating at home: a Rzeczpospolita poll finds more Poles against than for sending weapons ([Jul 12]), and Poland says Ukraine could start producing missiles within weeks (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Jul 9]).

New / Most Important: The oil-driven leadership rotation is the day's signal. A record index led by Orlen on Hormuz-linked crude, while copper and the zloty fall on the same conflict, means Warsaw is now trading as an energy-shock market rather than a rate-cut market. That is less comfortable than last week's bank-led rally: the dovish pivot plus an oil shock is precisely the combination the July projection flagged as lifting 2027 inflation, and it makes the "temporary" zloty weakness thesis dependent on the Gulf rather than the NBP.

Playbook ✓: Dovish shift plus stronger dollar equals weaker zloty, as expected; Orlen bid on higher crude is standard.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Cutting rates while projecting higher inflation is a credibility risk, and an oil shock imported through a weaker zloty would compound it, creating the self-reinforcing loop of currency weakness and inflation that the Council's hawks fear. Industry stalling while the index sets records is a second gap that earnings season will have to close.
