🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hawkish hold

Macro Stance: Inflation has reaccelerated and the RPP is holding, with Commerzbank warning that the slow hawkish shift weighs on the zloty. The data flow of the last two days hardened that stance: GUS reported enterprise wages up 6.8% y/y in July with employment down 0.8% (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 20]), and industrial output up 5.1% y/y (PAP Biznes, [Aug 20]) with construction lagging (xyz.pl, [Aug 21]). Parkiet called the wage beat a cold shower for the RPP [Aug 20], although PKO BP's economists argue labour data will not drive the council's decisions (wnp.pl, [Aug 20]). The price side offers no relief: core inflation is at a ten-month high (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]), German producer prices point to higher inflation ahead (Parkiet, [Aug 21]) and Strefa Biznesu notes both the ECB and the RPP hold with further moves tied to price pressure [Aug 19]. ING flagged today's August PMI and expected Fitch to leave the rating unchanged [Aug 21].

Markets: The week closed with a relief rally built on results. Dino was the star of the session (Parkiet, [Aug 21]), up 5.5% with the WIG20 gaining 1% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]), breaking out of its downtrend (Comparic.pl, [Aug 21]); Tauron was weakest (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 21]), and Orlen rose with oil after Trump's threats as PZU moved on results (FXMAG, [Aug 21]). Thursday had been the mirror image, with KGHM leading the declines (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 20]). The card, struck before today's bounce, shows the reset: EPOL 44.17 (+0.5% 1W, +8.3% 4W); banks lead, PKO 113.7 (+3.9% 1W, +6.0% 4W) and Pekao 259.6 (+1.8% 1W, +11.5% 4W), Orlen 156.0 (+3.3% 1W) recovered, while the energy-CIT losers sit at the bottom, PGE 10.2 (-6.8% 1W, -3.8% 4W) and KGHM 332.45 (-6.6% 1W, +10.1% 4W), with LPP 20,700 (-4.3% 1W), Allegro 44.62 (-3.9% 1W) and Dino 32.29 (-1.3% 1W) before today's jump. The zloty is the strong leg for external reasons: the dollar is the cheapest in months after Washington's move (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]) and USD/PLN is testing 3.69 with the euro below 4.32 (Comparic.pl, [Aug 21]); the card has USD/PLN 3.6918 (-1.2% 1W, -3.0% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3146 (+0.1% 1W, -0.4% 4W). On POLGBs, Hungarian bonds now yield less than Polish ones (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Aug 20]) and Business Insider Polska argues AI firms competing for capital are lifting Poland's debt-service cost [Aug 20].

Fiscal & Politics: Two external verdicts arrived on the day the tax bill was published. Fitch affirmed Poland's rating (Gov.pl, Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 21]) and S&P Dow Jones Indices upgraded Poland to developed-market status (Reuters, [Aug 21]), which Domański welcomed (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 21]); Rzeczpospolita adds that S&P's reaction to the PIT and CIT changes lets the government breathe [Aug 21]. The draft itself is bigger than the headline PIT relief: Portal Samorządowy counts over 50 billion zł for public finances [Aug 21], Gazeta Prawna reports a rate rising from 19 to 22% among further changes from 2027 [Aug 21], the ministry confirmed a new tax to raise 4.8 billion zł (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 21]) that mirrors the 4.8 billion zł of power-price relief for energy-intensive industry (Gov.pl, [Aug 21]), and Rzeczpospolita estimates Orlen, PGE, Enea and Tauron will add close to 7 billion zł to the budget [Aug 21]; ING Think calls the package fiscally neutral [Aug 20]. Tusk called an extra cabinet sitting on taxes (Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 21]) after the budget meeting (PAP, [Aug 21]) and Gazeta Prawna still sees a possible Nawrocki veto [Aug 21]. The deficit exceeded 141 billion zł after seven months (Bankier.pl, [Aug 17]) and defence spending is to reach 5% of GDP by 2027 (TodayPress.tv, [Aug 21]).

Corporate & Sectors: Dino's Q2 brought 400 million zł of net profit and 3176 stores (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 21]), revenue of 18 billion zł with 148 openings and capex near 1 billion zł (Omnichannel News, [Aug 21]) and guidance for a gradual LFL recovery in the second half (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]); pb.pl's verdict is that the absence of disappointment sufficed [Aug 21]. KGHM's half-year EBITDA rose 89% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 21]) on 5579 million zł of net profit (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 20]); the CEO set out expansion into the US and Africa with a possible listing (wnp.pl, [Aug 21]) and it is borrowing 1 billion zł for Sierra Gorda (Polski Przemysł, [Aug 21]). The 30% energy CIT is now a dividend question for Orlen and Unimot (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 21]) and Parkiet sees pressure on utility results [Aug 21]; PGE and Ørsted finished all 111 Baltica 2 monopiles on schedule (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 21]). PKO BP won KNF approval for portfolio management and set a new target (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]) while Franknews.pl warns of over 68 thousand repaid CHF contracts open to challenge [Aug 21]. CD Projekt rose ahead of its Gamescom showcase (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 21]); XTB won Indonesian CFD approval (TradingView, [Aug 21]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tarcza Wschód has reached initial operational readiness in many places after two years (Strefa Obrony, [Aug 21]) and the MON says the border can be mined within 48 hours (Interia Wydarzenia, [Aug 21]). Thursday's mass strike on Ukraine saw Polish jets scrambled and RCB alerts in two voivodeships (Radio ESKA, [Aug 20]); Romania says Russia's strikes increasingly spill into NATO (ABC News, [Aug 21]) and the US deployed a surveillance aircraft to Poland (Firstpost, [Aug 21]).

New / Most Important: Reclassification to developed-market status and a Fitch affirmation on the same day the government publishes a 50 billion zł tax package is the best possible sequencing for the sovereign and the worst for the utilities. The agencies have judged the package neutral, which anchors POLGBs and the zloty, but the money is raised from the sectors the WIG20 leans on, so the index is being re-weighted from Orlen, PGE and KGHM toward banks and domestic retail. Inflation reaccelerating removes any near-term easing optionality, and 6.8% wages confirm it.  |  Playbook ✓: Sticky CEE inflation is consensus; stay long banks and Dino, underweight the energy-CIT payers, keep duration short and treat the zloty bid as a dollar story.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Developed-market status arrives with a president threatening to veto the budget's funding mechanism: the index providers are upgrading the market while the politics that fund it are unresolved.
