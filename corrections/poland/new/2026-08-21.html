🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hawkish hold

Macro Stance: Inflation has reaccelerated and the RPP is holding: GUS confirmed July CPI at 3.0% with fuel up 13.9% in a month (Bankier.pl, 300Gospodarka.pl, [Aug 13]), core inflation rose to a ten-month high (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]), and Strefa Biznesu notes both the ECB and the RPP are holding with further decisions tied to price pressure [Aug 19]. The real economy is not helping the doves. Yesterday's GUS prints showed enterprise wages up 6.8% y/y with employment down 0.8% (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 20]) and industrial output up 5.1% y/y (PAP Biznes, [Aug 20]); Credit Agricole and mBank economists dissected the batch today (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Aug 21]), wGospodarce asked whether the wage jump was a seasonal spike [Aug 20], Direct Money cautioned that the 9500 zł average is not a typical pay packet [Aug 21], and xyz.pl flagged construction lagging the rest of the economy [Aug 21]. Parkiet added that German producer prices point to higher inflation ahead [Aug 21]. PAP's analysts argue the renewed fuel intervention does not materially change the CPI or rate outlook [Aug 14]. Today's August PMI is the next data point (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Aug 21]). Commerzbank's warning that the NBP's slow hawkish shift weighs on the zloty still frames the currency.

Markets: The week ended with gains: Parkiet called Dino the star of the session [Aug 21], Bankier.pl had Dino up 5.5% and the WIG20 up 1% [Aug 21], Investing.com cited EPS above forecasts with the stock up 5.8% [Aug 21], and Tauron was the weakest name (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 21]). Funds cut shorts on JSW and Pepco, and Dino and KGHM saw nearly 1.5 billion zł of turnover (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]); FXMAG noted PZU moving on results and Orlen rising with oil after Trump's threats [Aug 21]. The card, which predates today's bounce, shows the damage of the correction: EPOL 44.17 (+0.5% 1W, +8.3% 4W); PKO 113.7 (+3.9% 1W) and Pekao 259.6 (+11.5% 4W) the leaders; KGHM 332.45 (-6.6% 1W) and PGE 10.2 (-6.8% 1W, -3.8% 4W) the laggards after the energy-CIT news; LPP 20,700 (-4.3% 1W), Allegro 44.62 (-3.9% 1W), CD Projekt 255.4 (-2.2% 1W, +12.9% 4W), Orlen 156.0 (+3.3% 1W). The zloty is the strong leg: Bankier.pl reported the dollar at its cheapest in months on Washington's move [Aug 21], CEO Magazyn had USD/PLN down to 3.68 [Aug 21] and Comparic.pl saw it testing 3.69 with the euro below 4.32 [Aug 21]; the card has USD/PLN at 3.6918 (-1.2% 1W, -3.0% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3146 (+0.1% 1W, -0.4% 4W). POLGBs had no fresh auction headline; the debate is imported, with pb.pl quoting JPMorgan likening the US Treasury's bond intervention to paying a mortgage with a credit card [Aug 21] and XTB asking whether PMIs will pull attention away from bonds [Aug 21].

Fiscal & Politics: Two external verdicts landed today. Fitch affirmed Poland's rating (Ministerstwo Finansów, Gov.pl, [Aug 21]), as ING had expected [Aug 21], and S&P Dow Jones Indices upgraded Poland to developed-market status (Reuters, [Aug 21]), which Domański welcomed (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 21]); Rzeczpospolita added that S&P's reaction to the PIT and CIT changes lets the government breathe [Aug 21]. The government published the tax bill: Portal Samorządowy counts over 50 billion zł for public finances [Aug 21], Gazeta Prawna reports a rate rising from 19 to 22% among further changes from 2027 [Aug 21], and the ministry confirmed a new tax to raise 4.8 billion zł (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 21]) that funds 4.8 billion zł of power-price relief for energy-intensive industry (Gov.pl, [Aug 21]); Rzeczpospolita estimates Orlen, PGE, Enea and Tauron will add close to 7 billion zł to the budget [Aug 21]. Tusk called an extra cabinet sitting (Polskie Radio 24, [Aug 21]) after a budget meeting (PAP, [Aug 21]), and Gazeta Prawna still sees a possible Nawrocki veto [Aug 21]. Defence spending is to reach 5% of GDP by 2027 (TodayPress.tv, [Aug 21]). The deficit stood at 141.1 billion zł after July (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 17]).

Corporate & Sectors: Dino's Q2 showed 400 million zł of net profit and 3176 stores (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 21]), revenue of 18 billion zł with 148 new stores and capex near 1 billion zł (Omnichannel News, [Aug 21]), 58 thousand staff (Portal spożywczy, [Aug 21]) and guidance for a slow LFL recovery in H2 (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 21]); pb.pl's verdict was that the absence of disappointment was enough [Aug 21]. KGHM's EBITDA rose 89% y/y (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 21]), the CEO set out expansion into the US and Africa with a possible listing (wnp.pl, [Aug 21]), and a mining major may partner it in Chile (wnp.pl, [Aug 21]). The energy CIT is now a dividend question for Orlen (StockWatch.pl, [Aug 21]) and Parkiet sees pressure on utility results [Aug 21]; PGE and Ørsted installed all 111 Baltica 2 monopiles on schedule (Inwestycje.pl, [Aug 21]). PKO BP has KNF approval for portfolio management and a new target (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]), while Franknews.pl warns of over 68 thousand repaid CHF contracts open to challenge [Aug 21]; the finance ministry is analysing a KNF and bank proposal on mortgage pricing (Bankier.pl, [Aug 21]). CD Projekt rose ahead of its Gamescom showcase (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 21]) and dated Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 (CD PROJEKT RED IR, [Aug 21]). XTB won Indonesian CFD approval (TradingView, [Aug 21]).

Geopolitics & Security: After two years the Tarcza Wschód barrier has reached initial operational readiness in many places (Strefa Obrony, [Aug 21]) and the MON says the border can be mined within 48 hours (Interia Wydarzenia, [Aug 21]). Yesterday's mass strike on Ukraine had Polish jets scrambled and RCB alerts in two voivodeships (Radio ESKA, [Aug 20]); Romania says Russia's strikes increasingly spill into NATO (ABC News, [Aug 21]), NATO ran unannounced large-scale exercises near Kaliningrad (UNN, [Aug 21]) and the US deployed a surveillance aircraft to Poland (Firstpost, [Aug 21]). Military Watch argues air-defence donations to Ukraine leave Polish airspace exposed [Aug 21]. Poland has sheltered 1.73 million Ukrainians since the full-scale invasion (UNN, [Aug 21]).

New / Most Important: The rating affirmation and the developed-market reclassification arrive on the day the tax bill is published, and that sequencing matters: the agencies are pricing the package as fiscally neutral, so the sovereign story is stable even with the deficit at 141.1 billion zł. The cost sits in equities, where the 30% energy CIT is now an explicit earnings and dividend hit for Orlen, PGE and Tauron while consumer names like Dino get the PIT tailwind. The hawkish hold is unchanged; the sector mix has moved.  |  Playbook ✓: Sticky CEE inflation and a patient NBP are consensus; favour banks and domestic retail over utilities, keep duration light, and treat the zloty bid as a dollar story.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: KGHM at -6.6% on the week after an 89% EBITDA jump, versus Dino rallying on in-line numbers: the market is paying for predictability, not growth.
