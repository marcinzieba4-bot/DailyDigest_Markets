🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↑ hike risk

Macro Stance: The RPP debate has flipped from when-to-cut to whether-to-hike. GUS confirmed August CPI at 3.4% y/y (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 15]), the highest in 14 months (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 15]), with transport up 11.2% while food fell for a fourth month at the fastest pace in a decade (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 15]); PAP Biznes warned fuel-driven CPI raises second-round risk [Sep 15], and core inflation rose again on the NBP reading (Forsal.pl, [Sep 16]). An MPC member told Bloomberg a rate increase is possible on energy risks [Sep 18], Zarzecki maintains his hike call (FXMAG, [Sep 15]) and Glapiński told borrowers circumstances have changed (Bizblog.pl, [Sep 14]). Meanwhile August industrial output rose 4.3% y/y but missed forecasts (PAP Biznes, [Sep 18]) and ING flagged the softer industry as a risk to GDP [Sep 18]. Today's labour print disappointed: wages rose 5.6% while employment fell 0.8% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 21]), average pay was 9259.54 zł (Money.pl, [Sep 21]) and inflation is starting to eat into pay (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 21]). Construction is the bright spot, up 6.7% on infrastructure (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 21]).

Markets: Warsaw shrugged off the downgrade and printed new highs today: Business Insider Polska said the penultimate day of summer brought records on the GPW [Sep 21], Parkiet logged new maxima in the WIG20 uptrend [Sep 21] and Bankier.pl credited the banks, with Orlen ending its losing streak [Sep 21]; mBank led (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 21]). That followed a triple-witching Friday with almost 6 billion zł of turnover (FXMAG, [Sep 19]) and a mid-week dip below 4100 points under PZU's record dividend (Parkiet, [Sep 16]). The card shows the rotation: LPP at 24160 is up 12.1% on the week and 16.2% over four weeks, Allegro at 49.8 gained 10.8% on the week, while Orlen (151.14, -6.2% 1W) and KGHM (337.05, -4.2% 1W) lagged; PGE at 13.93 still carries a +36.7% four-week run and EPOL at 44.41 is down 2.5% on the week. The zloty is the weak leg: USD/PLN closed at 3.7979 (+1.9% 1W, +3.0% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3581 (+0.9% 1W, +1.2% 4W) after the Fed hiked and the dollar hit its dearest since July (Comparic.pl, [Sep 17]). POLGBs swung: the 10-year yield broke 6.5%, the highest since 2007 (Bankier.pl, [Sep 15]), then fell after the Fed in what Business Insider Polska called the best result in the world [Sep 17], and Polish bonds shrugged off the rating action (marketscreener, [Sep 21]).

Fiscal & Politics: Moody's cut Poland's rating for the first time since 2016 (Bloomberg, [Sep 18]), to A3, the lowest since 2002 (Notes From Poland, [Sep 19]), citing the high deficit, fast-growing debt and no fiscal consolidation (ING, [Sep 21]); Bloomberg called it a pre-election blow to Tusk [Sep 21]. Behind it: the state deficit reached 151.1 billion zł after August (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 16]), the budget will breach the prudential threshold (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 16]) and debt is nearing the 55% of GDP limit (TV Republika, [Sep 20]). The Fiscal Council's first opinion called the situation very serious (Money.pl, [Sep 14]). The response is revenue-side: the Sejm passed the fuel windfall tax a second time (PAP, [Sep 18]), the government counting on an extra 4 billion zł (XYZ, [Sep 18]) for a new CPN price variant if the president signs, while Nawrocki filed his own fuel bill (Portal Samorządowy, [Sep 17]). An alcohol excise rise is being prepared (Money.pl, [Sep 21]) and a savings tax starts in 2027 (INFOR.PL, [Sep 16]).

Corporate & Sectors: Retail earnings carried the week. LPP posted 768 million zł of net profit and 1744 million zł of EBITDA (BIZNES24, [Sep 17]), sales up 20% y/y so far this quarter, a 56.5-57% gross-margin guide (PAP Biznes, [Sep 17]) and record-high shares (wnp.pl, [Sep 18]). Allegro raised its 2026 outlook and hit a five-year high (Reuters, [Sep 17]), then lifted its buyback cap from 45 to 50 zł (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 21]) and tied a 6 billion zł facility to ESG targets. The FedEx and Advent consortium secured 89% of InPost in a €7.8 billion buyout (Reuters, [Sep 18]). Banks got a Citi upgrade (pb.pl, [Sep 21]) but face TSUE-driven interest refunds and rising WIBOR suits; NBP asked for a lifeline in the WIBOR-to-POLSTR switch (Bankier.pl, [Sep 17]). Orlen signed LNG and fuel memoranda with Naftogaz worth billions of zloty (TVN24, [Sep 21]) and sourced 16 new crude cargoes after the Saudi pipeline attack (Green-news.pl, [Sep 21]), but talk of involving it in PGZ hit the shares (Parkiet, [Sep 21]) and Czechia is taxing refineries including Orlen's (pb.pl, [Sep 21]). CD Projekt's record half-year came from licences, not games (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 18]). PKO BP expects house prices up 5% (Bankier.pl, [Sep 21]) while gas and CO2 push heat tariffs higher (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 18]).

Geopolitics & Security: A Russian drone hit a passenger train on the Ukrainian side of the border, narrowly missing a diplomatic train (Reuters, [Sep 14]), NATO jets downed a drone over Lithuania [Sep 15], and Tusk warned Russia may stage accidental strikes on NATO's eastern flank (TVP World, [Sep 17]). Poland scrambled jets four times in a week (The Sun, [Sep 19]), Lublin and Rzeszów airports were briefly closed [Sep 17], and the army is fortifying Medyka, Korczowa and Dorohusk with HESCO positions (Nowiny 24, [Sep 17]). Trump cited progress toward a US Army base in Poland (CBS News, [Sep 18]) and GUS put the war's cost to Poland at 490 billion zł of GDP (o2, [Sep 18]).

New / Most Important: The Moody's downgrade defines the week, not because the zloty or POLGBs panicked but because it formalises what 6.5% yields already priced: a 151 billion zł deficit with no consolidation plan into an election. Add an RPP debating hikes on 3.4% CPI and a labour market where employment is shrinking, and Poland has moved from a benign disinflation story to a stagflation-with-fiscal-overhang story. Equities ignore it because the earnings come from LPP, Allegro and the banks.  |  Playbook ✓: Stay with the retail and bank leadership that printed today's records; keep duration short while hike talk and supply fears coexist; treat zloty weakness near 3.80 as a Fed story first.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Record WIG20 against a first-ever rating cut and falling employment; Orlen down 6.2% on the week with oil above 108 dollars, as windfall tax and PGZ talk override the margin tailwind.
