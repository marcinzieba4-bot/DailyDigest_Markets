🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↓ easing expectations building

Macro Stance: The GUS flash put June CPI at 2.5% y/y, back at the NBP target and below forecasts (Parkiet, [Jun 30]); the lowest since February (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 30]), with GUS crediting fruit, vegetables and cheap imports (Bankier.pl, [Jul 2]). The market withdrew its bet on more expensive credit (Bankier.pl, [Jun 30]), Commerzbank noted that rate hike bets have vanished, and ING sees investors starting to play for cuts in the second half of 2026 [Jun 30]. The caveat is fuel: with the CPN package expired, Gazeta Prawna warned the withdrawal could lift inflation back toward 3% [Jun 30], and Orlen has already raised wholesale prices (wGospodarce, [Jul 3]). The RPP meets in July with rates most likely unchanged (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 2]); the finance minister said low inflation gives no grounds for hikes (Money.pl, [Jul 2]). The manufacturing PMI slumped to 46.1 in June against a 49.4 consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 1]). GUS put the January median gross wage at 7,447.16 zł (Prawo.pl, [Jul 2]).

Markets: The WIG20 closed the quarter up 7.38%, its third consecutive positive quarter (FXMAG, [Jul 1]), and kept going: Żabka, XTB and GPW itself hit all-time highs (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 2]) and on Jul 3 the index rose 1.57% with 19 of 20 constituents higher (FXMAG, [Jul 3]). Orlen led, jumping 4.78% after a record net profit (Investing.com Polska, [Jul 3]); the card has it at 132.76, +5.4% 1W but -9.0% 4W. The casualty is Dino, at its lowest since 2022 after Barclays cut its target on slower expansion (pb.pl, [Jul 3]): 28.69, -2.1% 1W, -4.4% 4W. CD Projekt (232.5, +4.1% 1W) rallied as Cyberpunk 2077 passed 40 mn copies sold (PAP Biznes, [Jul 3]); Allegro (38.73, +3.1% 1W, +9.6% 4W) is underpinned by a buyback of up to 42 mn shares at a maximum 45 zł (StockWatch.pl, [Jun 26]). Banks are steady: PKO 104.82 (+0.6% 1W, +4.4% 4W), Pekao 233.3 (+1.4% 1W). KGHM 332.05 (-10.3% 4W), PGE 9.54 (-9.0% 4W) and LPP 18,540 (-14.6% 4W) are the four-week laggards; EPOL closed 39.44, +2.3% 1W, -1.3% 4W. The zloty did not enjoy the print: EUR/PLN tested 4.30 (wnp.pl, [Jul 1]) with the pound at 5 zł (Bankier.pl, [Jul 1]), before weak US payrolls sank the dollar and lifted the zloty (Comparic.pl, [Jul 3]). Card: USD/PLN 3.7522 (-0.4% 1W, +2.9% 4W), EUR/PLN 4.2859 (+0.2% 1W, +1.2% 4W). On POLGBs, investors returned to Polish debt and bought bonds worth billions in May (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 3]).

Fiscal & Politics: The Sejm passed the OKI personal investment account, allowing up to 100 thousand zł to be invested free of the Belka tax (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 3]), with a catch in the design (Polskie Radio 24, [Jul 3]). The fuel shield is gone: the CPN package ended with pump prices up around 60 groszy per litre (wPolityce.pl, [Jul 1]), and a windfall tax on fuel companies is to recover the 4 bn zł cost (Biznes Wprost, [Jul 2]) after the Senate inserted a safety valve into the 74% levy (Wyborcza.biz, [Jun 26]). Debt optics are deteriorating: wPolityce.pl calculated borrowing at 1.5 bn zł a day in May 2026 against 0.9 bn zł in 2025 [Jun 26], the EU convergence report ranked Poland's public finances worst in the bloc (wPolityce.pl, [Jun 30]), and FXMAG asked whether EU funds could be cut off [Jun 29]. The finance minister said there is "no chemistry" with the president (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 2]), and the Sejm passed the fast-track law rescuing the nuclear schedule (Bankier.pl, [Jul 3]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks got the week's best news: the TSUE ruling of Jul 2 on void CHF contracts preserved lenders' right to recover disbursed capital, and Rzeczpospolita described champagne in the banks [Jul 2]. Against that, ZBP forecast the free-credit sanction could cut sector profit by close to 23% (Bankier.pl, [Jun 26]). Orlen raised 750 mn euro in bonds with demand more than twice the offer (pb.pl, [Jul 3]) and holds 95% of Energa with a squeeze-out on the table (Money.pl, [Jul 2]). KGHM unveiled Strategy 2055+: over 32 bn zł of capex to 2030 and 12 bn zł of annual EBITDA (pb.pl, [Jul 3]), plus Poland's first polyhalite mine near Puck on a 300 mn tonne deposit (Money.pl, [Jul 3]). Dino opened 148 stores in the first half to reach 3,176 (BIZNES24, [Jul 2]) but faces OPZZ strike threats and UOKiK (Biznes Wprost, [Jul 3]). Mortgage lending set records (Rzeczpospolita, [Jun 30]), home prices are rising faster than inflation (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 2]) and June vehicle registrations hit a 21st-century high (Reuters, [Jul 3]).

Geopolitics & Security: The US warned Warsaw that Russia is discussing an armed provocation or border incursion on Polish territory to test NATO (The Telegraph, [Jul 3]), and Tusk said the coming months may be "critical" (BBC, [Jul 3]). This followed the intelligence chief's line that Poland must operate as if war were imminent (TVP World, [Jun 29]). Tusk drew a limit on Ukraine aid ahead of the NATO summit, citing duties on the EU's eastern border (Forsal.pl, [Jul 3]), and Poland cancelled MiG-29 deliveries (The Times of India, [Jul 1]). Saab won a $4.3 bn Polish submarine order (Investing.com, [Jun 29]).

New / Most Important: The 2.5% flash has flipped the Polish rates narrative from hike to cut within a single data cycle, and the zloty's reaction tells you how: EUR/PLN pushed to 4.30 on the print, so lower expected real rates, not stronger fundamentals, are steering the currency. The equity record is narrow, with Orlen and the banks carrying the WIG20 while Dino is de-rated, and a PMI of 46.1 says the real economy is not confirming the euphoria. Layered on top is a US warning of a Russian provocation on Polish soil, a security premium the market has so far refused to price.  |  Playbook ✓: CEE disinflation tracking eurozone trends is on-script; keep bank exposure for the CHF ruling tailwind and stay selective in retail.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The NBP's turn from potential hiker to potential cutter is fast but not unprecedented for the region; the anomaly is GPW at all-time highs on the day the US warned of a Russian armed test of NATO on Polish territory.
