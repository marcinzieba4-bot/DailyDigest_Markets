🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, cuts delayed

Macro Stance: The day after the inflation surprise, the NBP governor cooled any remaining hopes on rates, saying Poland is entering a period of further structural changes (Interia Biznes, [Sep 1]). The backdrop is yesterday's GUS flash: August CPI at 3.4% y/y and 0.4% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Aug 31]) versus a 3.1% consensus (BIZNES24, [Aug 31]), driven by fuel up 24.2% y/y (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]), with mBank now expecting inflation to slightly exceed 4% at year-end (netTG.pl, [Aug 31]). Parkiet's read is that the economy is resilient to the oil shock but inflation is not [Sep 1]. Growth remains the counterweight: Q2 GDP was revised up to 3.9% y/y and 1.0% q/q (PAP Biznes, [Aug 31]) with investment up 8.4% (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]) and TVP World calling it an investment surge [Sep 1]; Eurostat put July unemployment at 3.4% (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 1]). The new soft spot is industry: the latest manufacturing PMI fell below the expansion line (Parkiet, [Sep 1]) and Business Insider Polska describes dark clouds and mounting pressure over manufacturing [Sep 1]. Import pass-through (BNY) and divergence against the ECB (ABN AMRO) remain the two-sided squeeze on the zloty, and the euro-area print of 3.3% inflation (Farmer.pl, [Sep 1]) keeps the ECB side of that equation hawkish.

Markets: Orlen's records were not enough: the WIG20 closed down 0.47% on Tuesday (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 1]) in what Parkiet called a sharp fall [Sep 1], with Grupa Kęty and KGHM the biggest losers (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 1]), KGHM off more than 3% (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 1]) and a correction across state-controlled names (pb.pl, [Sep 1]). Orlen was the exception, up 3.53% (FXMAG, [Sep 1]) and through 160 zł to an all-time high (CrypS.pl, [Sep 1]) as the end of the fuel-price cap lifted pump prices and StockWatch.pl credited geopolitics with lending downstream a hand [Sep 1]. The card, which predates today, already had Orlen at 154.22 (+3.5% 4W), PGE at 11.1 (+8.7% 1W, +6.3% 4W), Dino at 35.98 (+2.7% 1W, +10.8% 4W), Pekao at 259.1 (+5.2% 4W) and PKO BP at 113.96; the laggards are CD Projekt at 234.4 (-8.9% 1W, -7.6% 4W), with FXMAG noting investors rotating from CD Projekt toward KGHM on good long-term forecasts [Sep 1], and EPOL at 44.1 (-0.9% 1W, +2.7% 4W). The zloty is still losing (Comparic.pl, [Sep 1]) with the euro around 4.33 zł (FXMAG, [Sep 1]) and the dollar gaining again (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Sep 1]); the card has USD/PLN at 3.7233 (+1.0% 1W, -0.6% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3273 (+0.5% 1W, +0.4% 4W). POLGBs remain under pressure: the bond sell-off continues and the government is not helping (pb.pl, [Sep 1]), after 10-year yields moved back above 6% (Interia Biznes, [Aug 31]).

Fiscal & Politics: The debt arithmetic took centre stage. Rzeczpospolita reports Poland will borrow less next year but debt will still jump and servicing costs will exceed 100 bn zł [Sep 1]; Parkiet confirms the government is trimming borrowing needs somewhat [Sep 1]. Bankier.pl frames the 7% deficit as a threat of a rating downgrade, with Poland running on a credit of trust [Sep 1], and pb.pl says public debt is balancing on a dangerous line [Sep 1]. The draft itself carries a 282.6 bn zł state deficit, 7.1% of GDP general government and 3% growth in 2027 (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]). Politics turned hotter: Domański attacked President Nawrocki over the end of the CPN programme (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 1]) and said there is no money in the budget to bring government fuel prices back (Money.pl, [Sep 1]), while the ministry says it will find funds for a permanent US military base (Bankier.pl, [Sep 1]). At the G20 Domański pitched growth, deregulation and investment (pb.pl, [Sep 1]).

Corporate & Sectors: The structural story is in banks: PKO BP becomes the first lender to replace WIBOR with POLSTR in new mortgages from Thursday (Bankier.pl, [Sep 1]), with the rest of the sector given until year-end (pb.pl, [Sep 1]) and Rzeczpospolita warning it does not guarantee cheaper credit [Sep 1]; Simply Wall Street flags Polish banks facing sticky rates with PKO in focus [Sep 1]. PKO BP is also selling covered bonds again (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Sep 1]). PGE surprised the market with net profit above analysts' forecasts (Bankier.pl, [Sep 1]) but, with Polenergia, faces a new government bill on balancing costs (Strefa Inwestorów, [Sep 1]). Orlen froze wholesale prices diplomatically while ARA diesel is the dearest since April (Agro Profil, [Sep 1]) and fuel prices jumped after the cap ended (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 1]). Dino enters ministry-brokered mediation over working conditions on Wednesday (Business Insider Polska, [Sep 1]) even as Investing.com asks whether its correction is over [Sep 1]. CD Projekt reports results with analysts keeping their distance (FXMAG, [Sep 1]). KNF upheld a 20 mln zloty fine on XTB (TradingView, [Sep 1]).

Geopolitics & Security: Putin staged a missile show of force near the NATO border (Ynetnews, [Sep 1]) and the Atlantic Council mapped where he could expand the war beyond Ukraine [Sep 1], days after Tusk said the next seven to eight months are critical (Українська правда, [Aug 29]). Domański said Poland does not trust Russia after its finance minister's US visit (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 1]); Ukraine eased tensions with Poland over wartime exhumations (Financial Times, [Sep 1]).

New / Most Important: The combination of a contractionary PMI, a 3.4% CPI and a bond sell-off the government is not leaning against is the week's signal: stagflation-lite in industry alongside a central bank that has just told markets not to expect help. Orlen at a record on higher pump prices is the mirror image of the consumer's problem.  |  Playbook ✓: Sticky CEE inflation preventing cuts is consensus; stay long Orlen and PGE on the energy-price tailwind, short duration.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The market is selling KGHM and state names into a growth print of 3.9% and a record investment cycle; if the PMI dip is a European inventory blip rather than a Polish turn, today's correction in cyclicals is a buying opportunity rather than a warning.
