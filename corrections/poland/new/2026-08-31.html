🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, cuts pushed out

Macro Stance: Two GUS prints landed this morning and they pull in opposite directions. The August flash CPI came in at 3.4% y/y and 0.4% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Aug 31]) against a 3.1% consensus (BIZNES24, [Aug 31]), with fuel up 24.2% y/y the main driver (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]); pb.pl noted the fuel price cap came too late to show in the data [Aug 31]. The reaction was uniform: no cuts after the holidays (Interia Biznes, [Aug 31]), the door to cheaper credit is closed (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 31]), and mBank now sees inflation slightly above 4% at year-end (Portal Samorządowy, [Aug 31]). The NBP sits at 3.75% (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]). Growth is the good half: Q2 GDP was revised up to 3.9% y/y and 1.0% q/q (PAP Biznes, [Aug 31]), with investment up 8.4% (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]) and net exports helping (ING, [Aug 31]), while consumption slowed (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 31]). Import pass-through and divergence versus the ECB remain the zloty's structural headwinds.

Markets: The WIG20 opened the week higher with Orlen supplying the fuel (Parkiet, [Aug 31]): the state's appetite for dividends heated Orlen's quote while Text slumped on results (pb.pl, [Aug 31]) and Comparic flagged a fresh strong recommendation with 157 zł as the resistance to beat [Aug 31]. That follows a bruising week: Thursday was the biggest sell-off in over two months (Bankier.pl, [Aug 27]) with the WIG20 the weakest index in Europe (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 27]) as the CIA chief's Moscow trip triggered an exit from GPW and the zloty (Parkiet, [Aug 27]). The card confirms the damage and the winners: EPOL at 43.48 (-2.1% 1W, +1.5% 4W), CD Projekt at 236.5 (-8.6% 1W, -5.4% 4W), Orlen at 148.98 (-2.7% 1W) before today's bounce, Pekao at 255.5 (-1.3% 1W, +4.1% 4W); against that PGE at 10.89 (+6.9% 1W, +7.7% 4W), KGHM at 362.0 (+5.7% 1W, +17.6% 4W) and Dino at 35.56 (+2.2% 1W, +11.2% 4W). The zloty is losing ground hard today (Comparic.pl, [Aug 31]) after the euro moved near 4.34 and the dollar toward 3.75 around Jackson Hole (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 28]); the card has USD/PLN at 3.7427 (+1.5% 1W, +0.4% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3372 (+0.7% 1W, +0.8% 4W). POLGBs are the stress point: the 10-year yield is back above 6% (Interia Biznes, [Aug 31]; Analizy.pl, [Aug 31]), and today's inflation surprise gives the sell-off a domestic justification it lacked last week.

Fiscal & Politics: The 2027 draft budget adopted on Friday carries a state deficit of 282.6 bn zł and a general government deficit of 7.1% of GDP, with growth of 3.6% this year and 3% next (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 28]). The weekend commentary hardened: Rzeczpospolita calls it a record hole with the hard decisions deferred to after the elections [Aug 31], Gazeta Prawna says debt is a hair from a key threshold [Aug 29], and Bloomberg reports the government itself sees growth slowing to 2% by 2030 on fiscal tightening [Aug 29]. Spending lines are now visible: 8 bn zł for the nuclear plant plus 6 bn zł for mining (Bankier.pl, [Aug 31]), 16 bn zł for rail (Portal Samorządowy, [Aug 30]), nearly 5.8 bn zł for housing (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 30]) and billions in dividends expected from state companies (Interia Biznes, [Aug 29]). On revenue, the finance ministry is scrapping the internet tax relief (Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 30]), will not touch the zero-PIT relief for the young (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 31]) and wants higher taxes and fees on fuel companies, which Orlen does not oppose (WNP.PL, [Aug 31]). Domański and Glapiński are at the G20 in the US (Money.pl, [Aug 30]); Fitch's negative outlook (PropertyNews.pl, [Aug 24]) frames the risk.

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen is the week's swing name: beyond the dividend-driven rally, its SMR venture with Sołowow is in open conflict, with Orlen issuing an ultimatum and the project's liquidity reported at risk (Business Insider Polska and Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 31]). Banks keep printing records: mortgage lending reached 39.7 bn zł (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 27]) after July's 14.8 bn zł (Bankier.pl, [Aug 24]), the sector has 2.1 trillion in deposits (Bankier.pl, [Aug 28]); new treasury bonds are already issued without WIBOR (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Aug 28]). KGHM, now the world's largest silver producer (netTG.pl, [Aug 31]), faces unions demanding a 6.5 thousand zł bonus per employee (Radio Wrocław, [Aug 31]). CD Projekt's Gamescom showing left investors cold (INNPoland.pl, [Aug 31]) and the half-year report is on this week's calendar (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 30]). In energy, record renewable curtailments are eroding solar economics (Parkiet, [Aug 31]), and the CPN fuel-cap programme ends with diesel heading back above 8 zł (Next Gazeta.pl, [Aug 28]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk warned that Russia is preparing to escalate and that the next seven to eight months are critical, after speaking with Rutte about possible provocations against NATO states (Українська правда, [Aug 29]). US Green Berets are training Polish forces for drone warfare (AP News, [Aug 30]); Poland refuses to host nuclear weapons (Головне в Україні, [Aug 28]); Patriot stocks in Europe are beyond critical (PBS, [Aug 27]) while the EU lobbies members to send interceptors to Ukraine (Euronews, [Aug 31]).

New / Most Important: The 3.4% CPI flash against 3.1% expected is the development that matters. It removes the one cushion the budget had: a rate-cut cycle that would have lowered debt-service costs and lifted bank-heavy equities. With 10-year yields above 6%, a 7.1% deficit and a zloty already sliding on security risk, the NBP is now boxed in on hold while growth of 3.9% gives it no cover to ease.  |  Playbook ✓: PLN weakness on ECB divergence is standard; the hotter print adds a domestic reason to stay short duration and overweight the earnings-backed cyclicals, KGHM and PGE.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Higher-for-longer rates are a tailwind for bank margins, yet Pekao and PKO BP are down on the week; if the market keeps selling banks on fiscal and geopolitical risk, the rates trade and the equity trade are pointing in opposite directions.
