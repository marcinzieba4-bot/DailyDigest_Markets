🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, debt in focus

Macro Stance: The Sunday papers moved the argument from inflation to solvency. Business Insider Polska's analysis says Poland's debt is "rising at lightning speed" and explains where it came from [Oct 4], an analyst told Filary Biznesu public debt reaches "even 4 trillion zloty" [Oct 4], Gazeta na Niedzielę prices it at 70 thousand zł per head [Oct 3] and Super Biznes reads the government's own documents as confirming a "black scenario" of sharply slower growth [Oct 4], with xyz.pl describing a fiscal squeeze [Oct 4]. The monetary side is unchanged from the week: September flash CPI 4.0% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Sep 30]) driven by fuel up 36.1% (Farmer.pl, [Oct 2]), core ex food and energy down (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 30]), NBP staff lifting the 2027 forecast to 3.2% (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 30]), Glapiński vowing not to let above-target inflation "become entrenched" (RadioMaryja.pl, [Oct 2]) while an RPP member expects to be "forced to tighten" (TVN24, [Oct 1]) and PKO BP still sees no hike (Bankier.pl, [Sep 29]). Prudential thresholds matter here because the 55% line is passed in 2028 under the government's own strategy (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 29]) and the ECB hiking path, with eurozone inflation at 3.8% (Bankier.pl, [Oct 2]), tightens euro-area conditions that spill into CEE credit.

Markets: No session, so the card still shows Friday's close after a week in which banks were sold for five days (Parkiet, [Oct 2]) and the WIG20 posted its worst session since late August (WNP.PL, [Oct 1]): PKO 118.24 (-5.1% 1W, +1.1% 4W), Pekao 268.7 (-4.1% 1W, +1.8% 4W), Orlen 144.32 (-3.0% 1W, -8.7% 4W), LPP 24020 (-3.5% 1W, +12.6% 4W), Dino 33.66 (-2.3% 1W, -6.4% 4W), against PGE 15.02 (+15.4% 1W, +19.8% 4W), CD Projekt 253.5 (+3.6% 1W, +9.3% 4W), Allegro 50.39 (+1.7% 1W, +10.5% 4W) and KGHM 335.3 (+1.5% 1W after Friday's 4.39% gain per Inwestycje.pl [Oct 2]). EPOL sits at 43.03 (-4.1% 1W, -3.4% 4W). The zloty's last card print is USD/PLN 3.89 (+1.1% 1W, +4.7% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3731 (-0.1% 1W, +1.2% 4W), after the dollar touched 3.90 zł and the franc 4.70 zł (Bankier.pl, [Oct 2]) with US ten-year yields at 5.293% (UA.NEWS, [Sep 30]); pb.pl sees weak US data able to push the dollar back to 3.86 [Oct 2]. On POLGBs, Parkiet's "bonds became cheap again" [Oct 1] and Friday's slight easing of the global sell-off [Oct 2] are the latest words; Parkiet warns volatility "remains elevated" [Oct 2]. Oil is the weekend variable: TVN24 reports an attack and "one of the biggest crises" in crude [Oct 4] while Business Insider Polska advises drivers to wait because pumps should be cheaper shortly [Oct 4].

Fiscal & Politics: The finance ministry rebuked the Fiscal Council and Dudek replied that the response "was not substantive" (Rynek Zdrowia, [Oct 4]), a public rift a week after the Council flagged a large risk of breaching the prudential threshold (INFOR.PL, [Sep 27]) and days after the 2027 draft set a 281.2 bn zł deficit on 977.6 bn zł of spending (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 30]). Super Biznes concludes taxes must rise and 800+ must be trimmed [Oct 4], Zero.pl says repair should not wait [Oct 4] and Money.pl notes the minimum wage will "almost stand still" [Oct 4]. The fuel cap is law and in force at stations (blue News, [Oct 3]) with Orlen cutting diesel by 337 zł wholesale (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 3]); PKO BP says the windfall levy covers 2.5 months of it (wnp.pl, [Oct 2]) and Domański insists there is "no room" for pre-election gifts (FXMAG, [Oct 3]). Defence is the structural driver: IntelliNews reports Poland and the Baltics pushing spending toward 5% of GDP [Oct 4].

Corporate & Sectors: PKO Leasing is financing KGHM's US copper mine with a deal above 120 mln USD (PKO Bank Polski, [Oct 4]) on top of KGHM's 725 mln USD Sierra Gorda expansion that lifts copper output by 20% (LCA, [Oct 3]). Allegro entered the race for Orlen Paczka with more than a billion zloty at stake (Portal spożywczy, [Oct 4]). The state-assets ministry summarised board changes at Orlen and KGHM, stressing female appointments (Bankier.pl, [Oct 4]); Orlen itself carries a 2 bn zł windfall-tax estimate (Bankier.pl, [Oct 2]) and a strategy review with RES spending in question (Interia Biznes, [Oct 1]). The energy minister made a declaration on a "radical" rise in concession fees (PolsatNews.pl, [Oct 4]); Obajtek wants power prices down by up to 35% (Fakt, [Oct 3]). Banks: consumers are suing over WIBOR "ever more boldly" per new court data (Olsztyn.com.pl, [Oct 4]), Millennium moved mortgages to POLSTR (XYZ, [Oct 1]) and OKI accounts launch in January (Parkiet, [Oct 3]). Dino runs a 150 mln zł Silesian hub (Urbanity, [Oct 2]); LPP operates over 1600 robots in two centres (quasa.io, [Oct 4]); CD Projekt fixed the Remaster's weakest element after 11 years (Gram.pl, [Oct 4]). Bankier.pl finds Polish flats cheap to run but cramped and expensive to buy versus the EU [Oct 4].

Geopolitics & Security: The week's backdrop stands: Putin's "every weapon" warning over Kaliningrad (BBC, [Oct 1]), Warsaw calling Russian threats an escalation attempt (UA.NEWS, [Oct 2]), eased rules for shooting down drones and missiles from Russia (The Kyiv Independent, [Sep 28]) and a Polish general's view that Russia cannot attack NATO now but escalates through hybrid operations (Informat.ro, [Oct 1]).

New / Most Important: The debt debate has gone mainstream in a single weekend: 4 trillion zloty, 70 thousand per person and an open MF-Fiscal Council quarrel. That is the backdrop against which the nearshoring story and the bank bid will be judged. Nearshoring remains intact, but the ECB's hikes plus Poland's own 55% path leave little cushion if FDI momentum slows.  |  Playbook ✓: Keep bank exposure neutral into the RPP, hold KGHM and PGE, treat the Allegro-Orlen Paczka bid as a catalyst to watch, and keep POLGB duration short while the fiscal argument stays this loud.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A fuel cap that lowers pump prices by decree while the MF fights its own fiscal watchdog; CD Projekt and PGE at highs as the index posts its worst week since August.
