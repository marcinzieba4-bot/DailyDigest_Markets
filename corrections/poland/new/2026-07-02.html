🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, cut bias

Macro Stance: The RPP's July decision is now in view and the consensus is no change: CEO Magazyn expects rates most likely unchanged [Jul 2], Magazyn MANAGER+ says the Council will probably hold because the end of CPN raises inflation uncertainty [Jul 2], and a minister argued low inflation removes any basis for hikes (Money.pl, [Jul 2]). GUS explained why June CPI dived to 2.5%: fruit, vegetables and cheap imports (Bankier.pl, [Jul 2]), which makes the drop look less durable than the headline, and Rzeczpospolita's Fidziński argues CPN defused a political bomb rather than inflation [Jul 2]. The hike bets have vanished and cut expectations build, per ING [Jun 30], but the growth side deteriorated: the PMI fell to 46.1 in June against a 49.4 consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 1]). GUS put the January median wage at 7447.16 zł gross (Prawo.pl, [Jul 2]) and its new labour-market data surprised on age differences (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 2]). Home prices are rising again, faster than inflation (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 2]). The zloty remains under pressure as the market reprices the NBP path lower, which is the risk to any dovish pivot if it feeds imported inflation.

Markets: Thursday was a green session with Żabka, XTB and GPW itself at all-time highs (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 2]); Orlen led the WIG20, XTB set a record and a new developer debuted, with a US report pleasing investors (Bankier.pl, [Jul 2]), and CD Projekt and Modivo rebounded strongly (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 2]). Wednesday's closes on the card confirm the turn: EPOL 38.69 (+1.8% 1W, -3.7% 4W); PKO 104.6 (+1.2% 1W, +5.1% 4W); Pekao 230.8 (+0.9% 1W); Orlen 126.7 (+3.2% 1W, -11.8% 4W); KGHM 328.65 (+1.4% 1W, -12.7% 4W); PGE 9.43 (+1.6% 1W, -9.7% 4W); Allegro 38.57 (+3.3% 1W, +9.9% 4W); LPP 18500 (+2.2% 1W, -14.2% 4W); CD Projekt 224.5 (-0.7% 1W); Dino 29.0 (flat 1W, -3.2% 4W). The weekly turn is broad, but the four-week column still shows Orlen, KGHM, PGE and LPP deep in drawdown. Goldman Sachs led GPW equity turnover in June with BofA second (Bankier.pl, [Jul 2]). FX: USD/PLN closed at 3.7709 (flat 1W, +3.2% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.2904 (+0.2% 1W, +1.2% 4W); Comparic.pl has the dollar retreating and the zloty stabilising [Jul 2], while Bankier.pl notes the pound at 5 zł and the franc at its dearest since March [Jul 2].

Fiscal & Politics: The windfall tax is now explicitly the CPN bill: Biznes Wprost reports it will top up the budget with 4 bn zł for the package [Jul 2]. MF released a further 2 bn zł of reserves for NFZ (Rynek Zdrowia, [Jul 2]) and is targeting mechanics and builders in a crackdown on the grey economy (Bankier.pl, [Jul 2]). The finance minister attacked the president, saying there is no chemistry between them (Business Insider Polska, [Jul 2]), the president sent the e-cigarette excise law to the Constitutional Tribunal (Prawo.pl, [Jul 2]), and the Sejm took its final decision on the 800 plus benefit and a new payment (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 2]). Climate-fee limits rise (Fakt, [Jul 2]) and the sugar levy is being redesigned (Farmer.pl, [Jul 2]).

Corporate & Sectors: Banks got their CHF ruling: the CJEU held that banks keep the right to recover paid-out capital (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 2]), Money.pl called it the end of the dream of free flats [Jul 2], Rzeczpospolita reported champagne in the banks [Jul 2], and INFOR.PL notes the court set when limitation runs [Jul 2]; the Senate-passed CHF law still awaits the president's signature (Business Insider Polska, [Jun 25]). Orlen reached 95% of Energa and can announce a squeeze-out (pb.pl, [Jul 2]), opened its first fast-charging hub in Germany (Bankier.pl, [Jul 2]) and is heading into Ukraine (Strefa Biznesu, [Jul 2]). Dino opened 148 stores in the first half and had 3176 at end-June (BIZNES24, [Jul 2]), more than Biedronka plans for all of 2026 (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Jul 2]), yet Morgan Stanley cut Żabka and slashed Dino's valuation (pb.pl, [Jul 2]). KGHM's 725 mln USD Sierra Gorda plan (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 1]) left the market unconvinced, with the shares rising only sluggishly (INNPoland.pl, [Jul 2]). PGE's Turów mine kept its environmental decision, ending a years-long dispute (Money.pl, [Jul 2]), and PGE Baltica's offshore projects are gathering pace (Fakt, [Jul 2]). Energy policy turned supportive: the government wants business power prices down by a double-digit percentage (Bankier.pl, [Jul 2]), an ETS reform decision could lower prices (wnp.pl, [Jul 2]), and the energy minister expects fuel prices to slowly normalise (PolsatNews.pl, [Jul 2]).

Geopolitics & Security: Catholic leaders urged Poland and Ukraine to pursue reconciliation and disarm their language (EWTN News, [Jul 2]), the latest intervention in the rift that saw Poland cancel MiG-29 deliveries (The Times of India, [Jul 1]) and business press both capitals to end it (RTL Today, [Jun 29]). On the ground, the KOP border component returned to the eastern frontier (Defence24, [Jul 1]) and the president extended the Iraq and Latvia missions (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 1]).

New / Most Important: The CJEU ruling is the regime change that matters for Warsaw equities: with capital recovery confirmed, the CHF tail risk that has capped bank multiples for years shrinks, and it lands the same week the hike debate died. The shift from "will they hike?" to "when do they cut?" is done; the open question is whether a 46.1 PMI and a 4 bn zł windfall-tax bill make the cut cycle a growth rescue rather than a disinflation dividend. Record highs for XTB and GPW say the local bid is back.  |  Playbook ✓: Hold in July, cuts later; overweight banks on the CJEU outcome and PKO's dividend, use Orlen's squeeze-out of Energa as a catalyst trade, and stay underweight Dino after the Morgan Stanley cut.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: CPI at target but driven by fruit and imports; record exchange highs against a PMI at 46.1; Dino's 148 openings versus a slashed valuation; zloty weakness that could complicate the dovish pivot through imported inflation.
