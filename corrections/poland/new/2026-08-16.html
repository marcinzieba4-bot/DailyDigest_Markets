🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → on hold

Macro Stance: The week's releases leave the RPP with no reason to move. July CPI was confirmed at 3.0% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]) with the acceleration driven by fuel, up 13.9% in a month (300Gospodarka.pl, PAP Biznes, [Aug 13]), while core inflation climbed to a ten-month high (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 14]); CEO Magazyn concludes NBP rates can stay unchanged for longer [Aug 14]. Interia notes that shop inflation is the lowest on record but fuel can push bills back up [Aug 10], which is precisely the split the price caps starting Monday are meant to address. Q2 GDP grew 3.8% y/y and 0.9% q/q (PAP Biznes, [Aug 13]), more than forecast (Bloomberg, [Aug 13]), resting on investment and KPO funds rather than consumption (pb.pl, wGospodarce, [Aug 13]). The labour side is softer: the quarterly drop in the average wage was the sharpest in years (Forsal.pl, [Aug 12]). ING's record primary income deficit in the current account [Aug 13] is the one external flag. mBank's forecast of no rate change until year-end with growth of 3.7% (Bankier.pl, [Aug 11]) sits comfortably with all of this.

Markets: With Friday's close as the latest print, the card shows a market consolidating near its highs: EPOL 44.45 (+0.6% 1W, +10.2% 4W). Banks are the fresh leadership after PKO's results, with Pekao 259.2 (+2.5% 1W, +13.2% 4W) and PKO 111.08 (+1.9% 1W, +4.0% 4W) ahead of the index, Orlen 152.2 (+2.1% 1W) and CD Projekt 263.1 (+2.7% 1W, +13.3% 4W) also higher on the week, while the summer's big winners paused: KGHM 352.5 (-0.1% 1W, +20.0% 4W), PGE 10.79 (+0.4% 1W, +13.8% 4W), Dino 32.41 (-0.1% 1W, +11.2% 4W), Allegro 46.46 (+0.6% 1W) and LPP 21,820 (-1.8% 1W). Friday brought small index moves with KGHM and energy names falling (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 14]) after Thursday's all-red WIG20 session (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]). Norway's fund moved from Żabka into Allegro and took JSW (Parkiet, [Aug 12]). The zloty closed the week at USD/PLN 3.7353 (+0.1% 1W, -1.1% 4W) and EUR/PLN 4.3084 (+0.3% 1W, -0.5% 4W); Comparic.pl's weekend note says USD/PLN forecasts point to further zloty strength, with the dollar possibly losing another 9 groszy [Aug 16], against FXMAG's call for EUR/PLN at 4.31 or higher [Aug 12]. POLGBs saw a 10.72 billion zł switch tender (TradingView, [Aug 12]) while Germany pays the most for debt since the euro crisis (Money.pl, [Aug 15]). The week ahead is results-heavy: KGHM, Dino, PZU and Budimex are on the half-year calendar (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 16]) and CD Projekt reports Q2 soon (BitHub.pl, [Aug 16]).

Fiscal & Politics: Fuel policy restarts on Monday: the government is returning to regulated petrol and diesel prices (WysokieNapiecie.pl, [Aug 16]) through the CPN programme with a lower VAT rate for two weeks (Do Rzeczy, [Aug 14]), a cut Tusk valued at 1 zł a litre (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 13]) and a Pb95 cap of 6.41 zł (e-magazyny.pl, [Aug 14]). On taxes, Gazeta Prawna reports that after Nawrocki blocked the previous rules the government is already preparing a new levy, with first details out [Aug 12], while the 60 thousand zł tax-free amount is costed at over 60 billion zł (INFOR.PL, [Aug 14]); seniors will not get a 1600 plus payment (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 15]). The constraints are explicit: the Fiscal Council says there is no time left on the 2027 budget (Business Insider Polska, [Aug 12]), pb.pl calls the debt snowball faster than the government admits [Aug 12] and Poles worked a record 229 days for the state (Money.pl, [Aug 13]). Nawrocki signed the OKI law (PAP, [Aug 13]) and the nuclear amendment that could advance the plant by two years (GlobEnergia, [Aug 14]).

Corporate & Sectors: PKO BP's 2.77 billion zł Q2 and 5.29 billion zł first half (StockWatch.pl, Parkiet, [Aug 13]) came with a CEO view that 2026 can be the strongest year in its history (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]) and plans for another retail covered-bond issue (Bankier.pl, [Aug 13]); the overhang is legal, with 7666 free-credit suits and no reserve (CHF24, [Aug 14]) and mBank settlements collapsing (Franknews.pl, [Aug 15]). KGHM is drilling for a new mine under its 2055+ strategy (e-legnickie, [Aug 15]) and XYZ weighs what Congo's mineral export curbs mean for the company [Aug 14]. CD Projekt trimmed the Sirius team (IGN Polska, [Aug 12]) and analysts doubt the 2 billion zł incentive target (Strefa Inwestorów, [Aug 12]). Orlen enters the CPN fortnight after PiS questioned its margins (RadioMaryja.pl, [Aug 14]). Dino is running aggressive promotions into its results (BiznesINFO.pl, [Aug 16]). Energy stress is regional: Hungary's Paks runs at a fraction of capacity as the Danube dries (Wyborcza.biz, [Aug 16]), Romania shut its reactors (Energetyka24, [Aug 13]) and Azoty is hunting energy savings (Energetyka24.com, [Aug 16]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Telegraph reports Russia building secret drone bases within striking distance of NATO [Aug 16] and The Japan Times describes Putin trying the window after failing at the door [Aug 16], a day after Tusk promised Europe's largest army and four pillars of security (wnp.pl, The Kyiv Independent, [Aug 15]) and Nawrocki set God, honour and fatherland against SAFE (Rzeczpospolita, [Aug 15]). The thwarted Russian plot to kill a Ukrainian-American in Warsaw (The Guardian, [Aug 13]) and NATO's condemnation of airspace violations (Euronews.com, [Aug 12]) frame the week; WB Group's drone deal with Kongsberg underlines defence growth (ad-hoc-news.de, [Aug 9]).

New / Most Important: Monday's fuel caps are the hinge. They will pull the August CPI print down mechanically while core sits at a ten-month high, so the RPP will be looking through a headline it knows is administered; the market should too.
