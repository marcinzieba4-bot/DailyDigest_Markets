🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, fuel cap buys time

Macro Stance: Glapiński drew the line today: the NBP "has no influence on energy prices" but "will not allow inflation above target to become entrenched" (RadioMaryja.pl, [Oct 2]), warning that the energy shock "spreads and moves" through the economy (Money.pl, [Oct 2]; Parkiet, [Oct 2]). That follows an RPP member telling TVN24 the Council "will be forced to tighten" [Oct 1] and the September flash CPI of 4.0% y/y and 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Sep 30]) with fuel up 36.1% (Farmer.pl, [Oct 2]). The signed fuel law changes the arithmetic: Rzeczpospolita reports a "sudden turn" in economists' inflation forecasts after the president's decision [Oct 2] and Euronext carries the view that the fuel price cap could ease pressure on the central bank to tighten [Oct 2], while PKO BP calculates the windfall-tax take funds only 2.5 months of cuts (wnp.pl, [Oct 2]). Core ex food and energy fell in September (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 30]) and PKO BP still sees no hike (Bankier.pl, [Sep 29]). Externally, eurozone inflation surging to 3.8%, its highest in three years (Bankier.pl, [Oct 2]), keeps the ECB hawkish and ECB's Rehn argues rising bond yields will blunt the energy effect (pb.pl, [Oct 2]).

Markets: Friday closed red with Orlen dragging the WIG20 (Parkiet, [Oct 2]) after the company estimated its windfall tax at 2 bn zł for March to August (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 2]); WNP.PL called the levy a "clearly negative signal" for Orlen and Unimot [Oct 2] and oEnergetice.cz puts the rate at 60% [Oct 2]. KGHM bucked the tape, up 4.39%, while LPP and PGE fell sharply (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 2]), and Parkiet says investors "are turning away from banks" with the GPW relatively weaker than its surroundings [Oct 2]. Thursday's rout is now in the card: PKO 119.68 (-3.8% 1W, +4.3% 4W), Pekao 270.8 (-3.7% 1W, +4.6% 4W), KGHM 321.2 (-4.4% 1W, -8.8% 4W), Orlen 146.16 (-2.7% 1W, -6.3% 4W), Dino 34.57 (-3.5% 4W); the winners remain PGE 15.47 (+17.0% 1W, +26.5% 4W), CD Projekt 254.5 (+3.5% 1W, +9.4% 4W), LPP 24740 (+18.8% 4W) and Allegro 49.6 (+10.0% 4W). EPOL dropped to 43.25 (-3.3% 1W, -2.1% 4W). The zloty hit a new low: the dollar reached 3.90 zł and the franc brushed 4.70 zł in nervous trading (Bankier.pl, [Oct 2]), USD/PLN sat just under 3.91 (Comparic.pl, [Oct 2]) and the card has USD/PLN at 3.89 (+1.1% 1W, +4.7% 4W) with EUR/PLN at 4.3731 (-0.1% 1W, +1.2% 4W); pb.pl thinks weak US data could pull the dollar back to 3.86 [Oct 2]. Bonds: the global sell-off eased slightly (Parkiet, [Oct 2]) after Parkiet's "cheap again" call on POLGBs [Oct 1].

Fiscal & Politics: The president "backed down" and signed the windfall tax on fuel companies (Notes From Poland, [Oct 2]), while referring it to the Constitutional Tribunal (Energetyka24, [Oct 2]); the government launched the next CPN round with petrol below 7 zł and diesel below 8 zł per litre (Wyborcza.biz, [Oct 2]) and Domański gave a figure for the cut (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 2]). The fight moved to the finance ministry itself: PiS demanded Domański's dismissal (Money.pl, [Oct 2]), Nawrocki attacked Tusk over public finances and called for the minister to go (Business Insider Polska, [Oct 2]) and Czarnek threatened "freezing everything" (naTemat.pl, [Oct 2]). Rzeczpospolita says this is "the last year to brake the debt, then it will hurt" [Oct 2] and reports Poles rate cheap fuel above the budget [Oct 2]; the 2027 draft carries a 281.2 bn zł deficit on 977.6 bn zł of spending (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 30]) and interest costs next year exceed family spending (pb.pl, [Oct 1]).

Corporate & Sectors: KGHM began the 725 mln USD Sierra Gorda expansion, lifting resources to 3 bn tonnes of ore (StockWatch.pl, [Oct 2]); the CEO says foreign output must grow and the company is in one M&A process (BIZNES24, [Oct 2]), and Investing.com Polska asks whether the shares can jump 50% on the largest copper deficit in history [Oct 2]. Orlen Paczka is back on sale with a late Allegro surprise (Business Insider Polska, [Oct 2]). Bank profits are clearly melting (Business Insider Polska, [Oct 2]) and borrowers are heading to court with up to 284 bn zł at stake over WIBOR (Radio Zet, [Oct 2]). JSW will cut 21% of its workforce this year (Notes From Poland, [Oct 2]). Nuclear: the US side accepted Polish law and the plant contract is closer (Bankier.pl, [Oct 2]), Warsaw wants to sign this year (Portal Samorządowy, [Oct 2]) though the first reactor now starts in 2036 (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Oct 1]) and wnp.pl warns the 192 bn zł price tag may surprise [Oct 2]; Sołowow eyes a 2028 IPO for SGE (Bloomberg, [Oct 1]). Dino builds a 150 mln zł Silesian distribution centre with 300 jobs (Urbanity, [Oct 2]). CD Projekt has two titles in Steam's top ten (Strefa Inwestorów, [Oct 2]) and FXMAG calls the Remaster "a very good prelude" to the Witcher 4 campaign [Oct 2].

Geopolitics & Security: Warsaw called Russian threats against NATO an attempt at escalation (UA.NEWS, [Oct 2]) after Putin said Russia is ready to use "every weapon" over Kaliningrad (BBC, [Oct 1]) and Lavrov invoked nuclear doctrine against the Patriot plan (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Sep 29]); a Polish general judged Russia unable to invade but "constantly escalating" (TVP World, [Oct 1]).

New / Most Important: The windfall tax is now law, and its first casualty is Orlen's 2 bn zł bill rather than inflation: the fuel cap pulls headline CPI down mechanically, which is the argument the doves need to hold in October, but PKO BP's 2.5-month funding horizon says the relief is temporary. Glapiński's "will not allow entrenchment" is the hedge.  |  Playbook ✓: Underweight Orlen and Unimot into the levy, stay neutral banks after the rout, keep KGHM as the copper-deficit trade, and fade USD/PLN above 3.90 only against weak US data.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A fuel subsidy sold as anti-inflationary while the governor warns the shock "spreads"; PGE +17.0% in a week against the GPW's worst session in a month.
