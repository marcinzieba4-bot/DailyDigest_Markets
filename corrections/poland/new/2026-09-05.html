🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → rates on hold

Macro Stance: The August GUS flash reset the rate debate: CPI came in at 3.4% y/y and 0.4% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Aug 31]) against a 3.1% consensus (BIZNES24, [Aug 31]), with fuel up 24.2% y/y (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]) because the CPN fuel scheme ended too late to show in the data (pb.pl, [Aug 31]). Growth is the other half of the bind: Q2 GDP rose 3.9% y/y and 1.0% q/q on the GUS preliminary estimate (PAP Biznes, [Aug 31]), with investment +8.4% running three times faster than consumption (CEO Magazyn, [Aug 31]). Parkiet's verdict is that the economy grows while inflation ties the RPP's hands [Sep 3]; Interia reported the NBP governor cooling rate expectations [Sep 1], an RPP member told FXMAG there will be no hike in 2026 but no cut to count on [Sep 4], and Gazeta Prawna found the Council short of arguments for easing [Sep 5]. Labour stays tight: Eurostat unemployment at 3.4% in July (Do Rzeczy, [Sep 1]), retail sales +3.6% y/y against 1% in the EU (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 4]), a GUS median wage of 7530.45 zł for March (Prawo.pl, [Sep 3]) and a 2027 minimum wage of 4950 zł a month (FXMAG, [Sep 5]).

Markets: Warsaw closed the week at a record. The WIG20 printed a historic high on Friday (Parkiet, [Sep 4]) at 4090 points (Rankia Polska, [Sep 4]) after a mid-week consolidation in which the index lost 0.47% on Tuesday (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 1]). Leadership was state energy and banks: Orlen broke 160 zł for a record (CrypS.pl, [Sep 1]), PGE gained 3.21% and KGHM 2.34% on Thursday (FXMAG, [Sep 4]), and Strefa Biznesu credited banks and apparel [Sep 4] and then Orlen, PGE, Tauron, Enea and KGHM [Sep 5] with driving the rally. The card agrees: EPOL at 45.12 is +3.8% on the week; PGE at 12.55 is +15.2% 1W and +16.7% 4W, Orlen at 158.0 is +6.1% 1W, PKO (116.9, +3.0% 1W, +7.2% 4W) and Pekao (263.9, +3.3% 1W) carried the banks, and Dino (35.98, +10.9% 4W) stays the retail winner. Laggards are CD Projekt at 232.0 (-9.4% 4W), KGHM at 356.0 (-1.7% 1W) and LPP at 21340 (-4.0% 4W). Supply is building: XTB's founder sold shares worth PLN 1.5 billion (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 3]) and Allegro's tenth ABB saw MidEuropa place a stake worth over 192 mln zł (XYZ, [Sep 3]). The zloty is steady: USD/PLN closed at 3.715 (-0.0% 1W, -0.4% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.32 (-0.2% 1W, +0.6% 4W), with the euro testing 4.32 after Waller weakened the dollar (FXMAG and Bankier.pl, [Sep 4]); Commerzbank noted NBP language turning cautious after FX pressure (tmgm, [Sep 3]). POLGBs are the stress point: 10-year yields are back above 6% (Interia Biznes, [Aug 31]) in a global sovereign sell-off that is not only about oil (pb.pl, [Sep 2]).

Fiscal & Politics: The 2027 draft budget is the bond market's problem. The deficit stays above 7% of GDP (WNP.PL, [Aug 31]; ING, [Aug 31]), Bankier.pl paired it with a rating-downgrade threat [Sep 1], Filary Biznesu reported experts fearing the agencies' reaction [Sep 3], and Business Insider Polska said rising yields may force a budget correction [Sep 3]. Debt is a hair from the key threshold (Gazeta Prawna, [Aug 29]), Piotr Arak warned Poland nears the constitutional debt limit with no exit strategy (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 4]) and debt-service costs will exceed 100 bn zł even as borrowing falls (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 1]). The government blamed the president in the Sejm (pb.pl, [Sep 3]) and left the Constitutional Tribunal without a 2027 budget (Bankier.pl, [Sep 3]). Revenue measures are creeping in: the ministry wants to scrap a popular tax relief (Gazeta Prawna, [Sep 3]) and CPK gets a few billion less (Money.pl, [Sep 4]), while local governments receive 206.7 bn zł from PIT (XYZ, [Sep 5]). Bloomberg sees growth slowing to 2% by 2030 on fiscal tightening [Aug 29].

Corporate & Sectors: PKO BP became the first bank to sell POLSTR-based mortgages from Sep 3 (Parkiet, [Sep 1]), though Rzeczpospolita cautioned it does not guarantee cheaper credit [Sep 1]; banks hold 840 bn zł of government bonds (Subiektywnie o finansach, [Sep 5]). CD Projekt delivered a quarter-billion profit (Parkiet, [Sep 3]) but the stock reaction disappointed (INNPoland.pl, [Sep 3]) and the 2024-2027 incentive target looks out of reach (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 3]). Orlen lifted wholesale fuel prices to a four-year high (Money.pl, [Sep 4]), its CEO flagged a better 2026 result and a dividend (Bankier.pl, [Sep 3]) and it formed a Baltic Energy Initiative with 13 partners (Inzynieria.com, [Sep 4]). PGE's estimated Q2 net profit beat expectations (pb.pl, [Sep 2]) and it contracted at least 1859 MW in the capacity auction for 2027 (Inwestycje.pl, [Sep 4]). LPP launched the Sinsay marketplace targeting 100 thousand products in 2027 (StockWatch.pl, [Sep 2]) and trimmed openings to 750 from 950 stores (PropertyNews.pl, [Sep 4]); Allegro is testing bulky-item delivery for a 10 bn zł market (Strefa Biznesu, [Sep 4]); Dino faces a union escalation and strike threat (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Sep 3]). Gas storage is near 94% (Rzeczpospolita, [Sep 3]) but WNP.PL expects gas and power prices to surge in the hardest winter in years [Sep 4]. Warsaw's average flat price passed 18 tys. zł per square metre (Fakt, [Sep 4]).

Geopolitics & Security: Tusk warned Russia is preparing an escalation and called the next seven to eight months critical (Ukrainska Pravda, [Aug 29]) after talks with NATO's Rutte (PAP, [Aug 30]). Polish fighters intercepted a Russian Il-20 over the Baltic (UNITED24 Media, [Aug 31]), US Green Berets ran drone-warfare drills for Polish forces (AP News, [Aug 30]) and EUobserver said the wave of Russian sabotage exposes counter-intelligence gaps on the eastern flank [Sep 4]. Poland is to receive up to €430 million for weapons supplied to Ukraine (Militarnyi, [Sep 3]).

New / Most Important: The record WIG20 and the 6% POLGB yield are the same trade seen from two sides. A 3.4% CPI with 3.9% growth keeps the NBP put while the government borrows at a 7% deficit, so banks earn the carry, state energy earns the price spike and bonds absorb the fiscal risk.  |  Playbook ✓: Hawkish hold consistent with CEE central banks navigating war-proximity inflation; stay with banks and PGE momentum, keep duration short while the rating debate runs.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Equities at an all-time high while insiders sell PLN 1.5 billion of XTB and MidEuropa exits Allegro; CD Projekt printed a quarter-billion profit and is still down 9.4% over four weeks.
