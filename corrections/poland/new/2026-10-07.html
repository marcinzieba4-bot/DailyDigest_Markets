🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, Nov hike risk

Macro Stance: The RPP left all rates unchanged today, reference rate 3.75%, as expected (Strefa Inwestorów, StockWatch [Oct 7]). The communiqué was data-dependent in tone, with the Council watching energy prices and naming fuel prices and fiscal policy as the key inflation risks (Investing.com, Strefa Inwestorów [Oct 7]). Attention shifts to Governor Glapiński's press conference and the November projection, which Forex Club and CEO Magazyn [Oct 7] call decisive; Bankier [Oct 7] asks whether November brings a hike. The backdrop is a September flash CPI of 4.0% y/y and 0.7% m/m (GUS via PAP Biznes [Sep 30]), just under the 4.1% consensus but the highest in 15 months and outside the target band, driven by fuel up 36.1% y/y; pb.pl notes core CPI still holds the headline in check. Growth is the offset: GUS revised 2025 GDP to 3.8% from 3.6% (wnp.pl [Oct 6]) and the World Bank lifted its 2026 forecast to 3.6% (UA.NEWS [Oct 6]). GUS's April 2026 median wage of 7695 zł against an average of 9651 zł (Podatki.biz [Oct 7]) shows the headline wage overstates purchasing power. Retail sales rose 2.4% y/y in August per Eurostat (Strefa Inwestorów [Oct 6]); industry remains weak with declines only slowing (wnp.pl [Oct 1]).

Markets: The WIG set its second all-time closing record in two weeks on Oct 6 (IntelliNews [Oct 7]), with Budimex and mBank leading (Inwestycje.pl), after a 1.7% WIG20 rebound on Oct 5 (FXMAG). Today reversed that: Bankier [Oct 7] describes broad large-cap declines with a bounce in renewables, CrypS flags banks, CD Projekt and Allegro falling hard, and Parkiet [Oct 7] notes a bearish engulfing candle on WIG20, though Strefa Inwestorów still sees consolidation. The zloty is the weak link: USD/PLN closed 3.8818, up 0.9% on the week and 4.7% over four weeks, while EUR/PLN at 4.3681 is flat on the week and up 1.3% over four weeks, so the move is dollar-driven. Rzeczpospolita [Oct 7] pairs continued zloty losses with an ongoing POLGB sell-off, as US 10-year yields sit at 24-year highs above 5.30% (Invezz). EPOL closed 44.5, up 0.7% on the week but down 2.8% over four weeks. PGE at 15.02 is up 5.3% on the week and 9.7% over four weeks; KGHM at 335.3 is up 3.8% on the week but down 10.5% over four weeks; PKO at 118.24 (down 4.2%), Pekao at 268.7 (down 2.8%), Dino at 33.66 (down 4.9%) and LPP at 24020 (down 4.0%) lag; Orlen at 144.32 is down 9.0% over four weeks.

Fiscal & Politics: The 2027 budget had its first Sejm reading today (Gazeta Prawna [Oct 7]): 977.6bn zł of spending and a 281.2bn zł deficit (CEO Magazyn [Sep 30]). The Finance Ministry rejected the Fiscal Council's criticism (Bankier [Oct 7]) and insists record defence and health outlays pose no threat to stability (Interia [Oct 7]), while Rzeczpospolita [Oct 1] warns debt nears the prudential threshold and Balcerowicz urges privatisation and social cuts (Bankier [Oct 6]). The PIT package sets a 130 thousand zł second threshold, a new 24% rate from 150 thousand zł and 22% CIT for the largest firms, and a cadastral-tax bill (0.02% on first and second homes, 0.5% to 1.5% beyond) went to first reading (INFOR [Oct 5]). President Nawrocki signed the fuel windfall tax but sent it to the Constitutional Tribunal (Rzeczpospolita [Oct 1]); the price cap is in force and the Energy Minister cut maximum prices again for Thursday (Business Insider Polska [Oct 7]). A parliamentary probe largely endorsed accusations against the NBP governor (Bloomberg [Oct 6]).

Corporate & Sectors: Orlen cuts both ways: ABW detained former and current managers over fuel pricing, with alleged damage of 4.1bn zł (PAP [Oct 6]), yet the 1.2 GW Baltic Power farm has all 76 turbines installed (BIZNES24 [Oct 7]) and the company warns of a higher 2027 gas tariff (wnp.pl). KGHM launched the $725m Sierra Gorda expansion to lift copper output 20% (MuratorPlus [Oct 6]), and Orlen, PGE, Tauron, Enea and KGHM signed a critical-infrastructure pact (Energetyka24 [Oct 7]). CD Projekt broke a psychological level as The Witcher 3 set a Steam record (BitHub, FXMAG). Dino passed 3.2 thousand stores (Portal spożywczy [Oct 6]); Allegro hit a five-year high on Oct 6 (Parkiet) before today's drop. In banks, mBank moved new mortgages to POLSTR from today (Bankier), Millennium booked 154m PLN of CHF provisions for Q3 (Investing.com [Oct 7]), WIBOR litigation is put at up to 284bn zł (Radio Zet [Oct 2]) and profits are shrinking (Business Insider Polska [Oct 2]). Mortgage lending reached 75.7bn zł (Bankier [Oct 1]); JSW will shed 21% of its workforce (Notes From Poland [Oct 2]).

Geopolitics & Security: Sweden will deploy Patriot batteries to protect the NATO logistics hub in Poland (Reuters [Oct 6]), NATO's "Theory of Victory" is to be unveiled in Poland (TVP World [Oct 6]), and Poland with the Baltics is pushing defence spending toward 5% of GDP (IntelliNews [Oct 4]). Russia issued nuclear warnings over Kaliningrad (Reuters [Sep 30]) and is targeting the rail corridor to Ukraine (Defence24 [Oct 6]). Deloitte for UNHCR puts Ukrainian refugees' contribution at 2.4% of 2025 GDP, or 94bn zł (UNHCR [Oct 1]).

New / Most Important: The hold was priced; what matters is that the Council has tied its next move to fuel and fiscal policy, both of which the government is actively steering through the price cap and a 281.2bn zł deficit. That makes the November projection a genuine binary for POLGBs and the zloty, arriving just as the WIG has posted records and today's reversal shows how crowded the long is.

Playbook ✓: Stay neutral on duration into the Glapiński conference; favour PGE and Baltic Power-exposed utilities over banks, where POLSTR migration, CHF provisioning and WIBOR litigation compound rate uncertainty; treat today's WIG20 engulfing as a reason to trim, not to short, with 2025 GDP at 3.8%.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Zloty weakness against the dollar is largely imported (US yields at 24-year highs) rather than Poland-specific, as EUR/PLN's flat week shows; the local risk is the opposite tail, a November hike that the fuel cap is meant to make unnecessary but fiscal expansion could force.
