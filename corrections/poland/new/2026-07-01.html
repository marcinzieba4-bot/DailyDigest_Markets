🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  ↓ cut bias

Macro Stance: Two data points pull in opposite directions. The June flash put CPI exactly at the NBP target with prices down versus May (Obserwator Gospodarczy, [Jul 1]), the 2.5% print that Parkiet said ends the hike debate [Jun 30] and that ING thinks can open a market bet on cuts in the second half of 2026 [Jun 30]. But the manufacturing PMI collapsed to 46.1 in June against a 49.4 consensus (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 1]), the weakest in many months (Forsal.pl, [Jul 1]) and a sudden slump that surprised even analysts (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 1]). The disinflation is also about to be tested: the CPN fuel package ended and prices jumped (Notes From Poland, [Jul 1]), by about 60 groszy a litre per wPolityce.pl [Jul 1], with Gazeta Prawna's estimate that this could lift inflation toward 3% [Jun 30] now live. GUS put the January median wage at 7447 zł gross, so half of workers earn less (Forsal.pl, [Jul 1]) and Business Insider Polska says inequality is rising again [Jul 1]. NBP data show mortgages cheaper than a year ago (Bankier.pl, [Jul 1]). The NBP keeps a dovish lean that is weighing on the zloty, but a PMI at 46.1 turns the question from inflation to growth.

Markets: Quarter-end flattered Warsaw: the WIG20 gained 7.38% in the second quarter, a third positive quarter in a row (FXMAG, [Jul 1]), and Parkiet reported the exchange higher on Wednesday with gold and silver back to gains [Jul 1]; Modivo led and banks and retail drove the market (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 1]). Tuesday's closes on the card: EPOL 38.61 (-0.3% 1W, -3.6% 4W); PKO 103.14 (-1.2% 1W, +3.1% 4W); Pekao 228.8 (-1.0% 1W); Orlen 126.6 (-1.9% 1W, -12.4% 4W); KGHM 331.25 (-5.3% 1W, -5.6% 4W); PGE 9.44 (-3.0% 1W, -8.2% 4W); Allegro 37.81 (-2.9% 1W, +10.1% 4W); CD Projekt 224.4 (-1.9% 1W, -0.7% 4W); LPP 18280 (-1.1% 1W, -15.1% 4W); Dino 28.4 (-2.4% 1W). Orlen is losing momentum with 120 zł the level that decides direction (Comparic.pl, [Jul 1]) and INNPoland.pl links its fall to the end of CPN [Jul 1]; an analyst sharply raised Allegro's valuation (pb.pl, [Jul 1]). FX: USD/PLN closed at 3.7649 (+0.2% 1W, +3.3% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.2969 (+0.5% 1W, +1.5% 4W); EUR/PLN tested 4.30 before the zloty firmed with the market waiting for payrolls (wnp.pl, [Jul 1]), Bankier.pl says the euro broke its psychological barrier and the pound is at 5 zł as surprising inflation and a strong dollar hit the zloty [Jul 1]. Credit: Millennium BH placed 500 mln zł of covered bonds with demand above 830 mln zł (Bankier.pl, [Jul 1]) and MF published the July Treasury bond offer (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jul 1]).

Fiscal & Politics: MF put the cost of the CPN fuel programme at 4.7 bn zł and called it a success (XYZ, [Jul 1]); its end is Tusk's holiday "gift" of dearer fuel in wPolityce.pl's telling [Jul 1]. The spending pressure is immediate: Nowacka demands an 11% raise for teachers and says she will not let up on the finance minister (Bankier.pl, [Jul 1]), health-sector minimum pay rose from July (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 1]), and the government is closing a corporate loophole with a new tax on liquidations (wnp.pl, [Jul 1]). Interia Biznes sums it up as Poland will not go bankrupt, but things will happen [Jul 1]. The three-euro parcel customs charge started, with Allegro commenting (Money.pl, [Jul 1]). The windfall tax still carries the Senate's up-to-100% amendment (Strefa Inwestorów, [Jun 29]), and KS NGR updated the roadmap for the WIBOR reform (Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK, [Jul 1]).

Corporate & Sectors: KGHM will invest 725 mln USD to expand Sierra Gorda in Chile (StockWatch.pl, [Jul 1]), lifting ore throughput to around 60 mln tonnes a year (Inwestycje.pl, [Jul 1]). Orlen is tightening control of Energa, which may leave the exchange (Rzeczpospolita, [Jul 1]), and opened a bioLNG station in Płock (Parkiet, [Jul 1]) while raising wholesale fuel sharply as the shield ended (Super Biznes, [Jul 1]). PKO BP stopped counting the 800 plus benefit toward creditworthiness (Wyborcza.biz, [Jul 1]), launched instant SEPA euro transfers (CRN Polska, [Jul 1]) and its analysts warn home prices face several catches (Bankier.pl, [Jul 1]); flats above a million are becoming the norm in the Tricity (CEO Magazyn, [Jul 1]). pb.pl says the era of easy growth in mass fashion is over, a test for LPP, Pepco and Modivo [Jul 1]. Nuclear advanced: the Pomeranian voivode approved the second stage of site works (Do Rzeczy, [Jul 1]), though Energetyka24 notes the first plant slips later and the second starts over [Jul 1]. A Chinese EV maker picked Poland for a European launch (TVP World, [Jun 29]).

Geopolitics & Security: The Border Defence Component (KOP) returns to the eastern frontier with the 1st Podlasie Territorial Brigade (Defence24, [Jul 1]), and the president extended the military missions in Iraq and Latvia (Gazeta Prawna, [Jul 1]). The Kyiv rift persists: The Times of India reports Poland cancelled MiG-29 deliveries after a UAV technology snub [Jul 1], the FT reported the Polish opposition seeking to block Ukraine's EU bid [Jun 25], and Polish businesses pressed both capitals to end the quarrel (RTL Today, [Jun 29]).

New / Most Important: The PMI at 46.1 is the week's shock. A 2.5% CPI with a contracting factory sector is a growth problem dressed as a disinflation story, and it arrives the day fuel prices jump 60 groszy, so the NBP faces falling core demand and rising headline risk at once. The zloty's refusal to rally on good inflation says the market already treats a cut cycle as fiscally financed.  |  Playbook ✓: Cut bias intact but growth-driven; favour duration and banks over cyclicals, keep KGHM on hold while it commits 725 mln USD into a falling copper tape, and respect Orlen's 120 zł line.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: A WIG20 up 7.38% on the quarter against a PMI at 46.1; CPI at target while fuel jumps 60 groszy; mortgages cheaper and lending at records while the median wage is 7447 zł.
