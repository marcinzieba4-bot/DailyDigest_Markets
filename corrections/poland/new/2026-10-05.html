🇵🇱 Poland  Mixed  → hold, hike odds fading

Macro Stance: The hike trade is being unwound. Miesięcznik Finansowy BANK now calls a 2026 rate rise "less and less likely" [Oct 5], pb.pl says the government's CPN fuel cap "reduces the risk of a hike" [Oct 5] and Rzeczpospolita frames Nawrocki and Tusk as having "saved Poles from dearer credit", asking for how long [Oct 5]; TVN24 asks what the RPP will do at its coming decision [Oct 5]. The hawkish strand persists: FXMAG reports a "hawkish turn" by the NBP governor ahead of the meeting as eurozone inflation rises and the zloty weakens [Oct 5], after Glapiński said he would not allow above-target inflation to "become entrenched" (RadioMaryja.pl, [Oct 2]) and an RPP member expected to be "forced to tighten" (TVN24, [Oct 1]). The data behind both camps: September flash CPI 4.0% y/y, 0.7% m/m (PAP Biznes, [Sep 30]), fuel up 36.1% (Farmer.pl, [Oct 2]), core ex food and energy down (Serwis Ekonomiczny ING, [Sep 30]); Wszystko co najważniejsze warns inflation may surprise in 2027 despite weaker growth [Oct 5]. The ECB's Lane added that the energy shock "may limit the need for hikes" (Investing.com Polska, [Oct 5]), a line that eases the euro-area gravity on Polish rates. Power and gas prices for next year rose sharply (wnp.pl, [Oct 5]).

Markets: The GPW rebounded on the first day of the WATS trading system, which launched without disruption (Forex Club, [Oct 5]); the WIG20 gained 1.7% with banks recouping last week's losses (FXMAG, [Oct 5]), the WIG30 closed up 1.50% (Investing.com, [Oct 5]) and Parkiet says the indices "returned to the bull path" with a successful WATS debut [Oct 5]. Orlen, KGHM and PKO "yanked" the blue-chip index higher amid election-driven gains abroad (Bankier.pl, [Oct 5]), commodity names led (Portal Samorządowy, [Oct 5]) and PGE topped the table with LPP and Orlen strong (Inwestycje.pl, [Oct 5]). Parkiet nonetheless asks whether banks would profit from hikes "on the horizon" [Oct 5]. The card, still at Friday's close, shows what the rebound is retracing: PKO 118.24 (-5.1% 1W, +1.1% 4W), Pekao 268.7 (-4.1% 1W), Orlen 144.32 (-3.0% 1W, -8.7% 4W), LPP 24020 (-3.5% 1W, +12.6% 4W), Dino 33.66 (-6.4% 4W), against PGE 15.02 (+15.4% 1W, +19.8% 4W), CD Projekt 253.5 (+3.6% 1W, +9.3% 4W), Allegro 50.39 (+10.5% 4W) and KGHM 335.3 (+1.5% 1W). EPOL is 43.03 (-4.1% 1W, -3.4% 4W); Seeking Alpha pitches it as growth with some risks [Oct 5]. The zloty is weaker again: the card has USD/PLN at 3.8905 (+1.3% 1W, +4.9% 4W) and EUR/PLN at 4.3835 (+0.3% 1W, +1.7% 4W); today the dollar traded above 3.90 zł (CEO Magazyn, [Oct 5]) and approached 3.93 zł (Magazyn MANAGER+, [Oct 5]), the euro is at a 17-month low against the dollar (FXMAG, [Oct 5]) and FXMAG asks whether EUR/PLN revisits 4.4 [Oct 5]; Strefa Biznesu sees a chance of a rebound [Oct 5]. For POLGBs the standing reference is Parkiet's "cheap again" [Oct 1].

Fiscal & Politics: The tax package reached the Sejm: a second PIT threshold of 130 thousand zł and a new 24% rate with a 150 thousand zł threshold, plus a lower lump-sum limit (INFOR.PL, [Oct 5]); INFOR.PL also reports a floated cadastral-style levy at 0.02% of value on a first and second flat and 0.5% to 1.5% on third and further [Oct 5]. Domański dispelled doubts about Poland becoming a net payer to the EU (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 5]) even as the end of the KPO sharply shrinks the transfer surplus (Rzeczpospolita, [Oct 5]) and laid out billions for wind and nuclear to cut power prices (Bankier.pl, [Oct 5]). The 2027 draft stands at a 281.2 bn zł deficit (CEO Magazyn, [Sep 30]) with the MF and the Fiscal Council openly at odds (Rynek Zdrowia, [Oct 4]). The energy minister said the concession-fee proposal "will be changed" [Oct 5] and set Tuesday's maximum fuel prices with petrol dearer (Tysol.pl, [Oct 5]).

Corporate & Sectors: Dino unveiled a 2.5 bn zł expansion plan (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Oct 5]) after opening 256 stores in the first three quarters to reach 3283 (Investing.com Polska, [Oct 5]); LPP's Piechocki bought shares for almost 3.5 mln zł (Wiadomości Handlowe, [Oct 5]). PKO BP and BGK launched a dual-use investment loan with up to 20 years' tenor (Bankier.pl, [Oct 5]); banks are lifting margins on POLSTR mortgage promotions (Bankier.pl, [Oct 5]). KGHM's 725 mln USD Chilean build dominated coverage (Parkiet, [Oct 5]). Nuclear advanced: consent for the sea terminal for the first plant (Bankier.pl, [Oct 5]) and EDF sees "no obstacles" to a Konin project (pb.pl, [Oct 5]). Allegro Pay will appear in shops outside allegro.pl (Cashless.pl, [Oct 5]) and UOKiK suspects Google abused dominance in publisher-payment talks (marketscreener, [Oct 5]). CD Projekt says Cyberpunk 2077 is back on the sales charts (GRYOnline.pl, [Oct 5]);

Geopolitics & Security: Sikorski set out when NATO could invoke Article 5 against Russia (RBC-Ukraine, [Oct 5]), Newsweek reports the eastern flank planning a wartime footing [Oct 5] and NATO is drafting a "Theory of Victory" with AI and drones (The New Voice of Ukraine, [Oct 5]); an RCB alert put the army on full readiness (Fakt, [Oct 5]) after Putin's Kaliningrad nuclear warning (BBC, [Oct 1]).

New / Most Important: One Monday has reversed last week's narrative: the fuel cap turned the consensus from "forced to tighten" to "hike less likely", banks bounced with the WIG20 up 1.7%, and WATS went live cleanly. The zloty did not get the memo, pushing toward 3.93 per dollar, which is the price of a cap funded by a levy that PKO BP says lasts 2.5 months.
