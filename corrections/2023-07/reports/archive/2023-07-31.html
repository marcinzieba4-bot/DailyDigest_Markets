The US economy gave investors something they had spent much of the year doubting they could have together. Consumer spending rose in real terms as inflation eased. The Fed still raised rates. Intel could rally on a quarter in which its sales fell, and a company warning about its next interest payment could become one of the market’s most discussed stocks. What joined those trades was the starting expectation. Intel had to show the PC slump was easing. Tupperware needed another willing buyer before it needed another customer. Those are very different ways to make money. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Geographic Macro Overview

United States · Mixed Mixed

Macro Stance The most important detail in Friday’s inflation release was what Americans were doing while inflation fell. Real consumption rose 0.4% in June. Headline PCE inflation eased to 3.0% year over year, with core at 4.1% and up 0.2% on the month. Thursday’s advance estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 2.4% annual rate. A business planning its inventory could reasonably take heart from those numbers. A bondholder counting on an imminent rate cut still had to contend with core inflation and a Fed that had just raised its target to 5.25–5.50%. [1] [6] [2]

Rates and curve Friday’s two-year Treasury yielded 4.87% and the ten-year 3.96%. The ten-year sat 91 basis points below the two-year. Twenty Treasury observations earlier, on June 29, the short yield was the same and the ten-year was 3.85%. All 11 basis points of steepening came from the long end selling off. An equity investor interpreting that move as cheaper money would have the direction of the financing change backwards. The S&P 500 finished Friday at 4,582.23 and the Nasdaq Composite at 14,316.66. For expensive growth shares to hold those prices as long yields rise, actual earnings must take over some of the work previously done by valuation. [5] [7] [8]

Playbook Look for businesses that can keep unit volumes and gross margins together as inflation cools. A resilient consumer helps a retailer move inventory. It does much less for a company whose earnings depend on passing through another round of price increases. A weaker consumption print alongside persistent core inflation would damage both sides of that distinction. [1] [9]

Euro area · Bearish Bearish

Macro Stance The ECB announced another quarter-point increase on July 27. The deposit rate was set to reach 3.75% on August 2. The effective date matters to a treasury desk, but loan officers were already feeling the tightening. The ECB’s bank lending survey showed tighter credit standards and weaker business demand for loans. A manufacturer can pay a higher rate if its order book justifies expansion. When the same manufacturer scraps the investment altogether, the loss falls on its equipment suppliers as well as on its bank. Watch the next loan survey for businesses returning to the market and lenders willing to fund them. A rate pause by itself would leave that second condition unresolved. [10] [11]

China · Mixed Mixed

Macro Stance The headline recovery looked stronger over a year than it did over a quarter. Second-quarter GDP grew 6.3% year over year and 0.8% from the first quarter. June’s manufacturing PMI was 49.0. Over the first half, property development investment fell 7.9% and the floor area of commercial buildings sold fell 5.3%. A producer supplying export factories or unfinished developments could experience weak orders even as headline GDP accelerated against a depressed comparison. [12]

Credit and property New yuan loans reached RMB 3.05 trillion in June. A lender refinancing an existing obligation and a buyer borrowing for a newly sold home can both add to credit. They have very different implications for cement orders, developer cash receipts and consumer confidence. On July 24 the Politburo called for property policy to reflect changed conditions. The housing minister subsequently urged implementation of lower down payments and mortgage rates for eligible first-time buyers, along with delivery of presold homes. Completing a home already paid for can restore trust. A lower down payment is useful only when a household feels confident enough to sign. [13] [14] [15]

Playbook Treat policy-sensitive shares as a bet on implementation until local rules change and contracts are signed. Watch sales area and completions alongside credit volumes. Stronger borrowing while sales remain weak would help explain support for lenders without providing a reason to raise building-material volume forecasts. [12] [14]

Markets & Flows A property developer’s share price can move as soon as the probability of refinancing improves. Its contractors are paid only when financing is actually drawn and construction proceeds. A household considering an unfinished apartment faces a different decision again. A completed building lowers delivery risk, while a change in mortgage terms lowers the cost of buying. Track both before moving from a tactical position in developers to a broader position in household consumption and materials. [12] [14] [15]

Japan · Mixed Mixed

Macro Stance The Bank of Japan kept its short policy rate at −0.1% and its ten-year target around zero. The change was in how it would defend that target. The previous half-percentage-point bounds became references, with daily fixed-rate purchases offered at 1.0%. The distinction matters to a bank holding a large JGB book. The overnight funding rate can stay low while longer bonds lose value. Whether better reinvestment yields compensate depends on how quickly liabilities reprice and when bonds mature. [16]

Activity & Currency Japan’s preliminary June industrial production rose 2.0% month over month. METI published its preliminary estimate at 08:50 in Tokyo on July 31. That was genuine evidence of stronger factory activity. It gave exporters a useful counterweight to uncertainty over the yield curve. Watch whether orders sustain the rise before assuming that one month of production translates into a durable increase in profits. [17] [16]

Australia · Mixed Mixed

Macro Stance The RBA held its cash rate at 4.10% early in July. Later that month, the statistics bureau reported consumer prices up 0.8% in the June quarter and 6.0% over a year. June retail turnover fell 0.8% even in nominal terms. A retailer usually hopes an end-of-financial-year promotion will bring enough extra customers through the door to pay for the discount. A fall in receipts makes the margin question harder. The pause gave the bank time to see how much tightening was still moving through household budgets. Another soft retail reading would strengthen that argument, particularly if quarterly inflation slowed again. [18] [19] [20]

Poland · Mixed Mixed

Macro Stance The NBP held its reference rate at 6.75%. June industrial production fell 1.4% year over year and inflation-adjusted retail sales fell 4.7%. A bank and a retailer could read the same interest-rate decision quite differently. Santander Bank Polska reported PLN 6.3 billion of first-half net interest income. Its deposits were up 9% year over year while gross customer receivables grew 3%. High interest income can support the current quarter while sluggish credit demand and competition for deposits weigh on the next one. [21] [22] [23] [24]

Companies and households Grupa Kęty raised its 2023 profit forecast from PLN 388 million to PLN 487 million. Its revenue forecast edged down and management expected second-half margin pressure. Friday’s shares fell 2.61%. The upgrade gave shareholders a better estimate of full-year earnings while leaving a harder question about the earnings power of the next six months. On the household side, the Senate passed the increase in the monthly child benefit from PLN 500 to PLN 800. The measure awaited the president’s signature and envisaged payments from January 2024. July shop takings could tell investors about current purchasing power. They could tell them little about spending out of a future benefit. [25] [26] [27]

Security and markets The WIG20 finished Friday at 2,189.24. On Saturday, Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki said Wagner personnel in Belarus had moved towards the Suwałki corridor. A statement about a movement on the Belarusian side establishes a political and security concern. A forecast for a Polish freight operator requires evidence about delays, restrictions or actual routes. For domestic equities, track margins and real activity first and identify which firms have direct border exposure before assigning an earnings cost. [28] [29]

Playbook ✓ For a Polish bank, compare growth in customer balances with growth in loans and the spread earned between them. For an industrial exporter such as Kęty, test management’s profit increase against its view of future margins and the shares’ response. This gives a portfolio two separate ways to be wrong: domestic funding costs can catch up with lending yields even as European demand reduces margins for manufacturers. Concentrating on those company-specific tests keeps the border story in proportion. [24] [25] [26] [29]

Sector Deep Dive

Technology · XLK Bullish

Narrative Microsoft’s Azure and other cloud services grew 26% year over year, or 27% in constant currency. That shows customers still bought more computing capacity while companies scrutinised their budgets. For the AI trade the harder question is how much extra capacity customers will pay for once initial trials end. Cloud growth bundles established workloads and new products together. A better signal would be sustained growth alongside evidence that incremental sales cover the cost of new infrastructure. [30]

EPS Upside Watch For Microsoft, compare the growth of the cloud franchise with the investment needed to serve it. A new workload can add revenue quickly, while an equipment order commits cash irrespective of the utilization it eventually achieves. Strong results at a hardware vendor and healthy returns at its customer are different propositions. [30] [31]

Communication services · XLC Bullish

Narrative Meta delivered 34% more ad impressions than a year earlier while the average price per ad fell 16%. More inventory and better distribution can keep revenue growing while advertisers still pay less for each placement. If pricing stabilises while impression growth persists, there is room for operating leverage. If volume growth slows first, the same figures reveal how much recovery the stock already demands. [32]

EPS Upside Watch The useful comparison is Alphabet’s recovery across Search and YouTube and Meta’s need for additional impressions to offset lower pricing. If ad budgets recover broadly, pricing and volume can reinforce each other. If inventory grows faster than demand, the platform is doing more delivery work for each additional dollar. [32] [33]

Financials · XLF Mixed

Narrative U.S. Bancorp’s quarter-end deposits grew 3.2% sequentially while its average deposits fell 2.6%. Taxable-equivalent net interest income fell 4.7% from the prior quarter. The bank could enter July with more deposits and still have earned less on the funding it carried through spring. For the next quarter, follow average balances and what it costs to retain them. A stabilising account base may take time to repair the spread on a loan book. [34]

EPS Upside Watch A bank that prices deposits aggressively can protect its funding while undermining current net interest income. A bank that refuses to compete can appear temporarily more profitable before deposits leave. Look for stable balances, pricing and loan demand together when choosing among banks. [34] [11]

Energy · XLE Mixed

Narrative WTI settled at $80.58 and Brent at $84.99 on Friday, the fifth straight weekly advance. Chevron nevertheless reported second-quarter earnings of $6.0 billion against $11.6 billion a year earlier. The crude screen shows the marginal price today. The earnings release reflects volumes and realised prices over an entire quarter against an unusually profitable comparison. A bullish case needs prices to persist long enough to reach cash flow. A five-week rally and a year-over-year profit decline can comfortably coexist. [35] [36]

EPS Upside Watch Compare cash available after investment with distributions and distinguish last quarter’s realized oil prices from the latest front-month oil move. A company with rising production can still see earnings fall against an unusually profitable prior period. [35] [36]

Industrials · XLI Bullish

Narrative GE booked $8.3 billion of renewable-energy orders in the quarter. A large turbine order provides work, but signing it also locks in obligations for materials, labour, transport and performance. Track the conversion of those contracts into margin and cash. Aerospace service demand has a different clock, tied to engines returning for work. Looking at the two businesses separately gives a clearer earnings signal than treating a bigger group order book as cash already in hand. [37]

EPS Upside Watch Aircraft service revenue depends on the installed fleet and maintenance schedule. New equipment orders require materials and often long production lead times. A rising backlog can be favorable for demand and unfavorable for near-term working capital at the same time. [37]

Consumer discretionary · XLY Mixed

Narrative Ford Pro earned $2.4 billion of quarterly EBIT, while the Model e electric-vehicle business lost about $1.1 billion. The commercial division can help finance a ramp that still loses money. That transfer matters to a Ford shareholder in a way the industry’s EV delivery growth alone cannot capture. Watch whether improving electric-model volumes lower the loss per vehicle without requiring further discounts. A healthy commercial order book buys time to find out. [38]

EPS Upside Watch Ford’s commercial and electric businesses face different customers and different cost absorption. An order from a fleet operator reflects a vehicle’s operating economics. A retail EV promotion may bring volumes forward while lowering cash generated per car. Evaluate both before projecting revenue growth into cash flow. [38]

Consumer staples · XLP Mixed

Narrative Procter & Gamble’s 8% organic sales growth included seven percentage points from pricing, two from mix and a one-point volume decline. A household continuing to buy detergent at a higher price protects revenue. Buying a little less with each shopping trip eventually matters to factories and retailers. As inflation cools, compare unit volumes with the slowing price contribution. Flat prices with recovering units would be a stronger sign of underlying demand than another quarter of price-led nominal growth. [9]

EPS Upside Watch P&G’s mix benefit leaves a specific next-quarter question. If consumers switch toward cheaper formats as pricing moderates, reported organic sales can slow even if the physical quantity bought recovers. Look for volume improving together with stable profitability. [9]

Healthcare · XLV Mixed

Narrative Roche’s diagnostics division is a useful read-through for test and equipment suppliers. Its first-half sales fell 23% at constant exchange rates while sales excluding COVID products rose 6%. The headline fall combines a disappearing pandemic market with growth elsewhere. For peer comparisons, ask how much of the extraordinary test base remains in the prior-year numbers and whether routine diagnostics can support the installed equipment. [39]

EPS Upside Watch Roche’s diagnostics comparison includes a change in demand for COVID-related products. Separating routine testing helps distinguish a falling comparison from weakening laboratory utilization. A sustained advance in underlying diagnostics would matter more to forward margins than eliminating a difficult historical comparison. [39]

Materials · XLB Bearish

Narrative Dow reported sales of $11.4 billion, down 27% year over year. Local prices fell 18% and volumes fell 8%. Cheaper energy or feedstock can improve a producer’s spread, but operating a chemical plant below capacity still spreads fixed costs over fewer tonnes. For a turn in the cycle, watch shipment volumes and the difference between product and input prices together. A rebound driven solely by input inflation gives shareholders a very different margin outlook. [40]

EPS Upside Watch Dow has already reported weakness in both selling prices and volumes. Higher quoted commodity prices can precede restocking, but a chemical plant earns its return on the spread after feedstock, freight and utilization costs. Use actual shipments to confirm a turn. [40]

Real estate · XLRE Mixed

Narrative Prologis reported 97.5% average occupancy and a 48.1% cash rent increase on leases that commenced in the quarter. That increase describes the leases turning over. Existing contracts elsewhere in the portfolio still reset on their own schedules. As long yields rose, the offset for a warehouse owner was the ability to renew leases at higher rents. New leasing activity, occupancy and the cost of the next refinancing tell investors how much of the headline uplift accrues to equity. [41] [5]

EPS Upside Watch An industrial landlord has several years of rents set at different points in the cycle. The increase on leases signed this quarter is useful evidence of pricing power on that fraction of the portfolio. Vacancy at the next lease expiry is the downside that the headline spread leaves out. [41]

Utilities · XLU Mixed

Narrative NextEra’s adjusted earnings reached $0.88 per share against $0.81 a year earlier. Its renewables and storage backlog added roughly 1,665 MW since the previous earnings update. The margin between a project’s contracted revenue and its financing and construction costs matters more than the nameplate capacity. If financing costs stay high, investors should look for projects reaching service on time and at the economics assumed when contracts were signed. [42]

EPS Upside Watch A signed project adds potential earnings over its future life. Higher financing costs and construction delays can reduce that return before a turbine or battery enters service. Compare contracted economics with realized delivery rather than treating all megawatts as interchangeable. [42] [5]

Pricing and order mix are the near-term evidence for Mercedes. Delivery milestones and contractual progress are the better tests for the partnership. [43] [44]

Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Earnings Pattern Mixed

What the market rewarded By July 28, 51% of S&P 500 companies had reported. Four in five beat EPS estimates, but the aggregate surprise was 5.9%, below FactSet’s five-year average of 8.4%. Its blended estimate still put earnings 7.3% below the prior year. Forecasts had fallen far enough that a company could clear the hurdle while reporting less profit than last summer. Intel illustrates the trade. It closed Friday at $36.83, up 6.6%, after delivering positive adjusted EPS on revenue down 15% year over year. An investor buying that reaction was paying for a change in the direction of expectations. Sustained earnings growth still had to follow. [46] [3] [47]

What investors could distinguish Alphabet’s Cloud business earned $395 million in operating income on $8.031 billion of revenue, after an operating loss of $590 million a year earlier. Unlike a press release about generative AI, a change in reported segment profit can be followed through to group earnings. Even here the source of improvement matters. Better use of existing capacity can lift margins until another round of investment is needed. P&G faced the opposite question. How much of the margin it gained from pricing would it retain as inflation eased and customers reconsidered quantities? The common discipline is to identify which reported number could repeat and which benefited from an unusually low prior comparison. [33] [9]

Into the next reports Ahead of AMD, distinguish server demand from the PC inventory cycle. Ahead of Apple, compare the services contribution with demand for replacement hardware. A rally on better-than-feared sales is vulnerable if the next quarter still requires discounts or heavy investment to sustain them. [48] [31] [30]

Crypto & Digital Assets

Crypto Mixed

Market Context The July 13 Ripple ruling divided transactions in a way the market had initially compressed into a single verdict. The court found Ripple’s institutional sales constituted investment contracts and reached a different conclusion about its programmatic sales on the record before it. It expressly left ordinary secondary-market transactions outside the question it decided. Coinbase shareholders gained a narrower commercial opening. A relisted asset can generate fees when it changes hands. How long it does so depends on activity and the venue that captures it. [49] [50] [51]

New / Most Important CoinShares’ July 24 report added a useful check. Bitcoin investment products had $13 million of outflows for the week covered, short-bitcoin products also saw outflows, and Ether products took in $6.6 million. Both sides of a directional trade can shrink together when fund investors reduce exposure. Those subscriptions and redemptions describe a particular product universe. An investor interested in Coinbase’s revenue needs trading volume, market share and fees as well. Over the weekend Curve reported that several stable pools were affected by an incident. For holders relying on those pools to exit a position, available liquidity became an immediate question even if the broad crypto price held steady. [52] [53]

Playbook In a disruption to a lending or swap pool, the first risk is the ability to transact at the displayed price. Fees, slippage and the reliability of a particular route can matter before a broad market index signals trouble. Where a position depends on liquidity in an affected pool, verify the exit route independently of a token’s last screen price. [53]

Capital Flows & Liquidity Framework

Capital Flows and Liquidity Mixed

Where the cash went Lipper recorded $35.5 billion of net inflows across its US fund universe in the week ended July 26. Money-market funds took in $28.8 billion, equity funds $3.2 billion and taxable bond funds $2.9 billion. A week in which equity prices rise and investors add to equities can also be a week in which the much larger cash allocation earns a competitive return. It would be a mistake to treat the entire balance in money funds as a pending equity order. Some holders need cash for expenses, some have short investment horizons and some may prefer the available yield. [54] [2]

What retail added Reuters reported Vanda’s estimate of roughly $1.27 billion a day in US retail equity inflows month to date as of July 25. The same report identified an eleven-month high in retail net purchases of AMC on July 24. The broad estimate describes a category of investor, the AMC number one security and day, and Lipper the fund vehicles in its sample. Adding them together would count unlike things and could double-count transactions. The implication is narrower and more interesting. Cash accumulation, diversified equity demand and a concentrated bet on one distressed company all existed at the same time. [55] [54]

What price alone missed ETF.com recorded $125.2 million of net redemptions from the oil-and-gas equity ETF XOP on July 27. Crude finished its fifth rising week on Friday. An investor watching only the futures contract could tell that the oil price had risen. The fund data show that some capital was leaving a related vehicle during that rise. For banks, the same distinction shows up in deposits and margins. Follow both the amount of funding and what the bank paid for it. [56] [35] [34]

Portfolio Implication The combination of money-market inflows and equity subscriptions argues for keeping the use of cash explicit. A household can move idle balances into a higher-yielding vehicle at the same time as another investor buys equities. A fund receiving subscriptions can invest in shares already held by the household changing products. The direction of any one flow series needs a defined investor, vehicle and reporting period before it can justify a change in portfolio risk. [54] [55] [56]

Tupperware / TUP — A squeeze needs a buyer after the shorts cover

Why Retail Is Here The week's numbers make it easy to see the attraction. Tupperware closed at $0.90 on July 21 and $3.08 on July 28, a gain of about 242%. A familiar brand and a low nominal share price made the story easy to circulate, while heavy existing short interest offered a plausible reason it might go further. The ordinary investor buying at Friday's open took an entirely different ride. A $3.53 purchase was down nearly a quarter at the day's $2.67 low and still down almost 13% at the close. Someone can describe a winning stock and accurately describe a losing investment in the same sentence. [61] [4]

What Is Driving It Two forms of scarcity are being confused. A high borrow fee makes it expensive to maintain some short positions. Fintel's indicated fee reached 75.49% on Thursday, an annualized reading that can change quickly and varies with availability. Meanwhile the operating company needs cash. In a July 7 SEC filing it reported a limited lender waiver and deferred principal payments, but still forecast insufficient liquidity for its July interest payment. A higher secondary-market share price does not put the proceeds of someone else's stock sale into Tupperware's bank account. A new financing would have to specify who supplies money, on what terms and where common shareholders stand afterward. [60] [59]

The Social Signal Each retail measure answers a different question here. Reuters reported that Tupperware was among the most watched names on Stocktwits on Thursday with extremely bullish sentiment. CNN documented individual traders discussing the squeeze. Friday's Fidelity sample ranked TUP sixteenth by order count, with 1,614 buys and 1,676 sells. The social readings establish strong attention; the broker's actual entered orders were closely split. Order size, executions and activity elsewhere are unavailable. An enthusiastic holder posting repeatedly and a profitable holder selling a large block can coexist without either source being wrong. [4] [63] [62]

Historical Analog Bed Bath & Beyond in summer 2022 is a comparison the participants themselves were making. CNN quoted a Reddit user describing an earlier BBBY trade as the reason a small, consciously speculative Tupperware position appealed. The transferable mechanism is a limited cash stake pursuing a rapidly expanding price move in a distressed household name, with existing shorts potentially forced to buy. The financing structures and shareholder recoveries need separate analysis. A trader's prior success or willingness to lose a fixed stake offers no estimate of the remaining value of a different company's common equity. [63] [4] [59]

Smart Money View Libra Investment Services warned investors of a high risk of loss, according to CNN. That is a professional risk assessment. It includes no disclosed hedge fund short position. The creditor terms carry more weight than speculation about who is on the other side. Lenders granted limited deferrals and imposed conditions on the use of proceeds from an asset sale. Those facts show negotiations under constraint. They do not establish that the forecast missed interest payment actually occurred. Friday's 109.8 million shares traded were still extraordinary, but almost 48% below Thursday's 209.1 million. A new long now needs continued demand with less turnover behind it. [63] [59] [61]

When the Narrative Breaks Thursday's $3.75 high remains unreclaimed; Friday reached $3.57. Thursday's $2.97 close was breached intraday Friday and recovered by the bell.
