## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The government’s first estimate put June payroll growth at 209,000 and unemployment at 3.6%. Employers added jobs in government, health care, social assistance and construction. The breadth is different from a blanket vote of confidence in goods demand. It also shows exactly why Thursday’s much larger ADP figure could not be carried straight into a forecast for the official count. The labour market is adding income even while factories report softer conditions. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/adp-national-employment-report-private-sector-employment-increased-by-497-000-jobs-in-june-annual-pay-was-up-6-4-301871086.html?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-text-block>) [⁽4⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>)

**Rates & Curve** At the 2023-07-07 close, the two-year Treasury yielded 4.94% and the ten-year 4.06%. The 10-year minus 2-year spread was -88 bps. Over twenty completed observations from 2023-06-07, the two-year moved +38 bps, the ten-year +27 bps and the spread -11 bps. [⁽5⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value_month=202307&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve>) The S&P 500 closed at 4,398.95 points and the VIX at 14.83 points. [⁽6⁾](<https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/SP500>) [⁽7⁾](<https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/VIXCLS>) The front-end yield eased after the official jobs report, but the long yield remained above 4%. That is a different backdrop from broad relief across the curve. In a deeply inverted curve, rolling short exposure and buying long bonds are separate decisions. The first collects the rate available today. The second commits to a view about where policy and inflation will settle. An equity with cash flows a long way out implicitly asks the investor to make that second decision as well.

**New / Most Important** Nasdaq announced a special rebalance of the Nasdaq-100 before the July 24 open to address concentration. This creates a mechanical reason to trade an index constituent without changing a single profit forecast. Investors following reported volume should establish whether the trade changes the size of an indexed position or expresses a new view on the business. [⁽8⁾](<https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nasdaq-100-index-special-rebalance-be-effective-july-24-2023>)

**Playbook ✓** Maintain exposure to profitable US businesses with evidence of demand. Let forthcoming inflation figures determine the duration overlay. A quiet payroll headline with strong wage pressure would be a poor basis for treating a long-duration stock as a bond substitute. The first sign to review exposure is a change in operating margin while volumes hold up. That separates financing pressure from a collapse in end demand.

### Euro area · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The market’s growth debate is becoming geographical. American payrolls still rose by 209,000. The euro-area composite survey for June signalled contraction. A global exporter can still do well into US end demand if it can finance the order in Europe. A domestic cyclical whose order book and bank both sit in the euro area faces a harder comparison. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/euro-zone-business-activity-contracts-in-june-pmi-3119627>)

**Activity & Inflation** A composite reading below 50 makes the financing question more immediate. A company can reduce purchasing to preserve cash and make its suppliers’ order books look worse before final consumption has deteriorated by the same amount. Prefer the supplier paid as the goods move over one financing a long production schedule. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/euro-zone-business-activity-contracts-in-june-pmi-3119627>)

**New / Most Important** Strong US services employment gives an internationally diversified European company a possible offset to weaker home demand. It also makes short-dollar financing less likely to become cheap quickly. Inspect which subsidiaries earn dollars and which borrow them before labelling the position a transatlantic hedge. [⁽10⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-53-9-june-2023-services-ism-report-on-business-301870344.html>) [⁽11⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/press_conference/monetary-policy-statement/2023/html/ecb.is230615~3de9d68335.en.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Favour the company that can defend its spread after paying for working capital. Reduce exposure to a leveraged supplier when a rising share of orders is financed by the supplier itself. An improvement in repeat bookings and shorter collection periods would justify revisiting it.

### China · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The fine on Ant puts a concrete number on one part of Chinese fintech’s regulatory burden. It can make the remaining range of outcomes easier to value. It does not create new borrowers, improve a developer’s cash flow or raise the fee earned on every transaction. Platform franchises and the property sector therefore require different reasons to own them. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/china-fines-ant-71-billion-yuan-3121581>) [⁽13⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230615_1940629.html>)

**PMIs & Activity** The June official factory index was 49.0 points and the non-manufacturing index was 53.2. A diffusion index describes how many firms see change in one direction. It cannot tell an investor the size of each company’s change. A large state-linked project and a small private factory therefore deserve separate treatment. Use hard orders and shipments to decide if the improvement in survey direction is reaching cash. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230630_1940935.html>)

**Credit Impulse** June’s one-year loan prime rate was reduced to 3.55% and the five-year rate to 4.20%. The first has greater relevance to shorter corporate funding and the second to mortgage pricing. A lower benchmark relieves some interest costs. Only a household applying for a loan or a company drawing down funds changes the volume of credit supporting activity. [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202306/20/content_WS64919b92c6d0868f4e8dd089.html>)

**Property** January–May real estate development investment fell 7.2% versus a year earlier. Construction requires cash before completed units can be handed over. If buyers delay deposits, a developer can stop starting new projects while still needing to finance the units buyers have already paid for. The commercial distinction is between an announcement about completions and a genuine turn in new projects, which would affect a different group of suppliers. [⁽13⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230615_1940629.html>)

**Fiscal, Policy & Trade** Licences for specified gallium and germanium products introduce an administrative hurdle at the point of export. A buyer with stocks or a qualified alternative may be able to continue production. Another with tight inventories may have to finance a costly buffer. The market implication is dispersion among manufacturers even inside the same chip supply chain. The actual duration of licensing and shipment delays is what changes cash flow. [⁽16⁾](<https://finance.yahoo.com/news/2-china-restrict-exports-chipmaking-115405155.html>)

**Markets & Flows** Credit terms describe what a borrower could pay if it took a loan. Equity prices describe the changing value of outstanding shares. Neither by itself measures the volume of new bank lending or money entering an investment fund. Stronger evidence for domestic recovery would be a sequence of better orders, purchases financed on ordinary terms and more completed sales, observable across a borrower and its suppliers.

**New / Most Important** The fine on Ant gives a price to one specific regulatory issue. Valuing an online financial platform still requires an assumption about repeat customers, fee rates and the capital needed to serve them. Relief for an internet-platform multiple does not repair a developer’s cash-conversion cycle. Distinguish the policy risk attached to the franchise from final demand for its products. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/china-fines-ant-71-billion-yuan-3121581>)

**Foreign investment and supply decisions** US Treasury Secretary Janet Yellen’s Beijing visit brought direct discussions with American businesses and Chinese officials. In her concluding remarks she distinguished supply-chain diversification and targeted national-security measures from a broad separation of the economies. Improved communication can matter to an operating company that needs a timely answer about licenses, data rules or customer access. It reduces uncertainty about whom to ask. A board approving a long-lived plant still needs the actual terms, a reliable market for its output and some confidence that goods or services can move across the border after the investment is sunk. A diplomatic meeting alone supplies none of those contractual protections. [⁽17⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1593>) [⁽18⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1603>)

**Mapping the bottleneck** AXT’s decision to seek permits for exports from its Chinese manufacturing business illustrates the narrow question executives face. A semiconductor substrate can be made on schedule, at the expected cost, and still reach a foreign customer late. Work backwards from the required delivery date: qualification of an alternative supplier, the customer’s inventory buffer, application processing and the ability to finance stock during a delay all matter. A credible improvement would be a clear process with predictable outcomes. Until then a supplier with a diversified customer base and a buyer with more than one qualified source may have quite different exposures to the same rule. [⁽19⁾](<https://finance.yahoo.com/news/us-firm-axt-applying-permits-030110355.html>) [⁽20⁾](<https://tech.yahoo.com/business/articles/china-restrict-exports-chipmaking-materials-010515176.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Favour businesses whose earnings can improve with repeat use and limited new investment. Add exposure to the building supply chain only when orders tied to new projects recover alongside collections. ⚠ Divergence: a better service survey can support consumer franchises while lower property investment continues to restrain suppliers of industrial inputs. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230630_1940935.html>) [⁽13⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230615_1940629.html>)

### Japan · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The next question for exporters is whether US hiring in services and construction leads to orders for Japanese goods. A strong headline payroll number need not turn into demand for machinery or memory chips. Samsung’s preliminary quarterly operating profit gives a sobering external check on the idea that technology is recovering at the same pace as leisure or domestic services. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>) [⁽21⁾](<https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-earnings-guidance-for-second-quarter-2023>) [⁽22⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/statistics/tk/gaiyo/2021/tka2306.pdf>)

**Activity & Currency** The Bank of Japan’s June decision retained its short-rate policy and yield-curve framework. An exporter can show a gain from translating foreign revenues even if the number of units sold is unchanged. A domestic service business faces a different equation, with local demand and wages more important to the next adjustment in margin. Investors should compare the currency on the revenue line with the currency on the purchase orders. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/mpr_2023/k230616a.pdf>)

**Playbook ✓** Prefer Japanese domestic service businesses with repeat bookings and room to pass on wage costs. Add exporters when unit orders and realised margins confirm that translation gains are funding durable cash generation. A rebound in factory confidence without improvement in orders warrants patience. [⁽22⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/statistics/tk/gaiyo/2021/tka2306.pdf>)

### Australia · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The RBA’s policy pause and China’s weak factory survey remain the most direct tests for Australian cyclicals. US hiring can help a globally diversified company. Its effect on the ability of a local household to service a mortgage is indirect. Bank disclosures on deposit pricing and loan quality are more revealing for a domestic financial than broad US equity strength. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230630_1940935.html>) [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>)

**Demand & Financing** The price basket identifies housing and food among the main drivers of the annual rise. Those costs compete directly with the next discretionary purchase. In a mortgage book, higher repayments can stress a borrower well before a missed payment appears in an arrears statistic. In a retail account, the same pressure may first appear as smaller average baskets or substitution toward less expensive brands. [⁽25⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation/monthly-consumer-price-index-indicator/may-2023>)

**Playbook ✓** Prefer cash-generative businesses with control over inventory and funding. Revisit household cyclicals when selling volumes improve alongside easing living costs. For exporters, require evidence about the overseas buyer’s orders, since a stronger commodity quotation alone cannot tell whether its volume commitment changed.

### Poland · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The NBP held rates last week even though Polish manufacturing had weakened. CPI remains in double digits. Those conditions leave the marginal borrower facing two hurdles, the price of an existing loan and uncertainty over tomorrow’s wage or order book. The allocation question is which banks can fund a profitable new mortgage without paying away the spread to keep deposits. [⁽26⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) [⁽27⁾](<https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/PMI-Polska-czerwiec-2023-Poglebia-sie-deflacyjna-recesja-w-polskim-przemysle-8572306.html>) [⁽28⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/prices-trade/price-indices/flash-estimate-of-the-consumer-price-index-in-june-2023%2C21%2C12.html>)

**Markets** For a Polish investor the yield available on local savings, the price of a mortgage and the cost of financing stock at a distribution business need not move together. A business with euro-denominated purchases and zloty revenues also carries an exchange-rate exposure between placing its order and settling the invoice. Track the contractual hedge and liquidity needed during that interval before inferring a broad earnings benefit from currency direction.

**Fiscal & Politics** The government’s First Home programme subsidises eligible first-time buyers. What matters for a listed bank is the conversion of an inquiry into a signed loan and the cost of the deposits that support it. A buyer might substitute a subsidised new loan for one that would have been written anyway. Counting the whole principal as extra credit creation would overstate the boost to demand. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/pierwsze-mieszkanie>)

**Corporate & Sectors** Real retail sales fell 6.8% year over year in May. A shop can put through a higher price and still pay rent, utilities and staff across fewer units sold. A company with a variable-cost distribution network may manage this more readily than one with a large estate of fixed premises. For a Polish bank, the accompanying risk is the quality of the retailer’s working-capital line, distinct from the headline yield on the loan. [⁽30⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/22-06-2023.html>)

**Geopolitics & Security** The Polish and Lithuanian presidents have issued a joint declaration ahead of Vilnius. A statement about regional priorities can make future procurement more plausible. Its value to an individual business still depends on contracts, financing, delivery milestones and whether the customer is local or an allied government. Give a signed order a different weight from diplomatic intent. [⁽31⁾](<https://en.bbn.gov.pl/en/news/866%2CJoint-Declaration-of-the-Presidents-of-the-Republic-of-Poland-and-the-Republic-o.html>)

**New / Most Important** The MPC’s decision leaves the reference rate at 6.75% against 11.5% June headline inflation. An exporter cannot treat a further fall in the inflation rate as an automatic change in either the policy rate or foreign orders. Housing eligibility, deposit costs and demand from German customers sit on different decision calendars. Let these differences guide the choice among domestic equities. [⁽26⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>)

**Who benefited from the first leg of disinflation?** The Council said the June decline in annual inflation came chiefly from energy and, to a smaller extent, food and some other goods. It also noted annual declines in May retail sales, industrial output and construction output. A lower annual rate can relieve pressure on households before it rebuilds demand for discretionary purchases. For an industrial supplier, a lower energy cost improves the price it can quote, but weak factory volumes make fixed costs harder to absorb. The distinction supports a selective view of Polish equities rather than assuming the same inflation surprise lifts consumers, exporters and banks equally. [⁽26⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) [⁽32⁾](<https://www.pkobp.pl/media_files/b75df444-799b-4c82-af49-2bc4e795ab2b.pdf>)

**The mortgage funnel** June applicants requested an average PLN 382,060 according to BIK. Compare the number of people applying with the amount they request as the subsidised first-home program starts. Bigger average loans can lift the aggregate value of enquiries even if the applicant base changes little. The count is also a better first check on whether new people are entering the market, although it says nothing by itself about credit quality. A bank or developer trading on a surge in housing demand eventually needs approved financing and completed sales. Track these stages before treating a busy application system as an immediate improvement in earnings. [⁽33⁾](<https://media.bik.pl/informacje-prasowe/815354/o-26-5-wzrosla-wartosc-zapytan-o-kredyty-mieszkaniowe-w-czerwcu-2023-r>) [⁽34⁾](<https://www.gov.pl/web/rozwoj-technologia/bezpieczny-kredyt-2--zaczynamy>)

**Playbook ✓** Prefer the retailer whose real volumes and inventory turns stabilise together, or the bank whose funding mix protects its lending spread. Trim when nominal sales rise while units continue to fall and working-capital borrowing rises. ⚠ Divergence: falling consumer inflation supports household purchasing power, yet locked-in labour and finance costs can squeeze the companies selling to those same households. [⁽28⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/prices-trade/price-indices/flash-estimate-of-the-consumer-price-index-in-june-2023%2C21%2C12.html>) [⁽30⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/22-06-2023.html>)

## Sector Deep Dive

### XLK · Technology · Mixed

**Narrative** Samsung’s preliminary profit helps define how narrow any improvement in memory demand is likely to be.

**Reported** Micron reported revenue of $3.75 billion, up from $3.69 billion sequentially but well below the $8.64 billion of a year earlier. A slight improvement in shipments off a depressed comparison can coexist with an industry still losing money. It recorded a $1.90 billion GAAP net loss. [⁽35⁾](<https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2023/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2023-06-28-2023/default.aspx>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Look for a recovery in realised memory prices and utilisation alongside bit shipments. An order for a specialised component can matter for its supplier while having little effect on the margin of a producer selling into a broader oversupplied market.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A lower price for memory can improve the economics of a system assembler well before it helps the chipmaker. Allocate between those two exposures deliberately when semiconductor headlines move the entire technology basket.

### XLC · Communication Services · Mixed

**Narrative** Threads gives Meta a distribution advantage through its existing account relationships. The question an advertiser will ask is how often people return and what they are doing when they get there. [⁽36⁾](<https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/05/threads-metas-twitter-competitor-is-now-live/>)

**Reported** The launch report describes a new text service tied to Instagram. Reusing the existing connection between users can lower the friction of finding an initial audience and accelerate trial. The more difficult stage is retaining that audience once the novelty fades. [⁽36⁾](<https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/05/threads-metas-twitter-competitor-is-now-live/>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A higher rate of return visits plus evidence of stable conversion would support more valuable ad inventory. Without that combination, an additional minute online may carry little incremental revenue.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Ad buyers can benefit from competition even when platform shareholders do not. More places for a brand to reach similar people can weaken the price of an impression on every platform.

### XLF · Financials · Mixed

**Narrative** Large-bank capital and current credit demand answer different questions. A bank can withstand a severe hypothetical recession and still lose deposits to higher-paying alternatives or write fewer profitable loans this quarter.

**Reported** The Federal Reserve found that the 23 large banks examined could absorb more than $540 billion of losses in its severe scenario and continue lending. For the individual bank that result describes a cushion against a hypothetical deterioration. The actual near-term earnings depend on what the bank pays to keep deposits and what it charges on fresh loans. [⁽37⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2023-june-dodd-frank-act-stress-test-executive-summary.htm>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A slower rise in the cost of interest-bearing deposits would leave more of the asset yield for shareholders. Inspect funding costs, credit provisions and actual loan growth before using the policy rate to forecast bank earnings.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** The cash distribution can improve while the economics on the next loan worsen. A resilient capital ratio answers a solvency question. It offers less guidance on deposit competition in the next quarter.

### XLE · Energy · Mixed

**Narrative** Supply policy now competes with the factory slowdown. Saudi Arabia said it would extend its voluntary cut into August, while Russia announced a reduction in exports. The two measures affect the market through different channels and should not be counted mechanically as an immediate fall in physical consumption. [⁽38⁾](<https://www.brecorder.com/news/40250757/oil-rallies-on-saudi-and-russian-supply-cuts-for-august>)

**Reported** The June 28 EIA release showed US commercial crude inventories falling by 9.6 million barrels in the week ended June 23. Stocks can move on the timing of imports, exports and refinery runs. One large draw becomes more persuasive when product inventories and actual demand tell a consistent story. [⁽39⁾](<https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/archive/2023/2023_06_28/pdf/wpsrall.pdf>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Prefer an integrated business when its upstream production and refining margin have separate earnings support. A higher crude price can be a cost to the downstream operation if fuel prices fail to keep pace.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A disciplined producer can earn attractive cash flow on flat output. Conversely, a headline production cut can produce little equity value if cost inflation or weaker realised prices absorbs the effect.

### XLI · Industrials · Mixed

**Narrative** Manufacturing PMI of 46.0 puts a premium on the quality of a backlog. A customer can still receive machines ordered months ago while cutting the number of replacement orders it places today. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>)

**Reported** Japan’s Tankan large-manufacturer balance improved to +5. Its large non-manufacturing balance reached +23. Capital equipment suppliers have a closer link to the first group, while logistics and business services may have a closer link to the second. [⁽22⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/statistics/tk/gaiyo/2021/tka2306.pdf>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A shorter delivery schedule paired with stable order intake and good cash collections is constructive. Shorter lead times created by cancelled orders would imply the opposite for manufacturing margins.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A large backlog can become a financing burden when it fixes the sale price ahead of the cost of components. Seek evidence about collection terms and contract resets before celebrating backlog growth.

### XLY · Consumer Discretionary · Mixed

**Narrative** Demand is sensitive to what it costs a customer to use the product. An electric car, a pair of shoes and a cruise all count as discretionary purchases. Financing, markdowns and the timing of the deposit give those purchases very different implications for cash flow.

**Reported** Levi Strauss reported revenue down 9% year on year, or down 2% after adjusting for a shipment shift associated with an enterprise systems change. This is a useful example of how a shipping calendar changes comparisons without changing the consumer’s purchasing decision. [⁽40⁾](<https://investors.levistrauss.com/news/financial-news/news-details/2023/Levi-Strauss--Co.-Reports-Second-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)

**EPS Upside Watch** The earnings upside requires volume that does not depend on a deeper discount. In autos, track the realised selling price and contribution per vehicle. In apparel, watch the margin on full-price inventory and the size of promotions.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Demand can be robust at a low enough price and still leave the producer a poor investment. The right comparison is the cash and profit required to generate the incremental unit.

### XLP · Consumer Staples · Mixed

**Narrative** The distinction in staples is between restoring a margin after an input shock and retaining it after costs normalise. A business selling familiar brands can raise prices for a period while losing some unit demand to private label or smaller baskets.

**Reported** McCormick reported second-quarter sales up 8% and raised its adjusted profit outlook. In its consumer segment, pricing contributed 9% while volume fell 2%. The accounts therefore show both the power and the limit of price increases. [⁽41⁾](<https://ir.mccormick.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mccormick-reports-strong-second-quarter-performance-and-raises>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Watch the next volume comparison and how much of lower input cost is returned in promotion. A recovery in units with flat pricing would leave the margin improvement on a more sustainable footing.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A slower increase in revenue can be good news when freight costs fall and volume recovers. A high headline comparison driven entirely by price can deserve the lower multiple.

### XLV · Health Care · Bullish

**Narrative** The FDA’s conversion of Leqembi to traditional approval changes one barrier to wider use. The remaining commercial chain includes eligibility, coverage, monitoring, infusion capacity and the time a physician can devote to the patient. [⁽42⁾](<https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-converts-novel-alzheimers-disease-treatment-traditional-approval>)

**Reported** CMS said broader Medicare coverage would follow traditional approval for eligible patients treated by clinicians participating in a qualifying registry. This condition matters to the speed of reimbursed adoption even after the clinical evidence hurdle has been crossed. [⁽43⁾](<https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/statement-broader-medicare-coverage-leqembi-available-following-fda-traditional-approval>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Look for evidence that repeat services absorb the cost of clinicians, facilities and distribution. An expanded eligible population becomes recurring profit only as patients enter and stay in an economically workable treatment pathway.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** The distribution bottleneck can matter more than the number of potential patients. The early beneficiary of a complex rollout may be a provider already equipped for the service.

### XLB · Materials · Mixed

**Narrative** Licensing of certain gallium and germanium exports can cause a sharp change in the price and availability of specialist inputs without signalling stronger global manufacturing. Separate the value of possessing eligible material from the value of owning a broad mining basket. [⁽16⁾](<https://finance.yahoo.com/news/2-china-restrict-exports-chipmaking-115405155.html>)

**Reported** China’s June factory PMI stood at 49.0, while nonmanufacturing activity remained in expansion. A service-led rebound can be healthy for the economy yet require less incremental material per unit of output than a construction-led one. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230630_1940935.html>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Prioritise actual shipments, grade and customer drawdowns. A producer achieving a higher realised margin while inventory stays steady presents a cleaner case than one building stock in anticipation of stimulus.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Tighter supply of one minor metal and weaker aggregate Chinese building demand can coexist. The broad sector basket is an imprecise way to own a very particular licensing or scarcity story.

### XLRE · Real Estate · Mixed

**Narrative** The value of a lease depends on what is left after paying interest and funding the building. A rent increase can raise net operating income on the old loan and still leave less distributable cash after refinancing. Look first at the maturity schedule.

**Reported** May new-home sales were reported separately by Census and HUD. New construction can attract purchasers when owners of existing properties are reluctant to sell. More transactions for a builder do not establish higher rents or a lower capitalisation rate for a listed commercial landlord. [⁽44⁾](<https://fedprimerate.com/docs/New-Home-Sales/Fed-Prime-Rate-Housing--New-Home-Sales--May-2023.pdf>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Balance-sheet duration can produce a large earnings difference among landlords facing similar rents. Compare fixed-rate protection, the timing of maturities and actual lease renewal terms before valuing the equity off a single yield.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A landlord with limited new supply in its market may be able to protect rents even as financing costs rise. A building with attractive stated rent may be unattractive once tenant incentives, vacant space and the next debt maturity are included.

### XLU · Utilities · Mixed

**Narrative** Utilities earn through rates, allowed returns and the financing of capital investment. A bond yield repricing affects the return investors demand immediately. The regulated revenue or tariff that compensates an operator may adjust much later.

**Reported** US energy prices in the May PCE release were 13.4% below a year earlier while service prices rose 5.3%. Falling fuel cost can ease pressure on customers and bills. Its effect on shareholders depends on the fuel-adjustment rules and how quickly purchased power costs are passed through. [⁽45⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/personal-income-and-outlays-may-2023>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Seek evidence of approved cost recovery and funding secured for construction. A higher allowed return creates value only if the operator can build at a cost below the return it is permitted to earn.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Customer relief from cheaper energy can improve collections without lifting the utility’s own spread. Conversely, an attractive dividend yield gives little protection against a financing gap on a large project.

### SXAP · European Autos · Mixed

**Narrative** European manufacturers must defend price across different models, not simply sell more electric vehicles. A lower entry price may expand the addressable market but reduce the cash available to recover the cost of plants, software and new battery platforms.

**Reported** Ford’s second-quarter US sales release details the role of pickups, vans and commercial vehicles. Its product mix is a reminder that a market-share gain in one profitable category is a more relevant comparison than total industry units across different price points. [⁽46⁾](<https://media.ford.com/content/dam/fordmedia/North%20America/US/2023/07/06/Q2%202023%20Sales%20Final.pdf>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Compare orders by model, dealer inventory and the value of consumer incentives. Healthy deliveries out of an old backlog carry less information about the next quarter than a rise in fresh orders at an unchanged transaction price.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A cheaper vehicle can increase the buyer’s financing eligibility while transferring value from the manufacturer to the consumer. Volume leadership and shareholder return can diverge.

### SOXX · US–Asia Semiconductors · Mixed

**Narrative** Semiconductors have several distinct cycles. A memory producer seeks a price high enough to cover fabrication costs. A toolmaker needs a customer able to fund new capacity and permission to ship the particular system. A designer relies on demand for its finished part.

**Reported** Samsung guided to approximately KRW 60 trillion of second-quarter sales and KRW 0.6 trillion of operating profit. Those are preliminary company estimates. They strengthen the case for inspecting pricing and utilisation before assuming the memory cycle has cleared. [⁽21⁾](<https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-earnings-guidance-for-second-quarter-2023>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Look for evidence that customers are increasing spending on a type of capacity the supplier can actually service. A server build does not automatically create profitable demand for every memory product or every generation of lithography.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** The company with the most valuable installed base may collect service revenue even while new orders slow. The most sensitive share price can belong to a supplier with a narrower product mix and fewer opportunities for replacement sales.

## Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

### What the quarter is paying for

**Rewarded** Benchmark changes generate turnover without supplying evidence about demand. Separate the mechanical buyer from the customer paying the company. McCormick raised its adjusted profit outlook while holding its sales outlook. That is an earnings improvement the investor can examine in costs, volume and cash conversion, even when nominal sales growth begins to slow. [⁽41⁾](<https://ir.mccormick.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mccormick-reports-strong-second-quarter-performance-and-raises>)

**Punished** Nike’s fourth-quarter margin contraction is a more demanding comparison for brands relying on price increases. Markdown activity, product input costs and freight reduced what the company kept from the sale. An improvement in revenue without a corresponding increase in gross profit is a weaker recovery for a shareholder. [⁽47⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018723000026/q4fy23exhibit991er.htm>)

**Pattern** The important distinction is between bringing a sale forward and improving what that sale earns. A unit can be shipped to clear inventory, to meet a customer with a genuine waiting list or to fill a reseller’s warehouse. All three can raise revenue this quarter. Only repeat orders at viable economics extend the benefit into the next one.

**Attention into EPS** Look for management to reconcile reported revenue with units sold and average selling price, then trace the margin through inventory and actual cash collection. Micron’s sequential revenue rise alongside a material GAAP loss illustrates the difference between an industry passing its low point and a business earning enough to fund its own investment. [⁽35⁾](<https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2023/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2023-06-28-2023/default.aspx>)

**Playbook** Place greater weight on Levi’s distinction between reported sales and shipments shifted by the systems change. A lower reported comparison has different implications when it reflects timing rather than permanent loss of customer demand. Review the position if the timing difference fails to reverse or continued discounting absorbs the profit from recovered shipments. ## Underowned Themes — Attention and Exposure Gaps

### The operating cost of keeping inventory

**Attention Gap** The debate over nominal sales misses the capital required to protect delivery dates. A distributor holding more expensive input stock carries an interest bill between purchase and collection. The effect grows when borrowing rates are high even if the company has avoided a production delay.

**Exposure & Risk** Within XLI and XLK, favour companies with service businesses, short customer terms or contracts that reimburse procurement costs. A firm accepting a fixed-price contract while its capital needs rise is the more exposed side. The cost can surface in cash flow before the income statement.

**Trigger to Watch** Watch the relationship between sales, inventories and receivables at the next reporting round. Shorter collection periods with stable margins are positive. Faster sales funded by larger customer credit lines are less convincing.

### Services demand inside a factory slowdown

**Attention Gap** The US factory survey fell to 46.0 while China’s non-manufacturing survey stayed above 50. A service business that depends on repeat local demand offers a different earnings exposure from a provider selling capital equipment across borders. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230630_1940935.html>)

**Exposure & Risk** Consider domestic service franchises and fee-based providers before a broad industrial basket. The risk is a household squeeze that eventually reaches those services, especially where repeated discretionary purchases are easy to postpone.

**Trigger to Watch** Require returning customers or renewing contracts. A rebound caused by a one-time reopening comparison is less useful than evidence customers return after the comparison normalises.

### The bank’s deposit customer

**Attention Gap** The headline rate on a bank’s assets is transparent. The rate needed to retain a particular customer can change much faster than the price of a loan fixed months ago. Large-bank stress results provide context for resilience while leaving this everyday pricing question open. [⁽37⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2023-june-dodd-frank-act-stress-test-executive-summary.htm>)

**Exposure & Risk** Use XLF selectively to express deposit durability and funding flexibility. A high loan yield with an unstable funding base can leave less for shareholders than a lower-yielding book funded by stable customer balances.

**Trigger to Watch** At the next bank update compare the mix of non-interest-bearing deposits, interest-bearing balances and wholesale borrowing. If loan income improves only because more expensive funds replace lost deposits, reassess the earnings multiple.

### Reimbursement and delivery capacity

**Attention Gap** Traditional FDA approval for Leqembi and CMS coverage conditions make infusion capacity, the registry and patient selection part of the commercial story. A practical bottleneck can outlast the regulatory uncertainty that already attracted attention. [⁽42⁾](<https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-converts-novel-alzheimers-disease-treatment-traditional-approval>) [⁽43⁾](<https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/statement-broader-medicare-coverage-leqembi-available-following-fda-traditional-approval>)

**Exposure & Risk** Within health care, prefer businesses able to turn patient access into recurring reimbursed work without rebuilding distribution for each new service. A high level of fixed clinical staffing creates downside if the eligible population proves slow to arrive.

**Trigger to Watch** Follow reimbursement, completed visits and service-level margins. Revenue announced at a group level does less to resolve the issue than evidence one more patient can be served profitably.

## Capital Flows & Liquidity Framework

### Capital follows different clocks

**Global Liquidity** Easier terms somewhere in the system do not automatically release cash everywhere. China has lowered its benchmark lending rates while US financing remains expensive. A company with local-currency sales, a dollar credit facility and long customer-payment terms can feel pressure even if its domestic benchmark moves lower. The choice between consuming cash and drawing a line of credit shows up in working capital before it appears in sales. [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202306/20/content_WS64919b92c6d0868f4e8dd089.html>) [⁽50⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230614a.htm>)

**Rates & Dollar** At the 2023-07-07 close, the two-year Treasury yielded 4.94% and the ten-year 4.06%. The 10-year minus 2-year spread was -88 bps. Over twenty completed observations from 2023-06-07, the two-year moved +38 bps, the ten-year +27 bps and the spread -11 bps. [⁽5⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value_month=202307&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve>) The S&P 500 closed at 4,398.95 points and the VIX at 14.83 points. [⁽6⁾](<https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/SP500>) [⁽7⁾](<https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/VIXCLS>) Investors with predictable near-term liabilities can earn front-end carry without making a large bet on where rates settle. For a long bond to outperform, the decline in future yields must compensate the lower running yield and duration risk. The same funding choice appears inside equity valuations when an investor pays today for cash that may arrive much later.

**Credit Check** The Fed’s stress test examined a severe hypothetical economy and found capital resilience in the tested large banks. It did not measure a current loosening of business lending standards. An individual borrower still faces collateral demands, floating interest costs and a lender that may prefer liquidity. The operational check is whether a company can collect cash fast enough to finance the period between paying suppliers and receiving money from customers. [⁽37⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2023-june-dodd-frank-act-stress-test-executive-summary.htm>)

**Equity & Fund Flows** CoinShares’ $125 million reading covers subscriptions and redemptions in a defined universe of digital-asset products. It speaks to demand through a particular wrapper. A stock-market rally, a change in a benchmark yield or a rise in the value of fund assets cannot serve as a substitute for a measured flow. Treat shares outstanding and changes in unit value as separate events. [⁽48⁾](<https://mondovisione.com/media-and-resources/news/bullishness-continues-with-us125m-inflows-fund-flows-report-coinshares-resea-202373/>)

**Portfolio Implication** Keep liquid reserves against dates when cash is actually needed. For the equity book, compare the cash a business can generate internally with the external finance required to satisfy the next order. A company that increases unit sales by extending unusually generous customer terms may be spending liquidity to generate a headline. Reduce the weight if the order book grows more quickly than cash receipts and the credit facility becomes essential to ordinary trading.

## Anticipatory Positioning — Markets Pricing In Future Events

### Shelter the duration that actually hedges

**What Is Priced** An inverted curve supplies carry at the front and a possible growth hedge farther out. The hedge becomes dangerous if its payoff depends on the same decline in inflation that underpins a high-multiple equity book.

**Conditional Expression** Keep short government exposure for liquidity and add long-duration bonds in stages only as core price pressure and activity cool together. The distinction is between funding near-term obligations and owning sensitivity to the next change in economic expectations.

**Risk** A strong services recovery with sticky wages could raise yields on the long end even if purchasing managers in manufacturing remain downbeat.

### Protect a memory recovery against a low-margin mix

**What Is Priced** Micron’s revenue edged up sequentially, but its GAAP accounts still show a substantial net loss. A marginal order improvement is easier to achieve than full absorption of fabrication costs across the installed plant. [⁽35⁾](<https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2023/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2023-06-28-2023/default.aspx>)

**Conditional Expression** Prefer suppliers with a route from incremental shipments to realised price and operating cash. Increase the position if the next update shows both improvement in mix and discipline on inventory.

**Risk** A jump in demand for a narrow high-performance component could leave older memory inventories uncleared. A broad index position may have quite different exposure from the headline winner.

### Own a retail recovery only after the price test

**What Is Priced** Nike’s reduced margin and the Polish real retail sales decline both illustrate how active shops can coexist with fragile earnings. They describe different markets, but each forces the investor to distinguish receipts in currency from units bought at viable margins. [⁽47⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018723000026/q4fy23exhibit991er.htm>) [⁽30⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/22-06-2023.html>)

**Conditional Expression** Look for improved full-price sell-through paired with lower inventories. Pair a stronger brand with an underweight in a competitor still depending on heavy promotion when comparable units turn positive.

**Risk** Higher selling prices can cover weakening volumes for a period, while a promotion may clean up inventory at the expense of the near-term profit an investor was expecting.

### Separate the auto delivery from the margin release

**What Is Priced** Rivian’s production and deliveries are actual unit counts, while its full-year production target remains management guidance. Neither reveals the fully loaded profit on the marginal vehicle. [⁽51⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417823000033/ex-9912q23deliveryproducti.htm>)

**Conditional Expression** Use a limited position in the better-funded manufacturer, adding only when the price and unit-cost bridge supports the volume gain. Owners of suppliers should track customer schedules and contract terms independently.

**Risk** Aggressive incentives could make an impressive registration figure expensive for the manufacturer. A supplier paid by unit faces a different immediate risk from a carmaker sacrificing price to generate that unit.

It is also an unusually easy story to overstate. Reweighting changes the amount of each issuer in the index. It leaves the underlying cash flows of the businesses where they were. A trader trying to front-run purchases or sales before the weights are known is taking a view both on their size and on who has already prepared. [⁽8⁾](<https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nasdaq-100-index-special-rebalance-be-effective-july-24-2023>)

**Where It Breaks** The next check is the formal announcement. If the required changes are smaller than the positions traders have built in anticipation, a widely expected sale can cease to be a reason for further downside. Compare the stock's performance around the rebalance with peers outside the same weighting adjustment before using the move to revise an earnings outlook. An index shareholder holding throughout has a different risk from someone borrowing shares for a brief trade. [⁽8⁾](<https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nasdaq-100-index-special-rebalance-be-effective-july-24-2023>)

### Levi Strauss / LEVI — What the jeans racks are saying

**Why Retail Is Here** Levi Strauss cut its full-year outlook as weakness in the US wholesale business weighed on results. The tempting recovery trade treats a fall in orders from retailers as an inventory correction that will finish once their shelves are aligned with demand. Buying jeans directly from the company and buying them from a department store sit in different reporting channels, so improving direct sales cannot by itself establish that wholesale customers are ready to reorder. For a shareholder this is a question about the timing of cash and the prices at which surplus stock clears. [⁽40⁾](<https://investors.levistrauss.com/news/financial-news/news-details/2023/Levi-Strauss--Co.-Reports-Second-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)

**Where It Breaks** Watch whether direct sales can hold up without heavier promotion while retailers begin to replenish. Discounting may move inventory and produce a better-looking revenue comparison while compressing the profit on every pair. An upgrade to the stock based only on inventory moving out of a warehouse would be premature. A combination of stable pricing and returning wholesale orders would make the recovery more convincing. [⁽40⁾](<https://investors.levistrauss.com/news/financial-news/news-details/2023/Levi-Strauss--Co.-Reports-Second-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)
