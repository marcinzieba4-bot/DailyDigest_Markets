## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · Growth pulls the long yield back above four · Mixed

**Macro Stance** BEA’s advance estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 2.4% annual rate. Consumer spending and nonresidential fixed investment helped drive the increase. The result challenges the idea that restrictive rates must already be producing a broad decline in output. It does less for borrowers needing to refinance next quarter. A profitable business that invested several quarters ago can now deliver product and recognize revenue while a competitor contemplating its next factory faces a very different cost of capital. [⁽1⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/gross-domestic-product-second-quarter-2023-advance-estimate>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230726a.htm>)

**Rates & Curve** The two-year Treasury closed July 27 at 4.91% and the ten-year at 4.01%. The ten-year rose 15 basis points from Wednesday while the two-year rose nine, narrowing the inversion to 90 basis points. When the long yield rises faster, equities whose value rests on distant future profits lose some financing support even if near-term growth prospects improve. The move also came as investors digested reporting about a possible Japanese bond-policy change. Assigning the entire increase to the US GDP release would be too precise. [⁽2⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽4⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/nasdaq-futures-rise-after-meta-results-fed-optimism-3135559>)

**New / Most Important** Intel reported $12.9 billion of quarterly revenue, down 15% year over year, and positive GAAP earnings of $0.35 a share. A positive earnings surprise and a declining year-over-year business can coexist. The macro analogue is that reported US growth can be stronger than expected while credit-sensitive companies still have a difficult second half. Friday’s US inflation and personal spending figures remain ahead. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1637/intel-reports-second-quarter-2023-financial-results>) [⁽1⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/gross-domestic-product-second-quarter-2023-advance-estimate>) [⁽8⁾](<https://economics.bmo.com/en/publications/detail/ff2f8068-aecd-40d1-b0a0-b98461a7e2a2/>)

**Playbook ✓** Hold exposure where stronger demand translates into cash today. For a bond-sensitive growth company, test whether earnings revisions can offset a higher discount rate. A softer core price reading alongside firm real demand would be constructive. A large spending number driven entirely by prices would have a different implication. [⁽1⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/gross-domestic-product-second-quarter-2023-advance-estimate>) [⁽2⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽8⁾](<https://economics.bmo.com/en/publications/detail/ff2f8068-aecd-40d1-b0a0-b98461a7e2a2/>)

### Euro area · A new borrowing rate and an old credit problem · Bearish

**Rates & Activity** The ECB raised its key rates by 25 basis points. Its deposit facility rate will reach 3.75% on August 2. The effective date distinguishes the policy decision investors can price today from the cash rate actually paid on deposits. The bank lending survey had already recorded tighter standards and a pronounced decline in loan demand. A borrower postponing investment will not restart on favorable language alone while the rate on the financing goes up. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.mp230727~da80cfcf24.en.html>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.pr230725~8358d3939d.en.html>)

**Corporate Signal** Mercedes-Benz Cars reported a 13.5% adjusted return on sales. Its wealthy customers and model mix help it withstand financing costs that may prove harder on a mass-market vehicle buyer. High current profitability is informative about the franchise, but checking the prices of orders being signed now will matter more for the next quarter as waiting lists shorten. [⁽10⁾](<https://group.mercedes-benz.com/investors/reports-news/interim-reports/q2-2023/>) [⁽11⁾](<https://www.acea.auto/pc-registrations/new-car-registrations-17-8-in-june-battery-electric-15-1-market-share/>)

**Playbook ✓** Separate the exposure of banks, domestic borrowers and international exporters. A lender can earn more on assets already outstanding and still lose income if new issuance slows and deposit costs rise. For the broader domestic market, an improvement in new loan demand would provide a better entry signal than a single equity-market reaction to the ECB. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.mp230727~da80cfcf24.en.html>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.pr230725~8358d3939d.en.html>)

### China · An industrial bet alongside a property problem · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The Politburo’s commitment to adjust policy to changing property conditions remains material for a market worried that authorities would accept a prolonged downturn. The starting point matters. Property development investment fell 7.9% in the first half, and the floor area sold was below a year earlier. A cheaper mortgage can help someone willing to buy. It cannot resolve a purchaser’s concern that a development will not be completed. [⁽12⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>) [⁽13⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

**PMIs & Activity** The manufacturing PMI of 49.0 in June and sequential second-quarter GDP growth of 0.8% make a stronger argument for selective support than the 6.3% annual GDP comparison alone. Materials producers need customers to restock for use. An automaker can benefit from a technology agreement even while aggregate activity stays weak. The proposed Volkswagen investment in XPeng is a good example of a company-specific catalyst that is difficult to generalize. [⁽13⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/press-releases/more-e-models-for-fast-growing-e-mobility-market-in-china-vw-brand-and-audi-agree-strategic-cooperations-with-local-automakers-17497>)

**Credit Impulse** June new yuan lending reached 3.05 trillion yuan. Financing a state project, refinancing a local borrower and underwriting a household’s purchase all add to the aggregate, with different implications for private demand. Follow household sales and the cash flow of suppliers alongside the loan data before treating a higher credit figure as construction activity. [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽13⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Select companies whose next cash receipt depends on a funded project, a paying customer or a measurable product milestone. Expand the position if new demand appears at the household level. The divergence remains the distance between room for easier policy and evidence that consumers want additional debt. [⁽12⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/press-releases/more-e-models-for-fast-growing-e-mobility-market-in-china-vw-brand-and-audi-agree-strategic-cooperations-with-local-automakers-17497>) [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

### Japan · A newspaper report moves US bonds · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The Nikkei newspaper reported that the BOJ would discuss allowing greater flexibility in longer-term yields. Reuters linked the report to a rise in US yields and a reversal in US shares. A newspaper describing a policy option can change risk-taking ahead of a meeting. It cannot establish the decision. An investor short long-dated Japanese bonds is exposed to the exact form and implementation of any move, as well as its direction. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/nasdaq-futures-rise-after-meta-results-fed-optimism-3135559>) [⁽8⁾](<https://economics.bmo.com/en/publications/detail/ff2f8068-aecd-40d1-b0a0-b98461a7e2a2/>)

**Activity & Currency** Tokyo’s July headline CPI reading was 3.2% year over year, reported at 23:31 UTC Thursday. That is an early regional reading, distinct from the June national core inflation measure. It gives the BOJ another observation on local prices while leaving questions about wages and sustained nationwide inflation open. An exporter able to absorb a stronger yen and a bank able to fund a larger bond book are different ways to take Japanese risk. [⁽5⁾](<https://www.teletrade.org/analytics/market-analysis/market-news/3886953>) [⁽16⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230721_023912.pdf>)

**Playbook ✓** Wait for the formal statement before assigning any new cap or operation to the BOJ. If long yields move, compare the value of existing securities with the earning power of new loans. A sharp market move in either direction can precede that earnings improvement by several quarters. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/nasdaq-futures-rise-after-meta-results-fed-optimism-3135559>) [⁽8⁾](<https://economics.bmo.com/en/publications/detail/ff2f8068-aecd-40d1-b0a0-b98461a7e2a2/>)

### Australia · Disinflation helps, but households still owe the mortgage · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The June-quarter CPI increase of 0.8%, and 6.0% inflation over the year, give the RBA evidence that the pace is easing. The July cash rate of 4.10% still bears on household budgets. A reduced probability of further tightening does not reverse the increase in monthly payments already due. Look for evidence that volumes are stabilizing before treating a slowdown in price growth as a recovery in retail activity. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/cpi-rose-08-cent-june-2023-quarter>) [⁽18⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>)

**Demand & Financing** The labor market has offered support, with June unemployment at 3.5%. A family that keeps its income can defer a discretionary purchase without falling behind on its mortgage. That can mean better near-term bank asset quality and a difficult sales year for a discretionary retailer. The difference collapses if employment weakens materially. [⁽19⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/unemployment-rate-35-cent-june>) [⁽17⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/cpi-rose-08-cent-june-2023-quarter>)

### Poland · Kęty lifts profit guidance without lifting its sales outlook · Mixed

**Macro Stance** Grupa Kęty lifted its 2023 net profit forecast to PLN 487 million and its EBITDA forecast to PLN 810 million. The revised net profit figure is 25% higher than the previous target. Management kept its sales forecast broadly unchanged and warned of second-half margin pressure across its businesses. A raised headline can reflect a better first half or improved cost control while new demand remains difficult. Extrapolating the revision into higher second-half volumes misses the company’s own distinction. [⁽20⁾](<https://grupakety.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/2023Forecastupdate.pdf>) [⁽21⁾](<https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Grupa-Kety-podwyzsza-prognozy-na-23-zaklada-487-mln-zl-zysku-netto-i-810-mln-zl-EBITDA-8585811.html>)

**Markets and Companies** The WIG20 closed Thursday at 2,189.58. Santander’s first-half interest income of PLN 6.3 billion and the industrial guidance from Kęty give the index two very different economic exposures. In banking the issue is the spread over deposits and credit charges. In fabrication and packaging it is the amount earned per unit sold after the cost of inputs and plant utilization. A common move in the market index gives little guidance about either operating margin. [⁽22⁾](<https://countryeconomy.com/stock-exchange/poland?dr=2023-07>) [⁽23⁾](<https://media.erste.pl/informacje-prasowe/grupa-santander-bank-polska-i-polrocze-2023>) [⁽20⁾](<https://grupakety.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/2023Forecastupdate.pdf>)

**Domestic Demand** June real retail sales were down 4.7% year over year, while industrial production fell 1.4%. The NBP rate of 6.75% still places a high financing hurdle on inventory or a new production line. The operating leverage of a manufacturer can work particularly strongly in this setting. A comparatively small change in throughput alters how much depreciation, energy and labor cost each unit must absorb. [⁽24⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/21-07-2023.html>) [⁽25⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/20-07-2023.html>) [⁽26⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>)

**Playbook ✓** Buy an industrial recovery only with evidence of orders. For a bank, require evidence that interest income survives repricing of customer deposits and provisions. Watch shipping capacity and domestic grain-market pressure separately as governments respond to disruption of Black Sea exports. The divergence is upward profit guidance alongside a cautious second-half trading view. ## Sector Deep Dive

### Technology · Intel makes money on a smaller sales base · Mixed

**Reported** Intel’s $12.9 billion quarter brought positive GAAP earnings per share, yet revenue remained 15% below last year. Its outlook for next-quarter revenue of $12.9–13.9 billion makes stabilization plausible while still requiring execution. For the equity, the important comparison is future margin at the new volume level. A quarter helped by inventory restocking is less durable than a new cycle of end-user purchases. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1637/intel-reports-second-quarter-2023-financial-results>)

### Communication services · Advertising absorbs supply · Bullish

**Narrative** Meta’s greater number of impressions and lower price per ad show the volume needed to generate growth. Alphabet has also seen improvement in Search and YouTube. High nominal GDP can support advertisers’ budgets, but customers ultimately measure a sale, subscription or lead. Judge the recovery by the price advertisers will continue to pay once the platforms have added inventory. [⁽28⁾](<https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2023/Meta-Reports-Second-Quarter-2023-Results/default.aspx>) [⁽29⁾](<https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2023/q2/2023q2-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf>) [⁽1⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/gross-domestic-product-second-quarter-2023-advance-estimate>)

### Financials · A steeper curve can start with a loss · Mixed

**Narrative** The ten-year Treasury rose faster than the two-year on Thursday. Over time, lending at a higher long rate may improve returns for a lender with stable funding. Immediately, the rise reduces the market value of fixed-rate bonds and loans already held. Look for the maturity profile, funding cost and asset-quality disclosure before declaring Thursday’s steepening beneficial for all banks. [⁽2⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽30⁾](<https://www.usbank.com/about-us-bank/company-blog/article-library/us-bancorp-reports-second-quarter-2023-results.html>)

### Energy · Production and distribution policy · Mixed

**Reported** Chevron’s preliminary results provide a usable baseline of $6 billion in earnings and record quarterly Permian volumes. A rising US growth estimate can improve the demand outlook while an international producer still depends on realized global prices and downstream margins. Treat an increase in the price of oil as a scenario input rather than a substitute for the forthcoming full company accounts. [⁽31⁾](<https://www.chevron.com/newsroom/2023/q3/chevron-announces-second-quarter-2023-performance-highlights>) [⁽1⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/gross-domestic-product-second-quarter-2023-advance-estimate>)

### Industrials · Orders leave two different cash signatures · Bullish

**Reported** Boeing’s $2.6 billion of non-GAAP free cash flow reflected more deliveries and favorable receipt timing. GE raised its guidance following growth in aerospace and service demand. An engine returning for maintenance can produce recurring service revenue. A delivered aircraft releases working capital after what may have been a long period of production. Look for delivery and margin evidence in the appropriate business before treating the aerospace group as one trade. [⁽32⁾](<https://investors.boeing.com/investors/news/press-release-details/2023/Boeing-Reports-Second-Quarter-Results/default.aspx>) [⁽33⁾](<https://www.ge.com/news/press-releases/ge-announces-second-quarter-2023-results>)

### Consumer discretionary · Fleet and electric vehicles earn different margins · Mixed

**Reported** Ford’s commercial Pro unit earned $2.4 billion of EBIT on a 15% margin. The company raised its full-year adjusted EBIT outlook to $11–12 billion while electric-vehicle manufacturing remained in a costly expansion phase. A fleet customer buying uptime and service creates a different revenue stream from an EV buyer comparing promotional prices. Exposure to both makes a simple EV-volume multiple a poor guide to value. [⁽34⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38009/000003800923000044/exhibit99tojuly272023for.htm>)

### Consumer staples · Pricing power meets the next purchase · Mixed

**Narrative** Coca-Cola reported a 10% contribution from pricing and mix. That rewarded brand power, but the relevant next test is how often the customer returns at the new price. If price increases moderate and consumers buy more units, plant and distribution costs can be spread over a larger base. If quantities remain soft, revenue can slow before the margin fully recovers. [⁽35⁾](<https://www.coca-colacompany.com/media-center/coca-cola-reports-second-quarter-2023-results>) [⁽36⁾](<https://www.unilever.com/news/press-and-media/press-releases/2023/solid-first-half-performance-continued-growth-across-all-business-groups/>)

### Health care · Separate routine testing from pandemic demand · Mixed

**Reported** Roche reported growth in the base business of pharmaceuticals and diagnostics as lower demand for COVID-19 products weighed on overall results. A declining diagnostic comparison can reflect a disappearing testing program even if an ordinary hospital laboratory is running more assays. Prefer evidence on the underlying volumes and mix before interpreting a broad revenue decline as weakness in routine care. [⁽37⁾](<https://www.roche.com/investors/updates/inv-update-2023-07-27>)

### Materials · The difference between real investment and a policy mention · Bearish

**Narrative** The improvement in US nonresidential investment is constructive, yet Dow’s own quarter had falling prices and volumes. Product exposure, geography and customer inventories matter. Easier Chinese property policies could improve the outlook for selling construction products, but suppliers need to see financing reach projects and invoices get paid before the change deserves a full earnings revision. Favor occupancy and sustainable renewals over a blanket bet on falling yields. ## Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

### The headline surprise and the hurdle it sets · Mixed

**Rewarded** Intel’s bottom line moved into profit on lower year-over-year sales. Ford lifted group guidance while reporting substantially stronger commercial vehicle earnings than its EV expansion economics. In each case the existing valuation depends on which part of the result recurs. A PC inventory correction ending offers a lower hurdle than a sustained replacement cycle. Commercial fleet servicing can produce a recurring return that is invisible in a total vehicle count. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1637/intel-reports-second-quarter-2023-financial-results>) [⁽34⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38009/000003800923000044/exhibit99tojuly272023for.htm>)

**Punished** Enphase’s outlook makes the distinction between sell-in to distributors and demand for systems from households particularly important. Strong revenue recognized on shipments can precede an inventory adjustment just as the cost of financing a new installation rises. Roche offers an almost opposite comparison: lower pandemic-product revenue can obscure healthy demand for routine health care. [⁽40⁾](<https://investor.enphase.com/news-releases/news-release-details/enphase-energy-reports-financial-results-second-quarter-2023>) [⁽37⁾](<https://www.roche.com/investors/updates/inv-update-2023-07-27>)

**Playbook** Compare the relevant customer’s volume with the company’s reported sales and compare operating income with cash. An earnings improvement driven by inventory or guidance deserves a different position size from a profitable service contract already renewing. Use the next sales and margin disclosure to test the specific interpretation. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1637/intel-reports-second-quarter-2023-financial-results>) [⁽34⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38009/000003800923000044/exhibit99tojuly272023for.htm>) [⁽32⁾](<https://investors.boeing.com/investors/news/press-release-details/2023/Boeing-Reports-Second-Quarter-Results/default.aspx>)

## Underowned Themes — Attention and Exposure Gaps

### Commercial vehicles as a service franchise · Bullish

**Attention Gap** Ford Pro’s 15% EBIT margin illustrates the value of service, repair and keeping a working vehicle on the road. A fleet manager considers downtime alongside the sticker price. A useful follow-up is whether service revenue grows with the installed fleet while margins remain resilient as vehicle supply normalizes. [⁽34⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38009/000003800923000044/exhibit99tojuly272023for.htm>)

### Routine diagnostics inside a declining headline · Mixed

**Attention Gap** Roche’s COVID-related revenue decline changes the group comparison as a separate base business grows. Identify products tied to repeat clinical demand. For those products the most relevant check is whether laboratory use and new instrument placements support repeat orders for consumables. [⁽37⁾](<https://www.roche.com/investors/updates/inv-update-2023-07-27>)

### Meta Platforms / META — The calls have one day; the advertisers have a quarter

**Why Retail Is Here** A rapid recovery in a familiar consumer platform is the kind of trade that feels easy to understand. The latest evidence is real. Meta produced $32 billion of quarterly revenue and guided to $32–34.5 billion for the next quarter. The challenge for a Friday-expiry call buyer is that Thursday's market has already read the numbers. A business can meet the upper end of that range months from now without paying a cent to someone whose option falls short of its strike today. Identify which of those two investments is actually on the ticket. **What Is Driving It** The operating bridge is part audience and part auction. Across Meta's apps, advertising impressions rose 34% from a year earlier while the average price per ad fell 16%. Higher inventory can lift total revenue even as the individual ad fetches less. The next improvement with the most leverage would be advertisers paying more for access to a still-growing audience. There is a foreign-exchange element too. Meta assumed approximately a three-percentage-point tailwind to year-over-year revenue growth for the third quarter. An investor modeling demand should avoid crediting all of a favorable reported dollar comparison to the ad platform itself. The contract and its maturity show where a portion of the market concentrated its attention. Gross exchange volume includes professional and customer transactions, bought or sold, as well as hedges. It cannot establish that 307,000 calls were freshly bought by individual investors. [⁽48⁾](<https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2023/07/27/meta-stock-hits-52-week-high-after-strong-earnings>) [⁽51⁾](<https://www.forex.com/en-au/news-and-analysis/reddit-stocks-what-meme-stocks-are-trending-today-2023-07-25/>)

**Historical Analog** A familiar earnings rerating occurs when a business thought to be shrinking returns to growth. Early buyers are rewarded for having a less pessimistic forecast than the market. A $330 call bought at a positive premium needs the expiry price to exceed the strike by at least the premium to break even. An equity investor at a lower share price can enjoy a positive return even if that contract expires worthless. A model that takes this year's lower capex as a permanent cash saving while allowing future revenues to grow unimpeded is taking both sides of the timing benefit. If the whole market slows together, the appropriate revision may be to the size of the advertising pool.
