## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · Mixed

**Macro Stance** Producer prices for final demand rose just 0.1% both over the month and over the year in June. That follows a 0.2% monthly rise in the consumer price index. Easier costs create a possible margin tailwind for a distributor or manufacturer. The same numbers can mean lower realised selling prices for a producer. Delta and PepsiCo have supplied operating evidence about volumes and pricing that will matter long after the initial reaction to the price indices. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_07132023.htm>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_07122023.htm>) [⁽4⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>) [⁽5⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77476/000007747623000048/q220238-kxexhibit991.htm>)

**Rates & Curve** The US Treasury two-year yielded 4.59% and the ten-year 3.76% at the July 13 close. The ten-year less the two-year stood at −83 basis points. Both par yields fell eight basis points over twenty observations. The slope was effectively unchanged over that interval even as yields dropped sharply after the inflation releases. A company rolling near-term debt and one able to lock in longer fixed-rate borrowing face different costs even before their credit risks are taken into account. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽6⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202306>)

**New / Most Important** The two-year finished below 4.60% as the PPI release reinforced the consumer-price signal. Pricing has moved before the Federal Reserve changes policy. Banks’ forthcoming deposit and margin disclosures offer a fresh view of how much tightening households and firms already face. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_07132023.htm>) [⁽7⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>)

**Playbook ✓** A parallel fall in yields can improve the valuation applied to quite different earnings streams. Require company evidence before treating that as a durable rotation. Banks still need to show how much of recent rate increases reaches net interest income after deposit competition, and what provisions they have to take against the benefit. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>)

### European Union · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The US CPI and PPI releases have reduced one source of global bond-market pressure, but a German company’s selling price still depends on its own order pipeline. ZEW’s minus 14.7 expectations reading is a reason to seek order confirmation before treating lower market yields as a European industrial recovery. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>) [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_07132023.htm>)

**Credit & Inflation** June’s flash annual inflation estimate of 5.5% was lower than May’s 6.1%. A declining rate of inflation leaves the price level higher and does not reverse the higher borrowing costs businesses have already absorbed. The gap matters for retailers. A cash-constrained family can buy fewer goods even when the monthly increase in its grocery bill is smaller. [⁽9⁾](<https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/2995521/17075124/2-30062023-AP-EN.pdf/230735d1-74e6-4d30-2039-c9ed1e660296>) Destatis put German June inflation at 6.4% annually and 0.3% for the month. The national reading and the euro-area measure describe different baskets and geographies. Divergence between them can leave a German household paying materially higher bills while a multinational firm reports easing input expense from Asia. Read a company’s customer base and manufacturing location separately before choosing the relevant price index. [⁽10⁾](<https://www.destatis.de/EN/Press/2023/07/PE23_270_611.html>)

**New / Most Important** Mercedes reported that second-quarter deliveries rose 6% with top-end and battery-electric models contributing. The more informative question for suppliers is which platforms and regions account for the change. An increase concentrated in premium vehicles benefits different component and software suppliers from one driven by small, inexpensive vehicles. [⁽11⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/mercedes-sales-rise-in-q2-on-topend-electric-vehicles-3123004>)

**Playbook ✓** Look for pricing, order conversion and the source of financing before treating a cheaper European equity multiple as sufficient compensation. The most vulnerable supplier sells into an end market with shrinking orders and has little ability to reset prices against fixed labour or energy costs. Reassess if advance orders and actual output turn up together. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

### China · Mixed

**Macro Stance** June price data and credit flows point in different directions. Consumer prices were flat year over year and producer prices fell 5.4%. Banks can expand lending through the quarter end while nominal pricing for the companies receiving that credit remains weak. For shareholders the key distinction is between maintaining production and earning a satisfactory return on the assets that finance it. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>) [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**PMIs & Activity** Factory-gate deflation is a nominal signal. A producer may ship the same physical volume and receive less cash, or sell more tonnes while nominal revenue stagnates. Credit metrics measured in yuan and operating data measured in units can diverge. Ask whether lower input costs benefit the downstream manufacturer enough to compensate for the selling-price decline. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**Credit Impulse** New yuan loans reached RMB 3.05 trillion in June. Money supply M2 rose 11.3% year over year, while M1 increased 3.1%. The quantities indicate ample balance-sheet expansion but do not identify a marginal borrower or establish that new projects are clearing investment hurdles. Distinguish refinancing, public-sector spending and private-sector expansion before inferring industrial orders. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Property** A mortgage, a loan to finish an existing apartment and financing for a new land purchase can all raise lending. Their consequences for steel, cement, furnishings and household confidence differ. Watch presold homes handed over and transaction volumes alongside credit. A stronger banking aggregate on its own provides little basis for forecasting a developer’s sales or a European supplier’s construction orders. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Fiscal, Policy & Trade** Dollar exports fell 12.4% in June from a year earlier. That creates an obvious reason to examine external demand alongside domestic stimulus. Exports are measured in value and their year-on-year change can reflect price and product mix. A producer reporting full factories may have defended volume at the cost of margin. Read shipments against producer prices. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/chinas-june-exports-fall-124-imports-drop-68-3124753>) [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**Markets & Flows** A bank balance sheet, a foreign portfolio flow and a listed company’s earnings represent different claims. China’s lending data say much more about the first than the second. For industrial equities, demand a connection from credit to a customer’s capital expenditure and from that project to the supplier’s cash collection. The link can be long, especially when falling producer prices make additional inventory unattractive. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**New / Most Important** June trade adds an external demand constraint to the domestic price and financing story. A government able to expand domestic lending cannot by itself create final demand from overseas customers, making new orders and export margins especially important for earnings. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/chinas-june-exports-fall-124-imports-drop-68-3124753>) [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Use bank lending as an input and actual orders, home contracts and price realisation as the confirmation. A borrower able to roll debt has solved a financing problem. It may still have no economic reason to buy more machines. ⚠ Divergence: credit increased sharply in June while producer prices continued to decline. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### Japan · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The June Tankan put the business-conditions balance at plus 5 for large manufacturers and plus 23 for large nonmanufacturers. Optimism in services can support domestic revenue without turning the inventory cycle in export manufacturing. Machinery orders provide a more direct test of spending intentions than an aggregate equity multiple. [⁽15⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>)

**Activity & Currency** Core machinery orders fell 7.6% in May from the prior month, a result first reported at 23:51 UTC on July 11. A volatile month of orders can be dominated by the timing of large contracts. The cost of getting that interpretation wrong is largest at suppliers whose profit depends on running plants near capacity. Currency moves, imported costs and price discipline also influence the gain from any future volume recovery. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>) [⁽15⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Focus on companies that can distinguish order intake, backlog and deliveries in their disclosures. If another weak machinery release coincides with a deterioration in manufacturing respondents’ expectations, the capex thesis deserves a lower multiple. Stronger domestic service conditions offer a separate avenue for earnings rather than a wholesale hedge. [⁽15⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>) [⁽16⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>)

### Australia · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The Reserve Bank held its cash rate at 4.10% on July 4. A pause lets existing tightening work through mortgage payments and consumer spending. A household refinancing at a higher rate can reduce discretionary purchases even while still employed. That is a demand problem for a retailer before it becomes a credit-loss problem for the bank. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>)

**Demand & Financing** Westpac reported a rise in its consumer sentiment index from 79.2 to 81.3 in July. The improvement leaves sentiment weak in absolute terms. A recovery in a survey after the central bank pauses may reflect relief rather than a willingness to make a large purchase. Watch order values, cancellations and credit use before extrapolating the bounce to listed discretionary earnings. [⁽18⁾](<https://www.westpaciq.com.au/economics/2023/07/consumer-sentiment-july-2023>)

**Playbook ✓** Household-facing companies must preserve volume and pricing as mortgages reprice. Mining companies instead need capable customers in China and prices that cover production and project costs. An unchanged Australian rate does not repair deflation in Chinese producer prices. Analyse the two exposures independently. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>) [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### Poland · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The National Bank of Poland held its reference rate at 6.75%. A high rate changes how quickly an individual household or business can absorb a price shock. For banks, it can support interest income on repricing assets, but deposit holders seek better terms and a smaller number of creditworthy borrowers may want to take loans. [⁽19⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>)

**Markets** Focus on the transmission from financing to earnings. A bank’s interest margin measures a spread on its existing balance sheet. A manufacturer’s investment plan depends on the expected cash generated by a new project. Both face the same policy rate but the immediate benefit or cost can point in opposite directions. A change in the zloty can also help importers and hurt exporters in different proportions. [⁽19⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) The July NBP staff projection put average annual inflation at 11.9% in 2023, 5.2% in 2024 and 3.6% in 2025. These are conditional staff projections. An investor pricing government bonds against that path must consider the assumed interest-rate environment and uncertainty around food and energy prices. For a domestic retailer, the speed with which wages catch up matters at least as much as the point estimate for annual inflation. [⁽20⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Inflation-Report-%E2%80%93-July-2023.pdf>)

**Fiscal & Politics** The NBP statement cites preliminary June inflation of 11.5% year over year. The provisional figure provides a working measure of the strain on household purchasing power. Before using it to justify an earnings forecast, compare the company’s own wage and input bill with its ability to pass costs on and keep customer volumes. [⁽19⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>)

**Corporate & Sectors** Polish manufacturing PMI fell to 45.1 in June. An exporter serving German industry is exposed to customer confidence and order timing across the border. A domestic lender instead depends on payment quality, interest margins and the availability of profitable new credit. One national equity index can contain both stories and disclose little about the direction of the individual business. [⁽21⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>) [⁽8⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

**Geopolitics & Security** The Vilnius summit communiqué reaffirmed collective defence and described alliance commitments to the eastern flank. An alliance declaration matters for strategic confidence but says little by itself about the timing of an order for an individual Polish company. Distinguish a procurement contract from the longer-term case for defence expenditure. [⁽22⁾](<https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/official-texts-and-resources/official-texts/2023/07/11/vilnius-summit-communique?selectedLocale=ge>)

**New / Most Important** China’s June trade figures underline the global weakness confronting regional suppliers. A Polish industrial exporter can face soft customer demand even as lower imported goods prices gradually relieve local inflation. The gain for a retailer and the headwind for a plant need separate earnings assumptions. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/chinas-june-exports-fall-124-imports-drop-68-3124753>) [⁽21⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>)

**Playbook ✓** Track domestic price pressure, bank funding costs and industrial orders before extrapolating broad Polish market strength into domestic earnings. A reduction in inflation coupled with weak new manufacturing business could support bonds and disappoint industrial shareholders. ⚠ Divergence: policy rates remain high as June manufacturing sentiment signals contraction. [⁽19⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) [⁽21⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>)

## Sector Deep Dive

### XLK — Technology · Mixed

**Narrative** Industry attention is concentrated on data and artificial intelligence, while TSMC’s June revenue fell 11.1% year over year. Cloud services and chip fabrication occupy different points in the chain. Buying a technology index assumes much more than the proposition that one class of accelerator is scarce. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

**Reported** Alphabet and Nvidia each gained about 4.7% in the July 13 session as Google expanded Bard to additional markets. Nvidia sells capital equipment into the first stage of the buildout. Alphabet must recover growing computing expense through existing or new customer activity. A common price move therefore tells us little about the timing of cash generation at the individual companies. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>)

**EPS Upside Watch** TSMC’s NT$156.40 billion June revenue was down 11.4% from May. Monthly invoicing is noisy, but a decline of that size leaves the burden of proof on claims of a sector-wide upswing. Check several shipments and end markets before assigning the same multiple improvement to client computing and enterprise workloads. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A successful chatbot launch can make a search product more expensive to operate before the first additional advertising dollar arrives. Examine inference cost per query, the proportion of queries carrying ads and the price advertisers pay for those leads. Revenue reaches the supplier once the customer buys computing hardware; for the platform, that purchase begins a stream of costs which it must still recover. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>) [⁽23⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

### XLC — Communication Services · Mixed

**Narrative** Reuters reported more than 100 million sign-ups for Meta’s Threads in its first five days. An established social platform can prompt users to open another account at very low friction. That is valuable distribution, but it says little about repeat use or what an advertiser is prepared to pay for each visit. [⁽25⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/metas-twitter-rival-threads-overtakes-chatgpt-as-fastestgrowing-platform-3122632>)

**Reported** Alphabet rose roughly 4.7% on July 13 as Bard expanded to additional markets. Availability, account openings and initial interactions are straightforward early metrics. Commercial value also depends on the share of questions that can be monetised and the cost of answering them. Strong public curiosity need not immediately translate into higher operating profit for the search platform. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>)

**EPS Upside Watch** For an advertising platform, test user retention, advertiser demand and monetisable inventory in that order. A company can reach a large audience before it can sell sufficient advertising at attractive prices. A change in interest rates offers valuation support but cannot supply a missing advertiser. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A chatbot can expose a publisher to a fresh audience or provide enough information that the reader never visits the publisher. Search advertising, referral-dependent media and subscription content will therefore respond differently to a successful rollout. Check the advertising buyer’s cost per sale and the publisher’s traffic and subscriber acquisition costs before assigning all beneficiaries the earnings multiple of the platform. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>)

### XLF — Financials · Mixed

**Narrative** Reuters’ preview describes the market looking for higher interest income to offset sluggish dealmaking. Large banks have multiple earnings streams, with the cost of deposits and provisions by loan category particularly important to the consumer franchises. A steep EPS increase on a favourable funding spread may say little about new loan demand. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>)

**Reported** Ahead of large-bank earnings, Reuters described higher interest income competing with subdued investment banking. The Federal Reserve meanwhile put May consumer-credit growth at a 1.8% annual rate. Existing loans can reprice upwards as credit growth slows. The next question is what demand will replace maturing assets and at what deposit rate the bank must fund those new loans. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>) [⁽26⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**EPS Upside Watch** The loan/deposit spread and a bank’s underwriting discipline can both look favourable before credit losses surface. Read reserve changes alongside revolving credit and household income. Avoid equating a higher policy rate with a certain gain in shareholder return. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>) [⁽7⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** An inverted curve can work for a lender with transaction deposits that reprice slowly and hurt a lender paying current money-market rates to retain funds. Both can report higher average loan yields. Compare the funding concentration, deposit cost and share of loans approaching maturity. A smaller bank with durable customer balances may be better placed than its asset size suggests. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽7⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>)

### XLE — Energy · Mixed

**Narrative** The IEA’s July oil market report highlights the difference between exports, refinery runs and upstream production. It put Russian oil exports at 7.3 million barrels a day in June, down 600,000 barrels a day. A lower flow leaving the country can tighten particular markets without equivalent changes in global consumption. [⁽27⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

**Reported** The IEA’s July oil market report treats production, refinery throughput and exports separately. For a refiner crude is an input and the product barrel is the sale. For an independent producer it is crude that brings in revenue, with drilling and service costs below it. A tightening downstream market can give the two operations different earnings outcomes even at the same quoted oil price. [⁽27⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A producer with stable volumes and rising realised prices gains directly from tighter supply. A refiner depends instead on product prices relative to crude and the utilisation of its units. Track refining margins and the price achieved on actual cargoes before translating the direction of a futures contract into earnings. [⁽27⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** An exporter with flexibility to redirect supply may defend its selling price while a producer confined to one delivery point cannot. Look for pipeline capacity, freight costs and marketing discounts when comparing earnings estimates based on the same benchmark. A sudden loss of export volumes can tighten one region and leave another operator paying heavily to reach the customer. [⁽27⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

### XLI — Industrials · Mixed

**Narrative** Fastenal’s second quarter showed slower growth in sales and margins as demand softened. An industrial distributor sees orders close to the time customers use a product. That exposure is different from a capital-goods manufacturer recognising sales from a backlog negotiated many months earlier. Replenishment and final demand can come apart. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>)

**Reported** Fastenal’s second quarter provides evidence from small components sold into many factories. A distributor can see changes in daily orders before an equipment maker with a multiquarter backlog shows material revenue pressure. Compare the performance of established customers against growth from opening on-site operations: new locations can increase reported sales while average consumption at older sites falls. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>) [⁽29⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Delta’s record June-quarter revenue shows strength in passenger transport even as factory demand remains soft. Treat airlines and component distributors as separate earnings exposures. Their classification in industrials says little about how they respond to a purchase order or fuel shock. [⁽4⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>) [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Fasteners and other maintenance purchases can reveal customer activity but are also affected by distributor gain or loss of share. Examine established locations, frequency and size of orders, and where invoices are being collected more slowly. A quarterly gain won by converting a customer to on-site supply has a different implication for the industrial cycle from the same gain in underlying factory utilisation. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>)

### XLY — Consumer Discretionary · Mixed

**Narrative** Delta raised its full-year earnings outlook to $6–7 per share and kept its $3 billion free cash flow outlook. Consumers can protect travel plans while economising on discretionary merchandise. A strong airline result is therefore a particularly poor substitute for a retailer’s own unit figures. [⁽4⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)

**Reported** Delta raised its annual EPS outlook to $6–7 and kept its free-cash-flow outlook at $3 billion. Consumers can spend more on an eagerly awaited trip and less on discretionary goods in the same period. The airline’s bookings are evidence about a particular spending occasion; retailers still have to establish that merchandise sold without heavy promotions. [⁽4⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Watch volumes at actual transaction prices after incentives. Growth financed by a larger price concession changes operating leverage differently from growth at a stable contribution per sale. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**Rewarded** Delta announced quarterly operating revenue of $15.6 billion and lifted its full-year earnings outlook to $6–7 a share. **Playbook** Favour businesses whose price, unit volume and cash conversion can be read separately. Where the result relies on favourable mix, identify what would cause that mix to reverse. Size a position around the operating variable that actually drives the earnings revision.

## Capital Flows & Liquidity Framework

### Capital flows, liquidity and the price of duration

**Global Liquidity** Lipper’s July 13 report recorded a net $18.0 billion withdrawal across the US fund universe in the week ended July 12 even as taxable bond funds attracted $3.4 billion. Flows into one category can coexist with outflows from another. The figures do not support describing every improvement in a risk asset price as fresh money entering the system. [⁽39⁾](<https://lipperalpha.refinitiv.com/2023/07/u-s-weekly-fundflows-insight-report-taxable-bond-etfs-and-conventional-funds-attract-net-inflows-for-the-fund-flows-week/>)

**Rates & Dollar** The short Treasury maturity still offers a higher par yield than the ten-year. An investor choosing a longer bond trades away current yield in exchange for duration and a more durable rate lock. Evaluate that trade against the anticipated path of future short rates. Currency translation and hedging costs alter the calculation for non-US investors; Treasury yields alone do not establish a cross-border flow. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>)

**Credit Check** A borrower chooses whether to take another loan based on the expected cash return and the cost of servicing it. High short rates directly change the second parameter. Weekly changes in long bond prices affect collateral and valuation, but rebuilding credit demand can take longer. Check reported loan balances and loss provisions at the same time as the interest margin. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>) [⁽33⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230705a.htm>)

**Equity & Fund Flows** The July 12 week’s fund flows include both investment companies and ETFs. A positive bond price move may reflect buyers repricing a change in expected inflation; a measured inflow identifies net subscriptions across the specified vehicles. Read the two together, without summing figures from different fund universes. [⁽39⁾](<https://lipperalpha.refinitiv.com/2023/07/u-s-weekly-fundflows-insight-report-taxable-bond-etfs-and-conventional-funds-attract-net-inflows-for-the-fund-flows-week/>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_07122023.htm>) Alphabet and Nvidia each gained roughly 4.7% on July 13. One reported broader access to Bard while the other supplies processing capacity for AI applications. Shared sensitivity to the theme amplified the index response and complicates any inference about a general change in risk tolerance. Look for performance in companies with different earnings drivers before calling it a broad allocation change. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>)

**Portfolio Implication** Avoid a single risk-on label for movements in bank credit, bond yields and equity multiples. Where the thesis requires national credit to finance a real project, insist on evidence of new orders or transactions. Where it requires a securities fund to buy, seek product-level flows. Prices can change at the margin with little aggregate money changing hands. **Conditional Expression** A sustained cooling in service prices alongside continued job growth would favour businesses that can hold unit demand as the discount rate falls. A weaker inflation print produced by disappearing customer demand would carry a different earnings consequence. Assess the income statement before raising a valuation multiple simply because yields have fallen. [⁽30⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>) [⁽32⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-53-9-june-2023-services-ism-report-on-business-301870344.html>)

**Risk** If earnings expectations come down faster than market yields, a bond rally can coexist with weaker equities. Check realised activity and management guidance alongside policy pricing. [⁽33⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230705a.htm>)

### Chinese credit reaches final customers · Mixed

[⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Risk** Refinancing can absorb credit without creating construction volumes, and falling unit prices reduce nominal revenue even when physical deliveries hold up. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### The banking earnings hurdle · Mixed

## What Retail Is Playing

Coinbase has become the stock-market expression of a crypto ruling delivered in another company's case, and the distinction is central to what Thursday's buyer paid for.

### Coinbase / COIN — What the Ripple ruling really buys

**Why Retail Is Here** Thursday's market gave a crypto trader two ways to express relief. One was to buy XRP, the token at the centre of the litigation. Another was to buy a company that can earn fees when people trade it. Coinbase rose 24.5% to $107. That is an extraordinary move for a listed company in response to a ruling about somebody else's sales. The attraction is understandable. If an exchange can offer more assets and customers return to trade them, a legal development can improve both the revenue outlook and the valuation investors assign to that revenue. The magnitude of the move means the buyer is already paying for progress on both fronts. [⁽40⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/coinbase-leads-crypto-stock-gains-after-ripple-labs-legal-victory-3125598>) [⁽41⁾](<https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/SEC%20vs%20Ripple%207-13-23.pdf>)

**What Is Driving It** Judge Analisa Torres drew distinctions among how Ripple sold or distributed XRP. The court granted the SEC summary judgment on institutional sales and granted Ripple judgment on, among other things, its programmatic sales on the record before it. Coinbase said it would re-enable XRP trading. That decision gives a shareholder a tangible operating consequence to weigh against the share-price increase. It does not decide the securities treatment of every token or dispose of the SEC's separate action against Coinbase. A market maker can quote XRP again and customers can trade it, yet the exchange can still face substantial expense and uncertainty elsewhere. [⁽41⁾](<https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/SEC%20vs%20Ripple%207-13-23.pdf>) [⁽42⁾](<https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2023-07-13-coinbase-will-re-enable-trading-for-xrp-after-mixed-court-ruling-239522>) [⁽40⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/coinbase-leads-crypto-stock-gains-after-ripple-labs-legal-victory-3125598>)

**The Social Signal** The court ruling reached an audience already engaged with the stock. On Wednesday morning, before the decision, Coinbase was second in Quiver's WallStreetBets discussion ranking, behind Rivian and ahead of Robinhood. Tuesday's 9.8% rise had followed another piece of news: Cboe amended proposed spot bitcoin fund filings to include a surveillance agreement with Coinbase. A fresh high after a court judgment therefore has two plausible buyers. One expects more trading fees; the other is paying more for an exchange that might become a durable piece of financial infrastructure. The forum ranking establishes interest ahead of Thursday. It cannot tell the reader which thesis Thursday's buyers held. [⁽43⁾](<https://www.forex.com/en/news-and-analysis/reddit-stocks-what-meme-stocks-are-trending-today-2023-07-12/?amp=true>) [⁽44⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/cboe-exchange-to-partner-with-coinbase-on-bitcoin-market-surveillance-in-etf-push-3123717>)

**Historical Analog** Tuesday's move provides a recent comparison under quite different conditions. An exchange operator and ETF applicants can negotiate the terms on which information is shared, but their agreement cannot approve an investment product. Similarly, a ruling on specified transactions can change how exchanges assess a listing without providing a universal legal answer. The market has twice paid for a reduction in uncertainty with a jump in the same stock. A shareholder who treats the two developments as independent guarantees of future income risks giving the company credit twice for the same eventual return of trading customers. A better test is whether the business gains distinct, paying activities as each obstacle is addressed. [⁽44⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/cboe-exchange-to-partner-with-coinbase-on-bitcoin-market-surveillance-in-etf-push-3123717>) [⁽41⁾](<https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/SEC%20vs%20Ripple%207-13-23.pdf>)

**Smart Money View** There is an actual institutional trade to set beside the argument about professional sentiment. The Block reported on Wednesday that Ark Invest had sold 135,152 Coinbase shares on Tuesday. That sale took place before the Ripple decision. It is evidence of a holder cutting exposure into the earlier rally and says nothing about Ark's reaction to Thursday's news. The distinction is economically important. A portfolio manager with a large existing holding can sell to control position size while remaining constructive. A new buyer faces the opposite problem: deciding how much uncertain future transaction income to capitalise after the stock has already risen. Neither decision can be inferred from a manager's general enthusiasm for crypto. [⁽45⁾](<https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2023-07-12-cathie-woods-ark-invest-sold-coinbase-shares-as-stock-rallies-239084>) [⁽40⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/coinbase-leads-crypto-stock-gains-after-ripple-labs-legal-victory-3125598>)

**When the Narrative Breaks** The first price test is $107. If the stock pushes above Thursday's close on continued XRP excitement and then falls back while the token remains strong, that would weaken the case for paying a continuing exchange premium. The business test is whether turnover persists after the immediate relisting activity fades and produces fees that can be retained after costs. The legal test remains separate and specific to Coinbase. A reversal on news in that case could arrive when the equity market is closed even as tokens trade continuously. Someone who bought short-dated calls on the exchange cannot rely on a longer-lived improvement in crypto adoption to make good a premium lost in the interim. [⁽40⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/coinbase-leads-crypto-stock-gains-after-ripple-labs-legal-victory-3125598>) [⁽41⁾](<https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/SEC%20vs%20Ripple%207-13-23.pdf>) [⁽42⁾](<https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2023-07-13-coinbase-will-re-enable-trading-for-xrp-after-mixed-court-ruling-239522>)

### Nikola / NKLA — Counting trucks across five years

**Why Retail Is Here** BayoTech plans to buy up to 50 Nikola hydrogen fuel-cell trucks over five years, with the first twelve expected across 2023 and 2024. Nikola plans to buy hydrogen from BayoTech. The arrangement gives stock traders a clearer commercial story than an ambitious production target alone. A truck needs a reliable supply of fuel, and a fuel supplier needs a dependable customer. Addressing both parts with named counterparties can make deployment more plausible. The timing and the words up to matter: this announcement does not put fifty completed trucks into a single quarter's revenue. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/nikola-and-bayotech-partner-to-advance-zero-emission-hydrogen-delivery-301876318.html>)

**Where It Breaks** The first fleet deployments and the availability of fuel are practical checks on the proposition. If the first twelve slip, a long-term headline offers little support for a near-term revenue estimate. If the initial vehicles enter service and generate repeat orders, the company has a more valuable customer reference. A short-dated stock option can expire long before either question is settled, even if traders continue to pay attention to the agreement. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/nikola-and-bayotech-partner-to-advance-zero-emission-hydrogen-delivery-301876318.html>)

### Delta Air Lines / DAL — How much of summer survives September?

**Why Retail Is Here** Delta reported strong June-quarter earnings and lifted its full-year adjusted earnings outlook to $6–$7 per share. The travel trade is easy to recognise at an airport this summer. The harder question for a shareholder is how much each additional seat earns once capacity grows and some of the urgency to book a holiday fades. Delta expects September-quarter revenue 11%–14% above a year earlier on capacity growth of 16%. That combination offers a forward test of yields and mix instead of simply assuming the strength of one holiday season continues at the same rate. [⁽4⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)

**Where It Breaks** Watch demand for higher-priced cabins, realised fares and the expense of operating additional flights. Filling another aircraft may increase revenue while doing less for profit if the incremental passengers require discounts. A stock that fails to hold gains after a higher earnings outlook would signal that investors had already paid for some of the upside. For a fresh long, a stronger confirmation would be stable pricing as the new capacity enters service. [⁽4⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)

### Activision Blizzard / ATVI — The calendar comes back into play

**Why Retail Is Here** The FTC is appealing, reopening the timing question behind the cash spread to Microsoft's $95 offer. The agency's action comes after the district judge declined to grant a preliminary injunction. A shareholder considering the residual spread must now value the possibility that a further court order or a continuing UK process delays payment beyond the existing July 18 date. Those are significant differences for an options position even if the parties keep pressing ahead. Reaching the calendar deadline is not itself the event that places cash in the account. [⁽47⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-appeals-court-opens-docket-on-ftc-effort-to-overturn-loss-in-fight-against-microsoft-plan-to-buy-activision-3125460>) [⁽48⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/business/story/2023-07-11/microsoft-wins-u-s-court-nod-to-buy-activision-in-loss-for-ftc>)

**Where It Breaks** Follow the actual orders from the appeals court and any action by the parties on timing. If timetable uncertainty grows while the stock holds close to the offer, the compensation for the remaining risk has become thin. If a spread widens, the appropriate question is whether closing became less probable or merely more distant. A trader cannot recover the value of an expired call by being right about a later completion. [⁽47⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-appeals-court-opens-docket-on-ftc-effort-to-overturn-loss-in-fight-against-microsoft-plan-to-buy-activision-3125460>) [⁽48⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/business/story/2023-07-11/microsoft-wins-u-s-court-nod-to-buy-activision-in-loss-for-ftc>)
