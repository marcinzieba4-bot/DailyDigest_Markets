## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · Mixed

**Macro Stance** Factories entered the second half contracting. The ISM manufacturing index fell to 46.0 in June. A purchasing manager reducing backlogs can give a supplier a better quarter of shipments and still place fewer replacement orders. That is why industrial inventory is a less comfortable source of working capital than it first appears. Investors should ask which companies have already reduced purchasing commitments and which are still waiting for demand to clear the warehouse. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>)

**Rates & Curve** At the 2023-07-03 close, the two-year Treasury yielded 4.94% and the ten-year 3.86%. The 10-year minus 2-year spread was -108 bps. Over twenty completed observations from 2023-06-02, the two-year moved +44 bps, the ten-year +17 bps and the spread -27 bps. [⁽3⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value_month=202307&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve>) The S&P 500 closed at 4,455.59 points and the VIX at 13.57 points. [⁽4⁾](<https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/SP500>) [⁽5⁾](<https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/VIXCLS>) Monday’s further inversion rewards short-dated bonds while exposing holders of long-duration equities to two risks, weak volume and persistent financing costs. In a deeply inverted curve, rolling short exposure and buying long bonds are separate decisions. The first collects the rate available today. The second commits to a view about where policy and inflation will settle. An equity with cash flows a long way out implicitly asks the investor to make that second decision as well.

**New / Most Important** Rivian reported 12,640 deliveries and 13,992 vehicles produced. Its 50,000-vehicle expectation applies to production for the full year. A line moving faster matters for spreading fixed cost across vehicles. The smaller number is the more immediate check on whether stock is leaving the yard. [⁽6⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417823000033/ex-9912q23deliveryproducti.htm>)

**Playbook ✓** Use the US holiday to review average selling prices and inventory in the companies already owned. Wait for ordinary market liquidity before interpreting US earnings expectations from a thin price move. Abroad, a broader fall in new orders would strengthen the case for service revenues over factory exposure. The first sign to review exposure is a change in operating margin while volumes hold up. That separates financing pressure from a collapse in end demand.

### Euro area · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The European Council’s China conclusions called for reduced dependencies while keeping trade links. Monday’s Chinese licensing announcement gives a purchasing manager a reason to find a second source. Qualification and testing can take longer than the initial inventory buffer. The direct beneficiaries would be suppliers with qualified capacity. Simply sharing the industry label is a much thinner investment thesis. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2023/06/30/european-council-conclusions-on-china-30-june-2023/>) [⁽8⁾](<https://finance.yahoo.com/news/2-china-restrict-exports-chipmaking-115405155.html>)

**Activity & Inflation** Separating prices for energy and services is crucial to assessing the ECB. Falling energy bills lift real incomes. Labour-intensive service prices can still rise because a restaurant, hotel or transport provider has to pay wages on every additional sale. A lower headline rate therefore lifts consumer purchasing power before it necessarily lowers the bank rate. [⁽9⁾](<https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/2995521/17075124/2-30062023-AP-EN.pdf/230735d1-74e6-4d30-2039-c9ed1e660296>) [⁽10⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/key/date/2023/html/ecb.sp230627~b8694e47c8.en.html>)

**New / Most Important** The June ECB decision left a restrictive cost of credit in place. A supplier can report higher revenue because it raised prices and still need a larger credit line to finance receivables. Cash-interest coverage and the time customers take to pay are more useful early warnings than the reported growth rate alone. [⁽11⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/press_conference/monetary-policy-statement/2023/html/ecb.is230615~3de9d68335.en.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Favour the company that can defend its spread after paying for working capital. Reduce exposure to a leveraged supplier when a rising share of orders is financed by the supplier itself. An improvement in repeat bookings and shorter collection periods would justify revisiting it.

### China · Mixed

**Macro Stance** Beijing has put gallium and germanium export licensing on the table for August. The direct trade is a potential timetable change for specialist manufacturers. The industrial question is how customers respond while they wait. Extra inventories support current shipments at the cost of making orders after the deadline less representative of final demand. [⁽8⁾](<https://finance.yahoo.com/news/2-china-restrict-exports-chipmaking-115405155.html>)

**PMIs & Activity** The June official factory index was 49.0 points and the non-manufacturing index was 53.2. A diffusion index describes how many firms see change in one direction. It cannot tell an investor the size of each company’s change. A large state-linked project and a small private factory therefore deserve separate treatment. Use hard orders and shipments to decide if the improvement in survey direction is reaching cash. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230630_1940935.html>)

**Credit Impulse** June’s one-year loan prime rate was reduced to 3.55% and the five-year rate to 4.20%. The first has greater relevance to shorter corporate funding and the second to mortgage pricing. A lower benchmark relieves some interest costs. Only a household applying for a loan or a company drawing down funds changes the volume of credit supporting activity. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202306/20/content_WS64919b92c6d0868f4e8dd089.html>)

**Property** January–May real estate development investment fell 7.2% versus a year earlier. Construction requires cash before completed units can be handed over. If buyers delay deposits, a developer can stop starting new projects while still needing to finance the units buyers have already paid for. The commercial distinction is between an announcement about completions and a genuine turn in new projects, which would affect a different group of suppliers. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230615_1940629.html>)

**Fiscal, Policy & Trade** Licences for specified gallium and germanium products introduce an administrative hurdle at the point of export. A buyer with stocks or a qualified alternative may be able to continue production. Another with tight inventories may have to finance a costly buffer. The market implication is dispersion among manufacturers even inside the same chip supply chain. The actual duration of licensing and shipment delays is what changes cash flow. [⁽8⁾](<https://finance.yahoo.com/news/2-china-restrict-exports-chipmaking-115405155.html>)

**Markets & Flows** Credit terms describe what a borrower could pay if it took a loan. Equity prices describe the changing value of outstanding shares. Neither by itself measures the volume of new bank lending or money entering an investment fund. Stronger evidence for domestic recovery would be a sequence of better orders, purchases financed on ordinary terms and more completed sales, observable across a borrower and its suppliers.

**New / Most Important** The largest disagreement between macro indicators and an investable franchise is in capital intensity. A service business with an established customer base can sell one more booking without buying another factory. A property or materials business must finance inventory and construction before the next buyer signs. The difference matters when benchmark rates are falling but demand for new space is uncertain. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202306/20/content_WS64919b92c6d0868f4e8dd089.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230615_1940629.html>)

**Domestic orders and exporters** Caixin’s manufacturing PMI came in at 50.5 in June, down from 50.9. Its manufacturers reported a modest increase in overall orders and broadly unchanged new export business. Place that beside the official PMI of 49.0. The different survey panels give a picture of uneven activity around the expansion threshold. The Caixin report is particularly useful for understanding the source of what growth there was. A domestic customer taking delivery can keep a line running now; it tells a component producer much less about when overseas clients will be ready to rebuild their inventories. [⁽15⁾](<https://pmi.caixin.com/upload/CN_Manufacturing_ENG_2306_PR.pdf>) [⁽16⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202306/30/content_WS649e3ad1c6d0868f4e8dd604.html>)

**Pricing and working capital** Input costs and selling prices fell in the Caixin survey. A fall in both can improve or damage a manufacturer’s margins, depending on how quickly it reprices finished goods and what it paid for inventory already in the warehouse. A firm buying inputs at today’s lower cost and holding its output price has a different outlook from one discounting older goods to keep orders flowing. In an earnings review, put inventory turnover and collection of trade receivables alongside reported revenue growth. The next strong signal would be rising orders without a further sacrifice on selling prices. [⁽15⁾](<https://pmi.caixin.com/upload/CN_Manufacturing_ENG_2306_PR.pdf>)

**Playbook ✓** Favour businesses whose earnings can improve with repeat use and limited new investment. Add exposure to the building supply chain only when orders tied to new projects recover alongside collections. ⚠ Divergence: a better service survey can support consumer franchises while lower property investment continues to restrain suppliers of industrial inputs. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230630_1940935.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230615_1940629.html>)

### Japan · Mixed

**Macro Stance** Japan’s large-company Tankan balances show a domestic service economy with more confidence than manufacturing. Chinese export licensing adds a second question for factories. A producer can have improving order expectations and still have to carry more input inventory to protect deliveries. Monitor operating cash conversion alongside the survey. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/statistics/tk/gaiyo/2021/tka2306.pdf>) [⁽8⁾](<https://finance.yahoo.com/news/2-china-restrict-exports-chipmaking-115405155.html>)

**Activity & Currency** The Bank of Japan’s June decision retained its short-rate policy and yield-curve framework. An exporter can show a gain from translating foreign revenues even if the number of units sold is unchanged. A domestic service business faces a different equation, with local demand and wages more important to the next adjustment in margin. Investors should compare the currency on the revenue line with the currency on the purchase orders. [⁽18⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/mpr_2023/k230616a.pdf>)

**Playbook ✓** Prefer Japanese domestic service businesses with repeat bookings and room to pass on wage costs. Add exporters when unit orders and realised margins confirm that translation gains are funding durable cash generation. A rebound in factory confidence without improvement in orders warrants patience. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/statistics/tk/gaiyo/2021/tka2306.pdf>)

### Australia · Mixed

**Macro Stance** Today’s Australian cash-rate decision is still ahead. The known trade-off is an annual CPI indicator down to 5.6%, with housing costs still rising sharply. Retailers can benefit from cooler merchandise costs while household interest payments grow. Distinguish the price of stocking the shelf from the cash left in a customer’s bank account. [⁽19⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation/monthly-consumer-price-index-indicator/may-2023>)

**Demand & Financing** The price basket identifies housing and food among the main drivers of the annual rise. Those costs compete directly with the next discretionary purchase. In a mortgage book, higher repayments can stress a borrower well before a missed payment appears in an arrears statistic. In a retail account, the same pressure may first appear as smaller average baskets or substitution toward less expensive brands. [⁽19⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation/monthly-consumer-price-index-indicator/may-2023>)

**Playbook ✓** Prefer cash-generative businesses with control over inventory and funding. Revisit household cyclicals when selling volumes improve alongside easing living costs. For exporters, require evidence about the overseas buyer’s orders, since a stronger commodity quotation alone cannot tell whether its volume commitment changed.

### Poland · Mixed

**Macro Stance** Polish manufacturing PMI fell from 47.0 to 45.1 in June. The First Home programme opened on Monday. The survey and the subsidy point to different customer groups. Export factories need buyers to replenish inventories. A first-time local buyer can qualify for support even while those factories are cutting orders. This is a reason to separate bank origination from manufacturing earnings. [⁽20⁾](<https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/PMI-Polska-czerwiec-2023-Poglebia-sie-deflacyjna-recesja-w-polskim-przemysle-8572306.html>) [⁽21⁾](<https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/pierwsze-mieszkanie>)

**Markets** For a Polish investor the yield available on local savings, the price of a mortgage and the cost of financing stock at a distribution business need not move together. A business with euro-denominated purchases and zloty revenues also carries an exchange-rate exposure between placing its order and settling the invoice. Track the contractual hedge and liquidity needed during that interval before inferring a broad earnings benefit from currency direction.

**Fiscal & Politics** The government’s First Home programme subsidises eligible first-time buyers. What matters for a listed bank is the conversion of an inquiry into a signed loan and the cost of the deposits that support it. A buyer might substitute a subsidised new loan for one that would have been written anyway. Counting the whole principal as extra credit creation would overstate the boost to demand. [⁽21⁾](<https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/pierwsze-mieszkanie>)

**Corporate & Sectors** Real retail sales fell 6.8% year over year in May. A shop can put through a higher price and still pay rent, utilities and staff across fewer units sold. A company with a variable-cost distribution network may manage this more readily than one with a large estate of fixed premises. For a Polish bank, the accompanying risk is the quality of the retailer’s working-capital line, distinct from the headline yield on the loan. [⁽22⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/22-06-2023.html>)

**Geopolitics & Security** Poland has signalled that it will strengthen protection on the eastern border. For a portfolio this adds uncertainty around transport routes, public spending and insurance without establishing that any one domestic business has received an order. Where a company relies heavily on cross-border delivery, the prudent question is how a delay changes receivables and the cash required to finance them. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.voanews.com/a/poland-to-send-500-police-to-bolster-security-at-belarus-border-/7163855.html>)

**New / Most Important** A high nominal revenue comparison is a poor substitute for the volume figures in GUS’s May data. This distinction matters especially for distributors selling goods bought weeks earlier. Lower-cost stock can help the next gross margin, but discounting old stock also consumes some of that benefit. Check how fast finished goods are turning before treating the domestic market as a broad disinflation trade. [⁽22⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/22-06-2023.html>)

**Factories and backlogs** Poland’s manufacturing PMI dropped from 47.0 to 45.1 points in June. PKO Bank Polski reported pronounced declines in new orders and production and a thirteenth consecutive monthly reduction in outstanding work. A factory can use its backlog to keep skilled staff employed through a bad month. As that backlog gets smaller, today’s missing order becomes a more immediate threat to hours worked and the next shift. Inputs arriving sooner can look like better operating performance even when the buyer simply has fewer orders to place. Check whether new work is stabilising before treating faster delivery or lower costs as evidence of recovery. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.pkobp.pl/media_files/b75df444-799b-4c82-af49-2bc4e795ab2b.pdf>)

**Two domestic demand cycles** The opening of first-home mortgage applications makes domestic property a poor proxy for the factory cycle this week. A subsidised instalment can improve affordability for an eligible borrower while leaving an export manufacturer’s financing cost or order book untouched. The benefit to a bank arrives in stages: application, underwriting, approval and disbursement. A developer can benefit from a reservation before it recognises revenue. These lags matter if a front-page rush to submit paperwork lifts shares across both banking and construction. Compare conversion to completed transactions before extrapolating from the first stage into earnings. [⁽25⁾](<https://www.gov.pl/web/rozwoj-technologia/bezpieczny-kredyt-2--zaczynamy>) [⁽24⁾](<https://www.pkobp.pl/media_files/b75df444-799b-4c82-af49-2bc4e795ab2b.pdf>)

**Playbook ✓** Prefer the retailer whose real volumes and inventory turns stabilise together, or the bank whose funding mix protects its lending spread. Trim when nominal sales rise while units continue to fall and working-capital borrowing rises. ⚠ Divergence: falling consumer inflation supports household purchasing power, yet locked-in labour and finance costs can squeeze the companies selling to those same households. [⁽26⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/prices-trade/price-indices/flash-estimate-of-the-consumer-price-index-in-june-2023%2C21%2C12.html>) [⁽22⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/22-06-2023.html>)

## Sector Deep Dive

### XLK · Technology · Mixed

**Narrative** Input licensing turns working capital into a more informative near-term measure than a semiconductor order headline.

**Reported** Micron reported revenue of $3.75 billion, up from $3.69 billion sequentially but well below the $8.64 billion of a year earlier. A slight improvement in shipments off a depressed comparison can coexist with an industry still losing money. It recorded a $1.90 billion GAAP net loss. [⁽27⁾](<https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2023/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2023-06-28-2023/default.aspx>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Look for a recovery in realised memory prices and utilisation alongside bit shipments. An order for a specialised component can matter for its supplier while having little effect on the margin of a producer selling into a broader oversupplied market.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A lower price for memory can improve the economics of a system assembler well before it helps the chipmaker. Allocate between those two exposures deliberately when semiconductor headlines move the entire technology basket.

### XLC · Communication Services · Mixed

**Narrative** Twitter’s temporary limits on the number of posts users may read introduce a demand test for platforms dependent on recurring visits. An ad buyer pays for a reachable audience with measurable results, not for a registered account that never opens its feed. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/musk-says-twitter-applies-temporary-limit-to-address-data-scraping-system-manipulation-3118217>)

**Reported** The reading-limit announcement addressed access to Twitter’s own service. It did not establish an immediate transfer of advertising budgets to Meta, Alphabet or any other venue. An advertiser can pause a campaign just as easily as move it. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/musk-says-twitter-applies-temporary-limit-to-address-data-scraping-system-manipulation-3118217>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A higher rate of return visits plus evidence of stable conversion would support more valuable ad inventory. Without that combination, an additional minute online may carry little incremental revenue.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Ad buyers can benefit from competition even when platform shareholders do not. More places for a brand to reach similar people can weaken the price of an impression on every platform.

### XLF · Financials · Mixed

**Narrative** Large-bank capital and current credit demand answer different questions. A bank can withstand a severe hypothetical recession and still lose deposits to higher-paying alternatives or write fewer profitable loans this quarter.

**Reported** The Federal Reserve found that the 23 large banks examined could absorb more than $540 billion of losses in its severe scenario and continue lending. JPMorgan said its board intended to raise the quarterly dividend from $1.00 to $1.05 per common share. Those are a stress result and a proposed distribution. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2023-june-dodd-frank-act-stress-test-executive-summary.htm>) [⁽30⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000418/jpmc2023ccarresultsex99.htm>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A slower rise in the cost of interest-bearing deposits would leave more of the asset yield for shareholders. Inspect funding costs, credit provisions and actual loan growth before using the policy rate to forecast bank earnings.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** The cash distribution can improve while the economics on the next loan worsen. A resilient capital ratio answers a solvency question. It offers less guidance on deposit competition in the next quarter.

### XLE · Energy · Mixed

**Narrative** Supply policy now competes with the factory slowdown. Saudi Arabia said it would extend its voluntary cut into August, while Russia announced a reduction in exports. The two measures affect the market through different channels and should not be counted mechanically as an immediate fall in physical consumption. [⁽31⁾](<https://www.brecorder.com/news/40250757/oil-rallies-on-saudi-and-russian-supply-cuts-for-august>)

**Reported** The June 28 EIA release showed US commercial crude inventories falling by 9.6 million barrels in the week ended June 23. Stocks can move on the timing of imports, exports and refinery runs. One large draw becomes more persuasive when product inventories and actual demand tell a consistent story. [⁽32⁾](<https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/archive/2023/2023_06_28/pdf/wpsrall.pdf>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Prefer an integrated business when its upstream production and refining margin have separate earnings support. A higher crude price can be a cost to the downstream operation if fuel prices fail to keep pace.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A disciplined producer can earn attractive cash flow on flat output. Conversely, a headline production cut can produce little equity value if cost inflation or weaker realised prices absorbs the effect.

### XLI · Industrials · Mixed

**Narrative** Manufacturing PMI of 46.0 puts a premium on the quality of a backlog. A customer can still receive machines ordered months ago while cutting the number of replacement orders it places today. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>)

**Reported** Japan’s Tankan large-manufacturer balance improved to +5. Its large non-manufacturing balance reached +23. Capital equipment suppliers have a closer link to the first group, while logistics and business services may have a closer link to the second. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/statistics/tk/gaiyo/2021/tka2306.pdf>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A shorter delivery schedule paired with stable order intake and good cash collections is constructive. Shorter lead times created by cancelled orders would imply the opposite for manufacturing margins.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A large backlog can become a financing burden when it fixes the sale price ahead of the cost of components. Seek evidence about collection terms and contract resets before celebrating backlog growth.

### XLY · Consumer Discretionary · Mixed

**Narrative** Demand is sensitive to what it costs a customer to use the product. An electric car, a pair of shoes and a cruise all count as discretionary purchases. Financing, markdowns and the timing of the deposit give those purchases very different implications for cash flow.

**Reported** Tesla reported 466,140 deliveries. Nike’s quarterly gross margin fell 140 basis points to 43.6% as factors including markdowns and product costs outweighed offsets. Large unit shipments and preserving price can point in different directions. [⁽33⁾](<https://ir.tesla.com/press-release/tesla-vehicle-production-deliveries-and-date-financial-results-webcast-second-quarter-2023>) [⁽34⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018723000026/q4fy23exhibit991er.htm>)

**EPS Upside Watch** The earnings upside requires volume that does not depend on a deeper discount. In autos, track the realised selling price and contribution per vehicle. In apparel, watch the margin on full-price inventory and the size of promotions.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Demand can be robust at a low enough price and still leave the producer a poor investment. The right comparison is the cash and profit required to generate the incremental unit.

### XLP · Consumer Staples · Mixed

**Narrative** The distinction in staples is between restoring a margin after an input shock and retaining it after costs normalise. A business selling familiar brands can raise prices for a period while losing some unit demand to private label or smaller baskets.

**Reported** McCormick reported second-quarter sales up 8% and raised its adjusted profit outlook. In its consumer segment, pricing contributed 9% while volume fell 2%. The accounts therefore show both the power and the limit of price increases. [⁽35⁾](<https://ir.mccormick.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mccormick-reports-strong-second-quarter-performance-and-raises>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Watch the next volume comparison and how much of lower input cost is returned in promotion. A recovery in units with flat pricing would leave the margin improvement on a more sustainable footing.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A slower increase in revenue can be good news when freight costs fall and volume recovers. A high headline comparison driven entirely by price can deserve the lower multiple.

### XLV · Health Care · Mixed

**Narrative** A pharmacy network is both a health-care distributor and a retailer with fixed premises. The same storefront can benefit from recurring prescriptions and lose high-contribution demand when a temporary vaccination or testing cycle fades.

**Reported** Walgreens reported quarterly sales of $35.4 billion, up 8.6% year over year, and GAAP EPS of $0.14. It identified lower COVID-19 vaccination and testing activity as a material headwind. The sales line on its own conceals a significant change in mix. [⁽36⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1618921/000119312523175558/d504570dex991.htm>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Look for evidence that repeat services absorb the cost of clinicians, facilities and distribution. An expanded eligible population becomes recurring profit only as patients enter and stay in an economically workable treatment pathway.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** The distribution bottleneck can matter more than the number of potential patients. The early beneficiary of a complex rollout may be a provider already equipped for the service.

### XLB · Materials · Mixed

**Narrative** Licensing of certain gallium and germanium exports can cause a sharp change in the price and availability of specialist inputs without signalling stronger global manufacturing. Separate the value of possessing eligible material from the value of owning a broad mining basket. [⁽8⁾](<https://finance.yahoo.com/news/2-china-restrict-exports-chipmaking-115405155.html>)

**Reported** China’s June factory PMI stood at 49.0, while nonmanufacturing activity remained in expansion. A service-led rebound can be healthy for the economy yet require less incremental material per unit of output than a construction-led one. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230630_1940935.html>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Prioritise actual shipments, grade and customer drawdowns. A producer achieving a higher realised margin while inventory stays steady presents a cleaner case than one building stock in anticipation of stimulus.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Tighter supply of one minor metal and weaker aggregate Chinese building demand can coexist. The broad sector basket is an imprecise way to own a very particular licensing or scarcity story.

### XLRE · Real Estate · Mixed

**Narrative** The value of a lease depends on what is left after paying interest and funding the building. A rent increase can raise net operating income on the old loan and still leave less distributable cash after refinancing. Look first at the maturity schedule.

**Reported** May new-home sales were reported separately by Census and HUD. New construction can attract purchasers when owners of existing properties are reluctant to sell. More transactions for a builder do not establish higher rents or a lower capitalisation rate for a listed commercial landlord. [⁽37⁾](<https://fedprimerate.com/docs/New-Home-Sales/Fed-Prime-Rate-Housing--New-Home-Sales--May-2023.pdf>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Balance-sheet duration can produce a large earnings difference among landlords facing similar rents. Compare fixed-rate protection, the timing of maturities and actual lease renewal terms before valuing the equity off a single yield.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A landlord with limited new supply in its market may be able to protect rents even as financing costs rise. A building with attractive stated rent may be unattractive once tenant incentives, vacant space and the next debt maturity are included.

### XLU · Utilities · Mixed

**Narrative** Utilities earn through rates, allowed returns and the financing of capital investment. A bond yield repricing affects the return investors demand immediately. The regulated revenue or tariff that compensates an operator may adjust much later.

**Reported** US energy prices in the May PCE release were 13.4% below a year earlier while service prices rose 5.3%. Falling fuel cost can ease pressure on customers and bills. Its effect on shareholders depends on the fuel-adjustment rules and how quickly purchased power costs are passed through. [⁽38⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/personal-income-and-outlays-may-2023>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Seek evidence of approved cost recovery and funding secured for construction. A higher allowed return creates value only if the operator can build at a cost below the return it is permitted to earn.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Customer relief from cheaper energy can improve collections without lifting the utility’s own spread. Conversely, an attractive dividend yield gives little protection against a financing gap on a large project.

### SXAP · European Autos · Mixed

**Narrative** European manufacturers must defend price across different models, not simply sell more electric vehicles. A lower entry price may expand the addressable market but reduce the cash available to recover the cost of plants, software and new battery platforms.

**Reported** Tesla’s delivery disclosure sets a competitive volume reference for the European industry, with the margin and regional mix still to come. The count is global and should not be interpreted as measured European registrations. [⁽33⁾](<https://ir.tesla.com/press-release/tesla-vehicle-production-deliveries-and-date-financial-results-webcast-second-quarter-2023>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Compare orders by model, dealer inventory and the value of consumer incentives. Healthy deliveries out of an old backlog carry less information about the next quarter than a rise in fresh orders at an unchanged transaction price.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A cheaper vehicle can increase the buyer’s financing eligibility while transferring value from the manufacturer to the consumer. Volume leadership and shareholder return can diverge.

### SOXX · US–Asia Semiconductors · Mixed

**Narrative** Semiconductors have several distinct cycles. A memory producer seeks a price high enough to cover fabrication costs. A toolmaker needs a customer able to fund new capacity and permission to ship the particular system. A designer relies on demand for its finished part.

**Reported** ASML described new Dutch export controls focused on certain advanced semiconductor manufacturing technologies. The distinction between a licence and an industry-wide ban matters. A rule can delay one system while leaving another sale possible. [⁽39⁾](<https://www.asml.com/news/press-releases/2023/statement-regarding-export-control-regulations-dutch-government>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Look for evidence that customers are increasing spending on a type of capacity the supplier can actually service. A server build does not automatically create profitable demand for every memory product or every generation of lithography.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** The company with the most valuable installed base may collect service revenue even while new orders slow. The most sensitive share price can belong to a supplier with a narrower product mix and fewer opportunities for replacement sales.

## Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

### What the quarter is paying for

**Rewarded** Follow the transaction from the factory gate to the customer. Inventory decisions can temporarily change reported growth in either direction. McCormick raised its adjusted profit outlook while holding its sales outlook. That is an earnings improvement the investor can examine in costs, volume and cash conversion, even when nominal sales growth begins to slow. [⁽35⁾](<https://ir.mccormick.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mccormick-reports-strong-second-quarter-performance-and-raises>)

**Punished** Nike’s fourth-quarter margin contraction is a more demanding comparison for brands relying on price increases. Markdown activity, product input costs and freight reduced what the company kept from the sale. An improvement in revenue without a corresponding increase in gross profit is a weaker recovery for a shareholder. [⁽34⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018723000026/q4fy23exhibit991er.htm>)

**Pattern** The important distinction is between bringing a sale forward and improving what that sale earns. A unit can be shipped to clear inventory, to meet a customer with a genuine waiting list or to fill a reseller’s warehouse. All three can raise revenue this quarter. Only repeat orders at viable economics extend the benefit into the next one.

**Attention into EPS** Look for management to reconcile reported revenue with units sold and average selling price, then trace the margin through inventory and actual cash collection. Micron’s sequential revenue rise alongside a material GAAP loss illustrates the difference between an industry passing its low point and a business earning enough to fund its own investment. [⁽27⁾](<https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2023/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2023-06-28-2023/default.aspx>)

**Playbook** Own the business that can demonstrate both the transaction and the margin. Increase exposure as realised selling price holds and inventories decline without widening promotions. A weakening margin alongside volume growth should reduce the size of the position until the company can explain who paid for the growth.

## Underowned Themes — Attention and Exposure Gaps

### The operating cost of keeping inventory

**Attention Gap** The debate over nominal sales misses the capital required to protect delivery dates. A distributor holding more expensive input stock carries an interest bill between purchase and collection. The effect grows when borrowing rates are high even if the company has avoided a production delay.

**Exposure & Risk** Within XLI and XLK, favour companies with service businesses, short customer terms or contracts that reimburse procurement costs. A firm accepting a fixed-price contract while its capital needs rise is the more exposed side. The cost can surface in cash flow before the income statement.

**Trigger to Watch** Watch the relationship between sales, inventories and receivables at the next reporting round. Shorter collection periods with stable margins are positive. Faster sales funded by larger customer credit lines are less convincing.

### Services demand inside a factory slowdown

**Attention Gap** The US factory survey fell to 46.0 while China’s non-manufacturing survey stayed above 50. A service business that depends on repeat local demand offers a different earnings exposure from a provider selling capital equipment across borders. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>) [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202306/t20230630_1940935.html>)

**Exposure & Risk** Consider domestic service franchises and fee-based providers before a broad industrial basket. The risk is a household squeeze that eventually reaches those services, especially where repeated discretionary purchases are easy to postpone.

**Trigger to Watch** Require returning customers or renewing contracts. A rebound caused by a one-time reopening comparison is less useful than evidence customers return after the comparison normalises.

### The bank’s deposit customer

**Attention Gap** The headline rate on a bank’s assets is transparent. The rate needed to retain a particular customer can change much faster than the price of a loan fixed months ago. Large-bank stress results provide context for resilience while leaving this everyday pricing question open. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2023-june-dodd-frank-act-stress-test-executive-summary.htm>)

**Exposure & Risk** Use XLF selectively to express deposit durability and funding flexibility. A high loan yield with an unstable funding base can leave less for shareholders than a lower-yielding book funded by stable customer balances.

**Trigger to Watch** At the next bank update compare the mix of non-interest-bearing deposits, interest-bearing balances and wholesale borrowing. If loan income improves only because more expensive funds replace lost deposits, reassess the earnings multiple.

### Reimbursement and delivery capacity

**Attention Gap** Walgreens’ quarterly results demonstrate how quickly a temporary medical service can disappear from the earnings base even when total sales continue to rise. A clinic’s technical capability and its recurring customer volume are distinct assets. [⁽36⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1618921/000119312523175558/d504570dex991.htm>)

**Exposure & Risk** Within health care, prefer businesses able to turn patient access into recurring reimbursed work without rebuilding distribution for each new service. A high level of fixed clinical staffing creates downside if the eligible population proves slow to arrive.

**Trigger to Watch** Follow reimbursement, completed visits and service-level margins. Revenue announced at a group level does less to resolve the issue than evidence one more patient can be served profitably.

### Bitcoin, fund access and the exchange

**Market Context** CoinShares reported $125 million of weekly inflows into the investment products it tracks. An observable subscription to an existing vehicle is evidence of investor demand. It is not guaranteed money for a proposed US fund or the total cash changing hands on crypto exchanges. [⁽40⁾](<https://mondovisione.com/media-and-resources/news/bullishness-continues-with-us125m-inflows-fund-flows-report-coinshares-resea-202373/>)

**New / Most Important** Nasdaq’s renewed filing for the proposed BlackRock bitcoin product named Coinbase in the surveillance arrangement. This addresses how a listing exchange might monitor activity. It does not establish that the regulator has accepted the arrangement or that a proposed fund has any assets. The timing of regulatory review and the timing of actual custody or trading revenue are separate. [⁽41⁾](<https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2023-07-03-blackrock-refile-for-spot-bitcoin-etf-naming-coinbase-as-surveillance-partner-237563>)

**Playbook** Distinguish bitcoin exposure from an equity stake in a trading platform. A token holder requires a buyer willing to pay a higher spot price. An exchange owner needs profitable volume or another recurring revenue stream after costs and regulatory expenses. The strongest confirmation would be a regulatory decision followed by sustained subscriptions and evidence of who earns the fee. If the filing advances but volumes and fees weaken, those two positions can separate sharply.

## Capital Flows & Liquidity Framework

### Capital follows different clocks

**Global Liquidity** Easier terms somewhere in the system do not automatically release cash everywhere. China has lowered its benchmark lending rates while US financing remains expensive. A company with local-currency sales, a dollar credit facility and long customer-payment terms can feel pressure even if its domestic benchmark moves lower. The choice between consuming cash and drawing a line of credit shows up in working capital before it appears in sales. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202306/20/content_WS64919b92c6d0868f4e8dd089.html>) [⁽42⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230614a.htm>)

**Rates & Dollar** At the 2023-07-03 close, the two-year Treasury yielded 4.94% and the ten-year 3.86%. The 10-year minus 2-year spread was -108 bps. Over twenty completed observations from 2023-06-02, the two-year moved +44 bps, the ten-year +17 bps and the spread -27 bps. [⁽3⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value_month=202307&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve>) The S&P 500 closed at 4,455.59 points and the VIX at 13.57 points. [⁽4⁾](<https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/SP500>) [⁽5⁾](<https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/VIXCLS>) Investors with predictable near-term liabilities can earn front-end carry without making a large bet on where rates settle. For a long bond to outperform, the decline in future yields must compensate the lower running yield and duration risk. The same funding choice appears inside equity valuations when an investor pays today for cash that may arrive much later.

**Credit Check** The Fed’s stress test examined a severe hypothetical economy and found capital resilience in the tested large banks. It did not measure a current loosening of business lending standards. An individual borrower still faces collateral demands, floating interest costs and a lender that may prefer liquidity. The operational check is whether a company can collect cash fast enough to finance the period between paying suppliers and receiving money from customers. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2023-june-dodd-frank-act-stress-test-executive-summary.htm>)

**Equity & Fund Flows** CoinShares’ $125 million reading covers subscriptions and redemptions in a defined universe of digital-asset products. It speaks to demand through a particular wrapper. A stock-market rally, a change in a benchmark yield or a rise in the value of fund assets cannot serve as a substitute for a measured flow. Treat shares outstanding and changes in unit value as separate events. [⁽40⁾](<https://mondovisione.com/media-and-resources/news/bullishness-continues-with-us125m-inflows-fund-flows-report-coinshares-resea-202373/>)

**Portfolio Implication** Keep liquid reserves against dates when cash is actually needed. For the equity book, compare the cash a business can generate internally with the external finance required to satisfy the next order. A company that increases unit sales by extending unusually generous customer terms may be spending liquidity to generate a headline. Reduce the weight if the order book grows more quickly than cash receipts and the credit facility becomes essential to ordinary trading.

## Anticipatory Positioning — Markets Pricing In Future Events

### Shelter the duration that actually hedges

**What Is Priced** An inverted curve supplies carry at the front and a possible growth hedge farther out. The hedge becomes dangerous if its payoff depends on the same decline in inflation that underpins a high-multiple equity book.

**Conditional Expression** Keep short government exposure for liquidity and add long-duration bonds in stages only as core price pressure and activity cool together. The distinction is between funding near-term obligations and owning sensitivity to the next change in economic expectations.

**Risk** A strong services recovery with sticky wages could raise yields on the long end even if purchasing managers in manufacturing remain downbeat.

### Protect a memory recovery against a low-margin mix

**What Is Priced** Micron’s revenue edged up sequentially, but its GAAP accounts still show a substantial net loss. A marginal order improvement is easier to achieve than full absorption of fabrication costs across the installed plant. [⁽27⁾](<https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2023/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2023-06-28-2023/default.aspx>)

**Conditional Expression** Prefer suppliers with a route from incremental shipments to realised price and operating cash. Increase the position if the next update shows both improvement in mix and discipline on inventory.

**Risk** A jump in demand for a narrow high-performance component could leave older memory inventories uncleared. A broad index position may have quite different exposure from the headline winner.

### Own a retail recovery only after the price test

**What Is Priced** Nike’s reduced margin and the Polish real retail sales decline both illustrate how active shops can coexist with fragile earnings. They describe different markets, but each forces the investor to distinguish receipts in currency from units bought at viable margins. [⁽34⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018723000026/q4fy23exhibit991er.htm>) [⁽22⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/22-06-2023.html>)

**Conditional Expression** Look for improved full-price sell-through paired with lower inventories. Pair a stronger brand with an underweight in a competitor still depending on heavy promotion when comparable units turn positive.

**Risk** Higher selling prices can cover weakening volumes for a period, while a promotion may clean up inventory at the expense of the near-term profit an investor was expecting.

### Separate the auto delivery from the margin release

**What Is Priced** Rivian’s production and deliveries are actual unit counts, while its full-year production target remains management guidance. Neither reveals the fully loaded profit on the marginal vehicle. [⁽6⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417823000033/ex-9912q23deliveryproducti.htm>)

**Conditional Expression** Use a limited position in the better-funded manufacturer, adding only when the price and unit-cost bridge supports the volume gain. Owners of suppliers should track customer schedules and contract terms independently.

**Risk** Aggressive incentives could make an impressive registration figure expensive for the manufacturer. A supplier paid by unit faces a different immediate risk from a carmaker sacrificing price to generate that unit.

## What Retail Is Playing

Rivian steps out of Tesla's shadow with a delivery surprise of its own, while the US holiday leaves Monday's shortened session as the latest cash-market test.

### Rivian / RIVN — The smaller factory has its answer

**Why Retail Is Here** Rivian has an answer for anyone who thinks the weekend's electric-vehicle sales story belongs entirely to Tesla. It delivered 12,640 vehicles in the second quarter, compared with the 11,000 that Visible Alpha's analysts expected. On Monday its shares closed at $19.56, up more than 17%. That is a large premium for a release that covered one quarter's vehicles and contained no earnings. The appeal to a trader who missed Tesla is easy to understand. A smaller company can change the market's view of its prospects by getting a relatively modest number of extra trucks into driveways. [⁽43⁾](<https://ca.investing.com/news/economy/rivian-beats-estimates-for-secondquarter-ev-deliveries-3035980>) [⁽44⁾](<https://www.forbes.com/sites/antoniopequenoiv/2023/07/03/rivian-stock-surges-over-17-as-ev-manufacturer-beats-second-quarter-delivery-estimates/>)

**What Is Driving It** The company's plant in Normal, Illinois, built 13,992 vehicles. Management kept its forecast for annual production at 50,000. An investor can read the unchanged forecast two ways. A better quarter makes the existing goal more credible, giving shares room to recover if investors previously doubted it. A valuation that assumes a much higher target needs another piece of evidence. Production measures cars leaving the factory. Deliveries measure handovers to customers. A working capital investment sits between the two, and management can collect cash at a different point from the date on which a vehicle is completed. [⁽6⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417823000033/ex-9912q23deliveryproducti.htm>)

**The Social Signal** The observations from Monday distinguish an EV audience from a Rivian audience. Quiver's premarket ranking of US stocks mentioned on WallStreetBets was led by Tesla, followed by Nvidia and Apple. Rivian appeared sixth on the separate Bloomberg list of active premarket shares cited by StoneX. It would be easy to fold those two lists into a story about a single group of buyers. One measures what a forum discussed. The other measures where shares changed hands, including trades placed by institutions. What matters for Rivian is whether the extra activity in its own shares survives the return of normal trading hours. [⁽45⁾](<https://www.forex.com/en/news-and-analysis/where-next-for-tesla-stock-and-the-nasdaq-100-outlook/?amp=true>) [⁽46⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/wall-st-ends-slightly-higher-in-shortened-session-tesla-jumps-3118595>)

**Historical Analog** Tesla's release over the weekend showed how quickly a delivery headline can attract attention before the profits from the vehicles are known. The resemblance ends early. Tesla had cut prices on cars it could already build at scale. For Rivian the central uncertainty is how reliably it can increase factory output, hand vehicles to customers and spread manufacturing costs across those sales. Borrowing Tesla's sales momentum as a reason to own Rivian would miss that difference. A better analogy is a supplier coming out of a production bottleneck. The first easy comparison can be dramatic. The valuable improvement is repeating the pace without overtime, costly fixes or another buildup in inventory. [⁽47⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/tesla-beats-secondquarter-delivery-estimates-3118305>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417823000033/ex-9912q23deliveryproducti.htm>)

**Smart Money View** AJ Bell's Danni Hewson told Reuters that competition from established automakers had raised questions about which EV companies would stay in the race. Rivian's delivery surprise addresses a piece of that question. Demand held up as production improved. It leaves plenty for a shareholder to underwrite. A manufacturer can increase output and lose money on the additional vehicle. An investor expecting an eventual recovery in margins needs evidence in the production cost, warranty experience and cash required for the next increase, none of which the delivery release quantifies. A forecast for vehicles alone cannot settle the value of the equity. [⁽43⁾](<https://ca.investing.com/news/economy/rivian-beats-estimates-for-secondquarter-ev-deliveries-3035980>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417823000033/ex-9912q23deliveryproducti.htm>)

**When the Narrative Breaks** Use $19.56 to distinguish a follow-through trade from a patient bet on the factory. If shares push above Monday's close when the cash market reopens and quickly give the move back, the new buyer faces a queue of holders who already received the delivery premium. The longer test is whether subsequent handovers can keep pace with output, and how progress shows up in gross profit and cash. Rivian will release financial results after the close on August 8. A short-dated option expiring sooner turns that longer industrial argument into a bet on the next shareholder's willingness to pay. [⁽44⁾](<https://www.forbes.com/sites/antoniopequenoiv/2023/07/03/rivian-stock-surges-over-17-as-ev-manufacturer-beats-second-quarter-delivery-estimates/>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417823000033/ex-9912q23deliveryproducti.htm>)
