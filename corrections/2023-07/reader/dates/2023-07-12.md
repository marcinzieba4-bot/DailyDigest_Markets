## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · Mixed

**Macro Stance** June CPI is the immediate test. May consumer credit grew at only a 1.8% annual rate, but June payrolls still rose 209,000 and hourly earnings grew 0.4% on the month. A household able to fund spending from wages behaves differently from one relying on cards. Last month's small gain in credit cannot settle which group will set the next price for travel and services. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**Rates & Curve** The US Treasury two-year yielded 4.88% and the ten-year 3.99% at the July 11 close. The ten-year less the two-year stood at −89 basis points. The two-year was 29 basis points higher over twenty Treasury observations and the ten-year 24 basis points higher. Both maturities sold off, with the front end moving further. A company rolling near-term debt and one able to lock in longer fixed-rate borrowing face different costs even before their credit risks are taken into account. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽4⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202306>)

**New / Most Important** The next consumer-price release sits between two competing pieces of evidence. The manufacturing PMI at 46.0 shows broad contraction in the surveyed factory sector. Services PMI at 53.9 shows expansion. A durable slowdown in prices requires more than cheaper goods if service providers can pay higher wages and still pass costs to customers. [⁽5⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-53-9-june-2023-services-ism-report-on-business-301870344.html>) [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>)

**Playbook ✓** Separate businesses whose customers can absorb higher prices from those that need another price increase to preserve margins. If core services cool while employment remains firm, the opportunity extends beyond the companies investors already associate with disinflation. A persistent core print leaves the cash rate competing hard for the same capital. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230705a.htm>) [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>)

### European Union · Mixed

**Macro Stance** ZEW’s July expectations indicator fell to minus 14.7. Respondents were evaluating the outlook for the German economy at a moment when policy remained restrictive and Chinese factory prices were falling. A negative survey reading tells us something about willingness to commit capital. It gives little indication of the profit on existing deliveries from an older backlog. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**Credit & Inflation** June’s flash annual inflation estimate of 5.5% was lower than May’s 6.1%. A declining rate of inflation leaves the price level higher and does not reverse the higher borrowing costs businesses have already absorbed. The gap matters for retailers. A cash-constrained family can buy fewer goods even when the monthly increase in its grocery bill is smaller. [⁽10⁾](<https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/2995521/17075124/2-30062023-AP-EN.pdf/230735d1-74e6-4d30-2039-c9ed1e660296>) Destatis put German June inflation at 6.4% annually and 0.3% for the month. The national reading and the euro-area measure describe different baskets and geographies. Divergence between them can leave a German household paying materially higher bills while a multinational firm reports easing input expense from Asia. Read a company’s customer base and manufacturing location separately before choosing the relevant price index. [⁽11⁾](<https://www.destatis.de/EN/Press/2023/07/PE23_270_611.html>)

**New / Most Important** Mercedes reported 515,700 second-quarter vehicles and a 6% increase. Premium product demand provides a concrete exception to weak survey expectations. Near-term results can reflect the ability to clear backlogs, whereas the survey covers business expectations. Evidence in the next order book will resolve the tension. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/mercedes-sales-rise-in-q2-on-topend-electric-vehicles-3123004>) [⁽8⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

**Playbook ✓** Look for pricing, order conversion and the source of financing before treating a cheaper European equity multiple as sufficient compensation. The most vulnerable supplier sells into an end market with shrinking orders and has little ability to reset prices against fixed labour or energy costs. Reassess if advance orders and actual output turn up together. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

### China · Mixed

**Macro Stance** June price data and credit flows point in different directions. Consumer prices were flat year over year and producer prices fell 5.4%. Banks can expand lending through the quarter end while nominal pricing for the companies receiving that credit remains weak. For shareholders the key distinction is between maintaining production and earning a satisfactory return on the assets that finance it. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>) [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**PMIs & Activity** Factory-gate deflation is a nominal signal. A producer may ship the same physical volume and receive less cash, or sell more tonnes while nominal revenue stagnates. Credit metrics measured in yuan and operating data measured in units can diverge. Ask whether lower input costs benefit the downstream manufacturer enough to compensate for the selling-price decline. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**Credit Impulse** New yuan loans reached RMB 3.05 trillion in June. Money supply M2 rose 11.3% year over year, while M1 increased 3.1%. The quantities indicate ample balance-sheet expansion but do not identify a marginal borrower or establish that new projects are clearing investment hurdles. Distinguish refinancing, public-sector spending and private-sector expansion before inferring industrial orders. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Property** A mortgage, a loan to finish an existing apartment and financing for a new land purchase can all raise lending. Their consequences for steel, cement, furnishings and household confidence differ. Watch presold homes handed over and transaction volumes alongside credit. A stronger banking aggregate on its own provides little basis for forecasting a developer’s sales or a European supplier’s construction orders. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Fiscal, Policy & Trade** Existing credit channels give policymakers a way to sustain financing. A large June flow does not identify a new fiscal programme, quantify a new housing subsidy or establish a change in home-purchase rules. Give greater weight to dated policy decisions and subsequently observed transactions than to an assumption about what policymakers might eventually announce. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Markets & Flows** A bank balance sheet, a foreign portfolio flow and a listed company’s earnings represent different claims. China’s lending data say much more about the first than the second. For industrial equities, demand a connection from credit to a customer’s capital expenditure and from that project to the supplier’s cash collection. The link can be long, especially when falling producer prices make additional inventory unattractive. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**New / Most Important** The newly available June lending figure changes the starting point for any easing argument. Banks have already extended a considerable amount of credit. The next evidence investors should demand is what households and enterprises actually do with the additional financing. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Use bank lending as an input and actual orders, home contracts and price realisation as the confirmation. A borrower able to roll debt has solved a financing problem. It may still have no economic reason to buy more machines. ⚠ Divergence: credit increased sharply in June while producer prices continued to decline. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### Japan · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The June Tankan put the business-conditions balance at plus 5 for large manufacturers and plus 23 for large nonmanufacturers. Optimism in services can support domestic revenue without turning the inventory cycle in export manufacturing. Machinery orders provide a more direct test of spending intentions than an aggregate equity multiple. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>)

**Activity & Currency** Core machinery orders fell 7.6% in May from the prior month, a result first reported at 23:51 UTC on July 11. A volatile month of orders can be dominated by the timing of large contracts. The cost of getting that interpretation wrong is largest at suppliers whose profit depends on running plants near capacity. Currency moves, imported costs and price discipline also influence the gain from any future volume recovery. [⁽15⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Focus on companies that can distinguish order intake, backlog and deliveries in their disclosures. If another weak machinery release coincides with a deterioration in manufacturing respondents’ expectations, the capex thesis deserves a lower multiple. Stronger domestic service conditions offer a separate avenue for earnings rather than a wholesale hedge. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>) [⁽15⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>)

### Australia · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The Reserve Bank held its cash rate at 4.10% on July 4. A pause lets existing tightening work through mortgage payments and consumer spending. A household refinancing at a higher rate can reduce discretionary purchases even while still employed. That is a demand problem for a retailer before it becomes a credit-loss problem for the bank. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>)

**Demand & Financing** Westpac reported a rise in its consumer sentiment index from 79.2 to 81.3 in July. The improvement leaves sentiment weak in absolute terms. A recovery in a survey after the central bank pauses may reflect relief rather than a willingness to make a large purchase. Watch order values, cancellations and credit use before extrapolating the bounce to listed discretionary earnings. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.westpaciq.com.au/economics/2023/07/consumer-sentiment-july-2023>)

**Playbook ✓** Household-facing companies must preserve volume and pricing as mortgages reprice. Mining companies instead need capable customers in China and prices that cover production and project costs. An unchanged Australian rate does not repair deflation in Chinese producer prices. Analyse the two exposures independently. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### Poland · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The National Bank of Poland held its reference rate at 6.75%. A high rate changes how quickly an individual household or business can absorb a price shock. For banks, it can support interest income on repricing assets, but deposit holders seek better terms and a smaller number of creditworthy borrowers may want to take loans. [⁽18⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>)

**Markets** Focus on the transmission from financing to earnings. A bank’s interest margin measures a spread on its existing balance sheet. A manufacturer’s investment plan depends on the expected cash generated by a new project. Both face the same policy rate but the immediate benefit or cost can point in opposite directions. A change in the zloty can also help importers and hurt exporters in different proportions. [⁽18⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) The July NBP staff projection put average annual inflation at 11.9% in 2023, 5.2% in 2024 and 3.6% in 2025. These are conditional staff projections. An investor pricing government bonds against that path must consider the assumed interest-rate environment and uncertainty around food and energy prices. For a domestic retailer, the speed with which wages catch up matters at least as much as the point estimate for annual inflation. [⁽19⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Inflation-Report-%E2%80%93-July-2023.pdf>)

**Fiscal & Politics** The NBP statement cites preliminary June inflation of 11.5% year over year. The provisional figure provides a working measure of the strain on household purchasing power. Before using it to justify an earnings forecast, compare the company’s own wage and input bill with its ability to pass costs on and keep customer volumes. [⁽18⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>)

**Corporate & Sectors** Polish manufacturing PMI fell to 45.1 in June. An exporter serving German industry is exposed to customer confidence and order timing across the border. A domestic lender instead depends on payment quality, interest margins and the availability of profitable new credit. One national equity index can contain both stories and disclose little about the direction of the individual business. [⁽20⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>) [⁽8⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

**Geopolitics & Security** The Vilnius summit communiqué reaffirmed collective defence and described alliance commitments to the eastern flank. An alliance declaration matters for strategic confidence but says little by itself about the timing of an order for an individual Polish company. Distinguish a procurement contract from the longer-term case for defence expenditure. [⁽21⁾](<https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/official-texts-and-resources/official-texts/2023/07/11/vilnius-summit-communique?selectedLocale=ge>)

**New / Most Important** A July 11 ZEW reading of minus 14.7 offers a direct regional counterpart to the weaker Polish manufacturing poll. Orders and working-capital commitments can soften on both sides of the border before hard production data show the full effect. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>) [⁽20⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>)

**Playbook ✓** Track domestic price pressure, bank funding costs and industrial orders before extrapolating broad Polish market strength into domestic earnings. A reduction in inflation coupled with weak new manufacturing business could support bonds and disappoint industrial shareholders. ⚠ Divergence: policy rates remain high as June manufacturing sentiment signals contraction. [⁽18⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) [⁽20⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>)

## Sector Deep Dive

### XLK — Technology · Mixed

**Narrative** Industry attention is concentrated on data and artificial intelligence, while TSMC’s June revenue fell 11.1% year over year. Cloud services and chip fabrication occupy different points in the chain. Buying a technology index assumes much more than the proposition that one class of accelerator is scarce. [⁽22⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

**Reported** Salesforce shares advanced 3.9% on July 11 after disclosing higher prices, while Activision Blizzard gained about 10% on the US court ruling concerning Microsoft. One event changes what an enterprise may bill its software customer on renewal. The other changes the probability of concluding a transaction at an agreed price. Neither price move measures the growth in the total budget available for enterprise technology. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-11/wall-street-gets-a-late-push-ahead-of-inflation-data-activision-blizzard-jumps>)

**EPS Upside Watch** TSMC’s NT$156.40 billion June revenue was down 11.4% from May. Monthly invoicing is noisy, but a decline of that size leaves the burden of proof on claims of a sector-wide upswing. Check several shipments and end markets before assigning the same multiple improvement to client computing and enterprise workloads. [⁽22⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A software company can lift dollar sales by raising list prices as customers quietly reduce paid seats. A multiyear contract postpones the point at which management has to choose between defending the increase and protecting a relationship. Watch expansion by existing customers, staff counts at clients and the time required to close new orders. Apparent pricing power has a cost if it encourages customers to consolidate more tools at renewal. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-11/wall-street-gets-a-late-push-ahead-of-inflation-data-activision-blizzard-jumps>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-53-9-june-2023-services-ism-report-on-business-301870344.html>)

### XLC — Communication Services · Mixed

**Narrative** Reuters reported more than 100 million sign-ups for Meta’s Threads in its first five days. An established social platform can prompt users to open another account at very low friction. That is valuable distribution, but it says little about repeat use or what an advertiser is prepared to pay for each visit. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/metas-twitter-rival-threads-overtakes-chatgpt-as-fastestgrowing-platform-3122632>)

**Reported** Threads attracted more than 100 million registrations in five days, with Meta able to use its existing Instagram distribution. Importing an identity and finding familiar accounts removes friction from opening a new profile. Distinguish that cheap first visit from the harder job of retaining a participant after the launch cohort has tried the app and returned to its previous habits. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/metas-twitter-rival-threads-overtakes-chatgpt-as-fastestgrowing-platform-3122632>)

**EPS Upside Watch** For an advertising platform, test user retention, advertiser demand and monetisable inventory in that order. A company can reach a large audience before it can sell sufficient advertising at attractive prices. A change in interest rates offers valuation support but cannot supply a missing advertiser. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Advertisers pay for an audience at a particular moment and in a particular setting. An hour transferred from Instagram might represent valuable inventory on Threads and lost inventory on a better-monetised service. Watch incremental time across apps and the advertiser’s ability to measure outcomes alongside the cost of moderation. Growth on one platform can create meaningful competition for another business inside the same company. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/metas-twitter-rival-threads-overtakes-chatgpt-as-fastestgrowing-platform-3122632>)

### XLF — Financials · Mixed

**Narrative** Reuters’ preview describes the market looking for higher interest income to offset sluggish dealmaking. Large banks have multiple earnings streams, with the cost of deposits and provisions by loan category particularly important to the consumer franchises. A steep EPS increase on a favourable funding spread may say little about new loan demand. [⁽25⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>)

**Reported** Ahead of large-bank earnings, Reuters described higher interest income competing with subdued investment banking. The Federal Reserve meanwhile put May consumer-credit growth at a 1.8% annual rate. Existing loans can reprice upwards as credit growth slows. The next question is what demand will replace maturing assets and at what deposit rate the bank must fund those new loans. [⁽25⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**EPS Upside Watch** The loan/deposit spread and a bank’s underwriting discipline can both look favourable before credit losses surface. Read reserve changes alongside revolving credit and household income. Avoid equating a higher policy rate with a certain gain in shareholder return. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>) [⁽25⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** An inverted curve can work for a lender with transaction deposits that reprice slowly and hurt a lender paying current money-market rates to retain funds. Both can report higher average loan yields. Compare the funding concentration, deposit cost and share of loans approaching maturity. A smaller bank with durable customer balances may be better placed than its asset size suggests. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽25⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-banking-giants-to-report-higher-profits-even-as-dealmaking-drags-3123116>)

### XLE — Energy · Mixed

**Narrative** Saudi Arabia’s announced August cut gives the supply side a concrete support. China’s falling producer prices and weak factory demand leave the opposite side of the balance less reassuring. A price rise led by exporters withdrawing barrels implies different downstream demand from a move led by orders for travel or freight. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.brecorder.com/news/40250757/oil-rallies-on-saudi-and-russian-supply-cuts-for-august>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**Reported** The EIA’s July outlook anticipated greater US gas-fired electricity generation in July and August than a year earlier. A producer sells at a regional gas price and pays to move the commodity. The generator earns against the difference between the local power price and its delivered fuel cost. A rise in physical burn can affect both businesses without producing the same margin direction. [⁽27⁾](<https://www.eia.gov/pressroom/releases/press536.php>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Watch whether tighter supply reaches the prices producers actually realise and whether it reduces the margin of firms buying fuel. An airline can absorb a modest rise while demand is strong, but every additional dollar of fuel cost still needs fares or operational efficiency to pay for it. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.brecorder.com/news/40250757/oil-rallies-on-saudi-and-russian-supply-cuts-for-august>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A commodity rally presents management with three different uses of the additional cash: fund more drilling, reduce debt or return cash to owners. For a company with service contracts and a steep decline in output at existing wells, much of the gain can be absorbed maintaining volume. Read development commitments alongside realised selling prices before treating stronger spot prices as a recurrent increase in distributions. [⁽27⁾](<https://www.eia.gov/pressroom/releases/press536.php>)

### XLI — Industrials · Mixed

**Narrative** The manufacturing PMI of 46.0 and services PMI of 53.9 describe very different US business conditions. A parts distributor selling into factories may see falling orders at the same time service operators add jobs. Investigate whether the company’s order book reflects actual consumption or inventory held by customers. [⁽5⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-53-9-june-2023-services-ism-report-on-business-301870344.html>)

**Reported** Japanese core machinery orders fell 7.6% in May and the US manufacturing survey registered contraction in June. The former concerns volatile orders for specific capital goods. The latter measures how widespread a change is across companies. Together they justify examining where discretionary projects have slipped and whether an individual supplier has sufficient backlog to fill its production schedule. [⁽15⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>) [⁽5⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>)

**EPS Upside Watch** The Japanese machinery-orders fall adds caution on capital spending. Orders for long-lived equipment can be delayed while installed machinery still requires maintenance. A supplier of replacement parts can therefore retain demand after a new-equipment order book begins to shrink. [⁽15⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Customers that defer new equipment can keep older machinery in service for longer. That helps vendors earning on parts, inspections and maintenance until customers idle the equipment altogether. Contract attachment rates, the age of the installed base and utilisation at customer plants distinguish defensive maintenance revenue from the sales lost when factories run fewer shifts. [⁽5⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>) [⁽15⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>)

### XLY — Consumer Discretionary · Mixed

**Narrative** Ford’s F-Series sales rose 34% in the second quarter. Clearing supply bottlenecks and strong commercial demand may raise deliveries. The dealer’s price, customer’s financing payment and manufacturer’s incentive decide how much of the volume improvement becomes earnings. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38009/000003800923000041/exhibit99tojuly62023ford.htm>)

**Reported** Amazon gained about 1.3% on July 11, the first day of its Prime Day event. A promotional sale may be new spending, an order brought forward from later in July or one diverted from another merchant. Each produces different consequences for retailer margin and wider demand. Gross merchandise sold alone cannot distinguish customers who would have paid full price the following week. ## Capital Flows & Liquidity Framework

### Capital flows, liquidity and the price of duration

**Global Liquidity** Federal Reserve data show US consumer credit increased at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1.8% in May. Household credit is a borrowing measure, distinct from asset-manager subscriptions, bank reserves and trading turnover. Slow new credit with ongoing job growth can leave a consumer spending from wages instead of taking on another payment. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>) [⁽2⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>)

**Rates & Dollar** The short Treasury maturity still offers a higher par yield than the ten-year. An investor choosing a longer bond trades away current yield in exchange for duration and a more durable rate lock. Evaluate that trade against the anticipated path of future short rates. Currency translation and hedging costs alter the calculation for non-US investors; Treasury yields alone do not establish a cross-border flow. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>)

**Credit Check** A borrower chooses whether to take another loan based on the expected cash return and the cost of servicing it. High short rates directly change the second parameter. Weekly changes in long bond prices affect collateral and valuation, but rebuilding credit demand can take longer. Check reported loan balances and loss provisions at the same time as the interest margin. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>) [⁽7⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230705a.htm>)

**Equity & Fund Flows** Nasdaq’s announced special rebalance is a mechanical reason to trade particular constituents independently of any company-specific earnings development. A flow generated to match a new index weight is different from a discretionary view about future profitability. The rebalance was scheduled for implementation before the July 24 open. [⁽35⁾](<https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nasdaq-100-index-special-rebalance-be-effective-july-24-2023>) The July 11 gain strengthened late in the session. Activision rose about 10% after a court ruling, Salesforce gained 3.9% on new prices and Amazon advanced 1.3% on the opening day of Prime Day. Those are separate company events. An index lift driven by them does not establish that institutional investors rotated into equal-weight exposure or that the participating money will remain after the immediate catalysts. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-11/wall-street-gets-a-late-push-ahead-of-inflation-data-activision-blizzard-jumps>)

**Portfolio Implication** Avoid a single risk-on label for movements in bank credit, bond yields and equity multiples. Where the thesis requires national credit to finance a real project, insist on evidence of new orders or transactions. Where it requires a securities fund to buy, seek product-level flows. Prices can change at the margin with little aggregate money changing hands. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

## What Retail Is Playing

A court decision has turned Activision into a cash-takeover trade with a visible upside and a much less certain downside.

### Activision Blizzard / ATVI — Four dollars for the remaining risk

**Why Retail Is Here** What makes Activision so tempting is that the buyer can point to an actual price. Microsoft has agreed to pay $95 a share in cash. Activision closed Tuesday at $90.99 after a US judge rejected the Federal Trade Commission's request for a preliminary injunction. The remaining difference is $4.01, down from $12.30 at Monday's close. Someone buying now can imagine a tidy gain from waiting for a transaction already announced, negotiated and tested in court. The appeal is greatest precisely when the arithmetic looks easiest. The target's fallback value, and the length of time the buyer might have to finance a position, are much harder to calculate. [⁽36⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-judge-rules-microsoft-deal-to-buy-activision-can-go-forward-3123485>) [⁽37⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/business/story/2023-07-11/microsoft-wins-u-s-court-nod-to-buy-activision-in-loss-for-ftc>)

**What Is Driving It** The US judge ruled on whether the FTC had shown enough to stop the acquisition while its challenge proceeds. The UK's Competition and Markets Authority separately indicated it would consider proposals to resolve its concerns. The distinction matters in cash terms. Tuesday's news removes a significant immediate obstacle in one jurisdiction and opens a possible route in another. It does not put $95 in anyone's brokerage account. The parties have a July 18 deadline under their agreement. Delay, appeal, the terms of a UK remedy or a decision to extend the agreement can change the expected return even if the parties continue to want the same deal. [⁽36⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-judge-rules-microsoft-deal-to-buy-activision-can-go-forward-3123485>) [⁽37⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/business/story/2023-07-11/microsoft-wins-u-s-court-nod-to-buy-activision-in-loss-for-ftc>)

**The Social Signal** A Tuesday WallStreetBets thread on the judgment shows why a cash merger is a powerful retail story. Participants discussed the offer price and questioned why the buyer's shares might behave differently from the target's. One person referred to owning calls, a self-reported position that says nothing about aggregate order flow. The question about the two stocks is more useful than a vote count. An Activision shareholder is buying a claim on a stated cash payment if conditions are met. A Microsoft shareholder owns the acquirer, which must pay the purchase price and then earn an adequate return on it. Two people reading the same ruling can reasonably choose entirely different trades. [⁽38⁾](<https://www.reddit.com/r/wallstreetbets/comments/14wuwti>) [⁽36⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-judge-rules-microsoft-deal-to-buy-activision-can-go-forward-3123485>)

**Historical Analog** Monday's close gives a compact example of how merger pricing works. The spread was broad enough to pay for significant uncertainty. Tuesday's court result narrowed it sharply in one step. The next buyer takes the remaining regulatory and timing risk for a much smaller dollar reward. If the path clears, another comparable price jump would require something other than the existing offer. If a new obstacle appears, much of Tuesday's advance may be exposed. An investor who sees the old chart as a momentum breakout should ask what mechanism could carry the cash claim meaningfully above the contracted price. The economics differ from a company beating earnings and revising its own profit potential upwards. [⁽37⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/business/story/2023-07-11/microsoft-wins-u-s-court-nod-to-buy-activision-in-loss-for-ftc>)

**Smart Money View** Merger investors generally compare the present value of the cash offer with a probability-weighted range of outcomes if the transaction is delayed or fails. A formal probability cannot be extracted from the $4.01 spread without assumptions about the independent value of Activision, the time to payment and funding costs. The UK regulator's willingness to talk improves one input. The FTC's ability to pursue the matter and the approaching deadline affect others. A smaller investor can use the same framework without trying to match a fund's position size. A fund following several transactions can spread the risk of one adverse decision. A single short-dated call cannot. [⁽36⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-judge-rules-microsoft-deal-to-buy-activision-can-go-forward-3123485>) [⁽37⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/business/story/2023-07-11/microsoft-wins-u-s-court-nod-to-buy-activision-in-loss-for-ftc>)

**When the Narrative Breaks** Track each jurisdiction separately and read the conditions on any proposed agreement. A timetable slipping beyond the option's expiry would break a timing trade even if Microsoft ultimately pays in full. A successful appeal or more burdensome regulatory conditions could reopen the question of completion and return some of the spread very quickly. If the shares rise close to $95 before those questions are resolved, the residual dollar gain becomes small relative to the loss on a reversal. That is a sensible point to trim rather than treating the last few cents as free money. The next decision should follow the legal process and the purchase terms, not another game's sales figures. [⁽36⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-judge-rules-microsoft-deal-to-buy-activision-can-go-forward-3123485>) [⁽37⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/business/story/2023-07-11/microsoft-wins-u-s-court-nod-to-buy-activision-in-loss-for-ftc>)

The broker's historical buying gives market context; it offers no commitment that those clients will defend the same level again. ### Chinese equities / FXI — Cheap prices can mean weak pricing power

**Why Retail Is Here** Chinese producer prices fell 5.4% from a year earlier in June, while the consumer price index was flat year on year. Falling inflation gives policymakers more room to support credit or consumption, and markets can start pricing that before households see a change. There is a competing earnings effect. A company selling into weak final demand may have to take lower prices at precisely the point an equity investor is looking for a recovery in profits. [⁽40⁾](<https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-listings-of-chinese-stocks-edge-lower-after-june-cpi-data-3040529>)

**Where It Breaks** Look for signs that easing actually leads to stronger orders, credit demand or purchasing power. A policy announcement that lifts equities briefly while producer prices keep falling would leave much of the profit question unresolved. A large-cap index also has its own sector weights; it can rise on regulatory relief in one large holding even if conditions for industrial businesses keep worsening. Avoid treating that as a broad verdict on Chinese demand. [⁽40⁾](<https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-listings-of-chinese-stocks-edge-lower-after-june-cpi-data-3040529>)
