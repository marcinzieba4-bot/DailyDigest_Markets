## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The US picture is now easier to read against a concrete foreign comparison. June American service activity expanded according to ISM and US payrolls rose. China reported just 0.8% sequential growth in its second quarter despite a stronger annual comparison. A US domestic service company and a US-listed industrial selling into China face substantially different orders. Their common listing currency does little to reconcile the earnings paths. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-53-9-june-2023-services-ism-report-on-business-301870344.html>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>) [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

**Rates & Curve** The US Treasury two-year yielded 4.74% and the ten-year 3.81% at the July 17 close. The ten-year less the two-year stood at −93 basis points. The two-year yield was up 12 basis points and the ten-year nine over twenty observations. The short end retained a premium even with headline inflation down at 3.0%. A company rolling near-term debt and one able to lock in longer fixed-rate borrowing face different costs even before their credit risks are taken into account. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽5⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202306>)

**New / Most Important** The Black Sea grain agreement’s interruption raises potential food and freight costs even after a softer US inflation release. The route from global shipping to the price of a finished consumer product depends on contracts, inventories and substitute supplies. Treat this as a new risk to monitor rather than an immediate revision to every consumer price forecast. [⁽6⁾](<https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/press-events/2023-07-17/secretary-generals-press-encounter-the-black-sea-initiative>) [⁽7⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_07122023.htm>)

**Playbook ✓** Keep US service demand in the positive case while checking whether manufacturers and lenders see the same customer strength. A soft retail release paired with persistent wage growth would shift the focus from headline inflation to the distribution of disposable income. Companies selling a low monthly payment may face pressure first. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>)

### European Union · Mixed

**Macro Stance** China’s second-quarter release adds substance to the German ZEW warning. A European capital-goods producer can sell electric equipment into one growing segment and still experience delays across the wider Chinese industrial client base. Watch export orders and the mix of shipments, especially where the customer’s investment depends on real estate. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

**Credit & Inflation** June’s flash annual inflation estimate of 5.5% was lower than May’s 6.1%. A declining rate of inflation leaves the price level higher and does not reverse the higher borrowing costs businesses have already absorbed. The gap matters for retailers. A cash-constrained family can buy fewer goods even when the monthly increase in its grocery bill is smaller. [⁽10⁾](<https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/2995521/17075124/2-30062023-AP-EN.pdf/230735d1-74e6-4d30-2039-c9ed1e660296>) Destatis put German June inflation at 6.4% annually and 0.3% for the month. The national reading and the euro-area measure describe different baskets and geographies. Divergence between them can leave a German household paying materially higher bills while a multinational firm reports easing input expense from Asia. Read a company’s customer base and manufacturing location separately before choosing the relevant price index. [⁽11⁾](<https://www.destatis.de/EN/Press/2023/07/PE23_270_611.html>)

**New / Most Important** Mercedes reported that second-quarter deliveries rose 6% with top-end and battery-electric models contributing. The more informative question for suppliers is which platforms and regions account for the change. An increase concentrated in premium vehicles benefits different component and software suppliers from one driven by small, inexpensive vehicles. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/mercedes-sales-rise-in-q2-on-topend-electric-vehicles-3123004>)

**Playbook ✓** Look for pricing, order conversion and the source of financing before treating a cheaper European equity multiple as sufficient compensation. The most vulnerable supplier sells into an end market with shrinking orders and has little ability to reset prices against fixed labour or energy costs. Reassess if advance orders and actual output turn up together. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

### China · Mixed

**Macro Stance** Second-quarter real GDP rose 6.3% from a year earlier but only 0.8% from the preceding quarter. A favourable year-ago comparison can make annual activity seem strong to an exporter who has seen almost no sequential acceleration in orders. Use the quarterly change and the actual end market served by the company before assigning a growth multiple. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

**PMIs & Activity** June manufacturing PMI stood at 49.0. The combination of manufacturing below 50 and positive GDP growth is plausible when services brighten and factories order less new material. Avoid treating weakness in a metal-intensive sector as an economy-wide output collapse, or resilience in services as evidence of an industrial boom. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

**Credit Impulse** New yuan loans reached RMB 3.05 trillion in June. Money supply M2 rose 11.3% year over year, while M1 increased 3.1%. The quantities indicate ample balance-sheet expansion but do not identify a marginal borrower or establish that new projects are clearing investment hurdles. Distinguish refinancing, public-sector spending and private-sector expansion before inferring industrial orders. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Property** First-half property development investment fell 7.9% and floor area sold fell 5.3% year over year. Lower construction activity directly reduces materials demand. Falling contracted floor area can also weaken a developer’s future cash receipts. A lender rolling over an existing facility may buy time without restoring the cash inflow needed to start new projects. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Fiscal, Policy & Trade** Dollar exports fell 12.4% in June from a year earlier. That creates an obvious reason to examine external demand alongside domestic stimulus. Exports are measured in value and their year-on-year change can reflect price and product mix. A producer reporting full factories may have defended volume at the cost of margin. Read shipments against producer prices. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/chinas-june-exports-fall-124-imports-drop-68-3124753>) [⁽15⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**Markets & Flows** A bank balance sheet, a foreign portfolio flow and a listed company’s earnings represent different claims. China’s lending data say much more about the first than the second. For industrial equities, demand a connection from credit to a customer’s capital expenditure and from that project to the supplier’s cash collection. The link can be long, especially when falling producer prices make additional inventory unattractive. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽15⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**New / Most Important** The GDP release puts property alongside the aggregate numbers as the clearest source of divergence. A household may resume service consumption while avoiding the much larger commitment of a home purchase. Stronger sales at domestic service providers offer limited comfort to a developer waiting for contracted floor area to recover. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Use bank lending as an input and actual orders, home contracts and price realisation as the confirmation. A borrower able to roll debt has solved a financing problem. It may still have no economic reason to buy more machines. ⚠ Divergence: credit increased sharply in June while producer prices continued to decline. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽15⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### Japan · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The June Tankan put the business-conditions balance at plus 5 for large manufacturers and plus 23 for large nonmanufacturers. Optimism in services can support domestic revenue without turning the inventory cycle in export manufacturing. Machinery orders provide a more direct test of spending intentions than an aggregate equity multiple. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>)

**Activity & Currency** Core machinery orders fell 7.6% in May from the prior month, a result first reported at 23:51 UTC on July 11. A volatile month of orders can be dominated by the timing of large contracts. The cost of getting that interpretation wrong is largest at suppliers whose profit depends on running plants near capacity. Currency moves, imported costs and price discipline also influence the gain from any future volume recovery. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>) [⁽16⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Compare the improvement in Japanese business sentiment with the slower sequential Chinese growth figure. For a Japanese equipment exporter, a customer’s order and delivery schedule is a better trigger than the two countries’ headline GDP comparisons. Domestic service companies face a different demand test. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽16⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>)

### Australia · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The Reserve Bank held its cash rate at 4.10% on July 4. A pause lets existing tightening work through mortgage payments and consumer spending. A household refinancing at a higher rate can reduce discretionary purchases even while still employed. That is a demand problem for a retailer before it becomes a credit-loss problem for the bank. [⁽18⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>)

**Demand & Financing** Westpac reported a rise in its consumer sentiment index from 79.2 to 81.3 in July. The improvement leaves sentiment weak in absolute terms. A recovery in a survey after the central bank pauses may reflect relief rather than a willingness to make a large purchase. Watch order values, cancellations and credit use before extrapolating the bounce to listed discretionary earnings. [⁽19⁾](<https://www.westpaciq.com.au/economics/2023/07/consumer-sentiment-july-2023>)

**Playbook ✓** Treat the Chinese property release as a direct test for bulk-commodity exposures and the RBA decision as a separate test for Australian consumers. Strong Australian business conditions today can coexist with weaker demand for exports tied to Chinese construction. Watch actual customer orders and prices. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽18⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>)

### Poland · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The National Bank of Poland held its reference rate at 6.75%. A high rate changes how quickly an individual household or business can absorb a price shock. For banks, it can support interest income on repricing assets, but deposit holders seek better terms and a smaller number of creditworthy borrowers may want to take loans. [⁽20⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>)

**Markets** Focus on the transmission from financing to earnings. A bank’s interest margin measures a spread on its existing balance sheet. A manufacturer’s investment plan depends on the expected cash generated by a new project. Both face the same policy rate but the immediate benefit or cost can point in opposite directions. A change in the zloty can also help importers and hurt exporters in different proportions. [⁽20⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) The June core measure excluding food and energy eased to 11.1% year on year, down from 11.5%, and rose 0.3% from May. The remaining rate of underlying inflation matters when markets price future cuts from 6.75%. A bank can benefit from high rates on existing assets while its customer faces weaker cash flow and a higher hurdle for new borrowing. [⁽21⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/poland-s-core-inflation-eases-growth-further-to-11-1-y-y-in-june-284783/>) [⁽20⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>)

**Fiscal & Politics** Statistics Poland’s final June CPI showed prices 11.5% higher than a year earlier and unchanged from May. The price level remains demanding for consumers, even as monthly inflation slows. A transfer or wage adjustment stated in nominal zloty needs to be judged against that continuing pressure on purchasing power. [⁽22⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/prices-trade/price-indices/consumer-price-indices-in-june-2023,2,140.html>)

**Corporate & Sectors** Polish manufacturing PMI fell to 45.1 in June. An exporter serving German industry is exposed to customer confidence and order timing across the border. A domestic lender instead depends on payment quality, interest margins and the availability of profitable new credit. One national equity index can contain both stories and disclose little about the direction of the individual business. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

**Geopolitics & Security** Russia’s withdrawal of security guarantees for the Black Sea grain route introduces a transport and pricing question for Poland. Shipments diverted toward overland routes may increase demand for transport, yet local storage, rail availability and border policy determine who gets paid and at what cost. No supplier’s earnings can be inferred from a map alone. [⁽6⁾](<https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/press-events/2023-07-17/secretary-generals-press-encounter-the-black-sea-initiative>)

**New / Most Important** Black Sea shipping has become a more immediate issue than the broad security declarations from Vilnius. Monitor specific transit arrangements and delivery schedules when assessing Polish rail, storage and agricultural exposures. [⁽6⁾](<https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/press-events/2023-07-17/secretary-generals-press-encounter-the-black-sea-initiative>) [⁽24⁾](<https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/official-texts-and-resources/official-texts/2023/07/11/vilnius-summit-communique?selectedLocale=ge>)

**Playbook ✓** Track domestic price pressure, bank funding costs and industrial orders before extrapolating broad Polish market strength into domestic earnings. A reduction in inflation coupled with weak new manufacturing business could support bonds and disappoint industrial shareholders. ⚠ Divergence: policy rates remain high as June manufacturing sentiment signals contraction. [⁽20⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) [⁽23⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>)

## Sector Deep Dive

### XLK — Technology · Mixed

**Narrative** Industry attention is concentrated on data and artificial intelligence, while TSMC’s June revenue fell 11.1% year over year. Cloud services and chip fabrication occupy different points in the chain. Buying a technology index assumes much more than the proposition that one class of accelerator is scarce. [⁽25⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

**Reported** Alphabet and Nvidia each gained about 4.7% in the July 13 session as Google expanded Bard to additional markets. Nvidia sells capital equipment into the first stage of the buildout. Alphabet must recover growing computing expense through existing or new customer activity. A common price move therefore tells us little about the timing of cash generation at the individual companies. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>)

**EPS Upside Watch** TSMC’s NT$156.40 billion June revenue was down 11.4% from May. Monthly invoicing is noisy, but a decline of that size leaves the burden of proof on claims of a sector-wide upswing. Check several shipments and end markets before assigning the same multiple improvement to client computing and enterprise workloads. [⁽25⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A successful chatbot launch can make a search product more expensive to operate before the first additional advertising dollar arrives. Examine inference cost per query, the proportion of queries carrying ads and the price advertisers pay for those leads. Revenue reaches the supplier once the customer buys computing hardware; for the platform, that purchase begins a stream of costs which it must still recover. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>) [⁽25⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

### XLC — Communication Services · Mixed

**Narrative** Consumer attention and a profitable subscription have different unit economics. The spending data give a broad picture of household budgets. A platform reliant on advertising depends on the merchant’s expected return on that spending. A subscription service depends on retention after the initial customer signs up. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**Reported** AT&T shares fell nearly 7% on July 17 after concerns about old lead-sheathed cables prompted analyst downgrades. Investors still had to establish the network footprint, legal responsibility, condition of the cables and potential cost. A sudden loss of equity value records revised market expectations, without providing an invoice or expenditure schedule against which to test them. [⁽27⁾](<https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/att-shares-hit-threedecade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-3046948>)

**EPS Upside Watch** For an advertising platform, test user retention, advertiser demand and monetisable inventory in that order. A company can reach a large audience before it can sell sufficient advertising at attractive prices. A change in interest rates offers valuation support but cannot supply a missing advertiser. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A mature network and an advertising platform sit in the same sector with sharply different claims on cash. AT&T’s dividend depends on what remains after capital spending and any future liability. At a streaming company an outlay can enlarge a library or attract subscribers. A sector average for earnings or dividends obscures the financing needs and cash commitments that matter to each shareholder. [⁽27⁾](<https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/att-shares-hit-threedecade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-3046948>)

### XLF — Financials · Mixed

**Narrative** JPMorgan’s $14.5 billion profit includes First Republic. The additional assets, liabilities and acquisition-related accounting affect comparisons, so look at growth in net interest income, loan losses and the cost of deposits before assuming that the headline profit sets a sector-wide earnings run rate. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>)

**Reported** JPMorgan’s $14.5 billion quarterly net income includes the acquired First Republic franchise. The new assets, customers and purchase accounting all change the comparison with last year. Put interest income and operating costs on a consistent perimeter first, then examine the rate needed to retain deposits and the reserve required against new and existing borrowers. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>)

**EPS Upside Watch** The most useful comparison is the share of earnings arising from repricing assets versus from origination and fee growth. Competition for term deposits can outlast a policy-rate hold. Compare actual deposit rates and mix with credit-loss provisioning. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Deposits moving from a regional institution to a large franchise can improve one balance sheet and leave system-wide borrowing demand almost untouched. A broad bank rally can obscure the higher funding cost to the firm losing those accounts. Distinguish a customer coming for operational services from funds placed temporarily while clients wait for a more attractive yield. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>) [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>)

### XLE — Energy · Mixed

**Narrative** The IEA’s July oil market report highlights the difference between exports, refinery runs and upstream production. It put Russian oil exports at 7.3 million barrels a day in June, down 600,000 barrels a day. A lower flow leaving the country can tighten particular markets without equivalent changes in global consumption. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

**Reported** China reported just 0.8% sequential second-quarter growth. That demand-side reading arrives alongside the IEA’s analysis of physical production and exports. When a slower economy coincides with constrained quantities available from certain exporters, crude, refining and petrochemical margins can respond differently. The question for equity investors is which product the company sells and which input it must purchase. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽29⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A producer with stable volumes and rising realised prices gains directly from tighter supply. A refiner depends instead on product prices relative to crude and the utilisation of its units. Track refining margins and the price achieved on actual cargoes before translating the direction of a futures contract into earnings. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Global output and consumption expressed in barrels tell less about shareholders than the realised price after quality, transport and hedging. The oil benchmark can stabilise while local discounts widen or a refinery struggles to shift a particular product. Compare production with sales volumes and working capital, especially where export logistics can leave cargoes in transit at the reporting date. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>) [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

### XLI — Industrials · Mixed

**Narrative** Fastenal’s second quarter showed slower growth in sales and margins as demand softened. An industrial distributor sees orders close to the time customers use a product. That exposure is different from a capital-goods manufacturer recognising sales from a backlog negotiated many months earlier. Replenishment and final demand can come apart. [⁽30⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>)

**Reported** Fastenal’s second quarter provides evidence from small components sold into many factories. A distributor can see changes in daily orders before an equipment maker with a multiquarter backlog shows material revenue pressure. Compare the performance of established customers against growth from opening on-site operations: new locations can increase reported sales while average consumption at older sites falls. [⁽30⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>) [⁽31⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Delta’s record June-quarter revenue shows strength in passenger transport even as factory demand remains soft. Treat airlines and component distributors as separate earnings exposures. Their classification in industrials says little about how they respond to a purchase order or fuel shock. [⁽32⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>) [⁽30⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Fasteners and other maintenance purchases can reveal customer activity but are also affected by distributor gain or loss of share. Examine established locations, frequency and size of orders, and where invoices are being collected more slowly. A quarterly gain won by converting a customer to on-site supply has a different implication for the industrial cycle from the same gain in underlying factory utilisation. [⁽30⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>)

### XLY — Consumer Discretionary · Mixed

**Narrative** Delta raised its full-year earnings outlook to $6–7 per share and kept its $3 billion free cash flow outlook. Consumers can protect travel plans while economising on discretionary merchandise. A strong airline result is therefore a particularly poor substitute for a retailer’s own unit figures. [⁽32⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)

**Reported** Ford reduced the entry F-150 Lightning price by about 17%. Lower sticker prices reduce the amount financed by a new buyer but can also erode residual values on vehicles already on dealer lots or under lease. A manufacturer, its captive finance division, a car dealer and an existing truck owner therefore face different cash consequences from one showroom decision. [⁽33⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/ford-slashes-prices-of-f150-lightning-trucks-as-ev-wars-heat-up-3127006>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Watch volumes at actual transaction prices after incentives. Growth financed by a larger price concession changes operating leverage differently from growth at a stable contribution per sale. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Compare incremental gross profit on genuinely additional deliveries with the lost revenue on trucks that would have sold at the original price. If costs are falling rapidly the manufacturer can fund part of the reduction from the factory. Financing terms and used values determine whether the consumer sees an equally large improvement in the monthly payment. ## Capital Flows & Liquidity Framework

**Global Liquidity** Lipper’s July 13 report recorded a net $18.0 billion withdrawal across the US fund universe in the week ended July 12 even as taxable bond funds attracted $3.4 billion. **Rates & Dollar** The short Treasury maturity still offers a higher par yield than the ten-year. **Credit Check** JPMorgan’s result reflects a lending and deposit franchise that has changed in size. **Equity & Fund Flows** CoinShares’ bitcoin-led fund inflows concern a specialist product universe. Lipper’s bond figures describe a different set of vehicles and a different reporting week. [⁽45⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-17/wall-street-drifts-as-stocks-worldwide-stall-after-weak-chinese-data>) [⁽33⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/ford-slashes-prices-of-f150-lightning-trucks-as-ev-wars-heat-up-3127006>) [⁽27⁾](<https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/att-shares-hit-threedecade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-3046948>)

**Portfolio Implication** Avoid a single risk-on label for movements in bank credit, bond yields and equity multiples. Where the thesis requires national credit to finance a real project, insist on evidence of new orders or transactions. Where it requires a securities fund to buy, seek product-level flows. Prices can change at the margin with little aggregate money changing hands. ## Anticipatory Positioning — Markets Pricing In Future Events

### Lower inflation survives the next test · Mixed

**What Is Priced** US CPI rose 0.2% month over month in June, including the core index. The immediate disinflation case is visible, but a single observation leaves an open question about how long companies will be able to pass wage costs into service prices. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_07122023.htm>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>)

**Conditional Expression** A sustained cooling in service prices alongside continued job growth would favour businesses that can hold unit demand as the discount rate falls. A weaker inflation print produced by disappearing customer demand would carry a different earnings consequence. Assess the income statement before raising a valuation multiple simply because yields have fallen. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>) [⁽2⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-53-9-june-2023-services-ism-report-on-business-301870344.html>)

**Risk** If earnings expectations come down faster than market yields, a bond rally can coexist with weaker equities. Check realised activity and management guidance alongside policy pricing. [⁽37⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230705a.htm>)

### Housing activity confirms or contradicts China lending · Mixed

**What Is Priced** Chinese property investment fell 7.9% in the first half. The June lending total offers a potential source of funding; it has not yet repaired the physical activity numbers. For a materials supplier, contract awards and customer cash receipts will be the evidence of transmission. [⁽4⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Conditional Expression** Prefer suppliers whose order books show financed projects starting rather than general exposure to a broad Chinese credit aggregate. A customer advancing a deposit and accepting delivery creates a clearer signal than an announced lending facility. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Risk** Refinancing can absorb credit without creating construction volumes, and falling unit prices reduce nominal revenue even when physical deliveries hold up. [⁽15⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### The banking earnings hurdle · Mixed

**What Is Priced** JPMorgan’s early earnings set a high visible benchmark, but the changing perimeter following First Republic makes direct extrapolation to other banks unreliable. A bank with fewer low-cost customer balances may need to pay away more of the benefit of higher asset yields. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>)

**Conditional Expression** Read the operating margin alongside average deposits, product mix and the provision for future losses. A bank defending its deposit base with higher rates can report improved stability well before the cost of funding turns down. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**Risk** If a borrower’s income fails to keep pace with financing costs, loan-loss provisions can erode an apparent improvement in interest margins. The highest current yield may belong to the borrower taking the greatest risk. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>)

### A packaging customer resists higher prices · Mixed

**What Is Priced** PepsiCo’s higher organic growth guidance and Conagra’s more complicated volume and cost picture present two ways of earning from a household food budget. Store traffic on its own does not reveal which product the customer buys, or the promotion required to move it. [⁽34⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77476/000007747623000048/q220238-kxexhibit991.htm>) [⁽35⁾](<https://www.conagrabrands.com/news-room/news-conagra-brands-reports-fourth-quarter-results-prn-122856>)

**Conditional Expression** Look for stable volumes at an acceptable gross margin once last year’s price increases are in both periods. When unit growth and price both hold, there is less need for promotions to fill the revenue gap. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**Risk** A customer switching to a private-label rival can protect household consumption while hurting the branded producer’s volume. Headline consumer resilience may therefore mislead an investor modelled only on aggregate spending. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

## What Retail Is Playing

Monday gave the AT&T dip buyer something uncomfortable to think about. A dividend stock can be cheap for years and still fall sharply when an unpriced liability appears. In growth stocks the week’s questions are more familiar. Tesla needs to explain the economics behind its deliveries, Nvidia has become a test of earnings expectations, and Coinbase has to keep customers trading after a legal headline. The clearest one-sided retail order count belongs to the phone companies. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/VERIZON-COMMUNICATIONS-IN-4830/news/AT-T-shares-hit-three-decade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-44351429/>) [⁽47⁾](<https://discussion.fool.com/t/market-movers-for-14-jul-2023/94455>)

### AT&T and Verizon / T, VZ — The dividend buyer inherits the cable

**Why Retail Is Here** An investor who saw AT&T on Friday as a cheap claim on decades of wireless bills saw the same cash receipts on Monday, attached to a much less certain bill of a different kind. Reporting on decades-old lead-sheathed cables has raised questions about environmental testing, removal, litigation and how those costs would be allocated. The shares touched $13.68 Monday, the lowest level since 1993 according to Reuters. That is an easy price to compare with past dividends and an exceptionally difficult price to compare with an unknown legal obligation. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/VERIZON-COMMUNICATIONS-IN-4830/news/AT-T-shares-hit-three-decade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-44351429/>)

**What Is Driving It** Two judgments produce the bull case. First, significant sections of the old network may never need expensive remediation. Second, any eventual payments may be dispersed over enough years that wireless cash receipts, debt service and dividend payments can coexist. The bear case requires neither an immediate government order nor a court verdict. A long period without a credible liability estimate can lift the return shareholders demand and delay a recovery in the share price. An income investor might collect the dividend throughout that wait while reporting a poor total return. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/VERIZON-COMMUNICATIONS-IN-4830/news/AT-T-shares-hit-three-decade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-44351429/>)

**The Social Signal** Friday's Fidelity evidence is striking because it gives both sides of the ticket. On a 4.10% decline in T, customers entered 5,372 buy orders against 983 sells; on a 1.82% fall in VZ they entered 2,821 against 462. Counts are unequal even after allowing for AT&T's larger move. They cover one broker on one day. Calling Monday a second day of equally strong retail accumulation would require a fresh observation. It is entirely possible that some of Friday's buyers decided to wait after seeing the fresh low. [⁽47⁾](<https://discussion.fool.com/t/market-movers-for-14-jul-2023/94455>) [⁽46⁾](<https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/VERIZON-COMMUNICATIONS-IN-4830/news/AT-T-shares-hit-three-decade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-44351429/>)

**Historical Analog** Think of the way a high dividend behaves when a utility faces an environmental claim. The yield increases automatically when the price falls; the money available to pay the dividend does not. When investigation becomes a timetable of probable payments, analysts can estimate the bill and discount the stream. Until then a buyer is selling insurance against a wide range of outcomes in exchange for the yield. The analogy identifies the valuation problem. It supplies no evidence that a historical clean-up cost per mile can be transferred to a telephone network. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/VERIZON-COMMUNICATIONS-IN-4830/news/AT-T-shares-hit-three-decade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-44351429/>)

**Smart Money View** The professionals disagree on the size of precisely that distribution. Citi saw exposure difficult to quantify across a wide legacy network. JPMorgan pointed to AT&T's local and long-distance assets and said its industry contacts could not produce a reasonable liability estimate. Morningstar's Michael Hodel expected the sector to avoid substantial legal liability. These are competing judgments from people looking at the same public reports. None has the authority to assign remediation costs. A near-six-times forward earnings multiple looks inexpensive if estimates stand, and expensive if cash that once funded investment has to be diverted elsewhere. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/VERIZON-COMMUNICATIONS-IN-4830/news/AT-T-shares-hit-three-decade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-44351429/>)

**When the Narrative Breaks** The dip needs facts on who owns the cables, where they are, what tests show and what obligations follow. A costed plan within existing cash generation could invalidate the more aggressive bear case. A widening investigation coupled with a close below Monday's $13.68 low would make Friday's apparent bargain look premature. Watch Verizon alongside AT&T. Divergence after an issuer-specific disclosure would be evidence that investors can distinguish two liability profiles. Another simultaneous selloff on little new evidence would tell us the whole sector is still being charged for uncertainty. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/VERIZON-COMMUNICATIONS-IN-4830/news/AT-T-shares-hit-three-decade-low-as-lead-cables-risks-weigh-44351429/>) [⁽47⁾](<https://discussion.fool.com/t/market-movers-for-14-jul-2023/94455>)
