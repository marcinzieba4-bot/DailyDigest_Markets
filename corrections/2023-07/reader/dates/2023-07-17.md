## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The first large US bank results complicate an easy soft-landing interpretation. JPMorgan reported $14.5 billion in net income, a quarter including First Republic. Net interest income benefits when assets reprice ahead of deposits. The question is how long that advantage lasts and whether households can keep borrowing at the new rate. Separate earnings on the combined balance sheet from a change in the underlying demand for credit. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**Rates & Curve** The US Treasury two-year yielded 4.74% and the ten-year 3.83% at the July 14 close. The ten-year less the two-year stood at −91 basis points. Both maturities ended at the same yield as twenty observations earlier. The moves around the inflation releases mattered to traders despite leaving the twenty-observation curve almost exactly where it began. A company rolling near-term debt and one able to lock in longer fixed-rate borrowing face different costs even before their credit risks are taken into account. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽4⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202306>)

**New / Most Important** US consumer prices and producer prices softened over the month, while the early earnings season has begun to distinguish businesses that can preserve sales volumes from those relying on pricing. For the next stage, operating margins matter more than whether a company can beat one analyst number. [⁽5⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_07122023.htm>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_07132023.htm>) [⁽7⁾](<https://insight.factset.com/sp-500-earnings-season-update-july-14-2023>)

**Playbook ✓** Compare deposit costs and provision trends across lenders before applying a large bank’s result to smaller institutions. The next spending and production releases can indicate whether a strong quarter for the banking system coincided with resilient real activity. The rate curve alone offers little discrimination between the two. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>) [⁽8⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230705a.htm>)

### European Union · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The 12.4% year-over-year fall in China’s June dollar exports is a warning about world goods demand, not a direct measure of orders received by European exporters. A European producer selling equipment to a Chinese exporter nevertheless faces an indirect consequence if that customer delays capacity additions. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/chinas-june-exports-fall-124-imports-drop-68-3124753>) [⁽10⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

**Credit & Inflation** June’s flash annual inflation estimate of 5.5% was lower than May’s 6.1%. A declining rate of inflation leaves the price level higher and does not reverse the higher borrowing costs businesses have already absorbed. The gap matters for retailers. A cash-constrained family can buy fewer goods even when the monthly increase in its grocery bill is smaller. [⁽11⁾](<https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/2995521/17075124/2-30062023-AP-EN.pdf/230735d1-74e6-4d30-2039-c9ed1e660296>) Destatis put German June inflation at 6.4% annually and 0.3% for the month. The national reading and the euro-area measure describe different baskets and geographies. Divergence between them can leave a German household paying materially higher bills while a multinational firm reports easing input expense from Asia. Read a company’s customer base and manufacturing location separately before choosing the relevant price index. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.destatis.de/EN/Press/2023/07/PE23_270_611.html>)

**New / Most Important** Mercedes reported that second-quarter deliveries rose 6% with top-end and battery-electric models contributing. The more informative question for suppliers is which platforms and regions account for the change. An increase concentrated in premium vehicles benefits different component and software suppliers from one driven by small, inexpensive vehicles. [⁽13⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/mercedes-sales-rise-in-q2-on-topend-electric-vehicles-3123004>)

**Playbook ✓** Look for pricing, order conversion and the source of financing before treating a cheaper European equity multiple as sufficient compensation. The most vulnerable supplier sells into an end market with shrinking orders and has little ability to reset prices against fixed labour or energy costs. Reassess if advance orders and actual output turn up together. [⁽10⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

### China · Mixed

**Macro Stance** June price data and credit flows point in different directions. Consumer prices were flat year over year and producer prices fell 5.4%. Banks can expand lending through the quarter end while nominal pricing for the companies receiving that credit remains weak. For shareholders the key distinction is between maintaining production and earning a satisfactory return on the assets that finance it. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>) [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**PMIs & Activity** Factory-gate deflation is a nominal signal. A producer may ship the same physical volume and receive less cash, or sell more tonnes while nominal revenue stagnates. Credit metrics measured in yuan and operating data measured in units can diverge. Ask whether lower input costs benefit the downstream manufacturer enough to compensate for the selling-price decline. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**Credit Impulse** New yuan loans reached RMB 3.05 trillion in June. Money supply M2 rose 11.3% year over year, while M1 increased 3.1%. The quantities indicate ample balance-sheet expansion but do not identify a marginal borrower or establish that new projects are clearing investment hurdles. Distinguish refinancing, public-sector spending and private-sector expansion before inferring industrial orders. [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Property** A mortgage, a loan to finish an existing apartment and financing for a new land purchase can all raise lending. Their consequences for steel, cement, furnishings and household confidence differ. Watch presold homes handed over and transaction volumes alongside credit. A stronger banking aggregate on its own provides little basis for forecasting a developer’s sales or a European supplier’s construction orders. [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Fiscal, Policy & Trade** Dollar exports fell 12.4% in June from a year earlier. That creates an obvious reason to examine external demand alongside domestic stimulus. Exports are measured in value and their year-on-year change can reflect price and product mix. A producer reporting full factories may have defended volume at the cost of margin. Read shipments against producer prices. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/chinas-june-exports-fall-124-imports-drop-68-3124753>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**Markets & Flows** A bank balance sheet, a foreign portfolio flow and a listed company’s earnings represent different claims. China’s lending data say much more about the first than the second. For industrial equities, demand a connection from credit to a customer’s capital expenditure and from that project to the supplier’s cash collection. The link can be long, especially when falling producer prices make additional inventory unattractive. [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**New / Most Important** Before the next domestic activity numbers, the observable split is between June loan growth and weak export prices and volumes. Resist assuming that a stronger year-on-year headline will mean the same thing to a hotel operator and a construction materials supplier. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/chinas-june-exports-fall-124-imports-drop-68-3124753>) [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Use bank lending as an input and actual orders, home contracts and price realisation as the confirmation. A borrower able to roll debt has solved a financing problem. It may still have no economic reason to buy more machines. ⚠ Divergence: credit increased sharply in June while producer prices continued to decline. [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### Japan · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The June Tankan put the business-conditions balance at plus 5 for large manufacturers and plus 23 for large nonmanufacturers. Optimism in services can support domestic revenue without turning the inventory cycle in export manufacturing. Machinery orders provide a more direct test of spending intentions than an aggregate equity multiple. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>)

**Activity & Currency** Core machinery orders fell 7.6% in May from the prior month, a result first reported at 23:51 UTC on July 11. A volatile month of orders can be dominated by the timing of large contracts. The cost of getting that interpretation wrong is largest at suppliers whose profit depends on running plants near capacity. Currency moves, imported costs and price discipline also influence the gain from any future volume recovery. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>) [⁽16⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Focus on companies that can distinguish order intake, backlog and deliveries in their disclosures. If another weak machinery release coincides with a deterioration in manufacturing respondents’ expectations, the capex thesis deserves a lower multiple. Stronger domestic service conditions offer a separate avenue for earnings rather than a wholesale hedge. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230703_023877.html>) [⁽17⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-machinery-orders-mom-came-in-at-76-below-forecasts-1-in-may-202307112351>)

### Australia · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The Reserve Bank held its cash rate at 4.10% on July 4. A pause lets existing tightening work through mortgage payments and consumer spending. A household refinancing at a higher rate can reduce discretionary purchases even while still employed. That is a demand problem for a retailer before it becomes a credit-loss problem for the bank. [⁽18⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>)

**Demand & Financing** Westpac reported a rise in its consumer sentiment index from 79.2 to 81.3 in July. The improvement leaves sentiment weak in absolute terms. A recovery in a survey after the central bank pauses may reflect relief rather than a willingness to make a large purchase. Watch order values, cancellations and credit use before extrapolating the bounce to listed discretionary earnings. [⁽19⁾](<https://www.westpaciq.com.au/economics/2023/07/consumer-sentiment-july-2023>)

**Playbook ✓** Household-facing companies must preserve volume and pricing as mortgages reprice. Mining companies instead need capable customers in China and prices that cover production and project costs. An unchanged Australian rate does not repair deflation in Chinese producer prices. Analyse the two exposures independently. [⁽18⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### Poland · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The National Bank of Poland held its reference rate at 6.75%. A high rate changes how quickly an individual household or business can absorb a price shock. For banks, it can support interest income on repricing assets, but deposit holders seek better terms and a smaller number of creditworthy borrowers may want to take loans. [⁽20⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>)

**Markets** Focus on the transmission from financing to earnings. A bank’s interest margin measures a spread on its existing balance sheet. A manufacturer’s investment plan depends on the expected cash generated by a new project. Both face the same policy rate but the immediate benefit or cost can point in opposite directions. A change in the zloty can also help importers and hurt exporters in different proportions. [⁽20⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) ING’s July 14 reading of Poland’s balance of payments identified automotive exports, including lithium-ion batteries and commercial vehicles, among the areas of strength. It estimated a rolling twelve-month current account deficit of 0.3% of GDP in May after 0.7% in April. A better external balance can improve the country’s financing position while a specific factory still suffers a fall in its new order book. [⁽21⁾](<https://think.ing.com/articles/surprisingly-high-polands-current-account-surplus-in-may/>)

**Fiscal & Politics** Statistics Poland’s final June CPI showed prices 11.5% higher than a year earlier and unchanged from May. The price level remains demanding for consumers, even as monthly inflation slows. A transfer or wage adjustment stated in nominal zloty needs to be judged against that continuing pressure on purchasing power. [⁽22⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/prices-trade/price-indices/consumer-price-indices-in-june-2023,2,140.html>)

**Corporate & Sectors** Polish manufacturing PMI fell to 45.1 in June. An exporter serving German industry is exposed to customer confidence and order timing across the border. A domestic lender instead depends on payment quality, interest margins and the availability of profitable new credit. One national equity index can contain both stories and disclose little about the direction of the individual business. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>) [⁽10⁾](<https://www.zew.de/en/press/latest-press-releases/expectations-decrease>)

**Geopolitics & Security** The Vilnius summit communiqué reaffirmed collective defence and described alliance commitments to the eastern flank. An alliance declaration matters for strategic confidence but says little by itself about the timing of an order for an individual Polish company. Distinguish a procurement contract from the longer-term case for defence expenditure. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/official-texts-and-resources/official-texts/2023/07/11/vilnius-summit-communique?selectedLocale=ge>)

**New / Most Important** June's final consumer-price data confirm that disinflation is underway from a still-high level. The cleaner corporate test will be whether lower inflation preserves volumes or simply reduces firms’ ability to raise prices. [⁽22⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/prices-trade/price-indices/consumer-price-indices-in-june-2023,2,140.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Track domestic price pressure, bank funding costs and industrial orders before extrapolating broad Polish market strength into domestic earnings. A reduction in inflation coupled with weak new manufacturing business could support bonds and disappoint industrial shareholders. ⚠ Divergence: policy rates remain high as June manufacturing sentiment signals contraction. [⁽20⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) [⁽23⁾](<https://www.intellinews.com/mood-in-polish-manufacturing-worsens-sharply-in-june-pmi-indicates-283566/>)

## Sector Deep Dive

### XLK — Technology · Mixed

**Narrative** Industry attention is concentrated on data and artificial intelligence, while TSMC’s June revenue fell 11.1% year over year. Cloud services and chip fabrication occupy different points in the chain. Buying a technology index assumes much more than the proposition that one class of accelerator is scarce. [⁽25⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

**Reported** Alphabet and Nvidia each gained about 4.7% in the July 13 session as Google expanded Bard to additional markets. Nvidia sells capital equipment into the first stage of the buildout. Alphabet must recover growing computing expense through existing or new customer activity. A common price move therefore tells us little about the timing of cash generation at the individual companies. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>)

**EPS Upside Watch** TSMC’s NT$156.40 billion June revenue was down 11.4% from May. Monthly invoicing is noisy, but a decline of that size leaves the burden of proof on claims of a sector-wide upswing. Check several shipments and end markets before assigning the same multiple improvement to client computing and enterprise workloads. [⁽25⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A successful chatbot launch can make a search product more expensive to operate before the first additional advertising dollar arrives. Examine inference cost per query, the proportion of queries carrying ads and the price advertisers pay for those leads. Revenue reaches the supplier once the customer buys computing hardware; for the platform, that purchase begins a stream of costs which it must still recover. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>) [⁽25⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023024605/tsm-revenue2023710x6k.htm>)

### XLC — Communication Services · Mixed

**Narrative** Reuters reported more than 100 million sign-ups for Meta’s Threads in its first five days. An established social platform can prompt users to open another account at very low friction. That is valuable distribution, but it says little about repeat use or what an advertiser is prepared to pay for each visit. [⁽27⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/metas-twitter-rival-threads-overtakes-chatgpt-as-fastestgrowing-platform-3122632>)

**Reported** Alphabet rose roughly 4.7% on July 13 as Bard expanded to additional markets. Availability, account openings and initial interactions are straightforward early metrics. Commercial value also depends on the share of questions that can be monetised and the cost of answering them. Strong public curiosity need not immediately translate into higher operating profit for the search platform. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>)

**EPS Upside Watch** For an advertising platform, test user retention, advertiser demand and monetisable inventory in that order. A company can reach a large audience before it can sell sufficient advertising at attractive prices. A change in interest rates offers valuation support but cannot supply a missing advertiser. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A chatbot can expose a publisher to a fresh audience or provide enough information that the reader never visits the publisher. Search advertising, referral-dependent media and subscription content will therefore respond differently to a successful rollout. Check the advertising buyer’s cost per sale and the publisher’s traffic and subscriber acquisition costs before assigning all beneficiaries the earnings multiple of the platform. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-07-13/wall-street-rises-again-as-inflation-slows>)

### XLF — Financials · Mixed

**Narrative** JPMorgan’s $14.5 billion profit includes First Republic. The additional assets, liabilities and acquisition-related accounting affect comparisons, so look at growth in net interest income, loan losses and the cost of deposits before assuming that the headline profit sets a sector-wide earnings run rate. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>)

**Reported** JPMorgan’s $14.5 billion quarterly net income includes the acquired First Republic franchise. The new assets, customers and purchase accounting all change the comparison with last year. Put interest income and operating costs on a consistent perimeter first, then examine the rate needed to retain deposits and the reserve required against new and existing borrowers. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>)

**EPS Upside Watch** The most useful comparison is the share of earnings arising from repricing assets versus from origination and fee growth. Competition for term deposits can outlast a policy-rate hold. Compare actual deposit rates and mix with credit-loss provisioning. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Deposits moving from a regional institution to a large franchise can improve one balance sheet and leave system-wide borrowing demand almost untouched. A broad bank rally can obscure the higher funding cost to the firm losing those accounts. Distinguish a customer coming for operational services from funds placed temporarily while clients wait for a more attractive yield. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>) [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>)

### XLE — Energy · Mixed

**Narrative** The IEA’s July oil market report highlights the difference between exports, refinery runs and upstream production. It put Russian oil exports at 7.3 million barrels a day in June, down 600,000 barrels a day. A lower flow leaving the country can tighten particular markets without equivalent changes in global consumption. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

**Reported** The IEA’s July oil market report treats production, refinery throughput and exports separately. For a refiner crude is an input and the product barrel is the sale. For an independent producer it is crude that brings in revenue, with drilling and service costs below it. A tightening downstream market can give the two operations different earnings outcomes even at the same quoted oil price. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A producer with stable volumes and rising realised prices gains directly from tighter supply. A refiner depends instead on product prices relative to crude and the utilisation of its units. Track refining margins and the price achieved on actual cargoes before translating the direction of a futures contract into earnings. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** An exporter with flexibility to redirect supply may defend its selling price while a producer confined to one delivery point cannot. Look for pipeline capacity, freight costs and marketing discounts when comparing earnings estimates based on the same benchmark. A sudden loss of export volumes can tighten one region and leave another operator paying heavily to reach the customer. [⁽28⁾](<https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2023>)

### XLI — Industrials · Mixed

**Narrative** Fastenal’s second quarter showed slower growth in sales and margins as demand softened. An industrial distributor sees orders close to the time customers use a product. That exposure is different from a capital-goods manufacturer recognising sales from a backlog negotiated many months earlier. Replenishment and final demand can come apart. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>)

**Reported** Fastenal’s second quarter provides evidence from small components sold into many factories. A distributor can see changes in daily orders before an equipment maker with a multiquarter backlog shows material revenue pressure. Compare the performance of established customers against growth from opening on-site operations: new locations can increase reported sales while average consumption at older sites falls. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>) [⁽30⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-46-june-2023-manufacturing-ism-report-on-business-301868365.html>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Delta’s record June-quarter revenue shows strength in passenger transport even as factory demand remains soft. Treat airlines and component distributors as separate earnings exposures. Their classification in industrials says little about how they respond to a purchase order or fuel shock. [⁽31⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>) [⁽29⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Fasteners and other maintenance purchases can reveal customer activity but are also affected by distributor gain or loss of share. Examine established locations, frequency and size of orders, and where invoices are being collected more slowly. A quarterly gain won by converting a customer to on-site supply has a different implication for the industrial cycle from the same gain in underlying factory utilisation. [⁽29⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815556/000081555623000039/ex_99106302023earningsrele.htm>)

### XLY — Consumer Discretionary · Mixed

**Narrative** Delta raised its full-year earnings outlook to $6–7 per share and kept its $3 billion free cash flow outlook. Consumers can protect travel plans while economising on discretionary merchandise. A strong airline result is therefore a particularly poor substitute for a retailer’s own unit figures. [⁽31⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)

**Reported** Delta raised its annual EPS outlook to $6–7 and kept its free-cash-flow outlook at $3 billion. Consumers can spend more on an eagerly awaited trip and less on discretionary goods in the same period. The airline’s bookings are evidence about a particular spending occasion; retailers still have to establish that merchandise sold without heavy promotions. [⁽31⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Watch volumes at actual transaction prices after incentives. Growth financed by a larger price concession changes operating leverage differently from growth at a stable contribution per sale. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Strong airline receipts can signal willingness to allocate scarce vacation time or the return of a service previously hard to consume. Compare ticket yield with capacity and fuel costs. For consumer-goods names, the alternative use of that cash matters more than a broad label about a resilient consumer. Check inventory ageing and full-price sell-through. [⁽31⁾](<https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2023/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-June-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/default.aspx>) [⁽32⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>)

### XLP — Consumer Staples · Mixed

**Narrative** PepsiCo raised its expectation for full-year organic revenue growth to 10% and for core constant-currency EPS growth to 12%. Conagra’s release described higher organic sales and productivity offsetting cost inflation and pressure from operating leverage. Similar revenue headlines can conceal divergent unit trends. **Playbook** Favour businesses whose price, unit volume and cash conversion can be read separately. Where the result relies on favourable mix, identify what would cause that mix to reverse. Size a position around the operating variable that actually drives the earnings revision.

**Global Liquidity** Lipper’s July 13 report recorded a net $18.0 billion withdrawal across the US fund universe in the week ended July 12 even as taxable bond funds attracted $3.4 billion. **Rates & Dollar** The short Treasury maturity still offers a higher par yield than the ten-year. An investor choosing a longer bond trades away current yield in exchange for duration and a more durable rate lock. Evaluate that trade against the anticipated path of future short rates. Currency translation and hedging costs alter the calculation for non-US investors; Treasury yields alone do not establish a cross-border flow. **Credit Check** JPMorgan’s result reflects a lending and deposit franchise that has changed in size. Interest revenue and cash availability at an individual bank can improve while underwriting standards remain selective for marginal borrowers. Use credit performance and funding costs rather than a headline profit number as the signal for private lending conditions. **Equity & Fund Flows** The July 12 week’s fund flows include both investment companies and ETFs. Read the two together, without summing figures from different fund universes. **Portfolio Implication** Avoid a single risk-on label for movements in bank credit, bond yields and equity multiples. **What Is Priced** New Chinese lending rose sharply in June while producer prices fell. [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

**Conditional Expression** Prefer suppliers whose order books show financed projects starting rather than general exposure to a broad Chinese credit aggregate. A customer advancing a deposit and accepting delivery creates a clearer signal than an announced lending facility. [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Risk** Refinancing can absorb credit without creating construction volumes, and falling unit prices reduce nominal revenue even when physical deliveries hold up. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202307/t20230710_1941149.html>)

### The banking earnings hurdle · Mixed

**What Is Priced** JPMorgan’s early earnings set a high visible benchmark, but the changing perimeter following First Republic makes direct extrapolation to other banks unreliable. A bank with fewer low-cost customer balances may need to pay away more of the benefit of higher asset yields. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961723000425/a2q23erfexhibit991narrative.htm>)

**Conditional Expression** Read the operating margin alongside average deposits, product mix and the provision for future losses. A bank defending its deposit base with higher rates can report improved stability well before the cost of funding turns down. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**Risk** If a borrower’s income fails to keep pace with financing costs, loan-loss provisions can erode an apparent improvement in interest margins. The highest current yield may belong to the borrower taking the greatest risk. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>) [⁽32⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>)

### A packaging customer resists higher prices · Mixed

**What Is Priced** PepsiCo’s higher organic growth guidance and Conagra’s more complicated volume and cost picture present two ways of earning from a household food budget. Store traffic on its own does not reveal which product the customer buys, or the promotion required to move it. [⁽33⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77476/000007747623000048/q220238-kxexhibit991.htm>) [⁽34⁾](<https://www.conagrabrands.com/news-room/news-conagra-brands-reports-fourth-quarter-results-prn-122856>)

**Conditional Expression** Look for stable volumes at an acceptable gross margin once last year’s price increases are in both periods. When unit growth and price both hold, there is less need for promotions to fill the revenue gap. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

**Risk** A customer switching to a private-label rival can protect household consumption while hurting the branded producer’s volume. Headline consumer resilience may therefore mislead an investor modelled only on aggregate spending. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20230710/>)

## What Retail Is Playing

The most revealing trade going into Monday is an exchange stock whose customers received a fresh reason to trade. Coinbase gained nearly a quarter in a day, yet the following session's self-directed orders leaned toward selling. Across the same Friday sample a truck start-up attracted both enormous attention and fresh buying, and a battered telephone company drew dip buyers. Those are three different decisions hiding inside one word, risk-on. [⁽44⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/coinbase-leads-crypto-stock-gains-after-ripple-labs-legal-victory-3125598>) [⁽45⁾](<https://discussion.fool.com/t/market-movers-for-14-jul-2023/94455>)

### Coinbase / COIN — Buying the read-through from Ripple

**Why Retail Is Here** Thursday's court ruling gave a Coinbase shareholder two bets in one ticket. The immediate one is on trading fees. People who had ignored altcoins could reopen their apps to trade XRP or the tokens moving alongside it. The longer-lived one is on the range of products an American exchange can offer. If more tokens can trade without the issuer's original sale determining the status of every subsequent transaction, Coinbase has a business worth forecasting farther into the future. Both explanations can justify a higher price. They require different evidence after the first burst of trading. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.coinbase.com/institutional/research-insights/research/weekly-market-commentary/ripple-ruling-july-14-2023>) [⁽44⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/coinbase-leads-crypto-stock-gains-after-ripple-labs-legal-victory-3125598>)

**What Is Driving It** The judge separated Ripple's negotiated sales to institutional buyers from its programmatic sales through exchanges. Institutional sales at issue qualified as investment contracts; the programmatic sales at issue did not. For an exchange that distinction changes the legal argument around how buyers and sellers meet. It leaves open what a different judge might conclude about a different token, a different marketing promise or Coinbase's own conduct. Even Coinbase Institutional acknowledged the possibility of appeal. A shareholder paying up for many years of fee income is assigning weight to a legal argument that has just cleared its first important hurdle. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.coinbase.com/institutional/research-insights/research/weekly-market-commentary/ripple-ruling-july-14-2023>)

**The Social Signal** COIN ranked fourth by number of orders entered by Fidelity self-directed customers on Friday. The transcription records 2,902 buy orders against 3,553 sell orders as shares eased 1.58%. That is plenty of interest and a meaningful difference in direction from Thursday's price jump. Someone taking cash off the table and someone starting a short both enter a sell order. Sizes are unavailable. The defensible reading is that the legal news put COIN in front of individual investors and that their orders ran both ways. The totals do not identify a retail dollar balance across the market. [⁽45⁾](<https://discussion.fool.com/t/market-movers-for-14-jul-2023/94455>)

**Historical Analog** An exchange can benefit from a rush even if the asset responsible for it gives back the entire move. The familiar comparison is a stock exchange doing more business during a volatile earnings day. Its revenue follows transactions; the listed security's price follows the earnings. For the COIN trade this matters twice. A choppy XRP market could still generate attractive fees. Conversely, a token can hold a higher price on shrinking activity while producing less fee income than the first enthusiastic extrapolation assumed. Follow exchange activity alongside the token price. [⁽46⁾](<https://www.coinbase.com/institutional/research-insights/research/weekly-market-commentary/ripple-ruling-july-14-2023>)

**Smart Money View** Berenberg argued on Friday that investors had read too much from the Ripple decision into the SEC's separate lawsuit against Coinbase. That leaves a genuinely different frame from the exchange's own institutional commentary. The issuer can point to a favorable distinction in the law. A skeptical equity analyst can accept that distinction and still challenge the price paid for it. Neither assessment tells us who bought Thursday's rally. They tell us what an owner will have to believe after the legal headline has moved off the screen. [⁽47⁾](<https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2023/07/14/coinbase-rally-on-the-back-of-xrp-court-ruling-is-overdone-berenberg>) [⁽46⁾](<https://www.coinbase.com/institutional/research-insights/research/weekly-market-commentary/ripple-ruling-july-14-2023>)

**When the Narrative Breaks** The first reference is the $100 area that COIN cleared on Thursday. A close back below it, particularly while XRP remains firm, would suggest shareholders are taking a more cautious view of the exchange than token traders are taking of the ruling. A separate business test is whether customer activity survives the initial burst. A court reversal, an adverse development in Coinbase's own case, or a decline in volumes as token prices stabilize would each weaken a different leg of the original trade. Record which one fails before attributing every decline to crypto prices. [⁽44⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/coinbase-leads-crypto-stock-gains-after-ripple-labs-legal-victory-3125598>) [⁽46⁾](<https://www.coinbase.com/institutional/research-insights/research/weekly-market-commentary/ripple-ruling-july-14-2023>)

**The Setup** BayoTech's agreement covers up to 50 hydrogen trucks. Nikola is also due to buy hydrogen and transport equipment from its partner. At 317 million shares traded on Thursday, the market tried to put a price on the entire idea in a single session. JPMorgan placed Nikola second to Tesla in retail trading that day. Friday's Fidelity sample still showed 3,133 buys and 2,644 sells. [⁽48⁾](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/nikola-and-bayotech-partner-to-advance-zero-emission-hydrogen-delivery-301876318.html>) [⁽49⁾](<https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/NIKOLA-CORPORATION-44211889/news/Highly-shorted-Nikola-jumps-as-retail-army-scoops-up-shares-after-BayoTech-deal-44339413/>) [⁽45⁾](<https://discussion.fool.com/t/market-movers-for-14-jul-2023/94455>)

The next validation is scheduled delivery becoming completed delivery, with cash collections that support the cost of production and fueling. A quiet order book after the headline leaves the shareholder dependent on the next short seller buying back. A widening investigation, new testing costs or an open-ended timetable would keep that arithmetic unsettled. Look at whether the two shares begin to separate as evidence about each network emerges. Continuing to trade them as a single dividend pair assumes the operators inherited roughly comparable obligations. ### Tesla / TSLA — Deliveries meet the earnings hurdle

**The Setup** Tesla remained the most active symbol in the Friday broker sample, despite more sell orders than buys. A dealer can generate strong unit growth with discounts while a manufacturer sees less profit per vehicle. An investor writing down a revenue estimate from deliveries alone still needs selling prices and a margin assumption. [⁽51⁾](<https://ir.tesla.com/press-release/tesla-vehicle-production-deliveries-and-date-financial-results-webcast-second-quarter-2023>) [⁽45⁾](<https://discussion.fool.com/t/market-movers-for-14-jul-2023/94455>)

**What to Watch** A convincing update would connect volume to durable unit economics and cash generation. If margin erosion forces management to rely on another round of unusually rapid unit growth, the valuation is more sensitive to even a small delivery disappointment. Keep that question distinct from the weekly popularity contest. Fidelity's 9,721 buy orders and 11,358 sell orders show why the earnings matter to a large audience. They also show how easily that audience can contain profit-takers and committed owners at the same time.
