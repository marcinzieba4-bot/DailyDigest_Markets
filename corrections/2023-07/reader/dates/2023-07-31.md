**Monday, July 31, 2023** · Historical edition. Information available before 2023-07-31 00:00 UTC.

[July archive](../README.md) · [PDF](../../reports/2023-07/pdf/2023-07-31_Market_Intelligence.pdf)

The US economy gave investors something they had spent much of the year doubting they could have together. Consumer spending rose in real terms as inflation eased. The Fed still raised rates. Intel could rally on a quarter in which its sales fell, and a company warning about its next interest payment could become one of the market’s most discussed stocks. What joined those trades was the starting expectation. Intel had to show the PC slump was easing. Tupperware needed another willing buyer before it needed another customer. Those are very different ways to make money. ## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · Mixed · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The most important detail in Friday’s inflation release was what Americans were doing while inflation fell. Real consumption rose 0.4% in June. Headline PCE inflation eased to 3.0% year over year, with core at 4.1% and up 0.2% on the month. Thursday’s advance estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 2.4% annual rate. A business planning its inventory could reasonably take heart from those numbers. A bondholder counting on an imminent rate cut still had to contend with core inflation and a Fed that had just raised its target to 5.25–5.50%. [⁽1⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/personal-income-and-outlays-june-2023>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/gross-domestic-product-second-quarter-2023-advance-estimate>) [⁽2⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230726a.htm>)

**Rates and curve** Friday’s two-year Treasury yielded 4.87% and the ten-year 3.96%. The ten-year sat 91 basis points below the two-year. Twenty Treasury observations earlier, on June 29, the short yield was the same and the ten-year was 3.85%. All 11 basis points of steepening came from the long end selling off. An equity investor interpreting that move as cheaper money would have the direction of the financing change backwards. The S&P 500 finished Friday at 4,582.23 and the Nasdaq Composite at 14,316.66. For expensive growth shares to hold those prices as long yields rise, actual earnings must take over some of the work previously done by valuation. [⁽5⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽7⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202306>) [⁽8⁾](<https://en.stockq.org/stock/history/2023/07/20230728_en.php>)

**Playbook** Look for businesses that can keep unit volumes and gross margins together as inflation cools. A resilient consumer helps a retailer move inventory. It does much less for a company whose earnings depend on passing through another round of price increases. A weaker consumption print alongside persistent core inflation would damage both sides of that distinction. [⁽1⁾](<https://www.bea.gov/news/2023/personal-income-and-outlays-june-2023>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/80424/000008042423000059/fy2223q4amj8-kexhibit991.htm>)

### Euro area · Bearish · Bearish

**Macro Stance** The ECB announced another quarter-point increase on July 27. The deposit rate was set to reach 3.75% on August 2. The effective date matters to a treasury desk, but loan officers were already feeling the tightening. The ECB’s bank lending survey showed tighter credit standards and weaker business demand for loans. A manufacturer can pay a higher rate if its order book justifies expansion. When the same manufacturer scraps the investment altogether, the loss falls on its equipment suppliers as well as on its bank. Watch the next loan survey for businesses returning to the market and lenders willing to fund them. A rate pause by itself would leave that second condition unresolved. [⁽10⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.mp230727~da80cfcf24.en.html>) [⁽11⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.pr230725~8358d3939d.en.html>)

### China · Mixed · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The headline recovery looked stronger over a year than it did over a quarter. Second-quarter GDP grew 6.3% year over year and 0.8% from the first quarter. June’s manufacturing PMI was 49.0. Over the first half, property development investment fell 7.9% and the floor area of commercial buildings sold fell 5.3%. A producer supplying export factories or unfinished developments could experience weak orders even as headline GDP accelerated against a depressed comparison. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

**Credit and property** New yuan loans reached RMB 3.05 trillion in June. A lender refinancing an existing obligation and a buyer borrowing for a newly sold home can both add to credit. They have very different implications for cement orders, developer cash receipts and consumer confidence. On July 24 the Politburo called for property policy to reflect changed conditions. The housing minister subsequently urged implementation of lower down payments and mortgage rates for eligible first-time buyers, along with delivery of presold homes. Completing a home already paid for can restore trust. A lower down payment is useful only when a household feels confident enough to sign. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>) [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/28/content_WS64c2fcf5c6d0868f4e8de250.html>)

**Playbook** Treat policy-sensitive shares as a bet on implementation until local rules change and contracts are signed. Watch sales area and completions alongside credit volumes. Stronger borrowing while sales remain weak would help explain support for lenders without providing a reason to raise building-material volume forecasts. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>)

**Markets & Flows** A property developer’s share price can move as soon as the probability of refinancing improves. Its contractors are paid only when financing is actually drawn and construction proceeds. A household considering an unfinished apartment faces a different decision again. A completed building lowers delivery risk, while a change in mortgage terms lowers the cost of buying. Track both before moving from a tactical position in developers to a broader position in household consumption and materials. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>) [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/28/content_WS64c2fcf5c6d0868f4e8de250.html>)

### Japan · Mixed · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The Bank of Japan kept its short policy rate at −0.1% and its ten-year target around zero. The change was in how it would defend that target. The previous half-percentage-point bounds became references, with daily fixed-rate purchases offered at 1.0%. The distinction matters to a bank holding a large JGB book. The overnight funding rate can stay low while longer bonds lose value. Whether better reinvestment yields compensate depends on how quickly liabilities reprice and when bonds mature. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/mpr_2023/k230728a.pdf>)

**Activity & Currency** Japan’s preliminary June industrial production rose 2.0% month over month. METI published its preliminary estimate at 08:50 in Tokyo on July 31. That was genuine evidence of stronger factory activity. It gave exporters a useful counterweight to uncertainty over the yield curve. Watch whether orders sustain the rise before assuming that one month of production translates into a durable increase in profits. [⁽17⁾](<https://www.meti.go.jp/english/statistics/tyo/iip/b2020_202306se.html>) [⁽16⁾](<https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/mpr_2023/k230728a.pdf>)

### Australia · Mixed · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The RBA held its cash rate at 4.10% early in July. Later that month, the statistics bureau reported consumer prices up 0.8% in the June quarter and 6.0% over a year. June retail turnover fell 0.8% even in nominal terms. A retailer usually hopes an end-of-financial-year promotion will bring enough extra customers through the door to pay for the discount. A fall in receipts makes the margin question harder. The pause gave the bank time to see how much tightening was still moving through household budgets. Another soft retail reading would strengthen that argument, particularly if quarterly inflation slowed again. [⁽18⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>) [⁽19⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/cpi-rose-08-cent-june-2023-quarter>) [⁽20⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/less-spending-mid-year-sales-sees-retail-turnover-fall>)

### Poland · Mixed · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The NBP held its reference rate at 6.75%. June industrial production fell 1.4% year over year and inflation-adjusted retail sales fell 4.7%. A bank and a retailer could read the same interest-rate decision quite differently. Santander Bank Polska reported PLN 6.3 billion of first-half net interest income. Its deposits were up 9% year over year while gross customer receivables grew 3%. High interest income can support the current quarter while sluggish credit demand and competition for deposits weigh on the next one. [⁽21⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) [⁽22⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/20-07-2023.html>) [⁽23⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/21-07-2023.html>) [⁽24⁾](<https://media.erste.pl/informacje-prasowe/grupa-santander-bank-polska-i-polrocze-2023>)

**Companies and households** Grupa Kęty raised its 2023 profit forecast from PLN 388 million to PLN 487 million. Its revenue forecast edged down and management expected second-half margin pressure. Friday’s shares fell 2.61%. The upgrade gave shareholders a better estimate of full-year earnings while leaving a harder question about the earnings power of the next six months. On the household side, the Senate passed the increase in the monthly child benefit from PLN 500 to PLN 800. The measure awaited the president’s signature and envisaged payments from January 2024. July shop takings could tell investors about current purchasing power. They could tell them little about spending out of a future benefit. [⁽25⁾](<https://grupakety.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/2023Forecastupdate.pdf>) [⁽26⁾](<https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/WIG20-z-kolejnym-wzrostowym-tygodniem-Sesja-na-GPW-pod-dyktando-rynkow-bazowych-8586531.html>) [⁽27⁾](<https://www.gov.pl/web/family/family-800-the-bill-is-waiting-for-the-presidents-signature>)

**Security and markets** The WIG20 finished Friday at 2,189.24. On Saturday, Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki said Wagner personnel in Belarus had moved towards the Suwałki corridor. A statement about a movement on the Belarusian side establishes a political and security concern. A forecast for a Polish freight operator requires evidence about delays, restrictions or actual routes. For domestic equities, track margins and real activity first and identify which firms have direct border exposure before assigning an earnings cost. [⁽28⁾](<https://isbiznes.pl/2023/07/28/indeks-wig20-spadl-o-002-na-zamknieciu-w-piatek/>) [⁽29⁾](<https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2046373/some-wagner-mercenaries-move-towards-suwalki-gap-says-polish-pm>)

**Playbook ✓** For a Polish bank, compare growth in customer balances with growth in loans and the spread earned between them. For an industrial exporter such as Kęty, test management’s profit increase against its view of future margins and the shares’ response. This gives a portfolio two separate ways to be wrong: domestic funding costs can catch up with lending yields even as European demand reduces margins for manufacturers. Concentrating on those company-specific tests keeps the border story in proportion. [⁽24⁾](<https://media.erste.pl/informacje-prasowe/grupa-santander-bank-polska-i-polrocze-2023>) [⁽25⁾](<https://grupakety.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/2023Forecastupdate.pdf>) [⁽26⁾](<https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/WIG20-z-kolejnym-wzrostowym-tygodniem-Sesja-na-GPW-pod-dyktando-rynkow-bazowych-8586531.html>) [⁽29⁾](<https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2046373/some-wagner-mercenaries-move-towards-suwalki-gap-says-polish-pm>)

## Sector Deep Dive

### Technology · XLK · Bullish

**Narrative** Microsoft’s Azure and other cloud services grew 26% year over year, or 27% in constant currency. That shows customers still bought more computing capacity while companies scrutinised their budgets. For the AI trade the harder question is how much extra capacity customers will pay for once initial trials end. Cloud growth bundles established workloads and new products together. A better signal would be sustained growth alongside evidence that incremental sales cover the cost of new infrastructure. [⁽30⁾](<https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2023-q4/press-release-webcast>)

**EPS Upside Watch** For Microsoft, compare the growth of the cloud franchise with the investment needed to serve it. A new workload can add revenue quickly, while an equipment order commits cash irrespective of the utilization it eventually achieves. Strong results at a hardware vendor and healthy returns at its customer are different propositions. [⁽30⁾](<https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2023-q4/press-release-webcast>) [⁽31⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828023025146/a2q23e_withguidancexfinalx.htm>)

### Communication services · XLC · Bullish

**Narrative** Meta delivered 34% more ad impressions than a year earlier while the average price per ad fell 16%. More inventory and better distribution can keep revenue growing while advertisers still pay less for each placement. If pricing stabilises while impression growth persists, there is room for operating leverage. If volume growth slows first, the same figures reveal how much recovery the stock already demands. [⁽32⁾](<https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2023/Meta-Reports-Second-Quarter-2023-Results/default.aspx>)

**EPS Upside Watch** The useful comparison is Alphabet’s recovery across Search and YouTube and Meta’s need for additional impressions to offset lower pricing. If ad budgets recover broadly, pricing and volume can reinforce each other. If inventory grows faster than demand, the platform is doing more delivery work for each additional dollar. [⁽32⁾](<https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2023/Meta-Reports-Second-Quarter-2023-Results/default.aspx>) [⁽33⁾](<https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2023/q2/2023q2-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf>)

### Financials · XLF · Mixed

**Narrative** U.S. Bancorp’s quarter-end deposits grew 3.2% sequentially while its average deposits fell 2.6%. Taxable-equivalent net interest income fell 4.7% from the prior quarter. The bank could enter July with more deposits and still have earned less on the funding it carried through spring. For the next quarter, follow average balances and what it costs to retain them. A stabilising account base may take time to repair the spread on a loan book. [⁽34⁾](<https://www.usbank.com/about-us-bank/company-blog/article-library/us-bancorp-reports-second-quarter-2023-results.html>)

**EPS Upside Watch** A bank that prices deposits aggressively can protect its funding while undermining current net interest income. A bank that refuses to compete can appear temporarily more profitable before deposits leave. Look for stable balances, pricing and loan demand together when choosing among banks. [⁽34⁾](<https://www.usbank.com/about-us-bank/company-blog/article-library/us-bancorp-reports-second-quarter-2023-results.html>) [⁽11⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.pr230725~8358d3939d.en.html>)

### Energy · XLE · Mixed

**Narrative** WTI settled at $80.58 and Brent at $84.99 on Friday, the fifth straight weekly advance. Chevron nevertheless reported second-quarter earnings of $6.0 billion against $11.6 billion a year earlier. The crude screen shows the marginal price today. The earnings release reflects volumes and realised prices over an entire quarter against an unusually profitable comparison. A bullish case needs prices to persist long enough to reach cash flow. A five-week rally and a year-over-year profit decline can comfortably coexist. [⁽35⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/commodities-news/oil-prices-open-lower-on-demand-concerns-remain-on-track-for-weekly-gain-3136817>) [⁽36⁾](<https://chevroncorp.gcs-web.com/node/35026>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Compare cash available after investment with distributions and distinguish last quarter’s realized oil prices from the latest front-month oil move. A company with rising production can still see earnings fall against an unusually profitable prior period. [⁽35⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/commodities-news/oil-prices-open-lower-on-demand-concerns-remain-on-track-for-weekly-gain-3136817>) [⁽36⁾](<https://chevroncorp.gcs-web.com/node/35026>)

### Industrials · XLI · Bullish

**Narrative** GE booked $8.3 billion of renewable-energy orders in the quarter. A large turbine order provides work, but signing it also locks in obligations for materials, labour, transport and performance. Track the conversion of those contracts into margin and cash. Aerospace service demand has a different clock, tied to engines returning for work. Looking at the two businesses separately gives a clearer earnings signal than treating a bigger group order book as cash already in hand. [⁽37⁾](<https://www.ge.com/news/press-releases/ge-announces-second-quarter-2023-results>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Aircraft service revenue depends on the installed fleet and maintenance schedule. New equipment orders require materials and often long production lead times. A rising backlog can be favorable for demand and unfavorable for near-term working capital at the same time. [⁽37⁾](<https://www.ge.com/news/press-releases/ge-announces-second-quarter-2023-results>)

### Consumer discretionary · XLY · Mixed

**Narrative** Ford Pro earned $2.4 billion of quarterly EBIT, while the Model e electric-vehicle business lost about $1.1 billion. The commercial division can help finance a ramp that still loses money. That transfer matters to a Ford shareholder in a way the industry’s EV delivery growth alone cannot capture. Watch whether improving electric-model volumes lower the loss per vehicle without requiring further discounts. A healthy commercial order book buys time to find out. [⁽38⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38009/000003800923000044/exhibit99tojuly272023for.htm>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Ford’s commercial and electric businesses face different customers and different cost absorption. An order from a fleet operator reflects a vehicle’s operating economics. A retail EV promotion may bring volumes forward while lowering cash generated per car. Evaluate both before projecting revenue growth into cash flow. [⁽38⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38009/000003800923000044/exhibit99tojuly272023for.htm>)

### Consumer staples · XLP · Mixed

**Narrative** Procter & Gamble’s 8% organic sales growth included seven percentage points from pricing, two from mix and a one-point volume decline. A household continuing to buy detergent at a higher price protects revenue. Buying a little less with each shopping trip eventually matters to factories and retailers. As inflation cools, compare unit volumes with the slowing price contribution. Flat prices with recovering units would be a stronger sign of underlying demand than another quarter of price-led nominal growth. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/80424/000008042423000059/fy2223q4amj8-kexhibit991.htm>)

**EPS Upside Watch** P&G’s mix benefit leaves a specific next-quarter question. If consumers switch toward cheaper formats as pricing moderates, reported organic sales can slow even if the physical quantity bought recovers. Look for volume improving together with stable profitability. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/80424/000008042423000059/fy2223q4amj8-kexhibit991.htm>)

### Healthcare · XLV · Mixed

**Narrative** Roche’s diagnostics division is a useful read-through for test and equipment suppliers. Its first-half sales fell 23% at constant exchange rates while sales excluding COVID products rose 6%. The headline fall combines a disappearing pandemic market with growth elsewhere. For peer comparisons, ask how much of the extraordinary test base remains in the prior-year numbers and whether routine diagnostics can support the installed equipment. [⁽39⁾](<https://www.roche.com/investors/updates/inv-update-2023-07-27>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Roche’s diagnostics comparison includes a change in demand for COVID-related products. Separating routine testing helps distinguish a falling comparison from weakening laboratory utilization. A sustained advance in underlying diagnostics would matter more to forward margins than eliminating a difficult historical comparison. [⁽39⁾](<https://www.roche.com/investors/updates/inv-update-2023-07-27>)

### Materials · XLB · Bearish

**Narrative** Dow reported sales of $11.4 billion, down 27% year over year. Local prices fell 18% and volumes fell 8%. Cheaper energy or feedstock can improve a producer’s spread, but operating a chemical plant below capacity still spreads fixed costs over fewer tonnes. For a turn in the cycle, watch shipment volumes and the difference between product and input prices together. A rebound driven solely by input inflation gives shareholders a very different margin outlook. [⁽40⁾](<https://investors.dow.com/en/news/news-details/2023/Dow-reports-second-quarter-2023-results/default.aspx>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Dow has already reported weakness in both selling prices and volumes. Higher quoted commodity prices can precede restocking, but a chemical plant earns its return on the spread after feedstock, freight and utilization costs. Use actual shipments to confirm a turn. [⁽40⁾](<https://investors.dow.com/en/news/news-details/2023/Dow-reports-second-quarter-2023-results/default.aspx>)

### Real estate · XLRE · Mixed

**Narrative** Prologis reported 97.5% average occupancy and a 48.1% cash rent increase on leases that commenced in the quarter. That increase describes the leases turning over. Existing contracts elsewhere in the portfolio still reset on their own schedules. As long yields rose, the offset for a warehouse owner was the ability to renew leases at higher rents. New leasing activity, occupancy and the cost of the next refinancing tell investors how much of the headline uplift accrues to equity. [⁽41⁾](<https://ir.prologis.com/financials/sec-filings/content/0000950170-23-033263/pld-ex99_2.htm>) [⁽5⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>)

**EPS Upside Watch** An industrial landlord has several years of rents set at different points in the cycle. The increase on leases signed this quarter is useful evidence of pricing power on that fraction of the portfolio. Vacancy at the next lease expiry is the downside that the headline spread leaves out. [⁽41⁾](<https://ir.prologis.com/financials/sec-filings/content/0000950170-23-033263/pld-ex99_2.htm>)

If financing costs stay high, investors should look for projects reaching service on time and at the economics assumed when contracts were signed. ### US and Asian semiconductors · SOXX · Mixed

**Narrative** TSMC’s dollar revenue fell 13.7% year over year to $15.68 billion, while guidance of $16.7–17.5 billion pointed to a sequential improvement. Intel’s data-centre and AI segment revenue was down 15% year over year. ASML booked €4.5 billion of orders, including €1.6 billion of EUV.
