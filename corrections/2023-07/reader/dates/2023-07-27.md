## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · One hike delivered, the next is open · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The FOMC raised the federal funds target to 5.25–5.50%. Powell kept the next decision dependent on incoming evidence. For an equity investor the date of a final hike is less important than how much demand disappears while the policy rate stays here. Coca-Cola is still collecting more through price and mix. A homebuyer facing a higher mortgage payment cannot solve affordability as easily as a customer choosing a smaller bottle. The June new-home sales rate, at 697,000 annualized, provides a timely test of that difference. [⁽1⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230726a.htm>) [⁽5⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/mediacenter/files/FOMCpresconf20230726.pdf>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.coca-colacompany.com/media-center/coca-cola-reports-second-quarter-2023-results>) [⁽7⁾](<https://archives.hud.gov/news/2023/pr23-150b.cfm>)

**Rates & Curve** The July 26 Treasury close put the two-year yield at 4.82% and the ten-year at 3.86%. Both declined from Tuesday despite the hike, leaving the ten-year 96 basis points below the two-year. An overnight policy decision and a bond yield refer to different periods. An investor buying the two-year has already made an allowance for where the overnight rate will be after this meeting and the next. Owning the long bond is an additional bet on the price lenders demand to commit money for much longer. [⁽2⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>) [⁽1⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230726a.htm>)

**New / Most Important** Equities offered an equally divided response. The Dow extended its winning run for a thirteenth session, while the major indices split. Meta’s subsequent results showed that demand for advertising can grow by increasing the amount delivered while the price of an individual impression falls. Carrying that margin story over to a highly indebted homebuilder or utility would miss the channels through which policy actually works. [⁽8⁾](<https://finance.yahoo.com/news/dow-rises-for-13th-straight-day-stocks-split-after-fed-decision-stock-market-news-today-200415384.html>) [⁽4⁾](<https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2023/Meta-Reports-Second-Quarter-2023-Results/default.aspx>) [⁽7⁾](<https://archives.hud.gov/news/2023/pr23-150b.cfm>)

**Playbook ✓** Look for companies whose gains depend on utilization, cost control or customer demand already arriving. Be wary where the valuation requires a prompt fall in rates and the business also needs to refinance. A material weakening in hiring and continued cooling in prices would change the value of holding duration as protection. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_07072023.htm>) [⁽10⁾](<https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_07122023.htm>) [⁽1⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230726a.htm>)

### Euro area · A decision arrives after lending has weakened · Bearish

**Credit & Inflation** European banks reported tightening standards for businesses and weaker loan demand. That is pressure on the quantity of credit as well as its price. Companies postponing expansion reduce the future sales that could have serviced new loans. A pending ECB decision can move bond prices immediately. Its effect on a borrower already restricting investment will take longer to identify. [⁽11⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.pr230725~8358d3939d.en.html>) [⁽12⁾](<https://economics.bmo.com/en/publications/detail/ff2f8068-aecd-40d1-b0a0-b98461a7e2a2/>)

**New / Most Important** Stellantis posted €98.4 billion of first-half net revenue and a 14.4% adjusted operating margin. A manufacturer producing against previously secured orders can earn strongly while financing conditions for the next buyer worsen. Give more weight to orders received now, incentives and cash collection than to a comparison with a supply-constrained prior period. [⁽13⁾](<https://www.media.stellantis.com/em-en/corporate/press/first-half-2023-results>) [⁽11⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.pr230725~8358d3939d.en.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Separate a strong export franchise from domestic credit exposure. Add to a European cyclical basket when banks see credible new loan demand and companies are replenishing order books at profitable prices. [⁽11⁾](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2023/html/ecb.pr230725~8358d3939d.en.html>) [⁽13⁾](<https://www.media.stellantis.com/em-en/corporate/press/first-half-2023-results>)

### China · Technology can cross a border before demand recovers · Mixed

**Macro Stance** Volkswagen agreed to invest about $700 million in XPeng and to develop two electric models for China. It provides a tangible example of what China's manufacturing sector can sell besides a finished car: engineering, architecture and a faster route to a locally competitive product. The agreement is prospective and its execution will take time. It does not give a household a reason to buy an apartment or prove that the wider credit transmission mechanism is fixed. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/press-releases/more-e-models-for-fast-growing-e-mobility-market-in-china-vw-brand-and-audi-agree-strategic-cooperations-with-local-automakers-17497>) [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>)

**PMIs & Activity** The annual GDP growth rate of 6.3% partly reflects comparison with a weak prior-year quarter. Growth of 0.8% from the preceding three months and a June manufacturing PMI of 49.0 better capture the uneven near-term picture. An automaker securing a technology partner can gain business while a factory supplying construction materials loses it. Sector exposure matters as much as the headline view on China. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>) [⁽14⁾](<https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/press-releases/more-e-models-for-fast-growing-e-mobility-market-in-china-vw-brand-and-audi-agree-strategic-cooperations-with-local-automakers-17497>)

**Credit, Property & Policy** New yuan loans rose by 3.05 trillion yuan in June, and the Politburo has acknowledged changed conditions in housing. A larger lending number can include refinancing or credit directed toward industry. First-half property investment fell 7.9%, which is a reminder that a bank balance-sheet expansion does not automatically create new floor space or paid invoices for a contractor. Local measures that help deliver homes already bought may strengthen buyer confidence before new starts recover. [⁽17⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>) [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>) [⁽16⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Keep a technology partnership trade distinct from property reflation. Demand evidence on vehicle milestones for the former and on home sales and completions for the latter. A more supportive policy stance with private households still hesitant is the central divergence. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/press-releases/more-e-models-for-fast-growing-e-mobility-market-in-china-vw-brand-and-audi-agree-strategic-cooperations-with-local-automakers-17497>) [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>) [⁽16⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

### Japan · A bank and an exporter approach the same meeting · Mixed

**Macro Stance** June core inflation excluding fresh food was 3.3%. A change to the way the Bank of Japan manages long yields would expose one bank’s fixed-rate portfolio and another’s reinvestment opportunities differently. An exporter could see foreign earnings translate at a less favorable rate if the yen strengthened. The meeting outcome is still ahead. Do not treat either outcome as part of operating earnings before it happens. [⁽18⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230721_023912.pdf>) [⁽12⁾](<https://economics.bmo.com/en/publications/detail/ff2f8068-aecd-40d1-b0a0-b98461a7e2a2/>)

**Playbook ✓** Look at maturity and currency composition before treating a bank or automaker as a generic Japan trade. Earnings built on domestic unit demand should provide a better anchor if the exchange rate moves sharply. [⁽18⁾](<https://www.dir.co.jp/english/research/report/jsothers/20230721_023912.pdf>) [⁽19⁾](<https://www.acea.auto/pc-registrations/new-car-registrations-17-8-in-june-battery-electric-15-1-market-share/>)

### Australia · A slower quarter still leaves inflation at six · Mixed

**Demand & Financing** Australia’s consumer price index rose 0.8% in the June quarter and 6.0% over the year. The slowing pace eases the case for another immediate increase. It leaves a substantial rise in the level of household costs that has already occurred. A household with a resetting mortgage still has less cash for discretionary purchases even if its grocery bill grows more slowly from here. [⁽20⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/cpi-rose-08-cent-june-2023-quarter>) [⁽21⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>)

**Playbook ✓** The unemployment rate of 3.5% helps sustain disposable income. Favor lenders or consumer businesses that can show actual collections and volume retention. Lower inflation accompanied by job losses would have a quite different effect on their earnings from lower inflation with employment intact. [⁽22⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/unemployment-rate-35-cent-june>) [⁽20⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/cpi-rose-08-cent-june-2023-quarter>)

### Poland · Santander puts a number on expensive money · Mixed

**Macro Stance** A policy rate of 6.75% has translated into strong asset yields for banks and demanding monthly financing costs for borrowers. Santander Bank Polska reported first-half net interest income of PLN 6.3 billion. Customer deposits rose 9% year over year and gross receivables from clients rose 3%. The different growth rates change what the bank needs to do next. More deposits secure funding. Only assets that can be written on sound terms earn an attractive spread on it. [⁽23⁾](<https://nbp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Komunikat-RPP-lipiec-2023-ANG.pdf>) [⁽24⁾](<https://media.erste.pl/informacje-prasowe/grupa-santander-bank-polska-i-polrocze-2023>)

**Corporate & Sectors** Santander reported PLN 590 million of consolidated provisions, up 157% on the year. A bank can have both strong interest income and growing credit charges during a tightening cycle. For a shareholder, the additional income must be tested after provisions, taxes and any repricing needed to keep depositors. For a retailer, June’s 4.7% contraction in real sales points to a different pressure on the same borrowers. [⁽24⁾](<https://media.erste.pl/informacje-prasowe/grupa-santander-bank-polska-i-polrocze-2023>) [⁽25⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/21-07-2023.html>)

**Markets & Security** Bankier described a decline in the WIG20 ahead of central-bank decisions as Warsaw’s reporting season began. A softer index is useful context for the reaction to an individual bank, but it does not isolate credit quality from global rate sentiment. Poland also faces the economic consequences of interrupted Black Sea grain shipments. The impact depends on freight capacity and the destinations open to exporters. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Spadki-na-WIG20-przed-decyzjami-bankow-centralnych-Na-GPW-startuje-sezon-wynikowy-8585281.html>) [⁽27⁾](<https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/press-events/2023-07-17/secretary-generals-press-encounter-the-black-sea-initiative>)

**Playbook ✓** Use Santander’s next deposit and receivables disclosures to test the durability of the margin. Prefer domestically exposed retailers only when volumes stop declining. The divergence is strong bank income in an economy where high rates can weaken the future borrowers supporting that income. [⁽24⁾](<https://media.erste.pl/informacje-prasowe/grupa-santander-bank-polska-i-polrocze-2023>) [⁽25⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/21-07-2023.html>)

## Crypto & Digital Assets

### The cost of keeping a speculative position open · Mixed

**Market Context** The Fed’s new range means a dollar investor can earn a substantial yield without bearing the volatility of a crypto asset. That is a funding and opportunity-cost argument, not a prediction that bitcoin must decline on the day of a hike. A token’s price can rise if its marginal buyer sees a compelling catalyst even as cautious investors redeploy cash elsewhere. [⁽1⁾](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230726a.htm>) [⁽2⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>)

**New / Most Important** CoinShares reported $13 million leaving bitcoin products and inflows of $6.6 million to ether products for the reporting week. Even a measured outflow concerns a particular set of vehicles. It can coexist with high exchange volume after the XRP ruling and the opening of services to eligible Coinbase clients. Distinguish new cash, a changing mix of assets, and old positions changing hands.
