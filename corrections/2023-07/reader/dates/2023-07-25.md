## Geographic Macro Overview

### United States · Services still expand as the rate hurdle rises · Mixed

**Macro Stance** A country can have contracting factories and expanding services. The flash US composite PMI dropped to 52.0 from 53.2. The services measure remained positive at 52.4, while the manufacturing PMI improved to 49.0 from 46.3. The part of the economy that had been weakest is getting less weak. The larger service sector is losing momentum. This is a more useful description of business conditions than a binary recession call. A company buying new equipment still needs to see a customer order, whereas a credit-card issuer can collect transaction fees on services already purchased. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.pmi.spglobal.com/Public/Home/PressRelease/bbdd92c5f4a34bb5975b23263409ad3d>) [⁽3⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4962/000000496223000027/q223exhibit991.htm>)

**Rates & Curve** At the July 24 close the two-year yielded 4.81% and the ten-year 3.86%, producing an inversion of 95 basis points. Across twenty observations those yields rose 10 and 12 basis points respectively, narrowing the inversion by 2 basis points. For an equity investor the message is that a projected earnings improvement still competes with a substantial return on short maturities. For a borrower the difficult comparison is between its sharply higher marginal funding cost and the return on the next investment. [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>)

**New / Most Important** Domino’s US same-store sales rose just 0.1% in the quarter. Whirlpool reaffirmed full-year earnings guidance. A household may still buy dinner while postponing a large appliance purchase or a house move. The restaurant needs customers willing to pay the delivery charge. The manufacturer can sustain earnings with lower costs even without a volume recovery. Each release poses a different test of the consumer’s strength. [⁽4⁾](<https://dominos.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/dominos-pizzar-announces-second-quarter-2023-financial-results>) [⁽5⁾](<https://investors.whirlpoolcorp.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2023/Whirlpool-Announces-Solid-Second-Quarter-Results-Reaffirms-2023-Earnings-Guidance/default.aspx>)

**Playbook ✓** Reduce reliance on a general rebound in margins. Increase exposure where improved demand reaches operating cash even if short rates stay high. Today’s large technology-company results will provide that test for cloud and advertising demand, while the Fed decision remains ahead. A further slowdown in services combined with another failed manufacturing recovery would argue against widening the cyclical allocation. [⁽6⁾](<https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/week-ahead-economic-preview-week-of-24-july-2023>) [⁽7⁾](<https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/newsquawk-us-market-wrap-markets-trundle-into-the-weekend-ahead-of-pivotal-risk-events-21-07-2023>)

### Euro area · German manufacturing gives an early warning · Bearish

**Macro Stance** The euro-area flash composite PMI slipped from 49.9 to 48.9. Germany’s composite fell from 50.6 to 48.3 and its manufacturing PMI reached 38.8. The last reading describes how widespread deterioration has become; it is not the percentage decline in industrial production. Firms selling discretionary equipment into German factories now face a materially higher burden of proof. [⁽8⁾](<https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/eurozone-flash-pmi-signals-cooling-inflation-amid-rising-recession-risks-jul23>) [⁽9⁾](<https://www.pmi.spglobal.com/Public/Home/PressRelease/c23ef113fdfb4d149593c3b3d2840fe9>)

**Activity & Inflation** June inflation stood at 5.5%. Slower output and a still-high rate of inflation can coexist for months while wages, energy contracts and financing costs reset. The key question for lenders is how long better yields on existing assets can offset weaker demand for new loans. For leveraged customers it is whether today’s order book generates enough cash to fund the next refinancing. [⁽10⁾](<https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-euro-indicators/w/2-19072023-ap>)

**New / Most Important** Adidas narrowed its forecast operating loss for 2023 to €450 million from €700 million. The first sale of Yeezy inventory helped, and management said the underlying business was also slightly stronger than expected. A finite stock sale can release working capital and reduce a write-down even as underlying business surveys deteriorate. That distinction matters to the multiple placed on the resulting earnings. [⁽11⁾](<https://www.adidas-group.com/en/investors/statutory-publications/ad-hoc-news/adidas-ag-adidas-announces-preliminary-second-quarter-results-and-updates-full-year-guidance-2023-07-24-1>)

**Playbook ✓** Keep broad industrial exposure light until the next new-order readings improve. Prefer companies with identified cash releases or secure contracted sales. A less restrictive ECB path would help financing, but domestic demand still has to turn. Local political uncertainty in Spain adds a separate sensitivity for firms tied to public decisions. [⁽12⁾](<https://www.yahoo.com/news/spains-poised-bitter-victory-hung-211214430.html>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/week-ahead-economic-preview-week-of-24-july-2023>)

### China · Policy changes direction before the borrower gets cash · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The Politburo acknowledged insufficient domestic demand and a changed relationship between supply and demand in real estate. It called for timely, city-specific adjustment of housing policies. That last qualification determines the likely beneficiaries. A city short of affordable homes might ease access for buyers. A city with substantial unsold stock needs enough demand to clear it and enough finance to complete what has been sold. A uniform bet on the equity of every developer assumes a uniform policy the statement never promised. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>)

**PMIs & Activity** June’s national manufacturing PMI sat at 49.0 points and the services business activity measure at 52.8. China’s June retail sales were up 3.1% year on year, while industrial output rose 4.4%. Services provide some evidence of a reopening economy. They do not empty distributors’ inventories of industrial materials or guarantee a rebound in orders from overseas. Look at the sector that receives the spending before extrapolating the growth reading. [⁽14⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941276.html>)

**Credit Impulse** The new instruction calls for faster issuance and use of local-government special-purpose bonds. It takes three transactions to turn that wording into a supplier’s profit: the city raises money, awards a funded contract and pays for delivery. June’s 3.05 trillion yuan in new bank loans and 4.22 trillion yuan in aggregate social financing illustrate the scale of the system, but say little about whether the marginal loan refinanced an old obligation or created a new order. Watch those disbursements alongside the growth of private working balances. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>) [⁽15⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/11/content_WS64ad48bcc6d0868f4e8ddafb.html>)

**Property** The NBS reported that first-half commercial floor space sold fell 5.3%, while total sales value increased 1.1%. That pattern points investors toward mix and transaction volumes. An urban redevelopment project may be relatively well protected by location and public sponsorship. The solvency of an indebted private developer with unsold homes elsewhere depends on the disposal price and the legal priority of creditors. A bid for all property equity on the renovation language treats those claims as though they were identical. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230720_1941412.html>) [⁽17⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230715_1941253.html>)

**Fiscal, Policy & Trade** The leadership also singled out purchases of cars, electronics and household goods and urged stronger demand for services. A sector list is different from a purchase subsidy, a spending amount or a timetable. Fiscal revenue rose 13.3% year on year in the first half. That national figure leaves room for large differences between the fiscal capacity of individual project sponsors and the needs of local developers. The trade backdrop matters just as much to manufacturers exposed to reluctant customers abroad. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>) [⁽18⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/19/content_WS64b7ce9cc6d0868f4e8ddef8.html>) [⁽19⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/13/content_WS64af8fc9c6d0868f4e8ddbe3.html>)

**Markets & Flows** Choose a liquid basket appropriate to the economic exposure. A domestic-services business can gain from consumer activity while construction materials remain under pressure. A China internet basket can respond to less regulatory uncertainty well before it reports faster sales. In each case, benchmark-relative performance is a change in price. Evidence of capital flowing into companies would require issuance, subscriptions, lending or actual customer receipts.

**New / Most Important** The one- and five-year loan prime rates were fixed at 3.55% and 4.20% before the meeting. Prior private-business guidelines had already offered political support for investment. What is new is the explicit housing diagnosis and direction to local authorities. Compare the next local decisions with the last fixings: does an eligible family pay a smaller deposit, qualify for a loan, or gain confidence that the presold property will be delivered? Until one of those changes, the transmission to earnings remains conditional. [⁽20⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202307/20/content_WS64b8c763c6d0868f4e8ddf76.html>) [⁽21⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/policies/latestreleases/202307/19/content_WS64b7e139c6d0868f4e8ddf20.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Keep selective liquid exposure to domestic demand. Scale into developers, suppliers or bulk materials only when identified local measures lead to financed transactions and physical orders. The creditor receiving a repayment may gain before common shareholders receive residual value. ⚠ Divergence: the Politburo has changed its account of the housing problem, while the latest data still show falling contracted floor space and investment. [⁽16⁾](<https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202307/t20230720_1941412.html>) [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>)

### Japan · Weak new factory orders temper the translation trade · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The July flash manufacturing PMI fell to 49.4 from 49.8 as output and new orders weakened. A depreciation of the yen can lift the translated value of an existing export sale but cannot force a foreign customer to order another machine. For a manufacturer with high fixed costs, weak units and a favorable exchange-rate move can therefore point in opposite directions for profit. [⁽22⁾](<https://www.icis.com/explore/resources/news/2023/07/24/10908031/au-jibun-bank-s-japan-flash-july-manufacturing-pmi-falls-to-49-4/>)

**Activity & Currency** June CPI excluding fresh food increased 3.3% year on year. Persistently rising prices benefit a Japanese company that can raise selling prices faster than input costs. They are more difficult for businesses tied to fixed contracts or wage bills that are about to reset. A softer yen can help revenue translation for a foreign-facing business while worsening imported costs for a domestic operator. [⁽23⁾](<https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japan-national-cpi-ex-fresh-food-yoy-in-line-with-forecasts-33-in-june-202307202331>)

**Playbook ✓** Separate bank sensitivity to a steeper domestic curve from exporters’ sensitivity to the yen. The BoJ decision remains ahead and preliminary reporting about its intentions can be wrong. Keep the position small enough to wait for the wording and bond-purchase terms. If factory orders remain weak, currency translation alone is an insufficient reason to increase industrial exposure. [⁽24⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/economy/boj-leaning-towards-keeping-yield-control-steady-next-week--sources-3130568>) [⁽6⁾](<https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/week-ahead-economic-preview-week-of-24-july-2023>)

### Australia · Separate Chinese construction hopes from local service activity · Mixed

**Macro Stance** The Chinese policy message matters most for Australia through materials orders and realized export prices. At home, services PMI at 48.0 points tells a cautionary story about spending as higher financing costs reach households. The public statement in Beijing changes the outlook for a possible future order. It does not reverse the pressure on a customer deciding whether to buy furniture on credit today. [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>) [⁽25⁾](<https://investinglive.com/news/australia-preliminary-pmi-manufacturing-496-prior-482-services-480-prior-503-20230723/>)

**Demand & Financing** June unemployment remained at 3.5%. Employers often retain workers through a soft patch while households cut discretionary purchases as mortgage payments reset. The labour report can consequently stay firm longer than a forward-looking survey of sales and workload. The RBA’s 4.10% cash rate is particularly relevant to companies depending on indebted domestic customers. [⁽26⁾](<https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/unemployment-rate-35-cent-june>) [⁽27⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>)

**Playbook ✓** Retain low-cost resource exposure selectively. Check for actual procurement and physical offtake rather than treating the announcement as a completed transaction. For domestic consumer names, require stronger turnover without renewed discounting. Further weakness in services followed by slower hiring would justify a more defensive view of household cash flows. [⁽27⁾](<https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-16.html>) [⁽28⁾](<https://www.bhp.com/-/media/documents/media/reports-and-presentations/2023/230720_bhpoperationalreviewfortheyearended30june2023.pdf>)

### Poland · KGHM supplies the domestic test of the China rally · Mixed

**Macro Stance** KGHM released preliminary June production and sales data and highlighted its domestic copper production in the first half. Volume is the part of earnings most closely connected to operational execution. The selling price, exchange translation and energy bill can still overwhelm that contribution. The Chinese policy announcement makes the distinction especially relevant to Warsaw investors: a better narrative about customers’ future demand is not yet a higher realized price on metal sold today. [⁽29⁾](<https://kghm.com/en/preliminary-production-and-sales-data-kghm-group-june-2023>) [⁽30⁾](<https://media.kghm.com/en/news-and-press-releases/kghm-polska-miedz-with-record-domestic-production-in-the-first-half-of-the-year>) [⁽13⁾](<https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202307/24/content_WS64be6483c6d0868f4e8de104.html>)

**Markets** The WIG20 rose 0.85% on Monday to 2,173.80 points. An index containing lenders and metals producers may respond to overseas policy and risk appetite even when the Polish household is buying fewer goods. June real retail sales fell 4.7% from a year earlier. To treat the equity advance as a domestic recovery, look for improvement in retailer volumes, working capital and estimates. [⁽31⁾](<https://pl.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/indeks-wig20-wzrosl-o-085-na-zamknieciu-w-poniedzialek-418218>) [⁽32⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/21-07-2023.html>)

**Fiscal & Politics** NBP’s June M3 measure grew 7.9% year on year and edged down 0.03% from May. A deposit can finance spending, be set aside for a mortgage or remain idle. The aggregate does not settle which channel is growing. The first-home programme changes affordability for eligible households while leaving existing borrowers outside that programme to manage their own interest costs. Watch approvals and actual loan disbursements. [⁽33⁾](<https://strefainwestorow.pl/wiadomosci/20230724/podaz-pieniadza-m3-w-czerwcu-wzrosla-o-79-proc-rdr-mdm-spadla-o-003-proc-nbp>) [⁽34⁾](<https://www.gov.pl/web/primeminister/2-safe-mortgage-and-home-savings-account-now-available-to-poles>)

**Corporate & Sectors** For KGHM, compare payable production, recognized sales and the prices earned. For domestic banks, compare asset yields with the rate offered to retain deposits. For retailers, compare nominal revenue with the volume moved after promotions. These businesses can produce very different profit outcomes against the same background of strong nominal wages and weak physical output. Strong headline copper production provides most information about management’s physical execution. [⁽29⁾](<https://kghm.com/en/preliminary-production-and-sales-data-kghm-group-june-2023>) [⁽35⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/20-07-2023.html>)

**Geopolitics & Security** Poland’s decision to move some troops toward the east in response to the presence of Wagner personnel in Belarus is a specific security development. Distinguish the expenditure on preparedness, a potential risk premium and disruption to an operating business. A share price can reflect more perceived risk without any physical interruption. The trigger for changing an earnings forecast is more concrete: restrictions at a border crossing, delayed deliveries, new insurance terms or a company describing a direct operating cost. [⁽36⁾](<https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/poland-to-move-soldiers-to-east-of-country-due-to-wagner-riskspap-3130507>)

**New / Most Important** Germany’s flash manufacturing PMI at 38.8 gives Polish exporters an uncomfortable comparison with the optimism surrounding Chinese demand. A Polish supplier receives orders from its actual factory customer, not from a global index of cyclical shares. Weak German orders could offset a gain elsewhere. Distinguish a metal exporter exposed to commodity prices from a producer bound to an OEM’s near-term assembly schedule. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.pmi.spglobal.com/Public/Home/PressRelease/c23ef113fdfb4d149593c3b3d2840fe9>) [⁽37⁾](<https://new.stat.gov.pl/en/publications-portal/index-numbers-sold-production-industry-june-2023>)

**Playbook ✓** Maintain positions where the company can demonstrate its own operational improvement and a credible path to cash. Increase broad industrial exposure only if external order books and realization prices support it. A further weakening of German demand alongside high domestic labour costs would pressure margins. ⚠ Divergence: Monday’s index gain and stronger domestic copper output coexist with soft retail volumes and poor German factory sentiment. [⁽31⁾](<https://pl.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/indeks-wig20-wzrosl-o-085-na-zamknieciu-w-poniedzialek-418218>) [⁽30⁾](<https://media.kghm.com/en/news-and-press-releases/kghm-polska-miedz-with-record-domestic-production-in-the-first-half-of-the-year>) [⁽32⁾](<https://stat.gov.pl/en/release-calendar/21-07-2023.html>)

## Sector Deep Dive

### Software · The multiple now meets the earnings call · Mixed

**Narrative** Microsoft and Alphabet are due to report. Investors need to distinguish the purchase of infrastructure from the eventual sale of applications. The first can increase before the second is sufficiently large to fund it. [⁽7⁾](<https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/newsquawk-us-market-wrap-markets-trundle-into-the-weekend-ahead-of-pivotal-risk-events-21-07-2023>) [⁽38⁾](<https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2023/07/18/furthering-our-ai-ambitions-announcing-bing-chat-enterprise-and-microsoft-365-copilot-pricing/>)

**Reported** The Nasdaq-100 reweighting is in effect. The changes in weights were a mechanical adjustment. The next piece of information relevant to cash flow is what management says about demand, paid usage, margins and investment. [⁽39⁾](<https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nasdaq-100-index-special-rebalance-be-effective-july-24-2023>)

**EPS Upside Watch** The more interesting test is evidence of workload demand, cloud revenue and disciplined compute investment. A pilot announcement can create an attractive sales funnel without translating into a material contribution to this quarter’s profit.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Treat benchmark-related turnover as a technical event until price movement is corroborated by new operating information. A provider selling cloud infrastructure and a customer paying for that capacity can have opposite margin exposure.

### Media · Divide attention by the company actually paid · Mixed

**Narrative** Two box-office hits establish demand for an event. An ad platform, a film studio, a streaming service and an exhibitor each collect money under a different contract. Valuing them on a common measure of views is a poor substitute for tracing their share of receipts.

**Reported** The weekend’s estimated $155 million for Barbie and $80.5 million for Oppenheimer put the size of the audience in context. AMC’s July 24 filing addresses an entirely separate issue: the terms of its revised settlement. High attendance can help operations while the share-class question remains open. [⁽40⁾](<https://www.fox19.com/2023/07/23/barbie-1st-oppenheimer-2nd-fuel-historic-box-office-bonanza/>) [⁽41⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411579/000110465923083194/tm2321869d1_8k.htm>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Look for sustained admissions beyond opening weekend and evidence that advertising improves alongside engagement at the large platforms. The earnings effect differs by contract, cost structure and ownership of intellectual property.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Successful demand at the cinema can be excellent news for attendance but modest news for a subordinated equity claim on a heavily indebted operator.

### Financials · Spending activity does not pay for its own credit losses · Mixed

**Narrative** US service activity is expanding more slowly while the two-year Treasury remains expensive funding for incremental loans. A lender with existing borrowers who keep transacting may feel the effects later than a lender relying on new originations. [⁽2⁾](<https://www.pmi.spglobal.com/Public/Home/PressRelease/bbdd92c5f4a34bb5975b23263409ad3d>) [⁽1⁾](<https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202307>)

**Reported** American Express reported record revenue of $15.1 billion and a strong billed-business quarter. Compare its spending-sensitive revenue with Goldman’s 4.0% annualized return on common equity. Those are very different ways of participating in the financial cycle. [⁽3⁾](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4962/000000496223000027/q223exhibit991.htm>) [⁽42⁾](<https://www.goldmansachs.com/pressroom/press-releases/2023/2023-07-19-q2-results>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Look for a manageable credit-loss provision and slower growth in funding and acquisition costs. The best operating leverage comes from existing customers spending more at a profitable take rate.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** A lender can preserve asset quality by tightening origination while sacrificing future revenue. Judge the earnings trajectory against both outcomes.

### Energy · The oil price gains a Chinese policy premium · Bullish

**Narrative** Crude rose as tighter supply and the prospect of stronger Chinese activity supported sentiment. A sustained effect on earnings depends on realized prices and on whether refineries actually increase throughput. The change in expectations alone cannot specify who will buy the next barrel. [⁽43⁾](<https://au.investing.com/news/commodities-news/oil-rally-takes-a-breather-ahead-of-fed-ecb-rate-hikes-2923023>)

**Reported** Chevron’s preliminary $6.0 billion in profit and 11% year-on-year growth in Permian output provide the producer’s benchmark. SLB’s report provides a different view through service activity. Keep higher underlying output separate from a prospective policy-driven increase in demand. [⁽44⁾](<https://www.chevron.com/newsroom/2023/q3/chevron-announces-second-quarter-2023-performance-highlights>) [⁽45⁾](<https://investorcenter.slb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/slb-announces-second-quarter-2023-results/>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Track production per invested dollar, realized prices and capex requirements. The supplier should show profitable delivery of backlog; the producer should turn output into free cash rather than simply replace decline rates.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** More barrels can increase producer cash while weakening marginal commodity pricing. A low-cost operator can win even if the sector’s highest-cost projects struggle.

### Industrials · Weaker factory orders meet busy airline networks · Mixed

**Narrative** Germany’s flash manufacturing PMI of 38.8 and the US reading of 49.0 put the contrast between equipment orders and service activity in sharp relief. A company supplying aircraft spares faces a different customer than one selling machines to a factory cutting shifts. [⁽9⁾](<https://www.pmi.spglobal.com/Public/Home/PressRelease/c23ef113fdfb4d149593c3b3d2840fe9>) [⁽2⁾](<https://www.pmi.spglobal.com/Public/Home/PressRelease/bbdd92c5f4a34bb5975b23263409ad3d>)

**Reported** United and American have reported robust travel activity; Packaging Corporation’s earnings now show the cost of lower packaging volumes. Supplier exposure and end-market orders are more discriminating than an aggregate industrial index. [⁽46⁾](<https://united.mediaroom.com/2023-07-19-United-Airlines-Announces-Second-Quarter-Financial-Results-Achieves-Record-Setting-Financial-Performance>) [⁽47⁾](<https://americanairlines.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/american-airlines-reports-second-quarter-2023-financial-results>) [⁽48⁾](<https://ir.packagingcorp.com/node/16516>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Look for service orders tied to aircraft already flying. Maintenance backed by utilization can be more durable than new orders sensitive to financing and long delivery schedules.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** An airline buying fewer new aircraft can still increase spending to keep its existing fleet in service. That makes aftermarket exposure economically different from the original-equipment order book.

### Discretionary · Compare a pizza purchase with an appliance replacement · Mixed

**Narrative** A restaurant meal and a replacement appliance impose different demands on household cash. The fast transaction can be sensitive to delivery fees and promotions, while an expensive appliance purchase may wait on a home move or a financing decision.

**Reported** Domino’s US same-store sales grew 0.1%. Whirlpool reaffirmed annual GAAP EPS guidance of $13–$15. Adidas’s preliminary improvement partly reflects moving Yeezy inventory. Those three results capture customer traffic, cost discipline and inventory liquidation separately. [⁽4⁾](<https://dominos.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/dominos-pizzar-announces-second-quarter-2023-financial-results>) [⁽5⁾](<https://investors.whirlpoolcorp.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2023/Whirlpool-Announces-Solid-Second-Quarter-Results-Reaffirms-2023-Earnings-Guidance/default.aspx>) [⁽11⁾](<https://www.adidas-group.com/en/investors/statutory-publications/ad-hoc-news/adidas-ag-adidas-announces-preliminary-second-quarter-results-and-updates-full-year-guidance-2023-07-24-1>)

**EPS Upside Watch** Watch price realization, the cost of customer incentives and the rate at which product inventories turn. For leisure, repeat demand after a marquee event is the equivalent test.

**⚡ Breaking Convention** Favor a high-frequency discretionary purchase with good incremental margin over a durable-goods story based solely on unit volume. Both are exposed to spending, but only one needs the customer to commit to years of financing.

The resulting $3.60 spread was far below the roughly $6.01 spread of the Friday after-hours snapshot. The comparison uses two timed observations, not two closing prices. It shows how expensive it can be to confuse the first quote following a court ruling with the price available once everyone can trade. **What Is Driving It** The company's Monday filing supplies the missing context. Friday's court concern involved the scope of the legal release. That combination puts a ceiling on a popular interpretation. A common shareholder may value a delay in dilution while running the risk that the same delay makes financing more expensive. A strong box-office weekend helps operations; converting weeks of ticket sales into cash for longer-dated obligations is a separate question. **Historical Analog** A security that might be converted at a known ratio resembles a deal spread which can reopen when the timetable moves. A professional can be right about ultimate convergence and still be hurt by the cost of borrowing the more expensive security. Monday gives the analogy a useful empirical check. An arbitrageur, a long-term theater investor and a buyer of a weekly call all look at the same letter and have different reasons for valuing it. **When the Narrative Breaks** The live test is the spread following new court notices. Sufficient positive operating cash flow and a credible plan for financing obligations could improve the value of both claims even while the spread closes. If common rises alone after another headline, record the time, borrow conditions and contemporaneous APE quote before treating it as a new valuation of the theaters. Today's common-share premium is a price for several risks at once.
