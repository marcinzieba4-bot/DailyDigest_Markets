What was known
Each edition uses a 00:00 UTC cutoff on its cover date. Market observations use completed prior sessions. July 4 and July 5 both reference the abbreviated July 3 U.S. session. Crypto can also have a weekend observation. Japan’s preliminary June industrial output was released at 08:50 JST July 31, or 23:50 UTC July 30. The final edition includes that release. U.S. trading, Chinese survey results and European flash data released later on July 31 fall beyond that edition.

The U.S. Treasury benchmark table shows par yields in percent. The spread is the ten-year yield less the two-year in basis points. The regional and company commentary identifies report periods, market sessions, earnings results and pending events as applicable. Dates without a publication time are treated as ending at 23:59:59 UTC. Ordinary news is selected on a seven-day horizon, company results on a fourteen-day horizon and macro or policy material on a thirty-five-day horizon. Earlier information is separately dated where it establishes a continuing transaction, an accounting comparison or relevant history.
