What Is Driving It: The result and its reaction are the fresh catalyst. The mechanical similarity is the shift from an audience that is easy to observe to profits that are harder to forecast. The analogy is useful because it explains why a second wave of buyers can arrive after the operating improvement has already produced a price response.
