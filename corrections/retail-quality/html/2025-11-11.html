🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  reopening hopes / Fed still divided

Macro Stance: The market is buying the possibility of government reopening before it has the legislation, restored data flow or an agreed Fed response. Reuters describes stocks jumping with Nvidia and Palantir leading the AI surge. [1] The shutdown endgame is also reviving IPO hopes, Bloomberg reports. [2] Those are rational relief channels, but a reopening expectation is not a completed reopening and does not establish what the delayed figures will show. Available private data remain mixed: October ISM services was 52.4 and ADP reported 42,000 jobs, while consumer sentiment fell near a multi-year low. [3] [4] [5] Keep the macro stance mixed. The economy has enough activity to resist an immediate collapse narrative, but household confidence and policy uncertainty leave little room for a careless increase in leverage.

Rates & Curve: The 2Y closed at 3.58%, the 10Y at 4.13%, and the 10Y−2Y spread at +55 bps. The curve is positively sloped but still flat by historical standards. Over 20 observations the move was bear steepening (both up, 10Y up more): the 2Y rose 6 bps and the 10Y 8 bps. Over five observations the 2Y fell 2 bps while the 10Y was unchanged (US Treasury, prior close 2025-11-10). The S&P 500 gained +1.54% to 6,832.43, the Nasdaq 100 +2.20%, and VIX eased to 17.60 (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2025-11-10). That is a strong risk session without an equivalent rally in long-duration bonds. Equity relief and a clean duration hedge are different trades, especially if reopening allows firmer delayed data to challenge the easing narrative.

New / Most Important: Fed policymakers remain divided over more cuts, according to Reuters. [6] Miran favours a larger December move, while Jefferson argues for a slower approach as policy nears neutral and Hammack leans against further cuts because of inflation. [7] [8] [9] The useful information is the range of reactions, not a presumed consensus. A reopened government could reduce uncertainty while increasing short-term volatility as investors reassess the path from a backlog of releases. The first relief trade therefore needs a second confirmation through growth, inflation and credit. Avoid valuing a risk asset as if a political breakthrough automatically guarantees both stronger demand and cheaper financing.

Playbook ✓: Keep a quality equity core and add selectively to businesses whose earnings can support their investment plans. Pair AI demand exposure with healthcare and reliable consumer cash generation rather than make the reopening thesis a broad leveraged beta trade. Keep duration moderate until the long end confirms that relief is reaching financing costs. Upgrade the stance if breadth improves and delayed information points toward a manageable slowdown rather than an inflation problem. Reduce it if yields rise without better demand or if credit weakens through the equity rebound. The objective is to participate in relief while preserving enough liquidity to respond when the operating evidence catches up with the political expectation.

The relief from lower external tension is real as an option, but its value depends on orders and profitable sales following through. PMIs & Activity: The October official manufacturing survey missed estimates and showed a deeper factory slump, while the services survey slowed to a three-month low. [22] [23] Add unexpectedly weaker October car sales after an extended expansion and the demand picture remains uneven. [24] The next improvement needs breadth across orders, consumption and investment rather than a single headline price change. Do not use a metal rally as a substitute for those activity measures. When producers are competing intensely, output can expand while the profit pool stays weak. For portfolio construction, favour companies with independent demand and pricing advantages over a broad position that requires domestic customers to increase purchases immediately. The market can anticipate a turn, but the entry should leave room for a slower transmission than policy enthusiasts expect.

Credit Impulse: The latest available monthly lending evidence is still September's report of loans rising less than expected as credit demand remained weak. [25] A more recent operating sign is Reuters' report that internet firms are cautiously reviving lending as Beijing encourages consumer loans. [26] That is worth following, but cautious expansion is not a broad credit boom. Borrowers need confidence in income and asset values as well as access to a lending application. Watch the quality of demand, not only the availability of credit. The PBOC bought government bonds in October as trading resumed, according to Bloomberg. [27] That can influence liquidity without guaranteeing that households and private companies want to borrow for new spending. A turn becomes convincing when money moves into profitable consumption and investment rather than merely stabilising existing obligations.

Property: The latest housing evidence remains weak: September home prices fell at the fastest pace in 11 months, Reuters reports. [28] That older monthly reading should be treated as a baseline, not described as a fresh November development. A falling housing asset can affect confidence, collateral and the willingness to bring forward a purchase. The policy challenge is therefore larger than helping one developer service debt. Keep developers underweight until transactions and prices show a more durable floor. An attractive-looking share price can still be a poor claim on a business that needs additional equity. Prefer transparency about cash and senior obligations to a vague assumption that the state must protect every shareholder. The macro recovery and the outcome for a distressed security can be very different even if both are described as a property turnaround.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: China has suspended a ban on exports of selected metals used in chip and electronics manufacturing to the US. [29] That can ease a supply constraint without resolving the broader technology contest. Alibaba-backed Moonshot has released another AI update, providing an example of continuing domestic competition and investment. [30] Avoid treating industrial policy as a guaranteed return to shareholders: better capability can intensify a price contest or raise the investment required to stay relevant. The export contraction also limits the temptation to read a truce as an immediate trade-volume acceleration. The constructive scenario requires clearer orders and margins after the policy changes; until then, use small, specific positions rather than a basket that assumes exporters, consumers and developers all recover at once.

Markets & Flows: The Shanghai Composite finished at 4,018.60, +3.32% over 20 sessions. The Hang Seng gained +1.55% in its prior session, and KWEB rose +2.21% while remaining -0.08% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2025-11-10). A risk rally can occur while the demand data stay weak, particularly when investors are buying the policy option or following a global rebound. The PBOC is also promoting yuan borrowing abroad as part of currency internationalisation, Bloomberg reports. [31] That is a structural financing initiative, not proof of stronger domestic consumption. Keep the external currency strategy separate from the private credit impulse when choosing the investment expression.

New / Most Important: The most useful new tension is consumer inflation returning to growth while exports and car sales weaken. [21] [24] Price stabilisation can help profits, but only if it comes with sufficient demand and does not merely reflect a narrow change in selected goods. The broad equity call should therefore wait for a better combination of activity and margins. In technology, selective commercial adoption can work against that weak macro backdrop, but a product update needs a financial bridge. Investors who buy both a general China recovery and an expensive AI franchise may discover that they have paid twice for a policy expectation while owning two very different operating risks.

Playbook ✓: Keep broad China beta underweight and avoid distressed developers. Hold selected profitable platform or industrial franchises only where the business can work without an immediate domestic credit surge. Add cyclicals when orders, household borrowing and property begin improving together; remove the tactical risk if a policy rally fails to produce demand confirmation. Treat yuan-financing initiatives and strategic technology support as specific channels rather than a blanket easing signal. ⚠ Divergence: onshore shares are above their 20-session reference while export demand and the factory survey are weak. That can be a rational anticipation of support, but it raises the hurdle for the next release to validate expectations rather than merely avoid another disappointment.
