Historical Analog: The mechanical analogy is the recurring turnaround trade. A disappointing business becomes a memorable speculative name; an operating improvement then brings a second wave of investors who hope the earlier weakness has become the entry opportunity. A small common-equity position permits reassessment as the operating evidence develops.
