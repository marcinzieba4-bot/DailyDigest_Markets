🇨🇳 China  Mixed  household repair / policy options

Macro Stance: The next stage of China's recovery needs a household engine, rather than another assumption that manufacturing can carry everything. A manufacturer can produce more, a platform can process more orders and a bank can receive support without households becoming willing to spend more out of recurring income. A constructive China position should therefore be built around businesses whose earnings can improve under the policies actually being pursued. Avoid loading the whole portfolio onto the untested final step of a broad consumption revival.
