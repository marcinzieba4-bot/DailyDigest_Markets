PMIs & Activity: The factory contraction matters because an export reprieve cannot cure a weak pricing environment by itself. [20][21] Contrast that with CNBC's account of May retail sales growing at their fastest pace since December 2023 as subsidies helped consumption. [22] Look for repeat purchases, broader categories and better company margins before upgrading the whole consumer complex. The next factory survey should improve alongside profit evidence; a headline expansion alone would leave the quality of the recovery unresolved.

Credit Impulse: Judge financing support by the borrower response. A manufacturer facing falling profits may use easier funding to refinance liabilities rather than build a new plant; a household worried about property may save the benefit rather than consume. The May profit decline and the subsidy-assisted retail improvement therefore point to different channels of transmission. [20][22] The useful investment signal would be businesses describing healthier demand and greater willingness to commit capital, with lenders seeing that demand outside policy-favored projects. Until then, financial easing can support asset prices more quickly than operating earnings. Prefer balance-sheet strength and visible customer demand to the assumption that every increment of credit must become a new order. A credit-sensitive equity rally deserves a larger allocation only when that operating link becomes observable.
