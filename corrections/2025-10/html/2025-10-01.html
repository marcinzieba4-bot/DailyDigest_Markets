Reuters' investment-tool announcement suggests a channel directed toward projects, not evidence that household credit appetite has recovered. The earlier Reuters report about record loans for stock bets is a different kind of credit signal: borrowing that finances asset purchases can lift turnover and collateral values without producing the same immediate demand for goods and services as a mortgage or business expansion loan. Keep those channels separate. A rise in financial leverage would make the stock market more sensitive to volatility and collateral management, even if the central bank keeps aggregate liquidity comfortable. The stronger confirmation would be credit translating into durable private investment, housing transactions and consumption. [17] [24]
