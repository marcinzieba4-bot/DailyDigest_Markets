🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  easing hopes / visibility risk

New / Most Important: The missing official employment report changes the quality of the debate. [7] That makes political duration relevant to equity risk even when companies continue operating normally. A brief interruption and a persistent information blackout deserve different portfolio responses.

Playbook ✓: Retain profitable technology and a measured allocation to quality cyclicals, finance additions by trimming speculative positions after sharp advances, and keep Treasury exposure as protection against a clearer deterioration in hiring. Add broader equity risk only if business surveys and employment indicators improve together. Cut cyclical exposure if the shutdown persists and private hiring signals weaken further. A resolution would improve visibility; it would not by itself validate every growth assumption embedded in expensive shares. Avoid building a short position solely because the market has stayed firm through uncomfortable political news.

PMIs & Activity: The latest September manufacturing assessment improved while remaining in contraction, according to CNBC's report on the official PMI. [22] An investor can own a successful platform business against this backdrop, but broad consumer or industrial exposure needs a larger margin of safety. Credit Impulse: The useful credit distinction is between money supporting productive expenditure and money supporting financial assets. Reuters' September report described record loans for stock bets and warned about volatility. [23] China Daily reported increased China Development Bank lending support for infrastructure projects, while The Banker examined credit-shock exposure from lending to small firms. [24] [25] None of these channels is equivalent to a broad private-sector investment revival. Infrastructure finance can sustain contractors and equipment demand before households feel confident enough to borrow. Equity leverage can amplify a rally without increasing the economy's ability to service debt. Prefer beneficiaries with identifiable projects or customers; demand stronger cash collection and improving borrower demand before lifting the entire financial sector. The credit impulse that matters for a durable recovery is one that travels beyond balance-sheet refinancing and speculative turnover.

Avoid treating an isolated improvement in new-home prices as a blanket signal to buy leveraged property equities.
