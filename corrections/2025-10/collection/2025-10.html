The historical boundary

Each report uses information indexed before its date at 00:00 UTC and completed prior market sessions. Later results are excluded. Publication timestamps from a news index are checked for obvious release-date mismatches, but original article vintages are not independently archived. Market prices use a current-vintage historical archive with later stock splits reversed for displayed price levels; later vendor revisions cannot be ruled out. These are reconstructed historical briefings, prepared in October 2026.

All dates are report dates. Price changes exclude dividends; instrument sessions follow their local exchanges. No weekend editions.
