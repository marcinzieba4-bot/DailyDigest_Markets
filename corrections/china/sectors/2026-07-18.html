🛢️ XLE — Energy  Bullish

Reported: BP sees a boost from energy prices in the second quarter but flags a $1 billion impairment (Reuters, [Jul 14]). Oil companies are making billions, and US calls to tax the windfall are growing (NPR, [Jul 16]).

⚡ Breaking Convention: A Hormuz supply shock, record US output and falling energy employment at once mean the sector has de-risked its cost base: higher prices no longer feed the usual wage loop, making XLE more leveraged to price and a cleaner geopolitical hedge than in 2022.
