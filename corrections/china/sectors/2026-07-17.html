💻 XLK — Technology  Bearish

Narrative: The great tech unwind is underway: the Nasdaq 100 is down 2.8% vs 4W while the S&P 500 is up 1.0%, and the chip sell-off and fading faith in the AI chip trade are converging into "The Great Rotation" out of mega-cap tech.

Reported: Wipro lagged peers after an earnings miss and a weak forecast (Reuters, [Jul 16]), and Tech Mahindra's profit rose 28.4% but missed estimates (Business Standard, [Jul 16]). IBM plunged 25% after a second-quarter warning, its CEO admitting the company fell behind (Fortune, [Jul 15]).

EPS Upside Watch: Synopsys — raised its guidance, and JPMorgan says it is still too conservative (TIKR.com, [Jul 16]). Cloudflare — analysts are raising AI and security targets (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 16]).

⚡ Breaking Convention: The consensus held that AI capex would sustain tech multiples; the market is now learning that capex can be massive and margins compress at the same time. For most companies AI spending is a cost centre before it is a revenue line, and patience for that lag is fading as rates rise.
