💻 XLK — Technology  Bearish

Reported: IBM cut its 2026 revenue outlook after a Q2 miss (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]) as mainframe sales plunged 42% (MLQ.ai, [Jul 23]). ServiceNow beat on subscription growth (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]) and analysts increased their forecasts (Benzinga, [Jul 23]). Infosys cut its revenue forecast as IT service demand remains muted (Bloomberg.com, [Jul 23]).

EPS Upside Watch: Dell — raised guidance by $27 billion and the Street is still catching up (TIKR.com, [Jul 21]). Apple — Morgan Stanley raised its target even as Wall Street cuts Tesla (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 23]).

⚡ Breaking Convention: Tech CEOs, Huang and Nadella among them, publicly defended open-source AI on July 20 as Washington threatened a crackdown; restrictions would fragment the global AI ecosystem and could push capital toward non-US AI. The open question is whether this is 1999, overinvestment before a bust, or 1995, the early innings of a build-out.
