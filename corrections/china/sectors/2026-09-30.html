TD Synnex beat by $1.04 (Investing.com, [Sep 24]) as net income rose 83.5% to $416 million (Stock Titan, [Sep 24]). Concentrix beat by $0.21 but revenue fell short of estimates (Investing.com, [Sep 29]).

EPS Upside Watch: Akamai — Anthropic will pay it $11.6 billion over seven years for cloud services (WSJ, [Sep 24]), contracted AI revenue. Microsoft — climbed 4% as Oppenheimer lifted its target to $570 on the Copilot enterprise pivot (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 25]).

That only works for the top handful — second-tier tech with real debt is being crushed alongside the Russell 2000.

🏦 XLF — Financials  Mixed

Narrative: NIMs get only limited help from a still-flat curve (+37 bps 2s10s), while a 10Y at 5.29% builds credit-quality risk in CRE and small-business books.

Reported: Jefferies fell despite an earnings beat and record banking revenue (Barron's, [Sep 28]). Deutsche Bank dipped 4.5% on a soft Q3 investment-banking revenue signal even as it raised its 2026 NII view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 24]).

EPS Upside Watch: Deutsche Bank — targets 13%+ 2028 returns as wealth and the corporate bank gain momentum (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]). Alternative managers — the SEC's proposal to open private assets to retail investors is a structural tailwind for KKR, BX and APO.

⚡ Breaking Convention: Opening private assets to retail investors is structurally new: if enacted, it redirects flows from public equity into PE, private credit and real-estate funds — bullish for alternative managers, bearish for traditional fund complexes and, at the margin, for public-market liquidity. A slow-burn shift, but the fee pool moves with it.
