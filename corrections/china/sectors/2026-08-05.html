EPS Upside Watch: Duolingo — BofA says its Q2 bookings are likely to beat expectations (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 4]). Unity Software — the stock rose 6% on two target raises before earnings (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 3]).

This is a mid-cycle broadening pattern, not a tech top — yet.
