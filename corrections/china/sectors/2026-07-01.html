💻 XLK — Technology  Bearish

The driver is AI-spending scrutiny, with big tech facing questions ahead of Q2 earnings (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 29]).

Reported: BlackBerry lifted its annual revenue forecast as the QNX unit powered growth (BNN Bloomberg, [Jun 25]) and the stock climbed 20% on Q1 earnings that exceeded expectations (TradingView, [Jun 26]). Guidewire slid despite an earnings beat and a higher outlook (StocksToTrade, [Jun 26]), and Accenture's cut to revenue-growth guidance shocked the tech industry (The Hindu, [Jun 27]).

EPS Upside Watch: IBM — shares jumped 5% after JPMorgan upgraded the stock on software acceleration (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 24]). Fair Isaac — raised guidance to $2.45 billion in revenue (TIKR.com, [Jun 25]).

⚡ Breaking Convention: The SpaceX IPO is acting as a liquidity vacuum: institutions are selling mega-cap tech to fund allocations, so the marginal dollar into tech is now zero-sum rather than additive.

⛽ XLE — Energy  Bearish

Narrative: Bearish: WTI at 68.28 (↓27.2% vs 4W) has collapsed on US-Iran talks progress, and analysts are dialling down oil forecasts as the Hormuz reopening eases supply concerns (Reuters, [Jun 30]). The majors keep buying assets into the fall.

Reported: Energy Transfer raised its 2026 guidance by $750 million (TIKR.com, [Jun 29]). Worley, the engineering contractor to the energy and resources industries, issued a fresh profit warning (AFR, [Jun 25]). Oil-tanker earnings plunged by $200,000 as more ships returned to Hormuz (Bloomberg.com, [Jun 26]).

EPS Upside Watch: Big oil's trading arms — the majors' secretive trading desks are having an extraordinary year (The Economist, [Jun 28]), a profit source that does not depend on a higher oil price.

⚡ Breaking Convention: This is a supply shock, not demand destruction: China's official manufacturing PMI rose to 50.3 in June and US manufacturing hit a 4-year high in the June flash, yet oil fell 27.2%. Energy equities should find a floor faster than they would in a recession-led slide.
