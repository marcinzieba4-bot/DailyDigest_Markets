Reported: Zscaler jumped on strong quarterly results, its FY2027 outlook and AI security momentum (Quiver Quantitative, [Sep 14]). Across the group, software stocks got new life from strong earnings (Bloomberg.com, [Sep 18]).

EPS Upside Watch: Salesforce — the stock rose after it raised its fiscal 2030 revenue forecast above estimates (TIKR.com, [Sep 17]), and six analysts turned bullish (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 18]). Snowflake — surging earnings estimates signal upside (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 18]).

⚡ Breaking Convention: Tech rallying into a rate-hiking cycle defies the 2022 playbook: hyperscaler AI spending is treated as non-discretionary whatever the rate level. The skeptic case is building — the Japan Times and Guardian flag "AI's wobbly house of cards" — but with VIX at 15 it has not reached positioning.

The risk is political, not price — EU ministers will discuss taxing energy companies' windfall profits as oil prices surge (Yahoo Finance UK, [Sep 18]).
