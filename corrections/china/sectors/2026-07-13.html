⛽ XLE — Energy  Bullish (catalyst-driven)

Narrative: Refiners rallied on the reinstated Hormuz blockade. WTI at $75.80 is still down 13.6% over 4 weeks on OPEC oversupply, but the blockade adds an acute supply-disruption premium that favours refiners running non-Gulf crude.

Reported: Exxon signalled a Q2 profit windfall as higher oil prices boost the bottom line (Reuters, [Jul 7]). Shell raised Q2 gas output guidance and flagged stronger gas trading results (Reuters, [Jul 7]), and the shares gained (Yahoo Finance UK, [Jul 7]).

EPS Upside Watch: Devon Energy — earnings expectations have been upgraded on estimate revisions (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 10]). Valero and Marathon Petroleum — a blockade-driven widening of crack spreads raises product prices faster than input costs.

⚡ Breaking Convention: A June OPEC output jump of 3.05M bpd is colliding with a blockade of roughly 20% of global oil transit, so Gulf barrels cannot reach market: the Brent-WTI spread should widen and Atlantic-basin crude decouple. With US ISM at 53.3 and China's official PMI at 50.3, demand is not the swing variable — supply is.
