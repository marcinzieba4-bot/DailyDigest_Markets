⚡ Breaking Convention: The AI-capex reward has inverted: IBM's crash and Microsoft's $1.2T loss came alongside heavy AI investment, so the market has moved from rewarding spending intent to demanding a return on capital. With AI hardware draining software budgets, "AI beneficiary" baskets now hold both the winners and the victims.
