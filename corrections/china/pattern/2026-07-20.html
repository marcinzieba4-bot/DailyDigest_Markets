🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Beats with a raised outlook or a stronger fee and volume engine. BlackRock rallied 7% after its Q2 beat (TIKR.com, [Jul 16]), J.B. Hunt rose 9% on its beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 15]), Abbott jumped as it beat and raised its 2026 outlook (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 16]) and UnitedHealth surged as earnings momentum returned (Reuters, [Jul 16]).

Punished: Beats with a softer forward line, and AI hardware already in the price. Netflix sank 9% as a weak outlook overshadowed its beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 17]); Intuitive Surgical tumbled 11% despite topping estimates (TIKR.com, [Jul 17]) on slow procedure growth (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 17]); IBM crashed 25% on its earnings warning (CNBC, [Jul 14]). TSMC beat and raised guidance but the shares slid (NAI500, [Jul 16]).

Pattern: The beat itself is worth little; the guide and the volume line get paid. Where the next quarter looks better (fees, freight, medical costs) stocks rise; where it softens, or where AI demand is already priced, even a beat-and-raise sells.

Attention into EPS: Alphabet, Tesla and Intel headline this week's reports (Investopedia, [Jul 19]). Alphabet — BofA raised its target on cloud growth (Investing.com, [Jul 16]); the test is whether Google Cloud's $20B run justifies a $185B AI bet (BigGo Finance, [Jul 18]). Cloud acceleration is the rewarded side; capex without it is a TSMC-style sale. Tesla — four target hikes into the print (Investor's Business Daily, [Jul 14]) and a preview saying the numbers will beat but the narrative may not (Seeking Alpha, [Jul 18]): a beat without a forward story is the Netflix profile, so the risk is a sale. Intel — can AI CPU momentum survive the margin test (Moomoo, [Jul 19]); the margin guide decides it. Texas Instruments — likely to beat (Zacks Investment Research, [Jul 17]) before July 22 earnings (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 15]), but the stock is up 64% in 2026 (TIKR.com, [Jul 17]): only a raised recovery guide gets paid.

Playbook: Own guide-driven beats, fade crowded AI prints into the event: Wall Street is raising expectations for Goldman and other banks after strong earnings (Barron's, [Jul 18]), so pair the fee engine against mega-cap AI held into the report. Risk: an Alphabet cloud acceleration would break the sell-the-beat pattern and squeeze the short leg.
