🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat that comes with a raised outlook on real demand. Carnival surged 12% (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 29]) as record demand lifted yields and it raised 2026 EPS (BigGo Finance, [Sep 29]); CarMax jumped 6% as earnings hit $1.16 per share and buybacks resume (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 29]); Hapag-Lloyd rose as it lifted its 2026 earnings outlook again (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 29]); Vicor jumped 20% in a day after a guidance raise (TIKR.com, [Sep 23]).

Punished: Beats without growth, and margin or cost warnings. General Mills beat on pricing and mix (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]), yet the stock fell (Quartz, [Sep 23]); Paychex fell 9% after earnings (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 23]); Jefferies fell despite an earnings beat and record banking revenue (Barron's, [Sep 28]); Shein's earnings disappointed, sending the stock lower (WSJ, [Sep 29]); J.B. Hunt's profit warning sent shares lower as costs rose (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]).

Pattern: The beat itself earns nothing — the biggest earnings beats are becoming the worst trades on Wall Street (Invezz, [Sep 26]). The market pays for a raised outlook backed by bookings, freight rates or traffic, and sells beats built on pricing, cost cuts or one-offs; with the 10Y at 5.29%, even record revenue was sold at Jefferies.

Attention into EPS: Micron — JPMorgan sees it positioned for a beat-and-raise ahead of Q4 results (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 29]), while 24/7 Wall St. says its guidance may look weak but to buy anyway (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 29]): the raise is the whole trade, a beat alone sits with Jefferies. Nike — reports post-market on October 01 (Moomoo, [Sep 25]) with Q1 earnings expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 24]); the only bullish aspect is the extreme bearishness (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 28]): a cost-led beat on weak demand is the General Mills profile. PepsiCo — Deutsche Bank flags concerns over its North America recovery (TradingView, [Sep 28]): pricing without volume is punished. Accenture — reports Q4 on October 1 needing to break its slide (TIKR.com, [Sep 25]): only a better outlook pays.

Playbook: Own demand-led beat-and-raise stories and fade pricing-led staples into their prints (PepsiCo; Constellation Brands, whose Q2 earnings are expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 29])). Into Micron, size for the guide, not the beat. Risk: a Carnival-style demand surprise at Nike squeezes the fade.
