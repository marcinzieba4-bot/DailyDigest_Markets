🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised or credibly held outlook with demand behind it. Target rose 5% on a Q2 beat and a sharply raised full-year outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 19]); Estée Lauder jumped 11.8% (StockStory, [Aug 19]) as it raised margin guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 19]); Toll Brothers jumped after a beat in a 'tough' housing market (Barron's, [Aug 19]); Atlassian jumped 37.4% on a strong Q2 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 6]).

Punished: Any sign the core is slowing, even alongside a raise. TJX fell as earnings showed a consumer pullback (Barron's, [Aug 19]) although it raised its full-year profit forecast (Quartz, [Aug 19]); Coty fell as it warned of a 'transition' year ahead (Bloomberg, [Aug 19]); Klarna dropped 19% after cutting full-year guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 18]); Cisco slid 8% despite a beat and stronger-than-expected guidance (CNBC, [Aug 13]).

Pattern: The guide is the price, and the market now reads what sits underneath it: a raise backed by traffic or margin is paid, a raise that masks a slowing core is sold — TJX's 'self-inflicted' slowdown clouded its beat (Reuters, [Aug 19]). The beat itself buys nothing.

Attention into EPS: Walmart — nears $1 trillion before its earnings test (TradingView, [Aug 19]) after 25 straight quarters of revenue beats (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 19]): another beat is priced, so only a raise backed by traffic pays, on the Target template rather than TJX's. Nvidia — reports next week (Invezz, [Aug 19]), with previews asking what it will take to beat the bar (Moomoo, [Aug 19]): the guide and gross margin decide, as Cisco showed. Marvell — reports on August 27 (Barchart.com, [Aug 19]): custom-silicon demand is the line to watch. Alibaba — a mixed setup with high expectations from its AI business (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 19]) after Kuaishou's earnings dropped by a third on AI and creator costs (Bloomberg.com, [Aug 19]): AI cost discipline sets the reaction.

Playbook: Own demand-backed raisers into their prints and fade beats where the core is slowing; in retail, prefer turnaround stories with a raised guide over high-bar names already priced for a beat. Risk: FOMC minutes that flagged rate-hike optionality could compress multiples across both legs, turning stable guides into sells.
