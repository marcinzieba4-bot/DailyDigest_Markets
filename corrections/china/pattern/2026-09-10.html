🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Profit upside the market can see now — a raised guide backed by accelerating revenue or cost discipline. Snowflake soared 23% as its AI-fueled guidance raise topped estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]); Dell surged 9% after lifting its fiscal 2027 forecast on AI server strength (CNBC, [Sep 1]); Signet surged 14% as a raised profit outlook overrode flat sales guidance (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]); Oddity Tech rose 24% after a Q2 beat and updated outlook (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]).

Punished: Beats where cash flow, costs or the next quarter wobble. Chewy sank 11% as a free cash flow miss overshadowed its raised outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]); Navan beat and raised but tumbled on cost concerns (Reuters, [Sep 9]); NetApp raised guidance but tumbled on a free cash flow decline (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 2]); Broadcom sank despite its beat (CNBC, [Sep 2]); Casey's slid despite a beat as inside sales growth disappointed (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]).

Pattern: The beat and even the raise are table stakes; the reaction is decided by the quality line — free cash flow, costs and the next quarter. Revenue acceleration pays, as Snowflake's product revenue accelerated to 37% (Investing.com, [Sep 3]), and so does profit discipline, as at Signet; any cash-flow or cost question is sold, in AI and in retail alike.

Attention into EPS: Adobe — reports September 10 (AlphaStreet, [Sep 8]); the stock is down after 10 of its last 12 reports (Benzinga, [Sep 9]) and ARR concerns persist amid a freemium push (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 9]): a quality-line doubt going in, so only accelerating ARR pays. Micron — its Sept. 30 earnings could be the most important catalyst for AI memory stocks this year (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]): priced-for-perfection hardware that sits with Broadcom unless the guide accelerates. Lennar — may move 5.2% on its Sept. 16 report (Investing.com, [Sep 9]), with analysts expecting an earnings decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]): with mortgage rates above 7%, the guide is the line that matters. Kroger — options traders bet bullish (Schaeffer's Investment Research, [Sep 8]): after Casey's fell on inside sales, its reaction rests on the same-store line.

Playbook: Own beats with clean cash conversion and visible profit upside — the Snowflake / Signet profile — and fade prints that hinge on a quality line already in doubt, such as Adobe's ARR or Lennar's guide. Risk: a clean ARR beat at Adobe would reverse a stock that has fallen after most of its reports, squeezing the fade.
