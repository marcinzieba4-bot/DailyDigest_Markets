🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised forward line and demand behind it. Palantir soared 12% on a blowout quarter (CNBC, [Aug 3]) as its call produced its largest-ever guidance raise (TIKR.com, [Aug 4]); Atlassian jumped 37.4% (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 6]); Shopify jumped 25.1% in a strong quarter (StockStory, [Aug 5]); Airbnb soared 9% on strong guidance for the third quarter (CNBC, [Aug 6]); Amazon soared 12% as traders picked tech earnings winners and losers (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 31]).

Punished: A beat with a softer guide, or spending that outruns revenue. AMD's shares sank on lofty expectations even as data-centre sales doubled (The Straits Times, [Aug 5]); Datadog had its worst day in more than 6 years on a beat (Barron's, [Aug 6]); SpaceX hit a new all-time low as AI capex jumped (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 5]); Meta sank 9% after its Q3 revenue forecast missed (Investing.com, [Jul 29]); Apple sank 8% in the same tech sort (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 31]).

Pattern: Blockbuster earnings bolstered stocks' record run (WSJ, [Aug 9]), yet AI momentum stocks tumbled as earnings season shook the tech sector (Investing.com, [Aug 6]). The index is paid for aggregate beats; single stocks are paid only for a raised guide backed by booked demand, and sold when a beat comes with a softer outlook or capex without matching revenue.

Attention into EPS: Nvidia — Bank of America argues it is too cheap versus chip peers (investingLive, [Aug 7]) and IBD maps a trade around its report (Investor's Business Daily, [Aug 7]); with AMD sold on a beat, only a raised data-centre guide clears the bar. Lumentum — previewed into its report as lasers face a CPO scare (Moomoo, [Aug 9]): a raised guide puts it on the rewarded side, a soft one with Sandisk. CAVA — reports on August 11 after a 34% slide (TIKR.com, [Aug 5]), with Sweetgreen having cut its outlook (CNBC, [Aug 6]): the bar is low, so a held guide could be paid. Super Micro — previewed into its Q4 report (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 7]): AI hardware priced for a beat, so the outlook decides the move.

Playbook: Own guide-raisers with visible demand (Palantir, Atlassian, Airbnb) and fade beats that arrive with a soft outlook or capex outrunning revenue. Into prints, prefer low-bar names such as CAVA over crowded AI hardware priced for perfection. Risk: July CPI this week; a hot print hardens the September-hike path and hits the long-duration winners hardest.
