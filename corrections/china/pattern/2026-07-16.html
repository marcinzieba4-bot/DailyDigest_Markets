🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: clean revenue momentum from capital markets, AI hardware or a low bar. Goldman beat with the stock up 2% (Fox Business, [Jul 14]); BlackRock rose after its beat (Barron's, [Jul 15]); J.B. Hunt rose 9% on a Q2 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 15]); Aehr Test Systems popped 40% after hours on a beat and upbeat FY27 guidance (TradingView, [Jul 14]). Morgan Stanley posted record revenue and profit as equities trading surged 69% (CNBC, [Jul 15]).

Punished: beat-and-raise with a cost or segment caveat. J&J fell despite a beat-and-raise quarter (CNBC, [Jul 15]) as a medtech miss overshadowed it (Reuters, [Jul 15]); Elevance slid as its profit forecast hike was not enough to impress (Reuters, [Jul 15]); United fell after hours despite a beat and raised outlook (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 15]) with nearly $6 billion of fuel costs (Reuters, [Jul 15]); Citi tanked after flagging higher expenses (Reuters, [Jul 15]); IBM had its worst day on record, down 25%, after a Q2 warning (CNBC, [Jul 14]).

Pattern: beat-and-raise is no longer enough — any rising cost line (expenses, medical spending, fuel) or soft segment sinks the stock. The market pays only for revenue momentum on the right side of the trading and AI-hardware boom.

Attention into EPS: TSMC reports July 16 (Tech Times, [Jul 11]) with analysts expecting record revenue (Benzinga, [Jul 15]) — revenue is known, so only a guidance and capex step-up pays. Netflix reports July 16 (tastylive, [Jul 15]); the stock hasn't been this cheap in 21 months but analysts are growing cautious (Benzinga, [Jul 15]) — a low bar if engagement holds. UnitedHealth reports Thursday (Benzinga, [Jul 15]) at $4.85 EPS expected (AlphaStreet, [Jul 13]); analysts see improving medical cost trends (Seeking Alpha, [Jul 15]), but Elevance shows a raise can still sell. GE Aerospace could raise 2026 guidance by $500 million, RBC says (Benzinga, [Jul 15]) — a raise is priced, so costs decide.

Playbook: own trading and AI-hardware momentum into the remaining prints; underweight beat-and-raise stories with a visible cost line (managed care into UnitedHealth, fuel-exposed airlines). The risk is a clean UnitedHealth beat on falling medical costs, or TSMC guiding below a whisper that is already high.
