🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Demand-led revenue with the guide raised on top. Ross Stores surged 9% after beating and lifting guidance (TIKR.com, [Aug 21]); Deere rallied 7% after quarterly profit climbed and it raised its full-year forecast (TIKR.com, [Aug 21]); CoreWeave soared after booming AI demand boosted its outlook (Bloomberg.com, [Aug 12]); Scotiabank jumped on its report as the TSX hit a record high (Reuters, [Aug 25]).

Punished: A weak forward line or a slowing top line, even on a beat. Dick's Sporting Goods fell 30% as it missed and cited a 'challenging' footwear market (CNBC, [Aug 25]); Intuit tumbled despite an earnings beat as weak 2027 guidance weighed (TradingView, [Aug 25]); Walmart tumbled 9% after its outlook disappointed (CNBC, [Aug 20]) as US comparable sales growth slowed to just 2.6% (The Motley Fool, [Aug 20]); Cisco slid 8% despite a beat and stronger-than-expected guidance (CNBC, [Aug 13]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the quality of growth decides. Revenue accelerating on real demand is paid, a soft guide or slowing comps is sold, and one-off gains do not count, as Life360's 21% tumble on a one-time tariff benefit showed (TIKR.com, [Aug 17]).

Attention into EPS: Nvidia — 'a beat is a fail, a blowout is base case' (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Aug 25]) after the stock dropped after 5 of its last 6 reports (Benzinga, [Aug 24]); Raymond James lifted its target to $352 on CPU growth (Investing.com, [Aug 25]), so only an accelerating guide pays. Marvell — reports on August 27 (Moomoo, [Aug 21]) after Rosenblatt raised its target to $300 on AI chip growth (Investing.com, [Aug 25]): target hikes into the print put it on the Cisco side unless the guide steps up. Salesforce — rising Agentforce adoption is expected to aid sales (TradingView, [Aug 25]): an accelerating AI revenue line fits the rewarded profile, but Intuit shows a soft guide would be sold. Canada's other big banks — a packed earnings week (Finimize, [Aug 25]) after BMO and Scotiabank beat as capital-markets units surged (Bloomberg.com, [Aug 25]): the bar is a repeat of that line.

Playbook: Own demand-led accelerators that have not run into the print and fade crowded AI hardware on a plain beat: Dell's buyers sold even after analysts raised targets (TIKR.com, [Aug 21]). In retail, the Dick's crash argues for caution on mass-market names into Dollar General and Best Buy this week (Retail Brew, [Aug 24]). Risk: a blowout Nvidia guide re-rates the whole AI complex and squeezes the fade.
