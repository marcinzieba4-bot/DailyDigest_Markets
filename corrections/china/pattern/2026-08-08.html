🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised guide, in any sector. UnitedHealth's stock rallied 21% on raised 2026 guidance (TIKR.com, [Aug 7]); Insmed topped Q2 estimates and soared 34% on Brinsupri uptake (Zacks Investment Research, [Aug 7]); Atlassian surged 30% after earnings (Barron's, [Aug 7]); Motorola Solutions jumped 8% as strong Q2 earnings and raised guidance beat estimates (TIKR.com, [Aug 7]).

Punished: A cut or a soft outlook, even on record sales or a beat. The Trade Desk plunged 28% after an earnings miss triggered a downgrade avalanche (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 7]); Honeywell Aerospace plunged 24% after a guidance cut (AskTraders, [Aug 6]); Premium Brands sank 14% despite record sales as a guidance cut stole the show (kalkine.ca, [Aug 7]); Datadog had its worst day in more than 6 years even as earnings beat estimates (Barron's, [Aug 6]).

Pattern: The guide is the whole trade. Reactions to guidance changes are outsized in both directions and cut across sectors — a defensive managed-care name rallied like a software stock — while the quarter's beat barely registers.

Attention into EPS: Berkshire Hathaway — reports Saturday morning with the stock at a 52-week high yet still behind the S&P 500 this year (The Motley Fool, [Aug 7]): a high into the print raises the bar, so the reaction rides on operating commentary, not the headline. Nvidia — AMD's results point to massive AI demand (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 5]), but AMD fell 8% on its own beat (BeInCrypto, [Aug 4]): the most crowded print needs a data-centre guide above the bar. Cardinal Health — previews ask whether pharma and specialty demand will boost its Q4 (TradingView, [Aug 7]): a guide-raise candidate in the group where UnitedHealth's raise was paid. Jack in the Box — in preview (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 7]) after Wendy's scrapped its guidance (Barron's, [Aug 7]): value burgers sit on the punished side unless the outlook holds.

Playbook: Own likely guide-raisers in under-owned groups — healthcare and industrial services — into their prints, and fade crowded names where only a raise is priced, from AI hardware to ad-tech. Pair long managed-care guide-raisers against programmatic ad-tech. Risk: hiring weakness persists (ing think, [Aug 5]), and a softer labour backdrop could make every guide more cautious, hitting both legs.
