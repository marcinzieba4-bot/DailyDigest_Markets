🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Demand visible in volume or traffic, with a raised guide. Vicor jumped 20% in a day after a guidance raise (TIKR.com, [Sep 23]); Cracker Barrel rose 10% after a Q4 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]) as traffic trends improved (Barron's, [Sep 23]); Trip.com jumped over 5% pre-market as Q2 revenue beat estimates, led by international business (TradingKey, [Sep 16]); Next's shares rose after a guidance lift (WSJ, [Sep 17]) on booming overseas sales (Investors' Chronicle, [Sep 17]).

Punished: Beats built on price, cost cuts or refunds, and anything exposed to fuel and labour. General Mills beat as cost savings offset volumes (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]), yet the stock fell (Quartz, [Sep 23]); Macy's fell 5% despite a beat and a raised view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 11]) as tariff refunds masked margin pressure (Benzinga, [Sep 11]); JB Hunt fell 9% after warning of a quarterly earnings decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]) on fuel and driver costs (Simply Wall Street, [Sep 16]); KB Home fell after its beat (Barron's, [Sep 22]) as it cut its margin outlook (WSJ, [Sep 22]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes — H.B. Fuller topped its Q3 profit forecast (AlphaStreet, [Sep 24]), yet the stock dipped (The Motley Fool, [Sep 24]). The market pays for volume that proves demand at today's prices and sells EPS made by pricing, savings or refunds; the one exception is trade-down, with AutoZone's best day in more than 2 years (Barron's, [Sep 22]) on a refund-aided beat.

Attention into EPS: Micron — reports its fiscal earnings on Sept. 30 (International Business Times Australia, [Sep 23]) after Citi raised its target to $1,300 on stronger DRAM pricing and undersupply (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 23]): volume plus price is the rewarded profile, but only a raised guide pays. Nike — Q1 earnings are expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 24]): without volume it sits with Macy's. Carnival — expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 22]): travel demand is rewarded, but fuel is the JB Hunt trap. McCormick — likely to report lower Q3 earnings (Benzinga, [Sep 23]), like Conagra (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]): branded staples on the General Mills side, where a price-made beat gets sold.

Playbook: Own volume-led stories into the print (Micron, Carnival) and fade branded staples beating on price (McCormick, Conagra). Risk: Wall Street flipped net negative on S&P 500 earnings after a 23-week upgrade streak (Tech Times, [Sep 23]) and the 10Y is at 5.19%, so multiple compression can sink even a volume beat, Micron's most of all.
