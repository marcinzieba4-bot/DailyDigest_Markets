🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised forward line and booked demand behind it, even when spending rises. Amazon soared 12% (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 31]) after lifting investment plans on strong cloud sales (Reuters, [Jul 31]); Microsoft beat with $90B in revenue and jumped 9% (KOMO, [Jul 29]); Ford jumped 6% on a Q2 beat and raised 2026 guidance (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 29]); Lockheed Martin jumped on an earnings beat and raised guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]).

Punished: A softer next quarter, or costs rising without a revenue lift, even on a clear beat. Meta sank 9% after its Q3 revenue forecast missed estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 29]); Apple sank 8% (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 31]) on a forecast dogged by supply-chain struggles (Reuters, [Jul 30]); Reddit cratered 23% (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 31]) even as its results blew past estimates (CNBC, [Jul 30]); STMicro dropped 17% after its Q3 revenue outlook missed (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]).

Pattern: The AI trade has split (CNBC, [Jul 30]) along the forward guide: spending backed by cloud or pricing demand is paid, a weaker next quarter is sold, and the EPS beat is only table stakes. UPS tumbled despite a Q2 beat and guidance raise (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 28]) once it guided third-quarter domestic revenue flat (CNBC, [Jul 28]).

Attention into EPS: AMD — reports on August 04 (Moomoo, [Jul 29]) after surging with chip stocks (CNBC, [Jul 30]); AI hardware is paid only for the guide, and KLA fell despite a beat on high investor expectations (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 28]), so the data-centre outlook decides the move. Palantir — a $200 target ahead of critical earnings (TradingView, [Jul 27]) prices a beat; only a raised guide clears that bar. Novo Nordisk — reports on August 05 (Moomoo, [Jul 30]) after a trial miss (Simply Wall Street, [Jul 31]); healthcare has sold guidance cuts at AbbVie and Alnylam, so the outlook decides. Cheniere Energy — expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 30]); with Chevron beating on refining margins while Exxon missed (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 31]), energy prints are judged on margins, not the oil price.

Playbook: Own guide-raisers with booked demand into the print and fade beats where spending rises without a revenue lift. Pair long memory makers, which soared after Amazon and Apple flagged soaring costs and a supply crunch (TradingView, [Jul 31]), against memory buyers carrying the cost: Qualcomm issued light earnings guidance during the memory crunch (CNBC, [Jul 29]). Risk: a memory price spike that destroys device demand hits both legs.
