🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Beats where demand shows up in the top line, above all AI demand that lifts the guide. Snowflake soared 23% as an AI-fueled guidance raise topped estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]); Oracle's cloud infrastructure revenue more than doubled (CNBC, [Sep 10]) and its capex guidance sent Dell and Hewlett Packard Enterprise up 11% (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]); Copart jumped 6% on a revenue beat and a $2 billion acquisition (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]).

Punished: Beats with a soft forward line, AI read as a threat, rising costs or refund-flattered profits. Adobe slid 4% on conservative guidance (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]) as fears about AI's impact returned (Bloomberg.com, [Sep 10]); Lululemon dropped 18% to an 8-year low after a third guidance cut (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]); American Eagle sank 13% as a $161M tariff refund flattered its beat (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 10]); J.B. Hunt plunged 13% on a cost-driven profit warning (CNBC, [Sep 16]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes. The market pays when AI appears as demand in the revenue line and the guide rises, and sells beats where AI looks like a substitute, costs climb or refunds do the lifting; even AI hardware needs a raise above a high bar — Broadcom fell despite strong results (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]).

Attention into EPS: Micron — reports on Sept. 30 (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 15]); previews see a massive margin of safety (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 16]), but the stock dropped 4.9% on a $50 billion earnings bar (TechStock², [Sep 11]): only a raised guide clears that bar. KB Home — Q3 earnings are expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 15]) after Lennar's double miss (Benzinga, [Sep 16]): a deliveries cut would put it on the punished side. General Mills — expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]), but Kroger's beat came with a sales-outlook cut (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 14]): staples trade on the sales line. Nike — a preview sees improving risk-reward (Investing.com, [Sep 16]) with shares at a 12-year low on China, tariffs and turnaround doubts (investingLive, [Sep 16]): a low bar, but Lululemon shows another guide cut is still sold.

Playbook: Own AI infrastructure where capex guidance flows through (the Dell and HPE profile) and fade software beats exposed to AI substitution; in consumers, fade refund-flattered beats and prefer clean sales growth. Risk: a Micron blowout re-rates AI hardware and squeezes the fade, while a 10Y at 4.95% compresses every multiple.
