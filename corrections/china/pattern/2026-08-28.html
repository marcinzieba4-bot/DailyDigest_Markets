🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raise backed by AI demand that the market had not priced. Salesforce rocketed 22% for its second-best day ever (CNBC, [Aug 27]); Okta surged 20% pre-market as it raised full-year guidance for the second time this year (TradingKey, [Aug 27]); CrowdStrike soared 15% after a beat and a raised outlook (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Aug 26]); Nvidia surged 8% in pre-market trading on better-than-expected earnings (Moomoo, [Aug 27]); Dollar General rose as it hiked earnings guidance (Barron's, [Aug 27]).

Punished: A beat-and-raise into a crowded bar, or profits that lean on one-offs. Marvell raised its annual forecasts, but its shares fell as Google deal questions lingered (Reuters, [Aug 27]); Best Buy's stronger forecast failed to reassure investors (Reuters, [Aug 27]); Workday exceeded expectations, but the stock dropped (StockStory, [Aug 27]); Dollar Tree scored with tariff refunds, yet its stock fell (Barron's, [Aug 27]).

Pattern: Beat-and-raise is now the minimum, not the trigger: the market pays for an AI revenue line that accelerates the guide beyond what was priced, and sells raises that only meet a run-up or rest on refunds, as Life360's 21% tumble on a one-time tariff benefit showed (TIKR.com, [Aug 17]).

Attention into EPS: Palo Alto Networks — reports on September 01 (Moomoo, [Aug 26]) after JPMorgan raised its target (TIKR.com, [Aug 26]): CrowdStrike and Okta set the template, so a raised outlook is what pays. Credo — reports on September 01 (Moomoo, [Aug 26]) with Stifel reiterating Buy (Investing.com, [Aug 24]): an AI-networking name on the Marvell side of the pattern, where a raise alone may sell. Ollie's — reports on September 02 (Moomoo, [Aug 27]) after RBC said Ollie's will cut 2026 comp guidance as tariff refunds dull the profit impact (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 25]): the Dollar Tree profile, so a refund-led beat would be faded. Campbell's — Q4 earnings are expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 27]): with no growth priced, any volume stabilisation would be the surprise.

Playbook: Own AI-demand software into prints where the AI line is visible and fade crowded AI hardware and networking on a plain beat-and-raise. Risk: the software bid is already broadening beyond the earnings winners, with UiPath up 9% (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 27]), so a crowded rebound can reverse on the first soft guide.
