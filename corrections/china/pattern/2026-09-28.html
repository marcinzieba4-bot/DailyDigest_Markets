🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A better forward line carried by demand or traffic. Vicor jumped 20% in a day after a guidance raise (TIKR.com, [Sep 23]); Next shares rose after a guidance lift (WSJ, [Sep 17]) on booming overseas sales (Investors' Chronicle, [Sep 17]); Worthington Enterprises jumped 15% on strong earnings and a cash flow boost (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]); Cracker Barrel rose 10% after a Q4 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]) as traffic trends improved (Barron's, [Sep 23]).

Punished: Beats without volume, and margin, cost or guidance warnings. General Mills beat as cost savings offset volumes (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]), yet the stock fell (Quartz, [Sep 23]); KB Home fell after a beat (Barron's, [Sep 22]) as it cut its margin outlook (WSJ, [Sep 22]); Paychex fell 9% after earnings (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 23]); J.B. Hunt fell 9% after warning of a quarterly earnings decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]); Fluence plunged after cutting full-year guidance (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 16]).

Pattern: The beat itself earns nothing — the biggest earnings beats are becoming the worst trades on Wall Street (Invezz, [Sep 26]). What pays is a raised guide or improving traffic; a beat built on cost savings or paired with a margin cut is sold, and with the 10Y at 5.25% a guidance cut is punished hardest.

Attention into EPS: Micron — reports post-market on September 30 (Moomoo, [Sep 25]); analysts see over 350% revenue growth (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 27]) and Benzinga puts 80% odds on beating every estimate (Benzinga, [Sep 25]): the beat is priced, so only a raised guide on DRAM pricing pays, and after a 275% year (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 23]) the bar for that raise is high. Nike — reports post-market on October 01 (Moomoo, [Sep 25]) with Q1 earnings expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 24]): a cost-led beat on weak demand sits with General Mills. Carnival — expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 22]); with WTI at $92.99, fuel is the guide risk. CarMax — due to report (StockStory, [Sep 27]) with its share price rising faster than the underlying business (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 21]): a beat without a better outlook is the punished profile.

Playbook: Own the guide, not the beat: fade cost-led consumer beats into the print (Nike; McCormick, whose Q3 earnings are expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 24])) and trim Micron rather than chase the 80% beat odds. Risk: a demand-led raise like Vicor's squeezes both fades.
