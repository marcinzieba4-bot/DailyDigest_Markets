🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with an accelerating forward line behind it. Palantir skyrocketed 29% after 'otherworldly' results (CNBC, [Aug 4]); Shopify jumped 25.1% on a strong Q2 (StockStory, [Aug 5]); CoreWeave popped 14% as revenue doubled on accelerating AI infrastructure demand (CNBC, [Aug 11]); Babcock & Wilcox surged 40.5% after its Q2 beat and guidance rise (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 11]).

Punished: A beat where margins or the next-year guide disappoint, AI names included. AMD's revenue climbed 50% and data-centre sales doubled, but the stock fell (CNBC, [Aug 4]); Cisco slid 8% despite a beat and stronger-than-expected guidance (CNBC, [Aug 13]) on gross margin fears (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 13]); Tapestry sank 15% despite a Q4 beat because of its FY27 guide (TIKR.com, [Aug 14]); Sezzle plunged 34% as a cautious growth outlook overshadowed its beat (TIKR.com, [Aug 10]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes. The market pays for acceleration it can underwrite — revenue doubling, a raised guide — and sells any print where the margin or the forward guide falls short of the bar, as Applied Materials beat everything but Wall Street's expectations for margins (MarketBeat, [Aug 15]).

Attention into EPS: Nvidia — BofA says it is poised for a Q2 beat and guidance raise on Vera Rubin demand (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 10]): a beat is priced in, so as with AMD and Cisco the guide and gross margin decide. Walmart and Target — report this week (Investopedia, [Aug 16]) and are poised to benefit from value shoppers (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 16]), but BofA sent a warning on Target stock before earnings (TheStreet, [Aug 14]): in retail the guide has moved stocks more than the quarter. Home Depot and Lowe's — Oppenheimer says results are unlikely to show signs of recovery (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 14]), with Lowe's call on Aug. 19 (Lowe’s Corporate, [Aug 12]): a low bar, so a steady outlook could be paid. Snowflake and CrowdStrike — RBC raised targets ahead of earnings (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 14]): software is paid only on a raised guide, as Datadog's worst day in more than 6 years on a beat showed (Barron's, [Aug 6]).

Playbook: Own demand-backed guide-raisers into their prints and fade beats where margin expectations are stretched: prefer AI names whose revenue is accelerating over hardware already priced for a beat, and in retail favour value formats over apparel guides. Risk: with the 10Y at 4.71%, a rates shock hits the long-duration leg of the trade first.
