🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised guide outside the AI spending loop. Medpace soared 15% after beating and raising guidance (TIKR.com, [Jul 23]); Wabtec jumped 10% after earnings (TIKR.com, [Jul 23]); Roper climbed 5% as it raised full-year profit guidance (TIKR.com, [Jul 24]); Verizon rose on an earnings beat and raised guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 24]).

Punished: AI spending and a weaker next quarter, even on big beats. Alphabet fell more than 6% on capex growth expectations despite its beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]); Intel fell nearly 8% despite a big Q2 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 24]); STMicro dropped 17% after its Q3 revenue outlook missed (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]); American Airlines is down 13.1% after record revenue but a weaker profit outlook (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 24]).

Pattern: At report time the AI label is now a liability: the semiconductor index dropped 6% despite strong earnings (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 24]). The beat-and-raise that gets paid sits outside the AI capex loop (healthcare services, rail, software, Verizon's wireless), and any soft forward line is sold.

Attention into EPS: Big Tech earnings and the Fed dominate next week (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 24]). Microsoft — soaring capex versus a record-low valuation (Moomoo, [Jul 23]): after Alphabet and Intel, a capex lift is the sale unless AI revenue accelerates. Meta — analysts expect an earnings decline (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 22]): a low bar, but on the spending side of the pattern. Amazon — fell 4% on a Senate China probe and AI-spending jitters ahead of earnings (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 23]): part of the fear is priced. Apple — reports on July 30 (Moomoo, [Jul 24]) after Morgan Stanley raised its target (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 23]): the least capex-heavy giant, closest to the rewarded side. AMD — UBS raised its target to $730 on the server CPU outlook (Investing.com, [Jul 24]): high expectations into an AI-spending sell-off.

Playbook: Own guide-raisers outside AI, fade AI spenders into their prints. Verizon raised guidance for a second straight quarter (Stocktwits, [Jul 24]); pair a guide-raiser like it against a hyperscaler held into its report. Risk: with chips already down 6%, a Microsoft print showing AI revenue keeping pace with capex could spark a sharp AI rebound.
