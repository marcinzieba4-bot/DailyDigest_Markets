🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Beats driven by demand, traffic or cash flow. Vicor jumped 20% in a day after a guidance raise (TIKR.com, [Sep 23]); Worthington Enterprises jumped 15% on strong earnings and a cash-flow boost (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]); Cracker Barrel rose 10% after a Q4 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]) as traffic improved (Barron's, [Sep 23]); AutoZone climbed 6% as a profit beat offset a revenue miss (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 22]); AeroVironment jumped after a beat (Barron's, [Sep 10]).

Punished: Beats built on cost-out, a margin cut or a full multiple. General Mills beat as cost savings offset volumes (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]) and the stock fell (Barron's, [Sep 23]); Paychex fell 9% after earnings (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 23]); Cintas beat and raised, yet edged lower (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]); KB Home fell after a beat as high rates and cautious buyers lie ahead (Barron's, [Sep 22]); Macy's fell 5% despite a beat and raised view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 11]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the market pays for volume and cash flow, not cost-cutting. With the 10Y at 5.14% and Wall Street flipping net negative on S&P 500 earnings after a 23-week upgrade streak (Tech Times, [Sep 23]), a beat without demand behind it — or with a softer margin — is sold.

Attention into EPS: Micron — Citi lifted its target to $1,300 on DRAM pricing and undersupply (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 23]) into its Sept. 30 report (International Business Times, [Sep 23]): pricing-led demand, the rewarded side, but much is priced. Nike — Barclays cut its target to $48 ahead of Q1 (TradingView, [Sep 23]), due Oct. 1 (Benzinga, [Sep 22]): a beat without demand would be sold. Costco — Q4 previews flag margin risks (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 23]): it needs traffic growth to be paid. Conagra — Q1 earnings expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]); McCormick — likely lower Q3 earnings (Benzinga, [Sep 23]): after General Mills' reaction, a cost-out beat will not be paid. Carnival — expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 22]), with fuel the cost line to watch.

Playbook: Own volume and pricing stories into prints (Micron, value restaurants, repair retail) and fade cost-out beats in packaged food and premium-multiple compounders. Risk: falling yields would re-rate the defensives and squeeze the fades.
