🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: a beat with a clean forward line, or one that clears a low bar. AeroVironment surged 21% on record annual revenue and an earnings beat (TIKR.com, [Jun 30]); General Mills jumped 9.9% after its beat and up to $3 billion in savings (TradingView, [Jul 2]); Playtech jumped 18% as H1 beat expectations (NEXT.io, [Jul 9]); TCS rose 4% after Q1 (CNBC TV18, [Jul 10]) as AI momentum fuelled a revenue beat (Reuters, [Jul 10]).

Punished: a beat carrying a cost caveat, slowing growth or a peaking AI cycle. Delta dipped despite beating Q2 expectations (TIKR.com, [Jul 13]) as sky-high fuel costs cut profit (WSJ, [Jul 10]); PepsiCo beat but fell anyway (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 9]) on higher commodity costs (Reuters, [Jul 9]); DMart fell 4% after Q1 on slowing metro growth (Moneycontrol.com, [Jul 13]); SK Hynix plunged after its Nasdaq debut amid diminishing earnings optimism (The Business Times, [Jul 13]).

Pattern: the margin line decides, not the beat: a fuel, input-cost or growth caveat outweighs the quarter and even a guidance beat, while low-bar names that deliver get paid. In AI hardware the beat is assumed; only a bigger forward number moves the stock.

Attention into EPS: US banks: five of the biggest report Tuesday (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 13]) with booming revenue from the SpaceX IPO and Iran-war volatility expected (CNBC, [Jul 13]) — the trading beat is consensus, so NII, costs and credit decide. ASML reports July 15 (富途牛牛, [Jul 10]) at €7.01 a share expected (TradingView, [Jul 12]); "the bar just got higher" (EBC Financial Group, [Jul 13]) — orders must rise. TSMC reports July 16 (The Motley Fool, [Jul 13]) after Q2 revenue beat guidance on a 68% June surge (Quartz, [Jul 13]): the beat is known, the guide is the trade. Netflix sits near a 52-week low into Q2 (AskTraders.com, [Jul 13]) — the TCS low-bar setup. UnitedHealth reports July 16 with the Street at $4.85 EPS (AlphaStreet, [Jul 13]); any cost caveat puts it on the PepsiCo side.

Playbook: own the low bar (Netflix) against the high bar (ASML, TSMC on an unchanged guide). Fade a bank beat that comes without higher NII guidance; the risk is a trading blowout or a TSMC guide-up big enough to clear the raised bar.
