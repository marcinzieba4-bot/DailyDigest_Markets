🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Top-line acceleration from real demand, with the guide raised on top. Ross Stores surged 9% after beating and lifting guidance (TIKR.com, [Aug 21]); Deere rallied 7% after quarterly profit climbed and it raised its full-year forecast (TIKR.com, [Aug 21]); CoreWeave popped 14% as revenue doubled on accelerating AI infrastructure demand (CNBC, [Aug 11]); Maersk rose as higher freight rates triggered a guidance raise (WSJ, [Aug 13]).

Punished: Beats built on one-offs, a slowing top line or a sky-high bar. Walmart tumbled 9% after its outlook disappointed (CNBC, [Aug 20]) as US comparable sales growth slowed to just 2.6% (The Motley Fool, [Aug 20]); Life360 tumbled 21% as a one-time tariff benefit clouded its Q2 beat (TIKR.com, [Aug 17]); Cisco slid 8% despite a beat and stronger-than-expected guidance (CNBC, [Aug 13]); Applied Materials fell on high expectations even as it forecast revenue above estimates (Reuters, [Aug 13]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes and a raise alone is not enough: the market pays for revenue accelerating on demand and sells beats built on refunds or delivered by AI hardware already priced for a raise, as Fabrinet's tumble despite an upbeat outlook showed (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 18]).

Attention into EPS: Nvidia — reports Wednesday (TradingView, [Aug 23]) after dropping after 5 of its last 6 reports, where 'a simple beat may not be enough' (Benzinga, [Aug 24]); Jefferies' call for a $3B revenue beat (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 20]) is the bar, so only an accelerating guide pays. Marvell — reports on August 27 (Moomoo, [Aug 21]) after jumping 6% on bullish Wall Street signals (TradingView, [Aug 20]): the run-up puts it on the Cisco side unless its $120 billion Google deal (Barron's, [Aug 24]) lifts the guide. Salesforce — rallied into earnings as Agentforce topped $1 billion (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 21]): an accelerating AI revenue line fits the rewarded profile, but the run-up raises the bar. Canada's big banks — on four-quarter beat streaks (Investing.com Canada, [Aug 21]) with no room for missteps after the run-up (CityNews Toronto, [Aug 23]): a beat is priced, so a soft credit line would be sold.

Playbook: Own demand-led accelerators that have not run into the print and fade crowded AI hardware on a plain beat: Dell is up 235% this year (MarketWatch, [Aug 24]) and buyers sold even after analysts raised targets (TIKR.com, [Aug 21]). In retail, prefer trade-down winners into Dollar General's report this week (Retail Brew, [Aug 24]). Risk: a strong Nvidia guide re-rates the whole AI complex and squeezes the fade.
