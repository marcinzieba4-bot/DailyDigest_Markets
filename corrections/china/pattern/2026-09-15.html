🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raise that shows demand accelerating, above all AI demand reaching revenue. Dell surged 9% after lifting its fiscal 2027 forecast on AI server strength (CNBC, [Sep 1]); Snowflake soared 23% on an AI-fueled beat and raised outlook (TradingView, [Sep 2]); Oracle surged over 6% as cloud revenue growth drove its beat (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]). Outside tech, Medtronic surged nearly 9% on a beat and raised guidance (TradingKey, [Sep 1]), and Signet soared 24% on a beat and outlook raise (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 9]) built on tariff refunds and cost discipline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 13]).

Punished: Beats or guides with a soft forward line, or cost and supply pressure. Broadcom slid as its AI chip forecast underwhelmed (Bloomberg.com, [Sep 2]); HPE raised forecasts but fell on supply concerns (Reuters, [Sep 2]); American Eagle stuck to its annual sales forecast and its shares slumped (Reuters, [Sep 9]); Lululemon tumbled 17% after another guidance cut despite topping Q2 estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]); Bank of America plunged on its CEO's sales and trading revenue guidance (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 14]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the forward line decides. The market pays for an AI-driven raise or a lifted profit outlook and sells anything where the guide, costs or supply cap the upside — even AI winners like Broadcom, and now the banks. An unchanged forecast after a beat is sold (American Eagle), while a raised outlook is paid even when tariff refunds help build it (Signet).

Attention into EPS: Lennar — reports on Sept. 16 with a 5.2% move priced (Investing.com, [Sep 9]), its valuation near tangible book putting margins in the spotlight (HousingWire, [Sep 14]): a beat with a weak margin guide is the punished profile. Trip.com — expected to post CNY5.99 a share (TradingView, [Sep 13]), with a preview calling a regulatory penalty a buying opportunity (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 13]): it pays only if the outlook looks past the penalty. Micron — its Sept. 30 earnings are seen as the key catalyst for AI memory (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]), yet the stock dropped 4.9% on a $50 billion earnings bar (TechStock², [Sep 11]): only a raised guide clears it.

Playbook: Own AI-demand names where guidance can still rise — Oracle's capex guidance lifting Dell and HPE (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]) shows the server chain has room — and fade beats that come without a higher forecast. Risk: with the 10Y at 5.00%, higher yields can compress every rewarded multiple at once.
