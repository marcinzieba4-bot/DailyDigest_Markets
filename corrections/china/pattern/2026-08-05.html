🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised forward line backed by visible demand. Palantir skyrocketed 29% on 'otherworldly' results (CNBC, [Aug 4]) after raising guidance again as revenue grew 93% (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 4]); Caterpillar's shares jumped 9% on a beat (Investing.com, [Aug 4]) as it lifted its 2026 sales growth target on data-centre demand (CNBC, [Aug 4]); Wayfair jumped 30% on its strongest US growth since 2020 (CNBC, [Aug 4]); Digital Realty surged 11% after crushing estimates and raising full-year guidance (TIKR.com, [Jul 26]).

Punished: A beat that comes with a softer next quarter or a bigger cost bill. AMD beat estimates and fell 8% (BeInCrypto, [Aug 4]) as its AI-powered revenue forecast failed to wow investors (Reuters, [Aug 4]); Meta sank 9% after its Q3 revenue forecast missed estimates (Investing.com, [Jul 29]); Google fell more than 6% on capex growth expectations despite an earnings beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]); STMicro dropped 17% after its Q3 revenue outlook missed estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]).

Pattern: The guide, not the beat, sets the price. A raised outlook tied to demand the market can see (AI software, data-centre power, high-income spending) is paid; a beat paired with rising capex or a merely in-line guide is sold, and the higher the bar into the print, the harder the fall.

Attention into EPS: Unity — rose 6% on two target raises before earnings (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 3]) and may move 11% on the release (Investing.com, [Jul 30]): with targets already lifted, only a raised guide lands on the rewarded side. Duolingo — BofA says Q2 bookings are likely to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 4]): the beat is the consensus, so the forward bookings line decides. Sandisk — the preview runs from 80% gross margin guidance to an 84% buy-side bar (Moomoo, [Aug 3]): hardware priced above its own guide sits on the punished side unless margins clear it. Take-Two — reports August 7 (AlphaStreet, [Aug 3]) with all eyes on GTA 6 updates (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 4]): a pure forward-line story, so launch timing sets the reaction.

Playbook: Own guide-raisers whose demand already shows in revenue — AI software and data-centre equipment — into their prints, and fade high-bar AI hardware where a beat is priced, with AMD's drop the template. Pair long data-centre industrials against memory names priced for margin perfection. Risk: a hawkish Fed with three officials dissenting for a hike compresses multiples for winners and losers alike.
