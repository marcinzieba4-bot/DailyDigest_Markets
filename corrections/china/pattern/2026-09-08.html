🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raise backed by AI demand that is visibly accelerating the top line. Dell surged 9% after lifting its fiscal 2027 forecast on AI server strength (CNBC, [Sep 1]); Snowflake spiked 22% on healthy results and AI coding momentum (CNBC, [Sep 2]); Salesforce rocketed 22% for its second-best day ever (CNBC, [Aug 27]); CrowdStrike soared 15% after a beat and raised outlook (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Aug 26]). Medtronic beat and raised as shares surged nearly 9% (TradingKey, [Sep 1]).

Punished: Beats where margins, supply or the next guide disappointed. Broadcom sank in extended trading despite its beat (CNBC, [Sep 2]) on a Q4 guide miss (Investing.com, [Sep 2]); MongoDB beat every estimate and raised guidance, then lost 13.6% (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 2]); Credo's revenue grew 115% and the stock still fell (Kavout, [Sep 2]); Palo Alto beat but margin concerns sent shares lower (SiliconANGLE, [Sep 1]); Lululemon tumbled 17% after another guidance cut despite a Q2 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the market pays for acceleration with margins intact — Snowflake's third straight quarter of acceleration (TIKR.com, [Sep 3]), Dell's record $95 billion of unfilled orders (Stock Titan, [Sep 1]). A raise that arrives with margin pressure, a supply bottleneck or a soft next-quarter guide is sold, however strong the AI headline.

Attention into EPS: Oracle — reports this week (TradingKey, [Sep 6]) and rallies into it on Morgan Stanley's big target (Stocktwits, [Sep 7]), but the cash flow test remains (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 7]) and the report could move the stock 11% (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 4]): a raise without cash-flow and margin support risks Broadcom's reaction. Adobe — reports on September 10 (Barchart.com, [Sep 6]) after naming a new CEO as AI fears persist (Investor's Business Daily, [Sep 4]), while Citi sees an 'achievable set-up' (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 1]): a low bar that acceleration would clear. Micron — its Q4 report carries "impossible expectations" (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 3]): priced like the hardware names sold on beats. RH — reports on September 10 and is expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]), but Lululemon shows the guide decides.

Playbook: Own software where AI shows up as accelerating revenue into prints; fade AI hardware and networking names priced for a raise where margins or supply are the open question (Micron, Oracle's margin guide). Risk: an Oracle backlog surprise re-rates the whole AI-infrastructure complex and squeezes the fade.
