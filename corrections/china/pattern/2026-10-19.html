🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with revenue and a raised outlook. UnitedHealth rose 4% on a beat and raised full-year outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Oct 13]); BlackRock jumped as earnings and investor inflows beat expectations (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 14]); Marston's jumped 18% as brokers lifted forecasts (Yahoo Finance UK, [Oct 13]); Nordex jumped 13% on a profit beat and margin-guidance raise (Yahoo Finance Singapore, [Oct 16]).

Punished: EPS beats with a revenue miss, a soft segment or a cut outlook. Citizens fell 5.4% on a revenue miss despite an earnings beat (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 16]); Schwab slid on a revenue miss despite its beat (Zacks Investment Research, [Oct 15]); J&J dropped 5% as MedTech weakness overshadowed a raised outlook (BigGo Finance, [Oct 13]); Albertsons plunged 22% on a guidance cut (scanx.trade, [Oct 18]); FMC plunged 35% on an outlook cut (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]); CXMT fell 7.8% despite a record 9-month profit (South China Morning Post, [Oct 18]).

Pattern: The beat is assumed — 83% of S&P 500 early reporters beat EPS estimates (TradingView, [Oct 17]) — so the revenue line and the guide decide. The market's big losers had one thing in common: guidance cuts (Finimize, [Oct 16]), and with UK profit warnings at a two-year high (The Independent, [Oct 18]), costs are the new risk. In crowded AI names even records are sold.

Attention into EPS: Meta — reports this week (CNBC, [Oct 18]) on Truist's Street-high $1,000 target (TipRanks, [Oct 13]) while Goldman flags higher costs (TradingView, [Oct 14]): a cost guide-up lands on the punished side. Tesla — bulls argue Musk proves he can deliver (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 17]): a beat without revenue behind it will not hold. Netflix — among the reports that must show something this week (BeInCrypto, [Oct 18]) as retail turns bearish on growth pressure (Stocktwits, [Oct 15]): a low bar, so a revenue beat could squeeze. GM — reports October 20 with the Street expecting $3.63 EPS (AlphaStreet, [Oct 16]): only a raised outlook pays. Microsoft — UBS lifts its target to $675 (Foreign Policy Journal, [Oct 18]): the rewarded Azure profile, but priced for it.

Playbook: Own revenue-led beat-and-raise set-ups into the print (Microsoft on Azure, managed care after UnitedHealth) and fade EPS-only beats with soft revenue or a cost-heavy guide (regional banks, airlines). Risk: with the 10Y at 5.15% and BofA openly discussing an October hike, rates can sink good prints, while a low bar lets a "less bad" print rally.
