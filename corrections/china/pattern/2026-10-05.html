🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A revenue beat backed by forward demand. Accenture had its best day ever (Barron's, [Oct 1]) on record big-ticket client bookings (Benzinga, [Oct 1]); CarMax jumped 6% as earnings hit $1.16 per share and buybacks resume (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 29]); Hapag-Lloyd rose as it lifted its 2026 earnings outlook again (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 29]); Norwegian Cruise Line rose as it expects Q3 results above its prior forecast (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 30]).

Punished: EPS beats built on margins or costs while revenue or volume misses. Nike beat by $0.04 but revenue fell short (Investing.com, [Oct 1]), and the stock sank to its lowest level since 2013 (Benzinga, [Oct 1]); Progress Software posted an EPS beat but a revenue miss as shares slid (ChartMill, [Sep 30]); Conagra topped estimates, but weak volumes dragged sales and the stock slipped (BigGo Finance, [Sep 30]); Paychex fell 9% after earnings (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 23]).

Pattern: The top line is the tell. With all 8 S&P 500 firms so far beating EPS estimates (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 3]), the beat is table stakes: the market pays for revenue and bookings that extend the outlook, and sells beats made of SG&A cuts or pricing. Even a beat-and-raise is sold when the bar is high — Jabil fell despite strong guidance (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Sep 30]).

Attention into EPS: PepsiCo and Delta report this week (Investopedia, [Oct 4]). PepsiCo — a preview says operational tailwinds might not be enough to appease investors (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 1]): pricing without volume is the Conagra profile, so a beat alone fades. Constellation Brands — set to report (The Globe and Mail, [Oct 4]) with Q2 earnings expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 29]): only steady depletions pay. Delta — TD Cowen cut its target ahead of Q3 (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 2]): fuel makes the guide, not the quarter, the trade. Big US banks — Oppenheimer sees a tough earnings season (Finimize, [Oct 2]): Jefferies showed record revenue can still be sold. TSMC — a preview says to load up before the print (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 4]): a revenue-led AI story, on the rewarded side if the outlook rises.

Playbook: Own revenue-led prints (TSMC, IT services after Accenture) and fade pricing-led staples into their reports (PepsiCo, Constellation Brands). Pair freight forwarders against fuel-exposed airlines into Delta. Risk: a volume surprise like Tesla's, whose shares jumped 5% as Q3 deliveries beat (Investing.com, [Oct 2]), squeezes the fades.
