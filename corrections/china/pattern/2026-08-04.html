🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised forward line and booked demand behind it. Palantir soared 12% on a blowout quarter (CNBC, [Aug 3]) and lifted its annual revenue forecast (Reuters, [Aug 3]); Snap jumped 8% on a beat and a strong sales forecast (CNBC, [Aug 3]); Amazon soared 15.2% as analysts raced to raise targets (TradingView, [Jul 31]); Lockheed Martin soared on a Q2 beat and raised full-year guidance (ChartMill, [Jul 23]).

Punished: A softer next quarter, or a beat without a forward lift. Meta sank 9% after its Q3 revenue forecast missed estimates (Investing.com, [Jul 29]); Qualcomm dropped nearly 8% after hours as a weak Q4 outlook overshadowed a revenue beat (TradingView, [Jul 29]); Apple dropped the most in 16 months on a disappointing outlook (AFR, [Aug 1]); Robinhood beat expectations and the stock fell anyway (The Motley Fool, [Aug 3]).

Pattern: The guide is the trade. Palantir went in told that beating estimates would not lift the stock without a guidance raise (Tech Times, [Aug 3]), raised its forecast and soared; Qualcomm beat on revenue but guided lower and was sold, and Meta's weak forecast sank it. The EPS beat itself is table stakes.

Attention into EPS: AMD — the test is whether AI chip revenue and Helios orders push the stock above $600 (TradingKey, [Aug 3]); with the bar that high, only a raised data-centre guide pays. SanDisk — 80% gross margin guidance against an 84% buy-side bar (Moomoo, [Aug 3]): a beat that merely meets guidance falls on the wrong side of the pattern. SoftBank — a low bar, Arm risk and a beat-and-drop pattern (Investing.com, [Aug 3]) make the forward commentary decisive. Unity Software — rose 6% on two target raises before earnings (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 3]), so expectations are already moving up.

Playbook: Own guide-raisers with booked demand into the print and fade beats that only meet guidance. Long memory makers, which soared after Amazon and Apple flagged soaring costs and a supply crunch (TradingView, [Jul 31]), against memory buyers carrying the cost: Qualcomm issued light earnings guidance during the memory crunch (CNBC, [Jul 29]). Risk: SanDisk's 84% bar shows memory is priced for perfection, so an in-line guide hits the long leg first.
