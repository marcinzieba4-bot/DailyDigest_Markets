🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a stronger forward line. Jabil shares rallied as earnings and revenue topped expectations (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 17]); Credo is up 21.2% after its earnings beat and DustPhotonics deal (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 13]); Cracker Barrel beat and raised its full-year outlook (Quartz, [Jun 10]), and the stock soared 30% (TradingView, [Jun 10]); Powerfleet jumped on a strong fiscal 2027 outlook despite an earnings miss (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 15]).

Punished: A beat with a softer guide, a thinner margin or a bigger funding bill. Oracle shares tumbled 11% on an increased capital raise and cash concerns (CNBC, [Jun 11]); Accenture tumbled 16% on a guidance cut (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 18]); Carnival fell as its Q3 outlook missed estimates despite an earnings beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 23]); Cerebras fell 10% after forecasting a shrinking margin (CNBC, [Jun 23]). Adobe raised its outlook and still fell on ARR concerns (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 12]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the next-quarter line decides. A raised outlook with AI or demand visibility is paid even on a miss; a softer guide, a margin squeeze or heavier AI spending is sold, and AI-threatened software is sold even on a raise.

Attention into EPS: Micron — reports post-market on June 24 (Moomoo, [Jun 18]), with Citi forecasting Q3 earnings growth of 1,092% year-over-year (Moomoo, [Jun 21]) and options pricing a 14% move (Benzinga, [Jun 23]): the beat is priced, so the HBM and capex guide decides — a raise lands on the Jabil side, a capex jump on the Oracle side. Indian IT — Accenture's weaker bookings and guidance cut could mean a softer FY27 for Indian IT firms (The Statesman, [Jun 20]): punished side unless the guide holds. Intuit — downgraded to Hold at Stifel on an expected guidance cut (Seeking Alpha, [Jun 18]): a sale even on a beat. Southwest — UBS raised its target to $61 on the earnings outlook (Investing.com, [Jun 23]): cheaper fuel is a forward-line tailwind.

Playbook: Own forward-line visibility and fade at-risk guides: long AI builders with booked demand, short IT services carrying the Accenture read-across. On Micron, three analysts doubled their price targets ahead of earnings (TipRanks, [Jun 22]), so do not chase the print; add on a guide-led dip. Risk: BofA and Deutsche Bank expect the Fed to raise rates in September (Reuters, [Jun 22]), compressing multiples on both legs.
