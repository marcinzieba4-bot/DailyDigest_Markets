🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat that comes with a raised guide on real demand. Apogee Enterprises soared 13% on an earnings beat and raised guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]); Neogen rose 8% on better-than-expected Q1 results and guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]); Accenture stock rallied after its earnings beat (CNBC, [Oct 1]); Carnival surged 12% on its Q3 results (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 29]).

Punished: Revenue misses, warnings and sales trims, beat or not. Nike's stock dropped 10% as results disappointed (Baystreet.ca, [Oct 2]); AngioDynamics slipped 4% despite beating estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 8]); Jabil fell 4% despite a beat and a higher outlook (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 30]); Levi Strauss and Constellation Brands slid on guidance (Stocktwits, [Oct 8]).

Pattern: Earnings beats are now the norm (CNBC, [Oct 7]), so the guide decides: a raise backed by volume, bookings or AI demand is bought, a cut or a sales trim is sold even on a beat. The one exception is the washed-out stock — PepsiCo, at its lowest since 2020 into the print (TradingView, [Oct 7]), rallied despite cutting its profit guidance (CNBC, [Oct 8]).

Attention into EPS: Delta Air Lines — reports October 9 with the Street expecting $1.83 EPS (AlphaStreet, [Oct 5]), estimates already cut below its own guidance: a low bar, so a firm fuel and capacity guide pays and a bare beat does not. JPMorgan — reports October 13 with $5.93 EPS expected (AlphaStreet, [Oct 8]) as banks come under pressure from spiking yields (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 7]): after Jefferies fell despite a beat (Barron's, [Sep 28]), only a better NII or fee outlook lifts it. AMD — Stifel expects a beat-and-raise (TipRanks, [Oct 5]) and BNP hiked its target 60% to $960 (24/7 Wall St., [Oct 6]): the raise is priced, the Jabil risk. ASML — a Q3 preview sees all the ingredients for a breakout (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 7]): orders and the guide must confirm.

Playbook: Own guidance-raisers with visible demand into their prints and fade beats that arrive with a sales trim (Levi-type). Into bank week, prefer fee and trading franchises over regionals. Risk: a PepsiCo-style low bar lets Delta or the banks rally on "less bad".
