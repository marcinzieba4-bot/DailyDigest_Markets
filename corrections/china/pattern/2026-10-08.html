🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat that comes with a raised outlook. Apogee soared 13% on an earnings beat and raised guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]); Neogen rose 8% on better-than-expected Q1 results and guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]); Applied Digital delivered a Q1 double beat and its shares rose (Benzinga, [Oct 7]); ZIM rose after raising its 2026 guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 7]); Accenture had its best day ever (Barron's, [Oct 1]).

Punished: Revenue misses and guidance cuts, punished hard. Nike sank as revenue missed estimates and it expects to cut jobs (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 2]); LG Electronics dropped as Q3 preliminary earnings missed (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]); Blaize plunged on a guidance cut and DOJ subpoenas (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]); RGP missed and its shares slid after hours (Investing.com, [Oct 7]).

Pattern: Earnings beats are now the norm (CNBC, [Oct 7]), and analysts raised S&P 500 EPS estimates for a second straight quarter (FactSet Insight, [Oct 7]) — so the beat is priced and the raise is the trade. A profit beat built on one-offs earns little: Levi beat profit forecasts and its shares only edged higher (Investing.com Australia, [Oct 7]) as tariff refunds, not sales, drove its guidance raise.

Attention into EPS: PepsiCo — the stock is at its lowest since 2020 and one expert sees 19% downside on a weak report (TradingView, [Oct 7]): a volume-led beat would squeeze the bears, a price-led one fades. Delta — premium tickets could help it beat Q3 estimates (TheStreet, [Oct 7]), but it faces a fuel-driven reality check (TradingView, [Oct 7]): the guide decides. JPMorgan — a preview asks whether strong banking momentum can continue (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 7]) as banks sit under pressure from spiking yields (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 7]): fee growth with stable credit is the rewarded profile. ASML — could signal a stronger-than-expected 2027 outlook, JP Morgan says (Yahoo Finance UK, [Oct 7]): a raised outlook is exactly what pays.

Playbook: Own outlook-raise candidates into their prints — ASML, and AI-linked names such as AMD after BNP Paribas hiked its target 60% to $960 (24/7 Wall St., [Oct 6]) — and fade the one-off beats (tariff refunds, cost cuts) in staples and apparel. Risk: AI excitement already spills into 2027 and could hit an 'air pocket' (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 7]), so size for the guide, not the beat.
