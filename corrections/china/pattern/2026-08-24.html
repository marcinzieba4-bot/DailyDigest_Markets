🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised forward line and volume behind it. Ross Stores surged 9% after beating estimates and lifting guidance (TIKR.com, [Aug 21]); Deere rallied 7% as profit climbed and it raised its full-year forecast (TIKR.com, [Aug 21]); Estée Lauder surged on its beat and raised margin guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 19]); CoreWeave popped 14% as revenue doubled on accelerating AI infrastructure demand (CNBC, [Aug 11]).

Punished: A beat with slowing volume, a soft guide or margin doubts. Walmart beat and raised guidance, yet the stock crashed (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 21]) as its sales growth slowed (Reuters, [Aug 21]); Cisco slid 8% despite an earnings beat and stronger-than-expected guidance (CNBC, [Aug 13]); Tapestry sank 15% despite a Q4 beat because of its FY27 guide (TIKR.com, [Aug 14]); Advance Auto Parts plunged 21% as a revenue miss overshadowed its earnings beat (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 20]).

Pattern: With high expectations and positive revisions priced in (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 21]), the beat is table stakes; the market pays for accelerating volume plus a raised guide and sells any top-line slowdown or margin doubt, even under a higher guide.

Attention into EPS: Nvidia — reports Wednesday with Marvell following Thursday (TradingView, [Aug 23]), and the warning is that the stock may plunge even if it beats (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 23]): after four straight quarters of post-earnings drops, gross margin and Rubin hold the key (TradingKey, [Aug 22]). Salesforce — climbed 5% before earnings as Agentforce topped $1 billion in annual sales (BigGo Finance, [Aug 21]): the run-up raises the bar for the guide. Marvell — jumped 6% as top Wall Street firms turned bullish (TradingView, [Aug 20]): expectations ran up into the print, so a beat alone is likely sold. Canada's big banks — a fund manager sees no room for missteps after the run-up in lenders' stocks (CityNews Toronto, [Aug 23]); Bank of Montreal is expected to post C$3.74 a share (TradingView, [Aug 21]): priced for a beat, so only a raised outlook is rewarded.

Playbook: Own beat-and-raise names with visible volume, and fade AI names that rallied into the print. Pair long Ross Stores against TJX, whose "self-inflicted" slowdown clouded its beat (Reuters, [Aug 19]). Risk: tariff refunds flatter this season's beats — Walmart is using $2.9 billion of them to lower prices (CBS News, [Aug 20]) — and will not repeat.
