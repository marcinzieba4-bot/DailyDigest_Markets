🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised outlook where AI shows up as demand. Salesforce jumped 18% on AI growth and an Anthropic investment gain (CNBC, [Aug 26]); CrowdStrike soared 15% after an earnings beat and a raised outlook (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Aug 26]); Okta jumped 19.4% on a beat (Investing.com UK, [Aug 26]) as AI agents spurred demand (WSJ, [Aug 26]); Nvidia jumped after an early dip as AI fever stayed unabated (Reuters, [Aug 26]).

Punished: A soft next-quarter line, shrinking volumes or AI as a threat. Zoom fell 6% as soft Q3 profit guidance overshadowed a double beat (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 26]); Intuit sank 12% as AI disruption fears spread from TurboTax to QuickBooks (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 26]); HP slid as a drop in PC shipments overshadowed strong revenue growth (Reuters, [Aug 26]); Dick's Sporting Goods fell 30% as it missed and cited a 'challenging' footwear market (CNBC, [Aug 25]).

Pattern: The forward line decides, and the AI split now runs inside software: AI that drives demand and lifts the guide is paid, AI that threatens the core product is sold, and even a beat with stronger guidance can fall when the bar is high, as Cisco's 8% slide showed (CNBC, [Aug 13]).

Attention into EPS: Marvell — reports on August 27 (Moomoo, [Aug 21]), with a $20.8 billion swing in value possible after earnings (Benzinga, [Aug 26]) and previews asking what Nvidia's results mean for it (Investing.com, [Aug 26]): after Nvidia's beat-and-raise, only a raised AI guide pays. Workday — Canaccord raised its target on the growth outlook (Investing.com, [Aug 25]): it falls on the rewarded side only if AI agents add to demand, on Intuit's side if they look like disruption. Palo Alto Networks — reports on September 01 (Moomoo, [Aug 26]) after JPMorgan raised its target (TIKR.com, [Aug 26]): CrowdStrike's 15% jump sets the template, so a raised outlook is the bar. Hormel — faces flat revenue expectations (BigGo Finance, [Aug 26]): with no top-line growth priced, a soft guide would be sold.

Playbook: Own software whose AI line is driving demand and raised guides into the remaining prints, and fade volume-driven hardware such as PC makers on a plain beat. Risk: momentum and leveraged trading keep software stocks on a roller coaster (Reuters, [Aug 26]), so a crowded rebound can reverse hard on one soft guide.
