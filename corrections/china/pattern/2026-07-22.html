🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Beats with a raised outlook or record volumes. GM rose after it boosted full-year guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 21]), UnitedHealth surged as earnings momentum returned (Reuters, [Jul 16]), BlackRock rallied 7% after its Q2 beat (TIKR.com, [Jul 16]) and AMC rose 27% as record box office sales beat estimates (TIKR.com, [Jul 21]).

Punished: Beats with a softer forward line or a heavier spending bill. Danaher beat estimates yet dropped the most since 1991 (Barron's, [Jul 21]); Halliburton beat but tumbled on a tepid revenue forecast (Reuters, [Jul 21]); Netflix sank 9% as a weak outlook overshadowed its beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 17]). TSMC beat and raised its outlook, but higher capex weighed on sentiment (S&P Global, [Jul 21]).

Pattern: The beat itself is worth little; the guide gets paid. A raised outlook or record volume lifts the stock; a tepid next quarter or a bigger capex bill sinks even a clean beat, AI names included.

Attention into EPS: Google and Tesla kick off Big Tech earnings (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 21]). Alphabet — capex, not cloud growth, could decide the market's next AI trade (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 21]), with a Gemini delay and spending worries looming (Reuters, [Jul 21]): a capex lift without faster cloud is the TSMC-style sale. Tesla — the bar is sky-high (CNBC, [Jul 20]) on strong deliveries but murky profits (Electrek, [Jul 21]): a delivery beat without margin is the Netflix profile. Texas Instruments — Wall Street's high bars rest on Q2 margins and guidance (Seeking Alpha, [Jul 21]) into July 22 earnings (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 15]); only a raised recovery guide gets paid. Intel — the Street expects $0.21 EPS on July 23 (AlphaStreet, [Jul 20]) after a stunning target hike (Barchart.com, [Jul 21]), with sizzling server demand facing chilling PC headwinds (BigGo Finance, [Jul 21]); the margin guide decides it.

Playbook: Own the guide-raisers whose estimates still lag, and fade capex-heavy AI into the print. Vertiv has raised guidance twice this year and the Street is still behind (TIKR.com, [Jul 21]); pair it against a mega-cap spender held into its report. Risk: an Alphabet cloud acceleration would justify the capex, break the sell-the-beat pattern and squeeze the short leg.
