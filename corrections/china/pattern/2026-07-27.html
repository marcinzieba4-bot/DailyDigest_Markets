🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised guide, and companies that collect AI spending as revenue. Digital Realty surged 11% after crushing estimates and raising full-year guidance (TIKR.com, [Jul 26]); SLB gained 11% on a strong beat and a return to revenue growth (TIKR.com, [Jul 26]); Medpace soared 15% after beating and raising guidance (TIKR.com, [Jul 23]); Verizon rose on an earnings beat and raised guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 24]).

Punished: The AI spenders and a weaker next quarter, even on big beats. Alphabet fell more than 6% on capex growth expectations despite its beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]); Intel fell nearly 8% despite a big Q2 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 24]); Tesla plummeted 14% (Forbes, [Jul 23]) after a miss as AI spending soared (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]); STMicro dropped 17% after its Q3 revenue outlook missed (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]).

Pattern: Big Tech reports into a market in revolt over AI spending (Bloomberg.com, [Jul 26]). Spending more is sold even on a beat; collecting that spending with a raised guide, or beating and raising outside the AI loop, is paid.

Attention into EPS: Amazon, Apple, Meta and Microsoft report this week, alongside the Fed decision (Investopedia, [Jul 26]). Meta — Deutsche Bank expects strong earnings as AI tools boost advertiser returns (TIKR.com, [Jul 26]): visible AI ad revenue is the rewarded side, a capex jump the punished one. Microsoft — soaring capex versus a record-low valuation (Moomoo, [Jul 23]): a capex lift without faster AI revenue is the Alphabet sale. Amazon — fell 4% on a Senate China probe and AI-spending jitters ahead of earnings (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 23]): AWS revenue is the rewarded side, capex the punished one. Apple — reports on July 30 (Moomoo, [Jul 24]): the least capex-heavy giant, closest to the rewarded side. Coca-Cola — reports July 28 (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 25]): outside the AI loop, a beat pays only with a raised guide.

Playbook: Own the collectors, fade the spenders into their prints: a data-centre landlord raising guidance (Digital Realty) against a hyperscaler held into its report. Risk: a spender that shows AI revenue keeping pace with capex would squeeze the short leg, and any capex cut would hit the collectors first.
