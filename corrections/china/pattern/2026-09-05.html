🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raise backed by AI demand that is visibly accelerating the top line. Dell surged 9% after lifting its fiscal 2027 forecast on AI server strength (CNBC, [Sep 1]); Snowflake spiked 22% on healthy results and AI coding momentum (CNBC, [Sep 2]); Salesforce rocketed 22% for its second-best day ever (CNBC, [Aug 27]); CrowdStrike soared 15% after a beat and raised outlook (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Aug 26]). Outside tech, Medtronic beat across the board and raised guidance as shares surged nearly 9% (TradingKey, [Sep 1]).

Punished: Beats where margins, supply or the next guide disappointed. Broadcom sank in extended trading despite its beat (CNBC, [Sep 2]) on a Q4 guide miss (Investing.com, [Sep 2]); MongoDB beat every estimate and raised guidance, then lost 13.6% (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 2]); Credo's revenue grew 115% and the stock still fell 18% (Kavout, [Sep 2]); Palo Alto beat but margin concerns sent shares lower (SiliconANGLE, [Sep 1]); Lululemon tumbled 17% after another guidance cut despite a Q2 beat (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Sep 3]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the market pays for acceleration with margins intact — Snowflake's third straight quarter of acceleration (TIKR.com, [Sep 3]), Dell's record $95 billion of unfilled orders (Stock Titan, [Sep 1]). A raise that arrives with margin pressure, a supply bottleneck or a soft next-quarter guide is sold, however strong the AI headline.

Attention into EPS: Oracle — reports post-market on September 10 (Moomoo, [Sep 4]); Morgan Stanley lifted its target to $210 on the margin outlook (Investing.com, [Sep 4]) and the report could move the stock 11% (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 4]): a raise without margin support risks Broadcom's reaction. Adobe — named a new CEO as earnings approach and AI fears persist (Investor's Business Daily, [Sep 4]), while Citi sees an 'achievable set-up' (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 1]): a low bar, so evidence of acceleration would be paid. Micron — its Q4 report carries "impossible expectations" (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 3]): priced like the hardware names sold on beats. Casey's — reports post-market on September 08 (Moomoo, [Sep 2]); Couche-Tard's beat on fuel margins (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 2]) is the template. RH — reports on September 10 and is expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]); in premium retail Lululemon shows the guide decides.

Playbook: Own software where AI shows up as accelerating revenue into prints; fade AI hardware and networking names priced for a raise where margins or supply are the open question (Micron, Oracle's margin guide). Risk: an Oracle backlog surprise re-rates the whole AI-infrastructure complex and squeezes the fade.
