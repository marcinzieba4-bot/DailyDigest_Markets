🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Accelerating revenue with AI in the top line and a raised guide. Snowflake soared 23% on an AI-fueled beat and raised outlook (TradingView, [Sep 2]); Dell surged 9% after lifting its fiscal 2027 forecast on AI server strength (CNBC, [Sep 1]); Medtronic beat across the board and raised guidance as its shares surged nearly 9% (TradingKey, [Sep 1]); Salesforce posted its strongest one-day gain since 2020 (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 28]).

Punished: Beats with any caveat on margin, cash flow, supply or the forward line. Broadcom slid 5% despite its beat (The Tech Buzz, [Sep 2]); MongoDB beat every estimate and raised guidance, then lost 13.6% (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 2]); HPE fell 5% as supply constraints overshadowed raised guidance (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 3]); NetApp raised guidance but tumbled on a free cash flow decline (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 2]); Lululemon tumbled 17% after another guidance cut (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes and even a raise is not enough: the market pays for revenue acceleration where AI shows up as demand — Snowflake jumped on a third straight quarter of acceleration (TIKR.com, [Sep 3]) — and sells crowded AI names at the first margin, cash-flow or supply friction. Where AI looks like a threat, the beat is sold outright: UiPath fell 17% last week despite beating estimates (TIKR.com, [Sep 7]).

Attention into EPS: Oracle — due to report (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 8]) after rallying 5% as Morgan Stanley lifted its target (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 8]); OCI could grow 115% (Moomoo, [Sep 8]), but the cash flow test remains (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 7]): a run-up plus a cash-flow question is the HPE / NetApp profile, so only an accelerating guide pays. Adobe — reports September 10 (Barchart.com, [Sep 6]) under a new CEO as AI fears persist (Investor's Business Daily, [Sep 4]), though Citi sees an 'achievable set-up' (Stocktwits, [Sep 2]): it sits on UiPath's side unless AI shows up as revenue. Micron — its Sept. 30 report is seen as the key catalyst (BigGo Finance, [Sep 7]), but the report faces 'impossible expectations' (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 3]): priced-for-perfection hardware.

Playbook: Own software where AI is revenue into the print and fade AI infrastructure that has run into its report — Oracle after its rally, Micron into Sept. 30 — unless the guide accelerates. In retail, buy sales-led beats like Five Below's (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]) over refund- or cost-driven ones. Risk: an Oracle blowout re-rates the AI-infrastructure complex and squeezes the fade.
