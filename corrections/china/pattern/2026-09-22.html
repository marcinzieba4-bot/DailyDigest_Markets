🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Beats and raises backed by visible demand. AeroVironment jumped after an earnings beat (Barron's, [Sep 10]) as its backlog hit a record $1.5B (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]); Signet rose 24% on a Q2 beat and a raised FY'27 view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 10]); Next rose after a guidance lift (WSJ, [Sep 17]); Vicor rose after stronger Q3 guidance (Benzinga, [Sep 21]). Oracle's capex guidance sent HPE and Dell up 11% on AI server demand (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]).

Punished: Beats with a soft next-quarter guide, higher costs or a cut. Braze fell 11% as third-quarter guidance missed (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]); Navan beat and raised, but shares tumbled on cost concerns (Reuters, [Sep 9]); Macy's fell 5% despite a beat and raised view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 11]); Casey's slid despite a beat as inside-sales growth disappointed (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]). Cost warnings were hit hardest: J.B. Hunt fell 9% (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]), Fluence plunged on a guidance cut (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 16]) and Volkswagen sank 7.5% (NDTV Profit, [Sep 18]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the forward guide and the cost line decide the reaction. With the Fed hiking and WTI at $91.41, the market pays for demand that shows up in backlog and guidance, and sells any beat that arrives with rising costs or a softer next quarter.

Attention into EPS: Micron — the preview sets $31 per share vs $3 a year ago (Benzinga, [Sep 17]) into its Sept. 30 report (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 15]): the rewarded demand-led profile, but only a raised guide clears that bar. General Mills — RBC sees a miss on inflation and shipment timing (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 21]) while others expect a beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]): a cost-driven miss is the punished profile. KB Home — Q3 earnings expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 15]) after Lennar's miss and delivery cut (WSJ, [Sep 16]). Nike — faces target cuts before fiscal Q1 (SGB Media, [Sep 21]) after UBS warned results could trigger a selloff (BigGo Finance, [Sep 17]). Costco and BlackBerry — in the limelight (Breakingthenews.net, [Sep 21]); BlackBerry jumped as an analyst sees potential revenue and EPS beats (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 21]), so the beat is partly priced.

Playbook: Own demand-led prints with visible backlog (Micron) and fade beats exposed to fuel, labour and input costs — transport, packaged food, Nike. Risk: an oil reversal or a softer Fed path relieves the cost side and squeezes the fades.
