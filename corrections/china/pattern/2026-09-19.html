🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised outlook backed by visible demand — AI capex, defence orders, overseas sales. Oracle's cloud infrastructure revenue more than doubled (CNBC, [Sep 10]) and its capex guidance sent Dell and Hewlett Packard Enterprise up 11% (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]); Exosens jumped as it raised 2026 guidance again on a faster production ramp-up (Investing.com, [Sep 17]); Next rose after a guidance lift (WSJ, [Sep 17]); Salesforce rose after raising its fiscal 2030 revenue forecast above estimates (TIKR.com, [Sep 17]).

Punished: Beats with a soft or costly forward line, and cost-driven warnings. Adobe slid 4% on conservative quarterly guidance (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]); Braze fell 11% as Q3 earnings guidance missed estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]); Chewy sank 11% as a free cash flow miss overshadowed a raised outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]); Volkswagen sank 7.5% as it slashed its 2026 margin target (NDTV Profit, [Sep 18]); J.B. Hunt slumped 13% on a surprise profit warning (AskTraders.com, [Sep 18]).

Pattern: The guide is the print. A raise carried by demand is paid for; a beat with a soft guide, weak cash flow or a cost shock — fuel, China, write-downs — is sold, and warnings are punished hard. Demand is the tiebreaker: Barratt Redrow rose despite a lower completions target because demand held firm (Reuters, [Sep 16]).

Attention into EPS: Micron — previews put EPS at $31 a share vs $3 a year ago (Benzinga, [Sep 17]) into its Sept. 30 report (The Motley Fool, [Sep 15]): the beat is priced, so only a raised guide pays. Nike — UBS slashed its profit forecast and warned first-quarter results could trigger a selloff (BigGo Finance, [Sep 17]), with shares at a 12-year low (investingLive, [Sep 16]): the bar is low, but a weak guide still sits on the punished side. General Mills — reports on September 23 with the Street expecting $0.72 EPS (AlphaStreet, [Sep 18]) as GLP-1 shifts weigh on food companies (The New York Times, [Sep 18]): a beat on cost savings without volume growth would sit on the punished side. Darden — prepares to report Q1 (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 18]) as restaurant stocks slide on weaker August dining traffic (TradingView, [Sep 16]): growth from price rather than traffic sits on the punished side.

Playbook: Own raised-guide stories with demand behind them — the AI-capex chain, defence suppliers — and fade consumer and auto names exposed to cost shocks into their prints. Risk: a 5% 10Y compresses multiples regardless, and a Micron stumble against the $31 bar would hit the whole AI-hardware complex.
