🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised guide resting on visible demand. Dell surged 9% after lifting its fiscal 2027 forecast on AI server strength (CNBC, [Sep 1]); Medtronic beat across the board and raised full-year guidance, with shares up nearly 9% (TradingKey, [Sep 1]); Salesforce rocketed 22% (CNBC, [Aug 27]) after raising FY27 guidance by $300M as Agentforce ARR hit $1.5B (BigGo Finance, [Aug 26]); Nvidia added more than $400 billion in value after blowout earnings (CNBC, [Aug 27]).

Punished: Beats that come with thinner margins or a soft forward line. Palo Alto Networks beat, but margin concerns sent the shares lower (SiliconANGLE, [Sep 1]); Credo fell as margin pressure overshadowed its beat (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 1]); Walmart's stock tumbled 9% after its outlook disappointed (CNBC, [Aug 20]); Marvell raised annual forecasts, but its shares fell (Reuters, [Aug 27]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the market pays for a raise built on accelerating demand — AI servers, heart devices, software agents — and sells a beat that costs margin or comes with a soft outlook, even in AI. Big earnings failed to answer the big AI questions (Axios, [Sep 1]), so each AI print is judged on margin and guide.

Attention into EPS: Broadcom — reports on September 2 (The Motley Fool, [Aug 31]), with a preview warning a revenue beat may not be enough (Moomoo, [Aug 27]): like Marvell and Credo it will be judged on margin, so only a raised AI guide with stable margins pays. Oracle — poised to beat estimates again (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 1]), but the balance sheet is the issue (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 1]): a beat that leans on debt-funded capex sits on the punished side. Snowflake — analysts raised targets ahead of Q2, citing solid demand (TipRanks, [Aug 26]), as the AI trade moves into software (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 31]): demand-led software with a raise is the rewarded profile. Lululemon — expected to beat earnings estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 27]): a beat without a raised outlook would sit with Walmart.

Playbook: Own demand-led software into its prints (Snowflake) and stay selective in AI hardware: Dell shows a raise still pays, but Broadcom has margin questions to clear, so prefer owning it after the print rather than before. Risk: a blowout Broadcom guide re-rates the AI hardware complex as Nvidia's did.
