🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with revenue and a raised outlook. UnitedHealth rose 4% on a beat and raised full-year outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Oct 13]); BlackRock jumped as earnings and investor inflows beat expectations (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 14]); Cleveland-Cliffs jumped 8% on a profit beat and an EBITDA forecast above $4 billion (International Business Times Australia, [Oct 19]); Marston's jumped 18% as brokers lifted forecasts (Yahoo Finance UK, [Oct 13]); Nordex jumped 13% on a profit beat and margin-guidance raise (Yahoo Finance Singapore, [Oct 16]).

Punished: EPS beats with a revenue miss, a soft segment or a cut outlook. Citizens fell 5.4% on a revenue miss despite an earnings beat (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 16]); Schwab's stock slumped after mixed results (marketscreener.com, [Oct 15]); J&J dropped 5% as MedTech weakness overshadowed a raised outlook (BigGo Finance, [Oct 13]); FMC plunged 35% on an outlook cut (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]); CXMT fell nearly 8% despite its profit turnaround (CNBC, [Oct 19]).

Pattern: The beat is assumed — 83% of S&P 500 early reporters beat EPS estimates (TradingView, [Oct 17]) — so the revenue line and the guide decide. The market's big losers had one thing in common: guidance cuts (Finimize, [Oct 16]), and with UK profit warnings at a two-year high (The Guardian, [Oct 19]), costs are the new risk. In crowded AI names even records are sold.

Attention into EPS: Meta — reports this week (CNBC, [Oct 18]) facing a $145 billion question after the tech rout (investingLive, [Oct 19]): a bigger capex guide lands on the punished side even with strong ads. Tesla — previews ask whether it can reverse recent EPS and revenue misses (Benzinga, [Oct 19]): a beat without revenue will not hold. Netflix — "not the time to chill" (The Hollywood Reporter, [Oct 19]) as retail turns bearish on growth pressure (Stocktwits, [Oct 15]): a low bar, so a revenue beat could squeeze. GM — reports October 20 (AlphaStreet, [Oct 16]) with guidance in focus (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 19]): only a raised outlook pays. Microsoft — UBS lifts its target to $675 (Foreign Policy Journal, [Oct 18]): the rewarded Azure profile, but priced for it.

Playbook: Own revenue-led beat-and-raise set-ups into the print (Microsoft on Azure, managed care after UnitedHealth) and fade EPS-only beats with soft revenue or a cost-heavy guide (regional banks, Meta if capex jumps). Risk: with the 10Y at 5.15% and the Fed holding with a hike bias, rates can sink good prints, while a low bar lets a "less bad" print rally.
