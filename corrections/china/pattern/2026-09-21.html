🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Beats backed by visible demand — backlog, orders, a raised guide. AeroVironment jumped after an earnings beat (Barron's, [Sep 10]) as its backlog hit a record $1.5B (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]); Signet rose 24% on a Q2 beat and a raised FY'27 view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 10]); Sunbelt Rentals rose 3% on higher fiscal 2027 guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]); Next rose after a guidance lift (WSJ, [Sep 17]). Oracle's capex guidance sent HPE and Dell up 11% on AI server demand (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]).

Punished: Beats with a soft next-quarter guide, higher costs or a cut. Braze fell 11% as third-quarter guidance missed (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]); Navan beat and raised its forecast, but shares tumbled on cost concerns (Reuters, [Sep 9]); Macy's fell 5% despite a beat and raised view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 11]); Casey's slid despite a beat as inside-sales growth disappointed (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]). Cost warnings were hit hardest: J.B. Hunt fell 9% (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]), Fluence plunged on a guidance cut (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 16]) and Volkswagen sank 7.5% (NDTV Profit, [Sep 18]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the forward guide and the cost line decide the reaction. With the Fed hiking and WTI at 91.44, the market pays for demand that shows up in backlog and guidance, and sells any beat that comes with rising costs or a softer next quarter.

Attention into EPS: Micron — the preview sets $31 per share vs $3 a year ago (Benzinga, [Sep 17]) into its Sept. 30 report (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 15]): the rewarded demand-led profile, but only a raised guide clears that bar. General Mills — expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]), with $0.72 EPS expected on September 23 (AlphaStreet, [Sep 18]): without volume, only a firm guide pays. KB Home — Q3 earnings expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 15]) after Lennar's miss and delivery cut (WSJ, [Sep 16]): a guidance cut would be sold. Nike — UBS slashed its profit forecast and warned Q1 could trigger a selloff (BigGo Finance, [Sep 17]): the punished side. Costco and Darden — in the earnings spotlight (Investor's Business Daily, [Sep 18]); Oppenheimer sees Costco's core Q4 earnings missing (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]).

Playbook: Own demand-led prints with visible backlog (Micron) and fade beats exposed to fuel and labour costs — transport, restaurants, Nike. Risk: an oil reversal or a softer Fed path relieves the cost side and squeezes the fades.
