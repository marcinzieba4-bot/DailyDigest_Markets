🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with revenue and a raised outlook. UnitedHealth rose 4% on a beat and raised full-year outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Oct 13]); BlackRock jumped as earnings and investor inflows beat expectations (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 14]); Marston's jumped 18% as brokers lifted forecasts (Yahoo Finance UK, [Oct 13]); Nordex jumped 13% on a profit beat and margin-guidance raise (Yahoo Finance Singapore, [Oct 16]).

Punished: EPS beats with a revenue miss, a soft segment or a cut outlook. Citizens fell 5.4% on a revenue miss despite an earnings beat (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 16]); Schwab slid on a revenue miss despite its beat (Zacks Investment Research, [Oct 15]); J&J dropped 5% as MedTech weakness overshadowed a raised outlook (BigGo Finance, [Oct 13]); Bank of America fell 5% on a miss and a compliance penalty (The Motley Fool, [Oct 14]); FMC plunged 35% on an outlook cut (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]); TSMC's shares dropped despite a strong quarter (SiliconANGLE, [Oct 15]).

Pattern: The beat is assumed — 83% of S&P 500 early reporters beat EPS estimates (TradingView, [Oct 17]) — so the revenue line and the guide decide. The market's big losers had one thing in common: guidance cuts (Finimize, [Oct 16]). EPS-only beats are sold about 5%, and in crowded AI names great results won't be good enough (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 13]).

Attention into EPS: Microsoft — target hikes as TD Cowen sees "higher than normal" Azure beat potential (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]) before its October 28 report (ad-hoc-news.de, [Oct 16]): the rewarded profile, but priced for it. Meta — Truist's Street-high $1,000 target (TipRanks, [Oct 13]) against Goldman flagging higher costs (TradingView, [Oct 14]): a cost guide-up puts it on the punished side. Tesla — leads a big earnings week with Meta (Investor's Business Daily, [Oct 16]), with bulls arguing Musk proves he can deliver (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 17]): a beat without revenue behind it will not hold. GM — reports October 20 with the Street expecting $3.63 EPS (AlphaStreet, [Oct 16]): only a raised outlook pays. United Airlines — vulnerable to downgrades (Schaeffer's Investment Research, [Oct 15]) after Delta cut earnings guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 12]): the guidance-cut profile.

Playbook: Own revenue-led beat-and-raise set-ups into the print (Microsoft on Azure, managed care after UnitedHealth) and fade EPS-only beats with soft revenue or a fuel-cost guide (regional banks, United). Risk: with the 10Y at 5.10% and a hike back on the table, rates can sink good prints, while a low bar lets a "less bad" print rally.
