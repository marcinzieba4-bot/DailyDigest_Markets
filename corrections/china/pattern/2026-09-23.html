🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A profit beat or raised guide backed by visible demand or margin. AeroVironment jumped 4% after a Q1 beat (TIKR.com, [Sep 10]) as its backlog hit a record $1.5B (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]); Signet rose 24% on a Q2 beat and a raised FY'27 view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 10]); Vicor rose after stronger Q3 guidance (Benzinga, [Sep 21]); AutoZone climbed 6% as a profit beat offset a revenue miss (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 22]); Kingfisher's shares surged on a raised profit guide (AskTraders.com, [Sep 22]).

Punished: Beats with a softer forward line or margin, and any cost warning. Braze fell 11% as third-quarter guidance missed (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]); Navan beat and raised, but shares tumbled on cost concerns (Reuters, [Sep 9]); Macy's fell 5% despite a beat and raised view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 11]); KB Home fell after a beat (Barron's, [Sep 22]) as it cut its margin outlook (WSJ, [Sep 22]). J.B. Hunt dropped 9% on a profit warning (Investing.com, [Sep 16]) and Volkswagen sank 7.5% (NDTV Profit, [Sep 18]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the margin and the forward guide decide the reaction. With the 10Y at 5.10% and WTI at 91.49, the market pays for profit growth that survives costs — AutoZone was bought despite a revenue miss — and sells beats that arrive with a margin cut or a softer next quarter.

Attention into EPS: Micron — earnings set for a 10x jump (Benzinga, [Sep 22]), with $31 per share vs $3 a year ago in the preview (Benzinga, [Sep 17]): the rewarded profile, but only a raised guide clears that bar. General Mills — RBC sees a miss on inflation and shipment timing (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 21]) against hopes of a beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]): a cost-driven miss is the punished side. Nike — reports Oct. 1 after beating last quarter's EPS estimate by 54% (Benzinga, [Sep 22]), but faces target cuts (SGB Media, [Sep 21]). Costco — dropped to $895 ahead of earnings (TradingKey, [Sep 21]): expectations are reset lower. Carnival — expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 22]), but fuel is its cost line.

Playbook: Own margin-protected demand into prints (Micron, repair retail) and fade beats exposed to fuel, labour and input costs (packaged food, Nike, cruise lines). Risk: an oil reversal relieves the cost side and squeezes the fades.
