🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat that comes with a raised outlook on real demand. Carnival's beat and record 2027 bookings rallied the cruise stocks (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 29]) as record demand lifted yields and it raised 2026 EPS (BigGo Finance, [Sep 29]); CarMax jumped 6% as earnings hit $1.16 per share and buybacks resume (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 29]); Cracker Barrel rose 10% after its Q4 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]) as traffic trends improve (Barron's, [Sep 23]); Vicor jumped 20% in a day after a guidance raise (TIKR.com, [Sep 23]).

Punished: Beats without growth, cost warnings — and now AI raises that were already priced. General Mills beat on pricing and mix (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]), yet the stock fell (Quartz, [Sep 23]); Paychex fell 9% after earnings (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 23]); J.B. Hunt slumped 13% on a surprise profit warning (AskTraders.com, [Sep 18]); Jabil fell 4% despite its beat and higher fiscal 2027 outlook (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 30]); Jefferies fell despite record banking revenue (Barron's, [Sep 28]).

Pattern: The beat itself earns nothing — the biggest earnings beats are becoming the worst trades on Wall Street (Invezz, [Sep 26]). The market pays for a raised outlook backed by bookings, traffic or freight rates, sells beats built on pricing or cost cuts, and in AI hardware the raise must outrun what is already in the price, as Jabil showed.

Attention into EPS: Nike — reports post-market on October 01 (Moomoo, [Sep 25]) with too many headwinds setting a lower bar for Q1 (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 30]): a low bar helps, but a cost-led beat on weak demand is the General Mills profile; only a better outlook pays. PepsiCo — Deutsche Bank flags concerns over its North America recovery (TradingView, [Sep 28]): pricing without volume sits on the punished side. McCormick — Q3 earnings are expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 24]), the same staples profile. US banks — Morgan Stanley sees a quiet earnings season (Finimize, [Sep 25]) and Bank of America reports on Oct 14 (Pluang, [Sep 30]): after Jefferies, record revenue alone will not lift the stocks.

Playbook: Own demand-led beat-and-raise stories (cruise, used cars, freight) and fade pricing-led staples into their prints (PepsiCo, McCormick). Into Nike, size for the guide, not the beat. Risk: a Carnival-style demand surprise at Nike squeezes the fade.
