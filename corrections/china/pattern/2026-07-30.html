🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised guide with demand behind it, AI spenders included. Microsoft jumped 8% as it boosted capital spending plans, citing demand (CNBC, [Jul 29]); Bloom Energy surged 10% after raising its full-year revenue and profit forecast (TIKR.com, [Jul 29]); Ford jumped 6% on a Q2 beat and raised 2026 guidance (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 29]); Chipotle jumped as it hiked its same-store sales forecast (CNBC, [Jul 29]).

Punished: A weaker revenue guide, or results that only match AI-charged expectations. Meta sank 9% after its Q3 revenue forecast missed estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 29]); KLA fell as raised guidance failed to impress (Benzinga, [Jul 29]); SK Hynix's record profit missed forecasts and the shares slumped 10% (Reuters, [Jul 29]); Procter & Gamble beat but the stock fell (Barron's, [Jul 29]) as it forecast slower sales growth as costs weigh (Reuters, [Jul 29]).

Pattern: Capex is not the problem; capex without revenue is. Microsoft raised spending with demand behind it and was paid, while Meta's weak guide and dwindling free cash flow (CNBC, [Jul 29]) were sold, as Alphabet's capex lift was (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]). The forward revenue line, not the beat, sets the move.

Attention into EPS: Amazon — Alphabet just tied its $200 billion capex guidance, with the question whether Amazon raises the bar on July 30 (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]); AWS accelerates with an Anthropic boost, but free cash flow is the top concern (Moomoo, [Jul 28]): AWS growth behind the capex is the Microsoft outcome, a soft guide the Meta one. Apple — Goldman raised its target to $370 (AppleInsider, [Jul 28]) into its Q3 report (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 29]): little capex, so the iPhone guide decides. AMD — reports on August 04 (Moomoo, [Jul 29]); Wedbush says data-centre revenue is growing faster than expected (Seeking Alpha, [Jul 27]), but AI hardware has been sold on beats unless the guide surprises. AbbVie — a preview makes the case before July 31 (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 28]): outside AI, a raised guide is what pays.

Playbook: Trade the guide, not the print: own guide-raisers into reports (Seagate beat with an outlook that surprised to the upside (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 29])) and fade AI hardware where record numbers only match expectations. Risk: the same spending story moved Microsoft and Meta in opposite directions, so a single guidance line can flip either leg.
