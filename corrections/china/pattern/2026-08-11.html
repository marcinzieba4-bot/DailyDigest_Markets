🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised forward line and demand behind it. Palantir skyrocketed 29% after 'otherworldly' results (CNBC, [Aug 4]); Atlassian jumped 37.4% (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 6]); Shopify jumped 25.1% in a strong quarter (StockStory, [Aug 5]); Embraer surged on record second-quarter revenue and raised guidance (Reuters, [Aug 10]); Airbnb soared 9% on strong guidance for the third quarter (CNBC, [Aug 6]).

Punished: A beat with a softer guide, or spending that outruns revenue. AMD's shares sank on lofty expectations even as data-centre sales doubled (The Straits Times, [Aug 5]); Datadog had its worst day in more than 6 years on a beat (Barron's, [Aug 6]); SpaceX stock dived despite an earnings beat (Investor's Business Daily, [Aug 5]); Monday.com fell as its guidance trumped the beat (Investor's Business Daily, [Aug 10]); Meta sank 9% after its Q3 revenue forecast missed (Investing.com, [Jul 29]).

Pattern: Earnings beats have eased concerns over the record rally (Yahoo Finance UK, [Aug 9]), and earnings growth is no longer driven just by tech (MarketWatch, [Aug 10]). At the stock level, though, the beat is table stakes: the market pays for a raised guide backed by booked demand and sells any beat that comes with a softer outlook or capex without matching revenue.

Attention into EPS: Cisco — previewed into its Q4 report (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 10]) after raising its AI order target to $9 billion (The Motley Fool, [Aug 7]): booked AI orders sit on the rewarded side, so a higher guide should be paid. CoreWeave — previewed into Q2 (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 10]): an AI spender whose capex leads revenue, the SpaceX profile, so a beat without a margin path risks a sell-off. Coherent — previewed on whether AI optics can keep the momentum going (Moomoo, [Aug 10]), with Lumentum also previewed (Moomoo, [Aug 9]): demand-side optics, paid only on a raised outlook. Walmart — reports this week, with Walmart, Target and Costco consolidating ahead of earnings (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 10]); after PepsiCo's guidance cut on a cautious US consumer (investingLive, [Aug 10]), margin guidance matters more than the sales beat.

Playbook: Own guide-raisers with demand in the numbers (Palantir, Atlassian, Embraer) and fade beats with a soft outlook or capex outrunning revenue. Into prints, prefer booked-order stories such as Cisco over AI spenders such as CoreWeave. Risk: July CPI this week; a hot print hardens the September-hike path and hits the long-duration winners hardest.
