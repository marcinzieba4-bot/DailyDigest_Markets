🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A better forward line carried by demand or traffic. Vicor jumped 20% in a day after a guidance raise (TIKR.com, [Sep 23]); Next shares rose after a guidance lift (WSJ, [Sep 17]) on booming overseas sales (Investors' Chronicle, [Sep 17]); Kingfisher surged as it raised profit guidance with Screwfix offsetting weak B&Q (AskTraders.com, [Sep 22]); Cracker Barrel rose 10% after a Q4 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]) as traffic trends improved (Barron's, [Sep 23]).

Punished: Beats without growth, and margin or cost warnings. General Mills beat as cost savings offset volumes (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]), yet the stock fell (Quartz, [Sep 23]); Paychex beat EPS estimates but slow growth weighed on the shares (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 26]), down 9% after earnings (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 23]); KB Home fell after a beat (Barron's, [Sep 22]) as it cut its margin outlook (WSJ, [Sep 22]); Jefferies fell despite an earnings beat and record banking revenue (Barron's, [Sep 28]); J.B. Hunt fell 9% after warning of a quarterly earnings decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]).

Pattern: The beat itself earns nothing — the biggest earnings beats are becoming the worst trades on Wall Street (Invezz, [Sep 26]). What pays is a raised guide or improving traffic; a beat built on cost savings, slow growth or a margin cut is sold, and with the 10Y at 5.28% even record revenue at Jefferies was not enough.

Attention into EPS: Micron — reports post-market on September 30 (Moomoo, [Sep 25]); Baird hiked its target on a "surge in agentic AI demand" as the stock slipped (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 28]), and Benzinga puts 80% odds on beating every estimate (Benzinga, [Sep 25]): the beat is priced, only a raised guide pays, and Michael Burry boosted Micron shorts (NAI500, [Sep 23]). Nike — reports post-market on October 01 (Moomoo, [Sep 25]) with Q1 earnings expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 24]); the only bullish aspect is the extreme bearishness (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 28]): a cost-led beat on weak demand sits with General Mills. Carnival — expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 22]) and due pre-market on September 29 (Investing.com, [Sep 28]); fuel is the guide risk. Accenture — reports Q4 on October 1 needing to break its slide (TIKR.com, [Sep 25]): only a better outlook pays.

Playbook: Own the guide, not the beat: fade cost-led consumer beats into the print (Nike; McCormick, whose Q3 earnings are expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 24])) and trim Micron rather than chase the 80% beat odds. Risk: a demand-led raise like Vicor's squeezes both fades.
