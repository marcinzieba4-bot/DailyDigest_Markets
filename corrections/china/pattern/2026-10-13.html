🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised outlook and real demand behind it. Accenture had its best day ever (Barron's, [Oct 1]) as AI demand boosted results (WSJ, [Oct 1]); Aritzia surged most in 18 months on earnings and a better outlook (Bloomberg.com, [Oct 9]); TCS rose 4% as Q2 profit beat and AI revenue crossed $3.1 Bn (TradingView, [Oct 9]); Neogen rose on a beat and raised guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]); Tesco jumped after raising profit guidance (Invezz, [Oct 12]).

Punished: Guidance cuts, whatever the top line did. Delta reported record revenue but cut earnings guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 12]), and the stock fell (Benzinga, [Oct 9]); Nike sank to its lowest level since 2013 after Q1 (Benzinga, [Oct 1]); Levi Strauss and Constellation slid on guidance (Stocktwits, [Oct 8]); AngioDynamics fell 4.3% despite a Q1 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 8]); Jabil fell despite a beat and strong guidance (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Sep 30]).

Pattern: Earnings beats are now the norm (CNBC, [Oct 7]), so the beat decides nothing — the guide does. Raised outlooks backed by AI revenue, bookings or traffic are bought; cuts are sold even on record revenue. The exception is a washed-out valuation: PepsiCo rallied despite cutting profit guidance (CNBC, [Oct 8]) from the lowest P/E in five years (TIKR.com, [Oct 8]).

Attention into EPS: Big banks — Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley have earnings momentum into this week's reports (CNBC, [Oct 12]), while bank stocks are mired in a correction as yields rise (Yahoo Finance Singapore, [Oct 12]): a low bar like PepsiCo's, so a firm outlook pays and JPMorgan's miss today sets the risk. ASML — reports October 14 (24/7 Wall St., [Oct 12]), with the question whether 55% sales growth can still surprise (Moomoo, [Oct 11]): only the guide pays. TSMC — reports October 15 (Moomoo, [Oct 11]) after quarterly revenue jumped 51% (Bloomberg.com, [Oct 7]): revenue is known, so the outlook is the trade. United Airlines — Delta's cut puts United's next results in focus (Live and Let's Fly, [Oct 9]): fuel puts it on the punished side.

Playbook: Own AI-revenue beat-and-raise stories into their prints but size for the guide (TSMC, ASML); fade fuel-exposed airlines after Delta; treat the banks as a "less bad" setup, bought on a firm outlook rather than the beat. Risk: Dimon's warning that economic forces shift under the surface turns the bank bounce into a credit scare.
