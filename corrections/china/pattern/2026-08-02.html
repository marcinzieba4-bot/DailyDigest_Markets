🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised forward line and booked demand behind it, even when spending rises. Amazon soared 12% (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 31]) after lifting investment plans on strong cloud sales (Reuters, [Jul 31]); Microsoft beat with $90B in revenue and jumped 9% (KOMO, [Jul 29]); Ford jumped 4% on a Q2 beat and a guidance upgrade (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 28]); Lockheed Martin jumped on an earnings beat and raised guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]).

Punished: A softer next quarter, or costs without a revenue lift, even on a beat. Meta sank 9% after its Q3 revenue forecast missed estimates (Investing.com, [Jul 29]); Apple dropped the most in 16 months on a disappointing outlook (AFR, [Aug 1]); Reddit sank 11% on 'choppy' search referrals even as its results blew past estimates (CNBC, [Jul 30]); STMicro dropped 17% after its Q3 revenue outlook missed (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]).

Pattern: AI isn't a catch-all trade this earnings season (Bloomberg.com, [Aug 1]): the forward guide splits winners from losers. Spending backed by cloud or pricing demand is paid, a weaker next quarter is sold, and the EPS beat is only table stakes. UPS beat and raised its outlook but dropped on a Q3 revenue warning (Tech Times, [Jul 28]), guiding third-quarter domestic revenue flat (CNBC, [Jul 28]).

Attention into EPS: AMD — reports this week (TradingKey, [Aug 1]), with Wedbush seeing its 2026 and 2027 data-centre revenue growing faster than expected (Seeking Alpha, [Jul 27]) and the stock surging with chip stocks (CNBC, [Jul 30]); KLA fell despite a Q4 beat on high expectations (Seeking Alpha, [Jul 29]), so only the data-centre guide moves it. Palantir — also reporting this week, with a big beat and $200 seen as realistic (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 27]); that prices a beat, so only a raised guide clears the bar. Novo Nordisk — reports on August 05 (Moomoo, [Jul 30]) after a trial miss (Simply Wall Street, [Jul 31]); healthcare has sold guidance cuts at AbbVie and Alnylam, so the outlook decides. Valero — a strong refiner whose stock needs proof (Seeking Alpha, [Jul 29]); with Chevron beating on refining margins while Exxon missed (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 31]), energy prints are judged on margins, not the oil price.

Playbook: Own guide-raisers with booked demand into the print; fade beats where spending rises without revenue. Pair long memory makers, which soared after Amazon and Apple flagged soaring costs and a supply crunch (TradingView, [Jul 31]), against memory buyers carrying the cost: Qualcomm issued light earnings guidance during the memory crunch (CNBC, [Jul 29]). Risk: a memory price spike that destroys device demand hits both legs.
