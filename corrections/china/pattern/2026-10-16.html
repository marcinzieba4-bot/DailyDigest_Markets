🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Results that change the forward line — inflows, pricing or a raise on a new driver. BlackRock rose 5% on strong results (TradingView, [Oct 14]) as it drew $205 billion in client funds (Reuters, [Oct 14]); Ericsson jumped after a Q3 beat (Quartz, [Oct 15]) with price hikes as AI drives component costs (BigGo Finance, [Oct 15]); Marsh McLennan jumped on an earnings and revenue beat (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]); Brunello Cucinelli rose on a raised 2026 guide (Investing.com, [Oct 15]); UnitedHealth rose 4% on a beat and raise (24/7 Wall St., [Oct 13]).

Punished: Record beats that were already priced, and any cut. TSMC's stock dropped despite record profit (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]); Schwab fell despite beating estimates (Investor's Business Daily, [Oct 15]); Morgan Stanley beat, yet its shares fell (Investing.com, [Oct 14]); FMC plunged 35% as it cut its Q3 outlook (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]); Nestlé India plunged despite 36% profit growth on inflation concerns (Moneycontrol.com, [Oct 15]).

Pattern: Great earnings are being punished right now (24/7 Wall St., [Oct 15]) — in a punishing mood (Barron's, [Oct 14]) the beat is the entry fee. The market pays only when the forward line moves (new inflows, pricing power, a raise without a weak segment) and treats any cut as capitulation.

Attention into EPS: Microsoft — target hikes ahead of earnings, with TD Cowen seeing "higher than normal" Azure beat potential (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]): the beat is priced, so only a stronger Azure guide pays. Meta — reports Oct. 21 (The Motley Fool, [Oct 14]); Citi raised its target to $895 (TradingView, [Oct 15]), but Goldman flags higher AI costs (TradingView, [Oct 14]): the cost line decides it. Procter & Gamble — Q1 earnings expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]): a low bar, but Nestlé India shows inflation talk is sold. Elevance — will lower premiums weigh on results (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 15]): after UnitedHealth, a raise is the bar.

Playbook: Own forward-line stories (asset gatherers with inflows, managed care, firms with pricing power) and fade record-but-priced beats in semis and banks into their prints; size Microsoft for the Azure guide, not the beat. Risk: the Nasdaq's big rebound as a bond rally offset tech softness (Yahoo Finance UK, [Oct 15]) shows how fast punished quality can snap back.
