🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Demand visible in volume or traffic, with a raised guide. Vicor jumped 20% in a day after a guidance raise (TIKR.com, [Sep 23]); Cracker Barrel rose 10% after a Q4 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]) as traffic trends improved (Barron's, [Sep 23]); Next's shares rose after a guidance lift (WSJ, [Sep 17]); Exosens jumped as it raised 2026 guidance again on a faster production ramp-up (Investing.com, [Sep 17]).

Punished: Beats built on cost cuts, and anything exposed to fuel, labour or a weak top line. General Mills beat as cost savings offset volumes (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]), yet the stock fell (Quartz, [Sep 23]); JB Hunt fell 9% after warning of a quarterly earnings decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]) on fuel and driver costs (Simply Wall Street, [Sep 16]); Scholastic slumped 12% after its fiscal Q1 loss missed estimates (BigGo Finance, [Sep 25]); HelloFresh sank 14% after a second profit warning (Investing.com, [Sep 25]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes — H.B. Fuller topped its profit forecast yet dipped (The Motley Fool, [Sep 24]), and BlackBerry's beat-and-raise drew a 'relatively muted' response (Financial Post, [Sep 25]). The market pays for volume that proves demand and discounts EPS made by savings or refunds: Costco's beat shrinks to $0.07 without a tariff refund (TechStock², [Sep 25]). The exception is trade-down, with AutoZone's best day in more than 2 years (Barron's, [Sep 22]).

Attention into EPS: Micron — reports post-market on September 30 (Moomoo, [Sep 25]) with 80% odds of beating every estimate (Benzinga, [Sep 25]) after a 279% rally (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 25]): volume plus price is the rewarded profile, but a beat is priced in, so only a raised guide pays. Nike — reports post-market on October 01 (Moomoo, [Sep 25]) with Q1 earnings expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 24]): without volume it sits on the punished side. Accenture — reports Q4 on October 1 and needs to show enough to break its slide (TIKR.com, [Sep 25]): a low bar, so bookings growth would be rewarded. PepsiCo — earnings are imminent (Benzinga, [Sep 25]) after a surprising U-turn on snack prices (TradingView, [Sep 24]): a branded maker on the General Mills side unless volume shows.

Playbook: Own volume stories into the print and fade staples whose beats are cost- or refund-made. Risk: Wall Street flipped net negative on S&P 500 earnings after a 23-week upgrade streak (Tech Times, [Sep 23]) and the 10Y is at 5.18%, so multiple compression can sink even a volume beat — Micron's most of all.
