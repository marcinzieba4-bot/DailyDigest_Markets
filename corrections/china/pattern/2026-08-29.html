🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised guide with demand visible in the top line, above all a new AI revenue line. Salesforce rocketed 22% (CNBC, [Aug 27]) after raising FY27 guidance by $300M as Agentforce ARR hit $1.5B (BigGo Finance, [Aug 26]); CrowdStrike soared 15% after a beat and raised outlook (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Aug 26]); Nvidia broke its post-earnings curse with a $442 billion day (WSJ, [Aug 27]); Abercrombie jumped as it raised forecasts on robust apparel demand (Reuters, [Aug 26]).

Punished: Beats with a soft forward line, refund-flattered profits or a crowded hardware story. Intuit tumbled despite its beat as weak 2027 guidance weighed (TradingView, [Aug 25]); Zoom fell 6% as soft Q3 profit guidance overshadowed a double beat (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 26]); Kohl's fell 6% despite raised guidance and a $150M tariff refund (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 26]); Marvell raised annual forecasts, but its shares fell (Reuters, [Aug 27]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the market pays for revenue that is accelerating on demand, and Nvidia and Salesforce upended two bear narratives (CNBC, [Aug 27]) because AI showed up as growth, not as a threat. A softer next quarter, a bottom line built on refunds or AI hardware already priced for a raise is sold.

Attention into EPS: Broadcom — reports post-market on September 02 (Moomoo, [Aug 27]), with a preview seeing continued margin contraction (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 25]): outside Nvidia, AI hardware has been sold on beats, so only a raised guide with stable margins pays. Dell — analysts say earnings could propel it higher after a 235% run (MarketWatch, [Aug 24]), yet it is sliding ahead of Q2 (Benzinga, [Aug 28]): the run-up puts it on Marvell's side unless the guide rises. Snowflake — analysts raised targets ahead of Q2, citing solid demand (TipRanks, [Aug 26]): a demand-backed data-software name sits on Salesforce's side. Lululemon — UBS warns it may cut its annual outlook on weak US and China sales (TradingView, [Aug 25]) after it dropped 4% as Dick's tumbled (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 25]): a weak top line is the punished profile.

Playbook: Own demand-led software into its prints (Snowflake) and fade AI hardware that has run into the report (Dell, Broadcom). In retail, prefer demand-led apparel over refund-led beats. Risk: a Broadcom blowout like Nvidia's re-rates the whole hardware complex and squeezes the fade.
