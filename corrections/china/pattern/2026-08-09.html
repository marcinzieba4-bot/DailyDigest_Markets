🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised forward line and AI revenue already in the numbers. Palantir soared 12% on a blowout quarter (CNBC, [Aug 3]) and raised guidance again (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 4]); Shopify jumped 25.1% in a strong quarter (StockStory, [Aug 5]); Atlassian jumped 37.4% (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 6]); Caterpillar beat forecasts and its shares jumped 9% (Investing.com, [Aug 4]); Microsoft jumped 8% as it boosted capital spending plans, citing demand (CNBC, [Jul 29]).

Punished: A beat with a softer guide, or spending that outruns revenue. AMD slumped 9% despite its Q2 beat (TradingKey, [Aug 5]); Datadog had its worst day in more than 6 years on a beat (Barron's, [Aug 6]); HubSpot dropped 20.2% despite a sales beat (StockStory, [Aug 5]); SpaceX hit a new all-time low as AI capex jumped (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 5]); Meta sank 9% after its Q3 revenue forecast missed (Investing.com, [Jul 29]).

Pattern: S&P 500 earnings are booming, yet stocks aren't showing it (Barron's, [Aug 7]): with the index posting its biggest earnings beat ever (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 8]), the beat is table stakes. The market pays for a raised guide backed by booked demand and sells any beat that arrives with slower growth or capex without matching revenue.

Attention into EPS: Nvidia — reports in August (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 4]), and AMD's results point to massive AI demand (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 5]); but AMD was sold on a beat, so only a raised data-centre guide clears the bar. Cisco — reports this week, and it raised its AI order target to $9 billion (The Motley Fool, [Aug 7]): a booked-orders story on the rewarded side, so a higher guide should be paid. Commonwealth Bank of Australia — reports FY26 on Wednesday (The Motley Fool Australia, [Aug 8]); DBS hit a record high on a raised forecast (Reuters, [Aug 6]), but HSBC is starting to look priced for perfection (Reuters, [Aug 4]), so a beat without an upgrade risks a fade. Jack in the Box — previewed into Q3 (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 7]) after McDonald's US sales missed (New York Post, [Aug 4]): mass-market fast food sits on the punished side.

Playbook: Own guide-raisers whose AI revenue is visible in the quarter (Palantir, Atlassian, Caterpillar) and fade beats with a softer outlook or capex that outruns revenue. Pair long AI-power industrials against mass-market consumer names. Risk: CPI data due this week; a hot print would hit the long-duration winners hardest.
