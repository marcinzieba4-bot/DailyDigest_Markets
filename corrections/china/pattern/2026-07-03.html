🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Forward visibility — a raise, a backlog, scarce supply or a credible cost plan. Micron beat and its shares spiked 15% on a record $41.5 billion quarter (Forbes, [Jun 24]); AeroVironment soared 28% as its backlog grew to $1.2 billion (CNBC, [Jun 29]); Progress Software is skyrocketing (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 1]) on AI demand and a raised outlook (TradingView, [Jul 1]); General Mills jumped 9.9% on a beat and up to $3 billion of savings (TradingView, [Jul 2]); Rivian jumped on raised 2026 delivery guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 2]).

Punished: Beats paired with weaker guidance or rising costs — or a bar set too high. Carnival dipped 5% on weaker Q3 guidance despite the beat (TIKR.com, [Jun 24]); FedEx dipped 6% despite its beat (TIKR.com, [Jun 24]); Darden fell as Olive Garden weakness clouded its beat (Bloomberg.com, [Jun 25]); Cerebras fell 10% on a shrinking-margin forecast (CNBC, [Jun 23]); KPIT Tech tumbled 16% as weak Europe demand triggered a profit warning (Outlook Business, [Jul 1]). Tesla sank 7% despite a strong deliveries report (CNBC, [Jul 2]).

Pattern: The forward line beats the print, measured against the bar: where the bar was low, a beat plus a credible plan is bought — Nike jumped on its beat (The Motley Fool, [Jul 1]) despite a weak outlook (Reuters, [Jul 1]) — while where expectations had run up (Tesla) even a blowout is sold.

Attention into EPS: Indian IT — TCS reports Q1 on July 9 with its 24% operating-margin guidance in focus (Sahi, [Jun 30]), and the sector braces for a cold Q1 as guidance cuts loom (The Economic Times, [Jul 2]): the punished side, but with a low bar. Banks open the season (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 2]); Wells Fargo has lagged and earnings can change that (Barron's, [Jul 1]) — capital return after a clean stress test (Global Finance Magazine, [Jun 25]) is the forward line. Delta is near highs with oil below $70 into its report (Investor's Business Daily, [Jul 2]): fuel relief is priced, Tesla-style, so it needs a raise. Alphabet (Barchart.com, [Jul 2]) and Intel (Barchart.com, [Jul 2]) previews frame AI spend against revenue.

Playbook: Pair long AI hardware and backlog names against Indian IT services into TCS, where guidance-cut risk is the story; fade Delta into the print. Risk: Nifty IT already hit a five-year low (NDTV Profit, [Jul 1]), so a guide that merely holds could spark a Nike-style relief rally.
