🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Demand visible in capacity, revenue or sales — and its knock-on to suppliers. Oracle surged over 6% as cloud revenue growth drove an earnings beat (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]); HPE and Dell surged 11% as Oracle's capex guidance lifted AI server demand (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]); Copart jumped 6% after a revenue beat and a $2 billion marketplace acquisition (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]); RH's Q2 sales topped estimates and the stock soared (StockStory, [Sep 10]).

Punished: Beats carried by one-offs or followed by a conservative forward line. Macy's fell 5% despite a Q2 beat and a raised view (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 11]) as tariff refunds masked margin pressure (Benzinga, [Sep 11]); American Eagle sank 13% as a $161M tariff refund flattered its beat (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 10]); Adobe slid 4% on conservative quarterly guidance (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]); Cooper crashed 15% after a guidance cut (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]).

Pattern: The market prices the source of the beat and the next quarter, not the EPS print. Visible demand — AI capacity, revenue and sales beats — is paid and spreads to suppliers, as Oracle's capex lifted Dell and HPE; EPS padded by tariff refunds, a conservative guide or weak cash flow is sold even with a raise attached, as Chewy's 11% drop on a free cash flow miss showed (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]).

Attention into EPS: Micron — its Q4 might change the game (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 11]), with its Sept. 30 earnings seen as the key catalyst for AI memory stocks (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]): the most direct read-across from Oracle's capex, on the rewarded side if the guide confirms demand. Lennar — Zacks' Bear of the Day (Zacks Investment Research, [Sep 11]), expected to report lower Q3 earnings and revenue (AlphaStreet, [Sep 11]) ahead of its Sept. 16 report (Investing.com, [Sep 9]): no visible demand, so the guide is the bar, and with expectations this low a stable outlook could squeeze. Costco — its re-rating is "just getting started", per an FQ4 preview (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 11]): rewarded only if comps, not one-offs, lead. Darden — UBS sees it maintaining its outlook despite Olive Garden weakness (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 11]): a held guide over weak traffic is the Macy's profile.

Playbook: Own the AI-capacity chain into its prints — Micron into Sept. 30 after Oracle's capex lifted the server makers — and fade consumer beats that lean on refunds or cost cuts. Risk: a Fed hike next week would de-rate the AI chain on multiples even if demand holds.
