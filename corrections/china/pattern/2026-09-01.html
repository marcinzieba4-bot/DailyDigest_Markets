🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised guide with demand visible in the top line, above all a new AI revenue line. Salesforce rocketed 22% (CNBC, [Aug 27]) after raising FY27 guidance by $300M as Agentforce ARR hit $1.5B (BigGo Finance, [Aug 26]); CrowdStrike soared 15% after a beat and raised outlook (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Aug 26]); Nvidia added more than $400 billion in value after blowout earnings (CNBC, [Aug 27]); Deere rallied 7% as management raised its full-year forecast (TIKR.com, [Aug 21]), with sales guidance bolstered by data center construction (Manufacturing Dive, [Aug 21]).

Punished: Beats with a soft forward line or a slowing top line. Walmart's stock tumbled 9% after its outlook disappointed (CNBC, [Aug 20]) on its weakest sales growth in over six years (WSJ, [Aug 20]); Intuit is tumbling despite its beat as weak 2027 guidance weighs (TradingView, [Aug 25]); Kohl's fell 6% despite raised guidance and a $150M tariff refund (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 26]) as sales kept sliding (Quartz, [Aug 26]); Marvell raised annual forecasts, but its shares fell (Reuters, [Aug 27]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the market pays for revenue accelerating on demand, and Nvidia and Salesforce upended two bear narratives (CNBC, [Aug 27]) because AI showed up as growth, not as a threat. A raised guide built on tariff refunds or margins without sales growth is sold, and so is AI hardware already priced for a raise.

Attention into EPS: Broadcom — its results are the next hurdle for the rally (Reuters, [Aug 30]), and the preview question is whether AI growth can outrun financing fears (Moomoo, [Aug 30]): outside Nvidia, AI hardware has been sold on beats, so only a raised guide pays. Dell — goes in on soaring AI server demand (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 31]) after a massive 2026 rally (Moomoo, [Aug 31]): it sits on Marvell's side unless the guide rises. Palo Alto Networks — reports post-market on September 01 (Moomoo, [Aug 26]) with Q4 revenue expected to grow 32% (TradingKey, [Aug 31]): security software is on CrowdStrike's side, so a raise should pay. Lululemon — expected to beat (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 27]), but UBS expects an outlook cut on weak US and China sales (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 25]): a beat with a cut is the punished profile.

Playbook: Own demand-led software into its prints (Palo Alto) and fade AI hardware that has run into its report (Dell, Broadcom). In retail, prefer sales-led names over refund stories. Risk: a Broadcom blowout like Nvidia's re-rates the whole hardware complex and squeezes the fade.
