🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised outlook backed by visible demand — AI capex, defence, overseas sales. Oracle's cloud infrastructure revenue more than doubled (CNBC, [Sep 10]) and its capex guidance sent Dell and Hewlett Packard Enterprise up 11% (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]); Planet Labs surged on a beat driven by doubling defense revenue (Barron's, [Sep 4]); Exosens jumped as it raised 2026 guidance again on a faster production ramp-up (Investing.com, [Sep 17]); Next rose after a guidance lift (WSJ, [Sep 17]) on booming overseas sales (Investors' Chronicle, [Sep 17]).

Punished: Beats paired with a soft guide, cash burn or rising costs. Adobe slid 4% on conservative quarterly guidance (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]); Lululemon fell 17% after a guidance cut despite a Q2 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 4]); Chewy sank 11% as a free cash flow miss overshadowed a raised outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]); J.B. Hunt fell 9% after warning of an earnings decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]).

Pattern: The raise matters more than the beat, and only when demand rather than refunds or cost cuts carries it — even a cut is forgiven when demand holds, as Barratt Redrow rose on firm demand despite a lower completions target (Reuters, [Sep 16]). Even AI hardware gets no pass on a beat alone — Broadcom crushed earnings and the stock fell anyway (Barchart.com, [Sep 4]).

Attention into EPS: Micron — previews put EPS at $31 a share vs $3 a year ago (Benzinga, [Sep 17]) into its Sept. 30 report (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 15]): the beat is priced, so only a raised guide pays, as Broadcom showed. Nike — UBS slashed its profit forecast and warned first-quarter results could trigger a selloff (BigGo Finance, [Sep 17]), with shares at a 12-year low (investingLive, [Sep 16]): Lululemon's side of the pattern unless the outlook stabilises. Costco — UBS reiterated its Buy ahead of results (Investing.com, [Sep 17]) while Oppenheimer sees core Q4 earnings missing (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 16]): a split bar where clean traffic growth would be rewarded. Darden — reports next week with Wall Street expecting earnings growth (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 17]) as restaurant stocks slide on weaker August dining traffic (TradingView, [Sep 16]): growth from price rather than traffic sits on the punished side.

Playbook: Own raised-guide stories with real demand behind them — the AI-capex chain and defence — and fade consumer beats that arrive without a raise, with Nike the test case. Risk: a 5% 10Y compresses multiples regardless, and a Micron stumble against the $31 bar would hit the whole AI-hardware complex.
