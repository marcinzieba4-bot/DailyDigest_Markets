🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat that comes with a raised outlook. Apogee soared 13% on an earnings beat and raised guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]); Neogen rose 8% on better-than-expected Q1 results and guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]); Penguin Solutions popped on a Q4 beat and strong guidance (Benzinga, [Oct 6]); Carnival surged 12% on its Q3 results (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 29]); Accenture had its best day ever (Barron's, [Oct 1]).

Punished: Beats on margin without the top line, and guidance cuts. Nike's Q1 beat on margin gain, but the stock fell on a revenue miss (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 2]); Progress Software posted an EPS beat but a revenue miss as shares slid (ChartMill, [Sep 30]); Paychex fell 9% after earnings (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 23]); Blaize plunged as a guidance cut and DOJ subpoenas prompted downgrades (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 6]).

Pattern: The guide is the trade. All 8 S&P 500 firms so far beat EPS estimates (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 3]) and the AI boom puts US earnings on course for a third quarter of 25% growth (Financial Times, [Oct 6]), so the beat is assumed. Stocks move on revenue and a raised outlook; a margin-led beat with a revenue miss is sold.

Attention into EPS: PepsiCo — reports this week (Investopedia, [Oct 4]); one preview says the turnaround is accelerating (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 6]): it needs volume, not price, to land on the rewarded side. Delta — reports October 9 with the Street expecting $1.83 EPS (AlphaStreet, [Oct 5]), but airline profits won't follow fares higher (CNBC, [Oct 5]): only a raised outlook pays. Samsung — a record profit is expected, with the margin outlook in focus (Investing.com, [Oct 6]): the guide, not the record, moves it. Applied Digital — short sellers target the stock into its Q1 report (Schaeffer's Investment Research, [Oct 6]): a raise squeezes them. Citigroup — reports Q3 on October 13 after a yield-driven bank selloff (TIKR.com, [Oct 5]): growth durability, not the beat, is the test.

Playbook: Own beat-and-raise candidates into their prints — AI-linked names such as AMD, where Stifel expects a beat-and-raise (TipRanks, [Oct 5]) — and fade margin-led staples and airlines whose guide is capped by costs. Risk: with the S&P at records and the beat priced in, a single weak AI guide can flip the trade.
