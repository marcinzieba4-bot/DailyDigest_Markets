🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A top-line beat with demand behind it. Accenture had its best day ever (Barron's, [Oct 1]) as AI demand boosted results (WSJ, [Oct 1]); Carnival surged 12% on its Q3 results (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 29]); McCormick rose 5% as an acquisition boosted sales (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 1]); Norwegian Cruise Line rose as it expects Q3 above its prior forecast (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 30]); Cracker Barrel rose 10% after its Q4 beat (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]) as traffic trends improve (Barron's, [Sep 23]).

Punished: EPS beats with a soft top line or guide, and cost warnings. Nike beat by $0.04 but revenue fell short (Investing.com, [Oct 1]) and shares dropped on disappointing sales (CNBC, [Oct 1]); Progress Software slid on a revenue miss despite its EPS beat (ChartMill, [Sep 30]); General Mills beat on pricing and mix (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 23]), yet the stock fell (Quartz, [Sep 23]); J.B. Hunt slumped 13% on a surprise profit warning (AskTraders.com, [Sep 18]); Jabil fell 4% despite a beat and higher outlook (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 30]).

Pattern: The top line and the guide decide, not the EPS beat: revenue beats backed by bookings, traffic or AI demand are bought; EPS beats with revenue misses or outlook cuts are sold. A raise already in the price, as at Jabil, earns nothing.

Attention into EPS: PepsiCo — operational tailwinds might not be enough to appease investors (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 1]) after Deutsche Bank flagged its North America recovery (TradingView, [Sep 28]): McCormick shows pricing pays only if revenue tops; a volume miss is the Nike profile. Constellation Brands — could be at its valuation floor ahead of earnings (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 1]): a low bar, but only a better outlook pays. Citigroup — earnings should be alright despite macro risks (Seeking Alpha, [Oct 1]), with Morgan Stanley's earnings imminent (Benzinga, [Oct 1]): after Jefferies, the stocks need revenue guidance, not a beat. J&J — BofA sees Q3 earnings 12% below the Street (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 29]): a miss is in the estimate, so the guide decides.

Playbook: Own revenue-led beats with demand behind them (cruise, IT services on AI demand) and fade EPS-only beats with soft volumes into their prints (PepsiCo, Nike-style apparel). Risk: McCormick proves pricing can still top revenue estimates — a PepsiCo revenue beat squeezes the fade.
