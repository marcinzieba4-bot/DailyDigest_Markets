🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Forward visibility — a raise, a backlog, scarce supply or a credible cost plan. Micron beat and its shares spiked 15% on a record $41.5 billion quarter (Forbes, [Jun 24]); AeroVironment soared 28% as its backlog grew to $1.2 billion (CNBC, [Jun 29]); Progress Software is skyrocketing (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 1]) on AI demand and a raised outlook (TradingView, [Jul 1]); General Mills jumped 9.9% on a beat and up to $3 billion of savings (TradingView, [Jul 2]).

Punished: Beats paired with weaker guidance or rising costs — or a bar set too high. Carnival dipped 5% on weaker Q3 guidance despite the beat (TIKR.com, [Jun 24]); FedEx dipped 6% despite its beat (TIKR.com, [Jun 24]); Cerebras fell 10% on a shrinking-margin forecast (CNBC, [Jun 23]); KPIT Tech tumbled 16% as weak Europe demand triggered a profit warning (Outlook Business, [Jul 1]). Tesla sank 7% despite a strong deliveries report (CNBC, [Jul 2]).

Pattern: The forward line beats the print, measured against the bar: where the bar was low, a credible plan is bought even on a flat top line — General Mills beat with organic sales flat (TradingView, [Jul 1]) — while where expectations had run up (Tesla) even a blowout is sold.

Attention into EPS: Indian IT — TCS reports this week (NDTV Profit, [Jul 5]) as the sector braces for a cold Q1 with guidance cuts looming (The Economic Times, [Jul 2]): the punished side, but with a low bar. Delta reports this week (CMC Markets, [Jul 3]), near highs with oil below $70 (Investor's Business Daily, [Jul 2]) and BofA seeing a rare airline sweet spot (TheStreet, [Jul 4]): fuel savings should lift EPS, but that is priced, Tesla-style, so it needs a raise. Samsung — previews ask whether Q2 can push the shares toward 400,000 won (TradingKey, [Jul 5]) — and TSMC, which Citi sees raising 2026 guidance on AI demand (Investing.com, [Jul 5]), sit on the scarce-supply side, but with a high bar. Banks open the season (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 2]), where capital return is the forward line.

Playbook: Pair long memory and foundry (Samsung, TSMC) against Indian IT services into TCS, where guidance-cut risk is the story; fade Delta into the print. Risk: a chipmaker selloff already dragged the Nasdaq 100 lower (Foreign Policy Journal, [Jul 5]), and with Nifty IT at a five-year low (NDTV Profit, [Jul 1]) a guide that merely holds could spark a relief rally.
