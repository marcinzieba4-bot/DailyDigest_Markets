🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with a raised guide outside the AI trade. Ford jumped 4% as its Q2 beat sparked a guidance upgrade (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 28]); JetBlue advanced 9% on a beat and a 2028 profit target (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 28]); the Dow rose 659 points on Coca-Cola and Sherwin-Williams earnings (Quartz, [Jul 28]); Digital Realty surged 11% after crushing estimates and raising full-year guidance (TIKR.com, [Jul 26]).

Punished: AI hardware against AI-charged expectations, AI spending, and rising costs. SK Hynix posted a record Q2 profit but missed estimates and fell 9.6% (Investing.com, [Jul 28]); Corning plunged despite strong quarterly results (CNBC, [Jul 28]); Alphabet fell more than 6% on capex growth expectations despite its beat (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 23]); Barclays' profit rose 17% but the shares fell as costs are set to rise (Reuters, [Jul 28]).

Pattern: Earnings beats lift the Dow while chip stocks log a fourth straight loss (Tech Times, [Jul 28]). Record AI results are already in the price and capex is sold; the beat that gets paid comes with a raised guide in a business outside the AI loop.

Attention into EPS: Meta — can the ad engine outrun AI capex (Moomoo, [Jul 28]), with 33% ad growth against a $145 billion capex bill (tastylive, [Jul 27]): ads are the rewarded side, the bill the punished one. Microsoft — the outlook is the capex story above all else (Investing.com, [Jul 28]): without faster Azure it is the Alphabet sale. Amazon — AWS accelerates with an Anthropic boost, but free cash flow is the top concern (Moomoo, [Jul 28]): capex again decides. Apple — Goldman raised its target to $370 (AppleInsider, [Jul 28]) into its July 30 report (Moomoo, [Jul 24]): the least capex-heavy giant, closest to the rewarded side.

Playbook: Own beat-and-raise outside the AI loop into its prints (a preview makes the case for AbbVie before July 31 (24/7 Wall St., [Jul 28])) and stay underweight AI hardware, where a record quarter is not enough. Risk: a Microsoft or Meta capex raise backed by visible AI revenue would turn spending into a buy signal for suppliers after four straight losses and squeeze the underweight.
