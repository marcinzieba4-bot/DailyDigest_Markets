🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raise that shows demand accelerating, above all AI demand reaching revenue. Dell surged 9% after lifting its fiscal 2027 forecast on AI server strength (CNBC, [Sep 1]); Snowflake soared 23% on an AI-fueled beat and raised outlook (TradingView, [Sep 2]); Oracle surged over 6% as cloud revenue growth drove its beat (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]). Outside tech, Medtronic surged nearly 9% on a beat and raised guidance (TradingKey, [Sep 1]), and Signet surged 14% on a raised profit outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]) built on tariff refunds and cost discipline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 13]).

Punished: Beats with a soft next-quarter guide, cost or supply pressure, or a one-off doing the work. Broadcom slid as its AI chip forecast underwhelmed (Bloomberg.com, [Sep 2]); Adobe slid 4% on conservative quarterly guidance (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]); HPE raised forecasts but fell on supply concerns (Reuters, [Sep 2]); American Eagle sank 13% as a tariff refund flattered its beat (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 10]); Lululemon tumbled 17% after another guidance cut despite topping Q2 estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the forward line decides. The market pays for an AI-driven raise or a lifted profit outlook and sells beats where the next-quarter guide, costs or supply cap the upside, even for AI winners like Broadcom. A tariff refund pays only when it lifts the guide (Signet), not when it merely flatters the print (American Eagle).

Attention into EPS: Lennar — in focus this week (TradingKey, [Sep 13]) with a 5.2% move priced for its Sept. 16 report (Investing.com, [Sep 9]), and expected to post lower Q3 earnings and revenue (AlphaStreet, [Sep 11]): the guide, not the print, decides. Trip.com — expected to post CNY5.99 a share (TradingView, [Sep 13]), with a preview arguing a regulatory penalty creates a buying opportunity (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 13]): a beat pays only if the outlook looks past the penalty. Micron — its Sept. 30 earnings are seen as the key catalyst for AI memory (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]), yet the stock dropped 4.9% on a $50 billion earnings bar (TechStock², [Sep 11]): only a raised guide clears it.

Playbook: Own AI-demand names where guidance can still rise — Oracle's capex guidance lifting Dell and HPE (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]) shows the server chain has room — and fade beats built on refunds without a higher guide. Risk: with the 10Y at 4.94%, higher yields can compress every rewarded multiple at once.
