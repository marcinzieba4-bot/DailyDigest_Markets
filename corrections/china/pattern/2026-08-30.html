🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised guide with demand visible in the top line, above all a new AI revenue line. Salesforce rocketed 22% (CNBC, [Aug 27]) after raising FY27 guidance by $300M as Agentforce ARR hit $1.5B (BigGo Finance, [Aug 26]); CrowdStrike soared 15% after a beat and raised outlook (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Aug 26]); Nvidia broke its post-earnings curse with a $442 billion day (WSJ, [Aug 27]); Abercrombie jumped as it raised forecasts on robust apparel demand (Reuters, [Aug 26]).

Punished: Beats with a soft forward line, a shrinking top line or a crowded hardware story. Intuit tumbled despite its beat as weak 2027 guidance weighed (TradingView, [Aug 25]); Zoom fell 6% as soft Q3 profit guidance overshadowed a double beat (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 26]); Kohl's fell 6% despite raised guidance and a $150M tariff refund (24/7 Wall St., [Aug 26]) as sales kept sliding (Quartz, [Aug 26]); Marvell raised annual forecasts, but its shares fell (Reuters, [Aug 27]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the market pays for revenue accelerating on demand, and Nvidia and Salesforce upended two bear narratives (CNBC, [Aug 27]) because AI showed up as growth, not as a threat. A tariff refund lifts a stock only when sales grow too, and AI hardware already priced for a raise is sold.

Attention into EPS: Broadcom — its earnings feature in the week ahead with the US jobs report (CMC Markets, [Aug 28]), with a preview warning a revenue beat may not be enough (Moomoo, [Aug 27]): outside Nvidia, AI hardware has been sold on beats, so only a raised AI revenue guide pays. Dell — earnings are expected to grow (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 25]), yet it is sliding ahead of Q2 (Benzinga, [Aug 28]): it sits on Marvell's side unless the guide rises. Lululemon — expected to beat earnings estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 27]), but UBS warns of an outlook cut on weak US and China sales (TradingView, [Aug 25]): a beat with a cut is the punished profile. Ollie's — reports pre-market on September 02 (Moomoo, [Aug 27]); RBC expects a 2026 comp guidance cut as tariff refunds dull the profit impact (Yahoo Finance, [Aug 25]): a refund story without sales growth sits with Kohl's.

Playbook: Own demand-led software and fade AI hardware that has run into its print (Dell, Broadcom). In retail, prefer demand-led apparel over refund stories. Risk: a Broadcom blowout like Nvidia's re-rates the whole hardware complex and squeezes the fade.
