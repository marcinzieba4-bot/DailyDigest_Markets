🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: Forward visibility — a raise, a backlog or scarce supply. Micron beat and its shares spiked 15% on a record $41.5 billion quarter (Forbes, [Jun 24]); AeroVironment soared 28% as its backlog grew to $1.2 billion (CNBC, [Jun 29]); BlackBerry climbed 20% on an upbeat outlook (TradingView, [Jun 26]); Progress Software is skyrocketing (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 1]) on AI demand and a raised outlook (TradingView, [Jul 1]); MSC Industrial jumped after topping expectations (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 1]).

Punished: Beats paired with weaker guidance or rising costs. Accenture tumbled 16% on a guidance cut (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 18]); Carnival dipped 5% on weaker Q3 guidance despite the beat (TIKR.com, [Jun 24]); FedEx dipped 6% despite its beat (TIKR.com, [Jun 24]); Darden fell as Olive Garden weakness clouded its beat (Bloomberg.com, [Jun 25]); Cerebras fell 10% on a shrinking-margin forecast (CNBC, [Jun 23]). Warnings are sold hard: KPIT Tech tumbled 16% as weak Europe demand triggered a profit warning (Outlook Business, [Jul 1]).

Pattern: The forward line beats the print: a raise, a backlog or scarce supply is paid for, while weaker guidance, cost or margin pressure is sold, however large the beat. The exception is a guide already cut to the bone — Nike jumped on its beat (The Motley Fool, [Jul 1]) even as a weak outlook flagged a prolonged turnaround (Reuters, [Jul 1]).

Attention into EPS: Indian IT — TCS reports Q1 on July 9 with its 24% operating-margin guidance in focus (Sahi, [Jun 30]), while Motilal sees weak demand and Infosys and HCLTech may cut FY27 guidance (Business Today, [Jul 1]): the punished side, Accenture- and KPIT-style. Banks — Wells Fargo has lagged other banks and earnings can change that (Barron's, [Jul 1]); after a clean stress test (Global Finance Magazine, [Jun 25]) the forward line is capital return, not the beat. Celestica — "buy ahead of earnings" (Seeking Alpha, [Jul 1]) — sits on the AI-hardware side. Delta, with Citi lifting its target ahead of an expected strong airline season (Yahoo Finance, [Jun 29]), now needs guidance above the known fuel relief.

Playbook: Pair long AI hardware and backlog names (memory, Celestica) against Indian IT services into TCS, where guidance-cut risk is the story. Risk: Nifty IT already hit a five-year low (NDTV Profit, [Jul 1]), so a guide that merely holds could spark a Nike-style relief rally.
