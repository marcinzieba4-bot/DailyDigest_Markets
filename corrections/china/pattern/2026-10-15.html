🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A beat with something new behind it — inflows, a raise or a franchise line that surprised. BlackRock rose 5% on strong results (TradingView, [Oct 14]) as it drew $205 billion in client funds (Reuters, [Oct 14]); UnitedHealth rose 4% on a beat and raised outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Oct 13]); Goldman's shares rose on strong equities and IB revenues (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 13]); Marston's jumped 18% as brokers lifted forecasts (Yahoo Finance UK, [Oct 13]); Accenture had its best day ever (Barron's, [Oct 1]).

Punished: Plain beats, and any miss or cost surprise. Morgan Stanley beat, yet its shares fell (Investing.com, [Oct 14]); State Street fell despite a Q3 beat and record AUM (TradingView, [Oct 14]); BofA fell 5% on an earnings miss and compliance penalty (The Motley Fool, [Oct 14]); Fastenal fell as margins face pressure (TradingView, [Oct 14]); J&J's shares fell on weak medical device sales despite a raise (Reuters, [Oct 13]).

Pattern: The market is in a punishing mood (Barron's, [Oct 14]) — even strong bank earnings couldn't save their stocks (CNBC, [Oct 14]). With beats now the norm (CNBC, [Oct 7]), only a new driver (inflows, cost relief, a raise without a weak segment) is paid; a miss, a charge or a soft core line is sold hard.

Attention into EPS: TSMC — reports today (TradingView, [Oct 14]) with traders pricing a sizeable move (Investopedia, [Oct 14]): the AI line is expected, so only a guide that also clears capex worries pays. Tesla — Q3 earnings are expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 14]): a low bar, but the pattern sells beats without a new driver. Meta — reports Oct. 21 (The Motley Fool, [Oct 14]) after Goldman raised its target to $860 (TipRanks, [Oct 14]): ad revenue is priced, the cost line decides it. United Airlines — analysts expect an earnings decline (Yahoo Finance, [Oct 13]): fuel puts it on the punished side.

Playbook: Own raise-plus-driver stories (managed care, asset gatherers with inflows) and fade "beat-only" prints, especially in banks and airlines. Into TSMC, size for the guide and capex, not the revenue beat. Risk: after this sell-off, a clean quarter in megacap tech could flip the punishing mood quickly.
