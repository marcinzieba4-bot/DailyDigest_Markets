🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raised outlook that shows demand accelerating. Dell shares gained as strong AI server demand boosted its annual forecast (Reuters, [Sep 2]); Snowflake soared 23% on an AI-fueled beat and raised outlook (TradingView, [Sep 2]); Samsara jumped on an earnings beat and raised outlook (Barron's, [Sep 4]); Signet surged 14% on a raised profit outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]); JPMorgan rose after an upbeat revenue outlook (Yahoo! Finance Canada, [Sep 15]).

Punished: Beats or guides with a soft forward line, supply limits, or a one-off doing the work. Broadcom slid as its AI chip forecast underwhelmed (Bloomberg.com, [Sep 2]); HPE delivered a blowout quarter and raised guidance, but shares still fell (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 2]); American Eagle sank 13% as a tariff refund flattered its beat (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 10]); Lululemon tumbled 17% after another guidance cut despite topping Q2 estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]); Bank of America plunged on its CEO's sales and trading revenue guidance (Seeking Alpha, [Sep 14]); Dave & Buster's retreated 12% on a sales miss (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 15]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the forward line decides. The banks show it cleanly — same macro, opposite guides: JPMorgan's upbeat outlook lifted it while BofA's guidance sank it. A raise that is only in line with a high bar (Broadcom, HPE) is sold, and a refund pays only when it lifts the guide — Signet lifted guidance on tariff refunds and cost discipline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 13]) — not when it flatters the print (American Eagle).

Attention into EPS: Lennar — heads into its report with shares at 4-year lows (Benzinga, [Sep 15]): priced for bad news, so a stable margin guide could rally it, while a beat with weaker margins is the punished profile. KB Home — Q3 earnings are expected to decline (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 15]): the decline is priced; the order and margin outlook decides. Micron — stands out for accelerating growth (ChartMill, [Sep 14]) into its Sept. 30 report (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 15]), yet it dropped 4.9% on a $50 billion earnings bar (TechStock², [Sep 11]): only a raised guide clears it.

Playbook: Own AI-demand names where guidance can still rise — Oracle's capex guidance lifting Dell and HPE (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]) shows the server chain has room — and prefer the bank with the rising outlook over the sector. Fade beats built on refunds without a higher guide. Risk: with the 10Y at 4.95%, higher yields can compress every rewarded multiple at once.
