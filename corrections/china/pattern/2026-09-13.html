🧮 Earnings Pattern — What the Market Pays For

Rewarded: A raise that shows demand accelerating, above all AI demand reaching revenue. Dell surged 9% after lifting its fiscal 2027 forecast on AI server strength (CNBC, [Sep 1]); Snowflake leapt 23% on a beat and raised guidance (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 2]); Oracle surged over 6% as cloud revenue growth drove its beat (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]). Outside tech, Medtronic surged nearly 9% on a beat and raised guidance (TradingKey, [Sep 1]) and Signet surged 14% on a raised profit outlook (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 9]).

Punished: Beats with a soft next-quarter guide, cost or supply pressure, or a one-off doing the work. Broadcom slid as its AI chip forecast underwhelmed (Bloomberg.com, [Sep 2]); Adobe slid 4% on conservative quarterly guidance (TIKR.com, [Sep 11]); HPE raised forecasts but fell on supply concerns (Reuters, [Sep 2]); Navan tumbled on cost concerns (Reuters, [Sep 9]); American Eagle sank 13% as a tariff refund flattered its beat (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 10]); Lululemon tumbled 17% after another guidance cut despite topping Q2 estimates (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 3]).

Pattern: The beat is table stakes; the market pays only when the forward line accelerates — an AI-driven raise or margin self-help — and sells beats where the next-quarter guide, costs or supply cap the upside, even for AI winners like Broadcom. Refunds and one-offs earn nothing.

Attention into EPS: Micron — its Sept. 30 earnings are seen as the key catalyst for AI memory (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 9]), yet the stock dropped 4.9% on a $50 billion earnings bar (TechStock², [Sep 11]): a high bar means only a raised guide pays, or it trades like Broadcom. Lennar — reports on Sept. 16 with a 5.2% move priced (Investing.com, [Sep 9]) and is expected to post lower Q3 earnings and revenue (AlphaStreet, [Sep 11]): the forward guide, not the print, decides. Darden — UBS sees it maintaining its outlook despite Olive Garden weakness (Yahoo Finance, [Sep 11]): an unchanged guide over weaker traffic earns nothing.

Playbook: Own AI-demand names where guidance can still rise — Oracle's capex guidance lifting Dell and HPE (24/7 Wall St., [Sep 11]) shows the server chain still has room — and fade beats built on refunds without sales growth. Risk: with the 10Y at 4.97%, higher yields can compress every rewarded multiple at once.
