🇨🇳 China  Mixed  → easing bias

Macro Stance: Easing expectations have moved from the plumbing to the policy rate. Analysts say a surprise LPR cut cannot be ruled out this week (investingLive, [Aug 17]), the 10-year bond yield fell to a 13-month low (Financial Times, [Aug 17]), and the PBOC's first mid-month overnight reverse repo boosted bonds (The Edge Singapore, [Aug 14]) as liquidity management focuses on the overnight rate (The Standard (HK), [Aug 13]). The yuan broke through 6.74 per dollar, its highest in about three and a half years (BigGo Finance, [Aug 17]), with OCBC seeing policymakers guide measured RMB appreciation (FXStreet, [Aug 17]). Prices are soft: July CPI rose 0.5% (CGTN, [Aug 9]), a six-month low (TradingView, [Aug 8]), with pork prices down 13.4% (BigGo Finance, [Aug 17]), and PPI rose 3.5% (China Daily, [Aug 9]).

PMIs & Activity: The delayed July data confirm what sub-50 PMIs of 49.2 and 49.0 signalled (FXStreet, [Jul 31]). Industrial output grew 4.5% y/y and retail sales rose 0.6% (Reuters, [Aug 17]), with output, retail sales and investment all missing forecasts (The Business Times, [Aug 17]); fixed-asset investment fell 6.7% in the first seven months (Xinhua, [Aug 17]), more than expected (TradingView, [Aug 17]). The two-speed economy is in the detail: high-tech investment grew 5% (BigGo Finance, [Aug 17]), AI equipment investment surged 81.8% (Tech Times, [Aug 17]) and semiconductor output rose 23.1% in seven months (Global Times, [Aug 17]). ING says the growth imbalance worsened as domestic activity slowed (ING, [Aug 17]), while the statistics bureau says extreme weather hit but the full-year target is still supported (BigGo Finance, [Aug 17]).

Credit Impulse: July new bank loans posted a record contraction as credit demand faltered (Reuters, [Aug 14]), a $50 billion bank-lending contraction in The Asian Banker's terms (The Asian Banker, [Aug 17]), yet credit growth exceeded forecasts despite the rare loan slump (Bloomberg, [Aug 14]). Reconciled: aggregate financing was carried by channels outside bank lending while the loan book shrank, and the July output and investment misses show that financing is not reaching private demand. Corporate loan rates fell below 3% (finance.biggo.com, [Aug 14]), so price is not the constraint; lenders are hunting new collateral, tapping AI token use to lend to asset-light startups (Global Times, [Aug 16]), while China cuts 670 rural banks (Caixin Global, [Aug 15]).

Property: The July price survey needs reconciling. The 70-city index fell 3.2% y/y, the smallest drop in five months (BigGo Finance, [Aug 17]), yet Bloomberg reports new home prices falling at a faster pace (Bloomberg, [Aug 17]) and Reuters calls them stagnant as demand stays weak (Reuters, [Aug 17]): the year-on-year comparison is improving off a weaker base while the monthly decline quickened. First-tier prices were flat, ending a 4-month rebound (South China Morning Post, [Aug 17]), and property investment slumped nearly 20% (BigGo Finance, [Aug 17]). The WSJ sees the economy weakening on several fronts as the property bust worsens (WSJ, [Aug 17]), city-level easing has not turned prices (chinadailyhk, [Aug 12]), and expiring land leases remain a $148 billion threat (Bloomberg, [Aug 11]).

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: The reaction function is turning. Premier Li Qiang called for stabilising external demand as growth sputters (Reuters, [Aug 17]), urged faster implementation of the "Six Networks" to boost domestic demand (BigGo Finance, [Aug 17]) and stressed fulfilling full-year targets (English.gov.cn, [Aug 17]), while Beijing moves to unlock county-level consumption (chinadailyhk, [Aug 17]); analysts bet on extra stimulus (South China Morning Post, [Aug 17]). The tax crackdown is spooking the diaspora (AFR, [Aug 17]). On trade, Breakingviews says China's unplanned surpluses put the EU in a trade bind (Reuters, [Aug 17]), Beijing rejected US "tariff evasion" accusations (China Daily, [Aug 15]), and the US imposed 100% tariffs on some drones (The Japan Times, [Aug 14]).

Markets & Flows: Hong Kong rebounded past 25,000, with the Hang Seng up 1.34% on chip and optical stocks (BigGo Finance, [Aug 17]), as chipmakers rallied on strong earnings (The Economic Times, [Aug 17]). Long-term capital is warier: global private equity made zero deals in China (Financial Times, [Aug 17]), and Chinese hedge funds are rotating out of Nvidia and US hyperscalers (South China Morning Post, [Aug 17]).

New / Most Important: The delayed July data landed weak across output, retail sales, investment and home prices, and the policy response came the same day: Li Qiang's call to stabilise external demand and accelerate "Six Networks" investment, with a surprise LPR cut now in play. The data are bearish; the stance stays Mixed because the reaction function is turning and the bond market is already pricing it.

Playbook ✓: Weak domestic demand, an easing bias and pressure for more stimulus are on script.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Markets cheered chip earnings while fixed-asset investment fell 6.7% and property investment slumped nearly 20%, and global assets will feel the construction side first. Iron ore stocks at China ports were already at a five-week high (Reuters, [Aug 14]), leaving iron ore and AUD exposed as CommBank weighs what China's two-speed economy means for Australia (CommBank, [Aug 17]); European exporters face surpluses China cannot absorb at home; and WTI at $84.14 is a terms-of-trade tax on margins with PPI easing.
