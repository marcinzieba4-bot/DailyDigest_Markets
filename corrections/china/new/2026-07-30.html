🇨🇳 China  Mixed  → easing bias, stimulus signaled

Macro Stance: The PBOC is easing through volume while firming the currency. It set the overnight reverse repo rate steady at 1.25% (Reuters, [Jul 28]) and is maintaining liquidity support before month-end (TradingView, [Jul 28]), after injecting a total of 2.1 trillion yuan (Reuters, [Jul 24]). The fixing has turned into a signal: the yuan was steady as the PBOC set the fix at a 3-1/2-year high (Business Recorder, [Jul 29]), then rose to a 1-1/2-month high on the strong fixing and dollar softness as the Fed held (NST Online, [Jul 29]). A stronger yuan is the opposite of the export-led devaluation Europe fears. Prices are split: June CPI rose only 1% (Global Times, [Jul 9]) while PPI climbed 4.1% (finance.biggo.com, [Jul 8]), a near four-year high (Reuters, [Jul 8]).

PMIs & Activity: June's official manufacturing PMI was 50.3 and non-manufacturing 50.2 (Reuters, [Jun 29]); for July, factory activity is expected to have stalled (Reuters, [Jul 29]), so the official print is now the key test. Q2 GDP grew 4.3%, below the official target range (Bloomberg, [Jul 15]); June industrial output rose 5.3% y/y (BusinessLine, [Jul 15]) and exports surged 27% (AP News, [Jul 14]), but first-half retail sales were up just 1.3% (Ecns.cn, [Jul 15]). First-half industrial profits rose 18.7% as a tech boom masks the property slump (Caixin Global, [Jul 27]), at the slowest growth this year (Financial Times, [Jul 26]). Fortune describes goods consumption slumping even as exports and services boom ([Jul 29]); NEV retail sales dipped 2% in the first 26 days of July (CnEVPost, [Jul 29]), and Toyota's sales fell for a fifth month on a China slump (Yahoo Finance Singapore, [Jul 29]).

Credit Impulse: June new bank loans rose less than expected (The Star, [Jul 15]) and credit growth missed as the PBOC flagged a "new normal" (Caixin Global, [Jul 16]); household loans fell by 366.8 billion yuan in the first half (36Kr, [Jul 17]). The cleanest tell is that cash at Chinese banks has swelled to a record as borrowing slows (Nikkei Asia, [Jul 28]): liquidity is not the constraint, demand for credit is. Pricing is loosening quietly, with banks breaking with the lending benchmark (Bloomberg, [Jul 24]) and launching the first repo-linked loans (Yahoo Finance, [Jul 27]). Read: impulse flat at best, household impulse negative.

Property: June new home prices fell at a slower pace (Reuters, [Jul 14]) and first-tier prices extended a four-month rebound (South China Morning Post, [Jul 15]), but resale prices fell faster (Reuters, [Jun 30]) and property investment slumped 18 percent y/y in January–June (The Standard (HK), [Jul 15]). Prime land still clears for state-linked names: Poly Developments and China Resources Land spent 23.2 billion yuan in a day in Shanghai's inner ring (finance.biggo.com, [Jul 28]), and property stocks led gains on hopes for supportive policies (Moomoo, [Jul 28]). Regulators curbed offshore loans for local real estate firms (The Standard (HK), [Jul 23]), and Vanke completed its bond extensions with a Shenzhen Metro loan (China Economic Review, [Jul 23]).

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Messaging before the meeting leans to consumption: China Daily calls boosting H2 consumption a leading priority ([Jul 29]), while Caixin's analysis is that Beijing will likely forgo major stimulus as the growth target remains in reach ([Jul 28]). Stocks were mixed as the Politburo meeting looms (TradingView, [Jul 29]), so its outcome is still ahead. On trade, China says the US pledged to cap replacement tariffs at 20% (Bloomberg, [Jul 27]) and it held off on retaliation over Section 301 (South China Morning Post, [Jul 27]), but it is gearing up to fight overcapacity claims (Bloomberg, [Jul 28]) and has put EU entities on an export control watchlist targeting rare-earth supply chains (Shanghai Metals Market, [Jul 29]).

Markets & Flows: The chip shock keeps spreading: ASML slid on a report of China starting DUV tool production (Bloomberg, [Jul 27]) and the KOSPI plunged 11% on China chip competition fears (Nikkei Asia, [Jul 28]), while chip stocks dragged mainland shares as Hong Kong rose (Business Recorder, [Jul 29]). The Hang Seng surged nearly 500 points to reclaim 25,000 (BigGo Finance, [Jul 29]), and Chinese firms are readying the most buybacks since the trade war to stem the rout (Bloomberg, [Jul 29]).

New / Most Important: Two signals land on the eve of the data and the Politburo. A Reuters poll expects July factory activity to have stalled, and the PBOC set the yuan fix at a 3-1/2-year high. Beijing is accepting a stronger currency while manufacturing loses momentum, which raises the bar for the Politburo: without a consumption push that offsets slowing factories, the strong-yuan, tight-budget mix tightens conditions.

Playbook ✓: Steady policy rate, ample liquidity, tech- and export-led profits, weak property and a measured tariff response are on script.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: China's tech wins are a shock to others: Korean memory names fell on CXMT and its DUV push. A stalling factory PMI and an 18 percent property-investment slump keep construction-linked bulk demand soft, a drag on iron ore and AUD that hopes for Politburo support have not reversed. European exporters face weak China luxury and auto demand, though a firmer yuan eases their price pressure at the margin. EM inflows follow AI and buybacks, not a PMI or credit upturn, and oil near $84 remains a terms-of-trade hit for China.
