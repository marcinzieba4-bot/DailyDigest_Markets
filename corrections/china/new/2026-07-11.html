🇨🇳 China  Bullish  → Easing bias

Macro Stance: Policy signals are now explicit. China Daily stresses "appropriately accommodative" monetary policy ([Jul 10]) and the PBOC vowed flexibility amid an uneven picture (SCMP, [Jul 9]), flagging "structural divergences" as the AI boom masks consumer weakness (BigGo Finance, [Jul 9]). The currency is the instrument: the PBOC set the fixing below 6.8 per dollar for the first time since 2023 (Bloomberg, [Jul 10]), at 6.7804 (investingLive, [Jul 9]), and UOB sees tight ranges persisting against the dollar (FXStreet, [Jul 10]). June CPI rose 1% (Global Times, [Jul 9]) as inflation cooled with oil (Caixin, [Jul 9]) while producer inflation hit a near 4-year high (KLSE Screener, [Jul 9]) — a "scissors gap" that points to weak domestic demand (BigGo Finance, [Jul 9]).

PMIs & Activity: The June PMIs — manufacturing 50.3, non-manufacturing 50.2 (Reuters, [Jun 29]) — rest on exports, and second-quarter GDP, due in the coming week (MUFG Research, [Jul 10]), is expected to have cooled (Free Malaysia Today, [Jul 10]). Autos make the split stark: June car sales fell 23% (Just Auto, [Jul 10]) while passenger car exports rose 80% (AP News, [Jul 9]), and NEV retail sales fell 9% in the first week of July (CnEVPost, [Jul 10]). May retail sales fell 0.6% (Il Sole 24 Ore, [Jun 15]) and fixed-asset investment was down 4.1% in the first five months (Macau Business, [Jun 16]). The IMF nonetheless upgraded China's 2026 forecast to 4.6% (Global Times, [Jul 8]), and Societe Generale says targeted stimulus supports growth (FXStreet, [Jul 10]).

Credit Impulse: May total social financing grew 7.7% (CGTN, [Jun 12]) on government bonds (Bloomberg, [Jun 12]) while new loans missed (Reuters, [Jun 12]); the June print is due. Credit is being disciplined, not expanded: short-term LGFV bond sales were curbed (The Standard, [Jul 9]), up to 494.6 billion yuan of bonds face downgrade risk under the ratings crackdown (BigGo Finance, [Jul 8]), and Bloomberg sees the debt-cleanup milestone opening a fiscal rethink ([Jul 9]). In Hong Kong, HSBC plans to sell Hang Seng Bank's high-risk loans (The Standard, [Jul 10]).

Property: Vanke says its first-half loss may widen to $2.2 billion (Bloomberg, [Jul 10]), and Fitch expects new home sales to fall 11% to 13% in 2026 (Mingtiandi, [Jul 9]). Resale prices fell faster in June (Reuters, [Jun 30]) after new home prices fell faster in May (Reuters, [Jun 15]) and real-estate investment dropped 16.2% y/y in January–May (GMK Center, [Jun 18]). Bloomberg says demand for Soviet-style apartments shows the limits to the revival ([Jul 8]), and land finance is distorting budgets: one city "sold" the same plot 18 times (SCMP, [Jul 8]).

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: The State Council unveiled a carbon-peak pathway (China Daily, [Jul 10]): supply-side targets, not a demand package. China expanded its anti-sanctions toolkit, raising risks for foreign firms (Al Jazeera, [Jul 10]). The EU launched an anti-dumping probe into Chinese ducks (SCMP, [Jul 10]) after duties of up to 45% on tyres (Eunews, [Jul 9]), and European carmakers are paying for the China shift: BMW's sales fell after a 30% plunge in China (Bloomberg, [Jul 10]) and VW is slashing model lineups and capacity (Automotive News, [Jul 10]). With Washington, soybean buying has moved beyond the initial benchmark (RFD-TV, [Jul 10]).

Markets & Flows: BlackRock's strategist publicly called out China's growing appeal as a portfolio diversifier. Offshore flows are confirming: Hong Kong stocks are set for their strongest week in over a year on internet gains (Business Times, [Jul 10]) after a Bond Connect boost (BigGo Finance, [Jul 10]), though an AI lockup expiry hit the Hang Seng Tech Index (BigGo Finance, [Jul 10]) and the mainland dipped (India Infoline, [Jul 10]). The yuan is now a spillover channel: AUD/USD climbed on yuan strength (Mitrade, [Jul 10]).

New / Most Important: The fixing below 6.8 for the first time since 2023 is the newest signal, and it sharpens the BlackRock call. A firmer managed yuan, inflation at 1% and a central bank stressing accommodation add a currency tailwind to the diversification argument — notable from the world's largest asset manager after years of underweight positioning. It remains a contrarian positioning story: the domestic data, from car sales to Vanke's losses, still point down.

Playbook ✓: Weak demand plus deflation risk keep the easing posture; no bazooka, only targeted support.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: BlackRock's public pivot toward China diversification value, while non-consensus, could be the early signal of a capital flow rotation; given how underweight the world is, even modest rebalancing would be disproportionately large. The contrarian case is a currency and positioning bet, not a growth bet, and that is how it transmits. A stronger yuan is already lifting the AUD (Mitrade, [Jul 10]) and global iron ore trade hit a record on Chinese demand (S&P Global, [Jul 10]), yet property sales are forecast to keep falling, so the commodity bid rests on the yuan and trade flows rather than housing. European exporters face competition and a weaker Chinese buyer at once. If second-quarter GDP and June credit disappoint, the positioning trade will be tested.
