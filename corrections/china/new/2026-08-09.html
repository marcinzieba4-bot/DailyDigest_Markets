🇨🇳 China  Mixed  → easing

Macro Stance: July prices removed an obstacle to easing. CPI edged up 0.5% and PPI growth slowed to 3.5%, both missing expectations (BigGo Finance, [Aug 8]); consumer inflation is at a six-month low (TradingView, [Aug 8]) and factory-gate inflation at a 3-month low (Reuters, [Aug 8]), cooling as the impact of the Iran war eases (Financial Times, [Aug 8]), after June's 1.0% CPI and 4.1% PPI (finance.biggo.com, [Jul 8]). The PBOC had already pledged timely adjustment of its policy tools (Reuters, [Aug 2]), and China Daily writes that interest rate and RRR cuts may be on the table ([Aug 4]). Reserve management is active: the central bank extended its gold buying to 21 months with the largest purchase since 2023 (Caixin Global, [Aug 8]), while July FX reserves rose less than expected (The Standard, [Aug 7]). The yuan is firm near a 3-1/2-year high on robust exports (The Star, [Aug 7]).

PMIs & Activity: The official manufacturing PMI fell to 49.2 and the non-manufacturing PMI to 49.0 (FXStreet, [Jul 31]); the private factory index eased to 50.9 (Fibre2Fashion, [Aug 3]), a 4-month low (Reuters, [Aug 2]). Trade carries the economy, though the headlines need reconciling. Exports surged 23.9% and the surplus widened to $112.5B (Seeking Alpha, [Aug 7]), yet SCMP says the surplus narrowed ([Aug 7]) and AP that exports slowed slightly ([Aug 7]): growth eased from June's 27% (Reuters, [Jul 13]) and the surplus shrank from June while still beating forecasts (TradingView, [Aug 6]); the widening is against a year earlier. Total trade expanded 19.2% in July (CGTN, [Aug 7]) as AI demand lifted export prices (Caixin Global, [Aug 7]). Domestic demand is the gap after Q2 GDP growth of 4.3% fell below the target range (Bloomberg, [Jul 15]).

Credit Impulse: The July credit data are still to come and the previews conflict. A Reuters poll sees bank lending set to plummet on subdued demand and a seasonal slowdown ([Aug 7]), while Caixin expects loans to rebound on a low base ([Aug 7]). Reconciled: a sharp drop from June's seasonal peak that still compares well with a weak July last year. VT Markets reads July credit demand as weak, with low loan growth fuelling easing bets ([Aug 8]). June set the tone: new loans rose less than expected (The Star, [Jul 15]) and household loans fell 366.8 billion yuan in the first half (36Kr, [Jul 17]). Record low yields on long-dated offshore yuan bonds (Bloomberg, [Aug 4]) are the market's verdict on demand.

Property: Policy is moving in the top tier: Beijing further relaxed home-buying curbs (Reuters, [Aug 7]), following Shanghai (Bloomberg, [Aug 7]). The data explain the urgency. Housing prices fell 0.5% in July as sales volume weakened (Investing.com, [Aug 3]), the top-100 developer sales slump deepened (Trivium China, [Aug 5]) and property investment slumped 18 percent y/y in January–June (The Standard, [Jul 15]), even as June new home prices fell at a slower pace (Reuters, [Jul 14]). China Cinda warns of a first-half profit drop as the slump weighs (Caixin Global, [Aug 5]), and PIIE argues the property bust is spilling across China's borders ([Aug 7]).

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: SCMP reads the Politburo as cautious confidence ([Aug 3]), and Oxford Economics says an unspent fiscal arsenal targets a Q3 recovery (CNBC, [Aug 3]). The surprise tax on offshore trusts (CNBC, [Aug 4]) reaches offshore insurance income (Reuters, [Aug 6]), and a crackdown on capital outflow has hit Macau casinos (The Edge Singapore, [Aug 7]). Trade risk escalated: the US Senate cleared a Russia sanctions bill carrying 100% tariffs on India and China (Business Standard, [Aug 8]), though some senators oppose it (News18, [Aug 8]). That comes on top of a 15% US tariff on polysilicon (Nikkei Asia, [Aug 7]), while China's rare-earth exports to Japan and the US plunged after restrictions (Nikkei Asia, [Aug 8]).

Markets & Flows: China and Hong Kong stocks rose as strong trade data and AI demand lifted sentiment (The Economic Times, [Aug 7]), and cheap Chinese AI is shifting investors' focus to internet stocks (Moneyweb, [Aug 8]). Earlier in the week the Hang Seng slumped nearly 500 points as insurers sank (The Standard, [Aug 6]). Flows stay supportive: mainland investors extended their Hong Kong buying spree (South China Morning Post, [Aug 5]), Morgan Stanley lifted its Hang Seng target to 28,400 (BigGo Finance, [Aug 5]) and Chinese investors poured $1.2bn into gold ETFs (TradingView, [Aug 6]). NerdWallet's list of best-performing China stocks for August shows retail attention returning.

New / Most Important: July inflation cooled more than expected, with CPI at a six-month low and PPI at a 3-month low, just as previews point to weak July loan demand. With the PMIs below 50 and the yuan near a 3-1/2-year high, the case for the rate and RRR cuts China Daily has floated is now stronger. The Senate's 100% tariff bill is the risk that could force the timing.

Playbook ✓: Cooling inflation, easing signals, top-tier property relaxation and AI-led exports are on script.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Consensus treats cooling PPI as relief; with the PMIs contracting and loan demand weak, it reads more like fading demand. Iron ore at a year low (Tech Times, [Aug 4]) leaves AUD exposed into this week's RBA decision. European exporters face a Germany-China trade imbalance that widened further in July (Ghana Business News, [Aug 8]), and the Senate bill hits India and China together, a double shock for EM Asia. With oil at $78.18, cooling factory-gate prices squeeze margins further.
