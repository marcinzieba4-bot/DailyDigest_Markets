🇨🇳 China  Mixed  → easing bias

Macro Stance: Policy remains stimulative with an easing bias, delivered through liquidity and the currency rather than rates. The loan prime rates were left unchanged (China Daily, [Aug 20]) for a 15th month, alongside expanded consumer and small-business loan subsidies (The Asian Banker, [Aug 21]); the one-year LPR is 3% (Fibre2Fashion, [Aug 20]). The PBOC ran a 500 billion yuan MLF operation (BigGo Finance, [Aug 24]), will keep conducting overnight reverse repos (Fibre2Fashion, [Aug 26]), and banks now use the overnight rate to price bonds as its reform widens (Bloomberg, [Aug 25]). After the yuan surged to 6.72, a 42-month high (BigGo Finance, [Aug 20]), the PBOC set a weaker fix signalling tolerance for depreciation to bolster growth (VT Markets, [Aug 25]) and the yuan slipped from its 3-1/2-year high (The Star, [Aug 25]). July CPI slowed to 0.5% and PPI to 3.5%, both missing expectations (BigGo Finance, [Aug 8]).

PMIs & Activity: Activity is soft and profits are narrowing. July's official manufacturing PMI fell to 49.2 and non-manufacturing to 49.0 (FXStreet, [Jul 31]), the private factory index eased to 50.9 (Fibre2Fashion, [Aug 3]) and private services growth hit its slowest since September 2024 (Reuters, [Aug 4]). July industrial output grew 4.5% y/y and retail sales only 0.6% (Reuters, [Aug 17]), while exports jumped almost a quarter (Financial Times, [Aug 6]). The new print: industrial profits grew 17.6% in January-July (TradingView, [Aug 26]), down from 18.7% in the first half (Caixin Global, [Jul 27]), with growth cooling to the slowest in seven months as the slowdown deepens (CNBC, [Aug 26]) and AI-linked sectors outpacing the rest (Reuters, [Aug 26]). August NEV retail sales fell 12% in the first 23 days, a narrowing decline (CnEVPost, [Aug 26]). Goldman warns Q3 growth could fall below 4.5% (BigGo Finance, [Aug 24]); the August PMIs follow at month-end.

Credit Impulse: July bank loans posted a record contraction as credit demand faltered (Reuters, [Aug 14]), yet credit growth exceeded forecasts despite the rare loan slump (Bloomberg, [Aug 14]). Reconciled: the broad financing aggregate is holding up while the loan book shrank, so the impulse comes from outside bank lending. Policy works on price and targeting rather than volume: corporate loan rates fell below 3% (BigGo Finance, [Aug 14]), the subsidized loan program was expanded to revive demand (Caixin Global, [Aug 21]) and banks are launching "token loans" to fund AI firms (Caixin Global, [Aug 26]). Credit is flowing to the AI economy, not to households.

Property: July new home prices were stagnant as demand stayed weak (Reuters, [Aug 17]); the 70-city annual decline narrowed to 3.2%, the smallest in five months, while property investment slumped nearly 20% (BigGo Finance, [Aug 17]), and first-tier prices were flat, ending a four-month rebound (SCMP, [Aug 17]). Easing stays local and incremental: Shanghai is lowering second-home down payments (SCMP, [Aug 20]) and housing provident fund rules are being reshaped as the market enters a new phase (China.org.cn, [Aug 21]). The Evergrande chapter is closing in court, with the founder sentenced to life (Reuters, [Aug 20]), the liquidation accepted (SCMP, [Aug 21]) and liquidators cleared to pursue PwC globally (Financial Times, [Aug 26]). Hong Kong home prices dipped, snapping their longest growth run since 2018 (chinadailyhk, [Aug 26]).

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: China pledged timely fiscal support (Reuters, [Aug 20]) but state media signal no "bazooka" (Caixin Global, [Aug 25]), and the $119 billion policy financing tool faces a roll-out lag (Reuters, [Aug 24]). Fiscal stress is showing up as extraction: tax authorities require over 100 mainland-listed firms to pay 7.7 bln yuan in overdue tax (The Standard, [Aug 26]), an unprecedented clawback hammering listed companies (Bloomberg, [Aug 25]). On trade, the US eyes overcapacity tariffs of 7.5% before the Xi-Trump talks (Bloomberg, [Aug 24]), China warned it could retaliate over Iran sanctions (Financial Times, [Aug 25]) as lawmakers press Treasury to target Chinese banks over Iran (Politico, [Aug 26]), and Merz signals a tougher German stance on China trade (Bloomberg, [Aug 26]) ahead of the EU's October deadline (Euronews.com, [Aug 25]).

Markets & Flows: China stocks climbed on the Nvidia-led AI hardware rally while Hong Kong lagged (TradingView, [Aug 26]), and Z.ai surged 8% on an AI model running only on Chinese chips (CNBC, [Aug 26]); CXMT's market debut confirms the push for semiconductor self-sufficiency is accelerating. Beneath the AI tape, dividend stocks are back in vogue as bond yields fall (SCMP, [Aug 26]), and Alibaba's $10 billion stock sale (MarketWatch, [Aug 25]) keeps Hong Kong supply heavy. Business Insider sets out why investors should start paying attention to China (Business Insider, [Aug 26]), and US Treasury buybacks may be redirecting capital toward Asia.

New / Most Important: July industrial profits confirm the two-speed economy. Year-to-date growth slowed to 17.6%, the weakest this year (Bloomberg, [Aug 26]), and what growth remains sits in AI-linked sectors. At the same moment Beijing is pulling 7.7 bln yuan of back taxes from listed firms and ruling out a bazooka: corporate cash is being squeezed while the policy response stays targeted, which caps the upside for China's old-economy earnings into the August PMIs.

Playbook ✓: Targeted fiscal support, LPR on hold, a managed yuan and AI-led equity leadership fit consensus.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Capital chasing CXMT, Z.ai and AI hardware reads as a China recovery; the profits, the 49.2 PMI and a shrinking loan book say it is a tech enclave inside a soft economy. That matters abroad: a party journal warning that the steel sector must avoid the US rust belt's fate (SCMP, [Aug 26]) points to capacity discipline that is bearish for iron ore and the AUD, CNOOC's record profit as China pumps more oil at home (OilPrice.com, [Aug 26]) trims import demand behind WTI at 82.72, and European exporters face weak Chinese demand plus Germany's tougher line.
