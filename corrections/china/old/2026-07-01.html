New / Most Important: The Tooze analysis on "financial barriers" between the AI boom and Chinese capital is the key new framing. Copper at 6.18 (↓7.1% vs 4W) signals that China's industrial demand impulse remains weak despite policy stimulus.  |  Playbook ✓: Weak commodity demand aligns with consensus on China's sluggish recovery.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The "walled-in wealth" thesis is structurally important. Chinese capital cannot chase the global AI/tech boom due to capital controls, creating a two-speed global liquidity system: US assets absorb global capital inflows while Chinese savings are trapped in domestic property, bonds, and gold. This explains part of gold's run to 4,084 — Chinese retail and institutional investors are structural gold buyers as a capital-control workaround. If these walls crack (Hong Kong Connect expansion, QDII quota increases), the flow implications would be enormous for both Chinese equities and US tech.
