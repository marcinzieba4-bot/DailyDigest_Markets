🇨🇳 China  Mixed  → easing bias

Macro Stance: China remains in a stimulative posture with easing bias. Business Insider flagged this week that "investors should start paying attention to China's economy again," and CXMT's market debut signals Beijing's push for semiconductor self-sufficiency is accelerating.

New / Most Important: CXMT's market debut is the structurally significant event. ChangXin Memory Technologies going public represents the most important Chinese DRAM play and a direct challenge to Samsung/SK Hynix/Micron's oligopoly. This is not just a company IPO — it's a geopolitical statement about China's intent to close the memory chip gap despite US export controls. The copper price at 6.69 (+3.8% 4W) with the Oil/Copper ratio at 12.37 suggests the commodity complex is pricing some Chinese demand recovery, though the Gold/Copper ratio at 698 tempers enthusiasm. US Treasury buybacks potentially redirecting capital toward Asia (per Nation Thailand reporting) is a liquidity flow to watch — if the US is buying back off-the-runs and freeing up dealer balance sheet, some of that freed capital historically finds its way to EM Asia.

Playbook ✓: Copper strength alongside Chinese reopening/stimulus narrative fits consensus.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: CXMT's IPO in the current environment — where US semiconductor export controls were supposed to kneecap Chinese chip ambitions — is a direct contradiction of the "containment working" thesis. If CXMT can scale domestic DRAM production, it changes the margin structure for the entire memory industry and eventually pressures Micron and SK Hynix, particularly in commodity DRAM segments.
