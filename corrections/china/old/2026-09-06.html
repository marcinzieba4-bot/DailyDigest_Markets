🇨🇳 China  Mixed  → easing / stimulus

Macro Stance: China remains in counter-cyclical easing mode, now preparing a massive ~£40bn (~$50bn equivalent) stimulus package targeted at the financial sector amid persistent concerns over sluggish growth. This is notable because it targets financial sector recapitalization rather than infrastructure or consumption — suggesting authorities are worried about bank balance sheet stress.

New / Most Important: The Guardian reported the £40bn financial-sector stimulus this week. This is structurally significant because it signals Beijing is shifting from demand-side stimulus (property, infrastructure) to supply-side financial plumbing repair. This is the playbook Japan should have run in 1992 — recapitalizing the banking system before bad debts metastasize. The question is whether the size is sufficient given the scale of local government financing vehicle (LGFV) exposure and property developer NPLs. J.P. Morgan's "Rush for Resources" piece aligns with China's commodity hedging behavior as authorities attempt to secure physical inputs while financial restructuring occurs domestically.

Playbook ✓: Rolling stimulus, managed depreciation, financial sector support — this is the standard Beijing slow-burn deleveraging script.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Copper at $6.68 (↑1.7%) is resilient, which partially contradicts the China-weak narrative. Either strategic reserve buying or ex-China industrial demand is providing a bid.
