🇨🇳 China  Mixed  → easing bias

Macro Stance: The PBOC maintains an easing bias with ongoing liquidity support. Fiscal stance remains expansionary via local government special bonds and targeted stimulus.

New / Most Important: China's state-owned funds bought nearly $9 billion in shares to support the market — this is the largest reported single-wave intervention since early 2024. The CSRC's Wu explicitly vowed a "stable market" as A-shares rebounded on concerted buying. This is a structural signal: Beijing has effectively put a floor under equities at current levels, converting the stock market into a quasi-policy tool. The question for global allocators is whether this backstop makes China investable (downside truncated) or uninvestable (price discovery destroyed). Historically, SOE buying has provided 2-4 week rallies before fading unless accompanied by genuine economic acceleration. Copper at $6.34 (↓0.5%) suggests the real economy is not confirming any demand recovery, making this a liquidity-driven, not fundamentals-driven, stabilization.

Playbook ✓: State intervention to stabilize equity markets ahead of perceived weakness is fully on-script for Chinese policy.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The $9B buy size is outsized relative to prior interventions — signals more stress underneath than headline A-share levels suggest. Vietnam's VN-Index posted its strongest daily fall in nearly four months, possibly indicating capital outflows from the broader Asia-ex-Japan complex that China's backstop cannot contain.
