Credit Impulse: The credit mechanism remains constrained by asset quality and confidence. AFR reports property-related problem loans mounting at Chinese banks. These developments point to separate questions: how liquidity is managed, whether banks are willing to extend risk, and whether customers want to borrow. A more liquid banking system can still lend cautiously when collateral or repayment prospects are uncertain. Likewise, a household can choose to repay debt even if credit is available. Favor financial institutions and businesses whose earnings do not require an immediate acceleration in leverage. The constructive turn would involve healthier borrower demand and improving loan performance, rather than a label applied to a central-bank operation. [12] [13]
