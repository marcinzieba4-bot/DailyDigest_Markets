🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  Disinflation supports valuation; breadth and cash must follow

Macro Stance: US equities have another reason to welcome disinflation, with a growing need to distinguish benchmark strength from the breadth of operating improvement. Bloomberg reports a large producer-price decline, following CNBC's report of slower consumer inflation. That combination can ease valuation pressure without requiring an immediate collapse in demand. Reuters says technology helped the S&P 500 and Nasdaq reach another record close. Favor profitable growth, but require the company case to survive beyond the market's willingness to pay more for a narrow leadership group. A lower discount rate is useful only when the expected stream of cash remains credible, and stronger benchmarks should not excuse weaker underwriting of customers or balance sheets. [1] [2] [3]

Rates & Curve: The Treasury 2Y is 4.68%, the 10Y 4.24% and the 10Y–2Y spread -44 bps. Over twenty observations the 2Y fell 5 bps, the 10Y fell 12 bps and the spread narrowed 7 bps: the curve is inverted, with bull flattening (both down, 10Y down more). The S&P 500 ended at 5,433.74 index points, VIX at 11.94 index points and DXY at 105.20 index points (US Treasury, prior close 2024-06-13; Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-06-13). Bond relief and a firmer dollar index can coexist because inflation, policy and international risk affect different parts of the market.

New / Most Important: The Fed has kept rates unchanged and projected only one cut this year despite inflation progress, Reuters reports. That leaves a meaningful financing hurdle even as price evidence improves. CNN's account of rising homeownership costs also warns against treating nominal asset wealth as unrestricted spending power. A household or business can own valuable assets and still face a demanding cash budget. Compare self-funded demand with demand that needs cheaper borrowing, and assess the maturity of corporate obligations rather than assuming a rally in long bonds immediately benefits every borrower. [4] [5]

Playbook ✓: Maintain a core of cash-generative businesses, add duration selectively and retain reserves for a reversal in the inflation interpretation. Broaden equity risk if customer demand and earnings participation confirm the benchmark rally. Reduce financing-sensitive exposure if policy stays restrictive while operating cash weakens. A constructive view changes if disinflation becomes a symptom of a sharp demand loss rather than a benign improvement in costs. Conversely, continued price moderation with healthy volumes would justify less defensiveness. The practical trigger is the joint path of prices and cash generation; neither a low volatility index nor another record close can answer that question alone. Compare customer and financing exposures across holdings, because different sector labels can conceal dependence on the same demand or refinancing window.

Credit Impulse: Falling domestic bond yields deserve a careful credit interpretation. The central bank's relending initiative offers a specific route toward housing transactions, but the effectiveness depends on participation, price and the cash received by counterparties. Ask whether money reaches a viable borrower and generates an adequate return. A facility that lengthens survival without improving economics is a different proposition from financing that restores productive private investment. [16] [17]

Require completed transactions and healthier collections before upgrading the weakest balance sheets. A more stable housing system can be constructive without restarting a speculative expansion or restoring every shareholder's earlier return expectations. Compare companies by the affordability of adaptation and the profitability of customers served. New / Most Important: Pricing remains a barrier to converting activity into shareholder value. Playbook ✓: Maintain selective China exposure with attention to who receives cash and who must finance adjustment. Add property-sensitive risk when inventory policies improve transactions, delivery and collections. Add exporters when localization preserves profitable access without excessive capital needs. Reduce capacity-heavy businesses if discounting continues absorbing the benefit of volume growth, and reduce banks or developers if support merely extends unresolved pressure. ⚠ Divergence: targeted housing tools, overseas digital demand and corporate localization offer viable channels, while slow sales, weak pricing and uncertain trade responses prevent those channels from establishing a broad profitable recovery.
