Macro Stance: China has changed the policy conversation faster than it has changed the economy. Business Insider described a blitz of stimulus followed by a balancing game, while The Economist warned against celebrating too soon. Both ideas belong in the same investment view. A stronger official willingness to support assets can reduce the probability of a disorderly financial contraction. It does not instantly produce a willing private borrower, a profitable property project or a household prepared to spend. The appropriate stance is constructive on the policy response and selective on the assets used to express it. Chasing the entire market makes a future implementation disappointment unnecessarily expensive. [15] [16]
