# October 2024 source acquisition

All 23 Monday–Friday editions in October use report-date 00:00 UTC cutoffs, including exchange holidays. The catalogue contains neutral historical search terms. No November factual feed, card, report or spec is an October source.

`tools/expand_capped_news.py --workers 24` acquires the complete global plan: every catalogue weekly window, each report-cutoff prefix of a straddling window, and daily refinements for every capped multi-day response. Each physical URL is fetched once and preserves all topic/category/locale contexts. Prefixes use their own request upper bound; only a daily refinement receives a parent-boundary guard. An earlier edition never consumes a request ending after its cutoff or a daily child derived only from a later-ending parent.

Every response is preserved in `cache/rss_raw/<signature>.xml.gz`. The parsed response in `cache/news/<signature>.json.gz` binds original response bytes and compressed raw bytes by SHA-256. `audit/news_query_plan.json` inventories required physical requests, contexts, response/cache hashes, caps and local paths. `audit/news_provenance/<date>.json.gz` binds every own-date publication object to the actual qualifying physical request signatures.

News and previews have seven-day windows, company results fourteen days, macro releases and policy baselines thirty-five days. A query category is the maximum age. `semantic_category` conservatively tightens result-query hits without result language to seven days and named corporate hits discovered by macro queries to seven or fourteen days. Writers must still enforce meaning for ambiguous headlines. Publication timestamps must be aware, strictly pre-cutoff and within the resulting age window. Rolling/live headlines and malformed RSS are rejected.

`tools/ready_feeds.py` applies conservative release-outcome guards. A preview can establish a pending event, never the result. The calendar distinguishes NBP's October 2 decision from RBNZ's October 9 decision, excludes October 31 China official PMI/BOJ/US PCE/euro inflation outcomes at the final cutoff, and permits eligible Japanese early-local morning releases whose UTC time precedes midnight. Original article bodies and canonical publisher dates were not retrieved or certified. Historical Google selection and publisher RSS timestamps can be misleading, so every authored claim requires own-date headline and release-status review.

Calendar verification search batches in `audit/schedule_verification_searches.json` are editorial timing checks only, outside the author query plan, feed merges and claim evidence. The independent reviewer additionally verified official release calendars and records its evidence in the source acceptance receipt. Their later request boundary cannot contribute news to any October edition.

`tools/finalize_sources.py` rebuilds every candidate offline from the complete plan, stores actual candidate raw/ready/provenance bytes in `work/source_candidates`, compares all 23 candidates before changing any destination, and binds its comparison manifest into `audit/source_freeze.json`. All author IDs remain immutable after READY. A rerun must match every frozen ready feed byte for byte. `tools/expand_capped_news.py --replay` reconstructs unreviewed author IDs offline; finalization reconstructs screened IDs and provenance.

Limits: these are retrieval-time historical headline search responses, not archived original publisher bodies or contemporaneous RSS server captures. A capped one-day response cannot be split further without changing search semantics; residual daily caps are enumerated in coverage. Headline claims must remain within the headline's actual content. Dates, forecasts, published results, share reactions, social attention and measured flows each need their own explicit support.
