Narrative: Technology's next valuation test is whether product capability can become an attractive commercial model after the cost of delivering it. An impressive feature may create customer value while leaving the provider with a difficult pricing decision. The distinction matters when investors already expect a meaningful earnings contribution from AI.
Reported: Reuters, citing the Financial Times, reports Google planning to charge for an AI-powered search offering. [[n0947]] Reuters also reports Microsoft and Quantinuum claiming a quantum-computing breakthrough. [[n1919]] These are different developments: a reported pricing plan and a claimed technical advance, neither equivalent to a completed new profit stream.
EPS Upside Watch: Watch customer willingness to pay, service costs and the investment required to sustain differentiation. A pricing experiment can improve monetization, but it can also reveal that customers value a capability less than its delivery costs imply. Technical progress needs a commercially useful application before it should carry an earnings forecast.
⚡ Breaking Convention: Favor companies whose existing cash generation provides time to test new models. Add when economics become demonstrable at a sensible valuation. Reduce if the share price capitalizes a distant opportunity while implementation costs and customer demand remain insufficiently clear.

⚡ Breaking Convention: Prefer a deliberate instrument choice and a margin of safety that reflects uncertainty. Add only when the claim and valuation can be explained independently.
