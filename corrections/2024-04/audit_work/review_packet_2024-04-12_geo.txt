BLOCK geo:US  Mixed Rebound does not remove the earnings hurdle

[[n2231]] The useful stance is to distinguish a market capable of rebounding from an economy that has delivered enough disinflation to justify rapid easing. Companies still need sales, costs and investment to support earnings with the policy outlook less generous. A rebound can create room for selective participation while leaving the weakest financing and valuation assumptions exposed.

[[n0137]] The ECB has now held rates and signalled a prospective reduction, while the US inflation debate continues to argue for caution. [[n1439]] The divergence matters because the dollar and global capital costs can influence company earnings even where local policy is becoming more supportive. Keep each policy channel attached to its actual economic mechanism. A business might benefit from improved funding conditions while still facing a high required return on new investment.
Playbook ✓: Use the rebound to review the quality of risk being held rather than assume the inflation shock has passed harmlessly. Prefer companies that can turn demand into cash without needing immediate monetary relief. Add duration or more fragile cyclicality when the sustained price and earnings evidence supports it; reduce exposure if guidance reveals that high financing or operating costs are eroding the expected benefit of demand. Reuters' account of later and fewer prospective Fed cuts remains part of the policy debate. [[n2300]] The stance becomes more constructive when profit delivery and renewed disinflation reinforce each other. Until then, a strong session is a chance to discriminate between durable businesses and positions whose main catalyst is the hope of a cheaper cost of money.
