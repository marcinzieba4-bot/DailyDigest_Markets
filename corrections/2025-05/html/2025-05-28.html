Credit Impulse: CNBC reported benchmark lending-rate cuts, and Bloomberg reported banks lowering benchmark rates after central-bank easing. Reuters' account of savers resisting the push to consume is particularly relevant here: lower deposit income does not necessarily make a cautious household spend its principal. It can instead reinforce the desire to save more to reach an income target. The positive test is credit reaching viable private investment and household purchases, rather than remaining a sequence of lower administered prices. Keep bank margins and borrower demand in the same analysis. [14] [15] [11]
