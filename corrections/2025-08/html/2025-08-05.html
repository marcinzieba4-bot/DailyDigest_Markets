🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  earnings rebound / labour caution

Macro Stance: The rebound in equities offers evidence of continuing demand for earnings, rather than a clean rejection of the labour-market warning. Reuters reported the major indexes posting a strong daily recovery after the selloff. CNBC's analysis of the jobs report still described a sharply slowing economy. Both can be true: investors can buy businesses with resilient cash generation while marking down the aggregate growth path. The portfolio should reflect that distinction. Maintain exposure to companies whose current results fund their plans, with less willingness to finance a long period of losses on the assumption that cuts will arrive in time. A single recovery session improves the trading environment without settling the demand outlook. The best evidence for broadening would be operating strength beyond the familiar technology leaders. The equity response has become more constructive without a reversal of the easier rates picture. That gives a quality-growth allocation breathing room, but it should not be confused with confirmation that employment and customer demand have already stabilised. (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2025-08-04; US Treasury, prior close 2025-08-04)

New / Most Important: Broadcom has supplied a specific product test for the AI infrastructure thesis. Reuters reported a networking-chip launch aimed at data-centre connections, while Bloomberg described its role linking smaller facilities. The investment question is whether customer deployment converts that useful function into profitable orders. It is more assessable than claiming every company benefits equally from a larger industry spending budget, but a product launch does not establish the full commercial payoff. The result should be judged through adoption, margin and cash after the investment required to serve it. Prefer an identifiable role in the customer's architecture over a valuation resting chiefly on the industry's eventual size. [3] [4]

Playbook ✓: Retain profitable growth where delivery supports the investment budget, and use a measured amount of duration as a separate response to weaker activity. Add cyclical exposure after orders and labour evidence show that the slowdown can remain manageable. Avoid increasing leverage simply because volatility has eased for a session. Trim positions that require both a strong economy and an immediate rate cut to make the valuation work. A broader recovery in company guidance would justify moving some liquidity into diversified equities; renewed weakness alongside disappointing forecasts would favour keeping the allocation concentrated in financial resilience rather than speculative upside.

🇨🇳 China  Mixed  targeted support / margin repair

Macro Stance: China's investable opportunities continue to sit in targeted improvement rather than a fully repaired domestic cycle. The central bank's pledge to increase liquidity and reduce borrowing costs, reported by SCMP, provides support. Reuters' account of leaders targeting disorderly competition identifies a different route: improving the economics of business already taking place. Those channels should not be collapsed into a single large stimulus claim. Easier financing helps borrowers; less destructive competition can help margins; stronger household demand would improve the top line. A company benefiting from one channel may remain vulnerable through another. Prefer a business with a clear route from policy support to retained cash over an allocation that requires every channel to improve at once. [11] [12]

PMIs & Activity: The recent factory evidence still described cooling demand at home and abroad, according to Reuters. That makes the strength of particular technology or export niches insufficient to establish a broad industrial upturn. A company can gain share in a growing product market while domestic orders remain soft, and a national production figure can look resilient even when pricing erodes profitability. Assess the quality of activity through customer demand and margins. The next convincing signal would be stronger orders that do not depend on ever-larger subsidies or price reductions. Until that appears, use selective exposure to profitable demand rather than assuming the general manufacturing cycle has turned. Activity evidence should lead the growth argument, with asset prices providing a separate assessment of expectations. [13]

Credit Impulse: The new financial-stability committee reported by Reuters offers a mechanism for managing risk, not a measured new lending boom. Credit transmission is still constrained when borrowers are reluctant to invest and collateral values remain uncertain. HSBC's reported China losses and property-related credit pressure at Hong Kong lenders illustrate why a bank may use easier liquidity to protect its position rather than aggressively expand. That can reduce immediate stress without creating the same earnings opportunity as healthy new demand. Prefer borrowers with existing cash generation and lenders with credible provisioning. A larger financing facility becomes more valuable when customers use it for productive activity, rather than simply rolling an impaired exposure forward. Lower borrowing costs are a necessary input for some businesses, not a substitute for a viable customer. [14] [15] [16]

Property: Bloomberg's description of the deepening home-sales slump remains a central restraint on the domestic demand thesis. Falling prices can encourage households to delay a large purchase instead of treating it as a bargain, especially where completion and resale confidence are uncertain. That dynamic changes the effectiveness of a financing incentive. A household may prefer liquidity even if the interest rate becomes more attractive. The investment test is improving transactions, collections and delivery, rather than a rise in the share of a distressed developer. Keep property exposure smaller until customer behaviour improves. Technology and platform positions can have a different earnings engine, so their success should not be used as evidence that housing has repaired itself. [17]
