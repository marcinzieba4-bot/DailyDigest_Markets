Where It Breaks: Treat the purchase as evidence of one buyer's action, rather than proof that house transactions or financing costs have repaired the business. The crowd trade fails when attention fades and no operating improvement takes its place. Keep the exposure small and unleveraged, with an exit tied to the absence of the expected catalyst or a deteriorating cash explanation. A stock associated with housing is not interchangeable with owning a diversified property asset or a profitable mortgage lender.
