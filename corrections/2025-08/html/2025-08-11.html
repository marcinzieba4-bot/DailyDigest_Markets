💻 XLK — Technology  Bullish

Narrative: XLK sector exposure closed at 265.92 and rose 3.94% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2025-08-08). Technology should be owned through firms that can turn spending into customer receipts, rather than through a blanket bet that every AI supplier deserves the same premium. Reported: Reuters reported: “Palantir shares jump as soaring AI demand powers forecast upgrade.” [51] CNBC reported: “Super Micro stock sinks 18% after earnings, outlook disappoint.” [52] The useful earnings question is whether incremental demand improves the economics of the business being bought. EPS Upside Watch: Look for evidence that customers are expanding deployments after the initial project. A repeat order can say more about utility than an ambitious demonstration. Favor profitable software or scarce infrastructure capacity when guidance supports that distinction; trim exposure if the next update replaces actual demand with a larger addressable-market promise.

⚡ Breaking Convention: The counterweight is valuation sensitivity. A higher discount rate can overwhelm a good operating update when the starting share price already assumes an extended period of flawless delivery. Keep the position tied to order conversion and cash, and avoid treating every technology selloff as an automatic invitation to add.
