🇨🇳 China  Mixed  domestic rally / weak transmission

Macro Stance: China's equity opportunity has become more compelling than its demand story. The distinction matters because stimulus expectations can lift shares long before households or private firms decide to borrow. Bloomberg described the rally as having the makings of a durable bull run, while Reuters Breakingviews argued that the growth plan was going cold. [16] [17] Treat that disagreement as the central investment problem. Policy can reduce the penalty for owning selected domestic businesses without repairing every balance sheet exposed to housing. Favor a measured A-share allocation over a broad wager that cheap offshore internet stocks must catch up. The bullish case is a reallocation toward equities and a stabilization of competitive intensity; the bearish case is that this remains a financial-market recovery with insufficient cash-flow support. Both can operate at the same time.

PMIs & Activity: July's official manufacturing PMI edged down, with the National Bureau of Statistics citing seasonal factors and heavy rain, according to Global Times. [18] Reuters also reported that July factory-output and retail-sales growth missed forecasts. [19] Weather can explain some disruption, but the combination leaves demand too soft to dismiss the weakness as a temporary factory issue. The required confirmation is broader: improving new orders, more consistent consumer purchases and less dependence on selected promotional categories. July activity should therefore anchor earnings assumptions even when equity prices look through it. Do not read a local share-market rally as a substitute for a manufacturing recovery. The most attractive companies are those able to win share or sell abroad without needing a simultaneous turn in the household cycle.

Credit Impulse: The July bank-loan contraction is the harder constraint. Reuters described the first such contraction in 20 years, while the WSJ reported an unexpected decline in new yuan lending. [20] [21] Credit supply and credit demand are different things: a bank can be willing to lend while a business postpones expansion and a household protects cash. Reuters earlier in August reported a consumption loan-interest subsidy, a policy aimed at easing that transmission problem. [22] It can help at the margin, but a subsidy does not itself establish durable spending intent. Look for a recovery in borrowing alongside stronger orders rather than treating cheaper financing as an earnings event. For allocation, that favors established cash generators and targeted policy beneficiaries over highly levered businesses whose investment case requires a rapid reopening of credit demand.

Hong Kong adds a separate financing risk: Reuters highlighted mounting debt-repayment pressure in its property sector. [26] Avoid collapsing those markets into a single rebound trade. Housing stabilization can support consumption and bank confidence; a speculative rally in property equities need not establish either.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: The policy shift worth following is the attempt to restrain destructive price competition.
