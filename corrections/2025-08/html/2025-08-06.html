🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  services slowing / earnings selective

Macro Stance: The service economy has added to the demand warning without delivering a simple recession verdict. The July ISM services index was 50.1, according to the institute's release distributed by PR Newswire; the WSJ described activity slowing. That reading sits close to the boundary between expansion and contraction, so the useful investment message is limited momentum rather than a claim that every business is shrinking. Reuters reported the trade gap narrowing while tariffs pressured the service sector. A stronger accounting contribution from trade and a softer experience among customers can coexist. Prefer businesses able to earn through moderate demand, with less confidence in a recovery that requires the next policy move to solve both costs and sales at once. [1] [2] [3]

That divergence offers more information than a blanket risk-on or risk-off label. Easier longer-term yields coexist with a market still distinguishing earnings durability, valuation and sector exposure. (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2025-08-05; US Treasury, prior close 2025-08-05)

New / Most Important: The earnings distinction within AI has become sharper. Reuters reported Palantir shares rising after stronger demand supported a forecast upgrade, while AMD's data-centre results disappointed and Super Micro's quarterly results underwhelmed. A large industry budget does not guarantee the same shareholder economics at every layer. Software, processors and servers have different competitive, financing and margin structures. The portfolio should reflect those differences rather than repeatedly adding the next associated ticker. The proper question is whether the company is capturing enough of the customer's expenditure to support its own valuation and investment plan. A strong theme can remain intact while several expressions of it need to be reduced. [4] [5] [6]

Playbook ✓: Retain selected companies with a credible path from demand to retained cash, alongside a measured high-quality duration allocation. Add broader cyclicals only when services and customer orders stabilise rather than merely when yields fall. Reduce a speculative supplier if the customer-spending story remains strong but its own results cannot convert that demand into an adequate return. Cash remains useful when price discovery around earnings is abrupt. The constructive change would be broader operating strength with fewer concessions required to generate revenue; a further weakening of service activity and guidance would instead favour financial resilience and a smaller aggregate risk budget.

🇨🇳 China  Mixed  policy optionality / operating proof

Macro Stance: China's opportunity remains a combination of targeted support and selective business improvement. The central bank's pledge to boost liquidity and reduce borrowing costs, reported by SCMP, can improve financing. The risk is assigning the full benefit before households and companies have a viable reason to use it. A funded investment is not automatically a profitable one, and an economy can have abundant capacity while customer demand remains uneven. Prefer companies with an identifiable product advantage and customers able to pay, rather than every asset associated with an easing programme. The investment stance can be constructive in selected businesses without becoming an unqualified call that the domestic cycle has repaired itself. [10]

It does not establish that all Chinese industry has the same utilisation problem, but it provides an important investment test: can the owner use an asset enough to earn an adequate return after financing and maintenance? Read activity alongside its profitability. A policy-supported project can advance a strategic objective while delivering a less attractive near-term return to a minority shareholder. [11]

Credit Impulse: The new financial-stability committee, reported by Reuters, provides another mechanism for coordinating risk management. A governance change should not be described as a measured acceleration in lending or private borrowing appetite. Financing becomes most useful when a customer has profitable demand to serve; otherwise it can postpone a loss or sustain capacity that earns too little. Caixin reported Ant Consumer Finance tapping the bond market, which identifies a particular funding transaction rather than a country-wide consumer-credit boom. Assess the borrower, the use of proceeds and the economics of the underlying customer relationship.
