🇨🇳 China  Bearish  liquidity support / demand repair pending

Macro Stance: China remains a policy-supported equity market with a demand problem underneath it. The central bank vowed to increase liquidity and reduce borrowing costs, according to the South China Morning Post. That can support asset prices and refinancing before it changes household willingness to spend or businesses' appetite to invest. The bearish macro stance is therefore compatible with tactical gains in internet platforms and selected technology suppliers. The mistake is to turn a funding commitment into evidence that the property cycle has healed. Keep the distinction between financial conditions and end demand at the centre of sizing. [15]

[16] [17] [18] [19] Activity needs to become less dependent on production incentives and more dependent on customers accepting higher volumes at viable prices. Favour evidence of profitable sales and repeat purchasing over slogans about removing excess competition. The useful confirmation would be broader order strength rather than another isolated technology showcase. [20]

Credit Impulse: The most useful credit development is the attempt to lower financing friction, rather than an assumption that borrowers have already responded. Credit supply becomes economically powerful when balance-sheet repair and confidence allow recipients to use it. Until then, financial stability can improve faster than nominal growth. Treat cheaper money as an enabling condition and demand as the deciding condition. [22]
