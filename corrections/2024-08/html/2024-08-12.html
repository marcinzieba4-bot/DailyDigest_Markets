🌍 Geographic Macro Overview

🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  stabilisation / inflation test

Macro Stance: The market has regained its footing faster than investors can establish whether the growth scare was excessive. Reuters reports the S&P 500 ending higher and nearly flat for the week despite the steep opening sell-off. [1] A recovery from the steep sell-off is welcome, but it does not make the earnings outlook independent of the consumer or labour market. Keep the distinction between an investable stabilisation and a confirmed reacceleration. The first supports selective additions to sound businesses; the second would justify a broader cyclical allocation. For now, the combination favours quality cash generation with some protection against disappointing activity.

Rates & Curve: The official 2Y Treasury yield closed at 4.05% and the 10Y at 3.94%, leaving the 10Y−2Y spread at -11 bps. Over twenty observations the 2Y fell 40 bps and the 10Y fell 24 bps: the curve remains inverted, with bull steepening (both down, 2Y down more). Over five observations, however, the 2Y rose 17 bps and the 10Y rose 14 bps (US Treasury, prior close 2024-08-09). The shorter comparison warns against assuming a one-way rally in bonds. Equities can recover while yields regain some ground as immediate recession fears ease. The S&P 500 finished at 5,344.16 index points, down 4.83% over twenty sessions, while VIX fell to 20.37 index points and DXY stood at 103.14 index points (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-08-09). Volatility is lower, but the price history still describes a market rebuilding confidence. Do not confuse a reduction in market volatility with an unlimited willingness to pay for uncertain future profits.

New / Most Important: Attention now turns to inflation evidence: CNBC explicitly looks ahead to key inflation data, while Reuters' week-ahead assessment describes investors braced for further turbulence. [2] [3] The useful scenario is the interaction of prices and activity, rather than an isolated wish for a soft print. Inflation easing without a sharp demand deterioration would give policy more room and protect company revenue. A softer price reading caused by a much weaker customer would have a more mixed equity implication. Reuters' report of lower weekly jobless claims helps explain the immediate calming of fears, without settling that broader combination. [4]

Playbook ✓: Retain profitable equity exposure and a measured duration hedge; use the next economic evidence to decide whether to broaden. Add cyclical positions when demand stabilises alongside disinflation. If price pressure persists while activity weakens, favour financing certainty and defensive cash flows. A more constructive market can reward patience without requiring the portfolio to recover all its previous leverage. Rebalance after large relative moves, and judge a company by the earnings path that remains affordable at the new price.

[7] Temporary demand and composition effects can complicate an inflation reading without determining the underlying policy trend. Keep the earnings implication specific: a travel or event beneficiary may gain while another consumer business sees no comparable lift. Do not extrapolate a temporary pricing opportunity into a permanent margin.

Bloomberg.com's report of a stronger rise in German industrial output provides a useful contrary signal. [8] The question is whether production strength becomes sustained customer demand and cash generation.

Playbook ✓: Prefer domestic cash generators and industrial franchises that can fund their own transition. Add rate-sensitive exposure if inflation permits easing and order quality improves. Keep exporters sized for demand and currency uncertainty. Reduce the cyclical component if financing relief coincides with weaker sales rather than a stable operating base. A policy tailwind is most valuable when it reaches a business capable of earning an adequate return on the capital it already employs.

🇨🇳 China  Bearish  targeted support / demand question

Macro Stance: A firmer inflation reading does not yet resolve China's demand problem. Reuters attributes stronger consumer prices to weather disruption while stressing that domestic demand remains soft. [9] Bloomberg similarly pairs a pickup in inflation with continuing demand concerns. [10] The distinction matters for equity selection. A supply-related rise in prices can reduce household purchasing power without giving a broad set of companies better volumes or margins. The broad stance remains cautious, with room for businesses whose commercial advantage can work independently of a general reflation trade. Prefer a demonstrated route from customer demand to cash rather than buying an entire market on the assumption that a higher inflation number must mean stronger growth.

PMIs & Activity: Reuters' factory-PMI assessment continues to highlight weaknesses inside the export economy. [11] The relevant operating test is whether manufacturers can obtain profitable repeat orders, not whether they can keep producing. A company may increase shipments by accepting lower prices, while working capital and capacity absorb much of the benefit. Reuters' commentary on discounted solar modules reaching record exports illustrates that volume and pricing can move in different directions. [12] This is not an argument that every exporter is unattractive. It is a reason to identify a product, cost or distribution advantage capable of defending a return. An activity recovery becomes more compelling when it broadens beyond inventories and price concessions into better margins and customer confidence.

Credit Impulse: Policy is targeted rather than a guarantee of broad credit demand. Reuters reports the central bank planning to extend its low-carbon lending tool through the end of 2027. [13] The extension can support a particular channel of financing, but it does not establish how many economically attractive projects will use it. Reuters' preview says July bank lending is expected to dip, keeping attention on support. [14] That remains a forecast for the lending release, not the completed figure. Lower borrowing costs and available liquidity matter most when households and firms see a reason to borrow. If expected returns remain weak, financing can accumulate in defensive assets instead of productive investment. Examine demand for credit and the quality of the assets created, not merely the size of a policy facility.

Property: Property still demands a distinction between affluent buyers and a broad household recovery. The South China Morning Post reports Shanghai millionaires upgrading to premium homes despite the market slump. [15] A strong top-end segment is real commercial activity, but it cannot establish that the typical buyer has regained confidence. The same outlet's economist interview argues that oversupply remains unresolved. [16] A durable improvement would show up in transactions, completion and funding confidence across a wider set of households and projects. Until then, a developer can achieve isolated sales while still facing an awkward inventory and debt structure. Prefer balance-sheet visibility and cash collection over using an eye-catching luxury transaction as a proxy for the whole market.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: External demand brings opportunities and a growing need to manage market access. The South China Morning Post reports additional ASEAN trade barriers against Chinese products amid concern about overdependence. [17] CNBC reports that a changing yuan is reducing the profits of exporters that had enjoyed currency weakness. [18] These developments affect different parts of the export equation: the first concerns access, the second the margin earned on a sale. A company can respond with localisation, product differentiation or price adjustments, but each involves a cost. Avoid assuming a strong shipment trend translates directly into stronger shareholder returns. The cleaner opportunities have a route to customers that does not require continually surrendering price or committing more capital merely to preserve the same economics.

Markets & Flows: The Hang Seng closed at 17,090.23 index points, up 1.17% in the prior session but down 6.58% over twenty sessions. The Shanghai Composite stood at 2,862.19 index points, down 3.67% over twenty sessions. KWEB closed at USD 26.35, up 2.81% over five sessions and down 8.54% over twenty; ASHR stood at USD 23.33, down 2.43% over twenty sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-08-09). The contrast does not establish foreign flows, but it does show why a single China-beta description is inadequate. Internet franchises, mainland financial assets and offshore cyclicals face different commercial and valuation drivers. A higher offshore index can reflect a better global appetite for risk without proving a change in household credit demand.

New / Most Important: The authorities are paying attention to the bond market as well as the economy. Reuters reports instructions to some brokerages to check compliance in bond trading. [19] The South China Morning Post reports rural banks being named and shamed as the bond rally takes a toll. [20] Defensive demand for financial assets can itself attract a policy response, so a momentum position needs to account for regulatory risk. Meanwhile, Reuters reports EVs and hybrids accounting for half of July vehicle sales. [21] Product adoption can advance within a weak economy. The stock-selection task is to identify who retains the profit from that transition, rather than treating adoption as proof that all competitors can earn attractive margins.

Playbook ✓: Keep broad domestic China exposure restrained and favour cash-generative businesses with a credible product advantage. Add cyclical risk when property transactions, borrower confidence and profitable orders improve together. For targeted green investment, require project economics and cash collection to support the policy opportunity. Reduce exposure if a price rebound depends on easing alone while excess capacity continues to weaken returns. Revisit the cautious stance when demand repair becomes visible beyond a narrow premium or technology niche. ⚠ Divergence: targeted credit support and rapid adoption in selected products offer a path to improvement, while soft domestic demand and unresolved property supply still limit the case for broad reflation.

Playbook ✓: Own a measured mix of domestic cash generation and exporters with an operating advantage that survives currency volatility. Add if calmer funding and resilient company guidance reinforce each other. Reduce risk if another currency move overwhelms profit visibility or if policy communication again forces a rapid repricing. Keep the flexibility to separate a temporary market dislocation from a deterioration in a business's competitive position.

[41] That establishes activity and interest, not guaranteed returns. The opportunity can be attractive for a capable supplier while still requiring smaller position sizes and more working-capital flexibility than an equivalent domestic contract. New / Most Important: Affordability is increasingly visible in the form of the product, not just the headline selling price. Bankier.pl reports developers reducing apartment sizes. In digital consumption, Wiadomości Handlowe reports Kaufland entering marketplace competition dominated by Allegro. [44] A competitive offering can improve customer value while requiring incumbents to defend service quality and marketing efficiency. Prefer businesses whose value proposition survives comparison shopping without a continual increase in acquisition spending.

Playbook ✓: Maintain selective exposure to domestic demand, with preference for recurring cash generation and clear capital allocation. In banks, require sustainable earnings after provisions and a distribution policy consistent with the balance sheet. In apparel and energy, examine the cost and financing of resilience rather than treating a project or technology announcement as realised value. Add broader cyclical risk if wage-supported demand translates into profitable volumes while inflation pressure moderates. Reduce it if higher essential bills and funding costs compress the cash available for discretionary purchases. Keep geopolitical exposure within a risk budget that can tolerate interruption. ⚠ Divergence: wage growth and corporate investment provide real support, while depositors' purchasing-power squeeze and price-sensitive housing demand argue against assuming a uniformly strong consumer.
