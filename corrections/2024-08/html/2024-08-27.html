🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  Easing is clearer than the landing

Macro Stance: The investment question has shifted from whether the Federal Reserve will acknowledge room to ease toward whether easing can preserve demand. Reuters reports Powell saying the time has come to cut rates, while its labor-market reporting says job growth in the year through March was much lower than estimated. [1] [2] The revision concerns an earlier employment interval; it does not describe a sudden disappearance of current jobs. Nevertheless, it weakens the argument that restrictive policy carries little cost. Favor businesses whose customers can keep buying through a slower hiring environment. A lower discount rate supports valuation, but it cannot replace the sales required to earn that valuation. This distinction matters more after the recovery in the broad equity benchmark. The distinction also separates a strong balance sheet from a strong customer: a company may refinance comfortably while its buyers postpone purchases. Prefer evidence of both financing resilience and sustained end demand before interpreting lower yields as a broad earnings upgrade.

Rates & Curve: The 2Y Treasury yield is 3.91% and the 10Y 3.82%, leaving the 10Y–2Y spread at -9 bps (US Treasury, prior close 2024-08-26). Over twenty observations the 2Y fell 45 bps and the 10Y 35 bps; the spread widened 10 bps. The curve remains inverted, with bull steepening (both down, 2Y down more). The five-observation changes, -15 bps for the 2Y and -4 bps for the 10Y, show that nearer policy expectations have continued doing more of the work. That is constructive for refinancing prospects, although borrowers still need lenders willing to extend credit and cash flows adequate to service it. Easing expectations have already produced meaningful price support for duration.

New / Most Important: The S&P 500 closed at 5,616.84 index points, down 0.32% in one session, up 0.15% over five and 2.81% over twenty. VIX was 16.15 index points, while DXY was 100.85 index points, down 3.55% over twenty sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-08-26). Reuters separately reports the dollar falling after Powell greenlighted September easing. [3] Currency direction therefore fits the policy narrative without proving that all earnings exposures benefit equally. Importers, exporters and firms buying foreign inputs have different sensitivities. The latest semiconductor weakness also shows that a supportive macro message does not remove company-event risk.

Playbook ✓: Retain measured Treasury duration and equity exposure backed by identifiable customer demand. Add cyclical risk when operating updates confirm that easier financing is translating into purchases, rather than merely a higher market multiple. July manufacturing PMI was 46.8 index points against services at 51.4 index points, according to the respective ISM releases, so the activity picture still contains a clear sector split. [4] [5] Stronger orders and steadier hiring would support broadening beyond defensive earnings. Further employment weakness accompanied by deteriorating sales would favor duration over an indiscriminate small-company recovery basket.

The favorable scenario is that lower financing costs revive sound borrowing sufficiently to offset the margin pressure.
