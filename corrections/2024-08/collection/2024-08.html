The historical boundary

Each edition uses its own dated information before 00:00 UTC and completed prior market sessions. News-index timestamps are screened for release-date conflicts; original publisher vintages are not independently archived. Current-vintage market histories may contain later vendor revisions. Historical share units are mechanically restored using split events. Later outcomes are excluded from the analysis.

Report dates: 1–30 August 2024. All Monday–Friday dates receive editions, including exchange holidays. No weekend editions. Price changes exclude dividends.
