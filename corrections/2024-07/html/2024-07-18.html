[10] The luxury discussion reinforces a practical distinction within China: a large consumer market can remain an unattractive near-term earnings assumption for brands requiring discretionary willingness to spend. A selective portfolio should compare value-oriented consumption, premium goods, exports and productive investment rather than assign each the same recovery timetable. The central question is whether policy and income confidence generate demand that merchants can serve at a sustainable margin.

Credit Impulse: ing think reports What to expect from China’s monetary policy framework reforms. [13] Monetary-framework reform is a policy-design question, not a completed private-credit recovery. [14] The bond rally and policymaker response illustrate why cheaper government financing should not be read as a universal increase in risk appetite. Examine the destination and return on capital, rather than treat credit volume or bond-price strength as sufficient proof of demand.
