🇺🇸 United States  Bullish  Disinflation opens a broader earnings test

Macro Stance: The US market is repricing who benefits from disinflation, rather than simply buying every asset with duration. Reuters reported consumer prices falling in June, accompanied by a weaker dollar and stronger yen; the Wall Street Journal said milder inflation widened the opening for a September rate cut. [1] [2] That is a more supportive policy setup, with the decision still ahead. The investment opportunity now includes businesses whose financing burden can ease, but their earnings need to survive slower nominal demand. Keep a constructive allocation while shifting some attention from the most expensive growth stories to companies with credible operating improvement and manageable debt. Axios summarized Powell as recognizing that inflation was no longer the only economic risk. [3] Softer prices improve the policy balance, but the earnings test remains whether customer demand holds as financing relief develops. That distinction favors staged additions over a leveraged assumption of uninterrupted broadening.

Rates & Curve: The 2Y yield is 4.50% and the 10Y is 4.20%, leaving the 10Y–2Y spread at -30 bps. Across twenty observations, the 2Y declined 31 bps and the 10Y declined 19 bps; the spread increased 12 bps. The curve remains inverted, with bull steepening (both down, 2Y down more) describing the move (US Treasury, prior close 2024-07-11). Easier expected policy is helping the front end, but inversion still reflects a meaningful difference between current short-rate conditions and the longer-term outlook. The allocation should benefit from lower financing pressure without requiring a return to exceptionally cheap capital. Fiscal compensation and future inflation can still constrain the long end.

New / Most Important: The price contrast is sharp. The Russell 2000 closed at 2,125.04 index points, up +3.57% in the session, while the Nasdaq 100 closed at 20,211.36 index points, down -2.24%. The S&P 500 stood at 5,584.54 index points, down -0.88%; VIX was 12.92 index points and DXY 104.44 index points (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-07-11). This is evidence of relative price leadership changing, not a measured transfer of a stated amount of capital. TradingView asked whether the small-cap breakout could last, making persistence the relevant next test. [4] A single session cannot establish an enduring broadening of earnings.

Playbook ✓: Add rate-sensitive quality in stages, while retaining profitable technology with defensible valuations. The trigger for further broadening is improving guidance and financing alongside continued disinflation. Reduce small-cap or cyclical exposure if lower yields fail to stabilize revenue and credit quality. Reuters reported Goolsbee viewing the economy as back on track toward the inflation goal, a policymaker assessment rather than a completed rate decision. [5] The call becomes more cautious if pricing weakness erodes margins faster than lower borrowing costs help. Demand evidence and balance sheets should decide which laggards deserve capital.

🇨🇳 China  Mixed  Market support needs profitable demand

Macro Stance: China's equity rebound should be judged against a difficult domestic profit environment. Reuters reported an e-commerce price war leaving sellers struggling to make ends meet. [11] That is a direct warning about merchant economics, not merely a theoretical deflation argument. Lower consumer prices can improve affordability while weakening the supplier's ability to invest, hire or service debt. The policy challenge is to support demand and productive returns simultaneously. Broad exposure remains conditional, with better opportunities in companies that can retain a competitive advantage without relying on permanent price cuts. A cheap product is not automatically a profitable business, and a stronger market session does not settle that distinction.

PMIs & Activity: The macro pricing evidence is consistent with those competitive pressures. Reuters reported June consumer inflation missing forecasts amid anemic demand. [12] Bloomberg's account of inexpensive batteries identifies the other side of falling prices: cheaper inputs can enable new uses and reduce adoption barriers. [13] The investment comparison is therefore upstream versus downstream and unit volume versus retained margin. A buyer of components may benefit while a manufacturer faces excess capacity. Watch whether demand expands enough to offset lower prices, and whether working capital and investment absorb the cash from higher volumes. Industrial competitiveness creates value most reliably when a company retains some of the productivity benefit rather than passing all of it to customers. The cash cycle matters as well. A merchant discounting inventory may recover some working capital while earning less margin; a supplier extending payment terms may preserve sales while weakening collections. Those responses can temporarily support activity without creating the conditions for a sustained increase in private investment.

Credit Impulse: Reuters explained the central bank's new cash-management tool, while the South China Morning Post described more direct control over interest rates. [14] [15] These developments concern policy implementation and money-market conditions. They should not be treated as a released increase in private lending. Reuters' poll expected stronger June yuan loans under supportive policy, with the actual result still pending. [16] Even a favorable total would need to be examined for refinancing versus new productive spending. The useful trigger is credit reaching willing borrowers and producing collectible sales. Easier money-market control can improve stability before it changes private confidence, so the transmission should be evaluated in stages rather than assumed complete.

Property: Property continues to require separate tests for transactions, prices and developer finance. Reuters reported a liquidation petition against Guangzhou R&F in Hong Kong. [17] That is legal pressure, not a final liquidation outcome or known creditor recovery. The South China Morning Post described demand for Zhongshan apartments from Hong Kong buyers following the bridge connection. Broad housing confidence also depends on completion and collateral, so isolated hotspots should not be extrapolated into a uniform construction upswing.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Trade costs are now being passed into customer prices in at least one visible case. Reuters reported Tesla increasing European Model 3 prices because of tariffs on China-made vehicles. [19] The price response can preserve some margin while risking demand, and it does not establish that every manufacturer will use the same approach. Reuters also reported US and Mexican measures to prevent Chinese tariff circumvention. [20] That complicates a strategy based solely on moving production or routing goods through another market. Manufacturers need to evaluate rules, local capacity and end demand together. Preserving overseas access can require investment that reduces near-term cash returns even where export volumes remain resilient.

New / Most Important: Industrial and consumer technology offer selective opportunities if products solve a commercially useful problem. CNBC reported Samsung developing AI features specifically for China as it sought to regain market position. [22] This is a localization strategy, not a completed market-share recovery or known revenue gain. The Wall Street Journal described a new planning effort intended to revive a flagging economy. [23] Plans can alter investment priorities, but execution and returns remain the investment test. Businesses should be assessed on customer adoption and cash generation, rather than their alignment with a broad policy slogan. A technology capability is most valuable when it helps a firm defend pricing or create paid demand in a competitive market.

Playbook ✓: Keep broad China exposure modest and favor cash-generative franchises or adaptable producers. Add domestic cyclicals when private borrowing, company collections and profitable demand improve together. Reduce exposure if price wars spread or tariff costs erode margins faster than firms can adapt. A legal property event should be evaluated on its own terms, while local transaction strength remains local until broader evidence confirms it. ⚠ Divergence: equities are rebounding and policy tools are more active, while merchant price wars, weak consumer inflation, developer stress and trade-cost pass-through still challenge the idea that support measures have become a durable economy-wide profit recovery.
