Keep the distinction between the supplier and its customer visible when sizing AI exposure. Tight supply can support the seller while increasing the buyer's capital bill. The combined theme becomes more robust only when both sides demonstrate commercially useful returns, rather than when one company's forecast is projected across the entire chain.

The Wall Street Journal reports fresh pledges to shore up a flagging economy, while Reuters describes a turn toward consumer stimulus. Consumer support could help businesses with useful products and manageable costs, while a broad industrial expansion could worsen price competition in already crowded markets.
