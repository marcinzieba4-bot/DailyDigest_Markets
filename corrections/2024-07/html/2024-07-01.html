🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  Disinflation earns patience, not a blank cheque

Macro Stance: The US case is improving inflation with a less comfortable margin for earnings disappointment. Reuters reports that May inflation cooled, improving hopes for a Federal Reserve cut, while its account of the June policy meeting says officials still envisaged only one cut this year. The combination supports selective duration exposure; it does not establish an imminent easing cycle. [1] [2] Companies have to bridge the interval between relief in the inflation rate and relief in household financing costs. That makes a business with controllable expenses more attractive than a business whose recovery requires consumers suddenly to become less price sensitive.

Rates & Curve: The Treasury curve remains inverted: the two-year yield is 4.71%, the ten-year 4.36%, and the ten-year minus two-year spread -35 bps. Over twenty observations from 2024-05-30, the two-year declined 21 bps and the ten-year 19 bps, a bull steepening with both down and the two-year down more; the spread widened 2 bps. The latest five-observation ten-year increase is 11 bps, however, against a 1 bps two-year increase (US Treasury, prior close 2024-06-28). This is a useful distinction: the medium horizon offers discount-rate relief, while the shorter horizon warns against assuming every favourable inflation release must lower long financing costs.

New / Most Important: CNBC explicitly reports a rise in the ten-year yield after fresh inflation and consumer data. That is the conflicting signal to the disinflation trade, rather than a reason to disregard the release. [3] The S&P 500 closed at 5,460.48 index points, up 4.30% over twenty sessions, while VIX finished at 12.44 index points and DXY at 105.87 index points, up 1.10% over twenty sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-06-28). Equities have already enjoyed substantial relief without a correspondingly weak dollar. A quiet volatility reading describes option pricing; it cannot establish that investors have stopped hedging or that cash is entering equities.

Playbook ✓: Keep profitable growth exposure, but add cyclical risk only when operating evidence confirms the benefit of lower inflation. Reuters reports slumping new-home sales and elevated supply, while the Wall Street Journal describes a frozen housing market. [4] [5] Those are reasons to distinguish an eventual financing catalyst from present demand. Prefer firms that can improve cash generation through execution, and use renewed long-yield pressure as a reason to trim the most valuation-sensitive additions. The call becomes more constructive if household-facing guidance stabilises while yields absorb good inflation news without rising further; it becomes more defensive if weak sales force discounting that erodes the very earnings expected to justify elevated equity prices.

🇨🇳 China  Bearish  Activity needs demand, not another slogan

Macro Stance: China's problem is the gap between visible policy effort and evidence that private demand wants to accelerate. Reuters reports renewed contraction in June factory activity and slower services, while the South China Morning Post says calls for major measures coexist with doubts rooted in earlier reforms. [13] [14] The investment conclusion is cautious on broad recovery beta, with room for businesses supported by identifiable orders or distributions. A policy announcement can change a financing constraint; it cannot by itself establish that households or companies want to spend. The distinction matters because different parts of the market can respond to reassurance long before their customers do.

PMIs & Activity: The factory-services combination is more troublesome than an isolated manufacturing disappointment. Weaker factories could be explained by an inventory adjustment while services absorbed household spending; slower services makes that offset less dependable. Reuters separately reports that industrial-profit growth slowed sharply in May. [15] That places the focus on the earnings conversion of activity, not just the amount of production. China's exports rose solidly while slower imports tempered the outlook, according to Reuters' earlier macro report. [16] External demand can sustain selected producers even when domestic demand is soft. It is therefore inappropriate to extrapolate the factory signal uniformly across exporters, local consumer businesses and regulated utilities.

Credit Impulse: The credit question is whether attractive financing terms can produce new productive commitments rather than greater appetite for safety. Reuters reports money moving into Chinese bonds despite central-bank warnings about the risks; Bloomberg reports the long sovereign yield reaching the historical low comparison described in its headline on growth worries. [17] [18] These are actual descriptions of demand for bonds, but they do not establish broad credit creation or household borrowing. A lower discount rate helps existing assets before it necessarily produces new spending. Watch whether policy improves the willingness of borrowers to invest and whether lenders can fund credible projects without merely refinancing weak demand. Until then, bond strength can coexist with disappointing operating activity.

Property: Beijing has become the last major mainland city to ease housing rules, according to the South China Morning Post, while the Guardian's macro report describes new-home prices falling at the fastest pace in nearly a decade. [19] [20] Relaxing access addresses a transaction barrier; falling prices influence expectations and the attractiveness of waiting. The immediate transmission therefore depends on confidence as well as eligibility. Developers, home-related consumption and local revenues need sustained transactions rather than an announcement-driven bounce. Bloomberg's report of rich buyers spending on Shanghai property offers a meaningful counterexample to blanket pessimism, but it does not demonstrate a broad household recovery. [21] An affluent segment can clear while the wider market still needs confidence in delivery, affordability and future resale value.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Trade is an offset with its own political ceiling. CNBC reports the premier defending national competitiveness as tensions simmer, and Reuters says Indian ministries are discussing rising Chinese steel imports. [22] [23] An exporter may gain share by offering competitive prices while that very success invites a policy response abroad. Avoid treating external volume as an unconstrained replacement for domestic consumption. Reuters also reports a new rural collective law protecting farmers' land rights; that establishes a legal-policy development, not an immediate increase in spending or a quantified wealth effect. [24] The useful trigger is whether reforms reduce a concrete barrier to consumption or investment, rather than the breadth of their language.

Markets & Flows: The Hang Seng closed at 17,718.61 index points, down 2.81% over twenty sessions and 1.72% over five sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2024-06-28). That is compatible with cautious pricing, but a decline alone cannot prove foreign withdrawals. The Wall Street Journal reports that owning less exciting Chinese stocks has paid dividends while the wider market struggles. [25] This supports examining income resilience separately from recovery enthusiasm. It does not establish that every high-distribution company is safe. A durable payment needs cash generation, sensible reinvestment and a balance sheet that can withstand weaker demand; a headline yield without those supports would simply move the risk into a different wrapper.

New / Most Important: Consumer competition is the other important challenge. Reuters says the retail outlook dimmed after the midyear shopping festival disappointed, while the Wall Street Journal distinguishes sales at the shopping event from profits. [26] [27] The mechanism is straightforward: promotions can protect traffic while transferring the benefit to customers rather than shareholders. Product innovation remains possible; the South China Morning Post reports an AI search-and-answer feature at Zhihu and new game approvals including Tencent's Roco Kingdom title. [28] [29] Those are company and regulatory catalysts, not proof of economy-wide demand. Prefer identifiable monetisation opportunities over a broad claim that digital activity can immediately compensate for property weakness.

Playbook ✓: Keep broad China exposure restrained and build a watchlist around improving realised demand, not simply cheaper financing. The Straits Times reports the key political meeting scheduled for July 15 to 18; treat that as a pending policy event with uncertain content. [30] A more constructive stance would require measures that improve household incentives and evidence that transaction activity converts into profitable sales. Reduce exposure if stimulus language expands while industrial profitability and consumer margins continue to weaken. ⚠ Divergence: housing access is easing and selected consumer platforms have concrete catalysts, yet the latest factory and services account argues that those improvements have not established a broad demand recovery.
