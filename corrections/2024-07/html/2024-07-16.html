🇨🇳 China  Mixed  Domestic demand becomes decisive

Macro Stance: Reuters reports: China's economy falters, raises pressure for more stimulus. [10] The balance of risk has moved toward the quality of domestic demand. Strong external shipments can support factories while households remain cautious and property stress continues to restrict confidence. A policy meeting can attract attention without changing the immediate earnings arithmetic. The investment case should begin with what a company sells, who pays for it and whether the transaction generates cash, rather than assume a broad macro rescue lifts every security equally.

PMIs & Activity: The Guardian reports: China’s economic growth slows amid weak retail spending. [11] Weak retail spending is particularly relevant because it connects household confidence to corporate pricing. Consumers delaying discretionary purchases can force retailers to compete more aggressively, while fixed costs make the resulting margin damage larger than the sales slowdown alone suggests. Services, manufactured exports and domestic durables can therefore move in different directions. Reuters reports: China June consumer inflation misses forecasts amid anaemic demand. [12] Soft consumer inflation is consistent with limited pricing power, although its earnings impact varies by input costs and business model. Look for a recovery in profitable demand, not simply greater transaction counts achieved through discounts.

Credit Impulse: WSJ reports: Trillions in Hidden Debt Drove China’s Growth. Now It Threatens Its Future.. [13] Hidden debt complicates the route from easier financial conditions to better returns on investment. Replacing expensive liabilities can improve cash interest but may leave the original asset unable to earn its cost of capital. New lending can refinance old obligations rather than finance fresh private demand. Distinguish debt rollover, working capital and productive investment before making a broad credit-recovery call. Banks may be willing or encouraged to lend while households and businesses remain reluctant to increase leverage. The more convincing improvement would combine access to finance with commercial projects that can repay it from operating cash, reducing dependence on continuing policy support.

Property: Reuters reports: China tries to hit more birds with one stone in property rescue push. [14] A property rescue needs several links to work: assets must be deliverable, buyers must trust delivery, and financing must reach projects with viable economics. [15] Falling prices can encourage households to wait, limiting the effect of cheaper borrowing. Developer equity, supplier receivables and completed homes also have different claims on the same recovery. A policy that improves delivery for households need not generate a proportionate return for ordinary shareholders in a financially strained developer. Focus on collections, obligations and residual value rather than a generic rescue premium.
