🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  Progress needs confidence and earnings breadth

Reduce if easier labour conditions increasingly mean weaker revenues rather than a healthier supply balance. Keep liquidity for that distinction: a market can rally on eventual rate relief while the businesses most dependent on it still face a difficult earnings interval.
