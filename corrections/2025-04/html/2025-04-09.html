Playbook ✓: Keep equity beta below a full allocation and retain short maturity Treasury holdings as spending power. For tactical upside, use a small unlevered position with an exit tied to a failed recovery; avoid funding it by selling protection on the assumption that high volatility must immediately collapse. A durable improvement would combine less tariff escalation, steadier credit and participation beyond the largest technology names. A rally that lifts equities while long bonds continue weakening would leave the portfolio exposed to the same financing problem in a different disguise.
