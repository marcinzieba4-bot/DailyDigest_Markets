🌍 Geographic Macro Overview

🇺🇸 United States  Bearish  contraction / tariff uncertainty

Macro Stance: US risk should remain below a normal allocation because the tariff debate now meets a weaker manufacturing signal. The March ISM manufacturing PMI was 49 index points, according to the release distributed by PR Newswire. [1] A PMI is an index reading, and this one places manufacturing on the contracting side of the line. Reuters reported tariff worries depressing manufacturing and eroding labour demand. [2] The implication is a more difficult earnings environment before the full policy framework is clear. Businesses facing uncertain costs may delay orders and hiring, while their customers wait for a better sense of prices. A modest market rebound can relieve immediate stress without answering those operating questions. Favour resilience over a bet that every decline will be met by a swift return of demand.

Rates & Curve: The 2Y Treasury yield was 3.87%, the 10Y 4.17%, and the 10Y−2Y spread +30 bps. Over 20 observations, the 2Y fell 9 bps and the 10Y fell 5 bps, with the spread widening 4 bps. The curve was positively sloped; the move was bull steepening (both down, 2Y down more). That offers some discount-rate relief, but the equity tape still needs confirmation. The S&P 500 closed at 5,633.07 index points, up 0.38% in the prior session and down 2.51% over 20 sessions. VIX was 21.77 index points and DXY 104.26 index points. Lower volatility and firmer stocks are welcome, but neither by itself establishes that company expectations have stopped falling. (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2025-04-01; US Treasury, prior close 2025-04-01)

New / Most Important: The immediate policy issue is incidence: who ultimately absorbs the trade cost. CNBC quoted consumer, small-business and food executives warning that tariff costs would reach wallets because there was nowhere to absorb them. [3] BBC described major unknowns ahead of the planned tariff announcement. [4] That combination makes it hazardous to buy lower yields as a clean growth-stock signal. Prices can rise while real demand weakens, forcing the Fed to balance conflicting pressures. Portfolio construction should allow for this awkward middle state instead of choosing between an uncomplicated inflation boom and an uncomplicated recession.

Playbook ✓: Keep a core of defensive cash generators, maintain liquidity and use limited index protection against policy disappointment. Add broad risk only after the tariff detail becomes understandable and businesses can describe a credible cost response. A sustained improvement in manufacturing demand and company outlooks would invalidate the defensive bias. A relief rally driven solely by softer wording would justify covering some tactical hedges, while preserving protection against an earnings reset. Avoid concentrating the entire portfolio in either long duration or commodity inflation hedges, because the policy mix can move both growth expectations and inflation compensation unpredictably.

🇨🇳 China  Mixed  capital support / lending caution

Macro Stance: China's policy support is substantial, yet the latest banking signal argues against equating cheap funding with healthy credit. Reuters reported Chinese banks raising consumer-loan rates in an abrupt reversal as bad-debt risks increased. [10] This is the key tension: policymakers want demand, while lenders still need to price risk and preserve viable returns. The large-bank capital plan reported by Reuters gives institutions greater capacity to lend and absorb losses. [11] Capacity is valuable, but an investor should ask whether the next loan earns an acceptable return and finances a productive purchase. A healthy recovery cannot be built only on lower borrowing prices if borrowers remain uncertain about employment, housing values or business demand.

PMIs & Activity: The March factory-activity acceleration reported by South China Morning Post remains the constructive activity signal. [12] For consumption, Xinhua's January–February retail-sales increase of 4% provides a broader baseline. [13] Together they suggest that domestic support is reaching parts of the real economy, while the durability of demand remains open to testing. Factory output can respond to advanced export orders as well as domestic purchases, so company revenue mix matters. Focus on businesses whose customer demand can be verified over successive reports rather than assuming that a stronger factory reading lifts every household-facing franchise. The next confirmation should be breadth: more resilient sales, inventories that do not build involuntarily, and profits that improve with revenue rather than being consumed by competition.

Credit Impulse: Reuters reported the central bank injecting 800 billion yuan through outright repos in March. [14] That supplies funding, while recapitalisation improves the ability to carry risk. The consumer-loan repricing shows why those measures cannot be analysed in isolation. Banks can be liquid and well capitalised while still facing weak demand or poor loan economics. Reuters Breakingviews' commentary warned of new lending woes for Chinese banks. [15] Treat that as a cautionary interpretation, not a new official credit statistic. The relevant investment test is whether loan growth supports genuine spending and investment without creating another layer of stressed exposures. A banking rally deserves greater conviction only when asset quality and profitability move in the same direction as policy capacity.

Property: Property still limits the argument for a broad demand recovery. South China Morning Post reported home prices declining for a prolonged run in the latest March account, while Reuters' earlier report described the slump persisting despite stimulus. [16] [17] These are household balance-sheet conditions as much as developer conditions. A family worried about the value of its home may save even if credit becomes easier to obtain. South China Morning Post reported developers' profits continuing to slump, and Country Garden's annual loss narrowing amid tentative recovery signs. [18] [19] A narrower loss is progress, but investors should insist on cash collection and ordinary housing demand before extrapolating a sector turnaround. Refinancing relief can buy time without restoring economic value.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Regional cooperation is another offset to trade pressure, but the attribution must remain precise. Reuters reported Chinese state media saying China, Japan and South Korea would jointly respond to US tariffs; WSJ reported China's stated aim of coordinating a response. [20] [21] These accounts describe China's position and reported intentions, not a fully implemented regional policy. The commercial response may involve negotiation, diversification or adjustment of supply chains. Until the detail is settled, the portfolio should prefer businesses with flexible routes to customers and a defensible domestic franchise. Avoid assuming that a diplomatic statement has already removed the cost of restricted market access.

New / Most Important: The most investable fresh company catalyst is Alibaba preparing a flagship AI-model release as soon as April, according to Reuters citing Bloomberg News. [23] This is a planned product event, not a completed release or proof of incremental revenue. CNBC featured an investor arguing Alibaba was a way to avoid tariff turmoil. [24] That is a view about relative exposure, not immunity from the macro environment. The opportunity is to own a domestic technology franchise whose operating demand can be assessed independently of export manufacturing. The risk is paying for product excitement before monetisation or competitive economics are clear. Keep the thesis tied to customer adoption and cash generation.

Playbook ✓: Maintain selective China exposure through broad mainland and internet vehicles, with position sizes reflecting the difference between policy support and proven profit growth. Add only when activity improvement broadens or company execution validates the product and consumption stories. Do not chase bank shares merely because capital is being supplied; require a plausible return on that capital. Reduce exposure if lending caution spreads into weaker demand or if trade restrictions overwhelm domestic support. Keep property-linked businesses at a higher evidence hurdle until ordinary housing demand improves. ⚠ Divergence: stronger factory activity and substantial policy resources coexist with rising consumer-loan pricing and continued property stress, which can prevent financial support from becoming broad household confidence.

Trim if local relative strength weakens while the budget debate threatens shareholder economics. The mixed call would become bullish if disinflation, activity and corporate cash generation improve together. ⚠ Divergence: expectations of easier financing and strong local share performance coexist with weak industrial output, a strained budget and unresolved bank-restructuring plans.
