🇺🇸 United States  Bearish  patience / tariff risk

Macro Stance: The US allocation should stay defensive because the policy shock threatens demand and prices at the same time. Reuters reported that the S&P and Nasdaq suffered large quarterly percentage declines amid tariff turmoil. [1] This is a poor setting for buying every cyclical drawdown on the assumption that easier money will arrive quickly. Reuters quoted Richmond Fed President Barkin saying extensive uncertainty could hit demand while policy was in a good place to await clarity. [2] That leaves companies carrying the first adjustment through orders, margins and investment decisions. A lower equity price can compensate for some uncertainty; it cannot make an unknown trade regime predictable. The useful distinction is between businesses able to preserve cash while customers hesitate and businesses whose earnings require uninterrupted volume growth.

Rates & Curve: The 2Y Treasury yield was 3.89%, the 10Y 4.23%, and the 10Y−2Y spread +34 bps. Over 20 observations, the 2Y fell 7 bps while the 10Y rose 7 bps and the spread widened 14 bps. The curve was positively sloped; its move was steepening (10Y up, 2Y down/flat). This combination offers less reassurance than a broad fall in financing costs: front-end easing expectations coexist with persistent long-end compensation. The S&P 500 closed at 5,611.85 index points, up 0.55% in the prior session but down 4.07% over 20 sessions. VIX stood at 22.28 index points, while DXY was 104.21 index points. The rebound therefore sits inside a damaged trend with material volatility still attached. (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2025-03-31; US Treasury, prior close 2025-03-31)

New / Most Important: The inflation channel remains the obstacle to a simple recession hedge. Reuters reported Musalem's warning that tariffs could generate more persistent inflation. [3] Meanwhile CNBC's survey put first-quarter GDP growth at just 0.3% as tariffs stoked stagflation conditions; that is a survey expectation, not a completed GDP release. [4] A weak growth forecast and concern about persistent inflation can both be right. Import costs may rise before consumers reduce purchases, leaving earnings estimates exposed before inflation visibly improves. Avoid treating a weak confidence reading as an automatic signal that the Fed must rescue high-duration equities.

Playbook ✓: Own cash-generative defensive businesses and keep room to add risk only when the tariff framework becomes clearer and earnings expectations stop deteriorating. Hedge discretionary and semiconductor exposure with defined-loss index protection rather than adding leverage after a down week. The call would change if trade restrictions prove less damaging than feared and corporate outlooks stabilise together. Conversely, falling bond yields accompanied by continued equity weakness would argue for protecting capital, because lower yields alone would then reflect weaker demand. A durable improvement needs better operating evidence as well as a calmer discount rate. Keep individual positions small enough to survive an adverse policy announcement without forcing a sale.

🇨🇳 China  Mixed  bank support / property drag

Macro Stance: China has a more credible financing backstop, but the investment case still depends on converting policy capacity into private demand. Reuters reported a plan for the largest state banks to raise $71.6 billion to bolster capital. [11] This matters because a bank can have abundant funding and still be constrained by the capital required to expand lending or absorb losses. Capital support addresses that constraint; it does not guarantee that households want another mortgage or that companies see profitable projects. Treat the intervention as a reduction in financial tail risk, with the next stage of the trade dependent on loan demand and operating returns. A market rally driven by state support is easier to sustain when borrowers begin making commitments of their own.

PMIs & Activity: South China Morning Post reported that factory activity accelerated in March despite higher US tariffs. [12] The official activity improvement is useful evidence that production has not simply collapsed under trade uncertainty. It is less decisive about the durability of final demand: production can strengthen because customers advance orders, because inventories are rebuilt, or because domestic purchases improve. These mechanisms create different earnings outcomes. The earlier consumption baseline is the January–February retail-sales increase of 4% reported by Xinhua. [13] The constructive reading is that policy is helping both manufacturing and household spending; the harder test is whether the improvement survives a less favourable trade environment. Prefer businesses whose own sales can verify that connection rather than assuming a stronger factory survey reaches every consumer franchise.

Credit Impulse: Reuters reported that the central bank injected 800 billion yuan through outright repos in March. [14] This is a funding operation, while the bank recapitalisation supports balance-sheet capacity. The combination reduces the danger that banks retrench solely to preserve liquidity or capital. Yet South China Morning Post reported Chinese banks raising consumer-loan rates from record lows to protect margins. [15] That is an important qualification: banks need lending that earns an adequate return, and the cheapest possible credit is not automatically the healthiest credit. Watch whether new lending reaches productive private activity and household purchases instead of merely refinancing old obligations. Better funding conditions should ultimately produce a broader improvement in cash generation if the recovery thesis is working.

Property: Property remains the main reason to keep the stance mixed. South China Morning Post reported Country Garden's annual loss narrowing amid tentative recovery signs, while separate coverage said developer profits continued to slump. [16] [17] A smaller loss can be progress without establishing a repaired industry. The same outlet described a thriving Shanghai luxury market alongside deterioration in mass housing. [18] The distinction matters for banks, local demand and household confidence: a strong premium segment cannot alone repair the balance sheet of the ordinary homeowner. For property-linked exposure, require evidence of healthier mass-market demand and sustainable cash collection. A developer's asset transaction may help near-term funding, but a genuine operating recovery needs sales that do not depend on continual financial engineering.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Trade policy is the principal external risk to this improvement. Reuters reported China, South Korea and Japan agreeing to promote regional trade while US tariffs loomed. [19] That is a cooperative response, not an immediate substitute for threatened overseas demand. Reuters also reported Xi urging global chief executives to protect trade. [20] The investable implication is to favour businesses able to sell into several markets and to avoid assuming that diplomatic dialogue already translates into relief. Domestic demand support and external trade pressure may coexist for a long period. In portfolio construction, that argues for separating local consumption and internet exposure from goods exporters with more direct customs and supply-chain sensitivity.

New / Most Important: The most important development is the contrast between stronger bank capacity and still-fragile borrower economics. Reuters reported bank shares rising after the recapitalisation plan. [22] That reaction makes sense as a reduction in balance-sheet risk. It does not settle whether the added capital generates attractive returns. Reuters' account of stagnating large-bank profits and shrinking margins is the counterweight. [23] Investors should therefore judge the next leg on earnings quality rather than the size of another policy headline. Huawei's faster revenue growth alongside falling profit, reported by Reuters, illustrates the same wider issue: sales growth and shareholder economics can move differently when investment or competition absorbs the incremental revenue. [24]

Playbook ✓: Hold a selective position in broad China and internet exposure, adding only when activity improvement is matched by healthier margins or cash generation. Use ASHR for mainland breadth and KWEB for an internet expression, while recognising that neither isolates household property repair. Trim if the activity recovery fades or if trade restrictions impair the earnings case faster than domestic policy offsets it. A stronger bank rally without a broader improvement in borrower demand would justify taking some gains rather than chasing financials indiscriminately. Keep currency risk explicit when choosing local and offshore vehicles. ⚠ Divergence: improving factory activity and bank capital support coexist with weak developer profitability and evidence that ordinary housing remains under pressure.
