🌍 Geographic Macro Overview

🇺🇸 United States  Bearish  March lift-off priced; war premium rising

Macro Stance: The US story has shifted from rates alone to rates plus a geopolitical risk premium, and that pairing is hostile to equity multiples. Reuters reported consumer prices posting their largest annual gain in 40 years as inflation became widespread [1] , while activity is not cracking: The Real Economy Blog charted industrial production rebounding [2] and housing permits at a 15-year high [3] . That leaves the Fed little room to soften into a war scare. The S&P 500 fell 2.12% to 4,380.26 index points and the VIX jumped 3.82 points to 28.11 index points (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-02-17), a move in line with Al Jazeera's framing of US stocks taking a hit as the Ukraine crisis stoked fear [4] . We are Bearish on broad US risk: growth is fine, but the cost of holding it is rising.

Rates & Curve: The 2Y closed at 1.49% and the 10Y at 1.97%, a 10Y-2Y spread of +48 bps on a curve that remains positively sloped (US Treasury, prior close 2022-02-17). Over 20 observations the 2Y is up 41 bps and the 10Y up 14 bps, a 27 bps compression: bear flattening (both up, 2Y up more). The five-observation window tells the newer story: since 2022-02-10 the 2Y is down 12 bps and the 10Y down 6 bps, a haven bid layered on top of a hiking cycle. Reuters described the curve as pricing Fed tightening while showing fears of policy error [5] , and Bloomberg warned that Treasury market liquidity is eroding with the Fed's course a gamble [6] . Thin liquidity means front-end moves can overshoot both ways.

New / Most Important: The January minutes did not lock in a pace: Reuters read them as showing a Fed not wed to any particular speed of hikes [7] , while Al Jazeera stressed readiness to move faster if inflation persists [8] . The Fed's James Bullard again called for a front-loaded campaign, WSJ reported [9] , and a Reuters poll still had 25 bps in March with calls for 50 bps growing louder [10] . Mortgage rates at 3.92%, the highest in 33 months according to the Orange County Register [11] , show tightening already reaching housing. Add President Biden's warning, carried by PBS, of a very high risk of a Russian invasion in the next several days [12] , and markets must price a hawkish Fed and a war premium at once.

Playbook ✓: Action: keep an underweight in long-duration growth, with the Nasdaq 100 at 14,171.74 index points and -4.54% over 20 sessions, funded into pricing-power staples and energy, and hold a gold hedge with gold at 1,902.00 USD/oz (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-02-17). Trigger to add risk: a VIX close back below its 20-session-earlier 25.59 index points while the 2Y holds near 1.49%, which would show the haven bid fading without a hawkish repricing. What changes the call: if the 2Y retraces its five-observation decline while the VIX stays elevated, both the Fed and the war premium are tightening at once; then cut equity beta further.

🇨🇳 China  Mixed  PBOC easing against trade and property drag

Macro Stance: China is the one major economy where policy is leaning the other way, and that divergence is the core of our Mixed stance. Al Jazeera reported the central bank chief vowing support amid weak links [21] , Bloomberg had the PBOC pumping in more liquidity and spurring gains in Chinese stocks [22] , and Reuters reported that slowing inflation leaves room for policy easing [23] . Against that, external friction is rising and the property-credit tail has not been cut off. Markets are pricing the easing without pricing a reacceleration: the Shanghai Composite closed at 3,468.04 index points, -2.45% over 20 sessions, while the Hang Seng gained 2.37% to 24,792.77 index points over the same horizon (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-02-17).

PMIs & Activity: Activity entered 2022 with a soft handoff. Reuters reported the Caixin survey showing factory activity contracting in January as COVID lockdowns bit [24] . The 2021 base was strong, with Global Times reporting GDP growth of 8.1%, the fastest in a decade [25] , but Macau Business put fixed-asset investment up just 4.9 pct for the year [26] , a sign that the property drag was already weighing on capex. China Daily reported industrial profits rebounding in 2021 as production improved [27] . The mix argues for a recovery led by policy-directed infrastructure and manufacturing rather than consumers, while zero-COVID constraints persist: BBC reported Hong Kong's hospitals overwhelmed amid a spike in cases [28] .

Credit Impulse: Credit is where easing should show first. CNBC reported the central bank cutting key lending rates in January, including one for the first time in nearly 2 years [29] , and Fibre2Fashion reported cuts to medium-term loan and reverse repo rates [30] . Bloomberg framed it as the PBOC seizing the moment for stimulus while the Fed hikes [31] . The constraint is local-government leverage: the South China Morning Post reported Beijing telling banks to support rural infrastructure but not to add to hidden debt [32] . That is a deliberately narrow impulse channelled into approved projects, so the next credit print matters more for its composition, corporate medium-term lending versus short bills, than for its headline size.

Property: Property policy is easing at the margin, not pivoting. The Khmer Times reported that affordable rental housing is being excluded from property credit controls [33] , a targeted carve-out rather than a rescue. Business Recorder reported December new home prices falling less on promotions and easing curbs [34] . Stress is still surfacing elsewhere in the credit chain: Bloomberg, citing Caixin, reported that Cedar was unable to pay $3.1 billion overdue [35] , the kind of non-payment that keeps foreign high-yield buyers away from Chinese issuers generally. The sector is moving from free fall toward managed decline; that supports materials and banks at the margin but not a developer-equity rally.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Fiscal support is being steered into infrastructure, which China Briefing framed as spurring economic growth in 2022 [36] . The trade front is deteriorating. BBC reported Washington accusing China of serious harm to workers through its trade practices [37] , and the Peterson Institute found China bought none of the extra $200 billion of US exports in Trump's trade deal [38] . CNN reported the US accusing Tencent and Alibaba of letting sellers traffic fake goods [39] , and Reuters reported EU anti-dumping tariffs on Chinese steel fasteners [40] . Beijing is also policing commodities: Reuters reported China urging some iron ore traders to reduce inventory [41] , a signal that it will lean against raw-material inflation rather than let a stimulus impulse become a price spike.

Markets & Flows: Offshore internet is where sentiment turned first. Tencent closed at 454.94 HKD, +5.99% over 20 sessions, while Alibaba's Hong Kong line fell 4.28% to 123.00 HKD and Xiaomi lost 10.59% (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-02-17). Business Insider reported Tencent joining the list of the world's ten most valuable companies as Meta slid [42] , and CNBC reported banks from Credit Suisse to Goldman Sachs saying it is time to buy Chinese stocks [43] . Positioning is selective: Caixin Global reported Hillhouse gobbling up shares in NEV makers while ditching Alibaba and Bilibili [44] . Reuters reported the yuan slipping as divergent China-US monetary policy exerted pressure [45] , the natural cost of easing into a Fed hiking cycle; a softer currency helps exporters only while commodity prices cooperate.

New / Most Important: The key development is that policy intent and external risk are now both explicit. The central bank chief's pledge of support [21] arrives as Washington sharpens its trade rhetoric, with PBS reporting US officials saying China's aggressive trade policies cause serious harm [46] . In parallel, Hong Kong's Omicron wave is testing zero-COVID: Al Jazeera reported President Xi telling Hong Kong to control the outbreak [47] . Easing, trade friction and COVID containment pull in different directions, and the strain shows in the US-listed FXI, down 3.18% over 20 sessions even as the Hang Seng gained over its own 20-session window (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-02-17).

Playbook ✓: Action: own Hong Kong-listed internet leaders selectively, Tencent over Alibaba, as the cleanest expression of easing, and avoid property-linked credit and US-listed EV names such as NIO, -15.14% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-02-17). Trigger to add: the Hang Seng holding above its 20-session-earlier 24,218.03 index points while the next credit print shows a rising share of corporate medium-term loans. What changes the call: a new US enforcement step against platforms, or a Hong Kong lockdown that spills into mainland supply chains, would turn the internet trade into a funding source. ⚠ Divergence: monetary policy is easing and offshore tech is rallying, yet the Shanghai Composite is down over 20 sessions and the yuan is softening; markets believe the PBOC's intent more than its transmission.

Growth is holding up, with TVN24 reporting GDP rising in the latest data [65] , but Rzeczpospolita warned that a slowdown is being masked by statistics as inflation and rates already curb appetites [66] . We are Bearish: the hiking cycle is chasing inflation rather than leading it, and the geopolitical premium is rising.

Markets: Polish assets are trading as the front-line proxy. Fiscal & Politics: Fiscal policy is caught between inflation-boosted revenue and the backlash against Polski Ład. EU money stays conditional, a direct risk to the medium-term investment story.

Corporate & Sectors: Corporate news is dominated by energy security and cost pass-through. WysokieNapiecie.pl reported Rafako progressing only slowly on its coke project at JSW [80] ; the coking-coal producer fell 5.23% to 44.55 PLN in one session yet remains +10.55% over 20 sessions. CD Projekt released its next-generation Cyberpunk 2077 update and free trial, per its investor relations site [85] , but Eurogamer.pl reported gamers disappointed by 30 FPS on Xbox Series S [86] ; the stock is -5.26% over 20 sessions at 180.00 PLN.

Reuters reported the US sending 3,000 additional troops to Poland [87] , The National Interest reported additional US F-15s sent to bolster NATO's eastern flank [88] , and Notes From Poland reported a trilateral Ukraine-UK-Poland agreement in the face of Russian aggression [89] . New / Most Important: The decisive input is the interaction between rate hikes and the risk premium. Hikes should support bank net interest income, and mBank is +3.43% over 20 sessions at 470.00 PLN, yet the same lenders are first to be sold on geopolitical headlines, with PKO Bank Polski -3.43% and Pekao -4.37% over five sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-02-17).
