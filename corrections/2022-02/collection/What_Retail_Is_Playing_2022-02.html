GameStop / GME — one year on, the faithful buy again

Why Retail Is Here

The story the GameStop crowd tells itself has not changed in a year: the stock Reddit turned into a cause is the one Wall Street never understood, and every drawdown is a test of nerve. The New York Times boiled the creed down to buy GameStop, fight injustice, just don't selln0694. Monday handed the believers a double-digit day. GME closed at 108.93 USD, up 11.26% on the session and 8.77% over five, yet still 26.59% below its 148.39 USD year-end close (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-01-31). That discount is the hook: buy-the-dip faith and revenge on the shorts in a single ticker, arriving on the anniversary of the squeeze.

What Is Driving It

The fuel is macro relief more than company news. Al Jazeera wrote that Wall Street ended a volatile month on a high noten1666, and the rebound was loudest where retail congregates: ARKK gained 9.46%, the triple-leveraged TQQQ 9.69%, Robinhood 11.15% and AMC 6.64%, against 1.89% for the S&P 500 at 4,515.55 index points, while the VIX dropped 2.83 points to 24.83 index points, still 7.61 points above its 2021 close (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-01-31). What has not been relieved is the rate path. The 2Y Treasury yield closed at 1.18%, up 45 bps over 20 observations, in a bear-flattening move (US Treasury, prior close 2022-01-31), and Marketplace framed the debate as how many times the Fed will raise rates, not whethern1994. GameStop is bouncing on lower volatility, not on easier money.

The Social Signal

If the same crowd that sold into the lows now chases the rebound, the bounce can be sharp and short-lived.

Historical Analog

Alphabet / GOOGL — the split that lets small accounts in

Why Retail Is Here

The crowd's story is irresistible in its simplicity: one of the best businesses on earth is about to feel affordable. Alphabet announced a 20-for-1 stock split alongside its resultsn0806, and to a small account that reads as permission. The emotional hook is fear of missing out with a safety net: this is not a meme but Google, and the split feels like the velvet rope coming down. With the stock still 5.07% below its 2,899.83 USD close of early January (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-02-01), it also feels like quality on sale.

Socially, a split turns an abstract market value into a share price people can talk about.

Historical Analog

A split changes the unit, not the pie: twenty shares in place of each old one leave the company's value untouched. Its market effect runs through accessibility and attention, meaning whole-share buyers who dislike fractions, option contracts that cover a fixed block of shares and therefore cost less each, and a wave of coverage that reaches buyers who were not looking. That effect tends to be front-loaded. Enthusiasm arrives with the announcement, while the actual share change comes later, so the trade is often at its most crowded before anything mechanical has happened.

Smart Money View

The fundamental case does not need the split: Al Jazeera reported Alphabet beat earnings expectationsn0807, so the Street was behind the numbers. Professionals are still not uniformly leaning in. Bloomberg reported that Morgan Stanley's Sheets sees more pain ahead for US stocksn0390, even as Yahoo Finance carried Apple as Morgan Stanley's top pick for 2022n0647: cautious on the index, still willing to own a mega-cap leader. Reuters reported that Standard Chartered expects the Treasury yield curve to flatten and possibly invert this yearn2045, a signal that the Fed's path, not earnings, may set the multiple, Google's included. The split is a retail event; the institutional question is the discount rate.

When the Narrative Breaks

The first test is whether the after-hours pop survives the regular session; a fade back toward the 2,752.88 USD close (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2022-02-01) would say the split was priced on announcement. The second is the rest of the mega-cap reporting run. GeekWire's preview says analysts expect Amazon to post record revenue of $137 billion with lower profitsn0752, and a stumble from another giant would remind buyers that split enthusiasm does not insulate one name from its group. Leverage is the trap: short-dated calls bought ahead of a split turn a value-neutral corporate action into a decaying bet.
