🌍 Geographic Macro Overview

🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  ↑ rates rising / hawkish tilt

Macro Stance: The Fed under Chair Warsh has pivoted toward what markets are reading as a hawkish hold or outright tightening bias. Rate-hike bets have jumped following Jackson Hole commentary and the escalation of US military strikes on Iran. The 10Y yield stands at 4.76% (+7 bps vs 4W ago) while the 2Y sits at 4.34% (prior close, +6 bps vs 4W), leaving a +39 bps term spread — positively sloped but still flat by historical standards, with the front end repricing at least as fast as the long end. The US is hosting the G20 and pushing a "growth agenda" explicitly designed to calm debt market concerns around $40T in outstanding federal debt. Fiscal dominance is now the central macro variable.

New / Most Important: Three structural developments converged this week. First, US strikes on Iran have become a live kinetic conflict now six months old, pushing WTI to 85.59 (↑6.5% vs 4W ago) and creating a supply-shock overhang on the inflation outlook. Second, Warsh's Jackson Hole speech spooked Wall Street — his "quieter Fed" framework now includes a "smidgen of guidance" that markets interpret as leaving the door wide open for a rate hike, not a cut. This is a regime shift from the Powell-era forward guidance paradigm: Warsh is deliberately reducing the information content of Fed communication, which forces risk premia higher across the curve. Third, Bessent publicly taunted Druckenmiller's bond bet and signaled he expects BOJ to hike — the Treasury Secretary is now actively jawboning foreign central banks into tightening, a maneuver designed to attract capital into Treasuries via relative-rate convergence rather than absolute US rate cuts. This is a novel form of "beggar-thy-neighbor via rate-hike coordination" that I haven't seen in my career.

Playbook ✓: Rising oil → higher breakevens → hawkish Fed repricing → flatter curve → Nasdaq outperforms small caps (Russell/Nasdaq ratio ↓3.1%). Mega-cap quality dominance in a rising-rate, geopolitical-risk environment is textbook.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The S&P 500 at 7673 (↑1.0%) grinding higher alongside rising yields and an active shooting war is deeply unusual. VIX at 15.16 in a COMPLACENCY regime while the US is conducting strikes on Iran and rate-hike bets are jumping is a historic dislocation. The market is pricing the war as a contained oil-supply event rather than an escalation risk. Gold at 4483 (↑11.1%) is screaming a different message than equities. Gold on pace for its best month since February confirms this: real-money hedging via gold is intense while equities shrug. If this divergence resolves, it will resolve violently.

🇯🇵 Japan  Bearish (for carry trade)  ↑ rates rising sharply / hike imminent

Macro Stance: The BOJ is now under coordinated pressure from both domestic and US officials to hike rates. Japan's 2Y yield hit a 31-year high. The 10Y JGB rose to 2.95% post-Jackson Hole. The yen broke 160 and intervention risk is rising. This is the most aggressive BOJ tightening setup since the Volcker era for Japan.

New / Most Important: This is structurally the most important story in global macro right now. Bessent explicitly stated he expects Japan to "do the right thing" and boost the yen, while Oxford Economics says the BOJ will "hike higher and faster." A former BOJ official said groundwork is set for a hike next month. The US Treasury Secretary publicly pressuring the BOJ to tighten is unprecedented in the modern era — it represents a deliberate strategy to close the rate differential and strengthen the yen, which would: (a) reduce Japanese capital outflows that have been suppressing Treasury yields, (b) force unwinding of the massive yen carry trade that funds risk assets globally, and (c) create a self-reinforcing repatriation flow back into JGBs. The carry-trade unwind implications are enormous: Japan's 2Y at a 31-year high directly impacts levered positions across FX, credit, and EM that are funded in yen. KuCoin's note connecting this to Bitcoin is correct — the yen carry trade is the hidden leverage behind multiple asset classes. If the BOJ hikes next month as the feed suggests, we should expect an echo of the August 2024 vol shock but with even more structural force because yields are starting from a higher base.

Playbook ✓: Yen weakness → BOJ tightening → JGB yields surge → carry unwind pressure. Textbook.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Despite yen breaking 160 and yields at multi-decade highs, Bessent called yen moves "pretty contained" and "not disorderly." This calm rhetoric while simultaneously pushing the BOJ to hike is a contradiction — Bessent is trying to engineer an orderly repricing, but currency markets at intervention thresholds rarely comply with orderliness.
