Watch the dollar as the fulcrum — its strength is simultaneously attracting foreign equity flows and tightening financial conditions. New / Most Important: Eurozone growth has defied the energy shock, described as "too good to be true" by Euractiv. Playbook ✓: Resilient growth + sticky energy-driven inflation = ECB boxed in, unable to cut. The lending collapse is textbook tight-policy transmission with a lag.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The halving of bank lending in a single month while GDP surprises to the upside is a classic late-cycle divergence — the real economy is running on momentum while the credit pipeline is collapsing. The digital euro wholesale debut is structurally new: this is the first live deployment of a major CBDC in institutional financial plumbing. If it gains traction in repo and settlement, it could fundamentally alter how ECB monetary policy transmits through the banking system, potentially disintermediating commercial banks further and accelerating the very lending drought we're seeing. Watch whether this creates a two-speed monetary system — wholesale digital euro liquidity vs. traditional bank credit channels.

🇯🇵 Japan  Bearish  ↑ rate direction (tightening)

Macro Stance: The BOJ is in its most aggressive tightening cycle in decades — rates are at 31-year highs — yet the yen continues to weaken, nearing 155 against the dollar. This is a historically unusual and dangerous configuration.

New / Most Important: This is structurally the most important story in global macro this week. The yen is weakening despite the BOJ hiking, because the US-Japan rate differential remains wide enough that the yen carry trade has re-emerged at massive scale — $2.3 trillion according to one report, described as an "unwinding powder keg." An ex-BOJ policymaker said the BOJ could raise rates every quarter. JGB yields jumped on inflation worries. Eastspring identified a "reverse carry" trade opportunity on Japan's steep yield curve. The BOJ disappointed hawks at its last meeting, and the yen extended its losing streak on a Japanese holiday.

Playbook ✓: BOJ hiking → JGB yields rising → domestic financial conditions tightening. This is proceeding as expected.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The $2.3 trillion yen carry trade re-emergence while the BOJ is actively hiking is a structural powder keg unlike anything in recent memory. In a normal world, central bank tightening strengthens the currency and naturally unwinds carry trades. Instead, US yields surging to 5.18% have kept the rate differential wide enough that yen shorts remain profitable despite BOJ hikes — a treadmill scenario where the BOJ can't hike fast enough to close the gap. The risk is binary: if the BOJ accelerates to quarterly hikes as the ex-policymaker suggests, the carry trade unwind could be disorderly and violent, triggering a global cross-asset shock similar to but larger than August 2024. If they don't accelerate, yen weakness feeds imported inflation, forcing them to eventually hike more aggressively anyway. The "reverse carry" trade Eastspring identified — playing the steep JGB curve — is the smart money positioning for this inevitable resolution. This is the single biggest tail risk in global markets right now, and VIX at 14.87 tells you nobody is hedging it.
