🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  ↑ rates — hiking cycle, pause debated

New / Most Important: The September payrolls miss (29K) was the dominant market event this week. Paradoxically, stocks rose on the print — the S&P 500 rallied toward records — because markets read weakness as constraining Fed hawks. But bonds did NOT rally; yields continued surging. The NYT flagged a "powerful yet fragile force propping up stocks," and the WSJ highlighted an "amazing stat that's a red flag." The AI wealth effect (Axios) and continued AI capex (Deloitte) remain the narrow pillars supporting mega-cap leadership, with the Russell/Nasdaq ratio collapsing 8.7% vs 4W ago — extreme narrowness.

The more structural concern is that term premium is repricing for fiscal reasons (deficit trajectory, Treasury supply) rather than growth expectations, which means the usual "bad economic data → lower yields → equity support" transmission mechanism is broken. If the labor market continues softening while yields keep climbing, the equity market will eventually have to arbitrate between the growth story and the cost-of-capital reality. Russell 2000 down 4.6% suggests that arbitration has already begun outside mega-cap.
