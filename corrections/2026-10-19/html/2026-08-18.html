Chip stocks sold off sharply today — Micron down 7%, SanDisk down 9% — driven by a WSJ report on memory oversupply. Neocloud stocks (CoreWeave -7%, Nebius -5%) plummeted on rising AI infrastructure overspend fears. ECB economists warned a US tech stock correction is likely. Playbook ✓: The steepening curve with front-end easing expectations and long-end fiscal premium is textbook late-cycle. The 10Y yield climbing while the 2Y drifts lower tells you the market sees no near-term growth threat but is deeply uncomfortable with the Treasury's funding trajectory. When Wells Fargo revamps its gold price target upward in a rising-yield environment, you're watching a regime shift in how capital prices sovereign risk. The traditional framework where rising real rates kill gold is broken — the marginal gold buyer today is a central bank or sovereign wealth fund diversifying away from duration exposure, not a retail inflation hedger.

New / Most Important: This is the most structurally significant story globally right now. The ex-top FX diplomat warned of more yen intervention alongside faster hikes, confirming a coordinated tightening across policy levers. Japanese bond yields climbing on fiscal concerns echo the US dynamic — this is a global sovereign bond repricing, not a Japan-specific event. The yen staying weak despite tripling hike odds tells you either the market doesn't believe them, or capital outflows from Japanese institutions are overwhelming the rate differential story. Watch JGB 10Y yields closely — any BoJ attempt to let them rise further while hiking short rates risks a nonlinear move in global fixed income given Japan's role as the world's largest creditor nation.

Playbook ✓: Yen weakness despite hawkish noise is consensus — nobody trusts BoJ follow-through until it arrives.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Government support for faster hikes is the divergence itself — see above.
