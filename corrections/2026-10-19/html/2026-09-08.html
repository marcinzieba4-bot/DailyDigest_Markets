New / Most Important: The Dow fell ~600 points today as oil surged and the US-Canada trade war escalated. This is a structurally different regime from anything we've seen since 2023: the Fed may be hiking INTO a narrowing equity market (Russell/Nasdaq ratio ↓1.4%) with VIX at a complacent 15.26. That divergence between equity vol and bond stress is the single most dangerous signal in the snapshot. Capital flow implication: money is rotating defensively into mega-cap tech (Nasdaq 100 only ↓0.1% vs Russell ↓1.5%), gold ($4,439, ↑1.8%), and Bitcoin ($78,738, ↑13.7%) — classic "hide in hard assets and liquid large-caps" behavior.

Playbook ✓: Rising oil → rising yields → equity pressure → narrow leadership. Classic late-cycle stagflationary playbook.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Bitcoin outperforming gold by nearly 12 percentage points over four weeks is NOT in the standard playbook for a rate-hike scare. This suggests a parallel capital flow — likely driven by non-US buyers seeking dollar-denominated hard assets outside the Treasury complex. The Gold/BTC ratio at 0.0564 with BTC outrunning gold by 11.9pp indicates crypto is absorbing "fiscal debasement" flows that previously would have gone into long-duration Treasuries. If the Fed does hike while BTC rallies, it will confirm that crypto has structurally decoupled from traditional rate-sensitivity frameworks and is now functioning as a sovereign-risk hedge rather than a liquidity proxy. Watch for EM central bank and sovereign wealth fund BTC accumulation data — this is the next frontier of reserve diversification.

🇯🇵 Japan  Bullish  ↑ hiking

Macro Stance: The BOJ rate hike in September is now near-consensus, with Takaichi's reflationist aide explicitly projecting it. The yen has surged to a seven-month high, breaking below 152 against the dollar. This is the most important FX move in global macro right now.

New / Most Important: USD/JPY broke below 154 and reportedly hit the 152 level — a massive yen rally. Bessent's yen bet (per WSJ) is paying off, confirming that the Treasury Secretary has been actively positioned for BOJ normalization. This is structurally significant: a BOJ hike combined with a yen rally will accelerate the unwind of the world's largest carry trade. Japanese institutional investors (lifers, pension funds, Mrs. Watanabe retail) hold trillions in foreign bonds and equities. A stronger yen reduces the hedging cost advantage of staying abroad, pulling capital home. This repatriation flow is the single largest potential source of disruption to US Treasuries and European sovereign bonds. If the BOJ hikes and the yen strengthens further toward 145, expect UST selling pressure from Japanese lifers to compound the Fed-hike-driven yield surge. The Bessent connection is critical — it suggests the US Treasury is actively welcoming yen strength as a tool to weaken the dollar without Fed easing, a form of shadow currency intervention via allied central bank coordination.

Playbook ✓: BOJ normalization → yen strength → carry unwind. Textbook.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The speed of the yen move — seven-month highs — suggests this is not orderly. If Japanese investors begin repatriating in size during a period when the Fed is also hiking, UST could see a buyer's strike from both the marginal foreign buyer (Japan) AND the domestic central bank. This double whammy has no modern precedent.

If property prices crack, Australia's banks (heavily mortgage-exposed) become a systemic risk. Watch CBA, WBC, NAB for early stress signals.
