🇯🇵 Japan  Bearish (currency/bond stress)  ↑ BOJ hiking cycle accelerating

Macro Stance: Japan is the single most structurally important story in global macro right now. The BOJ is on track to hike further, with producer prices hitting the fastest pace since 2023, and one source (Japan Wire/Kyodo) indicating the BOJ eyes a policy rate of 2% by end-2027. FX Empire flagged "BOJ rate hike risk builds as USDJPY eyes 175." The yen is in what Business Insider called a "slow motion currency crisis."

New / Most Important: The most consequential development was Japan signaling a "massive pension shift to domestic assets" (Yahoo Finance, Jul 10), which sparked a rally in yen and JGBs. This is structurally enormous. Japan's Government Pension Investment Fund (GPIF) is the world's largest pension fund, and any reallocation from foreign assets back to domestic bonds and equities represents a potential reversal of decades of capital export. This is the BOJ-GPIF coordination trade: the central bank tightens policy, the pension fund repatriates — both forces strengthen the yen and absorb domestic duration. If GPIF materially reduces its foreign bond allocation, the marginal seller of US Treasuries becomes the world's largest asset owner. This has direct implications for US 10Y yields (currently rising at 4.57%) and could amplify the term premium the Fed is already struggling with. The Atlantic Council piece on the weak yen "spelling trouble at home and abroad" and MUFG's "fiscal unease" framing suggest the establishment is now treating yen weakness as a systemic risk, not a trade competitiveness feature. BTC and crypto underperforming in yen terms (per CoinDesk) is an early signal that Japanese retail, historically a major crypto flow, is pulling back.

Playbook ✓: BOJ hiking into rising producer prices is textbook — they're behind the curve and normalizing belatedly.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The GPIF domestic reallocation signal is the divergence. Consensus assumed Japan's savings glut would continue flowing abroad indefinitely. A reversal creates a reflexive loop: repatriation strengthens yen → reduces need for BOJ intervention → allows faster normalization → attracts more repatriation. This is the most important cross-border capital flow shift in years and is deeply underpriced by global fixed income markets.
