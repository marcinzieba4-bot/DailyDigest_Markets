Playbook ✓: Soft CPI → equities bid, gold bid, Fed holds — textbook.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The simultaneous rise in long-end yields AND gold (↑11.0%) is structurally unusual and worth serious attention. Bitcoin down 1.6% while gold surges 11% is a capital-flow fingerprint: central banks and sovereign wealth funds are buying gold, not crypto. This divergence rarely persists; either BTC catches up or gold is ahead of itself.

The US sale of euros to prop up the yen reportedly blindsided the ECB — this is a remarkable breakdown in G7 coordination. The euro steadied vs yen as ECB hike bets clashed with BoJ tightening hopes, creating a rare two-way hawkish squeeze in EUR/JPY that historically unwinds violently.

Playbook ✓: Sticky Eurozone services inflation forcing more tightening is the consensus read.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The ECB hiking INTO what looks like a weakening growth backdrop (Poland, a key EU economy, posted just 0.9% Q2 GDP growth) is a classic policy error setup. If the ECB hikes in September while the Fed holds, this creates a capital flow divergence that should strengthen EUR and tighten European financial conditions further — a pro-cyclical tightening into a soft patch. The US selling euros to support the yen without ECB coordination suggests the transatlantic monetary relationship is fraying. European equities' "unrecognised resilience" (per Investors' Chronicle) may be tested if the ECB follows through.

🇯🇵 Japan  Mixed  ↑ rates rising

Macro Stance: BoJ September rate hike is now described as "locked in" with government explicitly supporting faster hikes. SME bankruptcies hit a 12-year high, underscoring the domestic stress from yen weakness and rising input costs. US intervened by selling euros to prop up the yen — a historically extraordinary move.

New / Most Important: This is the most structurally important story globally this week. Japan's government publicly supporting faster BoJ hikes is a regime shift — the traditional dynamic of the government pressuring the BoJ to stay easy has inverted. The US selling euros (not dollars) to support the yen means Washington wants a stronger yen but doesn't want to weaken the dollar — this is a three-body problem that forces EUR lower and blindsides the ECB. Growing BoJ rate hike expectations pushed up JGB yields, and the yen's post-intervention gains now depend entirely on whether the BoJ delivers. If the BoJ hikes faster than expected, the carry trade unwind could be violent — we saw a preview of this in 2024. The equity market is paradoxically outperforming despite yen weakness, driven by export earnings translation, but a rapid yen appreciation from BoJ hawkishness would reverse that instantly. Bond yields rising in Japan tighten the one remaining source of cheap global liquidity — this is the most consequential macro flow story of H2 2026.

Playbook ✓: Yen intervention + BoJ hawkishness is the expected response to persistent weakness.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The US coordinating yen support by selling EUR rather than USD is unprecedented in recent memory. It reveals Washington's desire to avoid dollar weakness while still engineering yen strength — effectively exporting tightening to Europe. This triangular FX intervention breaks the standard bilateral playbook.
