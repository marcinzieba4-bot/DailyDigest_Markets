The ECB's own AI-crash warning creates a bizarre reflexivity: if they're worried about US tech contagion, why are they tightening into it? This is a central bank that is fighting the last war (inflation) while flagging the next one (financial stability).

If BOJ delivers hikes at consecutive meetings, the unwind could propagate through global risk assets the way August 2024 [verify if this reference is appropriate] briefly demonstrated. The key transmission channel: Japanese institutions repatriating capital from US Treasuries and European bonds back to now-attractive JGBs, which would add to the global term premium repricing already underway.

Playbook ✓: Yen strengthening as rate differentials compress; JGB yields surging on BOJ normalization expectations.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: Despite JGB yields at 27-year highs, the yen is described as "struggling" against some crosses — suggesting the carry trade unwind is not yet capitulatory but rather a slow grind.
