New / Most Important: The RBA hike is the headline event. The statement explicitly left the door open for further tightening, which is hawkish guidance at an already restrictive level. The big four banks raising rates the very next day ensures rapid transmission to household cashflows. This is a textbook policy squeeze on a leveraged household sector — Australia has one of the highest household-debt-to-income ratios in the developed world.

Playbook ✓: RBA hiking into sticky inflation with immediate bank passthrough is by the book.  |  ⚠️ Divergence: The divergence is the external backdrop. The RBA is tightening into a China that isn't reflating (see above). Australia's terms of trade are dependent on Chinese commodity demand that isn't arriving. This creates a vicious loop: higher rates crush domestic demand, the external sector doesn't compensate because China is stagnant, and the AUD weakens despite rate hikes because the capital account deteriorates. Historically, RBA hiking cycles supported AUD; this time, AUD risk is skewed bearish because the interest rate differential with the US is working against it while the commodity demand underpinning is absent.
