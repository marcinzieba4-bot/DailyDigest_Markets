The rate-sensitive end of the growth pulse is already softening — US existing home sales unexpectedly fell in June as the 30-year mortgage rate rose to 6.49% — so markets are right to price defensive hedges over cyclical optimism. Watch for a rapid repricing of rate-cut expectations into rate-hike expectations if CPI tomorrow prints hot on energy pass-through.

This is the single biggest capital-flow story in global macro right now.
