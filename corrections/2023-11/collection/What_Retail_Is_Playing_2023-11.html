Bitcoin / BTC-USD — Front-running the Wall Street ETF door

Why Retail Is Here

The story retail is telling itself fits on a sticky note: a spot bitcoin ETF is coming, and whoever owns the coin before that door opens gets paid by everyone who walks through it afterwards. For once the small account expects to be early and Wall Street late. Bitcoin closed at 34,667.78 USD, up 29.00% over 20 sessions from 26,873.32 USD (Yahoo Finance, prior completed daily close 2023-10-31). The emotional hook is vindication: holders who sat through the crypto winter now watch institutions queue for the same asset.

Historical Analog

The mechanism is the gamma squeeze.
