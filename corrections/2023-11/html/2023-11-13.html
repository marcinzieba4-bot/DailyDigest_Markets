🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  Fed on hold, hawkish lean

Macro Stance: The US enters the week on a split screen: equities are pricing a finished hiking cycle while bonds are still being asked to absorb supply and a hawkish chair. The S&P 500 closed at 4,415.24, up 1.56% on Friday and 2.02% over 20 sessions, the Nasdaq 100 jumped 2.25% to 15,529.12 and the VIX sank to 14.17 (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-10). That rally followed Powell's warning that the Fed will not hesitate to hike further if necessary [1] and that the inflation fight has a long way to go [2] . Growth is cooling rather than cracking: J.P. Morgan notes October added 150,000 jobs, below forecast [3] , unemployment rose to 3.9% [4] and the ISM manufacturing PMI sat at 46.7 [5] , after a Q3 that CNN called blistering [6] . That mix fits Goldman Sachs' view that the global economy will perform better than many expect in 2024 [7] .

Rates & Curve: The front end did the work. The 2Y closed at 5.04% and the 10Y at 4.61%, leaving the 10Y-2Y spread at -43 bps and the curve inverted (US Treasury, prior close 2023-11-10). Over five observations the 2Y rose 21 bps against 4 bps for the 10Y, yet over 20 observations the 2Y is unchanged and the 10Y is down 2 bps, so the curve is little changed on the month. Barron's says a 30-year auction that broke bad sank the stock market [8] , and Reuters described Powell as leaning hawkish in IMF remarks [9] . With the 3M bill at 5.53%, cash still out-yields the whole curve, which is why money keeps parking at the front. The DXY gained 0.80% over five sessions to 105.86 but is 0.74% lower over 20 (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-10).

New / Most Important: Moody's has moved the outlook on the US credit rating from stable to negative [10] , and Livemint reports Washington is furious [11] . That lands as Speaker Mike Johnson proposes a two-step stopgap to avert a government shutdown [12] and agencies receive initial shutdown communications [13] . Together they put term premium, not the policy rate, at the center of the next move in long yields. The offset is relief on borrowing costs: mortgage rates fell by the largest amount in a year [14] , and a Reuters commentary says October now looks like a bad bond dream [15] . BofA CEO Moynihan says the economy is headed for a soft landing [16] .

Playbook ✓: Stay long mega-cap quality and intermediate Treasuries, funded by underweighting small caps: the Russell 2000 fell 3.15% over five sessions to 1,705.32 while the Nasdaq 100 gained 2.85% (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-10). Add duration through IEF if the 10Y holds below 4.63%, its level 20 observations ago, after the stopgap vote and the next core inflation print. Cut duration and buy VIX calls, cheap at 14.17, if the stopgap fails and long-end selling narrows the -43 bps inversion from the back end. The call turns defensive if a hot core print validates Powell's hawkish lean.

🇨🇳 China  Bearish  Supply-side easing, deflation risk

Macro Stance: China remains the main deflationary force in the global system, and policy is still aimed at supply rather than demand. Reuters reports consumer prices are back in decline as the recovery wobbles [28] , the New York Times says falling prices are renewing fears of deflation [29] , and the WSJ writes that China is making too much stuff and other countries are worried [30] . The constructive counterpoint is official: the IMF upgraded its 2023 and 2024 growth forecasts [31] , and a central bank adviser told Reuters the economy is still in a medium-speed growth stage [32] . The stance stays Bearish for China-linked risk assets because the growth that exists is being exported as lower prices rather than converted into domestic profits.

PMIs & Activity: The Caixin survey confirmed an October manufacturing slump [33] . Trade was split: imports rose while exports fell for a sixth straight month [34] . September industrial profits extended gains on policy support [35] , but margins rebuilt on cost cuts are fragile while factory-gate prices fall. Consumption is the swing factor and Singles Day sent mixed signals: Statista says it blew away US Cyber Week in scale [36] , while Reuters Breakingviews argues it exposed buyers' commitment issue [37] . Reuters also reports China car sales are seen up 20% in November as makers eye sales goals [38] , volume that makers are pushing to hit targets, which says little about organic demand.

Credit Impulse: The credit story is about composition, not quantity. Reuters reports that loans to high-tech manufacturing are raising fears of a wave of cheap exports [39] , and the New York Times sums up the new economic plan as more semiconductors, less housing [40] . Credit routed into capacity rather than household balance sheets adds supply to a deflationary economy, depressing prices at home and export prices abroad. Vice Premier He Lifeng now heads the top financial policy bodies [41] , concentrating allocation decisions in one office. For global portfolios, the Chinese impulse supports industrial volumes but undermines pricing power for anyone competing with Chinese exports, from European autos to solar.

Property: Property is moving from denial to managed restructuring. Reuters reported exclusively that authorities asked Ping An to take a controlling stake in Country Garden [42] , while the South China Morning Post says the developer's sales remain at deflated levels amid collapsed demand [43] . New home prices fell 0.3% in September [44] , and Bloomberg said the decline accelerated in a blow to sentiment [45] . An insurer-led rescue would protect completion of pre-sold homes but shift developer risk onto an insurance balance sheet, and it cannot restore buyer confidence while prices are still falling. Reuters' list of measures to shore up the indebted sector [46] is long; the test is whether any of it reaches buyers rather than developers.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Fiscal policy is where the regime may be shifting. Bloomberg reports the 2024 budget deficit is seen above 3.5% in a break with past practice [47] , which would finally put the central balance sheet behind demand. Diplomacy is the near-term catalyst: Axios reports Biden and Xi have made their meeting official [48] , the Guardian says it will take place on the sidelines of the APEC summit [49] , and Reuters previews what to expect from the talks in San Francisco [50] . Technology friction persists beneath the summitry: CNBC reports Nvidia will sell new chips to China that still meet US rules [51] , while Reuters says Baidu ordered AI chips from Huawei in a shift away from Nvidia [52] . Beijing has also tightened exports of graphite, a key battery material [53] , a reminder of its leverage over supply chains.

Markets & Flows: Markets are voting with their feet. The Hang Seng fell 1.76% on Friday to 17,203.26 and is down 5.67% over 20 sessions, while the Shanghai Composite slipped 0.47% to 3,038.97; Alibaba's Hong Kong line lost 3.10% to 79.60 HKD, Tencent is down 4.36% and Baidu's ADR 15.37% over 20 sessions, while Xiaomi bucked the trend with a 26.70% gain (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-10). The WSJ says China is becoming a problem for investors [54] , and Vanguard has left a China partnership while keeping billions invested [55] . Domestic speculation has found odd outlets, with Bloomberg describing a meme-stock craze around the dragon zodiac [56] .

New / Most Important: Three items matter most. First, the request that Ping An take control of Country Garden [42] signals state-directed balance-sheet transfers rather than open-ended bailouts. Second, a deficit above 3.5% [47] would be the first credible demand-side lever of this cycle. Third, the Biden-Xi meeting [48] can freeze or extend export-control escalation, and CBS frames a fragile global economy as being at stake [57] . The cost of tech isolation is already visible: SMIC, China's largest chipmaker, posted an 80% drop in Q3 profit [58] , and Zeekr's US IPO paperwork revealed a wider loss as Geely shares fell [59] .

Playbook ✓: Stay underweight Hong Kong-listed China internet and property-linked exposure, funded against FXI, which fell 2.54% over five sessions; express any constructive view through US-listed PDD, up 2.77% over 20 sessions, rather than the index (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-10). Upgrade on confirmation of the larger 2024 deficit, a stop in the fall of new home prices and an APEC outcome that freezes new export controls; downgrade if the Country Garden rescue stalls. ⚠ Divergence: the IMF is raising China's growth forecasts [31] and November car sales are seen up 20% [38] while prices fall and equities slide, so real activity is firmer than the price signal implies; if that volume starts converting into profits, the underweight is wrong.

Corporate & Sectors: PKO BP, the country's largest bank, posted a record profit that Business Insider Polska says Swiss-franc borrowers did not derail this time [109] , and Pekao reported another strong quarter with mortgage sales up sevenfold [110] . Geopolitics & Security: Security news cuts both ways for the risk premium. Reuters reports Germany will withdraw its Patriot air-defence units from Poland [116] , while Warsaw agreed a £4 billion deal with the UK for air-defence missiles and launchers [117] . Polish truckers blocking border crossings leave Ukraine fuming, according to Politico Europe [118] , a trade irritant the new government inherits. Russian rhetoric stays hostile: Interia reports Vladimir Solovyov again threatening Poland with the line that Russia will not stop in Warsaw [119] . On the Belarus border, a Syrian was shot by a Polish soldier in what authorities called an unfortunate accident [120] . Defence spending is a fiscal claim on the next budget, but it is also an order book for domestic industry.

New / Most Important: The fight over the central bank is the dominant new risk. In his first appearance since PiS lost the election, Wyborcza notes, Glapiński played a film [121] ; he has since said that whoever attacks the central bank attacks Poland [122] and that he and the NBP are being attacked by people who want to adopt the euro [123] . Rzeczpospolita reports Mucha striking at Glapiński over a possible State Tribunal case [124] , a former adviser to President Duda has gone to war with Glapiński with serious accusations [125] , and Wyborcza describes a conflict between Glapiński, the NBP board and the RPP [126] . Council member Ludwik Kotecki says rates should be held for longer [127] . A hold that looks political makes the next cut harder to price, which supports bank margins and the currency but raises institutional risk if the confrontation escalates.

Playbook ✓: Stay overweight Polish banks through PKO BP and Pekao, hedged with an underweight in state-controlled PGE and Tauron, whose governance discount can widen during the handover. Add on a confirmed Tusk cabinet and a credible path to the EU funds whose future DW is questioning [128] ; cut on a formal State Tribunal motion against the NBP governor or a reversal in the currency. Keep KGHM underweight while copper sits at 3.58 USD/lb, down 2.48% over five sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-10). ⚠ Divergence: the NBP paused easing and projects a dark inflation scenario [88] while real wages accelerate, yet the zloty sits at record strength and banks price a benign handover; Business Insider Polska also reports the largest fall in currency reserves in history [129] , which does not fit a simple strong-currency story.
