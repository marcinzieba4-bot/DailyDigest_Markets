🌍 Geographic Macro Overview

🇺🇸 United States  Bullish  Fed on hold; curve bull-flattening

Macro Stance: The disinflation trade has matured into a 'Fed is done' rally, and the evidence is broad enough to take seriously. October CPI was flat on the month with core at a two-year low [1] , and producer prices slid 0.5%, the biggest drop since 2020 [2] . When both price gauges roll over together, the Fed's reaction function shifts from 'how high' to 'how long', which is exactly what the front end is now pricing. Equities have taken the benign reading: the S&P 500 closed at 4,547.38, up 7.83% over 20 sessions, and the VIX sank to 13.41, down 34.17% (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). The conflict is growth rather than inflation: the Conference Board sees the economy sputtering toward a 'very short recession' [3] , so the next leg depends on whether the cooling stays orderly.

Rates & Curve: The Treasury curve is doing the Fed's easing for it. The 2Y closed at 4.89% and the 10Y at 4.42% (US Treasury, prior close 2023-11-20), leaving 10Y-2Y at -47 bps and 10Y-3M at -112 bps. Over 20 observations the 2Y fell 16 bps and the 10Y 44 bps, so the spread moved 28 bps deeper: the curve shape is still inverted and the 20-observation move is a bull flattening, with 21 bps of the 10Y decline arriving in the last five observations. A long end falling faster than the front is term-premium relief rather than aggressive cut pricing, and it fed straight into the dollar index, down 2.07% in five sessions to 103.47 (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). The weak link is the buyer base, because a curve rally built on falling term premium needs a steady marginal buyer: the WSJ asks where all the foreign buyers of Treasury debt have gone [4] , so the long end now leans on domestic duration demand that can reverse quickly.

New / Most Important: The weekend's OpenAI turmoil lifted Microsoft: it hired former OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and its stock hit an all-time high [5] , closing at 377.44 USD, up 2.05%, while the Nasdaq 100 added 1.19% to 16,027.06 against 0.74% for the S&P 500 (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). Investors treated a governance crisis at a key partner as a consolidation of AI talent and compute inside a cash-rich incumbent. Nvidia's pending Q3 report, previewed bullishly by analysts [6] , now decides whether AI earnings can lift an index that has gone 471 days without an all-time high [7] .

Playbook ✓: Stay long duration and quality growth while the curve bull-flattens: hold 10Y exposure and Nasdaq 100 core positions funded from bills. Add rate-sensitive small caps, with the Russell 2000 up 5.96% in five sessions, only on dips. The call changes if the 10Y climbs back toward 4.86%, its level 20 observations ago, or if the VIX returns toward 20.37. With hedge-fund crowding at a new record [8] , protect through index puts rather than by cutting longs.

🇪🇺 European Union  Mixed  ECB on hold; German budget shock

Macro Stance: Europe is borrowing America's duration rally rather than generating its own growth. Brussels cut its EU growth forecast again to just 0.6% this year [9] , yet the Euro Stoxx 50 closed at 4,342.41 and the DAX at 15,901.33, each up 7.44% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). That gap between flat activity and rising prices is a valuation bet on lower discount rates, which makes European equities more rate-sensitive than their earnings justify. The DAX and the Euro Stoxx 50 are trading the US 10Y, not the German order book.

Activity & Inflation: Disinflation is genuine, with German inflation set to decline further as producer prices fall [10] , but the fiscal leg has turned negative. Germany's top court dealt a 60 billion euro budget blow to the Scholz government [11] just as German housebuilding is collapsing [12] . Lower inflation plus enforced restraint is a growth-negative mix, and the ECB, on hold for the first time in over a year [13] , has little reason to rescue it quickly. Real rates therefore tighten passively as inflation falls, the opposite of the US, where the curve is doing the easing.

New / Most Important: The sovereign channel looks contained: Reuters found Italian debt markets sanguine ahead of Moody's ratings test [14] . With the periphery calm, any spread stress is now more likely to originate in Germany's forced fiscal reshuffle, which hits public investment, construction and the domestic supply chain first. The STOXX 600 closed at 456.26, up 5.33% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20), lagging the US on both growth and policy support, and SAP, up 12.91% over 20 sessions, is the type of global software earner that still leads.

Playbook ✓: Prefer European duration and quality exporters to domestic cyclicals. Treat FEZ, up 11.28% over 20 sessions in dollar terms (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20), as a dollar-weakness trade rather than a growth call. Upgrade if Berlin restores investment spending or activity data lift; downgrade if Italian spreads start to react to the German shock.

🇨🇳 China  Mixed  Quantity easing; targeted property funding

Macro Stance: China is stabilising at the margin without reflating. Factory output and consumption beat forecasts, but property remained a drag [15] , and Beijing is adding administrative firepower rather than a bazooka: Premier Li Qiang was named head of a powerful financial body [16] . Putting the premier over financial policy centralises the response to local-government and developer stress, which markets read as a floor under tail risk rather than a demand shock. The Shanghai Composite closed at 3,068.32, up 4.39% over 20 sessions, and the Hang Seng rose 1.86% on the day to 17,778.07 (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). The stance stays Mixed because housing, the channel that matters for households, is still broken, and household confidence follows home prices rather than policy headlines.

PMIs & Activity: The activity mix is uneven. Exports fell for a sixth straight month even as imports rose [17] ; rising imports against contracting exports point to domestic restocking under policy support rather than an external recovery. Restocking also explains why construction-linked commodities, with copper up 6.70% over 20 sessions, have outrun consumer-facing Chinese equities such as Alibaba's Hong Kong line, down 5.16% (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). The bigger cross-border risk is the export of deflation: Reuters reports that China's high-tech manufacturing loans raise fears of a wave of cheap exports [18] . If that capacity meets weak global goods demand, it lowers tradable-goods prices abroad, which helps Western disinflation but squeezes European and Japanese competitors in autos, machinery and solar equipment.

Credit Impulse: Easing is coming through quantity, not price. The PBOC boosted its liquidity injection through a policy loan with the rate unchanged [19] , and China is eyeing $137 bln of new funding to boost the housing market [20] . That mix routes credit through policy banks to builders and state projects, so the impulse should show up first in construction materials and only later, if at all, in household mortgages, which depend on confidence that homes will be completed. The implication is a sequence of incremental packages rather than one decisive move, and a credit impulse that firms gradually instead of spiking. Watch the policy-loan channel rather than headline rate cuts: a further quantity injection would matter more to builders than a token cut to benchmark rates.

Property: Property remains the binding constraint. CNBC reports that China's unfinished property projects are 20 times the size of Country Garden [21] , and Moody's cut Longfor below investment grade [22] , a sign that even the stronger private developers are losing market access. That combination is the core problem: buyers are unlikely to pay deposits on off-plan homes while completions are in doubt, and developers cannot finish homes without presales or bond funding. Policy-bank money can break the loop for state-linked builders, but private developers stay on the wrong side of it until home-price declines slow. For bondholders that means recovery values, not coupons, now drive private-developer debt.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: The technology front is where the trade relationship keeps tightening: Applied Materials is under a US criminal probe over shipments to SMIC [23] , and Alibaba scrapped its cloud spin-off citing US chip export bans [24] . The second case shows export controls now reshape Chinese corporate structure, not just chip supply. Beijing's monetary diplomacy is the counter-move: its central bank signed a currency swap worth $7 billion with Saudi Arabia [25] , building renminbi settlement channels that reduce exposure to US financial leverage in energy trade. For multinationals the lesson is that China revenue now carries a regulatory discount set in Washington as much as in Beijing.

Markets & Flows: Money is rotating within China rather than into it. Bridgewater sold Xpeng, Yum and TSMC as hedge funds cut China bets [26] . Alibaba's Hong Kong line fell 7.69% in five sessions to 74.45 HKD, while Tencent rose 13.01% over 20 sessions to 326.60 HKD and KWEB gained 3.72% on the day (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). The pricing rule is clear: platforms with clean domestic cash generation are bought as policy winners, while any business whose structure depends on US chip access trades at a growing discount. ASHR, the CSI 300 tracker, is up 5.32% over 20 sessions and FXI 5.70%, well short of the Nasdaq 100's 9.74%, so China is participating in the global rally without leading it.

New / Most Important: The structural story is national-champion substitution. Hybrid vehicle sales are surging in China, posing a fresh threat to foreign automakers [27] ; plug-in hybrids at local price points attack exactly the profit pool that European and Japanese brands relied on. For portfolios, China exposure through foreign consumer brands is increasingly a share-loss bet, while domestic leaders take the policy tailwind: NIO's US line rose 5.55% on the day to 7.80 USD and JD.com 10.87% in five sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20).

Playbook ✓: Own China through cash-rich internet platforms such as Tencent and KWEB, and through materials tied to state-led construction, not through developers or foreign consumer brands. Add if the housing funding is deployed and home-price declines narrow; cut if exports keep contracting and the restocking impulse fades. Keep Alibaba underweight until its restructuring path clears, since analysts cut price targets by up to 25% after the snag [28] . ⚠ Divergence: iron ore holds near a two-year high [29] and Hong Kong equities rally on policy hopes, while developer credit and unfinished-home backlogs still deteriorate; markets are pricing a floor that the housing data have not yet confirmed.

Growth is the complication, with the economy shrinking far more than expected in the third quarter [32] , which leaves the BOJ normalising into weakness rather than strength, a harder path that argues for gradual rather than abrupt policy change. A firmer yen compresses exporters' translated profits at the same time as the BOJ debate points to higher domestic yields, so the earnings mix shifts toward banks and domestic demand. Within the index, SoftBank Group rose 6.91% in five sessions while Sony slipped 1.18% on the day, a split between global-tech asset stories and consumer-facing exporters.

Playbook ✓: Rotate from exporters to domestic reflation: favour banks and consumer names over autos, while keeping semiconductor equipment as the global-AI exception, with Tokyo Electron up 19.02% over 20 sessions to 23,905.00 JPY (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). 🇦🇺 Australia  Mixed  RBA hiked; housing-led inflation

Macro Stance: The RBA is the hawkish outlier among developed central banks. It hiked the cash rate by 25 basis points to 4.35% [36] , a decision that puts inflation control ahead of near-term growth. The reason is domestic: housing lies at the heart of the RBA's inflation problem [37] , because housing costs can keep services inflation sticky even as goods prices cool. The ASX 200 closed at 7,058.40, up only 3.13% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20), a laggard against global equities.

Demand & Financing: Households carry the adjustment through mortgage repayments, while the export side carries the economy. Reuters says iron ore defies commodity gloom on China property hopes [38] , and the miners have priced it: Fortescue is up 21.58% over 20 sessions to 25.30 AUD and BHP 7.80% to 46.74 AUD (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). That split, strong terms of trade alongside squeezed household cash flow, is why the RBA can tighten into weakness without a currency crisis.

Playbook ✓: Prefer miners to banks: Chinese construction support helps iron ore, while a cycle-high cash rate squeezes mortgage books, and CBA's 5.47% 20-session gain to 103.17 AUD (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20) already prices resilience. The call changes if iron ore loses its two-year high [29] or if the RBA signals a pause that revives housing-sensitive cyclicals. Lynas, up 11.04% over 20 sessions, offers a rare-earths angle on the same export strength without the China-steel concentration.

🇵🇱 Poland  Bullish  NBP on hold; post-election transition

Macro Stance: Poland is the region's cleanest transition trade: a pro-EU change of power after the October 15 election, an economy just out of contraction, and a central bank under political siege. GDP grew for the first time in two quarters, as 300Gospodarka put it, the economy 'bouncing off the bottom' [39] , and Bankier reports real wages rising at the fastest pace since February 2022 [40] , which puts consumption back in the driver's seat. TVN24 reports inflation came in higher than estimated [41] , which argues against further NBP easing. The stance is Bullish on Polish assets, with the institutional fight over the central bank, not growth, as the main tail risk; the combination of rising real wages and a firm currency is the classic setup for domestic-demand equities.

Markets: The zloty is the scoreboard. Rzeczpospolita says the currency 'impresses with strength', with the euro at its cheapest since September 2020 [42] , and Gazeta.pl reports the zloty at its strongest in years as 'another barrier' broke [43] . The move is part global, with the DXY down 2.07% in five sessions, and part domestic, consistent with a shrinking governance discount. Equities confirm it: the Beta ETF WIG20TR tracker closed at 40.77 PLN, up 10.97% over 20 sessions, EPOL rose 9.87% in five sessions to 22.04 USD, and PKO Bank Polski gained 20.06% over 20 sessions to 48.00 PLN, with Pekao up 19.66% to 139.40 PLN (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20). For dollar-based investors, currency and equity gains are compounding, which is why the rally has been so steep. Breadth is wide: mBank is up 18.27%, Dino 16.96% and LPP 16.25% over 20 sessions, while PZU lagged at 5.83%, so leadership sits in rate- and consumption-sensitive names rather than defensives.

Fiscal & Politics: Fiscal politics now revolve around who owns the deficit. Rzeczpospolita quotes Kotecki that Poland has the second-highest deficit in the EU and the European Commission will react [44] . The outgoing prime minister, Mateusz Morawiecki, is still attempting a cabinet, promising a government within 7-8 days and an offer to opposition MPs [45] , but the incoming coalition holds the bigger lever: Business Insider Polska reports the EU will unblock KPO recovery funds for Tusk 'as an incentive', with a catch attached [46] . The deficit debate therefore shifts from whether to spend to how fast EU grants can replace domestic borrowing, a substitution that would cap bond supply even if headline spending stays high. For bond investors the sequencing matters more than the politics: early EU money funds energy and infrastructure investment without crowding out domestic savers, who rushed into Treasury savings bonds again [47] .

Corporate & Sectors: Banks are the market's vote on the transition, though legal tails persist: law firms are 'going after WIBOR', but court decisions have so far disappointed them [48] , keeping litigation provisions contained for now. Allegro had a good third quarter and plans entry into further markets [49] . Pepco is the outlier: Wiadomości Handlowe reports brokers cut the recommendation and more than halved the target price [50] , yet the stock jumped 9.96% on the day to 21.30 PLN, which looks more like short covering than a re-rating. KGHM rose 12.16% in five sessions to 119.00 PLN as copper gained 4.10%, while Orlen, up 2.63% on the day to 63.90 PLN, is still down 2.31% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-20), carrying the political discount of a state champion. XTB, the retail broker, is up 9.77% over 20 sessions to 35.06 PLN, a direct play on higher trading activity in a rising market.

Geopolitics & Security: Security spending is a structural support for Polish industry. Money.pl reports PGZ will build a 'super-radar' for the Patriot system [51] , pulling defence work and technology transfer into domestic supply chains, and the threat backdrop justifies it, with Interia citing a report that NATO has five years to be ready [52] . The economic friction point is the eastern border, where Polish truck protests have left Ukrainian drivers stranded [53] , an irritant the new government inherits and one that matters for logistics firms and for Poland's role in Ukraine's reconstruction. Across the frontier, Onet reports Russia's Duma adopted a budget with record defence spending [54] , which locks in the rationale for Poland's own procurement for years.

New / Most Important: The NBP has become the political battleground. TVN24 reports the bank has complained to the ECB, saying 'the letters have been sent' [55] , as Glapiński faces a possible State Tribunal case over bond purchases [56] ; Morawiecki says the NBP president should in no way face the Tribunal [57] , and NBP's first deputy president, interviewed by Business Insider Polska, calls the reasons 'trumped-up and political' [58] , framing the dispute as a test of independence rather than of policy. The market implication cuts both ways: a credible, depoliticised central bank would compress the risk premium further, but a drawn-out legal war over its governor would make monetary policy look like a political prize and widen it, starting with the long end of the bond curve. For now the bank stocks are signalling that investors see the dispute as noise around a policy path that does not change.

Playbook ✓: Stay overweight Polish banks and broad equity through EPOL and the WIG20TR tracker, funding part of it from squeezed names such as Pepco rather than adding leverage. Add on confirmation of a Tusk-led cabinet and a KPO disbursement path; markets appear to treat the outgoing government's cabinet attempt as a formality. The call changes if the Tribunal fight escalates into open conflict over interest-rate decisions. ⚠ Divergence: the zloty and bank stocks price a smooth, EU-friendly transition, while the NBP-government clash and a deficit flagged as the EU's second highest [44] point to a messier fiscal and institutional path.
