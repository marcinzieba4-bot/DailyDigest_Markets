🇺🇸 United States  Mixed  Fed on hold, hike option kept

Macro Stance: The Federal Reserve held rates for a second straight meeting and, in the same statement, upgraded its assessment of economic growth, which keeps the next move a genuine two-way call rather than a done deal [1] [2] . Powell hedged on whether the tightening campaign is over and said rate cuts are not on the radar right now, so the relief on offer is about the end of hikes, not the start of easing [3] [4] . The economy runs at two speeds: third-quarter GDP grew at a 4.9% annual pace [5] , while the October ISM manufacturing PMI printed 46.7, still in contraction [6] . We stay Mixed: friendly to duration, not yet a green light for cyclicals.

Rates & Curve: The 2Y closed at 4.95% and the 10Y at 4.77% (US Treasury, prior close 2023-11-01), leaving 10Y-2Y at -18 bps, so the curve is still inverted. Over 20 observations the 2Y fell 20 bps and the 10Y 4 bps, a bull steepening; over five observations the 10Y dropped 18 bps against 13 bps for the 2Y, consistent with a term premium being priced out of the long end. That relief now has to survive supply: Treasury is stepping up the size of most of its auctions to manage a growing debt load at higher rates [7] [8] . The 3M at 5.57% sits 80 bps above the 10Y, so cash still competes with every risk asset. TLT gained 1.82% on the session and IEF 0.87% (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-01).

New / Most Important: The data into the decision softened at the margin. ADP said private payrolls rose 113,000 in October, less than expected [9] , and the ISM factory gauge stayed below the expansion line [6] . Wall Street rallied on hopes that the Fed's hikes are done [10] : the S&P 500 closed at 4,237.86, up 1.05% on the session, the Nasdaq 100 rose 1.77% and the VIX fell 7.00% to 16.87 (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-01). The bounce starts from a damaged base, after the index had fallen 10% from its July peak [11] . DXY at 106.88 index points rose only 0.21% (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-01), so this was a rates trade rather than a dollar trade.

Playbook ✓: Action: add intermediate duration, preferring IEF to TLT, and buy quality growth on dips while keeping cyclical exposure light. Trigger to extend: an official payrolls print that confirms the ADP softness, the labour data CNBC framed as a major theme of the week [12] , together with orderly demand at the larger auctions. What changes the call: a 10Y back above its 4.81% level of 20 observations ago, which would say refunding supply rather than growth is driving yields, or Fed speakers leaning into the open door for another hike [13] .

We are Bearish on European cyclicals and constructive on euro-area duration as the hedge.

The October PMI sat near a three-year low, stirring recession worries [20] , banks tightened credit standards further [21] , and October inflation eased further than expected [22] . New / Most Important: Markets are pricing relief, not recovery. DW now asks whether German industry, the backbone of the economy, can be saved [26] .

Playbook ✓: Action: underweight European autos and industrials, hold quality defensives and duration. Trigger to turn less negative: a PMI rebound from the three-year low alongside stabilising bank credit standards. What changes the call: a renewed inflation upturn that revives hike talk, since Vujcic's end to hikes holds only for now [15] .

🇨🇳 China  Bearish  Fiscal push meets PMI relapse

Macro Stance: China is trying to borrow its way out of a confidence problem, and October's data say it has not worked yet. Factory activity unexpectedly contracted in October as demand dropped [27] , and the Caixin survey confirmed the manufacturing slump a day later [28] . The policy answer is fiscal: government debt supply surged in October to a 2023 high [29] , and Beijing is choosing fiscal muscle over big reforms to revive the economy [30] . We are Bearish on Chinese cyclical equities and the property chain; the fiscal impulse is real but too slow to arrest a demand-led relapse in the fourth quarter.

PMIs & Activity: The October relapse matters because it broke a tentative stabilisation. September industrial profits had extended their gains with the help of policy support [31] , and The Independent framed the new contraction as a faltering pandemic recovery [32] . Prices tell the same story: consumer prices stalled and factory deflation persisted in September [33] . OilPrice.com tied the weakness directly to crude, arguing China's economic woes are weighing on oil [34] ; Brent fell 6.10% over five sessions to 84.63 USD/bbl (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-01). The pockets of strength are specific rather than broad: Xpeng and Li Auto delivered a record number of cars in October [35] , and BYD posted a record third-quarter profit after beating Tesla and others on deliveries [36] .

Credit Impulse: Credit is tightening at the short end even as Beijing leans on bond issuance. Short-term money rates spiked and markets now expect more policy easing [37] ; Reuters reported some Chinese institutions borrowing at a 50% rate as liquidity was squeezed [38] . That is the signature of a bond-supply surge colliding with a cautious central bank: the heavy government debt supply [29] has to be absorbed by the same banks whose margins are already shrinking, as ICBC's scant profit gain shows [39] . A credit impulse routed through state banks into local projects can support metals demand at the margin, but it does not repair household balance sheets, which is where the consumption gap sits.

Property: Property remains the drag that fiscal policy cannot quickly offset. New home prices fell 0.3% in September [40] , and Bloomberg described the faster pace of decline as a blow to sentiment [41] . China Evergrande's winding-up hearing was adjourned again as it seeks a new debt deal [42] , keeping the restructuring overhang alive, and Al Jazeera documented the crumbling, unfinished homes that keep buyers away [43] . The WSJ reported that Xi Jinping is looking for someone to blame for the property bust [44] , a hint that the political response may favour discipline over a broad bailout. Developers stay uninvestable until presales stabilise; the policy put sits with state banks and local governments, not with private developers' equity holders.

Fiscal, Policy & Trade: Personnel and diplomacy are moving faster than the economy. He Lifeng, Beijing's economy tsar, was made director of a key party commission [45] , elevating the official already steering economic policy. Abroad, the White House is aiming for a Biden-Xi meeting in San Francisco in November and says there is an agreement in principle [46] [47] , although the SCMP warns that differing objectives will limit what it delivers [48] . Supply-side policy is still being used as leverage: China tightened exports of graphite, a key battery material [49] , is mulling copper curbs to reduce overcapacity and emissions [50] , and has imposed growth limits on its vast oil-refining industry [51] . Those curbs mildly support global commodity prices even as domestic demand softens.

Markets & Flows: Onshore and offshore equities are flatlining rather than capitulating. The Shanghai Composite closed at 3,023.08, up 1.65% over five sessions but down 2.55% over 20, and the Hang Seng sits at 17,101.78, down 1.32% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-01). Reuters noted micro-caps shining in a flagging share market [52] , and Hong Kong stocks rose by the most in four weeks as more Chinese companies joined buy-back plans [53] . China's biggest fund manager has been raising bets on liquor distillers and selling tech giants [54] , and Moutai jumped after its first price increase in six years [55] . Internet names lag: KWEB is down 2.11% and Tencent 4.52% over 20 sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-01). Even gold is being packaged for domestic savers, with a Chinese firm launching stock indices to tap the rush into the safe haven [56] .

New / Most Important: The newest signals are consumer-facing and mixed. Canada Goose cut its sales forecast on a rocky China recovery [57] , Yum China shares fell to a one-year low as quarterly results disappointed [58] , and e.l.f. Beauty's CEO said the company will stay away from China [59] . Against that, Qualcomm beat estimates on China strength [60] , and Alibaba upgraded its Tongyi Qianwen AI model and released industry-specific versions [61] . The split is consistent: Chinese demand is still there for upgrade cycles in smartphones, EVs and premium liquor, but not for discretionary Western brands.

Playbook ✓: Action: stay underweight Chinese cyclicals, developers and Western consumer names with China exposure, and express any China upside through policy-favoured pockets such as EV leaders, premium consumer and onshore A-shares via ASHR, up 2.30% over five sessions (Yahoo Finance, prior close 2023-11-01), rather than through broad offshore beta. Trigger to cover: a confirmed Biden-Xi meeting that eases export controls, plus a PMI back above the expansion line. What changes the call: direct support for household income instead of another round of infrastructure borrowing. ⚠ Divergence: industrial profits are still rising with policy support [31] and government bond supply has hit a 2023 high [29] , so the fiscal impulse could surprise to the upside even while both PMIs contract.

New / Most Important: The news flow is moving from arithmetic to portfolios.
