Own the change in the earnings opportunity while retaining clear limits on how much macro recovery is embedded in the valuation.

PMIs & Activity: The August manufacturing evidence remains divided. Credit Impulse: July bank loans unexpectedly contracted, Reuters reported, leaving a weak starting point for the private-credit cycle. [17] The investment implication is that funding availability and demand for borrowing must be analyzed separately. A company issuing a convertible to finance cloud capacity can have a stronger investment pipeline than a household considering a mortgage or a small firm facing uncertain sales. Corporate capital-market access is therefore an advantage for selected leaders, not proof of a broad credit impulse. Watch whether future lending data show a wider appetite for productive investment. If the stock market strengthens while private borrowing remains hesitant, retain technology exposure but keep a higher bar for domestic financial and property beta.
